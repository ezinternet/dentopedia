## Why Ingested

This ex vivo study directly tests a question [[wiki/suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]]'s decision framework leaves open — whether the *choice* of flap-advancement technique (not just its advancement magnitude) affects graft stability during primary wound closure — and adds membrane stabilization via periosteal suturing (PS) as a second, independently manipulated variable. It is also one half of a same-week concurrent ingest pair with Zhou et al. 2025 (BMC Oral Health), a companion study on flap advancement and membrane stabilization for graft displacement in GBR.

## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo preclinical study (porcine hemimandible model), 68 GBR procedures on 34 fresh pig hemimandibles, comparing two flap-advancement techniques — modified periosteal releasing incision (MPRI) vs. mucosal detachment technique (MDT) — each with and without periosteal suturing (PS), on graft material displacement during primary wound closure.

Periosteal suturing significantly reduced graft material displacement at the implant-platform level (ΔGMT −14.0% ± 11.1% with PS vs. −33.2% ± 16.9% without, p < 0.001), while flap-advancement technique (MPRI vs. MDT) and soft-tissue phenotype (keratinized mucosa width, flap thickness) had no significant effect on displacement or on flap advancement itself.

Clinicians should not expect the choice between MPRI and a pouch-style MDT to protect graft material during closure — membrane stabilization by periosteal mattress sutures is the variable that matters; limitation: ex vivo pig-mandible model without postoperative loading, swelling, or healing dynamics.

## 세줄요약

돼지 반측하악골(hemimandible) 모델을 이용한 사체(ex vivo) 전임상 연구로, 총 34개 반측하악골에 68건의 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)을 시행해 두 피판전진술식 — 변형 골막이완절개 (Modified Periosteal Releasing Incision, MPRI) vs 점막박리술식 (Mucosal Detachment Technique, MDT) — 을 골막봉합 (Periosteal Suturing, PS) 유무와 함께 비교했다.

골막봉합은 임플란트 플랫폼 수준에서 이식재 변위를 유의하게 감소시켰으나(ΔGMT −14.0%±11.1%(PS) vs −33.2%±16.9%(무PS), p<0.001), 피판전진 술식(MPRI vs MDT)과 연조직 표현형(각화점막폭·피판두께)은 이식재 변위나 피판전진량 자체에 유의한 영향을 미치지 않았다.

임상적으로는 MPRI와 파우치형 MDT 중 어느 술식을 택하든 이식재 보호 효과 차이는 기대하기 어렵고, 골막 매트리스 봉합을 통한 막 안정화가 관건이다; 한계는 술후 부하·부종·치유 과정이 없는 돼지 사체 모델이라는 점.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to isolate flap-advancement **technique** (as opposed to advancement magnitude alone) and membrane stabilization by periosteal suturing (PS) as two independently randomized variables affecting intraoperative graft material displacement during GBR primary wound closure.
- Shows periosteal suturing significantly reduces graft displacement at the crestal/implant-platform level regardless of which flap-advancement technique is used (no significant MPRI-PS vs. MDT-PS or MPRI+PS vs. MDT+PS interaction).
- Shows two mechanistically distinct flap-advancement techniques (full-thickness flap with periosteal releasing incision vs. split-thickness pouch/mucosal-detachment flap) achieve statistically equivalent flap advancement (~8.3 mm) and equivalent graft protection — countering the assumption that pouch-style flaps better contain graft material.
- Establishes a standardized 0.1 N flap-tension threshold (via spring dynamometer) as the operational definition of "adequate" primary-closure advancement, based on prior clinical evidence that tensions above 0.1 N increase dehiscence risk.
- Confirms soft-tissue phenotype (keratinized mucosa width, flap thickness at 3/6/9 mm) does not predict either flap advancement achieved or graft displacement magnitude in this model.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Ex vivo preclinical study, randomized 2×2 factorial (flap-advancement technique × periosteal suturing), split-site design (two sequential GBR procedures per hemimandible, PS order randomized).
- **Model**: Fresh hemimandibles from 30-week-old pigs (local butcher), n = 34 hemimandibles, second-premolar site (post-coronectomy).
- **n**: 68 GBR procedures total, 4 groups of 17 (MDT+PS, MDT−PS, MPRI+PS, MPRI−PS). Sample size (n=17/group) powered at 80% for a mean difference of 0.6 (SD 0.5, α = 0.05, ANOVA).
- **Defect**: Standardized 2-walled box-shaped defect, 8 × 6 × 3 mm (mesio-distal × apico-coronal × oro-facial).
- **Implant**: SPI Element 4.0 × 11 mm MC (Thommen Medical), centrally placed, platform aligned to lingual bone crest, contour 1 mm within bony housing.
- **Graft**: Particulate deproteinized bovine bone mineral (Bio-Oss, Geistlich) soaked in radiocontrast agent (Gastrolux), overcontoured 2 mm beyond the defect, covered with a native (non-crosslinked) collagen membrane (Bio-Gide, Geistlich). In MDT groups the membrane was tucked into the pre-created buccal periosteal pouch.
- **Periosteal suturing (PS)**: Absorbable vertical mattress sutures (Novosyn 5-0), placed mesially and distally, connecting the periosteum apically (at the horizontal releasing incision) to the lingual mucoperiosteal flap, mechanically securing the membrane.
- **Flap-tension standardization**: A spring dynamometer measured closure tension via a single midcrestal suture; further release (stretching for MPRI, detachment for MDT) continued until tension ≤ 0.1 N (a threshold chosen because forces > 0.1 N were previously shown to increase postoperative dehiscence). Flap advancement (FA) was then measured with a periodontal probe relative to the gingival margin of adjacent teeth.
- **Imaging/outcome**: CBCT (8×8 cm, 80 kV, 6 mAs, PaX-Reve3D) taken before and after primary wound closure. Graft material thickness (GMT) — encompassing both membrane and graft — was measured perpendicular to the implant surface at 7 levels from the implant platform (L0) to 6 mm apical (L6), in 1 mm increments, using a standardized transparent-foil overlay on cross-sectional images. ΔGMT (absolute mm and relative %) computed pre- vs. post-closure.
- **Phenotype**: Keratinized mucosa width (KMW, PCPUNC15 probe) and flap thickness at 3/6/9 mm apical to the gingival margin (FT3/FT6/FT9, transmucosal probing with an endodontic file), recorded pre-surgically.
- **Reliability**: Intra-class correlation coefficient (ICC) = 0.94 for repeated CBCT measurements (half the dataset re-assessed after 1 month) by a single calibrated, blinded examiner.
- **Statistics**: Kruskal–Wallis (multi-group), Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney (pairwise), Spearman's rank correlation (phenotype associations).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Flap advancement (technique comparison)**: MPRI 8.3 ± 1.1 mm vs. MDT 8.3 ± 1.5 mm — no significant difference.

**Overall ΔGMT by technique at L0 (implant platform)**: MPRI −24.3% ± 13.7% vs. MDT −23.0% ± 14.3% (p ≥ 0.085 at every level, L0–L6 — no significant difference at any level).

**Overall ΔGMT by PS status**:
| Level | No PS | PS | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | −33.2% ± 16.9% | −14.0% ± 11.1% | < 0.001 |
| L1 | −21.3% ± 14.3% | −8.5% ± 7.0% | < 0.001 |
| L2 | −11.5% ± 11.0% | −5.4% ± 8.9% | 0.041 |
| L3–L6 | n.s. | n.s. | ≥ 0.12 |

**Within-technique PS effect at L0**: MPRI−PS −35.1% ± 14.9% vs. MPRI+PS −13.5% ± 12.4% (p < 0.001); MDT−PS −31.4% ± 18.8% vs. MDT+PS −14.6% ± 9.9% (p = 0.020). No significant MPRI-PS vs. MDT-PS or MPRI+PS vs. MDT+PS difference at any level (p ≥ 0.267 and p ≥ 0.54 respectively) — i.e., PS's protective effect is independent of which flap-advancement technique is used.

**Phenotype**: KMW 6.8 ± 0.9 mm overall (MPRI 6.9 ± 0.8 mm, MDT 6.7 ± 1.1 mm); FT3 1.6 ± 0.5 mm, FT6 1.0 ± 0.6 mm, FT9 0.8 ± 0.3 mm. No significant association with ΔGMT (p ≥ 0.238) or with FA (p ≥ 0.264).

**Discussion benchmark comparison**: the paper's Discussion cites a prior ex vivo GBR study (citation numbers were stripped in the extracted full text and could not be recovered) reporting −42.8% ± 17.9% GMT reduction without membrane stabilization, reduced to −20.2% ± 18.9% with a block-type graft and −22.9% ± 21.2% with two-pin membrane fixation — broadly consistent in direction with this study's PS effect, though the exact comparator study is not independently confirmed here.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only two flap-advancement techniques within a single flap-design lineage (MPRI, MDT) were tested, both with and without PS; other techniques or graft-stabilization methods (e.g., pins, block grafts, fibrin sealant) may yield different results.
- Primary closures were standardized to ≤ 0.1 N flap tension, but potential confounding from tension variability *within* that sub-threshold range could not be excluded.
- Defect dimensions were standardized, but graft material quantity/condensation was not — condensation's effect on displacement remains uncertain.
- The ex vivo design cannot capture postoperative confounders present clinically: swelling, hematoma formation, mucosal movement from mastication/facial expression, or patient touch.
- The authors flag two areas for future research: the effect of pin number or fibrin sealant on graft displacement, and the long-term durability of the PS stabilizing effect (absorbable-suture degradation, knot/periosteum stretching over time).
- The extracted PMC full text has multiple points where italicized in-text technique abbreviations (MPRI/MDT/PS) and reference-citation numbers were silently dropped by the JATS-to-text conversion, most visibly in one Discussion sentence about hypothesis rejection ("Therefore, H01 and H03 were rejected, while H02 was not") that appears to contradict both the Abstract's plain-language conclusion and the numeric Results (no significant technique/phenotype effect, significant PS effect). This source page follows the Abstract/Results numbers, which are internally consistent, rather than that literal sentence.

## 6. Related Work

- Plonka 2017 (`suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy`): systematic review providing the flap-advancement taxonomy (minor/moderate/major, PASS principle) this study experimentally tests within — MPRI and MDT both fall in the "major" (≥7 mm) advancement stratum here.
- Moslemi 2016 (`suture-wound-closure/moslemi-2016-periosteal-releasing-incision-diode-laser-gbr`): case series on diode-laser execution of the periosteal releasing incision maneuver — an alternative instrumentation for the same PRI concept used here (MPRI).
- Attia 2023 (`suture-wound-closure/attia-2023-coronally-advanced-lingual-flap-titanium-mesh`) and Wang 2024 (`suture-wound-closure/wang-2024-poncho-technique-titanium-mesh-bone-regeneration`): alternative flap-advancement/closure techniques for tension-free GBR closure over titanium mesh, part of the same technique family.
- Raabe 2025, defect-morphology/membrane-fixation study (`bone-regeneration/raabe-2025-defect-morphology-membrane-fixation-graft`): companion ex vivo study by an overlapping author group, using the same pig-hemimandible/CBCT/GMT(L0-L6) methodology but testing defect morphology and pin-based membrane fixation instead of flap technique/PS; its Discussion explicitly echoes this study's null phenotype finding.
- Zhou et al. 2025 (BMC Oral Health; concurrently being ingested as a companion paper at the time of this ingest): a parallel study on flap advancement and membrane stabilization's effect on bone graft displacement in GBR — closely related methodological pair, not yet cross-linked as a typed relation because its wiki page did not exist yet at ingest time.

## 7. Glossary

- **MPRI (Modified Periosteal Releasing Incision)**: A full-thickness flap technique using a shallow (~0.5 mm) periosteal releasing incision plus lateral stretching, as opposed to a deeper (1–3 mm) classic PRI.
- **MDT (Mucosal Detachment Technique)**: A split-thickness "combination flap" / periosteal pouch technique — a split-thickness flap is elevated, then the alveolar bone is locally deperiostated in a pouch-like fashion at the graft site.
- **PS (Periosteal Suturing)**: Absorbable mattress sutures connecting the released periosteum to the opposing (lingual) mucoperiosteal flap, mechanically stabilizing the graft/membrane without pins or screws.
- **GMT (Graft Material Thickness)**: CBCT-measured perpendicular thickness of the graft+membrane complex at defined levels relative to the implant platform.
- **ΔGMT**: Change in GMT (absolute mm or relative %) between pre-closure (GMT_0) and post-closure (GMT_1) CBCT.
- **FA (Flap Advancement)**: Distance the flap margin advances coronally to achieve a standardized ≤0.1 N closure tension, measured with a periodontal probe.
- **KMW (Keratinized Mucosa Width)**: Width of keratinized tissue apico-coronally at the site, probed pre-surgically.
- **FT (Flap Thickness)**: Transmucosal soft-tissue thickness measured at fixed apical distances (3/6/9 mm) from the gingival margin.
