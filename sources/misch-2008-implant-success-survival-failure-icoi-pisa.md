## Why Ingested

The wiki's survival and failure pages ([[implants/survival/alrowis-2025-factors-affecting-dental-implant-failure-retrospective]] and others) report success and failure rates without a shared definition. This ICOI Pisa consensus (Misch 2008) is the source that proposes an implant-specific "Health Scale" for success, survival and failure, so later outcome comparisons can be read against a stated definition.

## Three-line Summary

Consensus conference statement (ICOI, Pisa, Oct 2007; expert panel; no primary data in abstract) — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The paper argues success criteria for endosteal implants must put support of a functional prosthesis first, adapts indices developed for natural teeth into an implant-specific "Health Scale", and updates the definitions of implant success, survival and failure.

Use it as a definitional anchor when comparing implant outcome studies; the abstract gives no numeric outcome data, so it should not be cited for any survival percentage.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Defines an implant-specific "Health Scale" adapted from natural-tooth indices.
- Restates implant success, survival and failure, with prosthetic support and patient esthetic satisfaction as core criteria (as stated in the abstract).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Consensus conference (Pisa, Oct 2007). The abstract does not describe the voting or grading process, nor the literature search.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Qualitative only in the abstract: the Health Scale and the updated success/survival/failure definitions. No numeric benchmarks are reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Expert-consensus document; definitional, not evidence-graded.
- Full text not retrieved; the scale's item-level content is not verified here.
- Outcome studies that use other definitions remain non-comparable with this scale.

## 7. Glossary

- **Survival**: implant remains in place. **Success**: implant meets prosthetic and clinical criteria. **Failure**: implant is removed or cannot support the prosthesis.
