## Why Ingested

같은 연구팀(Peking University School and Hospital of Stomatology)의 임상 후속 연구 [[complete-denture/li-2022-digital-edentulous-maxillomandibular-relationship-jaw-tracking]]의 방법론적 전신(precursor)으로 인제스트. 본 논문은 그 광학 악간추적 시스템(Optical Jaw Tracking System)을 교합기 상 모형으로 벤치 검증한 원 연구이며, 다음 해 같은 팀이 환자 대상 임상연구로 확장한다. 두 논문을 나란히 두면 벤치 정확도가 구강 내 정확도로 얼마나 열화되는지가 정량적으로 드러난다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro accuracy study establishing a recording method for edentulous jaw relations using an optical jaw tracking system, validated against a positioning-cylinder control method on maxillary/mandibular edentulous arch models and complete dentures mounted on an articulator.

Four eccentric jaw relations were simulated — protrusion (A), left laterotrusion (B), right laterotrusion (C), small opening (D) — with 3D mandibular deviation of 131±39 μm, 133±44 μm, 120±51 μm, and 112±52 μm respectively; mean absolute displacement values were all under 200 μm.

Authors conclude bench accuracy is "acceptable for clinical demand," but this is a bench claim on rigid models with no soft tissue, muscle, or mucosal displacement — they themselves flag that patient studies are still required to verify it clinically.

## 세줄요약

광학 악간추적 시스템(Optical Jaw Tracking System)을 이용한 무치악 악간관계(Jaw Relation) 기록법의 벤치(in vitro) 정확도 검증 연구. 상하악 무치악 모형과 총의치(Complete Denture)를 교합기(Articulator)에 장착하고, 위치결정 원기둥(Positioning Cylinder) 좌표를 대조군으로 삼아 검증했다.

전방위(Protrusion, A)·좌측방위(Left Laterotrusion, B)·우측방위(Right Laterotrusion, C)·소개구위(Small Opening, D) 4가지 편심위(Eccentric Position)를 재현했고, 하악 3차원 편차(3D Deviation)는 각각 131±39 μm·133±44 μm·120±51 μm·112±52 μm였다. 절대변위 평균값은 모두 200 μm 미만.

저자들은 "임상적 요구에 acceptable"이라 결론지었으나 이는 어디까지나 경조직 모형·교합기 위의 벤치 수치이며 연조직·근육·의치상 변위가 전혀 없다 — 저자 스스로도 환자 대상 검증이 추가로 필요하다고 명시했다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Establishes and quantitatively validates a recording method for edentulous jaw relations using an optical jaw tracking system, benchmarked against a positioning-cylinder-based control method on the same models.
- Reports sub-200 μm 3D mandibular deviation across four eccentric jaw relations (protrusion, left/right laterotrusion, small opening) — an order of magnitude below the 0.74-0.86 mm that [[complete-denture/singh-2026-nick-notch-intraoral-gothic-arch-jaw-relation]] treats as clinically perceptible, which is consistent with this being a rigid bench measurement rather than a mouth measurement.
- Direct methodological precursor to the same group's patient study [[complete-denture/li-2022-digital-edentulous-maxillomandibular-relationship-jaw-tracking]] (n=10 edentulous patients), which reports 0.76-1.35 mm whole-arch 3D deviation and concludes accuracy "needs to be further improved" — the bench-to-mouth gap is roughly 6-10×, a concrete illustration of how far in vitro accuracy claims travel once soft tissue, muscle, and mucosal displacement are introduced.
- Scopes the validation to eccentric/border-adjacent positions (protrusive and lateral excursions, small opening), not the centric relation record itself — this paper validates trajectory-capture accuracy of the tracking hardware, not centric relation determination.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: in vitro accuracy study, articulator-mounted edentulous arch models and complete dentures.
- **Test group**: optical jaw tracking system recorded 3D trajectories of targets from intercuspation to four positions — protrusion (A), left laterotrusion (B), right laterotrusion (C), small opening (D) — aided by metal foil wax records; trajectories used to rehabilitate digital arch relations for A-D.
- **Control group**: six pairs of positioning cylinders pasted onto axial model surfaces; center points of the cylinder bottom surfaces in A-D measured directly to rehabilitate digital arch relations.
- **Registration**: maxilla used as the common reference area; test-group arch relations registered onto the control group; 3D deviations of the mandible calculated.
- **Outcome decomposition**: displacements analyzed in horizontal left/right, horizontal anterior/posterior, and vertical directions, in addition to overall 3D deviation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**3D mandibular deviation by simulated jaw relation**

| Position | 3D deviation |
|---|---|
| Protrusion (A) | **131 ± 39 μm** |
| Left laterotrusion (B) | **133 ± 44 μm** |
| Right laterotrusion (C) | **120 ± 51 μm** |
| Small opening (D) | **112 ± 52 μm** |

- Mean absolute values of mandibular displacement (horizontal L/R, horizontal A/P, vertical) were all less than 200 μm.
- Authors' conclusion: "the accuracy of jaw relation records as measured on an articulator was acceptable for clinical demand." Immediately qualified by the authors themselves: "Further investigations among patients are required to clinically verify the results of this study."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest — full text not retrieved (paywalled, no OA copy). Exact statistical tests, sample size per position, cylinder placement details, and any discussion/limitations section are not available from the abstract alone.
- **Bench study, not clinical validation.** Rigid arch models and complete dentures on an articulator have no soft tissue, no muscle activity, no mucosal displacement, and no denture-base movement under functional load — all of which affect jaw-relation recording accuracy in the mouth. "Acceptable for clinical demand" is the authors' bench-derived claim; they explicitly state patient studies are still needed.
- Validates eccentric/border positions (protrusion, left/right laterotrusion, small opening) — not centric relation itself. Scope should not be over-read as validating centric relation determination.
- Authors' own stated next step — "Further investigations among patients are required" — was carried out by the same group and reported as [[complete-denture/li-2022-digital-edentulous-maxillomandibular-relationship-jaw-tracking]]: in n=10 edentulous patients, whole-arch 3D deviation rose to 0.76-1.35 mm (95% CI, p<0.001), and that paper concludes accuracy "needs to be further improved" — a roughly 6-10× degradation from the ~0.11-0.13 mm bench figures reported here. This is the single most important caveat for anyone citing this paper's "acceptable for clinical demand" line.

## 7. Glossary

- **Optical jaw tracking system**: hardware that records 3D trajectories of targets fixed to the maxillary/mandibular arches during jaw movement, used here to reconstruct digital arch relations at defined eccentric positions.
- **Protrusion (A)**: forward eccentric mandibular position.
- **Laterotrusion (B/C)**: lateral eccentric mandibular position, left and right.
- **Small opening (D)**: eccentric position with limited mandibular opening from intercuspation.
- **Positioning cylinder**: reference cylinder pasted onto axial model surfaces; its bottom-surface center point serves as the control-group ground truth for arch-relation rehabilitation.
- **3D deviation**: composite positional error of the mandible in the test group relative to the control group, decomposable into horizontal left/right, horizontal anterior/posterior, and vertical components.
