## Three-line Summary

Pediatric case review (Temple University 2013; Pediatr Dent 35(1):71-3) documenting a 7-year-old child who lost consciousness during dental treatment due to neurocardiogenic syncope, with description of clinical manifestations and remedial management.

The case illustrates that neurocardiogenic syncope (a synonym for vasovagal syncope) frequently occurs in children, and dental treatment is a known trigger; the abstract reports loss of consciousness during the appointment and successful recovery with standard supportive management.

Abstract-only; no quantitative outcomes, no comparator — clinical teaching case for pediatric dentists.
