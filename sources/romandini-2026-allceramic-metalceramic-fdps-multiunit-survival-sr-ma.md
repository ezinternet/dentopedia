## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Part 2, 41 studies, January 2014–December 2024 + 17 prior SR studies; Poisson regression) on survival, failure, and complications of tooth-supported multi-unit FDPs: veneered dense-sintered Zr 92.9%, metal-ceramic 91.3%, glass-infiltrated alumina 88.4%, monolithic dense-sintered Zr 87.9%, LD 82.5% at 5 years; LD significantly lower than MC (p<0.05); 71.0% complication-free at 5 years.

All-ceramic FDPs had higher rates of marginal caries and loss of retention than metal-ceramic; framework fractures prevalent in LD and glass-infiltrated alumina FDPs (>10% at 5yr); monolithic zirconia had least chipping of all ceramic options.

Veneered and monolithic densely sintered zirconia FDPs demonstrate comparable 5-year survival to metal-ceramic; LD and glass-infiltrated alumina are unsuitable for multi-unit FDPs due to high fracture rates; monolithic zirconia is the preferred all-ceramic FDP material.

## One-line Summary

SR+MA (41 studies, n=2,132 FDPs) showing veneered/monolithic dense-sintered zirconia FDPs achieve comparable 5-year survival to metal-ceramic (92.9%/87.9% vs 91.3%), while LD FDPs have significantly lower survival (82.5%) and high fracture rates, making monolithic zirconia the preferred all-ceramic multi-unit option.

## 한줄요약

다유닛 FDP SR+MA(41편): 치밀소결 Zr(베니어드/모놀리식) ≒ MC(92.9%/87.9% vs 91.3%), LD 82.5%(유의하게 낮음) + 프레임워크 파절 10% 이상 → 다유닛 전세라믹 FDP는 모놀리식 지르코니아 우선.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA specifically for multi-unit FDPs covering 2014–2024 (Part 1 covered single crowns)
- Establishes veneered dense-sintered Zr FDPs as clinically equivalent to MC FDPs
- Demonstrates LD FDPs have significantly lower survival than MC in multi-unit scenario (unlike single crowns)
- Confirms monolithic Zr as best all-ceramic FDP option: least chipping + survival comparable to MC
- 71.0% complication-free rate at 5 years for all FDPs

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** SR+MA
- **Search:** MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL; January 2014–December 2024; + 17 studies from prior SRs (earlier periods)
- **Inclusion:** RCTs + prospective studies on tooth-supported FDPs; mean follow-up ≥ 3 years
- **Sample:** 41 studies; 600 metal-ceramic + 1,532 all-ceramic FDPs
- **Statistics:** Robust Poisson regression models → pooled 5-year survival estimates

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Material | 5-Year Survival | vs MC |
|---|---|---|
| Veneered dense-sintered Zr | 92.9% | NS |
| Metal-ceramic (reference) | 91.3% | — |
| Glass-infiltrated alumina | 88.4% | NS |
| Monolithic dense-sintered Zr | 87.9% | NS |
| LD glass-ceramic | 82.5% | p<0.05 (sig. lower) |

- Complication-free at 5 years: 71.0%
- All-ceramic vs MC: higher marginal caries + loss of retention
- Framework fractures: LD + glass-infiltrated alumina >10% at 5yr (highest)
- Chipping: least in monolithic Zr

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Mixed study designs (RCT + prospective) reduces homogeneity
- Short search window start (2014) addressed by supplementing prior SRs
- Multi-unit FDP data remains scarcer than single crown data
- No long-term (≥10yr) data for newer materials
