## Why Ingested

Adds first-in-wiki cohort data on a machined long-neck (basal-anchored) implant placed in the atrophic anterior ridge, reporting 1-year survival, success, marginal bone loss and CBCT bone dimensions. It extends the ARi case report in [[implants/park-2025-ari-implant-severely-resorbed-ridges]] to a 117-implant cohort and gives a Korean-setting comparator for basal-implant survival data such as [[implants/survival/hassan-2023-basal-implant-condensing-osteitis-hyperdense-mandibular]] and 1-year MBL benchmarks in [[implants/mbl/malak-2024-implant-failure-marginal-bone-loss]]. → [[implants/mbl/malak-2024-implant-failure-marginal-bone-loss]]

## Three-line Summary

Single-centre retrospective cohort (Mir Dental Hospital, Daegu, South Korea; 58 patients, 117 machined long-neck ARi implants, anterior/premolar sites, placed Feb 2022 to Dec 2023) with 1-year post-loading follow-up.

Implant-level cumulative survival was 97.43% (3 failures; patient-level 94.83%), success (no mobility/symptoms/radiolucency, MBL under 1.5 mm) was 91.45% implant-level and 82.76% patient-level, mean MBL was 0.46 +/- 1.05 mm, and CBCT bone width/height changes were about 0.2 mm or less.

Short-term outcomes were acceptable, but follow-up was only 1 year, GBR was non-randomized, CBCT data covered only 32 implants, and authors include implant-manufacturer employees/founder, so the evidence is preliminary.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research (2026); DOI 10.1111/cid.70126; PMID 41848046; PMCID PMC12997391
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, STROBE-reported; IRB approval P01-202504-01-021
- **Setting**: Mir Dental Hospital, Daegu, South Korea
- **Period**: implants placed February 2022 to December 2023; 1-year follow-up after prosthetic loading
- **Population**: 58 patients (30 men, 28 women; mean age 54.36 +/- 9.32 years), 117 implants; no smokers (0/58); diabetes in 5 patients (7 implants)
- **Conflicts of interest**: two authors employed by the implant manufacturer; one author is the manufacturer's founder

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First cohort in the wiki on a novel implant with a threaded body for dense basal bone engagement plus a machined, micro-grooved long neck (cuff 4-10 mm) for ridge-deficient anterior sites.
2. Implant-level 1-year survival 97.43% and success 91.45%; patient-level 94.83% and 82.76%.
3. Mean 1-year MBL 0.46 +/- 1.05 mm with small CBCT horizontal and vertical bone changes.
4. GBR vs non-GBR mixed-model subgroup: localized benefit at mid-buccal width and palatal vertical height only.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Implant**: Ari system (Megagen), external connection; threaded body fully embedded in bone, machined cuff positioned at dehiscence sites. Platform aligned to crestal bone level (equicrestal). Diameters 3.8 mm (34) and 4.3 mm (83); lengths 5 mm (1), 7 mm (83), 9 mm (33); cuff heights 4, 6, 8, 10 mm (19, 80, 11, 7). Sites: maxillary anterior 53, maxillary premolar 25, mandibular anterior 26, mandibular premolar 13. Splinted restorations 89, single 28.
- **Surgery**: placement at least 2 months after extraction, CBCT planning, envelope full-thickness flap; GBR (resorbable membrane + synthetic or bovine graft) when dehiscence was seen at the cuff (90 of 117 implants, 76.92%). Provisional restoration at least 3 months after placement, then cement-retained definitive prostheses.
- **Survival**: functional and in situ; failure = removal for mobility, infection, persistent pain or severe bone loss; early failure within 1 year, late after 1 year. Kaplan-Meier at implant and patient level (lifelines); survival time from placement to last follow-up or failure; non-failures censored.
- **Success**: no mobility, no recurrent symptoms, no continuous radiolucency, MBL under 1.5 mm at 1 year after loading.
- **MBL**: panoramic radiographs at prosthesis delivery and 1 year, ImageJ, mesial and distal averaged, single calibrated examiner.
- **CBCT**: superimposed scans at 0, 1, 3, 5 mm apical to implant shoulder (buccal and palatal/lingual width) plus buccal and palatal vertical height; single examiner.
- **GBR vs non-GBR**: linear mixed-effect model to handle multiple implants per patient; significance at p under 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Flow**: 117 implants/58 patients enrolled; 3 failures (3 patients) excluded from follow-up analysis; 114 implants/55 patients radiographically evaluated; 32 implants/16 patients had CBCT analysis.
- **Survival**: 97.43% implant-level (3 failures), 94.83% patient-level. Early failure 1.71% (2/117). Failures: (1) osseointegration failure at 17 days, insertion torque 20, patient with hypertension and hyperlipidaemia; (2) retrograde peri-implantitis at 203 days, torque 70, patient with osteoporosis; (3) late peri-implantitis at 553 days, torque 30, patient with osteoporosis. All three had simultaneous GBR; the paper states no causal association was established. Insertion torque unit printed as N in the table.
- **Success**: 107 implants successful, 91.45% implant-level (7 implants had MBL above 1.5 mm); 82.76% patient-level.
- **MBL**: mean 0.46 +/- 1.05 mm at 1 year.
- **CBCT width change** (buccal, 0/1/3/5 mm): -0.03 +/- 0.28, -0.07 +/- 0.24, -0.05 +/- 0.16, -0.07 +/- 0.15 mm. Palatal/lingual: -0.09 +/- 0.27, -0.08 +/- 0.22, -0.10 +/- 0.20, -0.07 +/- 0.19 mm.
- **CBCT height change**: buccal -0.05 +/- 0.26 mm; palatal/lingual -0.15 +/- 0.36 mm.
- **GBR (24 implants) vs non-GBR (8)**: HW3_b greater preservation with GBR, mean difference 0.167 mm (p = 0.0338); VH_p gain 0.348 mm (p = 0.0144); VH_b p = 0.5950; no differences at HW5_b, HW1_b, HW0_b or any palatal width level.
- **Discussion benchmarks cited by authors**: basal implant 1-year survival 97.5% and 99.3% in other cohorts; conventional-implant early failure approximately 3.2%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single centre; no control over patient selection or surgical protocol; no comparison group of conventional implants.
- GBR applied only where clinically indicated, not randomized; CBCT subgroup small (32 implants, 24 vs 8).
- Follow-up limited to 1 year after loading; survival Kaplan-Meier curve reflects few events (3).
- No probing depth, bleeding on probing or plaque index recorded; MBL and CBCT each measured by a single examiner; MBL from panoramic radiographs.
- Cohort contained no smokers and few diabetic patients, limiting risk-factor inference; two of three failures occurred in osteoporotic patients but no adjusted analysis was done.
- Success percentage 91.45% equals 107/117 although the text describes 114 implants assessed; denominator is not explicitly reconciled in the paper.
- Industry involvement: employees and founder of the implant manufacturer are authors.
- Authors call for long-term follow-up and RCTs against conventional implants.

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Machined long-neck implant | Implant with a threaded body plus a machined, micro-grooved cuff of 4-10 mm placed at the bone dehiscence |
| Basal bone | Dense bone apical to the alveolar process, minimally resorbed after extraction |
| GBR | Guided bone regeneration |
| MBL | Marginal bone loss |
| HW_b / HW_p | Buccal / palatal horizontal bone width at 0, 1, 3, 5 mm apical to implant shoulder |
| VH_b / VH_p | Buccal / palatal vertical bone height change |
| Early / late failure | Failure within 1 year / after 1 year |
