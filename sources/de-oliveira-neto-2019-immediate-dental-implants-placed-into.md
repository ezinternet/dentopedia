## Why Ingested

감염 소켓 즉시식립에 대해 위키가 보유한 SR+MA들([[immediate-implant/infected-socket/saijeva-2020-immediate-implant-placement-non-infected-sockets]] RR=0.99, [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/lee-2018-comparison-immediate-implant-placement-infected]] 생존 차이 NS, [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]])는 모두 "감염 여부로 생존율 차이 없음"인데, 이 2019 MA는 유일하게 통계적으로 유의한 실패 위험 증가(RR=2.99)를 보고한다. 서베일런스(2026-09-29, 급성 감염 IIP)에서 발견한 상충 근거로, 상충의 근원(포함 연구·감염 분류·비뚤림 위험)을 기록하기 위해 인제스트.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42018092156; 7 databases to May 2018; 8 human clinical trials, 935 implants; Cochrane risk of bias: unclear overall, high for participant/personnel blinding in all studies) comparing immediate implants placed into infected versus non-infected sockets, restricted to studies with antibiotic therapy, ≥1 year follow-up and non-medically-compromised patients.

Implant failure risk ratio 2.99 for infected sites (95% CI 1.04–8.56; p=0.04; I²=0%), survival across studies 90.8–100%; peri-implant bone loss (MD −0.03 mm), plaque, bleeding, probing depth and keratinized mucosa width showed no significant difference, although weighted yearly bone loss was numerically higher in infected groups (0.62 vs 0.49 mm/yr).

The authors conclude infection is a real failure risk factor, but the CI lower bound is 1.04, all studies are non-randomized two-group designs at unclear-to-high risk of bias, and infected sites were pooled without acute/chronic or endodontic/periodontal stratification — which contradicts later, larger syntheses reporting no survival difference.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD42018092156; 2018년 5월까지 7개 DB; 사람 임상연구 8편·임플란트 935개; Cochrane 비뚤림 위험 전반 불명확, 참여자·시술자 눈가림은 전 연구 high risk)로, 항생제 투여·추적 1년 이상·전신질환 없는 환자만 포함해 감염 vs 비감염 발치와 즉시식립을 비교.

감염 부위의 임플란트 실패 위험비 2.99 (95% CI 1.04–8.56, p=0.04, I²=0%), 연구별 생존율 90.8–100%; 변연골 소실(MD −0.03 mm)·치태·출혈·탐침깊이·각화점막 폭은 유의차 없음 (단 연간 가중평균 골소실은 감염군 0.62 vs 0.49 mm로 수치상 높음).

저자는 감염을 실패 위험인자로 결론짓지만 신뢰구간 하한이 1.04이고, 전 연구가 비무작위 2군 설계(불명확~높은 비뚤림 위험)이며, 감염 부위를 급성/만성·근관성/치주성으로 나누지 않고 합산했다 — 이후 더 큰 종합 연구들의 "생존율 차이 없음"과 상충한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First meta-analysis (per the authors) to report a statistically significant higher failure risk for immediate implants in infected vs non-infected sites (RR 2.99).
- Restricted to human clinical trials (earlier SRs mixed animal and human data) and to studies with antibiotic therapy, ≥1 year follow-up, and no medically compromised patients.
- Adds weighted-mean, follow-up-adjusted secondary outcomes (yearly marginal bone loss, plaque index) as exploratory data.
- Methodological recommendation: primary studies should use ≥3 groups (non-infected, endodontic-origin infection, periodontal-origin infection) and report which infection type preceded each failure so subgroup analysis becomes possible.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: SR+MA, PRISMA-P and Cochrane Handbook, protocol registered a priori (PROSPERO).
- Search: PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, CENTRAL, LILACS, Open Grey from inception to May 2018; no language restriction; 3,253 hits → 64 duplicates removed → 3,154 excluded on title/abstract → 34 full texts → 26 excluded → 8 included (kappa 0.90).
- Eligibility: randomized or non-randomized clinical trials comparing immediate implants in infected vs non-infected sites. Excluded: survival not the primary outcome, no non-infected control, follow-up <1 year, no antibiotic therapy, medically compromised patients, duplicates.
- Outcomes: primary implant survival/failure; secondary peri-implant bone loss, plaque index, bleeding index, probing depth, keratinized mucosa width.
- Statistics: RR (dichotomous) and MD (continuous), random-effects model, 95% CI, I² (>50% substantial), Review Manager 5.3; the implant is the unit of analysis. Weighted means by follow-up period calculated in Excel.
- Risk of bias: Cochrane tool, one reviewer with second-reviewer check (kappa 0.81).
- Note: Tables 1–4 and Figures 1–3 (excluded-study list, included-study characteristics incl. acute/chronic infection labels AI/CI, forest plots) are not present in the retrieved PMC text; only captions.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Included studies: 8; survival range 90.8–100%.
- Implant failure: RR 2.99 (95% CI 1.04–8.56), p=0.04, I²=0%, 935 implants.
- Peri-implant bone loss (5 studies, 399 implants): MD −0.03 mm (−0.09 to 0.04), p=0.46, I²=0%.
- Bleeding index (5 studies): MD 0.05 (−0.01 to 0.10), p=0.08, I²=0%. Plaque index (5 studies): MD −0.01 (−0.04 to 0.02), p=0.54, I²=0%.
- Probing depth (4 studies, 124 implants): MD −0.30 mm (−0.64 to 0.05), p=0.09, I²=79%. Keratinized mucosa width (4 studies): MD 0.31 mm (−0.11 to 0.73), p=0.15, I²=25%.
- Weighted means per year of follow-up (mean follow-up 2.6 ± 1.1 years): bone loss 0.62 ± 0.67 mm/yr (infected) vs 0.49 ± 0.39 (non-infected); plaque 0.62 ± 0.67 vs 0.21 ± 0.13; bleeding 0.20 vs 0.21; probing depth 1.33 vs 1.53 mm; keratinized mucosa 1.57 vs 1.27 mm.
- Risk of bias: overall unclear; participant/personnel blinding high risk in all studies; random sequence generation and allocation concealment mostly high risk.
- Funnel plot and sensitivity analysis not performed (too few studies / all same type).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All included studies were non-randomized two-group comparisons at unclear-to-high risk of bias; the authors state this may have influenced results.
- The lower bound of the failure-risk CI is 1.04, so the significance is borderline; only 8 studies.
- Infected sites were pooled: no stratification by acute vs chronic, or endodontic vs periodontal origin, appears in the text (Table 2 legend defines AI = acute infected, CI = chronically infected, but its contents were not retrievable).
- Exclusion of studies without antibiotic therapy and of medically compromised patients limits generalizability.
- Authors' interpretation that failures reflect inadequate socket disinfection is inference, not tested in the meta-analysis.
- Suggested future work: three-group designs and per-failure reporting of infection type.

## 7. Glossary

- **Infected site**: extraction socket with clinical/radiographic signs of infection (endodontic or periodontal origin); in this paper not further stratified in the text.
- **Risk ratio (RR)**: ratio of failure probabilities, infected vs non-infected; 2.99 means ~3-fold.
- **I²**: percentage of variability due to heterogeneity rather than chance; 0% here for implant failure.
- **PRISMA-P / PROSPERO**: SR reporting protocol standard / international SR protocol registry.
- **Cochrane risk-of-bias tool**: domain-based judgment (randomization, allocation concealment, blinding, attrition, reporting).
- **Weighted mean per year of follow-up**: authors' own exploratory calculation of yearly outcome values across studies with different follow-up.
