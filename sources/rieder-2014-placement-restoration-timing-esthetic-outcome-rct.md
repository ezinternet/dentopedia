## Why Ingested

식립·수복 시기에 따른 전치부 심미 결과 RCT — [[rieder-2014-placement-restoration-timing-esthetic-outcome-rct]] 페이지의 1차 근거; 즉시 식립 + 즉시 임시 수복 프로토콜의 심미 영역 적용 근거.

## Source Summary

4-arm RCT (n=48) investigating the combined effect of implant placement timing (immediate vs. early) and restoration timing (immediate vs. early) on esthetic outcomes in maxillary anterior single implants. Primary outcome: Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at final restoration delivery.

## Study Design

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial, 4 arms
- **Population**: 48 patients with a single failing maxillary incisor
- **Allocation**:
  - Group 1a: Immediate placement + immediate restoration (IIPIR)
  - Group 1b: Immediate placement + early restoration (IIPER)
  - Group 2a: Early placement + immediate restoration (EPIR)
  - Group 2b: Early placement + early restoration (EPER)
- **Primary outcome**: Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at final restoration delivery
- **Full text**: Not retrieved (abstract-only)

## Key Results

| Group | Protocol | PES (mean ± SD) |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | Immediate placement + immediate restoration (IIPIR) | 8.47 ± 2.08 |
| 1b | Immediate placement + early restoration (IIPER) | 7.93 ± 3.21 |
| 2a | Early placement + immediate restoration (EPIR) | 6.62 ± 3.24 |
| 2b | Early placement + early restoration (EPER) | 8.10 ± 3.25 |

**Significant pairwise differences**:
- Group 2a vs Group 1a: p = 0.015 (2a significantly worse)
- Group 2a vs Group 2b: p = 0.047 (2a significantly worse)

## Conclusions

Immediate implant placement with immediate restoration is a viable alternative to early placement protocols when performed by an experienced surgeon. The combination of early placement with immediate restoration (Group 2a/EPIR) yielded the poorest esthetic results and should be avoided.

## Three-line Summary

4-arm RCT randomized 48 patients with failing maxillary incisors into immediate/early placement × immediate/early restoration groups, measuring Pink Esthetic Score at final restoration. Early placement combined with immediate restoration (EPIR) produced significantly lower PES (6.62) than both immediate placement + immediate restoration (IIPIR: 8.47, p=0.015) and early placement + early restoration (EPER: 8.10, p=0.047). The EPIR combination should be avoided; immediate placement + immediate restoration is viable in experienced hands.

## 세줄요약

상악 전치부 단일 임플란트 48명을 식립시기(즉시/조기) × 수복시기(즉시/조기) 4군으로 무작위 배정하고 최종 수복 시 분홍 심미 지수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES)를 측정했다. 조기식립+즉시수복(EPIR) 군은 PES 6.62로 즉시식립+즉시수복(IIPIR: 8.47, p=0.015) 및 조기식립+조기수복(EPER: 8.10, p=0.047) 군보다 유의하게 낮았다. 결론: EPIR 조합은 회피해야 하며, 경험 있는 술자라면 즉시식립+즉시수복이 실행 가능한 대안이다.
