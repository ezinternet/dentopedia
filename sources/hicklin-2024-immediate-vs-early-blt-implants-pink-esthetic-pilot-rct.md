## Why Ingested

First multicenter pilot RCT using Straumann bone-level tapered (BLT) implants to directly compare immediate vs early placement radiographic and pink esthetic outcomes at 12 months; extends the Puisys 2022 and Asghar 2023 body of evidence with a specific implant-system (BLT) and multi-ceramic-crown perspective. Wikilink to [[immediate-implant/puisys-2022-immediate-implant-placement-vs-early]] and [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/ickroth-2025-immediate-implant-intact-nonintact-sockets]].

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter pilot RCT, n=60 patients (60 Straumann BLT implants, 5 centers, Geneva/Zurich/Belgrade), 12-month follow-up comparing immediately placed vs early placed bone-level-tapered implants restored with all-ceramic crowns cemented to titanium-base abutments in anterior and premolar areas.

At 12 months, 98.3% implant survival; mean implant-shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact 0.15 ± 0.59 mm (no group difference); median ISQ rose from 75.5 to 78.5; mean buccal recession 0.1 ± 0.70 mm (no group difference); papilla gain 0.5 ± 1.47 mm mesially and 0.4 ± 1.36 mm distally.

Both immediate and early BLT placement protocols achieve equivalent bone level stability, ISQ trajectory, and pink esthetic outcomes at 1 year; pilot size (n=60) limits statistical inference; descriptive analysis only.

## 세줄요약

다기관 파일럿 무작위대조시험(RCT), n=60명 (스트라우만 골수준 테이퍼형 임플란트, Bone-Level Tapered, BLT, 5개 기관, 제네바·취리히·베오그라드), 전치부·소구치 부위에서 즉시식립 vs 조기식립 비교, 티타늄 베이스 지대주에 전도재관 세멘트 고정, 12개월 추적.

12개월 생존율 98.3%; 임플란트 숄더~최초 골-임플란트 접촉 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 0.15 ± 0.59 mm (군간 차이 없음); 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 중앙값 75.5→78.5; 협측 퇴축 0.1 ± 0.70 mm (군간 차이 없음); 유두 높이 증가 근심 0.5 ± 1.47 mm, 원심 0.4 ± 1.36 mm.

즉시식립과 조기식립 BLT 임플란트 모두 1년 시점 골 레벨 안정성·ISQ·핑크 심미 결과 동등; 파일럿 규모(n=60)로 통계적 추론 한계, 기술통계 분석만 제공.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Implant-system-specific data: Straumann BLT implants only, both immediate and early placement arms
- Confirms no radiographic bone level difference between placement protocols at 12 months (mean 0.15 mm from shoulder — within normal crestal remodeling range)
- ISQ trajectory (75.5 → 78.5 median) shows increasing osseointegration stability regardless of placement timing
- Pink esthetics (buccal recession, papilla height) equivalent between groups — both improved over the observation period
- All-ceramic crowns on titanium-base abutments evaluated as final restorative solution in both arms
- Multicenter design (5 centers, 3 countries) increases external validity despite small n

## 3. Methodology
- **Design**: Parallel-group multicenter pilot RCT
- **Randomization**: Immediate vs early placement (1:1)
- **N**: 60 patients, 60 implants (Straumann BLT)
- **Sites**: Anterior + premolar area
- **Restoration**: All-ceramic crowns cemented to titanium-base abutments
- **Follow-up**: 12 months
- **Analysis**: Descriptive; level of significance 0.05 (no powered inferential statistics planned for pilot)

## 5. Limitations
- Pilot study: n=60 is underpowered for formal inferential statistics; results are hypothesis-generating
- Descriptive analysis only — no powered comparison between groups
- 12-month follow-up; long-term bone and soft-tissue stability not yet assessed
- Includes both anterior and premolar regions (not exclusively esthetic zone)
- Abstract-only ingest: detailed patient selection criteria, exact randomization, and dropout data not fully extractable
