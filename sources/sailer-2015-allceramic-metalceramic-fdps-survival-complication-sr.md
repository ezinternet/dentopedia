## Three-line Summary

SR of 67 studies (4,663 metal-ceramic + 9,434 all-ceramic SCs; PubMed/Embase/CENTRAL 2006–2013; robust Poisson regression) establishing clinical equivalence of leucite/LD glass-ceramics and oxide ceramics to metal-ceramic SCs, with feldspathic ceramics significantly inferior and zirconia carrying high chipping burden.

5-year survival: metal-ceramic 94.7%, LD glass-ceramic 96.6%, alumina 94.6–96.0%, zirconia 92.1%; feldspathic ceramics significantly lower (p<0.001); zirconia: significantly more framework fracture + loss of retention (p<0.001) despite adequate survival.

The recommendation that zirconia-based SCs should not be the primary option (due to technical complications) was later refined by Pjetursson 2026, which showed monolithic zirconia performs comparably to metal-ceramic when veneering is avoided.

## 세줄요약

67편 SR(금속도재관 4,663개 + 전세라믹 9,434개, 2006–2013): LD/알루미나/지르코니아 전세라믹 단단관은 도재(feldspathic) 제외 금속도재관과 5년 생존율 동등.

5년 생존율: 금속도재관 94.7%, LD 96.6%, 알루미나 94.6–96.0%, 지르코니아 92.1%; 도재 세라믹 유의하게 열등(p<0.001); 지르코니아는 베니어 치핑·유지력 상실 유의 증가(p<0.001).

지르코니아 1차 선택 비권장 권고는 2026년 Pjetursson SR에서 모놀리식 디자인으로 재평가 — 베니어링 회피 시 금속도재관과 동등 성능.

## Why Ingested

Sailer 2015 is the foundational SR establishing all-ceramic SCs as equivalent to the gold-standard metal-ceramic SCs — directly superseded/extended by [[prosthetic-materials/pjetursson-2026-metal-ceramic-all-ceramic-crowns-survival-sr-ma]], providing the 2006–2013 benchmark data that the 2026 update (2014–2024) builds upon.

## One-line Summary

SR of 67 studies (4,663 metal-ceramic + 9,434 all-ceramic SCs; 2006–2013) showing all-ceramic SCs achieve similar 5-year survival as metal-ceramic except for feldspathic ceramics; zirconia carries high chipping risk.

## 한줄요약

## 2. Key Contributions

- Established that leucite/lithium-disilicate glass-ceramics and oxide ceramics (alumina, zirconia) achieve equivalent 5-year survival to metal-ceramic SCs
- Confirmed feldspathic/silica-based ceramic SCs are significantly inferior, especially in posterior region
- Documented that zirconia SCs — despite adequate survival — carry significantly higher rates of veneering ceramic fracture and loss of retention
- Provided first large-scale pooled estimates: metal-ceramic 94.7%, LD glass-ceramic 96.6%, zirconia 92.1% (5-year survival)
- Identified that densely sintered zirconia SCs should NOT be primary option due to chipping burden

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** Systematic review with meta-analysis (robust Poisson regression)
- **Search:** Medline (PubMed), Embase, CENTRAL (2006–2013) + hand search + 34 studies from prior SR
- **Inclusion:** Clinical studies on tooth-supported FDPs, mean follow-up ≥3 years
- **Studies included:** 67 studies (17 metal-ceramic, 54 all-ceramic, some overlap)
- **Sample:** 4,663 metal-ceramic SCs + 9,434 all-ceramic SCs
- **Outcomes:** 5-year survival rate, biological complications (caries, periodontitis, tooth fracture), technical complications (chipping, framework fracture, loss of retention)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Material | 5-year Survival | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Metal-ceramic | 94.7% (95% CI: 94.1–96.9%) | Gold standard |
| Leucite/lithium-disilicate glass-ceramic | 96.6% (95% CI: 94.9–96.7%) | Equivalent |
| Glass-infiltrated alumina | 94.6% (95% CI: 92.7–96%) | Equivalent |
| Densely sintered alumina | 96.0% (95% CI: 93.8–97.5%) | Equivalent |
| Densely sintered zirconia | 92.1% (95% CI: 82.8–95.6%) | Equivalent survival BUT high chipping |
| Feldspathic/silica-based | Significantly lower (p<0.001) | Not recommended posterior |

- Zirconia: significantly more framework fractures + loss of retention vs metal-ceramic (p<0.001)
- Anterior vs posterior: feldspathic and zirconia showed significantly lower survival in posterior (p<0.0001)
