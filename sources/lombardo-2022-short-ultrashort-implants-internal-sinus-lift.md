## Why Ingested

Unique 5-year retrospective dataset on ultra-short (5 mm) locking-taper implants placed with **simultaneous transcrestal/internal sinus lift** (osteotome + β-TCP) in atrophic posterior maxilla (RCBH <5 mm). Adds case-series dimensional data (sinus lift gain, crestal bone loss at 5 yr) that the SR/MA-level syntheses in [[overviews/short-implant-vs-sinus-augmentation-decision]] and [[implants/short-narrow/alenezi-2025-short-long-implants-sinus-floor-elevation]] lack — those meta-analyses compare short vs long+SFE but do not isolate the simultaneous-internal-sinus-lift sub-protocol for implants as short as 5 mm. Provides 94.84% survival and RCBH/sinus-height trajectory benchmarks for ultra-short implants at the single-crown level.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective 5-year study evaluated short (6–8 mm) and ultra-short (5 mm) locking-taper implants placed with modified osteotome sinus floor elevation and restored with single crowns in atrophic posterior maxilla.

Overall implant survival was 94.84% (147/155); 5 mm, 6 mm, and 8 mm groups had 93.75%, 94%, and 100% survival; mean residual bone height increased from 4.45 mm pre-op to 9.25 mm post-placement, settling at 5.25 mm at 5 years.

Internal sinus lift with short/ultra-short implants supporting single crowns is a predictable treatment for atrophic posterior maxilla with RCBH <5 mm, with stable bone gain and minimal crestal resorption at 5 years.

## 세줄요약

단축(6–8 mm) 및 초단축(5 mm) locking-taper 임플란트를 변형 골절개 상악동 거상술과 함께 식립하고 단일 크라운으로 수복한 5년 후향적 연구.

전체 임플란트 생존율 94.84%(147/155); 5 mm·6 mm·8 mm군 각각 93.75%·94%·100%; 평균 잔존골 높이는 술 전 4.45 mm → 술 후 9.25 mm → 5년 5.25 mm로 안정화.

RCBH <5 mm 위축성 상악 구치부에서 단일 크라운을 지지하는 단축/초단축 임플란트+내부 상악동 거상술은 5년째 안정적 골증가 및 미미한 치조정 흡수를 보이는 예측 가능한 치료법.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Materials 2022, 15(22): 7995 (MDPI)
- Institution: University of Verona, Italy
- Design: Single-center retrospective case series
- Sample: 79 patients, 155 implants (5.0 mm n=32, 6.0 mm n=100, 8.0 mm n=23)
- Follow-up: 5 years (mean)

## 2. Key Contributions
- 5-year radiographic trajectory of RCBH and sinus lift height for ultra-short (5 mm) implants with simultaneous internal sinus lift.
- Demonstrates viability of single-crown restoration with ≤5 mm implants in RCBH <5 mm sites without lateral window sinus lift.
- Quantifies β-TCP resorption and sinus height change over 5 years.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Bicon locking-taper implants (5, 6, 8 mm length; 5–8.5 mm diameter).
- Modified osteotome sinus floor elevation with β-TCP graft (simultaneous with implant placement).
- Single crowns via Integrated Abutment Crown (IAC) technique.
- CBCT and periapical radiographs at all timepoints.
- Outcomes: survival rate, RCBH, sinus lift height, crestal bone loss (CBL), F-BIC shift, membrane perforation, patient satisfaction (VAS).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall survival: 94.84% (147/155) at 5 years
- Survival by length: 5 mm 93.75%, 6 mm 94.00%, 8 mm 100%
- RCBH: pre-op 4.45 mm → post-placement 9.25 mm → 5 yr 5.25 mm
- Mean sinus lift at placement: 4.8 mm; at 5 yr: 1.46 mm (partial resorption of β-TCP)
- Mean crestal bone loss: −0.36 mm over 5 years
- F-BIC shift: −0.62 mm
- Membrane perforation: 12/155 (7.74%); no implant loss attributed to perforation
- Patient satisfaction VAS: 8.2 (baseline) → 9.0 (5 yr)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design; no control group
- Non-homogeneous group distribution (100 six-mm vs 32 five-mm vs 23 eight-mm)
- Single center
- β-TCP graft resorption reduces sinus gain over time (4.8 → 1.46 mm)
- Crown-to-implant ratio not analyzed by length group

## 6. Related Work
- Bicon locking-taper platform studies (manufacturer-affiliated literature).
- Osteotome/transcrestal sinus floor elevation literature (Summer technique).
- Mester 2023, Alenezi 2025 SR+MAs compare short vs long+SFE without isolating simultaneous-internal-sinus-lift protocol.

## 7. Glossary
- RCBH: 잔존 치조정 골 높이 (Residual Crestal Bone Height)
- ISL: 내부 상악동 거상술 (Internal Sinus Lift)
- F-BIC: 변연골-임플란트 접합부 (First Bone-to-Implant Contact)
- IAC: 통합 지대주 크라운 (Integrated Abutment Crown)
- β-TCP: 베타-삼인산칼슘 (β-tricalcium phosphate)
- CIR: 크라운-임플란트 비율 (Crown-to-Implant Ratio)
