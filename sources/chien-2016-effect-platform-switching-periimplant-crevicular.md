## Why Ingested

PS(플랫폼 스위칭)의 골보존 효과를 설명하는 여러 논문이 인용하는 "임플란트-지대주 접합부 미세간극(microgap)에서 염증 침윤이 멀어진다"는 기전 가설을, 위키 최초로 임플란트 주위 열구액(Periimplant Crevicular Fluid, PICF) 사이토카인(IL-6, MIP-1β, TNF-α) 수치로 직접 검증한 생물학적 근거 수준 논문. [[wiki/implants/mbl/juan-montesinos-2022-platform-switching-conventional-sr-ma]]가 확립한 방사선학적 MBL 이점의 "왜"에 해당하는 초기 치유기 생물학적 데이터를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Nonrandomized, controlled, split-mouth observational clinical trial (14 patients, 1 platform-switched (PS) + 1 platform-matched (PM) implant per patient, PICF and clinical parameters sampled at 1/2/4/6 weeks post-abutment-connection) comparing early soft-tissue healing and periimplant crevicular fluid (PICF) cytokine content between PS and PM implants.

Probing depth was significantly deeper in PM than PS at week 1 only (3.5±0.4mm vs 2.9±0.2mm, p=0.03); PICF total volume decreased significantly over time only in PS (p=0.0005); IL-6 and MIP-1β decreased significantly in both groups, but TNF-α decreased significantly only in PS and was paradoxically ~3x higher in PS than PM at week 1 (17±7pg vs 6±1pg, p=0.005).

Overall periimplant soft-tissue and cytokine response was mostly similar between PS and PM during early healing, with only time- and mediator-specific differences — the higher initial TNF-α in PS complicates a simple "PS = less inflammation" narrative and remains mechanistically unexplained.

## 세줄요약

비무작위 대조 스플릿마우스 관찰 임상시험(14명, 환자당 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PS) 1개 + 플랫폼 매칭(Platform Matching, PM) 1개 임플란트, 지대주 연결 후 1/2/4/6주 시점에 임상지표·임플란트 주위 열구액(Periimplant Crevicular Fluid, PICF) 사이토카인 측정) — PS vs PM 초기 연조직 치유·PICF 사이토카인 함량 비교.

탐침깊이(Probing Depth, PD)는 1주차에만 PM이 PS보다 유의하게 깊었음(3.5±0.4mm vs 2.9±0.2mm, p=0.03); PICF 총량은 PS군에서만 유의하게 감소(p=0.0005); 인터루킨-6(IL-6)·MIP-1β는 양군 모두 유의하게 감소했으나, 종양괴사인자-α(Tumor Necrosis Factor-α, TNF-α)는 PS군에서만 유의 감소했고 1주차엔 오히려 PS가 PM보다 약 3배 높음(17±7pg vs 6±1pg, p=0.005).

전반적 연조직·사이토카인 반응은 PS·PM 간 대체로 유사했고 시점·매개체 특이적 차이만 존재 — 1주차 TNF-α가 PS에서 더 높은 역설적 소견은 "PS=염증 감소"라는 단순 서사에 들어맞지 않으며 기전은 미확정.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Implant Dentistry, 2016 Oct (PMID 27504534, PMCID PMC5523451)
- **DOI**: 10.1097/ID.0000000000000463
- **Institutions**: Graduate Periodontology Clinic, The Ohio State University (IRB protocol no. 2011H0355)

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study reporting proinflammatory cytokine expression in PICF of PS implants during early soft-tissue healing after abutment installation, compared directly against PM implants in a split-mouth design.
- Uses a 20-cytokine multiplex bead-based assay (Bio-Plex) on PICF, narrowing to 10 detectable cytokines and finding only 3 (IL-6, MIP-1β, TNF-α) with meaningful time- or group-dependent differences.
- Identifies a paradoxical finding: TNF-α is ~3x higher in PS than PM at week 1 (the only cytokine with a significant between-group difference), which does not support a simple "PS reduces inflammation" mechanism and is explicitly flagged by the authors as unproven/speculative.
- Reinforces that PD, mPI, and mSBI differences between PS and PM are transient/absent, converging by week 6.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Nonrandomized, controlled, observational clinical trial; split-mouth (2 implants per patient, different quadrants). For patients with two 5.0mm-diameter implants, group assignment (PS vs PM healing abutment) was randomized by coin flip; for patients with mismatched implant diameters (5.0mm=PS, 4.0mm=PM), both received the same 4.1mm healing abutment (forced by anatomy, not randomized).
- **Population**: Recruited Jan–Dec 2012; 17 patients enrolled, 14 completed (5 men, 9 women, age 25–73, mean 58.7). 3 excluded (1 noncompliant, 2 early implant failure). Inclusion: adults 18–75, non-smokers, systemically/periodontally healthy. Exclusion: smokers, immediate/one-stage placement, untreated periodontal disease, pregnancy/lactation, IV or >3yr oral bisphosphonate history, other systemic conditions affecting healing.
- **Implants**: Osseotite tapered Certain implants (BIOMET 3i), 2-stage (submerged) protocol, 2–4 months submerged healing. Second-stage: H-shaped flap incision, standardized 4mm abutment height, Certain Bellatek Encode healing abutments (4.1mm = PS test; 5.0mm = PM control), same surgeon (H.W.M.), both implants uncovered same appointment.
- **Clinical measurements**: mPI, mSBI, PD at 1/2/4/6 weeks post-uncovering, 6 sites/implant averaged, single calibrated examiner (Cohen's kappa 0.78), UNC-12 probe.
- **PICF sampling**: PerioPaper strips, mesial+distal, 2 collections 5 min apart per timepoint, volume via Periotron 8000, 4 strips pooled per implant per timepoint, stored at −20°C.
- **Cytokine assay**: Bio-Plex multiplex bead assay, 20 cytokines screened (IL-1ra, IL-4, IL-6, IL-8, IL-10, IL-12, IL-17, MIP-1α, MIP-1β, TNF-α, eotaxin, FGF, PDGF, RANTES, IL-1β, VEGF, IP-10, IFN-γ, G-CSF, GM-CSF).
- **Statistics**: SAS 9.2 + SPSS 20; linear mixed-model regression for repeated measures (within/between group); repeated-measures ANOVA for week 1 vs week 6 within-group and PM-vs-PS between-group comparisons; Bonferroni correction, α=0.05. Power: 16/group needed for 95% power to detect 1 SD effect; n=14 achieved gave ≥93% power.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | PM week 1 | PM week 6 | PS week 1 | PS week 6 | Within-PM p | Within-PS p | Between-group p (wk1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD (mm) | 3.5 ± 0.4 | 2.3 ± 0.1 | 2.9 ± 0.2 | 2.0 ± 0.1 | 0.0005 | 0.0005 | 0.03 (PM deeper) |
| PICF volume (μL) | 1.8 ± 0.25 | 1.4 ± 0.2 | 2.0 ± 0.35 | 1.2 ± 0.24 | NS | 0.0005 | NS |
| IL-6 (pg total) | 33 ± 7 | 0.4 ± 0.1 | 47 ± 15 | 1 ± 0.4 | 0.0005 | 0.0005 | NS |
| MIP-1β (pg total) | 17 ± 3 | 5 ± 1 | 21 ± 6 | 6 ± 2 | 0.03 | 0.003 | NS |
| TNF-α (pg total) | 6 ± 1 | 3 ± 1 | 17 ± 7 | 4 ± 1 | NS (>0.05) | 0.001 | **0.005 (PS higher)** |

- mPI and mSBI decreased in both groups from week 1 to 6 but neither the within-group (week1 vs 6) nor between-group (PM vs PS) differences reached significance (p>0.05) — minimal gingival inflammation throughout (mSBI <0.2 both groups).
- No between-group PD difference at week 6 (convergence by 6 weeks) — the week-1 PD difference is interpreted as transient, possibly due to PS having higher mechanical resistance to probing from the inward-shifted implant-abutment junction / soft-tissue folding rather than true health difference.
- 10 of 20 cytokines were negligible/undetected with no differential expression (IL-1ra, IL-4, IL-10, IL-12, IL-17, MIP-1α, eotaxin, FGF, PDGF, RANTES).
- Among the 10 reported cytokines, only IL-6, MIP-1β, and TNF-α showed meaningful differences; IP-10 (anti-inflammatory) showed a nonsignificant increase concomitant with the proinflammatory decrease.
- TNF-α at week 1 in PS was almost 3x that of PM (17±7 vs 6±1 pg, p=0.005) — the only cytokine with a significant between-group difference, and it favors *higher* early inflammation in PS, not lower.
- Authors speculate the PS finding may reflect soft-tissue exposure to the (minimally rough) implant seating platform surface activating inflammatory cells, but explicitly state "this explanation... remains to be proven."
- Overall conclusion: periimplant soft-tissue response around PM and PS implants is "mostly similar with time- and mediator-specific differences" during early healing — not a broad PS-favors-less-inflammation result.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=14), powered only to detect a large effect size (1 SD); smaller true differences may be missed (authors note larger samples might reveal further significant differences).
- Split-mouth design: PM vs PS difference is confounded with individual patient systemic/local response; a parallel-groups design (PM-only vs PS-only patients) might yield different results.
- Nonrandomized overall design; randomization by coin flip applied only to the subset of patients with matched (5.0mm/5.0mm) implant diameters — patients with mismatched diameters had forced (non-random) abutment assignment.
- Mechanism for the higher week-1 TNF-α in PS is speculative (rough seating-platform-surface hypothesis) and unproven within this study.
- Single surgeon/examiner, single institution (Ohio State University), 6-week observation window only — does not address longer-term (radiographic MBL) outcomes.

## 7. Glossary

- **Platform Switching (PS)**: using an abutment narrower than the implant platform diameter, shifting the implant-abutment microgap inward and away from the crestal bone.
- **Periimplant Crevicular Fluid (PICF)**: osmotically mediated inflammatory exudate originating from the periimplant gingival vessel plexus, analogous to gingival crevicular fluid (GCF); its cytokine profile reflects periimplant mucosal health status.
- **TNF-α (Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha)**: proinflammatory cytokine produced mainly by macrophages, implicated in stimulating bone resorption and periodontal/periimplant catabolic processes.
- **IL-6 (Interleukin-6)**: proinflammatory cytokine central to host response to tissue injury and infection.
- **MIP-1β (Macrophage Inflammatory Protein-1β / CCL4)**: chemokine produced by macrophages after bacterial endotoxin stimulation; chemoattractant for NK cells and monocytes.
- **Modified Plaque Index (mPI) / Modified Sulcus Bleeding Index (mSBI)**: clinical indices for plaque accumulation and gingival/mucosal bleeding on probing.
