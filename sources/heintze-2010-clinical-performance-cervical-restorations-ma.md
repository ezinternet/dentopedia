## Why Ingested
Added while answering a question on enamel bevel and dentin roughening in cervical composite restorations. This is the earlier (2010) meta-analysis from the same group as the held 2015 update, and it supplies an independent-era anchor for the "bevel no effect, roughening helps, isolation no effect" findings; it differs from the 2015 update on isolation → [[resin-bonding/heintze-2010-clinical-performance-cervical-restorations-ma]]

## Three-line Summary
Meta-analysis of prospective clinical studies (observation of at least 18 months) of cervical restorations made of composite and glass-ionomer derivatives: 50 clinical studies involving 40 adhesive systems, analyzed with linear mixed models containing study and experiment effects.

On average 10% of cervical fillings were lost and 24% showed marginal discoloration at 3 years (range 0-50% retention loss, 0-74% discoloration); secondary caries was hardly detected; 2-step self-etch adhesives performed best and 1-step self-etch worst, restorations on prepared/roughened dentin/enamel showed significantly higher retention than on unprepared dentin (p<0.05), while enamel beveling and isolation type (rubber dam vs cotton rolls) had no significant influence.

The authors advise 2-step self-etch or 3-step etch-and-rinse adhesives over 1-step self-etch and glass-ionomer derivatives, and roughening dentin (and enamel) before placement; this is an abstract-only record, and the roughening and isolation findings come from between-experiment comparisons rather than randomized contrasts.

## 세줄요약
관찰기간 18개월 이상의 치경부 수복물 전향적 임상연구를 대상으로 한 메타분석 (Meta-Analysis): 복합레진 및 글라스아이오노머 계열 수복물, 임상연구 50편·접착 시스템 40종, 연구 효과와 실험 효과를 포함한 선형 혼합모형으로 분석.

3년 시점 평균 10%가 탈락 (유지 소실), 24%가 변연 변색을 보였고 (범위: 탈락 0-50%, 변색 0-74%) 이차우식은 거의 없었다; 2단계 자가산부식 접착제 (Two-step Self-etch Adhesive)가 가장 좋고 1단계 자가산부식 접착제 (One-step Self-etch Adhesive)가 가장 나빴으며, 상아질·법랑질을 삭제·조면화한 수복물이 비처리 상아질보다 유지율이 유의하게 높았으나 (p<0.05), 법랑질 사면 형성 (Beveling)과 격리 방식 (러버댐 (Rubber Dam) 대 면구)은 유의한 영향이 없었다.

1단계 자가산부식 접착제·글라스아이오노머 계열보다 2단계 자가산부식 또는 3단계 산부식-수세 접착제 (Etch-and-Rinse Adhesive)를 권장하고 수복 전 상아질 (및 법랑질) 조면화를 권고했다; 초록만 확보한 기록 (전문 미확보)이며 조면화·격리 결과는 무작위 대비가 아닌 실험 간 비교에서 나온 것이라는 한계가 있다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Dent Mater 2010 Oct;26(10):993-1000 (Epub 2010 Jul 16)
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.dental.2010.06.003
- **Institution**: R&D, Ivoclar Vivadent AG, Schaan, Liechtenstein (corresponding author affiliation)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Early quantitative benchmark: about 10% retention loss and 24% marginal discoloration at 3 years across cervical restorations.
- Ranks adhesive/restorative classes by influence on retention loss.
- Finds dentin/enamel preparation (roughening) beneficial and enamel beveling and isolation type non-influential.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Meta-analysis of prospective clinical studies, at least 18 months of observation
- **Databases**: not stated in the abstract
- **n**: 50 clinical studies, 40 adhesive systems
- **Outcomes**: retention loss and marginal discoloration (secondary caries reported as rare); linear mixed models with study and experiment effects

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- At 3 years: ~10% of fillings lost (0-50% range), 24% marginal discoloration (0-74% range).
- Adhesive/restorative class had the most significant influence: 2-step self-etch best, 1-step self-etch worst; 3-step etch-and-rinse, glass ionomers/resin-modified glass ionomers, 2-step etch-and-rinse and polyacid-modified resin composites ranked in between.
- Prepared/roughened dentin/enamel: significantly higher retention than unprepared dentin (p<0.05).
- Enamel beveling and isolation type (rubber dam/cotton rolls): no significant influence.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: effect sizes, confidence intervals, search databases and bias assessment not available.
- Between-experiment comparisons of preparation and isolation are observational in nature (confounding by adhesive, operator, lesion type is possible).
- Corresponding-author affiliation is an adhesive manufacturer (Ivoclar Vivadent); funding/disclosure statement not seen in the abstract.

## 6. Related Work
- [[resin-bonding/mahn-2015-bonding-parameters-cervical-restorations-ma]]: later, larger update from the same group (81 trials); also finds beveling without effect, but associates rubber dam plus roughened dentin with higher retention.
- [[resin-bonding/luhrs-2020-composite-restorations-nccl-cavity-preparation]]: 7.7-year RCT supporting dentin roughening.

## 7. Glossary
- **Retention loss**: partial or complete loss of the restoration from the cavity.
- **Linear mixed model**: regression model with random study/experiment effects accounting for clustering of experiments within studies.
- **Beveling**: preparation of an angled finish on the enamel margin.
