## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of correlation coefficients, 47 qualitative studies (16 in meta-analysis), multiple databases — evaluating how well digital plaque assessment technologies correlate with conventional reference methods.

Pooled Pearson r=0.77 (all digital technologies); AI-aided methods r=0.87; 3D intraoral scanners ρ=0.91; disclosing agents raised correlation from r=0.53 (without) to 0.81 (with).

Extreme heterogeneity (I²>98%) and high risk of bias (96% of studies) yielded GRADE very low certainty — strong associations observed but insufficient to recommend specific digital platforms for clinical adoption.

전체 디지털 기술 Pearson r=0.77; AI r=0.87; 3D IOS ρ=0.91; 착색제 사용 시 r 0.53→0.81.

극심한 이질성 (I²>98%)·고위험 비뚤림 (96%)으로 GRADE 근거 수준 매우 낮음 — 강한 상관에도 특정 플랫폼 임상 채택 권고 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA quantifying association strength between digital plaque assessment and reference methods
- Pooled Pearson r = 0.77 (95%CI 0.64–0.86); Spearman ρ = 0.76 (95%CI 0.50–0.89) — strong overall
- AI-aided methods: r = 0.87 (very strong subgroup)
- 3D intraoral scanner (IOS): ρ = 0.91 (very strong)
- Disclosing agents improve association: r without = 0.53 vs with = 0.81 — clinically important finding
- Despite strong associations: GRADE very low — clinical adoption not yet justified by evidence alone

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA of correlation coefficients
- Databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, Ovid MEDLINE, Cochrane
- Screening: 4981 records → 47 qualitative → 16 in meta-analysis
- Statistical method: Fisher's z transformation; pooled Pearson r and Spearman ρ
- Bias tool: QUADAS-2; certainty: GRADE
- Subgroup analyses: by technology type, disclosing agent use, reference method

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Technology | Metric | Pooled association |
|---|---|---|
| Overall (all digital) | Pearson r | 0.77 (95%CI 0.64–0.86) |
| Overall (all digital) | Spearman ρ | 0.76 (95%CI 0.50–0.89) |
| AI-aided methods | Pearson r | 0.87 (very strong) |
| 3D intraoral scanner | Spearman ρ | 0.91 (very strong) |
| Without disclosing agent | Pearson r | 0.53 (95%CI 0.42–0.64) |
| With disclosing agent | Pearson r | 0.81 (95%CI 0.68–0.89) |

- Heterogeneity: I²>98% (extreme) — pooled estimates should be interpreted cautiously
- Risk of bias: 96% of studies high or unclear risk (QUADAS-2)
- GRADE certainty: very low overall

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Extreme heterogeneity (I²>98%) undermines pooled estimates
- 96% of included studies: high or unclear risk of bias
- Very low GRADE certainty — cannot confidently recommend any specific digital technology
- Need: standardized protocols, comprehensive outcomes, diverse patient populations
- Most technologies have only a few studies each — insufficient for precise subgroup estimates

## 7. Glossary
- QUADAS-2: Quality Assessment of Diagnostic Accuracy Studies (tool for diagnostic test accuracy studies)
- Fisher's z transformation: statistical method to combine correlation coefficients in meta-analysis
- IOS: Intraoral Scanner — 3D digital impression device
- GRADE: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation
- Disclosing agent: dye solution (erythrosine, D&C Red) that stains plaque for visualization
