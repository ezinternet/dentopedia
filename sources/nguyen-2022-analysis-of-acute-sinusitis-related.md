## Why Ingested

조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure) 묶음에서, 상악동거상술 후 급성 부비동염 (acute sinusitis)과 연관된 조기 실패 3례를 회수 임플란트의 표면 분석 (SEM-EDS)과 조직 분석 (TEM, 조직학)으로 해석한 드문 기전 보고라서 수집했다. 위험요인 메타분석 중심의 [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]에 표면 오염·이식재 요인이라는 기전적 관점을 보탠다. → [[implants/survival/clauser-2022-early-implant-failure-grafted]]

## Three-line Summary

Case series of three early implant failures (all within 1 month of placement with sinus lifting and allograft bone grafting in the posterior maxilla) treated at Seoul National University Dental Hospital, with retrieved fixtures and peri-implant tissue examined by SEM, EDS, TEM, and toluidine-blue histology.

Failed fixtures showed heterogeneous surfaces, a low degree of osseointegration, organic matter coverage, no detectable bacteria, and unusual elements on the surface (Au up to 40.11% in case 3, Zr 11.10% and Tl 2.42% in single cases), while case 1 soft tissue showed neutrophils, macrophages with metal particles, osteoclasts around bone particles, and multinucleated giant cells.

The authors attribute failure to implant surface contamination and graft-material-related factors on top of sinus complications (graft displacement suggesting membrane perforation); with only three cases and no controls, this is hypothesis-generating, not evidence of causation.

## 세줄요약

서울대학교치과병원에서 상악 구치부 상악동거상술 및 동종골 이식과 함께 식립 후 1개월 이내 조기 실패한 임플란트 3례를 대상으로, 회수한 고정체와 주위 조직을 주사전자현미경 (scanning electron microscopy, SEM), 에너지분산형 X선 분광 (energy-dispersive X-ray spectrometry, EDS), 투과전자현미경 (transmission electron microscopy, TEM), 조직학으로 분석한 증례 보고.

실패한 고정체는 불균일한 표면, 낮은 골유착 정도, 유기물 피복이 관찰되었고 세균은 검출되지 않았으며, 금 (Au, 증례 3에서 40.11%)·지르코늄 (Zr 11.10%)·탈륨 (Tl 2.42%) 등 이례적 원소가 단일 증례에서 검출되었고, 증례 1의 연조직에서는 호중구·대식세포 (금속 입자 포함)·파골세포·다핵거대세포가 관찰되었다.

저자들은 상악동 합병증(이식재 변위로 추정되는 막 천공)에 더해 임플란트 표면 오염과 이식재 관련 요인이 실패에 기여했다고 보지만, 3례·대조군 없음이라 인과 증거가 아닌 가설 생성 수준이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First report (per the authors) on acute sinusitis-related early implant failure; the introduction states no prevalence study for this entity exists.
- Combines clinical course with SEM-EDS surface analysis of the failed fixtures and TEM/histology of peri-implant tissue.
- Documents unusual surface contaminants (Au, Zr, Tl, Si, S) and the absence of detectable bacteria on all three fixtures.
- Proposes that graft-material dissolution (Ca/P) and organic coverage of the implant surface may interrupt initial osseointegration.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: three-case report; IRB approval Seoul National University (S-D20200007); written informed consent from all patients.
- **Case 1**: 68-year-old woman (hypertension on aspirin, diabetes mellitus; non-smoker); two 4.0 x 10 mm tissue-level Stella implants at #26/#27 with sinus lift and Oragraft allograft; pain and mobility at 1 month; pseudocyst on Water's view; implants removed; re-placed 4 months later.
- **Case 2**: 54-year-old man (chronic sinusitis, prior endoscopic sinus surgery on the left); two 4.0 x 8.5 mm bone-level Luna implants at #15/#16 with sinus lift and Oragraft; odor and exudate from 10 days, mobility about 2 weeks; graft displacement into the sinus; biopsy chronic maxillary sinusitis; re-placed 5 months later.
- **Case 3**: 30-year-old woman; implant and sinus lift at a local clinic; sinusitis diagnosed at 1 week; treated with apex cutting and endoscopic sinus surgery; mobility at 2 months after apex cutting; implant at #25 removed; re-placed 5 months later.
- **SEM-EDS**: fixtures fixed in 2.5% glutaraldehyde; 10 kV; points U (upper third), M (middle third), B (attached bone), A (apical third); x10,000; major >10 wt%, minor 1-10 wt%, trace <1 wt%.
- **TEM/histology**: 1 x 1 x 1 mm tissue block, 70-80 nm sections; 1 um toluidine-blue sections; x3000 screening for immune cells, x6000-10,000 for organelles and metal particles.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- All three fixtures showed a heterogeneous surface and low osseointegration with sandblasted, acid-etched morphology; surfaces mostly covered by C- and O-rich organic matter; no bacteria or other organisms detected.
- Case 1: bone attached only to the upper part of the implant; no cells or lacunae in the attached bone; no P signal with low Ca in bone; Tl 2.42%, Si 1.90%, S 0.41% in that area; surface Ti 56.31%, O 21.03%, C 16.90% with micro-fissures; graft particles Ca 23.03% with no P and N 17.72%.
- Case 2: "distant" osseointegration; upper region Au 26.73% and Ti 8.98%; Zr 11.10% in the middle region; apical Au 7.99%.
- Case 3: apical surface with fibers and organic matter; no Ca; Au 40.11%; Si 11.86%.
- TEM (case 1 soft tissue): neutrophils, macrophages, eosinophils; metal particles in macrophage cytoplasm.
- Histology (case 1): graft bone particles scattered in inflammatory tissue, metal particles near bone particles, osteoclasts around particles, multinucleated giant cells.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Three cases, no control group, no implants from successful sites for comparison; causal claims (surface contamination, graft material) are untested.
- EDS is semi-quantitative and surface-sensitive; the authors note that C, N, Na, and Cl signals can come from adsorption during specimen preparation, and "distant" osseointegration might arise during removal.
- Contaminant sources (Au, Zr, Tl) are speculative; the authors say Au origin needs further study.
- Case heterogeneity: different implant systems (tissue-level and bone-level), a prior chronic sinusitis history in case 2, systemic disease in case 1, and case 3 surgery performed at another clinic with sinusitis treatment before removal.
- Possible reporting inconsistency: the introduction defines acute sinusitis-related failure within 1 month, while case 3 implant mobility appeared 2 months after the apex-cutting surgery (about 2.5-3 months after placement).
- The authors themselves cite studies reporting that sinusitis without chronic change can be treated with medication and that direct impact of sinusitis on implant survival is unproven.
- Future work: larger well-designed studies of material-related factors in acute sinus complications and early failure.

## 6. Related Work

- Cites a meta-analysis (Kim et al.) in which preoperative sinusitis and Schneiderian membrane perforation were the only factors affecting postoperative sinusitis, and smoking and residual bone height affected implant failure (failure 5.19 times higher with residual height under 5 mm); this is quoted from the paper's discussion, not checked against the original.
- Cites an earlier chronic sinusitis-related late-failure analysis by the same group (contamination, microorganisms, apex perforation).
- Cites a study of graft materials (Oragraft Ca/P ratio 2.17, extensive early Ca dissolution) to interpret the missing P signal.

## 7. Glossary

- **조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure)**: lack of osseointegration before functional loading.
- **SEM-EDS**: scanning electron microscopy with energy-dispersive X-ray spectrometry for surface morphology and elemental composition.
- **TEM**: transmission electron microscopy for cellular ultrastructure.
- **MESS**: modified endoscopic-assisted sinus surgery (as used in the paper).
- **"Distant" osseointegration**: the authors' term for bone attached away from the implant surface, interpreted as failure to integrate.
- **Oragraft**: allograft mix of 70% mineralized and 30% demineralized ground cortical bone.
