## Why Ingested

This double-blind RCT directly tests iodine mouthwash against saline for chemora­diotherapy-induced oral mucositis prevention, providing controlled evidence for a low-cost antiseptic agent whose role in OM prophylaxis remains contested. Its null result is clinically informative alongside the broader mouthwash rankings in [[oral-medicine/mucositis/wang-2023-mouthwash-oral-mucositis-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

This was a single-center, prospective, double-blind, randomized controlled trial evaluating the efficacy of an in-house iodine solution (IS) mouthwash versus normal saline solution (NSS) for prophylaxis of concurrent chemoradiation (CCRT)-induced oral mucositis (OM) in 20 head and neck cancer patients (1:1 randomization) conducted at Khon Kaen University, Thailand, from January to December 2019. The primary outcome was weekly Oral Mucositis Assessment Scale (OMAS) score, assessed from baseline through 4 weeks post-CCRT.

No statistically significant differences were found between the IS and NSS groups in mean weekly OMAS (overall mean difference -0.40, 95% CI -3.95 to 3.15, p=0.825; p>0.999 for each week), pain score, or impact on swallowing score. The mean onset to OM (all grades) was 12.70 vs 11.80 days (p=0.704), and median duration of OM was 72.00 vs 88.00 days (p=0.365). Severe OM (WHO grade 3-4) occurred in 2 patients in the IS group and 1 in the NSS group.

The study failed to demonstrate statistically significant superiority of IS mouthwash over NSS for preventing CCRT-induced OM, likely due to small sample size and possibly insufficient iodine concentration. Larger-scale studies are warranted to confirm or refute any potential benefit.

## 세줄요약

본 연구는 태국 콘켄 대학에서 2019년 1월부터 12월까지 수행된, 단일기관, 전향적, 이중맹검, 무작위대조 임상시험으로, 동시항암방사선치료(CCRT)를 받는 두경부암 환자 20명(1:1 배정)을 대상으로 자체 제조한 요오드 용액(IS) 가글의 구강점막염(OM) 예방 효과를 정상 생리식염수(NSS)와 비교 평가하였다. 1차 평가변수는 주간 구강점막염 평가 척도(OMAS) 점수로, CCRT 시작 전부터 치료 종료 4주 후까지 측정되었다.

IS군과 NSS군 간 주간 평균 OMAS 점수(전체 평균 차이 -0.40, 95% CI -3.95~3.15, p=0.825; 매주 p>0.999), 통증 점수, 연하 영향 점수에서 통계적으로 유의한 차이는 없었다. 전체 등급 OM 발생까지의 평균 기간은 12.70일 대 11.80일(p=0.704), OM 지속 기간 중앙값은 72.00일 대 88.00일(p=0.365)이었다. 중증 OM(WHO 3-4등급)은 IS군 2명, NSS군 1명에서 발생하였다.

본 연구는 IS 가글이 CCRT 유발 OM 예방에 NSS 대비 통계적으로 우월함을 입증하지 못했으며, 이는 적은 표본 수와 불충분한 요오드 농도에 기인할 가능성이 있다. 추후 더 큰 규모의 연구가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First double-blind RCT evaluating an in-house iodine solution mouthwash for prevention of CCRT-induced OM in head and neck cancer patients.
- Demonstrated no significant difference between IS and NSS in weekly OMAS scores, with p > 0.999 for every week.
- No significant differences in pain score, swallowing impact, onset, or duration of OM between groups.
- No secondary oral infections (bacterial or fungal) observed in the IS group, while one case of oral candidiasis occurred in the NSS group.
- Highlights the need for larger sample sizes and potentially higher iodine concentrations in future studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Single-center, prospective, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial.
- Randomization: Stratified simple random sampling by primary cancer site and stage; opaque envelopes with cards marked A or B.
- Blinding: Double-blind (patients and investigators). NSS was colored light brown to mimic IS; mouthwashes coded by the manufacturer and revealed at study end.
- Sample size: 20 patients enrolled (10 per group); 5 withdrew; 14 completed the study; ITT analysis performed (n=20).
- Participants: Head and neck cancer patients, aged 18-70, receiving definitive or postoperative CCRT with platinum-based agents, oral mucosa in radiation field, planned dose >50 Gy. Excluded: ECOG >2, iodine/seafood allergy, unable to follow up, pregnant/lactating, prior RT, multiple primaries, hematologic malignancies, altered fractionation, prior hyperbaric oxygen.
- Intervention: 30 mL of IS (free iodine 0.025%) or NSS, gargled for 30 seconds, three times daily after meals, from first day of CCRT to completion.
- Outcomes: Primary = OMAS; Secondary = pain score (0-10), impact on swallowing (0-10), incidence/severity/onset/duration of OM (WHO grading), analgesic use, treatment break, secondary infection.
- Statistics: GEE model with Bonferroni correction for weekly OMAS/pain/swallowing; Independent T-test or Mann-Whitney U for continuous variables; Fisher's exact or Chi-square for categorical. Significance set at p < 0.05. Software: SPSS v19, STATA v10.1.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome (overall OMAS) —** IS: 7.44 (±6.45) vs NSS: 7.28 (±6.25); mean difference -0.40 (95% CI -3.95 to 3.15, p=0.825). Weekly p > 0.999 for every week.
- **Pain score —** Overall mean IS: 3.93 vs NSS: 3.14; weekly p > 0.999.
- **Impact on swallowing —** Weekly p > 0.999.
- **Onset to OM (all grades) —** IS: 12.70 ± 5.72 days vs NSS: 11.80 ± 4.66 days (p=0.704).
- **Duration of OM (all grades) —** IS: median 72.00 (IQR 67-75) vs NSS: 88.00 (IQR 71.5-92) days (p=0.365).
- **Severe OM (WHO 3-4) —** IS: n=2, NSS: n=1 (p=NA). Median onset: 45.50 vs 33.00 days; duration: 8.00 vs 57.00 days.
- **Treatment break —** IS: 15.25 ± 5.91 vs NSS: 7.00 ± 6.10 days (p=0.131).
- **Secondary infection —** Oral candidiasis in 1 patient (NSS group); none in IS group.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n=20) leading to underpowered study and inability to detect significant differences.
- Low concentration of free iodine (0.025%) in the IS mouthwash; prior positive studies used 0.5–1% PVP-I concentrations.
- Higher mean oral cavity radiation dose in the IS group (59.30 Gy vs 49.72 Gy) may have biased results against the intervention.
- Use of modern radiation techniques (3D-CRT/IMRT) may have reduced OM severity overall, requiring larger sample to show added benefit.
- Outpatient compliance with mouthwash was not strictly monitored.
- Unfavorable taste/odor of IS may have affected compliance (nausea reported in one patient from each group).

## 6. Related Work

- Rahn et al. 1997 — PVP-I mouthwash delayed onset and shortened duration of OM in patients receiving radiochemotherapy (mean onset 2.25 weeks vs 1.5 weeks; duration 2.75 weeks vs 9.25 weeks).
- Madan et al. 2008 — 1% PVP-I significantly reduced OM severity (mean WHO score 0.30) in head and neck RT patients (RT alone, not CCRT).
- Rao et al. 2014 — 0.1% PVP-I did not show significant benefit in preventing radiation-induced OM in CCRT or RT-alone patients.
- Antunes et al. 2013 — Low-level laser therapy reduced severity of CCRT-induced OM.
- Chitapanarux et al. 2018 — Benzydamine HCl mouthwash was superior to basic oral care for reducing OM severity during CCRT.

## 7. Glossary

- **OM (Oral Mucositis)**: 구강점막염. 방사선 또는 항암화학요법으로 인해 구강 점막에 발생하는 염증성 궤양성 병변.
- **CCRT (Concurrent Chemoradiation)**: 동시항암방사선치료. 항암화학요법과 방사선치료를 동시에 시행하는 치료 방식.
- **OMAS (Oral Mucositis Assessment Scale)**: 구강점막염 평가 척도. 점막 병변의 범위와 중증도를 정량화하는 도구 (Sonis et al., 1999).
- **PVP-I (Povidone-Iodine)**: 포비돈-아이오딘. 요오드-폴리비닐피롤리돈 착화합물로, 광범위 항균 및 항염 효과를 가지는 구강 세정제.
- **IS (Iodine Solution)**: 본 연구에서 사용된 in-house 요오드 용액. 유리 요오드(free iodine) 농도 0.025% (0.25 mg/mL).
- **HNC (Head and Neck Cancer)**: 두경부암. 구강, 인두, 후두, 비강 등 두경부 영역에 발생하는 악성 종양.
- **NSS (Normal Saline Solution)**: 정상 생리식염수. 0.9% 염화나트륨 수용액으로, 본 연구에서 위약 대조군으로 사용됨.
