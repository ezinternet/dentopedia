## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/endodontics/anatomy/ribeiro-2022-guided-endodontics-static-dynamic-review]]는 유도 근관치료(guided endodontics, SN/DN) narrative review로 "SN vs DN 직접 비교 RCT 부재"라는 근거 공백을 지적했는데, 본 umbrella review(Puri 2024, PROSPERO CRD42024564150)는 그 이후 발표된 6편의 체계적 문헌고찰(2편은 메타분석 포함)을 AMSTAR 2로 재평가·종합하여 정적 항법(Static Navigation, SN) 근단 위치 성공률 96.8%, 근관 위치 성공률 98.1% 등 정량적 수치로 그 narrative를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42024564150) of 6 systematic reviews on guided endodontics (static navigation, SN / dynamic navigation, DN; 2 of the 6 included meta-analyses), quality-appraised with AMSTAR 2.

Pooled evidence shows high success rates for computer-aided navigation — apex-location success 96.8% (SN, endodontic microsurgery) and root-canal-location success 98.1% (access cavities, no significant SN-vs-DN difference), with SN ~27x more effective than conventional endodontic microsurgery; average drill-tip deviation was 0.46 mm.

AMSTAR 2 scores ranged from moderate (10/16) to high (14.5/16) quality; evidence is dominated by case reports/series with limited follow-up, so further high-quality RCTs are needed before routine clinical adoption.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰(umbrella review, PROSPERO CRD42024564150): 유도 근관치료(guided endodontics; 정적 항법 Static Navigation, SN / 동적 항법 Dynamic Navigation, DN)에 관한 체계적 문헌고찰 6편(그 중 2편은 메타분석 포함)을 AMSTAR 2로 질 평가.

종합 근거상 컴퓨터 유도 항법의 성공률이 높음 — 근단 위치(apex-location) 성공률 96.8%(SN, 근관 미세수술), 근관 위치(root-canal-location) 성공률 98.1%(접근와동, SN vs DN 유의차 없음); SN은 통상적 근관 미세수술 대비 약 27배 더 효과적; 평균 드릴팁 편차 0.46 mm.

AMSTAR 2 점수는 중등도(10/16)~고품질(14.5/16)로 다양했고, 증례보고·증례군 중심으로 추적관찰이 부족해 일상 임상 적용 전 고품질 RCT가 추가로 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review synthesizing systematic-review-level evidence specifically on the clinical impact of guided endodontics (SN and DN) on treatment success.
- Applies AMSTAR 2 across all 6 included systematic reviews, exposing methodological-quality variability (10/16 to 14.5/16) that had not been consolidated before.
- Consolidates quantitative outcomes from the two SRs that performed meta-analysis (Zubizarreta-Macho Jan 2021 and Mar 2021): apex-location success 96.8% (SN, microsurgery) and root-canal-location success 98.1% (access cavities), with SN ~27x more effective than conventional endodontic microsurgery.
- Documents a consistent quantitative safety/precision signal (average drill-tip deviation 0.46 mm) that recurs across independent reviews (Pena-Bengoa 2023, Zehnder 2016, Connert 2017, Nayak 2018).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, EBSCOhost, Cochrane (2016–July 2024) + manual/grey-literature search; search string `(((guided endodontics) OR (static navigation)) OR (dynamic navigation)) AND (systematic review)`.
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers (title/abstract, then full text); disagreements resolved by a third reviewer.
- **Eligibility**: Included SRs/MAs of guided endodontics comprising RCTs, randomized clinical trials, case series, and case reports, English language. Excluded non-SR reviews, non-English articles, in vitro/ex vivo studies, randomized experimental (in vitro) studies, animal studies, narrative reviews, expert opinions, short communications, editorials.
- **Data extraction**: First author/year/title/journal, databases searched, country, search period, language, meta-analysis Y/N, number of included studies, study design, endodontic application, tooth involved, quality, outcome-evaluation method, quality-assessment instrument.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR 2 (16-item checklist; Yes/Partial Yes/No/N/A per item), classifying reviews as high/moderate/low/severely-low quality.
- **PRISMA flow**: 112 records → 87 duplicates + 2 automation-excluded + 2 other-reason excluded → 11 assessed full-text → 5 excluded (2 pilot, 3 in vitro) → 6 included SRs (2 with meta-analysis).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| SR (year) | AMSTAR 2 | Meta-analysis | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pena-Bengoa 2023 | 10.5/16 (moderate) | No | High success locating calcified canals; only 2 failures among 45 studies; average drill-tip deviation 0.46 mm |
| Zubizarreta-Macho Jan 2021 | 14/16 (high) | Yes (7 studies) | SN apex-location success 96.8% (CI 93.0–100%); 27x more effective than conventional microsurgery (OR 27.7, 95% CI 11.3–68.1, p<0.0001) |
| Zubizarreta-Macho Mar 2021 | 14.5/16 (high) | Yes (13 studies) | Root-canal-location success 98.1% (CI 95.7–100%); SN 98.5% vs DN 94.5% (NS, p=0.451); in vitro 96.2% vs in vivo 100% |
| Moreno-Rabie 2020 | 11.5/16 (moderate) | No | Guided access cavity and surgery highly accurate; "acceptable" precision defined by canal locatability + apical healing at follow-up (Buchgreitz 2019, n=50) |
| Vasudevan 2022 | 12.5/16 (high) | No (heterogeneity too high) | DN mean 3D deviation 0.67 mm coronal / 0.9 mm apical, 2.50° angular; reduced iatrogenic errors; treatment time 11.5 sec–<45 min |
| Poirazi 2023 | 10/16 (moderate) | No | Guided fiber-post removal: 100% success, no fractures/perforations across case reports |

Overall conclusion: guided endodontics (SN and DN) shows consistently high accuracy/success (generally >95%) for locating calcified canals, endodontic access cavities, microsurgery apex location, and post removal, outperforming conventional freehand approaches, at the cost of a small but consistent drill-tip deviation (~0.46 mm) and reliance on case-report/series-level evidence for most applications.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Underlying evidence base is dominated by case reports and case series with limited or absent long-term follow-up, restricting durability/success conclusions.
- Only 2 of 6 included SRs performed formal meta-analysis; the rest are narrative/qualitative syntheses, and AMSTAR 2 scores for the non-meta-analysis SRs were generally lower (10–12.5/16).
- Funding-source reporting was frequently absent (Pena-Bengoa, Moreno-Rabie, Poirazi scored "No" on AMSTAR 2 item 10), and publication bias is a plausible concern across the case-report-heavy literature.
- Guide-design-dependent drill-tip deviation (0.46 mm) makes SN less suitable for complex anatomy (e.g., C-shaped canals); DN's real-time adjustment may mitigate this but adds cost/learning-curve burden.
- Authors call for high-quality RCTs, long-term clinical follow-up, standardized protocols, and cost-effectiveness analyses before guided endodontics can be considered routine standard of care.

## 7. Glossary

- **Guided endodontics**: Use of computer-aided navigation (static or dynamic) combined with preoperative CBCT imaging to guide root canal access/treatment with a physical or virtual drilling template.
- **Static navigation (SN)**: Pre-planned, 3D-printed surgical template (CAD/CAM, rapid prototyping) fixed to guide drill trajectory; not adjustable intraoperatively.
- **Dynamic navigation (DN)**: Real-time optical/stereoscopic tracking system with motion-tracking cameras on handpiece and patient, allowing intraoperative trajectory adjustment.
- **AMSTAR 2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews 2 — 16-item checklist for appraising SR/MA methodological quality (high/moderate/low/critically-low quality).
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews.
- **Umbrella review**: A review that synthesizes evidence from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a topic, rather than primary studies directly.
