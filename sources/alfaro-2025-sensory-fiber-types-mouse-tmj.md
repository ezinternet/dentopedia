## Why Ingested

Establishes the first comprehensive neuronal subtype map of the TMJ retrodisc, anterior disc, and joint capsule using immunohistochemistry (IHC) and transgenic reporter mice. This is foundational for understanding WHY retrodiscal tissue is the primary pain source in TMD — it is the only TMJ structure harboring non-peptidergic nociceptors (MrgprD/A3/C11) in addition to the dominant CGRP+ peptidergic fibers. No equivalent page exists in the wiki. Reinforces `[[tmj/kim-2012-diagnosis-treatment-temporomandibular-disorder]]` (retrodiscal tissue pain mechanism explanation).

## Three-line Summary

The articular disc is completely nerve-free, confirming that TMJ disc displacement alone does not directly cause pain. The retrodisc uniquely contains ~20% non-peptidergic nociceptors (MrgprD, MrgprA3, MrgprC11) absent elsewhere in the joint, explaining its exceptional pain sensitivity. Absence of A-LTMRs (non-nociceptive mechanoreceptors marked by parvalbumin/NFH+CGRP−) across all TMJ tissues means mechanical stimulation of the joint is predominantly nociceptive rather than proprioceptive.

## 세줄요약

관절원판 (articular disc)에는 신경이 전혀 없어 원판 변위 자체가 직접 통증 원인이 아님을 확인했다. 후방조직 (retrodisc)에만 비펩타이드성 통각수용기 (non-peptidergic nociceptor) — MrgprD/A3/C11 — 이 ~20% 존재하며, 이는 관절낭·전방원판에는 없는 고유한 특성이다. 모든 TMJ 조직에서 저역치기계수용기 (A-LTMR, low-threshold mechanoreceptor)가 부재하여, TMJ의 기계적 자극은 대부분 통각으로 처리됨을 시사한다.

## Key Contributions

1. **First comprehensive sensory fiber subtype map** of individual TMJ tissues (joint capsule, anterior disc, retrodisc) using IHC + transgenic reporter mice — no prior study performed this level of tissue-by-tissue resolution.
2. **Articular disc confirmed nerve-free** (pgp9.5 negative) — pain in TMD does not originate from the disc itself; disc displacement is a structural event, not a direct pain generator.
3. **Retrodisc uniquely contains ~20% non-peptidergic nociceptors** (MrgprD, MrgprA3, MrgprC11 subtypes) — distinct from all other TMJ structures, which are >95% peptidergic (CGRP+).
4. **Absence of A-LTMRs** (non-nociceptive mechanoreceptors: parvalbumin+ fibers, NFH+CGRP− myelinated fibers) across all TMJ tissues explains why mechanical stimuli are predominantly nociceptive.
5. **Retrodisc Htr3a expression ~50%** vs ~99% in capsule/anterior disc — reflects broader nociceptor subtype diversity in retrodisc and potential serotonin receptor modulation differences.
6. **Perivascular innervation**: blood vessels are surrounded by both sensory (~35% pgp9.5+) and sympathetic (~40% TH+) fibers — relevant to neurogenic inflammation and vascular regulation in TMD.

## Methodology

- **Animal model**: Adult wild-type C57Bl/6 mice; multiple transgenic reporter lines (CGRP-cre/tdTomato, MrgprD-GFP, MrgprA3-cre/tdTomato, MrgprC11-cre/tdTomato, Htr3a-cre/tdTomato, Sst-cre/tdTomato, PV-cre/tdTomato, etc.)
- **Tissue sectioning**: 2D cryosection IHC at 20 μm thickness
- **Immunohistochemistry markers**:
  - pgp9.5: pan-neuronal marker (all nerve fibers)
  - NFH (neurofilament heavy chain): myelinated fibers
  - CGRP (calcitonin gene-related peptide): peptidergic sensory fibers
  - TH (tyrosine hydroxylase): sympathetic fibers
  - α-SMA: vascular smooth muscle (vessel identification)
  - MrgprD, MrgprA3, MrgprC11: non-peptidergic nociceptors
  - Htr3a (5-HT3a receptor): C-LTMRs and some nociceptors
  - Somatostatin (SST): inhibitory interneurons/some sensory fibers
  - Parvalbumin (PV): non-nociceptive mechanoreceptors (A-LTMRs)
- **Fiber quantification**: Manual counting from ≥3 sections per condition
- **Statistics**: 2-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post-hoc correction; p<0.05 threshold

## Key Results

### Fiber distribution by tissue

| Marker | Joint Capsule | Anterior Disc | Retrodisc |
|---|---|---|---|
| pgp9.5+ (all fibers) | positive | positive | positive |
| Articular disc nerve-free | — | — | n/a (disc itself negative) |
| Myelinated (NFH+) | 28.1 ± 7.6% | 30.3 ± 6.1% | 30.2 ± 12% |
| CGRP+ peptidergic | 95.9% | 98.9% | 75.4 ± 9.9% |
| Htr3a+ | 99.9% | 90.3% | 46.6 ± 5.5% |
| A-LTMRs (NFH+CGRP−) | absent | absent | absent |
| Parvalbumin (PV+) | absent | absent | absent |
| MrgprD+ | <5% | <5% | ~10.8% |
| MrgprA3+ | <5% | <5% | ~20% combined Mrgpr+ |
| MrgprC11+ | <5% | <5% | included above |
| Somatostatin+ | <5% | <5% | <5% |

### Vascular innervation
- Blood vessels: ~40% TH+ (sympathetic) + ~35% pgp9.5+ (sensory) perivascular association
- Implies dual autonomic and sensory regulation of TMJ vasculature

### Absence of A-LTMRs
- No PV+ fibers in any TMJ tissue
- No NFH+CGRP− (non-peptidergic myelinated) fibers = no Aβ low-threshold mechanoreceptors
- Clinical implication: proprioceptive/non-nociceptive mechanical transduction is absent; all mechanosensation in TMJ is likely nociceptive

## Limitations

- Mouse model — anatomical and neurochemical differences from human TMJ may limit direct translation
- Sex bias: MrgprA3-tdTomato experiments used only female mice; Htr3a-tdTomato and several others used only male mice
- Manual fiber counting may over/underestimate due to branch confusion in 2D sections
- 2D IHC cannot capture 3D neural architecture or true fiber density
- Cannot distinguish passing fibers vs terminal innervation from section data alone
- Reporter mice may have incomplete Cre recombination, potentially underestimating some populations

## Glossary

- **pgp9.5** (protein gene product 9.5): pan-neuronal ubiquitin carboxyl-terminal hydrolase; marks all peripheral nerve fibers
- **CGRP** (calcitonin gene-related peptide): neuropeptide released by peptidergic sensory C-fibers and Aδ-fibers; key mediator of neurogenic inflammation
- **NFH** (neurofilament heavy chain): cytoskeletal protein marking myelinated nerve fibers
- **A-LTMR** (low-threshold mechanoreceptor, Aβ type): non-nociceptive mechanoreceptors mediating touch/proprioception; absent in TMJ
- **MrgprD/A3/C11** (Mas-related G protein-coupled receptors): non-peptidergic nociceptors; MrgprD responds to β-alanine, MrgprA3 to chloroquine, MrgprC11 to BAM8-22
- **Htr3a** (5-HT3a receptor / serotonin type 3a receptor): marks C-LTMRs and some nociceptors; involved in serotonin-mediated pain signaling
- **TH** (tyrosine hydroxylase): enzyme marker for sympathetic (adrenergic) nerve fibers
- **Retrodisc** (retrodiscal tissue): highly vascularized and innervated tissue posterior to the articular disc; primary pain generator in disc displacement TMD
- **IHC** (immunohistochemistry): technique using antibodies to detect specific proteins in tissue sections
