## Why Ingested

This SR+MA directly addresses post-FESS nasal irrigation efficacy — a core postoperative modality for chronic rhinosinusitis patients, including odontogenic maxillary sinusitis cases managed with FESS. It extends [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/kim-2025-treatment-of-non-chronic-odontogenic]]'s socket irrigation approach by providing high-level evidence on solution selection (saline, budesonide, hypertonic, povidone-iodine) for sinonasal irrigation after endoscopic sinus surgery.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 14 RCTs evaluating various nasal irrigation solutions versus normal saline in post-FESS patients with chronic rhinosinusitis.
Primary outcomes (SNOT-22 and LKES scores) showed budesonide irrigation significantly outperformed normal saline, though overall pooled SMD for symptoms (−0.69, 95% CI −1.64 to 0.27, p=0.157) and endoscopic scores (−0.48, 95% CI −1.32 to 0.36, p=0.264) did not reach statistical significance due to high heterogeneity.
Clinical implication: budesonide nasal irrigation appears most effective for post-FESS symptom and endoscopic score improvement, but substantial heterogeneity and publication bias warrant cautious interpretation.

## 세줄요약

기능적 내시경 부비동 수술(FESS) 후 만성 비부비동염 환자 14개 RCT를 대상으로 다양한 비강 세척액(생리식염수, 부데소나이드, 고장성 식염수, 포비돈요오드 등)을 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석으로 비교.
주요 결과(SNOT-22, LKES)에서 부데소나이드 세척이 생리식염수 대비 유의하게 우수했으나, 전체 풀링된 증상(SMD −0.69, 95% CI −1.64~0.27, p=0.157) 및 내시경 점수(SMD −0.48, 95% CI −1.32~0.36, p=0.264)는 높은 이질성으로 통계적 유의성 미도달.
임상적 함의: FESS 후 비강 세척 시 부데소나이드가 증상과 내시경 소견 개선에 가장 효과적이나, 상당한 이질성과 출판 편향이 있어 신중한 해석 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First comprehensive SR+MA on post-FESS irrigation solutions** — synthesizes 14 RCTs (n≈1,000+ patients) comparing normal saline against budesonide, hypertonic saline, povidone-iodine, amphotericin B, hypochlorous acid, and other agents.
2. **Dual validated outcome metrics** — uses both patient-reported (SNOT-22) and clinician-assessed (Lund-Kennedy Endoscopic Score, LKES) endpoints, enabling symptom–endoscopy correlation.
3. **Subgroup analysis by solution type** — isolates budesonide as the only agent with consistent superiority over normal saline across both outcomes.
4. **Risk-of-bias transparency** — applies Cochrane RoB 2 tool; most trials had "some concerns" (blinding of outcome assessors), informing GRADE certainty downgrade.
5. **Heterogeneity & publication bias quantified** — I² >75% for primary outcomes; funnel plot asymmetry and Egger's test (p<0.1) signal small-study effects.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

### Search & Eligibility
- **Databases**: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus (inception to March 2023)
- **Design**: Parallel-arm RCTs only; adults (≥18 yr) undergoing FESS for CRSwNP or CRSsNP
- **Intervention**: Any nasal irrigation solution (steroid, hypertonic saline, antimicrobial, surfactant, etc.)
- **Comparator**: Normal saline (0.9% NaCl) irrigation
- **Outcomes**: SNOT-22 (primary), LKES (secondary); follow-up ≥4 weeks post-op

### Data Synthesis
- **Model**: Random-effects (DerSimonian-Laird) with standardized mean difference (SMD) for continuous outcomes
- **Heterogeneity**: Cochran's Q, I² statistic; I² >75% = substantial
- **Subgroups**: Pre-specified by irrigation agent (budesonide, hypertonic saline, povidone-iodine, others)
- **Sensitivity**: Leave-one-out; exclusion of high-RoB trials
- **Publication bias**: Funnel plots, Egger's regression test
- **Software**: R (meta, metafor packages); PRISMA 2020 flow diagram

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Primary Outcome: SNOT-22 (6 studies, n≈400)
- **Overall pooled SMD**: −0.69 (95% CI −1.64 to 0.27), p=0.157 — **not statistically significant**
- **I² = 89%** (substantial heterogeneity)
- **Subgroup — Budesonide** (3 studies): SMD −1.42 (95% CI −2.10 to −0.74), p<0.001 — **significant**
- **Subgroup — Hypertonic saline** (2 studies): SMD −0.31 (95% CI −1.12 to 0.50), p=0.45 — NS
- **Subgroup — Povidone-iodine** (2 studies): SMD −0.58 (95% CI −1.45 to 0.29), p=0.19 — NS

### Secondary Outcome: LKES (6 studies, n≈400)
- **Overall pooled SMD**: −0.48 (95% CI −1.32 to 0.36), p=0.264 — **not statistically significant**
- **I² = 84%** (substantial heterogeneity)
- **Subgroup — Budesonide** (3 studies): SMD −1.15 (95% CI −1.78 to −0.52), p<0.001 — **significant**
- **Subgroup — Hypertonic saline** (2 studies): SMD −0.22 (95% CI −0.98 to 0.54), p=0.57 — NS
- **Subgroup — Amphotericin B / HOCl / PVP-I**: inconsistent, wide CIs

### Risk of Bias (Cochrane RoB 2)
- **Randomization**: 10/14 low risk, 4/14 some concerns
- **Allocation concealment**: 8/14 low, 6/14 some concerns
- **Blinding (participants/personnel)**: 12/14 some concerns (irrigation solutions hard to blind)
- **Blinding (outcome assessment)**: 9/14 some concerns (endoscopic scoring often unblinded)
- **Incomplete outcome data**: 12/14 low risk
- **Selective reporting**: 11/14 low risk

### Publication Bias
- Funnel plot asymmetry visible for both SNOT-22 and LKES
- Egger's test: p=0.08 (SNOT-22), p=0.06 (LKES) — suggestive of small-study effects
- Trim-and-fill adjustment did not materially change conclusions

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

1. **High heterogeneity (I² >80%)** — driven by varied irrigation protocols (volume, frequency, duration), FESS extent, and baseline severity; limits pooled estimate reliability.
2. **Predominantly Asian study populations** (India, China, Korea, Malaysia, Thailand, Turkey) — generalizability to other ethnicities uncertain.
3. **Blinding limitations** — most trials could not blind participants or outcome assessors to irrigation type; performance/detection bias likely.
4. **Short follow-up** — most trials 4–12 weeks; long-term mucosal healing and revision surgery rates unknown.
5. **No cost-effectiveness or patient adherence data** — budesonide irrigation requires prescription and may have adherence barriers.
6. **CRSwNP vs CRSsNP not separately analyzed** — polyp phenotype may modify irrigation response.
7. **GRADE certainty: LOW to VERY LOW** for primary outcomes due to RoB, inconsistency, imprecision.

**Future research needs**: standardized irrigation protocols (volume, frequency, duration); head-to-head budesonide vs other steroids; long-term outcomes; cost-effectiveness; microbiome impact.
