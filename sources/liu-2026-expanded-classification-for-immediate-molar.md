## Three-line Summary

Conceptual development study with a three-round Delphi consensus and exploratory retrospective review of 64 immediately placed molar implants (38 maxillary, 26 mandibular), proposing a six-category anatomy-based case-selection classification.

Type A (complete septal containment) was most predictable — insertion torque (IT) >30 N/cm and survival >90% — while Type P (palatal root socket) showed implant stability quotient (ISQ) ≥60 and early marginal bone loss (MBL) 0.11–0.19 mm at 6–24 months; cohort means were IT 35 ± 12 N/cm, ISQ 72 ± 8 at placement rising to 78 ± 6 at 4 weeks, and ΔMBL −0.4 ± 0.3 mm at 12 months.

The framework extends the Smith–Tarnow septal system with root-socket anchorage and a treatment hierarchy, but is explicitly decision support rather than outcome validation — prospective multicenter validation is required before it can be used as a predictive tool.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Proposes a **six-category classification** for molar IIP combining septal bone types (A: complete septal containment; B: partial septal support; C: peripheral socket engagement) with root-socket anchorage types (D: distal; M: mesial; P: palatal root socket).
- Defines **standardized diagnostic parameters** per type: ≥3 mm circumferential septal support for Type A; primary stability target ~30 N/cm and ISQ ≥60; ≥2 mm clearance from maxillary sinus / inferior alveolar nerve; prosthetic emergence feasibility; long-term predictability benchmark MBL <0.5 mm over 12–24 months.
- Provides an explicit **treatment hierarchy**: prioritize Type A, then Type P in the maxilla and Types D/M when root-socket anatomy is favorable; reserve Types B/C or delayed placement for higher-risk scenarios.
- States **type-specific contraindication thresholds**: no Type P when palatal bone height <4 mm or sinus-floor contact <1 mm; no Type D when prosthetic emergence angle >25° or buccal wall defect >4 mm; no Type C when buccolingual ridge width <6 mm.
- Positions Type P as a **minimally invasive alternative to sinus augmentation** in selected maxillary molars with adequate palatal root length/bone density.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Development phase**: structured literature review (PubMed/MEDLINE 2000–2023), CBCT-based anatomical analysis (Blue Sky Plan), retrospective case-pattern evaluation, and a three-round Delphi process (6 specialists: 3 implantologists, 2 prosthodontists, 1 oral radiologist; ≥10 y experience each).
- **Reliability**: CBCT measurement intraobserver ICC 0.92 (95% CI 0.85–0.97, n=13 re-reads); Delphi pre-training κ 0.78 (95% CI 0.62–0.94) → post-training κ 0.92 (95% CI 0.81–0.98); final case assignments 100% agreement after round 3 across 64 cases.
- **Exploratory phase**: 64 consecutive single-molar IIP cases (Kaohsiung Medical University Hospital, Jan 2018–Dec 2023), ≥12-month follow-up; Astra Tech (n=37) / Osstem (n=27) moderately rough implants; flapless in 59/64 (92%); root sectioning in all multi-rooted sites.
- **Stability/gap protocol**: IT recorded by torque wrench, RFA by Osstell Beacon at placement and 4 weeks; primary stability threshold ≥30 N/cm or ISQ ≥60; Type A/B self-contained (no graft), Types C/D/M/P xenograft granules ± resorbable membrane.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Type distribution** (n=64): A 12 (19%), B 15 (23%), C 14 (22%), D 10 (16%), M 8 (12%), P 5 (8%). Types A and P more frequent in the maxilla; B/C/D/M more frequent in mandibular molars.
- **Overall stability**: mean IT 35 ± 12 N/cm; mean ISQ 72 ± 8 at placement, 78 ± 6 at 4 weeks; mean marginal bone level change −0.4 ± 0.3 mm at 12 months.
- **Type A**: IT >30 N/cm, survival >90% in well-selected cases; immediate provisionalization feasible when primary stability adequate.
- **Type B**: small gaps <2 mm may heal without augmentation; larger defects require grafting and delayed loading; loss of buccal plate may indicate conversion to delayed protocol.
- **Type C**: wide-diameter implants (~6–7 mm) and "hourglass" morphology for cortical anchorage; mandibular second molars cited with buccolingual ridge width ~9 mm and cortical thickness ~9 mm.
- **Type P**: ISQ typically ≥60, early MBL 0.11–0.19 mm at 6–24 months, mean palatal inclination 9.6°, often avoiding sinus augmentation.
- **Safety**: no major complications (early failure, sinus perforation, MBL >2 mm at 12 months) across any type; exploratory association between socket class and primary-stability parameters r = 0.67, P < 0.01 (descriptive only).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Explicitly a **conceptual development investigation with exploratory clinical applicability**, not an outcome-based, predictive, or comparative validation trial; not powered for inter-type survival comparisons or predictive accuracy.
- Authors waive predictive-validity interpretation of the r = 0.67 association; definitive validation requires multicenter prospective studies with standardized protocols, formal reliability testing, and head-to-head comparison against established classifications.
- Evidence base largely retrospective/single-center with short-to-mid-term outcomes; heterogeneity in surgical technique and implant systems limits generalizability.
- Operator-dependent: requires careful CBCT interpretation and intraoperative socket assessment; misclassification possible in borderline/mixed-morphology cases.
- Biomechanical and patient risk modifiers (implant macrodesign, loading strategy, bruxism, periodontal history, occlusal pattern, bone quality, soft-tissue conditions) not yet incorporated.

## 7. Glossary

- **IIP** — Immediate Implant Placement (즉시식립).
- **Septum / interradicular septum** — bone between molar roots (치조중격); the basis of the Smith–Tarnow A–C system.
- **IT (Insertion Torque)** — 삽입토크, measured in N/cm in this paper.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)** — 임플란트 안정성 지수, from resonance frequency analysis (RFA).
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)** — 변연골 흡수; ΔMBL = follow-up minus baseline platform-to-first-bone-contact distance.
- **Delphi process** — structured expert-consensus method with iterative rounds and predefined agreement threshold (here ≥80%).
- **Type P** — palatal root socket anchorage; proposed as a sinus-augmentation-sparing option in maxillary molars.
