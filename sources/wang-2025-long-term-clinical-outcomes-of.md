## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA) of RCTs on regeneration of deep intrabony defects (≥3 mm) with ≥5 years follow-up: 17 RCTs (2004-2022), 501 defects, follow-up 5-20 years; 13 studies with some concerns and 4 at high risk of bias.

At ≥5 years GTR gave CAL gain 3.27 mm and PD reduction 4.04 mm versus baseline; biologics/grafts/both gave CAL 3.21 mm and PD 3.92 mm. GTR versus OFD favored GTR (CAL 1.52 mm, 95% CI 0.06-3.10; PD 0.89 mm, 95% CI 0.22-1.99) but not significantly (p = 0.06; p = 0.115).

Regeneration keeps clinical gains over baseline long term, yet superiority over OFD is not conclusively shown; certainty was low for CAL and PD, high for recession.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA): 5년 이상 추적한 깊은 골내결손 (≥3 mm) 재생술 RCT 17편 (2004-2022), 결손 501개, 추적 5-20년; 편향위험 우려 있음 13편, 높음 4편.

5년 이상에서 조직유도재생술 (Guided Tissue Regeneration, GTR)은 기저 대비 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 3.27 mm 획득, 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 4.04 mm 감소; 생물학제제·골이식·병용은 CAL 3.21 mm, PD 3.92 mm. GTR vs OFD는 GTR 우세 경향 (CAL 1.52 mm, 95% CI 0.06-3.10; PD 0.89 mm, 95% CI 0.22-1.99)이나 유의하지 않음 (p = 0.06; p = 0.115).

재생술의 임상 이득은 장기 유지되나 OFD 대비 우월성은 확정되지 않음; 증거 확실성은 CAL·PD 낮음, 퇴축 (REC) 높음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Restricts to ≥5-year follow-up RCTs of intrabony regeneration (5-20 years)
- Reports meta-regression associations with follow-up time, surgical technique, membrane type and baseline measurements
- Rates certainty of evidence (low for CAL and PD, high for REC)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA with meta-regression, random-effects; PRISMA
- **Databases**: electronic and manual searches (databases not named in the abstract)
- **n**: 17 RCTs (2004-2022), 501 defects
- **Outcomes**: PD reduction, CAL gain, recession depth (REC), tooth loss

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- GTR vs baseline at ≥5 y: CAL +3.27 mm (95% CI 2.90-3.65); PD -4.04 mm (95% CI 3.69-4.38)
- Biologics/bone graft/both vs baseline at ≥5 y: CAL +3.21 mm (95% CI 2.72-3.70); PD -3.92 mm (95% CI 3.39-4.44)
- GTR vs OFD: CAL 1.52 mm (95% CI 0.06-3.10), PD 0.89 mm (95% CI 0.22-1.99); not significant (p = 0.06; p = 0.115)
- Certainty: low for CAL and PD; high for REC

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 4 of 17 RCTs at high risk of bias; 13 with some concerns
- Low certainty for CAL and PD; wide CI for GTR vs OFD
- Tooth-loss results not reported in the retrieved abstract
- Abstract-only: study-level data not verified
