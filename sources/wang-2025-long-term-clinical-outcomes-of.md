## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD420250650648) of RCTs on regeneration of deep intrabony defects (≥3 mm) with ≥5 years follow-up: 1159 records yielded 17 RCTs (33 eligible cohorts in 15 unique trials; 501 defects in 356 patients), mean follow-up 9.32 ± 4.75 y (range 5-20); no trial at low risk of bias, 4 at high risk.

At ≥5 years GTR gave CAL gain 3.27 mm (95% CI 2.90-3.65) and PD reduction 4.04 mm versus baseline; biologics/grafts/both gave CAL 3.21 mm and PD 3.92 mm. GTR versus OFD favored GTR but not significantly (CAL -1.52 mm, 95% CI -3.10 to 0.06, p = 0.0601; PD -0.89 mm, 95% CI -1.99 to 0.22, p = 0.1152); tooth loss was 0.01 (<10 y) vs 0.06 (≥10 y).

Regeneration keeps clinical gains over baseline long term, yet superiority over OFD is not conclusively shown; GRADE certainty was low for CAL and PD (risk of bias, inconsistency) and high for recession, with significant publication bias in regenerative-procedure CAL/PD funnel plots.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD420250650648): 5년 이상 추적한 깊은 골내결손 (≥3 mm) 재생술 RCT를 검색해 1159건 중 17편 선정 (15개 고유 시험의 적격 코호트 33개; 환자 356명, 결손 501개), 평균 추적 9.32 ± 4.75년 (5-20년); 편향위험 낮음 0편, 높음 4편.

5년 이상에서 조직유도재생술 (Guided Tissue Regeneration, GTR)은 기저 대비 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 3.27 mm (95% CI 2.90-3.65) 획득, 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 4.04 mm 감소; 생물학제제·골이식·병용은 CAL 3.21 mm, PD 3.92 mm. GTR vs 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD)은 GTR 우세 경향이나 유의하지 않음 (CAL -1.52 mm, 95% CI -3.10~0.06, p = 0.0601; PD -0.89 mm, 95% CI -1.99~0.22, p = 0.1152); 치아 상실 비율은 10년 미만 0.01, 10년 이상 0.06.

재생술의 임상 이득은 장기 유지되나 OFD 대비 우월성은 확정되지 않음; 증거 확실성은 CAL·PD 낮음 (편향위험·비일관성), 퇴축 (REC) 높음이며 재생술 CAL·PD 깔때기 도표에 유의한 출판 편향이 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Restricts to ≥5-year follow-up RCTs of intrabony regeneration (mean 9.32 y, up to 20 y)
- Pools GTR, biologic/graft regeneration and OFD versus baseline and GTR versus OFD for CAL, PD, REC and tooth loss
- Meta-regression of follow-up time, membrane type, surgical technique and baseline measurements; GRADE certainty and Egger-based publication-bias assessment

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA with random-effects meta-analysis and mixed-effects meta-regression; PRISMA; RoB 2; GRADE
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Library, Scopus plus manual search; last search 9 Mar 2025
- **Flow**: 1159 records, 1082 screened, 270 full texts, 253 excluded (follow-up <5 y n = 200; non-RCT n = 42; animal/in vitro n = 11), 17 RCTs (2004-2022)
- **Pool**: 38 arms minus 5 = 33 cohorts in 15 unique trials; 501 defects, 356 patients; 9 parallel, 6 split-mouth, 2 mixed designs
- **Outcomes**: PD reduction, CAL gain, REC, tooth loss (proportion)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- GTR vs baseline: CAL +3.27 mm [2.90; 3.65]; PD -4.04 mm [3.69; 4.38]
- OFD vs baseline: CAL +2.49 mm [1.46; 3.52] (20-y subgroup not significant, 2.19 [-0.68; 5.05]); PD -3.55 mm [2.35; 4.75]
- Biologics/grafts/both vs baseline: CAL +3.21 mm [2.72; 3.70]; PD -3.92 mm [3.39; 4.44]; grafts-only CAL significantly below biologics-only (0.937 [0.11; 1.75]) and biologics + grafts (1.233 [0.44; 2.02])
- GTR vs OFD: CAL -1.52 mm [-3.10; 0.06], p = 0.0601; PD -0.89 mm [-1.99; 0.22], p = 0.1152 (both include zero)
- REC pooled 0.73 mm [0.55; 0.91] (OFD subgroup not significant)
- Tooth loss: 0.01 (<10 y) vs 0.06 (≥10 y), p = 0.0073; overall 0.02 [0.01; 0.04]
- Meta-regression (multivariate): CAL — nonresorbable membrane, follow-up time, baseline CAL (MPPT not confirmed, p = 0.233); PD — follow-up time, M-MIST, nonresorbable membrane; REC — baseline REC
- RoB 2: none low, 4 high; GRADE: low for CAL and PD, high for REC
- Publication bias (Egger): significant for regenerative CAL (p = 0.0158) and PD (p = 0.0003) and tooth loss (p < 0.0001); not for GTR CAL/PD or REC

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No low-risk-of-bias trial; GRADE low for CAL and PD (risk of bias, inconsistency)
- Significant publication bias in regenerative-procedure funnel plots and tooth loss; wide GTR-vs-OFD CIs
- Missing baseline defect morphology in many long-term reports; attrition/survivorship bias possible
- No quantitative synthesis for radiographic, pocket-closure or patient-reported outcomes
- Abstract printed GTR-vs-OFD CIs without minus signs (0.06-3.10; 0.22-1.99); full-text Results give -3.10 to 0.06 and -1.99 to 0.22, consistent with the non-significant p-values. The Conclusion also describes CAL/PD change versus baseline as a "nonsignificant trend", contradicting the Results (p < 0.0001); Results values are used here
- Future work: large multicenter long-term RCTs with standardized protocols and reporting

- **MPPT / M-MIST**: modified papilla preservation technique / modified minimally invasive surgical technique
