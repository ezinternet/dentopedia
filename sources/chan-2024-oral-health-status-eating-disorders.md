## Why Ingested

The intrinsic-acid arm of dental erosion etiology named eating disorders only as a category in [[dental-erosion/kanzow-2016-etiology-pathogenesis-dental-erosion]]. This SR/MA supplies systematic comparative evidence on erosion prevalence (among 12 oral outcomes) in people with eating disorders versus controls, with a GRADE certainty rating.

abstract-only — full text not retrieved

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (33 included studies from 2,322 records; five databases; GRADE) comparing oral health of individuals with eating disorders (EDs) against healthy controls across 12 outcomes, including dental erosion.

Caries and gingival inflammation severity, and prevalence of erosion, myofascial pain, malocclusion, oral lesions and dentine sensitivity were higher in EDs; salivary flow and pH were lower; bruxism did not differ; oral hygiene and periodontal status were inconclusive.

All effect estimates carry very low certainty of evidence; dentists may detect EDs at routine visits and should refer early (abstract-level findings only).

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA): 5개 데이터베이스 2,322건 중 33편을 포함하여 섭식장애 (Eating Disorders, ED) 환자와 건강 대조군의 구강건강 12개 지표(치아 침식 (Dental Erosion) 포함)를 비교.

ED군에서 우식·치은염증 중증도와 침식·근막통증·부정교합·구강병변·상아질 지각과민 유병률이 높았고 타액 분비량·pH는 낮았음; 이갈이 (Bruxism)는 차이 없음, 구강위생·치주 상태는 결론 불충분.

모든 효과추정치의 근거 확실성 (GRADE)이 매우 낮음; 치과의사가 일상 진료에서 ED를 발견해 조기 의뢰할 수 있음 (초록 수준 정보만).

## 1. Document Information

Chan CCK, Chen AM, Lam PPY. J Dent 2024;151:105427. Published 2024-10-19. DOI: [10.1016/j.jdent.2024.105427](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdent.2024.105427). Source: PubMed (PMID 39433151); no PMCID, abstract only.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled comparison of ED patients vs healthy controls on caries, erosion, oral hygiene, periodontal status, salivary flow/pH/composition, myofascial pain, bruxism, malocclusion, oral lesions, dentine hypersensitivity, and OHRQoL.
- Subgroup analysis by ED type and GRADE certainty grading.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Five electronic databases searched; two independent reviewers screened, extracted data, assessed risk of bias; sensitivity and subgroup (by ED type) analyses; GRADE. 2,322 records yielded 33 included articles.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Higher in EDs: caries and gingival inflammation severity; prevalence of erosion, myofascial pain, malocclusion, oral lesions, dentine sensitivity. Lower salivary flow and pH. No significant difference in bruxism. Inconclusive for oral hygiene and periodontal status. Effect sizes are not given in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Very low certainty of evidence for the estimates. Full text was not retrieved, so pooled effect sizes, heterogeneity and per-ED-type results are not available here.
