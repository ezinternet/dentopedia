## Why Ingested

위키는 이미 짧은 임플란트 vs 상악동 이식 결정을 **임상 결과(생존율·MBL)** 로 판정한다 ([[overviews/short-implant-vs-sinus-augmentation-decision]], [[overviews/implant-length-selection-why-not-always-short]], [[sinus-lift/lateral/aleman-2025-short-vs-long-implants-sinus-lift-5yr-sr-ma]]). 이 논문은 그 위에 **연구 활동의 지형도**를 얹는다 — 누가(저자·기관·국가), 어느 저널에서, 어떤 주제가 부상·소진되고 있는지. 즉 *효과성*이 아니라 *활동량*의 지도다. 위키가 어떤 근거를 이미 담았고 무엇을 아직 안 담았는지 점검하는 커버리지 감사(coverage audit) 용도로 유용하다. 방법론적 자매편으로 [[immediate-implant/socket-shield/fang-2025-socket-shield-ai-bibliometric-analysis]]와 같은 bibliometric 계열.

## Three-line Summary

Bibliometric / science-mapping analysis (Bibliometrix R) of 626 Web of Science articles on short implants published 1994–2024, using performance, keyword, co-citation, and thematic-map analyses.

Annual publication growth 12.69% with a 2019 peak (57 papers) then decline; Clinical Oral Implants Research and Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants were the leading journals, Esposito M and Felice P the most influential authors, and thematic mapping resolved four research areas (clinical outcomes/survival, atrophic-posterior-jaw rehabilitation, prosthetic-biomechanical, comparisons with standard implants/augmentation).

This maps *research activity*, not clinical effectiveness — it tells you where the field's attention and evidence-production have concentrated (and where gaps remain: long-term outcomes in compromised bone, RCTs vs alternatives, digital/patient-specific designs), not which treatment works better.

## 세줄요약

Web of Science에서 1994–2024년 짧은 임플란트 관련 논문 626편을 Bibliometrix(R)로 분석한 계량서지·과학지도(bibliometric·science-mapping) 연구로, 성과·키워드·동시인용·주제지도 분석을 수행했다.

연간 발행 증가율 12.69%, 2019년 정점(57편) 후 감소; 최다 영향 저널은 Clinical Oral Implants Research·Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants, 최다 영향 저자는 Esposito M·Felice P였고, 주제지도는 4개 영역(임상결과/생존, 위축 후방 악골 재건, 보철-생역학, 표준 임플란트/증대술과의 비교)을 도출했다.

이 논문은 임상적 *효과성*이 아니라 *연구 활동*의 지도다 — 어느 주제에 근거 생산이 집중됐고 어디에 공백(취약골/전신질환자 장기결과, 대안 대비 RCT, 디지털·환자맞춤 설계)이 남았는지를 보여줄 뿐, 어떤 치료가 더 우수한지를 말하지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First bibliometric/science-mapping analysis dedicated to **short implants** (author states no prior bibliometric analysis on this specific topic existed).
- Quantifies the field's shape: production trend, most influential journals/authors/articles, keyword co-occurrence structure, and a four-theme map with a four-period thematic-evolution trajectory.
- Identifies research gaps and future directions to guide subsequent work.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Search string** (Topic field): ("Short Implant" OR "Short Implants" OR "Short Dental Implant" OR "Short Dental Implants").

**Selection funnel** (PRISMA-adapted):
- 888 records found →
- 692 after WoS category = Dentistry Oral Surgery Medicine →
- 664 after document type = Article OR Review →
- 640 after language = English →
- 627 after WoS index = SCI-EXPANDED, ESCI →
- **626 after timespan 1994–2024** (1 record dropped as still-entering database).

**No length cut-off applied**: because "short implant" definition varies (some studies ≤8 mm, others include 4 mm), all articles self-describing implants as "short" were included for comprehensive coverage.

**Analyses**: (1) performance (main info, annual publication/citation trends, source & author local impact, most-cited documents), (2) word analysis (word cloud/frequency, author-keyword co-occurrence network), (3) co-citation (paper, author, source networks; Louvain, top 30 papers), (4) thematic analysis (thematic map + thematic evolution, top 250 author keywords appearing ≥8 times).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Descriptive metrics (Table 1)**
- Timespan 1994–2024; sources (journals) n=72; documents n=626.
- Annual growth rate **12.69%**; document average age 8.31 y; average citations per document **33.9**; references n=10,030.
- Keywords Plus 943; author's keywords 911.
- Authors n=2,099; single-authored docs 10 (text says 11); international co-authorship **31.31%**; average 5.09 authors/article.
- Document types: articles 507 (81%), reviews 103, plus 1 retracted publication, 6 early-access articles, 7 proceedings papers.

**Production/citation trend**: continuous rise since 1994; publication peak **2019 with 57 papers**, then slight fluctuation/decline after 2019–2020. Average-citation peak **1999 (7.6 avg)**, declining through the 2010s–2020s (recent papers partly disadvantaged by citation lag). Author attributes the post-2019 decline to attention shifting toward patient-specific subperiosteal implants, zygomatic-implant protocols, and augmentation-material innovations.

- Clin Implant Dent Relat Res — h26, 58 NP, 1,892 TC.

**Top authors (Table 3, by h-index)**

- Felice P — h30, g45, **2,616 TC (highest total)**, 45 NP (m≈1.77).
- Pistilli R — h20, 1,524 TC, 29 NP.

**Most globally cited articles (Table 5)**
1. Chiapasco M 2009 — *Bone Augmentation Procedures in Implant Dentistry* (IJOMI), 557 citations.
2. Goodacre CJ 1999 — *Clinical Complications of Osseointegrated Implants* (J Prosthet Dent), 435.
3. Renouard F 2006 — *Impact of Implant Length and Diameter on Survival Rates* (Clin Oral Implants Res), 411.

Most **locally** cited (Table 4): Renouard F 2006 (LC 169), Pistilli R 2013 (LC 147). Most-cited works skew to systematic reviews and RCTs — a strong high-level-evidence emphasis.

**Keyword analysis**: highest-frequency keyword "short implants" (287 occurrences), then "dental implants" (221). Author-keyword co-occurrence resolved into four clusters (red/blue/green/purple).

**Thematic map (Fig 9) — four research areas**
1. Clinical outcomes and survival of short implants.
2. Rehabilitation of atrophic posterior jaws.
3. Prosthetic and biomechanical considerations.
4. Comparisons with standard implants / augmentation-related approaches.

**Thematic evolution (Fig 10)**: early research centred on implant length, survival, and basic clinical feasibility → shifted toward atrophic-posterior-jaw rehabilitation, augmentation-procedure comparison, and prosthetic/biomechanical topics — a move from foundational feasibility questions to specialized, practice-oriented research.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single database (WoS only); English-only; no length threshold enforced (heterogeneous "short" definitions pooled).
- Descriptive/mapping study — quantifies *activity and citation structure*, not treatment effectiveness; citation counts penalize recent papers.
- Stated future-research gaps: long-term outcomes of short implants in diverse/compromised-bone/systemic-condition patients; RCTs comparing short implants with alternative posterior modalities; digital workflows and patient-specific implant designs; effects of these newer technologies on short-implant survival/success.

## 7. Glossary

- **Bibliometric analysis**: quantitative study of publication and citation patterns in a body of literature.
- **Science mapping**: visualization of a field's intellectual/collaborative structure (keyword co-occurrence, co-citation, thematic maps).
- **h-index / g-index / m-index**: productivity-plus-impact metrics; g-index weights highly cited papers more; m-index = h / years-since-first-publication (career-length-adjusted).
- **Local vs global citations (LC/GC)**: citations from within the analyzed corpus (local) vs total citations in the database (global).
- **Co-citation**: two documents cited together by a third; frequent co-citation implies topical relatedness.
- **Thematic map (centrality × density)**: centrality = importance within the field; density = internal development; quadrants = motor / basic / niche / emerging-or-declining themes.
