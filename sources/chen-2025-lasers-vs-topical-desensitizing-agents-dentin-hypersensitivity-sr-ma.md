## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA; MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane, WOS, ClinicalTrials.gov, Scopus; RCTs only; random-effects model throughout because I² > 50% in all subgroups) comparing lasers with topical desensitizing agents for dentin hypersensitivity (abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

Lasers were superior to topical agents only in the long-term air-blast subgroup; other subgroups showed no difference. The medium-term tactile group showed publication bias, and the medium-term air-blast and short-/medium-term tactile groups failed sensitivity analysis; GRADE certainty was very low.

The evidence is insufficient to say whether lasers or topical agents work better; the authors regard laser irradiation as a feasible and reliable option and call for standardized methods and cost-effective laser protocols.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석(PRISMA, RCT만, 모든 하위군 I² > 50%로 랜덤효과모형): 상아질 과민증(Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH)에서 레이저와 국소 탈감작제를 비교 (초록만 확보 — 전문 미확보).

레이저는 장기 공기분사(air blast) 하위군에서만 우월했고 나머지 하위군은 차이가 없었음; 중기 촉각(tactile) 군은 출판 편향, 중기 공기분사·단·중기 촉각 군은 민감도 분석에서 견고하지 않았고 GRADE 근거 확실성은 매우 낮음.

어느 쪽이 더 효과적인지 단정하기에 근거가 불충분하나, 저자들은 레이저를 실행 가능하고 신뢰할 만한 치료로 보며 방법 표준화와 비용 효율적 레이저 연구를 제안.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct laser vs topical-agent comparison, stratified by stimulus (air blast vs tactile) and follow-up (short/medium/long).
- Applies sensitivity analysis, publication-bias assessment and GRADE; reports very-low certainty.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PRISMA; six databases; RCTs only; extracted design, arms, n, age, sex, follow-up, assessment scale, mean and SD.
- Random-effects model only (I² > 50% in all subgroups).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Diode lasers and fluoride compounds were the most commonly used interventions.
- Long-term air-blast: lasers superior; other subgroups: no difference. Effect sizes, study counts and n are not in the abstract.
- Medium-term tactile: publication bias. Medium-term air-blast and short-/medium-term tactile: not robust to sensitivity analysis. GRADE: very low.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Very-low GRADE certainty, high heterogeneity, fragile subgroups; only one subgroup (long-term air blast) favoured lasers.
- The abstract's study flow reads "4,480 of 4,455 studies were excluded", which is arithmetically impossible (exclusions exceeding records); the true screening counts were not verified without full text.
- Authors call for standardized methods and cost-effective laser applications.

## 6. Related Work
- Complements laser-only SR+MAs (Faraoni 2023; Shan 2021) and the long-term agent SR+MA (Corrêa 2025).

## 7. Glossary
- Air blast: evaporative stimulus (dental-unit air) used to assess DH.
- Tactile: probe-stroke stimulus used to assess DH.
- GRADE: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluation.
