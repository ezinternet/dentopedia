## Why Ingested

Convergent CBCT-based SR+MA evidence alongside [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/yildirim-2026-cbct-earr-clear-aligner-vs-fixed-sr-ma]], which found CA-vs-FA MD −0.50 mm on CBCT linear measurements; Samulak 2026 independently confirms the same direction of effect on anterior teeth (CA WMD −0.47 mm vs FA WMD −0.54 mm) in a distinct but overlapping evidence base, reinforcing the signal that CA produces less OIIRR than FA in the anterior region.

## One-line Summary

SR+MA of 4 high-quality CBCT cohort studies (1,204 anterior teeth) found clear aligner therapy associated with significantly less orthodontically induced inflammatory root resorption than fixed appliances (WMD −0.47 mm vs −0.54 mm; p<0.01), most pronounced in non-extraction maxillary central incisors.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to report OIIRR specifically for the **anterior tooth group** in a CA-vs-FA CBCT design, quantifying tooth-type-specific vulnerability.
- Pooled WMD for CA (−0.47 mm) and FA (−0.54 mm) from CBCT measurements allows a head-to-head comparison on the same imaging standard.
- Identifies **non-extraction maxillary central incisors** as the subgroup showing the most pronounced CA–FA differential — a clinically actionable subgroup finding.
- Conclusion supports using CA as the preferred technique for patients at elevated OIIRR risk (e.g., thin roots, prior resorption history, planned significant anterior movement).
- Absolute difference between CA and FA is modest (0.07 mm), tempering clinical over-interpretation while still confirming CA's statistical superiority.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (SR+MA)
- **Data source**: 4 high-quality cohort studies; CBCT-based linear root length measurement
- **Sample**: 1,204 anterior teeth
- **Intervention / Control**: Clear aligner therapy (CA) vs. fixed orthodontic appliances (FA)
- **Primary outcome**: Orthodontically induced inflammatory root resorption (OIIRR) measured by CBCT pre- and post-treatment
- **Statistical method**: Weighted mean difference (WMD) with 95% CI; significance threshold p<0.01
- **Subgroup analyses**: extraction vs. non-extraction; tooth type (maxillary central incisor highlighted); arch position
- **Limitations of methodology**: Only 4 studies included; cohort designs limit causal inference; CA and FA cases may differ systematically in case complexity

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **CA group OIIRR**: WMD −0.47 mm (95% CI −0.55 to −0.40)
- **FA group OIIRR**: WMD −0.54 mm (95% CI −0.75 to −0.33)
- **Between-group difference**: statistically significant (p<0.01) — CA causes significantly less anterior root resorption than FA
- **Absolute difference**: ~0.07 mm (modest in clinical terms)
- **Most affected tooth / subgroup**: Non-extraction maxillary central incisors show the most pronounced differential between CA and FA
- **Clinical conclusion**: CA may be preferable for patients at elevated OIIRR risk; the absolute difference is modest and unlikely to alter treatment decisions for average-risk patients

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 4 eligible studies limits statistical power and subgroup analyses
- Cohort designs (no RCTs): case-mix confounding (CA cases may be less complex, inherently lower OIIRR risk)
- Abstract-only ingest — detailed inclusion/exclusion criteria, full risk-of-bias table, and heterogeneity statistics (I²) are not available for this summary
- Future work: RCTs matched for case complexity; longer follow-up to assess permanence; root morphology (dilaceration, pipette root) as subgroup moderator

## 6. Related Work

- **Butsabul 2024** (DOI 10.1016/j.identj.2024.04.008): SR+MA of CA-only CBCT data — overall incisor EARR −0.56 mm; maxillary central incisor worst at −0.74 mm; provides absolute baseline without FA comparator.
- **Yıldırım 2026** (DOI 10.3390/healthcare14111547): CBCT-exclusive CA-vs-FA SR+MA (6 studies, 392 patients) — pooled MD −0.50 mm (95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001) favoring CA; independent convergent evidence.
- **Bespalez-Neto 2026** (DOI 10.1111/ocr.70082): RCT with AI-based 3D CBCT volumetric EARR — no significant CA-vs-FA group difference overall (−0.72 mm); discordance with linear-measurement SR+MAs attributed to measurement modality (3D surface vs. linear) and smaller RCT sample.

## 7. Glossary

- **OIIRR**: Orthodontically Induced Inflammatory Root Resorption — apical resorption driven by orthodontic force-related PDL inflammation; synonymous with EARR in this context.
- **EARR**: External Apical Root Resorption — radiographically detectable shortening of the root apex; OIIRR is the mechanistic subtype.
- **WMD**: Weighted Mean Difference — pooled difference in mean root length change (mm), CA minus baseline and FA minus baseline, weighted by study precision.
- **CBCT**: Cone-Beam Computed Tomography — 3D imaging modality providing linear root-length measurements superior to 2D radiography for EARR quantification.
