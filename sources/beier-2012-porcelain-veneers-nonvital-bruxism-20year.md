## Why Ingested

20년 후향적 데이터로 비생활치(Nonvital tooth, ETT) 라미네이트 실패 위험을 정량화한 가장 긴 관찰 기간 연구. 기존 위키 [[veneers/gresnigt-2019-ceramic-laminate-veneers-ids-11year]] 및 [[veneers/etienne-2025-ceramic-veneers-dentin-vitality-15year]]와 상반되는 결과(ETT 유의한 실패 위험 증가, P=0.0012)를 보여 — 진단 시 결과 불일치의 원인(재료·프로토콜 차이)을 이해하는 근거로 인제스트.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective clinical study (Int J Prosthodont 2012; 84 patients, 318 silicate glass-ceramic porcelain laminate veneers, Innsbruck Medical University, treatment period 1987–2009, mean observation 118 ± 63 months) using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and Cox proportional hazards regression.

Estimated survival at 5/10/20 years: 94.4%/93.5%/82.93%; ceramic fracture was the dominant failure mode (44.83% of 29 failures); nonvital (ETT) teeth had significantly higher failure risk (p=0.0012), bruxism carried a 7.7× greater failure risk (p=0.0004), and smokers showed significantly more marginal discoloration (p<0.01).

The ETT failure finding contradicts more recent studies using modern IDS adhesive protocols (Gresnigt 2019, Etienne 2025), suggesting era-specific material and technique differences explain the divergence; bruxism remains the strongest identifiable patient-level risk factor across all eras.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective study (n=318 silicate glass-ceramic PLVs, 84 patients, up to 20 years) found estimated survival of 94.4%/93.5%/82.93% at 5/10/20 years; nonvital teeth significantly higher failure risk (p=0.0012) and bruxism 7.7× greater failure risk (p=0.0004).

## 한줄요약

후향적 연구 (318개 장석계 PLV, 84명, 최장 20년): 5/10/20년 생존율 94.4%/93.5%/82.93%; 비생활치 실패 위험 유의 증가(P=0.0012), 이갈이 7.7배 위험(P=0.0004), 흡연자 변연 변색 유의 증가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Longest available follow-up for porcelain laminate veneers (~20 years, treatment period 1987–2009)
- Directly identified nonvital (ETT) as a statistically significant risk factor for LV failure (P=0.0012) — contradicted by more recent studies using improved adhesive protocols
- Quantified bruxism risk: 7.7× greater failure risk (P=0.0004) — highest impact modifiable risk factor
- Showed 20-year survival drops to 82.93%, with main failure mode being ceramic fracture (44.83%)
- Demonstrated smoking increases marginal discoloration significantly (P<0.01)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design**: Retrospective clinical study
- **Period**: 1987–2009 (Innsbruck Medical University)
- **Population**: 84 patients (38 men, 46 women)
- **Sample**: 318 porcelain veneer restorations (anterior teeth, maxilla + mandible)
- **Material**: Silicate glass-ceramic (Empress/conventional feldspathic era)
- **Evaluation criteria**: Modified California Dental Association / Ryge criteria
- **Variables assessed**: Esthetic match, porcelain surface, marginal discoloration, integrity
- **Special populations**:
  - Bruxism: 42 patients (50%)
  - Smokers: 23 patients (27.38%)
  - Nonvital teeth: 15 veneers (included but not fully separated in subgroup data)
- **Mean observation time**: 118 ± 63 months (~9.8 years)
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier survival analysis; Cox regression for risk factors

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Total failures**: 29 (absolute survival 82.76%; failure rate 17.24%)
- **Main failure mode**: Ceramic fracture (44.83% of failures)
- **Estimated survival rates**:
  - 5 years: 94.4%
  - 10 years: 93.5%
  - 20 years: 82.93%
- **Nonvital teeth**: Significantly higher failure risk (p=0.0012)
- **Bruxism**: 7.7× greater risk of failure (p=0.0004)
- **Smokers**: Significantly more marginal discoloration (p<0.01)
- **Mean observation**: 118 ± 63 months

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Older material and adhesive protocols (1987–2009 era) — may not reflect modern LV performance
- Silicate glass-ceramic differs from modern lithium disilicate in mechanical properties
- Nonvital teeth subgroup not fully isolated with sample size data
- No IDS protocol or modern dentin management techniques applied
- Single-institution retrospective design
