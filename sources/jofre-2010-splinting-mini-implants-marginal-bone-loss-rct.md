## One-line Summary
RCT + FEA (n=45, 90 mini-implants, 24 months): splinted mini-implants (bar) showed less marginal bone loss (0.92 mm) than unsplinted (ball, 1.43 mm) and significantly lower rate of vertical bone resorption.

## 한줄요약
RCT+FEA(n=45, 미니임플란트 90개, 24개월): 바 스플린팅군(0.92mm) < 볼 어태치먼트 비스플린팅군(1.43mm) 변연골 소실; 수직 골흡수 형태도 스플린팅군이 유의하게 적음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: International Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Implants, 2010;25(6):1137–1144
- **PMID**: 21197490
- **Institution**: Universidad de Concepción, Chile (public health center patients)
- **Funding**: Not disclosed
- **Follow-up**: 5, 10, 15, and 24 months after placement

## 2. Key Contributions
- Combined FEA + RCT design addresses both mechanism and clinical outcome
- FEA shows splinted implants reduce peak bone stress from -118 MPa to -56.8 MPa (52% reduction)
- Clinical RCT confirms FEA prediction: splinting reduces marginal bone loss
- Vertical bone resorption morphology significantly more prevalent in unsplinted group (chi-square)
- Provides biomechanical rationale for bar splinting in mini-implant overdentures

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: RCT (two-arm parallel) + Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
- **Patients**: 45 completely edentulous patients (public health center)
- **Groups**:
  - Group-ball (n=22, 44 implants): two single ball-type mini-implants (unsplinted)
  - Group-bar (n=23, 46 implants): two mini-implants splinted with prefabricated bar
- **Placement**: Flapless, anterior mandible; immediate loading
- **Radiographic assessment**: Standardized retroalveolar radiographs at 5, 10, 15, 24 months
- **FEA**: Mathematical model comparing bone stress around splinted vs. unsplinted mini-implants

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **FEA bone stress**:
  - Unsplinted: -118 MPa (minimum principal stress)
  - Splinted: -56.8 MPa (52% lower)
- **Clinical MBL at 24 months**:
  - Group-ball: 1.43 ± 1.26 mm
  - Group-bar: 0.92 ± 0.75 mm (trend toward lower, not statistically significant in continuous measure)
- **Vertical bone resorption prevalence**: Group-ball significantly higher (chi-square, two-tailed, p<0.05)
- All implants immediately loaded; no implant failures reported in abstract data

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-level data only (full text limited in PDF — PubMed page)
- Small sample size per group
- Chilean public health population — specific socioeconomic context
- Mini-implants only (not standard diameter) — limited generalizability
- Follow-up 24 months — longer-term data needed

## 7. Glossary
- **Mini-implant**: Small-diameter implant (typically <3 mm) used for overdenture retention
- **Ball attachment**: Single-unit attachment (unsplinted) for overdenture retention
- **Bar splinting**: Rigid connection bar between implants distributing occlusal load
- **FEA**: Finite Element Analysis — computational stress modeling
- **Vertical bone resorption**: Bone loss in vertical direction around implant (angular pattern)
