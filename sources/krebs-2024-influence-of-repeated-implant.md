## Why Ingested

First study to apply established case definitions (BOP + progressive radiographic bone loss) to the question of whether repeated abutment manipulation changes the **prevalence of peri-implant diseases**, not just marginal bone level — the outcome gap explicitly left open by [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]] and [[implants/peri-implantitis/lin-2025-influence-of-prosthetic-designs]]. Its unusually large patient-level difference (5.6% vs 88.9% peri-implantitis) directly tests the direction those MBL-based reviews infer indirectly.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective analysis of 27 edentulous-mandible patients (108 immediately loaded Morse-taper implants, 3–15 years; mean 10.2 ± 2.8) comparing one-abutment-one-time (OAOT, n=72 implants) with abutment replacement (AR, n=36 implants) under complete-arch double-crown prostheses.

Peri-implantitis prevalence at patient/implant level was 5.6%/5.6% in OAOT versus 88.9%/55.6% in AR; each additional abutment replacement raised the odds of mucositis/peri-implantitis (OR 6.13; 95% CI 2.61–14.39; p<0.001), and estimated mean radiographic bone loss was 1.38 mm larger in AR (p=0.0190).

First direct prevalence evidence favouring OAOT, but a retrospective, small, single-system cohort in which the case definition post-dated ethical approval — the magnitude of the effect should not be read as a causal estimate.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clin Implant Dent Relat Res. 2024;26:1260–1269
- **DOI**: 10.1111/cid.13381
- **Institution**: Department of Oral Surgery and Implantology, Goethe University, Frankfurt, Germany
- **Study type**: Retrospective analysis (STROBE)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First report of peri-implant **mucositis and peri-implantitis prevalence** stratified by one-abutment-one-time vs repeated abutment replacement, using defined case criteria rather than bone level alone.
- Dose–response signal: number of abutment replacements (not merely presence) predicted diagnosis (OR 6.13; 95% CI 2.61–14.39; p<0.001).
- Long mean observation window for this question (10.2 ± 2.8 years; range 3–15).
- Keratinized mucosa (KM) category was **not** a significant co-founder/confounder in the mixed model — contrary to the usual peri-implant risk hierarchy.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, 27 edentulous mandibles (16 female, 11 male; mean age 74.2 ± 9.3), treated 1998–2015.
- **Implants**: 4 interforaminal subcrestal Ankylos® Morse-taper implants per patient (n=108); OAOT restored with SynCone prefabricated conical abutments + double-crown resin prosthesis; AR converted to lab-made zirconia primary crowns with electroformed gold secondary crowns, requiring implant-level impressions and several abutment replacements.
- **Case definitions**: peri-implantitis = BOP + progressive radiographic bone loss >1.50 mm (twice measurement error of calibrated panoramic radiographs); mucositis = BOP without radiographic bone loss; health = no inflammation signs.
- **Statistics**: Mixed-effects model regression for diagnosis (healthy / mucositis / peri-implantitis); predictors = abutment replacement (yes/no), number of replacements, KM category, radiographic bone loss.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective, single-centre, very small sample (27 patients; 9 in control arm) — wide implicit uncertainty despite significant p-values.
- Single implant system (Ankylos Morse taper) and one prosthetic concept (SynCone double crown); generalisability to other connections/retentions unproven.
- 2018 case definition published after ethical approval; earlier records were re-interpreted against it.
- KM was not significant, plausibly from low power rather than absence of effect.
- Groups differed in prosthetic material and laboratory workflow, not only in abutment manipulation — residual confounding by reconstruction type.

## 7. Glossary
- **OAOT (One-Abutment-One-Time)**: definitive abutment connected once and never removed during prosthetic procedures.
- **AR (Abutment Replacement)**: conventional protocol with repeated abutment disconnection/reconnection for impressions, try-in and delivery.
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)**: bleeding evident within 30 s after probing.
- **KM (Keratinized Mucosa)**: mid-vestibular keratinized tissue width at the implant site.
- **BL (Bone Loss)**: radiographic bone loss measured on panoramic images.
