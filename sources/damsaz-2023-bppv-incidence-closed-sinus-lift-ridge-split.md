## Why Ingested

양성돌발성두위현훈 (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo, BPPV)-폐쇄형 상악동거상술 합병증 관련 문헌을 목적적으로 스윕(sweep)하던 중 요청되어 인제스트. 본 연구는 폐쇄형 상악동거상술(closed sinus lift, 3.1%)과 치조제 분할술(ridge splitting, 0%)의 BPPV 발생률을 직접 비교한 최초의 코호트 데이터를 제공한다. 기존 [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/stacchi-2022-transcrestal-sinus-complications-low-rbh]]가 경치조골 상악동거상 후 BPPV 발생률 0.5%를 보고한 바 있는데, 본 연구는 대조군(치조제 분할술)을 둔 통제된 비교로 그 합병증 데이터를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort study (n=112 patients, 51 women/61 men, mean age 48.4±9.5y) at Mashhad Dental School comparing BPPV incidence after closed (transcrestal) sinus lift vs ridge splitting surgery, diagnosed via Dix-Hallpike maneuver pre/post-op.

Overall BPPV incidence was 1.8%; 2 patients (3.1%) in the closed sinus lift group developed BPPV vs 0 patients (0%) in the ridge split group, though the difference was not statistically significant (p=0.509).

Closed sinus lift may carry a higher BPPV risk than ridge splitting, but the study is underpowered (n=112, only 2 events) — larger studies are needed to confirm the association; abstract-only — full text not retrieved.

## 세줄요약

마쉬하드 치과대학(Mashhad Dental School)에서 시행된 전향적 코호트 연구(n=112명, 여51/남61, 평균연령 48.4±9.5세)로, 폐쇄형(경치조골) 상악동거상술과 치조제 분할술(ridge splitting) 후 딕스-홀파이크 검사(Dix-Hallpike maneuver)로 진단한 양성돌발성두위현훈(BPPV) 발생률을 비교.

전체 BPPV 발생률 1.8%; 폐쇄형 상악동거상술군 2명(3.1%) BPPV 발생 vs 치조제 분할술군 0명(0%)이었으나 통계적으로 유의하지 않음(p=0.509).

폐쇄형 상악동거상술이 치조제 분할술보다 BPPV 위험이 더 높을 가능성이 시사되나 표본(n=112, 사건 2건)이 작아 검정력 부족 — 대규모 후속 연구 필요; 초록만 확보(abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct incidence comparison of BPPV between closed sinus lift and ridge splitting surgery using a standardized diagnostic protocol (Dix-Hallpike maneuver, pre- and post-operative).
- Reports overall symptom prevalence spectrum (hypertension 10.7%, headache 36.6%, dizziness 27.7%, nausea 12.5%, BPPV 1.8%) in a posterior maxillary augmentation cohort.
- Notes a significant association between certain (unspecified in abstract) health conditions and BPPV onset, suggesting patient-level risk stratification may matter more than technique alone.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective cohort study, single surgeon, single center.
- **Population**: 112 healthy patients requiring posterior maxillary closed sinus lift or ridge split surgery, September 2021–September 2022.
- **n**: 112 patients (51 women, 61 men); mean age 48.4 ± 9.5y; mean BMI 21.5 ± 2.4; mean surgery duration 31.9 ± 6.6 min.
- **Outcomes**: Primary — BPPV occurrence (Dix-Hallpike maneuver pre/post-op). Secondary — hypertension, headache, dizziness, nausea occurrence.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 23, significance threshold p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Symptom prevalence across full cohort: hypertension 10.7%, headache 36.6%, dizziness 27.7%, nausea 12.5%, BPPV 1.8%.
- BPPV by group: closed sinus lift 2/patients (3.1% of that group) vs ridge split 0 patients (0%); between-group difference NOT significant (p=0.509).
- No statistically significant between-group difference for the other symptoms measured.
- A significant association was found between certain (unspecified) health conditions and BPPV onset.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only source — full methodological detail (exact BPPV case descriptions, which health conditions were associated, group sizes for sinus lift vs ridge split individually) not available; full text not retrieved via PMC.
- Small event count (2 BPPV cases total) severely limits statistical power for the group comparison (p=0.509 despite a 3.1% vs 0% raw difference).
- Single surgeon, single center — generalizability limited.
- Authors themselves note further studies with larger samples are needed.

## 6. Related Work
- stacchi-2022 (`sinus-lift/transcrestal/stacchi-2022-transcrestal-sinus-complications-low-rbh`): reports BPPV incidence 0.5% in a larger (n=430) multicenter transcrestal sinus lift cohort — this paper's 3.1% closed sinus lift BPPV rate is notably higher, though from a much smaller sample.

## 7. Glossary
- **BPPV (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo)**: transient vertigo triggered by head position change, caused by displaced otoconia in the semicircular canals; can be provoked by vibration/positional stress during osteotome-based sinus surgery.
- **Dix-Hallpike maneuver**: clinical positional test used to diagnose posterior/anterior canal BPPV.
- **Closed sinus lift**: transcrestal/osteotome approach to maxillary sinus floor elevation without a lateral window.
- **Ridge splitting**: surgical technique to widen a narrow alveolar ridge by splitting buccal and lingual cortical plates, used as the comparator (non-sinus, non-osteotome-percussion) procedure in this study.
