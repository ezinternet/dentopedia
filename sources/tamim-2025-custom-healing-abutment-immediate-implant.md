## Why Ingested

Custom healing abutments (CHAs) for immediate implant placement (IIP) remain a topic of interest for esthetic soft-tissue outcomes. This SR+MA was ingested to quantify the effect of CHAs vs stock healing abutments (SHAs) on pink esthetic score (PES) and midfacial mucosal recession (MMR), and to relate findings to existing [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/wu-2026-immediate-provisionalization-natural-crown-frc]] work on immediate esthetic soft-tissue management.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (6 RCTs, n=249 implants) comparing custom healing abutments (CHA) vs stock healing abutments (SHA) for immediate implant placement in the esthetic zone.
CHA significantly improved PES (MD 1.02 at 3-6 mo; MD 0.89 at 12 mo) and reduced midfacial mucosal recession (MD -0.21 mm at 3-6 mo; MD -0.32 mm at 12 mo) vs SHA, with no differences in peri-implant health parameters or implant survival.
Findings support CHA use in IIP for esthetic outcomes over 12 months, though evidence certainty is moderate and data are limited to RCTs.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립(IIP) 시 맞춤형 치유지대주(custom healing abutment, CHA)와 기성 치유지대주(stock healing abutment, SHA)를 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석(6건 RCT, 총 249개 임플란트).
CHA군이 SHA군 대비 분홍심미지수(PES)가 유의하게 높고(3-6개월 MD 1.02, 12개월 MD 0.89) 중안면 점막퇴축(midfacial mucosal recession)이 유의하게 적었으며(MD -0.21 mm at 3-6 mo, MD -0.32 mm at 12 mo), 주위조직 건강 지표나 임플란트 생존율에는 차이가 없었다.
즉시식립에서 CHA는 12개월까지 심미적 측면에서 유리한 결과를 보여주나, 근거의 확실성은 중등도이며 제한된 RCT 수에 근거한다.

## Key Contributions

- First/updated synthesis quantifying esthetic effect of custom vs stock healing abutments specifically in immediate implant placement.
- Demonstrates consistent improvement in PES at both early (3-6 mo) and mid-term (12 mo) timepoints.
- Demonstrates reduction in midfacial mucosal recession at both timepoints with moderate certainty (GRADE).
- Confirms no negative impact on KMW, PD, BOP, PI, or implant survival over 12 months.

## Methodology

- Databases searched: PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library (up to May 2025)
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing CHAs vs SHAs in immediate implant placement with ≥3 months follow-up
- Primary outcome: Pink Esthetic Score (PES)
- Secondary outcomes: midfacial mucosal recession (MMR), keratinized mucosa width (KMW), probing depth (PD), bleeding on probing (BOP), plaque index (PI), implant survival
- Risk of bias: RoB2; certainty of evidence: GRADE
- Meta-analysis: 6 RCTs, 249 implants (124 CHA, 125 SHA)

## Key Results

- PES at 3-6 months: MD 1.02; 95% CI 0.58-1.45; P<.001; I²=0%
- PES at 12 months: MD 0.89; 95% CI 0.38-1.40; P=.001; I²=0%
- MMR at 3-6 months: MD -0.21 mm; 95% CI -0.37 to -0.05; P=.01; I²=0% (less recession with CHA)
- MMR at 12 months: MD -0.32 mm; 95% CI -0.52 to -0.12; P=.002; I²=0%
- No significant differences for KMW, PD, BOP, PI, or implant survival
- GRADE certainty moderate for PES and MMR

## Limitations

- abstract-only — full text not retrieved; detailed methodological nuances (CHA fabrication protocols, timing, restoration type) not fully available from abstract.
- Only 6 RCTs, relatively small total sample (n=249 implants).
- Follow-up limited to 12 months; longer-term esthetic/stability data lacking.
- Heterogeneity in CHA design/protocols possible despite low I² in reported outcomes.
