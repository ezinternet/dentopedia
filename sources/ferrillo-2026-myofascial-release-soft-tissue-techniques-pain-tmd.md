## Why Ingested

Adds a 2026 MFR-specific, RoB 2 + GRADE-graded, MCID-benchmarked SR of RCTs to the TMD manual-therapy cluster already held ([[wiki/tmj/calixtre-2015-manual-therapy-tmd-pain-rom-sr]], [[wiki/tmj/demelo-2020-manual-therapy-myofascial-pain-tmd-sr]], [[wiki/tmj/menendez-torre-2023-dry-needling-vs-manual-therapy-tmd-nma]]); it qualifies those earlier "MFR works" conclusions by asking whether the pain effect is clinically meaningful, not just significant. Source: PubMed / PMC (DOI [10.1111/joor.70271](https://doi.org/10.1111/joor.70271)).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis; PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251161805, searched to 31 Jan 2026) of 7 RCTs (n=359, 2018-2025) testing myofascial release (MFR) and related soft-tissue techniques for pain in myogenous temporomandibular disorders (TMD).

Most trials reported statistically significant pain reductions (e.g., Serrano-Hernanz VAS 7.2 to 4.9 post-treatment and 4.4 at 3 months vs sham), but only a subset cleared the a priori MCID of 15-22 mm; MFR was not different from oral analgesics (Gurudut, NPRS p=0.066) or from PBM-combined arms (Brochado); RoB 2: 0 low, 6 some concerns, 1 high; GRADE low for pain and mouth function, very low for QoL.

MFR can be considered within a multidisciplinary TMD plan, but effects cannot be attributed to MFR alone because interventions were heterogeneous and often multimodal; low-certainty evidence and sub-MCID effects in several trials mean isolated-MFR RCTs are still needed.

## 세줄요약

근육형 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 통증에 대한 근막이완술 (Myofascial Release, MFR) 및 관련 연조직 기법의 무작위배정 임상시험 7편 (총 359명, PRISMA 2020, 2026년 1월 31일까지 검색) 체계적 문헌고찰이며 메타분석은 시행하지 않았다.

대부분의 시험에서 통계적으로 유의한 통증 감소가 있었으나 (예: Serrano-Hernanz 시각통증척도 7.2에서 치료 직후 4.9, 3개월 4.4, 가짜치료 대비 유의), 사전 설정한 최소 임상적 중요 차이 (Minimum Clinically Important Difference, MCID) 15-22 mm를 넘은 시험은 일부뿐이었고, 비뚤림 위험 (RoB 2)은 낮음 0편·일부 우려 6편·높음 1편, GRADE 확실성은 통증·개구 기능 낮음, 삶의 질 매우 낮음이었다.

MFR은 다학제 TMD 치료의 일부로 고려할 수 있으나 중재가 이질적이고 복합 처치가 많아 MFR 단독 효과로 귀속할 수 없으며, 근거 확실성이 낮고 일부 효과가 MCID 미만이어서 MFR 단독 시험이 더 필요하다.

## 1. Document Information

Ferrillo M et al., J Oral Rehabil 2026;53(10):1972-1984. Systematic review of RCTs (no meta-analysis). PMID 42458694, PMC13583377. Registered in PROSPERO (CRD420251161805) on 5 October 2025; registration was not fully prospective (screening had begun).

## 2. Key Contributions

- MFR-specific RCT-only SR with RoB 2 and GRADE, with intervention-type subgroups (direct MFR, instrument-assisted MFR, soft-tissue mobilization, multimodal including MFR).
- Explicit MCID benchmarking (15-22 mm on a 100 mm scale) of pain results, separating statistical from clinical significance.
- Cautions against attributing effects to MFR alone in multimodal protocols.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science from inception to 31 January 2026 plus reference/citation/registry searching; English-only RCTs in adults with myogenous TMD (RDC/TMD or DC/TMD). Exclusions: growing children, TMJ trauma, rheumatic/inflammatory disease, congenital/neoplastic lesions, cross-over designs. Two reviewers; RoB 2 (weighted kappa 0.84); GRADE; narrative synthesis (no pooling because of heterogeneous comparators, techniques, frequencies, and follow-up). 857 records, 421 screened after deduplication, 58 full texts, 7 included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 7 RCTs, n=359 (71 M, 288 F), mean ages 23-46 y; treatment 10 days to 5 weeks, 1-3 sessions/week, 10-45 min each; follow-up beyond end of treatment in 4 studies.
- Brochado 2018 (n=51; MT, PBM, MT+PBM): all groups improved by day 14 (MT 4.4 to 1.7; PBM 4.1 to 1.9; MT+PBM 5.2 to 2.4; p<0.001), no between-group difference, stable to day 90.
- Leite 2020 (n=34 women): diacutaneous fibrolysis better than sham on VAS at 4 weeks (between-group p reported as 0.02 in text and <0.01 in table).
- Serrano-Hernanz 2023 (n=72): pressure release technique VAS 7.2 to 4.9 (post) to 4.4 (3 months) vs sham (p<0.001); sham reduction below the 1.2 cm a priori threshold.
- Romeo 2024 (n=62): physiotherapy plus splint plus education vs splint plus education; odds of clinically important improvement OR 3.06 (VAS opening, p=0.04) and 6.89 (VAS chewing, p<0.01).
- Espejo-Antunez 2024 (n=46 women): soft-tissue mobilization plus electro-massage beat mobilization alone; between-group difference -1.59 cm post-treatment and -1.90 cm at 4 weeks.
- Ferreira 2024 (n=60): dry needling and manual therapy both reduced VAS over time, control (CBT) did not; rated high risk of bias.
- Gurudut 2025 (n=34): MFR/massage vs chlorzoxazone plus aceclofenac for 7 days; NPRS between-group p=0.066 (not significant).
- RoB 2: 0 low, 6 some concerns, 1 high. GRADE: low (pain, mouth function), very low (QoL).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Authors cite: registration not fully prospective, few studies, risk-of-bias concerns in more than half, heterogeneity of modalities and follow-up, no cost-effectiveness analysis, no meta-analysis, no DC/TMD Axis II stratification, QoL inconsistently measured. Source inconsistencies noted on reading: the Leite row reports the same age (44.5 +- 17.1 y) as the Brochado/Ferreira rows; Ferreira group sizes in Table 2 (20+20+control 30) do not sum to n=60; the Leite VAS figures (3.9 [0.7-8.2] vs 0.6 [0-2.3]) are printed as in the paper without a clear arm label; the text calls Romeo and Ferreira "clinically meaningful" without listing their between-group mm differences.

## 7. Glossary

- MFR (myofascial release): slow, sustained low-load manual pressure to restricted fascia (direct or indirect).
- MCID: minimum clinically important difference (here 15-22 mm VAS).
- PBM: photobiomodulation. DF: diacutaneous fibrolysis. PPT: pressure pain threshold.
