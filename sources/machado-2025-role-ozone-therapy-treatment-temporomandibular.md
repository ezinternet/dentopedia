## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (6 databases, searched July 2021, updated November 2023; 315 records, 7 clinical trials included, no meta-analysis) of ozone therapy for articular and muscular temporomandibular disorders (TMD).

Ozone was reported to reduce pain intensity and palpatory pain and to improve pressure pain thresholds and mandibular range of motion; risk of bias (RoB 2) was low in 2 trials, some concerns in 2, and high in 3.

Ozone is a potential adjunct, but the evidence lacks robustness and needs further high-quality randomized controlled trials.

## 세줄요약

관절성·근육성 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 오존 치료를 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰 (6개 DB, 2021년 7월 검색·2023년 11월 갱신, 315편 중 임상시험 7편 포함, 메타분석 없음).

오존은 통증 강도·촉진통 감소, 압통역치 (Pressure Pain Threshold, PPT) 및 하악 운동범위 (Range of Motion, ROM) 개선을 보였고, 비뚤림위험 (RoB 2)은 저위험 2편, 일부 우려 2편, 고위험 3편이었음.

오존은 TMD 증상 완화의 잠재적 보조요법이나 근거가 탄탄하지 않아 고품질 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이 더 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Dedicated systematic review of ozone therapy for TMD with RoB 2 appraisal.
- Covers both articular and muscular TMD.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Six databases searched July 15 2021, updated November 17 2023; Portuguese, English, Spanish; no year limit.
- Included clinical trials only; excluded animal, retrospective, case reports, reviews, meta-analyses, and ozone use outside the orofacial region.
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 315 articles identified; 7 studies included.
- RoB 2: 2 low risk, 2 some concerns, 3 high risk.
- Reported reductions in pain intensity and palpatory pain; improved pressure pain thresholds and mandibular range of motion.
- Numeric effect sizes not available in the abstract; no pooled estimate.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 7 trials, 3 at high risk of bias; evidence "lacks robustness".
- Abstract-only ingestion; ozone dose, route, comparators, and follow-up not retrieved.
- Authors call for further high-quality RCTs.
