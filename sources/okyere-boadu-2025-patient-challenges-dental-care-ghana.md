## Why Ingested
비보험 치료 상담 문헌 보강을 위해 인제스트. 환자의 비용 장벽과 커뮤니케이션 불만족이 치료 미수락의 핵심 경로임을 실증한 유일한 혼합방법론 치과 연구. 기존 [[behavioral-dentistry/communication-relationship/ho-2025-overview-dentist-patient-communication-quality]] 커뮤니케이션 축에 환자 관점의 현장 근거로 추가.

## One-line Summary
Mixed-methods study (n=130, Ghana): 55.4% of dental patients encountered barriers — cost and appointment availability ranked 100%; during visits, anxiety (77%), long waits (63.2%), high cost (60.9%), and inadequate communication (40.2%) were the top challenges.

## 한줄요약
혼합방법론(n=130, 가나): 55.4%가 치과 이용 장벽 경험 — 비용·예약 가용성이 100% 인용; 진료 중 장벽은 불안(77%)·대기(63.2%)·고비용(60.9%)·불충분한 커뮤니케이션(40.2%) 순.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: PLoS ONE, Volume 20, Issue 6, e0325136 (2025 Jun 2)
- Setting: Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital (KATH), Oral Health Directorate, Kumasi, Ghana
- Funding/COI: Not reported; ethical clearance CHRPE/AP/965/23 (KNUST School of Medicine and Dentistry)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First mixed-methods study documenting dental care barriers among Ghanaian hospital outpatients
- Quantifies communication gap as a discrete barrier (40.2% inadequate communication during visits; 20.7% lack of transparency about treatment/costs)
- Shows cascading harm: cost barrier → care delay → worsened disease → higher treatment cost (vicious cycle documented qualitatively)
- Identifies education (p=0.009), marital status (p=0.012), and gender (p=0.00) as demographic predictors of treatment-seeking behavior
- Patient-generated recommendations include jargon-free explanations and credit/payment plan options — directly applicable to consultation design

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Mixed methods — cross-sectional questionnaire (quantitative) + semi-structured interviews (qualitative), thematic analysis
- Setting: KATH OPD Oral Health, October 30–November 10, 2023
- n=130 (137 calculated; 94.9% response rate); systematic sampling (every 5th patient), 14/day over 10 days
- Inclusion: patients with ≥1 prior dental visit (not first-time); exclusion: critical/unconscious
- Cronbach's α=0.80 (questionnaire internal consistency); qualitative saturation reached; composite study method for anonymized qualitative vignettes
- Statistics: descriptive, Chi-square associations; SPSS IBM v23

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Pre-visit barriers (among 55.4% who encountered any barrier):
| Barrier | Prevalence |
|---|---|
| Cost | 100% |
| Appointment availability | 100% |
| Insurance coverage | 72.2% |
| Anxiety/fear | 63.9% |
| Accessibility | 63.9% |
| Past experiences | 41.7% |
| Mobility | 36.1% |

### In-visit challenges (n=130, all patients):
| Challenge | Prevalence |
|---|---|
| Dental anxiety | 77.0% |
| Long waits + privacy issues | 63.2% |
| Limited appointment + high cost | 60.9% |
| Discomfort during procedures | 41.4% |
| Inadequate communication | 40.2% |
| Lack of transparency (treatment/costs) | 20.7% |

- 43.1% more likely to avoid/delay care; 23.6% less likely; 20.8% sometimes; 12.5% unaffected
- Education positively associated with treatment-seeking behavior (p=0.009)
- Marital status: married more likely to postpone (p=0.012)
- Gender: p=0.00 (men less likely to seek preventive care)

### Qualitative themes (patient-experienced cascades):
1. Cost barrier → delay → worsened disease → more expensive treatment
2. Transparency gap: unclear treatment plans/costs → unmet expectations → dissatisfaction
3. Communication gap: jargon, dismissiveness, multiple observers → reduced trust, avoidance of follow-up procedures

### Patient-suggested interventions:
- Jargon-free explanations; transparent cost communication before treatment
- Payment plans, subsidies, credit; student clinic lower-cost options; dental savings plans
- Tele-dentistry; same-day/online appointment systems
- Preventive care education; anxiety reduction techniques; privacy during procedures

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-center (KATH, tertiary referral hospital) — may not represent community dental practice
- Did not capture healthcare practitioner perspectives
- Small n=130 limits regression power
- Cross-sectional design: cannot establish causality for barrier → delay → outcome chain
- Systematic sampling may miss patients who gave up and left queue

## 6. Related Work
- Global trend: financial and systemic barriers disproportionately affect low-income populations (multi-country literature)
- Dental anxiety as both driver and result of negative experiences (feedback loop supported by literature)
- Communication gaps compound cost dissatisfaction
- This study situates Ghanaian oral health in the global access framework

## 7. Glossary
- **KATH**: Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital, largest referral hospital in Ashanti/Bono regions, Ghana
- **Composite study**: qualitative technique combining excerpts from multiple participants sharing similar experiences into anonymized vignettes
- **CHRPE**: Committee on Human Research, Publications, and Ethics (KNUST)
