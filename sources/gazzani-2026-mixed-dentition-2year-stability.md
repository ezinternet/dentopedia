## Why Ingested

Only available 2-year post-treatment stability study for Invisalign First (mixed dentition, molars-move-first) WITHOUT retention; directly fills surveillance target "retention/relapse/stability 2023–2026." Novel finding: transversal expansion stable at 2 years without retention appliance. → [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/gazzani-2026-mixed-dentition-2year-stability]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (n=20, mean age 8.3y, Invisalign First) measuring dental arch changes at baseline, end of treatment, and 2-year follow-up without any retention appliance. Significant transversal expansion (U6-6 +2.1mm, L6-6 +2.4mm) and molar derotation were maintained; only lower left molar torque showed significant relapse (-1.1°). Alignment stability (Little's Index) was maintained at 0.4mm at 2-year follow-up.

## 세줄요약

n=20(평균 8.3세) Invisalign First 후향적 연구, 치료 종료~유지장치 없이 2년 추적. U6-6 측방 확장 +2.1mm, L6-6 +2.4mm 유의한 회귀 없이 유지. 하악 좌측 제1대구치 토크만 -1.1° 유의한 재발; Little 지수 0.4mm(치료 직후)→0.4mm(2년 후)로 안정.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Retrospective cohort (no control group)
- **Journal**: Children (Basel) 13(2):298 (2026), DOI 10.3390/children13020298
- **PMID/PMCID**: 41749653 / PMC12939305
- **Sample**: n=20 (12F, 8M), mean age 8.3±0.4y, early mixed dentition, treated Jan 2021–Dec 2022, follow-up until T2 (24mo post-treatment); Saint Camillus International University, Italy

## 2. Key Findings

- T1-T0 (end of treatment): U6-6 mesial +2.1mm, L6-6 mesial +2.4mm (both p<0.05); Henry's angle improvement (molar derotation) both sides; Little's 7.5→0.4mm
- T2-T1 (2-year post-treatment): all changes non-significant EXCEPT LL-6 torque -1.1° (p<0.05)
- T2-T0 (net 2-year effect): expansion maintained; derotation maintained; alignment maintained (Little's 0.4mm)
- No retention appliance used post-treatment
- Treatment: Invisalign First, "molars move first" protocol, 4-6mm planned expansion, 0.25mm/stage, 7-day intervals, ~60 aligners/arch

## 3. Methodology

Retrospective; initial pool 55 patients → 20 after exclusions. Digital scans (iTero); Viewbox 4.0 software measurements. Method error: Kendall's W >0.95. Friedman ANOVA + Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. No control group. T0-T1 interval: 17±1.8 months; T1-T2: 24±1.4 months.

## 4. Clinical Implications

Invisalign First with the molars-move-first protocol achieves durable expansion and alignment in mixed dentition that appears stable at 2 years even without formal retention. However, n=20 and retrospective design warrant caution; clinician discretion on retention remains appropriate.

## 5. Limitations

Small sample (n=20); retrospective; no control; physiological growth may have contributed to some stability; single setting; no patient-reported outcomes; 2-year follow-up may not capture adolescent growth spurt relapse.

## 7. Glossary

- **Invisalign First**: Align Technology's clear aligner system for children in mixed dentition
- **Little's Irregularity Index**: sum of contact point displacements in mm; <1mm = excellent alignment
- **Henry's angle**: radiographic angle measuring molar rotation/derotation
- **Molars move first**: expansion sequencing protocol — posterior expansion precedes anterior segment
