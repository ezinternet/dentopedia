Alongside the reversal narrative reviews already held ([[drug/anticoagulants/yee-2019-emergency-reversal-of-anticoagulation]], [[drug/anticoagulants/aldhaeefi-2023-practical-guide-for-anticoagulant-and]]), it is the RCT-only counterweight to the observational PCC meta-analyses, and it covers both VKA and factor Xa reversal — so it also tests the DOAC reversal mapping the wiki took from [[drug/anticoagulants/choudhary-2025-bleeding-thrombosis-noac-dental-management]]. → [[drug/anticoagulants/brekelmans-2017-benefits-and-harms-of-4]]

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of RCTs only (5 trials, 825 participants; GRADE + Trial Sequential Analysis) of prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC) in VKA- or factor Xa-related critical bleeding.
VKA: PCC vs FFP showed no difference in mortality (RR 1.05, 0.27–4.05) or serious adverse events (RR 1.33, 0.94–1.88), but PCC conclusively reduced tardy INR correction (RR 0.41, 0.32–0.52); factor Xa: PCC had poorer haemostatic efficacy than andexanet alfa (RR 1.46, 1.15–1.85) with a borderline lower thromboembolism signal (RR 0.55, 0.30–1.00).
PCC corrects INR better than FFP but RCTs cannot yet show it improves patient outcomes; guidelines still uniformly recommend PCC first-line for life-threatening VKA bleeding.

## 세줄요약

VKA 또는 Xa 인자 억제제 관련 치명적 출혈에서 프로트롬빈 복합체 농축물 (Prothrombin Complex Concentrate, PCC) 을 본 RCT 한정 체계적 고찰 (5개 시험, 825명; GRADE + 시험순차분석 (Trial Sequential Analysis, TSA)).
VKA: PCC vs 신선동결혈장 (Fresh Frozen Plasma, FFP) 사망 (RR 1.05, 0.27–4.05)·중대 이상반응 (RR 1.33) 차이 없음, 그러나 지연된 국제정상화비율 (International Normalized Ratio, INR) 교정은 PCC가 확정적으로 감소 (RR 0.41, 0.32–0.52); Xa 억제제: PCC가 안덱사넷 알파 (Andexanet Alfa) 보다 지혈 효과 열세 (RR 1.46), 혈전색전은 경계선상 적음 (RR 0.55, 0.30–1.00).
PCC는 FFP보다 INR을 잘 교정하지만 환자 예후 개선은 RCT로 아직 입증되지 않았다 — 그럼에도 가이드라인은 생명을 위협하는 VKA 출혈의 1차 치료로 PCC를 일관되게 권고한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Restricts evidence to randomised trials (and requested clinical study reports from regulators).
- Separates INR correction (a laboratory surrogate) from patient-relevant outcomes and shows they diverge.
- Covers factor Xa reversal: PCC vs FFP and PCC vs andexanet alfa.
- Summarizes guideline consensus: PCC first-line for VKA critical bleeding, FFP second-line; 4F-PCC or activated PCC for DOAC bleeding if specific antidotes are unavailable; no RCT compares idarucizumab with PCC.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review of RCTs with meta-analysis, Trial Sequential Analysis, Bayes factors, GRADE
- **Databases**: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, LILACS, BIOSIS, Web of Science, trial registries; clinical study reports from competent authorities
- **n**: 12,670 records → 27 publications → 5 RCTs, 825 participants (VKA: 3 trials, 291; factor Xa: 2 trials, 534)
- **Primary outcomes**: all-cause mortality, health-related quality of life, serious adverse events
- **Secondary/exploratory**: thromboembolism, functional outcome, tardy INR correction, haemostatic efficacy, RBC transfusion
- **Risk of bias**: all trials open-label, all judged high risk of bias

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Comparison | Outcome | Result |
|---|---|---|
| VKA: PCC vs FFP (2 RCTs) | All-cause mortality | RR 1.05 (0.27–4.05), I²=72.4% |
| | Serious adverse events | RR 1.33 (0.94–1.88) |
| | Thromboembolic events | RR 1.60 (0.71–3.61) |
| | Tardy INR correction | **RR 0.41 (0.32–0.52)**, I²=0% — TSA superiority boundary crossed |
| | Poor haemostatic efficacy | RR 0.68 (0.44–1.06) |
| | RBC transfusion (1 RCT) | RR 1.08 (0.81–1.45) |
| VKA: PCC+FFP vs FFP (1 RCT) | Mortality | RR 0.65 (0.16–2.59) |
| Factor Xa: PCC vs FFP (1 RCT) | Mortality | RR 0.79 (0.20–3.09) |
| Factor Xa: PCC vs andexanet (1 RCT) | Poor haemostatic efficacy | **RR 1.46 (1.15–1.85)** — PCC worse |
| | Thromboembolism (usual care vs andexanet) | RR 0.55 (0.30–1.00), borderline |

- Certainty of evidence: low or very low for all patient-relevant outcomes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Few, small, open-label trials; information size insufficient for any patient-relevant outcome.
- Andexanet comparison is indirect (86% of usual care received PCC).
- Critical (mostly intracranial) bleeding only; no dental or minor-bleeding data.
- Authors warn the evidence gap should not "inspire therapeutic nihilism".

## 7. Glossary

- **Trial Sequential Analysis (TSA)**: cumulative meta-analysis method that tests whether enough information exists to confirm or reject an effect.
- **Tardy INR correction**: failure to reach target INR within the trial's time window.
- **Andexanet alfa**: recombinant decoy factor Xa that binds factor Xa inhibitors.
- **Critical bleeding**: life-threatening bleeding (intracranial, major GI, etc.).
