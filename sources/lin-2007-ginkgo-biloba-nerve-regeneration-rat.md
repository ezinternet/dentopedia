## Why Ingested

Provides preclinical dose-response evidence for Ginkgo biloba extract (EGb) as a neuroprotective/neuroregenerative agent in peripheral nerve injury — directly relevant to IAN paresthesia adjunct pharmacology discussions. Links to and extends [[oral-surgery/nerve-injury/lee-2015-inferior-alveolar-nerve-injury-management]] by supplying dose-level detail missing from clinical reviews.

## Three-line Summary

Rat sciatic nerve transection-and-repair model (n=120 Sprague-Dawley rats, 4 groups) testing EGb at 0, 50, 100, and 200 mg/kg/day.

EGb significantly improved sensory regeneration distance, SFI, motor nerve conduction velocity, CMAP, axon regeneration index, and muscle mass vs saline; all parameters except SFI were best in the high-dose (200 mg/kg/d) group.

EGb promotes peripheral nerve regeneration in a dose-dependent manner, supporting its potential as an adjunct in IAN injury management, though translation from rat sciatic to human IAN requires further clinical study.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic dose-response characterization of EGb on peripheral nerve regeneration using a controlled 4-group rat sciatic nerve transection model
- Demonstrated that EGb's neuroregenerative effect is dose-dependent across the tested range (50–200 mg/kg/d)
- Multi-parameter functional, electrophysiological, and histological outcome assessment provides a comprehensive efficacy profile

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Animal**: 120 Sprague-Dawley rats, sciatic nerve transection and epineural repair
- **Groups (n=30 each)**:
  - Group 1: Normal saline (control)
  - Group 2: EGb 50 mg/kg/day (low dose)
  - Group 3: EGb 100 mg/kg/day (moderate dose)
  - Group 4: EGb 200 mg/kg/day (high dose)
- **Outcome measures** at multiple postoperative intervals:
  - Sensory regeneration distance (pin-prick test)
  - Sciatic functional index (SFI) — walking-track analysis
  - Motor nerve conduction velocity (MNCV)
  - Compound muscle action potential (CMAP) amplitude
  - Axon regeneration index (histology)
  - Muscle mass (gastrocnemius weight)
- **EGb preparation**: Ginkgo biloba extract (standardized — EGb 761 formulation implied; 24% flavonoid glycosides, 6% terpene lactones)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Saline | EGb 50 | EGb 100 | EGb 200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All neuroregeneration metrics | Baseline | ↑ sig. | ↑↑ sig. | ↑↑↑ sig. |
| SFI | Lowest | Higher | Higher | Higher (≈ same as 100) |
| MNCV | Lowest | ↑ | ↑↑ | ↑↑↑ |
| CMAP | Lowest | ↑ | ↑↑ | ↑↑↑ |
| Axon regeneration index | Lowest | ↑ | ↑↑ | ↑↑↑ |
| Muscle mass | Lowest | ↑ | ↑↑ | ↑↑↑ |

- SFI was the only parameter that did NOT show a statistically significant dose-response gradient (all EGb doses improved SFI over saline, but 50 ≈ 100 ≈ 200)
- All other parameters: dose-response relationship confirmed (200 > 100 > 50 > saline)

**Mechanism (proposed)**: EGb's flavonoids and terpene lactones act via:
- Neuroprotection (anti-apoptotic, anti-oxidant)
- Schwann cell proliferation promotion (bilobalide component — refs 12–14)
- Anti-glutamate neurotoxicity
- Nitric oxide inhibition in macrophages
- Improved neuronal energy metabolism (EGb 761 — ref 10)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Rat sciatic nerve ≠ human IAN (anatomy, diameter, regeneration distance, sensory specificity differ significantly)
- No pharmacokinetic data — optimal human equivalent dose unknown
- Observation period not specified in abstract; long-term functional plateau not described
- No direct comparison with other neuroprotective agents (methylcobalamin, corticosteroids, PDRN)
- Mechanism was not directly confirmed in this study (referenced from prior work)

## 6. Related Work

- Defeudis 2002 (Pharmacol Res): Bilobalide neuroprotection — foundational EGb mechanism
- Hsu et al. 2004 (J Biomater Appl): EGb 761 + Schwann cells in PLGA conduit for nerve regeneration
- Bruno et al. 1993 (Planta Med): Motor nerve regeneration with bilobalide
- Hoyer et al. 1999 (J Neural Transm): EGb 761 improves neuronal energy metabolism

## 7. Glossary

- **EGb**: Ginkgo biloba extract (standardized, typically EGb 761)
- **SFI**: Sciatic Functional Index — walking-track-based functional nerve recovery score
- **MNCV**: Motor Nerve Conduction Velocity
- **CMAP**: Compound Muscle Action Potential
- **Axon regeneration index**: Histological ratio of regenerated axons to total axon count
- **Bilobalide**: Sesquiterpene trilactone component of EGb; key neuroactive constituent
