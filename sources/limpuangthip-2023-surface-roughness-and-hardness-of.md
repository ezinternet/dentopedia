## Why Ingested

Multipurpose ceramic polishing kit (EVE Diacera) evaluated across three CAD/CAM material types — translucent zirconia (VITA YZ), zirconia-reinforced lithium disilicate (Celtra Duo), and lithium disilicate glass ceramic (IPS e.max CAD) — providing chairside-relevant data on surface roughness reduction, Vickers hardness preservation, and polishing bur wear after sequential grinding + polishing.

## Three-line Summary

A multipurpose diamond polishing kit (EVE Diacera) reduced surface roughness of three CAD/CAM ceramics — VITA YZ zirconia, Celtra Duo ZLS, and IPS e.max CAD — to levels comparable with enamel (mean Ra 0.573 µm) and lab as-received specimens after 60 s coarse + 60 s fine polishing. VITA YZ showed the greatest Ra reduction (ΔRa 1.243 µm total) due to its homogenous fine-grained crystal structure, while IPS e.max CAD showed the least (ΔRa 0.793 µm); Vickers hardness was unchanged across all materials post-polishing. Coarse polishing bur weight loss correlated with material hardness (VITA YZ > Celtra Duo > IPS e.max CAD), whereas fine polishing bur weight loss was not significant for any material.

## 세줄요약

범용 다이아몬드 연마 키트(EVE Diacera)로 세 가지 CAD/CAM 세라믹을 60초 거친 연마 + 60초 미세 연마한 결과, 모든 소재의 표면조도(Ra)가 법랑질 수준(평균 Ra 0.573 µm) 및 실험실 완성품과 유사해졌다. 균일한 미세 결정 구조를 가진 VITA YZ 지르코니아가 가장 큰 Ra 감소(ΔRa 1.243 µm)를 보였고, IPS e.max CAD가 가장 작은 감소(ΔRa 0.793 µm)를 나타냈으며, 연마 후 모든 소재의 비커스 경도는 변하지 않았다. 거친 연마 버의 중량 감소는 소재 경도 순서(VITA YZ > Celtra Duo > IPS e.max CAD)와 일치했으나 미세 연마 버는 세 소재 모두 유의한 중량 감소가 없었다.

## Key Contributions

1. First study to systematically evaluate the multipurpose EVE Diacera polishing kit across all three CAD/CAM ceramic material classes (glass-matrix, polycrystalline, and ZLS) at multiple time points (every 15 s).
2. Demonstrated that a single multipurpose polishing kit achieves optimal surface roughness comparable to enamel and lab as-received specimens across all three material types.
3. Polishing efficacy depends on the relationship between polishing bur grain size and ceramic crystalline structure, not on material hardness per se.
4. Coarse polishing bur abrasion correlates with material hardness; fine polishing bur shows negligible wear regardless of material.
5. 60-second duration established as the clinical plateau point for all three materials.

## Methodology

**Design**: In vitro, one-way and repeated-measures ANOVA, n = 10 per group for Ra, n = 4 for hardness.

**Materials tested**:
- IPS e.max CAD (LT, A3) — lithium disilicate glass ceramic (Ivoclar Vivadent)
- VITA YZ (XT, A3) — translucent zirconia (VITA Zahnfabrik)
- Celtra Duo (LT, A3) — zirconia-reinforced lithium disilicate / ZLS (Dentsply Sirona)

**Polishing sequence**:
1. Grinding: fine diamond bur 881F 014 (27–76 µm grit), 1 N, 200,000 rpm, 15 s
2. Coarse polishing: EVE Diacera H2DCmf (green; 25–35 µm diamond in polyurea), 1 N, 10,000 rpm, 60 s
3. Fine polishing: EVE Diacera H2DC (pink; 3–6 µm diamond in polyurea), 1 N, 10,000 rpm, 60 s

**Measurements**:
- Surface roughness (Ra, µm): non-contact optical profilometer (Alicona InfiniteFocusSL, 50×), 5 areas per specimen, measured every 15 s
- Vickers hardness: FM-810, 100 g/15 s, 5 indentations per specimen
- Polishing bur weight: Radwag analytical balance, before and after polishing 5 specimens, n = 6 burs per group
- SEM: JSM-6400 (JEOL) at 300×, 1,000×, 5,000×, 10,000×

**Comparators**: Lab as-received specimens (high-gloss lab polished), human enamel (n = 30 teeth, Ra 0.573 ± 0.167 µm)

## Key Results

**Surface roughness (Ra, µm, mean ± SD) — selected time points**:

| Material | Lab as-received | Post-grind | Post-coarse 60 s | Post-fine 60 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPS e.max CAD | 0.698 | 1.440 | 0.864 | 0.647 |
| VITA YZ | 0.668 | 1.838 | 0.803 | 0.595 |
| Celtra Duo | 0.637 | 1.663 | 0.768 | 0.593 |

- After complete fine polishing, Ra values of all three materials were NOT significantly different from each other, from enamel (0.573 µm), or from lab as-received specimens.
- Greatest total ΔRa: VITA YZ (1.243 µm) > Celtra Duo (1.070 µm) > IPS e.max CAD (0.793 µm)
- Coarse polishing accounted for the majority of Ra reduction in all materials.
- VITA YZ Ra plateaued after 30 s of fine polishing (no further reduction beyond that point).

**Vickers hardness (HV, mean ± SD)**:

| Material | Pre-polishing (lab as-received) | Post-fine polishing |
|---|---|---|
| IPS e.max CAD | 557.9 ± 30.7 | 554.7 ± 4.2 (NS) |
| VITA YZ | 1235.9 ± 20.3 | 1191.6 ± 20.1 (NS) |
| Celtra Duo | 640.7 ± 35.6 | 632.4 ± 5.1 (NS) |

Hardness order: VITA YZ > Celtra Duo > IPS e.max CAD (p < 0.05 between groups, but no within-material change post-polishing).

**Polishing bur weight loss (coarse bur after 5 specimens)**:
- Statistically significant weight loss in coarse bur for VITA YZ > Celtra Duo > IPS e.max CAD
- Fine polishing bur: no significant weight loss in any group

**SEM findings**: Grinding produced grooves with scale-like (IPS e.max CAD, Celtra Duo) or bead-like wavy (VITA YZ) patterns. Progressively smoother surface from coarse to fine polishing, matching lab as-received appearance.

## Limitations

- In vitro only — clinical intraoral polishing conditions (temperature, saliva, angulation variability) not replicated
- Only one shade (A3) and one translucency per material — may not generalize across all options
- Custom pressure-control device used (1 N standardized) — clinical hand pressure more variable
- Only one multipurpose polishing kit tested (EVE Diacera); results may differ with ZiLMaster or other systems
- Hardness measured with n = 4 per group only
- Sample size relatively small for hardness subgroup (n = 4)

## Glossary

- **Ra** (표면조도, surface roughness, Ra): arithmetic mean surface roughness in µm; lower = smoother
- **ZLS** (지르코니아 강화 리튬 디실리케이트, Zirconia-reinforced Lithium Silicate): ZrO₂ dissolved in lithium disilicate glass matrix; combines esthetics and mechanical properties
- **CAD/CAM** (컴퓨터 보조 설계/제조, Computer-Aided Design/Manufacturing): digital milling workflow for ceramic restorations
- **EVE Diacera** (다목적 세라믹 연마 키트): diamond-impregnated polyurea multipurpose ceramic polishing kit; originally developed for zirconia
- **Vickers Hardness (HV)** (비커스 경도, HV): indentation hardness under defined load; reflects resistance to permanent deformation
- **ΔRa**: change in surface roughness (Ra before minus Ra after); larger = more effective polishing
- **3Y-TZP** (3몰% 이트리아 안정화 정방정계 지르코니아): standard translucent zirconia formulation
