## Why Ingested

Specifically examines PDT as a non-surgical adjunct for peri-implantitis — distinct from the broader [[periodontics/jervoe-storm-2024-photodynamic-therapy-periodontal-periimplant-diseases]] Cochrane review by focusing exclusively on peri-implantitis non-surgical management and including biomarker outcomes. Fills the peri-implantitis PDT gap identified in the Cochrane review.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 25 studies (including RCTs) evaluating PDT as adjunct to non-surgical mechanical debridement (MD) for peri-implantitis; PDT provided significant additional short-term improvements in BOP, PD, PI, and crestal bone loss at 3 months.

At 3-month follow-up: BOP SMD=0.90 (p=0.005), PD SMD=1.15 (p<0.0001), PI SMD=0.74 (p=0.0004), CBL SMD=0.38 (p=0.04); benefits for CAL, mucosal recession, and inflammatory biomarkers were less pronounced.

No significant additional benefits from PDT were observed at extended follow-up periods — adjunct PDT appears beneficial for short-term peri-implant parameter control but lacks evidence for sustained effects.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 주위염(peri-implantitis) 비외과적 치료에서 PDT(광역동치료)의 보조 효과를 평가한 SR+MA; 25편 연구(RCT 포함), 3개월 시점에서 BOP·PD·PI·변연골 소실(CBL) 유의 개선.

3개월: BOP SMD=0.90(p=0.005), PD SMD=1.15(p<0.0001), PI SMD=0.74(p=0.0004), CBL SMD=0.38(p=0.04); CAL·점막퇴축·염증 바이오마커에 대한 추가 효과는 제한적.

장기 추적에서는 PDT 추가 효과 없음 — 단기 임플란트 주위 임상 지표 개선에는 유용하나 지속 효과 근거 부족.

## 2. Key Results

**Included studies**: 25 (including RCTs from PubMed, Web of Science, CENTRAL; n=4675 screened)

**At 3 months (MD+PDT vs MD alone):**
- BOP: SMD=0.90 (95% CI: −1.53 to −0.28; p=0.005)
- PD: SMD=1.15 (95% CI: −1.72 to −0.58; p<0.0001)
- PI: SMD=0.74 (95% CI: −1.15 to −0.33; p=0.0004)
- CBL: SMD=0.38 (95% CI: −0.73 to −0.03; p=0.04)

**Less pronounced benefits**: CAL, mucosal recession, inflammatory biomarkers

**Extended follow-up**: No significant additional PDT benefits

**Baseline improvements**: Both MD alone and MD+PDT significantly improved outcomes vs baseline

## 3. Limitations

- Abstract-only source; full text not available via PMC
- Heterogeneous PDT protocols (light sources, photosensitizers, timing)
- Short follow-up dominates evidence
- 25 studies vs larger body in periodontitis literature

## 4. Glossary

- **MD**: Mechanical debridement
- **PD/PPD**: Probing depth
- **CBL**: Crestal bone loss
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing
- **PI**: Plaque index
- **CAL**: Clinical attachment level
- **SMD**: Standardized mean difference
