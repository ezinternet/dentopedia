## Why Ingested

이 논문은 상악동 거상술 (Sinus Floor Elevation, SFE) 후보 환자에 대한 이비인후과 (ENT) 사전 평가의 프로토콜을 최초로 체계화한 포지션 페이퍼로, [[sinus-lift/lateral/kim-2019-maxillary-implants-otolaryngologist-perspective]] 및 [[sinus-lift/lateral/craig-2024-ent-oral-surgeon-collaboration-sinus-elevation]]이 다루는 ENT-임플란트 전문의 협진 framework의 문헌적 선행 근거가 된다. 가역적 금기증의 분류 체계와 기능적 내시경 부비동 수술 (Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery, FESS) 교정 후 수술 진행이라는 임상 의사결정 구조를 위키에 추가하기 위해 인제스트.

## Three-line Summary

This 2008 Italian position paper and narrative review presents a 3-step ENT management protocol for maxillary sinus lift candidates: preventive-diagnostic, preventive-therapeutic, and diagnostic-therapeutic phases.

Preoperative nasal endoscopy and CT imaging are essential to identify reversible contraindications (OMC variants, rhinosinusitis, polyposis, oro-antral fistula) correctable via FESS before sinus lift, versus irreversible contraindications (cystic fibrosis, Kartagener's syndrome, immunodeficiency, malignant tumors) that preclude surgery.

Multidisciplinary ENT–implantologist collaboration is recommended to minimize postoperative maxillary sinusitis and optimize surgical outcomes; the paper establishes a risk-stratification framework rather than original clinical data.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 상악동 거상술 후보 환자 관리를 위한 이비인후과(ENT)의 3단계 관리 프로토콜(예방적-진단적, 예방적-치료적, 진단적-치료적)을 제시한 2008년 이탈리아 포지션 페이퍼 및 내러티브 리뷰.

줄2: 술전 비내시경(Nasal Endoscopy)과 CT로 비강-상악동 개구부 복합체 (Ostiomeatal Complex, OMC) 변이, 비부비동염, 비용종, 구강-상악동 누공 등 가역적 금기증을 확인하고 기능적 내시경 부비동 수술 (FESS)로 교정 후 거상술을 진행하는 반면, 낭성 섬유증·원발성 섬모 이상운동증·면역결핍·악성 종양은 비가역적 금기증으로 분류.

줄3: 이비인후과 전문의와 임플란트 의사의 협진 체계(multidisciplinary collaboration)를 권고하나 원본 임상 데이터 없이 전문가 의견 기반의 내러티브 리뷰로 근거 수준이 낮음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic ENT protocol framework for stratifying sinus lift candidates by preoperative ENT risk
- Classification of contraindications into **reversible** (correctable via FESS) vs **irreversible** (absolute contraindications)
- Three-phase management algorithm: (1) preventive-diagnostic, (2) preventive-therapeutic, (3) diagnostic-therapeutic
- Emphasis on nasal endoscopy + CT as mandatory preoperative workup when sinus pathology is suspected
- Identification of postoperative maxillary sinusitis as the main ENT complication of sinus augmentation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Position paper and narrative review based on anatomy/physiology of the maxillary sinus and a synthesis of contemporary evidence (2008). Describes:
- Maxillary sinus anatomy (mucociliary clearance, ostiomeatal complex, Schneiderian membrane)
- Three-step management algorithm applied to sinus lift candidates
- Role of FESS in reversing ENT contraindications pre-surgery
- No original clinical data; no systematic search; no statistical analysis

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Reversible contraindications (correctable via FESS before sinus lift):**
- OMC anatomical variants compromising ostium drainage
- Chronic rhinosinusitis and nasal polyposis
- Oro-antral fistula

**Irreversible contraindications (absolute — sinus lift precluded):**
- Cystic fibrosis
- Kartagener's syndrome (primary ciliary dyskinesia)
- Primary immunodeficiency
- Malignant sinonasal tumors

**Postoperative complication:** Sinus lift may transiently or chronically disturb maxillary sinus homeostasis, leading to postoperative maxillary sinusitis — the most important ENT sequela.

**Protocol steps:**
1. Preventive-diagnostic: ENT assessment + nasal endoscopy + CT if sinus pathology suspected
2. Preventive-therapeutic: FESS to correct reversible contraindications before sinus lift
3. Diagnostic-therapeutic: ENT management of postoperative sinusitis when it occurs

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review design; no systematic literature search, no PRISMA
- No original patient data; all recommendations are expert-opinion based
- Evidence base is 2008-era; newer consensus (e.g., multidisciplinary protocols) has since evolved
- No quantitative thresholds for mucosal thickness or specific ENT findings
- "Sinus lift" used broadly — lateral and transcrestal approaches not separately analyzed

|---|---|

| FESS (Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery) | 기능적 내시경 부비동 수술 — minimally invasive surgical correction of sinonasal obstruction |
| Schneiderian membrane | 슈나이더막 — maxillary sinus mucoperiosteal lining elevated during sinus lift |
