## Three-line Summary

A narrative review for primary care clinicians outlining both oral and extra-oral (systemic) causes of halitosis, including psychogenic variants, and providing a framework for diagnosis and referral.

## 세줄요약

구강 및 구강 외 원인(전신질환 포함)을 망라한 구강 악취(Halitosis) 고찰로, 혈액매개성(blood-borne) 악취·가성 구취(pseudo-halitosis)·공포증(halitophobia)을 포함한 감별진단 및 1차 진료 의뢰 지침을 제공한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Prevalence estimate**: Halitosis affects 50–65% of the population; widely underdiscussed as a clinical "taboo."
2. **Intra-oral predominance**: 90% of halitosis cases have oral causes; up to 15% involve extra-oral or systemic etiology.
3. **Blood-borne halitosis**: Introduces the category of systemic malodor delivered via the bloodstream to the lungs, distinguishable from intra-oral sources.
4. **Psychogenic variants**: Defines pseudo-halitosis (perceived but absent odor) and halitophobia (exaggerated fear of halitosis) as distinct clinical entities requiring separate management.
5. **Practical clinical framework**: Provides a decision pathway — oral cause → dentist referral; extra-oral cause → treatment of underlying systemic condition.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Prevalence: 50–65% of the population affected.
- Oral causes: ~90% of cases.
- Extra-oral causes (alone or combined): up to 15% of cases.
- Systemic diseases implicated include gastrointestinal, respiratory, hepatic, renal, and metabolic conditions.
- Early presentation of halitosis may serve as a diagnostic signal for underlying serious pathology.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review; no systematic search or quality assessment reported.
- Prevalence range (50–65%) is broad, reflecting heterogeneous measurement methods across studies.
- Psychogenic halitosis management strategies not detailed in abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- **혈액매개성 구취 (Blood-borne Halitosis)**: 혈류를 통해 폐로 전달되어 호기에 나타나는 구강 외 악취
- **가성 구취 (Pseudo-halitosis)**: 실제 악취는 없으나 환자가 주관적으로 호소하는 경우
- **구취공포증 (Halitophobia)**: 구취에 대한 과장된 공포; 실측치와 무관하게 지속됨
- **구강 외 원인 (Extra-oral Etiology)**: 소화기·호흡기·신장·간 등 전신 질환에서 기원하는 악취
