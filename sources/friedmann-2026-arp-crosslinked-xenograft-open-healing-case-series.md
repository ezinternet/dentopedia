## Why Ingested
Open-healing ARP is a key topic flagged in the decay audit (open-healing-arp decay axis 0.50). The wiki has [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/ionescu-2022-open-healing-flapless-ridge-preservation]] for open-healing with DBBM+collagen membrane; Friedmann 2026 adds a distinct scaffold (sugar-crosslinked collagen/HA sponge — SCLC/HA) without primary closure, showing complete xenograft remodeling on late reentry biopsy. This fills the "SCLC/HA open-healing" gap identified by the decay sweep.

## One-line Summary
Case series (n=49 patients, 62 sites) using SCLC/HA sponge with open healing (cross-suture only, no membrane closure): all sites healed without complication, 72% needed no additional augmentation at implant placement, 1-year implant survival 98.2%.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates feasibility of SCLC/HA sponge open-healing ARP without membrane coverage or primary flap closure
- Inclusion criterion "≥50% residual buccal bone" defines protocol applicability envelope
- Quantifies augmentation rates at implant placement: 72% (39/54 implant sites) needed no additional augmentation; 22% needed lateral GBR; 6% needed staged augmentation
- Late reentry biopsy (>6 months) showed complete xenograft remodeling → tissue quality preserved for osseointegration
- 1-year implant survival 98.2% (1/54 failures) validates downstream implant outcome

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Case series, prospective
- Population: 49 patients, 62 extraction sites
- Scaffold: SCLC/HA — sugar-crosslinked collagen sponge with hydroxyapatite
- Protocol: No primary closure; cross-suture only at day 7; open healing
- Inclusion: ≥50% residual buccal bone post-extraction
- Outcomes: healing complications, augmentation rate at implant placement, implant survival at 1 year, histology on late reentry biopsy

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- All 62 sites: healed without complication
- Soft tissue cover at 3–4 weeks: achieved in all sites
- Of 54 implant-designated sites:
  - 39/54 (72.2%): no additional augmentation needed
  - 12/54 (22.2%): concomitant lateral GBR required
  - 3/54 (5.6%): staged augmentation required
- Implant survival at 1 year: 98.2% (53/54; 1 failure)
- Late reentry biopsy (>6 months): complete xenograft remodeling — no residual graft particles noted

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Case series: no control group (no comparison to spontaneous healing or primary closure ARP)
- Single-center, small n (62 sites)
- Abstract-only source — additional histomorphometric data, dimensional change data not available
- Inclusion limited to ≥50% buccal bone: results not generalizable to severe dehiscence sockets
- Long-term follow-up (>1 year) not reported

## 6. Related Work
- Ionescu 2022: open-healing DBBM+collagen membrane (n=104, 5yr) — 98.5% implant survival; Friedmann 2026 uses different scaffold without membrane
- Benekou 2025: pooled residual graft 20.49% in ARP vs spontaneous healing; Friedmann 2026 late reentry shows complete remodeling — distinct from slow-resorbing xenograft pattern

## 7. Glossary
- **SCLC/HA**: Sugar-crosslinked collagen/hydroxyapatite sponge
- **Open healing**: Socket closure by secondary intention without primary flap advancement or membrane coverage
- **Late reentry**: Biopsy at >6 months from grafting, typically at implant uncovering
