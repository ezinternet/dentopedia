## Why Ingested

Directly addresses the gap in post-ARP implant readiness assessment: does CBCT Hounsfield Unit (HU) value reliably predict clinical bone quality in sites that have been previously grafted? The finding is critical for socket preservation / ridge preservation clinical workflow. Extends [[wiki/implants/raghavan-2025-bone-density-implant-dentistry-review]] (Misch D1–D4 classification) by adding the major caveat that HU is unreliable after xenograft ARP.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective pilot study (n=118 implant sites, 66 patients, 38 grafted sites) correlating preoperative CBCT HU with intraoperative clinical bone quality (Misch classification) in sites with and without prior bone grafting.

In non-grafted sites, HU significantly predicted clinical bone quality (p<0.001); in grafted sites the correlation became non-significant — especially with xenograft, where grafted sites showed higher HU but softer clinical quality compared to non-grafted controls.

CBCT HU should be interpreted with caution after bone grafting, particularly xenograft — radiographic density does not reflect true clinical bone quality in these sites.

## 세줄요약

후향적 파일럿 연구 (n=118 임플란트 부위, 66명, 이식재 부위 38개): 술 전 CBCT 하운스필드 단위(Hounsfield Unit, HU)와 술 중 임상 골 질(Misch 분류) 간 상관관계 — 이식재 유·무별 비교.

비이식재 부위: HU가 임상 골 질을 유의하게 예측(p<0.001); 이식재 부위: 상관관계 비유의(이식재 종류 불문) — 특히 이종골(Xenograft) 부위에서 HU는 높으나 임상 골 질이 더 낮은 역설적 결과.

치조제보존술(Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 등 이식재 시술 후 CBCT HU는 임상 골 질의 신뢰할 수 있는 지표가 아님 — 특히 이종골 이식 부위에서 판독 시 주의 필요.

## Key Contributions

- First study to specifically test whether HU–bone quality correlation holds in **previously grafted** vs non-grafted implant sites
- Grafted sites paradoxically showed **higher HU but softer clinical bone quality** (Misch grade) than non-grafted sites — radiopacity of graft residue inflates apparent HU
- Allograft sites: HU–quality relationship similar to no-graft sites (p≥0.07) — allograft remodels more predictably
- Xenograft sites (Bio-Oss–type materials): HU–quality relationship **significantly different** from no-graft (p=0.02) — xenograft particles persist, dominate CBCT density without reflecting native bone quality
- Practical caveat: in post-ARP sites with xenograft (the most common ARP material), preoperative CBCT HU cannot reliably guide surgical protocol selection

## Methodology

- Design: retrospective review of surgical records + preoperative CBCT scans (2017–2019)
- n: 273 implants with adequate preoperative CBCT → 118 sites met inclusion criteria (66 patients, mean age 58 yrs, 65% female)
- Graft group: 38 sites (bone grafts placed at an earlier surgical stage); no-graft group: 80 sites
- Clinical bone quality: Misch classification (D1–D4) assessed intraoperatively by tactile sense + surgical instrument resistance
- Radiographic bone density: CBCT grayscale HU measured at implant site preoperatively
- Statistics: regression controlling for jaw location; correlation magnitude tested across graft subgroups
- Limitations: pilot sample, single institution, graft materials not individually specified in detail

## Key Results

| Comparison | Finding | p-value |
|---|---|---|
| Graft vs no-graft: clinical bone quality | Grafted sites: softer clinical quality | 0.003 |
| Graft vs no-graft: CBCT HU | Grafted sites: higher HU | <0.001 |
| HU–quality correlation (overall) | Significant positive | ≤0.01 |
| HU–quality correlation (no graft) | Significant positive (stronger) | <0.001 |
| HU–quality correlation (any graft) | Non-significant | NS |
| Allograft vs no-graft correlation | Not significantly different | ≥0.07 |
| Xenograft vs no-graft correlation | Significantly different | 0.02 |

## Limitations

- Pilot study — small n, especially graft subgroups; underpowered for subgroup analysis
- Single institution, single country
- Graft material specifics and timing of grafting relative to implant placement not fully detailed
- Misch classification is subjective (tactile) — inter-examiner reliability not reported
- Bone grafting is heterogeneous: socket preservation, ridge augmentation, sinus lift — not differentiated

## Glossary

- **Misch classification (D1–D4)**: intraoperative tactile grading of bone quality — D1 = dense cortical (>1250 HU), D4 = fine trabecular only (150–350 HU)
- **Hounsfield Unit (HU)**: CT grey-scale unit proportional to x-ray attenuation; CBCT-derived HU approximates but does not equal multi-slice CT HU
- **Xenograft residue effect**: radiopaque xenograft particles (e.g., Bio-Oss) persist in healed socket, increasing apparent CBCT density without equivalent increase in native bone stiffness
- **Allograft**: human-derived bone graft; resorbs more completely than xenograft → HU after allograft ARP more likely to reflect native bone
- **Ridge preservation (ARP, 치조제보존술)**: socket grafting at extraction to limit dimensional changes before implant placement
