## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/endodontics/diagnosis/karamifar-2020-endodontic-periapical-lesion-an-overview]]는 조직병리가 육아종(granuloma) vs 낭종(cyst) 감별의 gold standard라고만 서술하는데, 본 case-control IHC 연구는 그 감별을 뒷받침하는 구체적 분자 기전(TGF-β1·CD68 발현 차이)을 정량 데이터로 제공해 근거를 보강한다. 만성 치근단병변 (Chronic Periapical Lesion) 감별진단에 관련.

## Three-line Summary

Case-control histological/immunohistochemical study of human periapical tissue (n=50: 25 radicular cysts [RCs], 25 periapical granulomas [PGs]; plus n=4 healthy third-molar pulp controls) from Cairo University Endodontics department.

PGs showed more severe inflammatory infiltration (median score 4) than RCs (median score 3, p<0.001); both TGF-β1 and CD68 immunoexpression were significantly higher in PGs and RCs vs control (p<0.001), and significantly higher in PGs than RCs (TGF-β1 52.75±1.37% vs 25.43±1.34%; CD68 17.15±1.02% vs 11.94±1.14%, both p<0.001), with a negative TGF-β1–CD68 correlation (PGs r=-0.88, RCs r=-0.14, p<0.05).

Clinically, distinct TGF-β1/CD68 immunoprofiles support histology-based typing of periapical lesions and highlight PGs as the more actively macrophage-driven inflammatory lesion; small control group (n=4) and cross-sectional design limit causal/temporal inference.

## 세줄요약

카이로대학교 근관치료과 사례-대조 조직학·면역조직화학 연구 (n=50: 치근낭종(Radicular Cyst, RC) 25례, 치근단육아종(Periapical Granuloma, PG) 25례; 대조군 건강한 제3대구치 치수 n=4).

PG의 염증침윤 중증도(median score 4)가 RC(median score 3)보다 유의하게 높았고(p<0.001), 형질전환성장인자-베타1 (Transforming Growth Factor-beta 1, TGF-β1)과 대식세포 표지자 (CD68) 발현 모두 PG·RC가 대조군보다 유의하게 높았으며(p<0.001) PG가 RC보다도 유의하게 높음(TGF-β1 52.75±1.37% vs 25.43±1.34%; CD68 17.15±1.02% vs 11.94±1.14%, 모두 p<0.001), 두 표지자 간 음의 상관관계(PG r=-0.88, RC r=-0.14, p<0.05) 확인.

임상적으로 TGF-β1/CD68 면역표현형 차이는 조직학 기반 병소 유형 감별을 뒷받침하고 PG가 더 활성화된 대식세포 주도 염증 병소임을 시사; 대조군이 작고(n=4) 단면 연구 설계라 시간적·인과적 추론에는 제한이 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies histological inflammatory severity (4-point scoring, ×400 field counts) showing PGs (median score 4, severe) exceed RCs (median score 3, moderate) in inflammatory cell density.
- First study to directly correlate TGF-β1 and CD68 area-percentage immunoexpression between PGs and RCs and control pulp using ANOVA + post hoc Tukey.
- Demonstrates a significant negative Pearson correlation between TGF-β1 and CD68 within both PG (r=-0.8803) and RC (r=-0.1389) groups, proposed to reflect TGF-β1's shift from pro-inflammatory (macrophage recruitment) to immunosuppressive (macrophage activity reduction) signaling over lesion evolution.
- Supports the biological plausibility that PGs are the more "active" immune-response lesion and RCs the more "stable"/chronic lesion, consistent with the Malassez-epithelial-rest cyst-transformation hypothesis discussed in prior literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Case-control, cross-sectional tissue study.
- **Sample**: 25 human RCs + 25 human PGs from patients undergoing periapical surgery for chronic periapical lesions unresponsive to routine endodontic therapy (age 20–40, systemically healthy, no pregnancy/medication); control = healthy dental pulp from 4 patients undergoing sound third-molar extraction.
- **Diagnosis criteria**: RC = nonvital tooth + fluid-filled cavity on surgical exploration + histological nonkeratinized stratified squamous epithelial lining. PG = granulomatous tissue with lymphocyte/macrophage/PMN/plasma cell infiltrate, no epithelium.
- **Histology**: H&E staining; inflammatory infiltrate graded 1 (none/few) – 4 (>125 cells/field, severe) across 10 fields at ×400, blinded examiner.
- **IHC**: Anti-TGF-β (clone TB21, 1:1000) and anti-CD68 (clone KP1, 1:200) primary antibodies, biotinylated secondary, DAB chromogen, Mayer's hematoxylin counterstain, PBS negative control.
- **Morphometry**: Leica Quin 500 image analyzer, area % of immunostaining across 10 fields at ×400.
- **Statistics**: Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality → ANOVA + post hoc Tukey (parametric, morphometric data); Kruskal-Wallis (non-parametric, morphology scores); Fisher exact / T-test (demographics); Pearson correlation (marker relationships). Significance threshold p≤0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Demographics**: PGs skewed female (76%), younger (mean age 25.5±5y), smaller lesions (<1cm in 84%); RCs skewed male (52%), older (mean age 32.5±4.77y), larger lesions (≥1cm in 76%). Age and lesion size differed significantly between groups (p<0.001); gender, symptom status, and anatomic location did not.

**Histology**: Control pulp = normal, no inflammatory infiltrate. RCs = nonkeratinized stratified squamous epithelium lining, dilated/hypertrophic vasculature, "looped and arcaded" epithelium, moderate inflammatory score (median 3). PGs = granulation tissue, macrophage/plasma cell/lymphocyte-dominant infiltrate, severe inflammatory score (median 4). Kruskal-Wallis p<0.001 across groups.

**Immunohistochemistry / morphometry** (mean area % ± SD):

| Marker | Control (n=4) | RCs (n=25) | PGs (n=25) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TGF-β1 | 0.142 ± 0.057 | 25.43 ± 1.335 | 52.75 ± 1.369 | <0.001 (all pairwise, Tukey) |
| CD68 | 0.148 ± 0.066 | 11.938 ± 1.136 | 17.15 ± 1.016 | <0.001 (all pairwise, Tukey) |

**Correlation** (Pearson r, p<0.05 for both): TGF-β1 vs CD68 in PGs r=-0.8803; in RCs r=-0.1389 — both negative, PG correlation much stronger.

RC epithelial cells additionally showed positive TGF-β1/CD68 staining (not just stromal cells).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small control group (n=4) drawn from a biologically distinct tissue (healthy pulp, used as a pragmatic proxy for periodontal ligament, which was unavailable).
- Small overall sample size (n=50 lesions) limits generalizability; authors call for larger cohorts.
- Soft-tissue-only biopsies — no assessment of hard-tissue/bone remodeling correlates.
- Cross-sectional design cannot capture temporal dynamics of TGF-β1/CD68 expression across lesion development/healing stages.
- Precise mechanistic role of TGF-β1/CD68 interplay (why the negative correlation) remains hypothesis-generating, not established; authors flag prior literature is inconsistent (some studies found no PG-RC CD68 difference, one found the opposite direction).
- Future work: longitudinal/comparative studies tracking marker dynamics over lesion progression/healing; explore markers as therapeutic targets.

## 7. Glossary

- **형질전환성장인자-베타1 (Transforming Growth Factor-beta 1, TGF-β1)**: Multifunctional cytokine secreted by platelets, fibroblasts, macrophages, and inflammatory cells; dual pro-/anti-inflammatory immunomodulatory roles depending on signaling context; implicated in fibrosis/collagen deposition and periapical lesion pathology.
- **대식세포 표지자 (CD68)**: Widely used immunohistochemical marker for macrophages; used here to quantify macrophage-mediated inflammation in periapical tissue.
- **치근단육아종 (Periapical Granuloma, PG)**: Localized mass of chronic granulomatous inflammatory tissue at a tooth apex, without epithelial lining, containing lymphocytes/macrophages/PMNs/plasma cells.
- **치근낭종 (Radicular Cyst, RC)**: Cystic periapical lesion lined by nonkeratinized stratified squamous epithelium (thought to arise from Malassez epithelial rest stimulation), containing fluid/semifluid cavity contents.
- **면역조직화학 (Immunohistochemistry, IHC)**: Technique using antibody-antigen binding with chromogenic detection (here, DAB) to visualize/quantify specific protein expression (TGF-β1, CD68) in tissue sections.
- **형태계측 분석 (Morphometric Analysis)**: Quantitative image-analysis method (here, Leica Quin 500) measuring immunostained area percentage across standardized microscopic fields.
