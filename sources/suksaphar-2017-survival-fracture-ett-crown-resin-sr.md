## Why Ingested

Posterior-tooth counterpart to the same Mahidol University group's anterior-tooth cohort [[post-and-core/phengudom-2021-survival-fracture-endodontic-anterior]] — establishes that crown-vs-resin-composite equivalence for endodontically treated teeth with limited structural loss isn't tooth-position-specific, and predates [[inlay/dekuijper-2023-clinical-performance-direct-composite-resin]]'s broader direct-vs-indirect SR+MA on the same question.

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review synthesized evidence from 1 randomized controlled trial and 2 cohort studies (total N=623 posterior endodontically treated teeth) comparing survival against fracture between full-coverage crowns and direct resin composite restorations. Pooled survival rates ranged from 94% to 100% for crowns and 91.9% to 100% for resin composite, with no significant difference observed for teeth with up to 3-surface loss. Current evidence suggests that direct resin composite restorations may be a viable alternative to crowns in posterior endodontically treated teeth with minimal to moderate structural loss, though generalization is limited by study heterogeneity and confounding by indication.

## 세줄요약

이 체계적 문헌고찰은 무작위대조시험 1편과 코호트 연구 2편 (총 623개 근관치료된 구치)의 증거를 종합하여 전부피개관 (full-coverage crown)과 레진 복합 (resin composite) 수복물 간의 파절 생존율을 비교하였다. 전체 생존율은 관 (crown)이 94–100%, 레진 복합이 91.9–100% 범위였으며, 3면 이하의 치질 소실이 있는 치아에서는 유의한 차이가 없었다. 현재 증거는 중등도 이하의 치질 소실이 있는 근관치료 구치에서 레진 복합 수복이 관의 대안이 될 수 있음을 시사하나, 연구 간 이질성과 적응증 편향으로 일반화에 제한이 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review to include both randomized and non-randomized cohort studies comparing crown vs. resin composite survival in endodontically treated posterior teeth.
- Pooled analysis of 507 teeth restored with crowns and 116 teeth with resin composite restorations from 3 included studies.
- Survival rates against fracture were 94%–100% for crowns and 91.9%–100% for resin composite restorations.
- No significant difference in survival was found for teeth with ≤3 surface loss, based on the included RCT (Mannocci et al., 2002).
- Identified that tooth type (premolar vs. molar), number of remaining walls, and post placement are important confounding factors affecting fracture survival.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review without meta-analysis.
- Databases & search window: PubMed, ScienceDirect, Web of Science, MEDLINE, SCOPUS from 1980 to 2016; limited to English-language clinical studies.
- PRISMA compliance: Not explicitly stated; follows systematic review methodology with defined inclusion/exclusion criteria and flow diagram (Figure 2).
- Inclusion criteria: Clinical studies on posterior endodontically treated teeth (ETT) with indirect full-coverage crowns or direct non-cuspal coverage resin composite restorations, presenting comparison of survival rates; could be randomized/non-randomized controlled trials or retrospective/prospective cohort studies.
- Exclusion criteria: Case reports, case series, literature reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses; studies without evaluation of survival against fracture, without detail on restoration type, or combining anterior and posterior teeth.
- Included studies: 3 studies — 1 randomized controlled trial (LOE level 2), 1 prospective cohort (LOE level 3), 1 retrospective cohort (LOE level 3).
- Risk of bias assessment: LOE assessed using Oxford Centre for Evidence Based Medicine (OCEBM) Levels of Evidence.
- Outcomes: Survival rate against fracture as primary outcome; tooth surface loss, tooth type, post placement as secondary factors.
- Statistics: Narrative synthesis; meta-analysis not performed due to heterogeneity.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome — Survival against fracture** (crown): Range 94%–100% across 3 studies (N=507).
- **Primary outcome — Survival against fracture** (resin composite): Range 91.9%–100% across 3 studies (N=116).
- **RCT evidence (Mannocci et al., 2002)** — Premolars with 2–3 surface loss: 3-year survival >90% for both crown and resin composite (no significant difference).
- **Cohort studies** — Cagidiaco et al. (2007): 2-year survival 100% for both (fiber post used in all teeth). Dammaschke et al. (2013): crown 94%, resin composite 91.9% (mixed post types, 1–3 surface loss).
- **Subgroup patterns** — Teeth with 1–3 surface loss had higher survival than those with >3 surface loss; premolars had higher survival than molars in referenced studies.
- Heterogeneity: Studies were too heterogeneous for meta-analysis (different designs, follow-up periods, tooth types, post protocols).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 3 studies met inclusion criteria; limited number of resin composite restorations (n=116) compared to crowns (n=507).
- Study heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis; designs included RCT, prospective, and retrospective cohorts with different follow-up durations (2–3 years to longer).
- Confounding by indication: in cohort studies, resin composite restorations were preferentially placed in teeth with less structural loss, which may bias survival estimates.
- Tooth type not consistently separated (premolars vs. molars) in most studies; occlusal forces differ significantly.
- Fiber post placement was used in some but not all studies; its independent effect on survival could not be isolated.
- Long-term (>5 years) evidence is lacking; bond degradation over time may affect resin composite survival.

## 6. Related Work

- Aquilino and Caplan (2002) — Relationship between crown placement and survival of endodontically treated teeth; crown placement improves survival.
- Sorensen and Martinoff (1984) — Intracoronal reinforcement and coronal coverage; cuspal coverage improves clinical success.
- Nagasiri and Chitmongkolsuk (2005) — Long-term survival of endodontically treated molars without crown coverage; survival depends on remaining walls.
- Ferrari et al. (2012) — RCT of endodontically treated and restored premolars; fiber post and resin composite outcomes.
- Sequeira-Byron et al. (2015) — Cochrane systematic review on single crowns vs. conventional fillings for root-filled teeth; only 1 RCT included.

## 7. Glossary

- **ETT (Endodontically Treated Tooth)**: 근관치료된 치아
- **Cuspal protection / cuspal coverage**: 교두 피개; 치아 교두를 완전히 덮는 수복 방식
- **Non-cuspal coverage restoration**: 교두 비피개 수복; 교두를 덮지 않는 직접 레진 수복 등
- **Full-coverage crown**: 전부 피개관; 치아 전체를 덮는 인공 치관
- **Fiber post**: 섬유 포스트; 근관 내에 삽입하여 수복물을 유지하는 섬유 강화 복합재료 포스트
- **LOE (Level of Evidence)**: 근거 수준; Oxford CEBM 기준에 따른 연구 설계의 증거 강도
- **Surface loss**: 치질 소실 면수; 근관치료 전 상실된 치아 표면의 수 (1~5면)
- **Confounding by indication**: 적응증 편향; 치료 선택이 예후에 영향을 미치는 교란 변수
