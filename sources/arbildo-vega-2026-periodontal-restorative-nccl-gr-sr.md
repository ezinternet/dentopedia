## Why Ingested

Combined defects (gingival recession + NCCL) sit on the boundary between [[wiki/periodontics/cairo-2025-root-coverage-single-grd-sr]] (root coverage for single GRDs, no NCCL stratification) and [[wiki/nccl/peumans-2020-nccl-treatment-when-why-how]] (NCCL restoration "sometimes combined with root coverage", narrative only). This updated SR+MA (RoB 2.0 + GRADE) answers the chairside question "should the NCCL be restored with the CAF+CTG?" — mostly no difference, except dentin sensitivity (combined better) and a small PD increase — and extends [[wiki/periodontics/gualtieri-2025-mcaf-gingival-recession-sr-ma]] to the NCCL-affected root.

Reading-order note (SR+MA lane of [[wiki/overviews/evidence-appraisal-toolkit]]): I² low for most outcomes but 60% for PD and 79% for professional esthetics (MRES); publication bias not assessable (< 10 studies); leave-one-out unstable for PD, MRES, DS. All 4 RCTs from one Brazilian group (Santamaria), and two reports (2013/2014) share one cohort — the effective independent evidence base is 3 trials.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420261278151) of 4 parallel RCTs (36–40 patients each, 1–2 y follow-up, all Santamaria group, Brazil) comparing CAF+CTG plus NCCL restoration (composite resin or RMGIC) vs CAF+CTG alone for gingival recession associated with NCCLs.

No significant difference for GR (MD 0.24 mm, p=0.47), CAL, KTT, KTW, patient/professional esthetics, CDH, CDC, RC or CRC (OR 1.09, 80.4% vs 78.6%); the combined approach significantly improved dentin sensitivity (OR 9.25, 95% CI 2.39–35.77; 92.1% vs 55.3%) but increased PD (MD 0.47 mm, 95% CI 0.14–0.80, p=0.01; values stayed < 3 mm).

GRADE low for all outcomes (very low for professional esthetics); single research group and small samples — absence of difference is not proof of equivalence; restore primarily for DS relief, and material choice (nanofilled CR > RMGIC for color stability) drives esthetic heterogeneity.

## 세줄요약

치은퇴축(GR)과 비우식성 치경부 병소(NCCL) 복합결손에서 CAF+CTG+수복(CR 또는 RMGIC) vs CAF+CTG 단독을 비교한 SR+MA — 병렬 RCT 4편(각 36–40명, 1–2년 추적), 전부 브라질 Santamaria 그룹.

GR(MD 0.24 mm, p=0.47)·CAL·KTT·KTW·환자/전문가 심미·CDH·CDC·RC·완전근면피개(CRC, OR 1.09; 80.4% vs 78.6%) 모두 유의차 없음; 상아질 과민(DS)만 복합치료가 유의하게 우수(OR 9.25, 95% CI 2.39–35.77; 92.1% vs 55.3%), 탐침깊이(PD)는 복합치료에서 0.47 mm 증가(p=0.01, 3 mm 미만 건강범위).

GRADE 전 결과 low(전문가 심미는 very low), 단일 연구그룹·소표본 — "차이 없음"을 동등성으로 읽으면 안 됨; 수복은 주로 DS 완화 목적, 심미 이질성은 수복재(나노필 CR > RMGIC 색안정성)가 좌우.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Updated synthesis of combined GR+NCCL defects beyond the earlier Rovai et al. SR+MA, adding RoB 2.0, GRADE, leave-one-out sensitivity analysis and patient-centered outcomes (DS, combined defect coverage, professional esthetics).
- First pooled estimate showing DS benefit of restoring the NCCL at the time of root coverage (OR 9.25).
- Quantifies the PD penalty of restoring under the flap (MD +0.47 mm), attributed to a long junctional epithelium over the restoration.
- Explicitly flags single-group dependence (all trials from Santamaria et al.).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **PICO**: healthy adults (>18 y) with GR associated with NCCL; I = CAF + CTG + CR or RMGIC; C = CAF + CTG without restoration; O = GR, PD, CAL, KTT, KTW, VAS, MRES, DS, CDH, CDC, RC, CRC.
- **Search**: PubMed, Cochrane, Scopus, WoS, Embase, SciELO + Google Scholar, OpenAIRE, ProQuest; inception–Dec 2025; no language restriction.
- **Eligibility**: RCTs (parallel or split-mouth), ≥ 1-year follow-up; related cohort reports handled to avoid double counting.
- **Flow**: 199 records → 163 screened → 8 full texts → 4 RCTs.
- **RoB 2.0**: two reviewers (κ = 0.95); all 4 trials "some concerns".
- **Stats**: MD (inverse variance) / OR (Mantel–Haenszel), random effects, I²; GRADEpro; leave-one-out; no funnel plot (< 10 studies).

Included trials:

| Trial | n patients | Follow-up | Restoration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Santamaria 2018 | 40 (20/20 teeth) | 1 y | Composite resin |
| Santamaria 2016 | 36 (18/18) | 1 y | Composite resin |
| Santamaria 2014 | 36 (18/18) | 2 y | RMGIC (same cohort as 2013; esthetics only) |
| Santamaria 2013 | 36 (18/18) | 2 y | RMGIC |

Teeth: canines and premolars only.

- Leave-one-out: most outcomes stable; PD, MRES and DS sensitive to individual trials.
- Discussion data: Santamaria reported DS falling from 94% to 5.5% (combined) vs 94% to 44.4% (surgery alone). RMGI: up to 44.1% of restorations fail color match at 2 years; nanofilled composite has better color stability and higher MRES — the likely driver of MRES heterogeneity.
- PD increase attributed to a long junctional epithelium over the restoration (no new connective tissue attachment apically); values remain < 3 mm.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 4 RCTs, 36–40 patients each, 1–2 y follow-up; 2013/2014 reports share one cohort.
- All trials from one Brazilian research group (Santamaria et al.) — external validity limited.
- GRADE low/very low (imprecision, RoB some concerns); publication bias not assessable.
- CR vs RMGIC not compared as a formal subgroup; material effect on esthetics is a narrative inference.
- Only canines and premolars; no molars or multiple-recession defects.
- Briefing numbers matched the paper text; no discrepancies.
- Authors call for independent multicenter RCTs with longer follow-up.

## 7. Glossary

- **GR**: gingival recession
- **NCCL**: non-carious cervical lesion
- **CAF**: coronally advanced flap
- **CTG**: connective tissue graft
- **CR**: composite resin
- **RMGIC**: resin-modified glass ionomer cement
- **PD / CAL**: probing depth / clinical attachment level
- **KTT / KTW**: keratinized tissue thickness / width
- **VAS**: visual analog scale (patient esthetics)
- **MRES**: modified root coverage esthetic score (professional esthetics)
- **DS**: dentin sensitivity
- **CDH / CDC**: combined defect height / combined defect coverage
- **RC / CRC**: root coverage (%) / complete root coverage
