## Why Ingested

Tests whether the surface-roughness number inside the SLA family matters clinically — Ra 3.09 µm (Sewon Medics IH2) versus the 2.50 µm benchmark (Osstem TSIII SA) — which is the question the roughness-optimization literature (Knabe's 3.43 µm claim) leaves open at 1 year → [[implants/surface/kim-2021-comparison-sandblasted-acid-etched-surface]] and [[implants/surface/kim-2020-sa-implant-acid-etched-alumina-long-term]].

## Three-line Summary

Randomized clinical trial analyzed as a record review (J Korean Assoc Oral Maxillofac Surg 2022;48:225-231, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital; 55 patients, 80 implants registered, 75 analyzed) comparing two tapered internal-hex SLA implants differing only in surface roughness — Ra 3.09 µm (Sewon Medics IH2, test) versus Ra 2.50 µm (Osstem TSIII SA, control).

Primary ISQ was 79.7 (test) vs 82.9 (control) and secondary 82.1 vs 84.4; success and survival were 100% in both groups at 1 year, MBL was 0.07 vs 0.00 mm (P = 0.0778), and the only soft-tissue difference was a lower plaque index in the rougher group (0.00 vs 0.29, P = 0.0004).

Within the SLA family, a 0.6 µm difference in Ra produced no clinically meaningful difference in stability, bone loss, success or safety — the rougher surface's plaque advantage is too small and too isolated to be a selection criterion.

## Key Contributions
- Isolates surface roughness as the only deliberate difference between two otherwise similar tapered, internal-hex, 11° Morse taper, submerged SLA implants.
- Confirms no meaningful clinical difference at 1 year: ISQ, MBL, success, survival and safety all equivalent.
- Documents a lower plaque index on the rougher (Ra 3.09 µm) surface — the sole statistically significant soft-tissue finding.
- Adds a clinical counterpoint to bench claims that a specific Ra (Knabe: 3.43 µm) is optimal.

## Methodology
Clinical record review of a randomized trial. 55 patients (mean age 53.0 years), 80 SLA implants placed: 38 implants in 29 subjects with Ra 3.09 µm (test group, Sewon Medics IH2 SLA) and 42 implants in 31 subjects with Ra 2.50 µm (control group, Osstem TSIII SA). Five patients received both implant types. After dropouts (1 test, 4 control; two controls excluded for >3 missing teeth 8 weeks before implantation) 37 test and 38 control implants were analyzed.

Outcomes at 1 year: primary and secondary ISQ, success and survival, MBL, and soft tissue (PPD, PI, GI, BOP). Groups were balanced on sex, age, bruxism, drinking and smoking.

## Key Results

| Parameter | Test (Ra 3.09 µm, n=37) | Control (Ra 2.50 µm, n=38) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary ISQ | 79.7 ± 10.22 | 82.9 ± 10.00 | >0.999 |
| Cases with primary ISQ <60 | 1 | 0 | >0.999 |
| Secondary ISQ | 82.1 ± 11.12 | 84.4 ± 10.31 | >0.999 |
| Success / survival (1 yr) | 100% / 100% | 100% / 100% | — |
| MBL (mm) | 0.07 | 0.00 | 0.0778 |
| Plaque index (1 yr) | 0.00 | 0.29 | **0.0004** |
| PPD / GI / BOP | no difference | no difference | NS |

Site distribution: test 24.3% maxilla / 75.7% mandible; control 26.3% / 73.7%, with molars dominating both (test 28 of 37 mandibular; control 23 of 28 mandibular).

One test implant had primary ISQ <60 but achieved stable secondary stability — the single unstable-start case recovered.

## Limitations
Described as a clinical record review of randomized treatment rather than a prospectively followed trial, so outcome ascertainment is retrospective. Short follow-up (1 year) — the surface difference would if anything express itself in long-term MBL and peri-implantitis, not at 12 months. Small sample with site distribution skewed to mandibular molars. Two different manufacturers again, so roughness is confounded with thread design and manufacturing; "only Ra differs" is asserted, not demonstrated. No power calculation reported.

## Glossary
- **SLA:** Sandblasted with Large-grit and Acid-etched surface.
- **Ra:** Arithmetic mean surface roughness (µm).
- **ISQ:** Implant Stability Quotient.
- **MBL:** Marginal Bone Loss.
- **PPD / PI / GI / BOP:** Probing Pocket Depth / Plaque Index / Gingival Index / Bleeding on Probing.
- **Morse taper:** Conical implant-abutment connection (11° here).
