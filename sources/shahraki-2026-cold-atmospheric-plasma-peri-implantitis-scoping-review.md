## Why Ingested

Most recent (July 2026) scoping review synthesizing the full CAP literature for peri-implantitis — integrates both decontamination (31 studies) and osseointegration (46 studies) into one PRISMA-ScR review; provides the most comprehensive up-to-date synthesis available. Directly complements the existing [[implants/surface/plasma/alqutaibi-2023-cap-bacterial-reduction-implants-sr]] (antibacterial focus only) and [[implants/surface/plasma/jungbauer-2021-cold-atmospheric-plasma-antimicrobial-sr]] with an integrated dual-domain synthesis.

## One-line Summary

Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR, 77 studies, 31 decontamination + 46 osseointegration): CAP demonstrates consistent antibacterial effects and improved osseointegration parameters preclinically, but clinical evidence for peri-implantitis management is lacking.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First scoping review integrating BOTH decontamination (31 studies) and osseointegration (46 studies) for CAP in peri-implantitis context — dual-domain synthesis fills gap between prior antibacterial-only reviews (Alqutaibi 2023, Jungbauer 2021) and osseointegration-only reviews (Barausse et al.)
- Establishes CAP's dual mechanism of action: (1) RONS-mediated biofilm disruption, (2) surface energy enhancement → osteoblast attachment
- Quantifies extreme heterogeneity in protocols: exposure 10s–18 min; working gases (argon, helium, air, O2); distance 1–30 mm — no standardized protocol exists
- Identifies temporal limitation: CAP's osseointegration benefit is greatest early (4 weeks, diminishes at 8 weeks per Jang et al.)
- Coaxial DBD = more homogeneous surface treatment than jet systems (important for clinical translation)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Scoping review, PRISMA-ScR
- Search: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar; up to July 2025
- Initial: 452 records → 77 included (31 decontamination, 46 osseointegration)
- Inclusion: in vitro, ex vivo, animal studies (no clinical trials)
- Two independent screeners (K.S., M.A.); third reviewer (N.Z.) for disagreements

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Decontamination (31 studies):**
- Argon-based CAP, 60-180s, 2-5mm distance = most common protocol
- Multispecies peri-implant biofilm (Panariello): 1-5 min → significant CFU reduction
- Combined CAP + mechanical debridement > either alone
- Mature biofilms (≥72h) more resistant than younger biofilms
- Surface microstructure preserved after CAP treatment

**Osseointegration (46 studies):**
- Han et al.: 6-fold surface energy increase; enhanced fibronectin adsorption
- Giro et al.: BIC >100%, BAFO >82% at 3 weeks (CaP-coated implants, argon plasma, canine)
- Coelho et al.: BIC >300%, BAFO >30% (rough Ti, argon, canine)
- Jang et al.: BIC/bone volume up at 4 weeks, effect diminished at 8 weeks
- Teixeira et al.: 20-60s = increased surface energy, improved biomechanical fixation
- ALP, osteocalcin enhanced; IL-1β, IL-17 reduced (Zhou et al.)

**Heterogeneity findings:**
- Argon plasma = most studied; oxygen plasma (Smeets) = superior for zirconia cell proliferation
- Coaxial DBD = homogeneous; jet systems = longer exposure needed
- SLA titanium = best-characterized; zirconia, CaP-coated = less evidence

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Literature search: 4 databases only; English only → may miss Asian-language literature
- No standardized peri-implantitis model across studies
- All evidence preclinical — no clinical trials included by design
- Cannot determine optimal CAP parameters from current evidence
- Plasma devices highly heterogeneous — no commercial device comparison

## 7. Glossary

- **CAP**: Cold Atmospheric Plasma — plasma generated at atmospheric pressure and near-ambient temperature
- **RONS**: Reactive Oxygen and Nitrogen Species — primary antimicrobial agents generated by CAP
- **BIC**: 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact) — histomorphometric osseointegration measure
- **BAFO**: Bone Area Fraction Occupied — percentage of implant thread area occupied by bone
- **PRISMA-ScR**: Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses Extension for Scoping Reviews
- **Coaxial DBD**: Coaxial Dielectric Barrier Discharge — plasma configuration providing homogeneous surface coverage
