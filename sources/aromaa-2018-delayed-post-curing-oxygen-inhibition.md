## Why Ingested

Mechanistic backbone for the oxygen-inhibited layer (OIL) phenomenon: quantifies how oxygen continues to suppress free-radical polymerization during the delayed post-curing stage (DPCS) after light cessation, with direct implications for direct composite finishing and inlay cement margin protocol.
→ [[resin-bonding/patangia-2025-effect-oxygen-inhibited-layer-dental]]

## Three-line Summary

In vitro FTIR + VHN study on BisGMA-TEGDMA model resin (n=6/group) comparing oxygen-exposed vs oxygen-protected surfaces during the delayed post-curing stage (DPCS) up to 360 min post light-cure. Degree of conversion (DC%) rose 50%→65% in oxygen-protected specimens but stagnated in air-exposed ones; surface Vickers hardness diverged to 9.10 vs 4.80 VHN (p<0.005) at 6 h. Oxygen inhibition of free-radical polymerization persists well beyond light cessation, reducing both conversion and mechanical surface quality.

## 세줄요약

BisGMA-TEGDMA 모델 레진으로 광조사 후 지연 후경화 단계(Delayed Post-Curing Stage, DPCS)에서 산소 노출군 vs 차단군을 비교한 인 비트로(in vitro) 연구(n=6/군, FTIR + 비커스 경도). 전환율(Degree of Conversion, DC)은 차단군 50%→65% 상승, 노출군은 정체; 6시간 후 표면 경도(Vickers Hardness Number, VHN)는 차단 9.10 vs 노출 4.80(p<0.005). 광조사 종료 이후에도 산소가 자유라디칼 중합을 지속적으로 억제하며, 이것이 표면 기계적 특성 저하의 직접 원인임을 확인.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that oxygen inhibition of free-radical polymerization is **not limited to the curing phase** — it continues throughout the DPCS (up to 6 h monitored)
- Quantifies the surface hardness penalty: oxygen-exposed surfaces achieve only ~53% of the hardness of oxygen-protected surfaces at 6 h
- Strong correlation between DPCS duration and microhardness: r=0.950 (O-protected) vs r=0.940 (O-exposed), p<0.001
- Correlation between DC% and DPCS time: r=0.941 (O-protected) vs r=0.780 (O-exposed), p<0.001
- Provides in vitro mechanistic basis for clinical protocols that use oxygen-excluding barriers (glycerin, Mylar strips) at restoration margins

## Methodology

- **Design**: In vitro, controlled lab experiment
- **Material**: BisGMA-TEGDMA (60:40 wt%) + camphorquinone (CQ)–amine initiator–activator system
- **Curing**: Light-initiated, 430–480 nm, 1200 mW/mm², 20 s on ATR-FTIR sensor
- **Groups**: O-exposed (DPCS in air) vs O-protected (protected from direct air contact), n=6 each
- **Monitoring**: DC% via ATR-FTIR at 4 timepoints up to 360 min; VHN at same timepoints
- **Statistics**: Significance p<0.005 for VHN difference; Pearson r for correlations

## Key Results

| Metric | O-protected | O-exposed | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| DC% at baseline (end of light cure) | ~50% | ~50% | — |
| DC% at 360 min | ~65% | ~50% (no change) | — |
| VHN at 360 min | 9.10 | 4.80 | <0.005 |
| VHN–DPCS correlation r | 0.950 | 0.940 | <0.001 |
| DC–DPCS time correlation r | 0.941 | 0.780 | <0.001 |

## Limitations

- Model resin only (BisGMA-TEGDMA without fillers) — clinical filled composites will differ
- Small n per group (n=6)
- In vitro; no clinical or ex vivo validation
- Single initiator system (CQ-amine); results may not generalize to other photoinitiators (e.g., TPO, Ivocerin)
- Monitoring ended at 360 min; longer DPCS not assessed

## Glossary

- **DPCS (Delayed Post-Curing Stage)**: Continued free-radical polymerization that occurs after the curing light is switched off, driven by residual reactive species
- **DC% (Degree of Conversion)**: Percentage of methacrylate C=C double bonds that have polymerized; measured by FTIR peak ratio
- **VHN (Vickers Hardness Number)**: Surface hardness metric using pyramidal diamond indenter; proxy for degree of polymerization at the surface
- **ATR-FTIR (Attenuated Total Reflectance Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy)**: Near-surface analytical technique for tracking C=C bond consumption during polymerization
- **BisGMA-TEGDMA**: Common dimethacrylate monomer system; backbone of most modern dental composites
