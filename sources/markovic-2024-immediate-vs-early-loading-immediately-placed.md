## Why Ingested

Pilot RCT providing direct head-to-head comparison of immediate versus early loading for immediately placed implants in full-arch maxillary rehabilitation — a clinically important loading-timing question addressed by few prospective RCTs. Complements [[immediate-implant/loading-protocol/wittneben-2023-immediate-implant-loaded-single-crown-sr]] and the broader loading-protocol evidence base.

## Three-line Summary

Pilot prospective RCT (n=24) comparing immediate loading (temporary restoration at surgery) vs early loading (no temporization until week 7) for 6 immediately placed bone-level tapered implants in full-arch maxillary rehabilitation, with 2-year follow-up. ISQ dynamics were similar between groups (Penguin p=0.268; Ostell p=0.552); MBL remained at submillimeter level; cumulative survival 91.80% (immediate) vs 97.22% (early) was not significantly different (p=0.162); OHRQoL improved significantly in both groups (p<0.001). Both immediate and early loading protocols are clinically adequate for full-arch maxillary rehabilitation with immediately placed implants.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=24)으로 전악 상악 수복에서 즉시 부하 (Immediate Loading, IL) vs 조기 부하 (Early Loading, EL)를 비교; 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 변화는 두 군 동등 (p>0.05), 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 역시 1 mm 미만 수준. 누적 생존율 (Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR) 91.80% vs 97.22%로 유의차 없어 (p=0.162), 즉시 부하 시행 시 조기 부하 대비 안전성 비열등.

- **PMID**: 38693759

## Key Contributions

- First pilot RCT specifically comparing immediate vs early loading for immediately placed implants in full-arch maxillary rehabilitation
- ISQ stability trajectories (both Penguin RFA and Ostell ISQ) confirmed equivalent between loading protocols over 2 years
- Submillimeter MBL achieved in both groups regardless of loading timing
- High patient satisfaction and significant OHRQoL improvement confirmed for both protocols at 2 years
- Insertion torque: immediate group 33.0±4.87 Ncm, early group 29.26±8.31 Ncm — both within immediate-loading threshold range

## Methodology

- **Design**: Pilot prospective RCT
- **n**: 24 patients (12 immediate loading, 12 early loading)
- **Population**: Maxillary failing dentition patients requiring full-arch rehabilitation
- **Implant**: Bone-level tapered dental implants with hydrophilic surface (6 implants per arch)
- **Immediate loading**: Temporary restorations placed at surgery
- **Early loading**: No temporization; final screw-retained complete PFM prosthesis at week 7
- **Final prosthesis**: Screw-retained complete PFM at week 7 (both groups)
- **Follow-up**: 2 years post-loading

## Key Results

- ISQ dynamics similar for both groups: Penguin p=0.268; Ostell p=0.552
- MBL at submillimeter level in both groups
- Cumulative survival rate: 91.80% (immediate loading) vs 97.22% (early loading), p=0.162 (not significant)
- OHIP total score decreased significantly over time in both groups (p<0.001)
- High patient satisfaction at 2 years regardless of loading protocol
- Insertion torque: immediate 33.0±4.87 Ncm, early 29.26±8.31 Ncm

## Limitations

- Abstract-only — full methodology, implant specifications, bone augmentation details, randomization/blinding procedures, and per-implant data not available
- Pilot study (n=24) with limited statistical power; underpowered to detect small between-group differences in survival (91.80% vs 97.22%)
- Short follow-up (2 years) — long-term MBL and prosthesis survival unknown
- Single-center, single jaw (maxilla) — generalizability to mandible or multi-center populations limited
- Full-arch rehabilitation only; single-tooth/partial-arch loading comparisons excluded

## Glossary

- 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ): 공명 주파수 분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)으로 측정하는 임플란트 안정성 지표 (1–100 스케일)
- 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL): 임플란트 주위 변연 치조골의 수직 소실량 (mm 단위)
- 즉시 부하 (Immediate Loading, IL): 임플란트 식립 직후 48시간 이내 임시 수복물 연결
- 조기 부하 (Early Loading, EL): 식립 후 1주~2개월 사이 부하 적용
- 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL): OHIP-49 등 환자보고 설문으로 측정
- 누적 생존율 (Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR): Kaplan-Meier 방법 등으로 산출하는 임플란트 누적 생존율
