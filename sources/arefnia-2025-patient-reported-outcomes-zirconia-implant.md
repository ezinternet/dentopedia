## Three-line Summary

PRISMA systematic review (Journal of Patient-Reported Outcomes 2025, Medical University of Graz, 596 records screened → 12 controlled clinical trials included, n unspecified) — the first SR focused specifically on patient-reported outcomes (PROs) for zirconia dental implants.

Most PROMs — aesthetics, speaking, comfort, chewing ability, and general satisfaction — improved significantly from pretreatment to prosthetic delivery, with high overall patient satisfaction; however, high heterogeneity across PROM instruments precluded quantitative pooling.

The main evidence weakness is the absence of standardized, validated PROM instruments across studies; without a common measurement tool, cross-study comparison and clinical benchmarking remain impossible, and no meta-analysis was feasible.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 체계적 문헌고찰 (Journal of Patient-Reported Outcomes 2025, 그라츠 의과대학, 596건 스크리닝 → 대조 임상시험 12편 포함) — 지르코니아 (Zirconia) 임플란트의 환자보고결과 (Patient-Reported Outcomes, PRO)만을 대상으로 한 최초의 SR.

대부분의 환자보고측정도구 (Patient-Reported Outcome Measure, PROM) — 심미·발음·편안함·저작능력·전반적 만족도 — 가 치료 전 대비 보철물 장착 시점에 유의하게 개선되었고 전반적 환자 만족도가 높았으나, PROM 도구 간 이질성이 커 정량적 통합(메타분석) 불가.

핵심 근거 약점은 표준화·검증된 공통 PROM 도구의 부재로, 표준 측정 도구 없이는 연구 간 비교와 임상 기준점 설정이 불가능하며 향후 지르코니아 임플란트 전용 표준 PROM 개발이 필요.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal of Patient-Reported Outcomes 2025;9:7. Medical University of Graz. PRISMA, 6 databases.
- MINORS bias scoring for nonrandomized studies.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR focused specifically on patient-reported outcomes for zirconia implants.
- Documents consistent PRO improvement from pretreatment to prosthetic delivery.
- Flags lack of standardized PROM instruments as the main evidence weakness.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PRISMA systematic review; 596 records → 189 screened → 12 controlled clinical trials included.
- Extracted PROMs, follow-up, survival, implant system, MINORS bias score, evidence level. English-only, controlled trials.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Most PROMs (aesthetics, speaking, comfort, chewing ability, general satisfaction) significantly improved at prosthetic delivery vs pretreatment.
- High patient satisfaction overall.
- High heterogeneity across PROM instruments precluded pooling.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Few controlled studies; heterogeneous, non-validated PROM tools; possible publication/selection bias; no meta-analysis. Need standardized zirconia-implant PROMs.

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- PRO: patient-reported outcome. PROM: patient-reported outcome measure. MINORS: Methodological Index for Non-Randomized Studies.
