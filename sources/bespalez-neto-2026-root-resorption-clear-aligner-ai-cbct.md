## Why Ingested

투명교정 대 고정교정 후 치근 흡수를 AI 기반 CBCT 3D 표면 모델로 정량화한 RCT로, 교정 치료의 치근 흡수 위험에 대한 정밀 근거를 제공한다. 기존 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/ronchi-lemos-2026-adverse-effects-orthodontic-aligners-sr]]의 메타분석 결과를 3D 정량화로 보강.

## Three-line Summary

This RCT compared external apical root resorption (EARR) on incisors after non-extraction treatment of Class I malocclusion using clear aligners (n=15) vs fixed appliances (n=17) with novel 3D CBCT surface analysis. Overall median EARR was −0.72 mm with no significant difference between groups; upper lateral incisors showed significantly greater EARR than lower incisors (p=0.002) and upper central incisors (p<0.001). No significant predictor for EARR was found for age, sex, crowding severity, or treatment duration.

## 세줄요약

비발치 Class I 부정교합 환자에서 투명교정(n=15) 대 고정교정(n=17) 후 전치의 외부 유치단 흡수(EARR)를 AI 기반 CBCT 3D 표면 분석으로 비교한 RCT. 전체 중앙값 EARR는 −0.72mm로 두 그룹 간 유의한 차이 없었음; 상악 측절치가 하악 전치(p=0.002) 및 상악 중절치(p<0.001)보다 유의하게 더 큰 EARR를 보임. 나이·성별·혼잡도·치료 기간은 EARR의 유의한 예측인이 아니었음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Novel 3D AI-based CBCT surface analysis methodology for EARR quantification
- First RCT using this methodology to compare CA vs fixed appliances
- Upper lateral incisors identified as most susceptible to EARR
- No appliance-type effect on EARR magnitude

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial
- **n**: 32 adults (CA: 15, fixed: 17), mean age 22.3y
- **Treatment duration**: Mean 24.2 months
- **Malocclusion**: Class I with moderate crowding (Little's Index 4.76 mm)
- **Outcomes**: 3D EARR via CBCT surface models (AI-based)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall median EARR: −0.72 mm
- CA: −0.71 mm; Fixed: −0.72 mm (NS between groups)
- Upper lateral incisors: significantly greater EARR (p=0.002 vs lower, p<0.001 vs upper central)
- No significant predictor: age, sex, crowding severity, treatment duration

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=32)
- Non-extraction cases only
- Single-center study
- 3D analysis methodology requires specialized software
- Long-term stability of results unknown

## 7. Glossary
- **EARR (External Apical Root Resorption)**: 외부 유치단 흡수 (pathological shortening of root apex)
- **CBCT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography)**: 원추형 빔 전산화 단층촬영
- **Little's Index**: 하악 전치 혼잡도 측정 지수
- **3D Surface Model**: AI로 추출한 3D 표면 모델 (for volumetric analysis)
