## Why Ingested

"Modified periosteal releasing incision (MPRI)" appears as a named comparator flap-advancement technique in [[suture-wound-closure/raabe-2025-impact-two-flap-advancement-techniques]] (MPRI vs. MDT, ex vivo GBR graft-displacement study) and is quantified as the largest single flap-advancement contributor in [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]]'s taxonomy — but neither page originates or defines the term. This paper is the plausible technique/update source for MPRI, filling that definitional gap and adding a mental-nerve-visualization safety rationale (posterior mandible) that neither existing page addresses.

## Three-line Summary

Technique-description/update paper (abstract-only; no reported patient cohort, n, or outcome data) on the "modified periosteal releasing incision" (MPRI) — a trauma-reduced variant of the standard periosteal releasing incision (PRI) used to achieve tension-free primary closure during flap advancement in periodontal/GBR surgery.

The paper updates the MPRI technique to emphasize visualizing the mental nerve during significant flap advancement in the posterior mandible, positioning MPRI as a safer alternative to traditional PRI (which "may necessitate deeper or more incisions" and risks edema, bleeding, paresthesia, and discomfort).

No efficacy or outcome data are reported in the abstract; the authors themselves state "future research is needed to explore its benefits and applications in various clinical scenarios," so this page should be read as a technique-rationale source, not evidence of clinical superiority.

## 7. Glossary

- **MPRI (Modified Periosteal Releasing Incision)**: a technique-modified variant of the periosteal releasing incision designed to minimize trauma into the submucosa relative to the traditional/standard PRI, while still achieving sufficient flap advancement for tension-free primary closure.
- **PRI (Periosteal Releasing Incision)**: an incision through the periosteum (the inelastic layer deep to a full-thickness mucosal flap) that releases the flap, enabling coronal advancement; the single largest contributor to flap-advancement magnitude in GBR per [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]].
- **Mental nerve**: a sensory terminal branch of the inferior alveolar nerve, exiting the mandible at the mental foramen (typically near the mandibular premolars), supplying sensation to the lower lip and chin; at risk of stretch/transection injury (paresthesia) during posterior-mandible flap elevation and advancement.
- **Primary closure**: wound closure achieved without residual gap or tension along the incision line immediately after a procedure — a prerequisite (per the PASS principle) for uneventful GBR/periodontal-surgery healing.
