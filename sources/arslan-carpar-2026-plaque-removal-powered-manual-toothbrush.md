## Why Ingested

투명교정 부착물(attachment) 주변의 플라크 제거 효율에 대한 수동·전동칫솔 비교 RCT로, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/raghavan-2023-wsls-plaque-clear-aligners-fixed]]가 보고한 CA의 치주위생 우위를 환자 자가관리(칫솔 선택) 측면에서 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

This single-blind RCT compared manual vs powered toothbrush plaque removal around clear aligner attachments in 14 adults (244 teeth). Both groups showed significant plaque reduction from baseline (p<0.05), but no significant differences between toothbrush types were found across any region or surface (all p>0.05). Distal and gingival surfaces had higher plaque levels, while occlusal surfaces had lower levels (p<0.001).

## 세줄요약

투명교정 부착물 주변에서 수동칫솔 대 전동칫솔의 플라크 제거 효율을 비교한 단일 맹검 RCT로, 14명 성인(244치아) 대상. 두 그룹 모두 기저 대비 유의한 플라크 감소(p<0.05)를 보였으나, 칫솔 유형 간 유의한 차이는 없었음(모든 p>0.05). 원심면·치은면에서 플라크 수준 높고(편심·근심), 교합면에서 낮았음(p<0.001).

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health 2026;26:1100
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-026-08395-3
- **Institution**: Mersin University, Turkey

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT comparing manual vs powered toothbrushes specifically around clear aligner attachments
- Demonstrates plaque removal depends more on brushing technique/surface accessibility than toothbrush type
- Identifies non-uniform plaque distribution patterns around attachments

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Single-center, single-blind RCT
- **n**: 14 adults (7 per group), 244 teeth with attachments
- **Outcomes**: Modified Plaque Index (MPI) on 4 surfaces per attachment (T0→T1)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Plaque reduction: both groups significant (p<0.05)
- Manual vs powered: no significant difference (all p>0.05)
- Surface distribution: distal/gingival > mesial > occlusal (p<0.001)
- Attachment type: no significant influence on plaque reduction
- Clinical response rates: comparable between groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=14)
- Single-brushing conditions only (not longitudinal)
- Standardized conditions may not reflect real-world brushing
- Limited to clear aligner patients with >6 months treatment
