## Why Ingested

기존 [[tmj/ferland-2026-patient-education-self-management-tmd-sr-ma]]와 [[tmj/cimino-2025-counselling-vs-splint-tmd-neck-pain-rct]]는 통증·삶의질 결과를 다루지만, 운동공포(kinesiophobia)·통증 파국화(pain catastrophizing) 같은 심리사회적 매개 변수에 대한 보존 치료 효과는 위키에 없었다. 이 SR이 그 공백을 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis; 12 studies, 815 participants, mean age 42.2 y, 85% female, mostly myofascial/pain-related TMD) of non-pharmacological conservative interventions on kinesiophobia and pain catastrophizing.

Cognitive behavioural therapy, pain neuroscience education plus exercise, and manual therapy may reduce pain catastrophizing, and pain neuroscience education and manual therapy may improve kinesiophobia (all low to very low certainty); hypnosis and occlusal splinting appeared ineffective for catastrophizing (very low certainty).

Psychologically informed and education-based care look promising for the psychosocial layer of TMD, but certainty is low to very low, so the findings are hypothesis-generating.

## 세줄요약

성인 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)에서 비약물 보존 치료가 운동공포 (Kinesiophobia)와 통증 파국화 (Pain Catastrophizing)에 미치는 효과를 본 체계적 문헌고찰 (메타분석 없음; 12편, 815명, 평균 42.2세, 여성 85%, 대부분 근막·통증형 TMD).

인지행동치료 (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, CBT), 통증 신경과학 교육 (Pain Neuroscience Education, PNE)+운동, 수기치료가 통증 파국화를, PNE와 수기치료가 운동공포를 줄일 수 있음 (확신도 낮음~매우 낮음); 최면과 교합안정장치는 파국화 감소에 효과 없어 보임 (매우 낮음).

TMD의 심리사회적 층위에는 교육·심리 기반 치료가 유망하나 근거 확신도가 낮아 가설 생성 수준이다.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: Musculoskeletal Science and Practice 2025;77:103328 (published 2025-04-08), PubMed PMID 40233599, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2025.103328)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes conservative-intervention effects on two psychosocial mediators (kinesiophobia, pain catastrophizing) rather than pain intensity.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Searched PubMed, Embase, CINAHL, Cochrane Central; interventional studies in adults with diagnosed TMD.
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2; narrative synthesis by outcome and intervention; certainty by modified GRADE.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 12 studies, 815 participants (mean age 42.2 y, 85% female).
- Interventions: CBT, PNE +/- exercise, manual therapy, occlusal splinting, hypnosis.
- Pain catastrophizing: CBT, PNE plus exercise, manual therapy may reduce (low to very low confidence).
- Kinesiophobia: PNE and manual therapy may improve (low to very low confidence).
- Hypnosis and occlusal splinting: ineffective for catastrophizing vs other conservative interventions (very low confidence).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract only; per-study effect sizes, RoB distribution, and timepoints not available to us.
- No meta-analysis; low to very low certainty; authors call for higher-quality research.

## 7. Glossary

- Kinesiophobia: fear of movement due to anticipated pain or reinjury.
- Pain catastrophizing: exaggerated negative cognitive-emotional response to actual or anticipated pain.
