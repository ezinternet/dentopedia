## Why Ingested

4-year follow-up of the Diehl 2022 (2-year) case-control study of 3.3mm TiZr NDIs in T2DM vs normoglycemic patients; extends the medium-term evidence base showing non-significant MBL differences, maintaining the conclusion that tissue-level NDIs are predictable in poorly-controlled diabetics.

→ [[implants/isq/diehl-2022-narrow-diameter-implant-stability-hyperglycemic]]

## Three-line Summary

4-year follow-up case-control study (11 T2DM [HbA1c >6.5%] vs 15 normoglycemic [HbA1c <6.0%]) of 3.3mm tissue-level TiZr (Roxolid/SLActive) NDIs in posterior maxilla/mandible; Witten/Herdecke University, Germany; originally 32 patients, 26 reached 48 months.

Overall survival 100% for both groups at 48 months; all clinical parameters (PD, CAL, BOP, PBI, recession) and radiographic ΔMBL (T2DM 0.09±0.89mm vs control 0.15±0.83mm, p=0.875) showed non-significant differences between diabetic and normoglycemic patients.

Tissue-level 3.3mm TiZr NDIs demonstrate equivalent peri-implant biological response in T2DM (HbA1c >6.5%) and healthy patients at 4 years, supporting their use as a minimal-invasive alternative to augmentation in posterior zones with reduced ridge width.

## 세줄요약

4년 추적 케이스-컨트롤 연구 — T2DM군 (당뇨병 2형, HbA1c >6.5%, 11명) vs 정상혈당군 (HbA1c <6.0%, 15명); 3.3mm 조직수준 티타늄-지르코늄 (TiZr) 협경임플란트 (Roxolid/SLActive), 후방부 배치; 독일 Witten/Herdecke 대학; 원래 32명 중 26명 48개월 도달.

48개월 기준 전체 임플란트 생존율 (Implant Survival Rate) 100%; 탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD), 임상적부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL), 탐침출혈 (BOP), 변연골소실 (MBL) 모두 두 군 간 통계적 유의차 없음 (ΔMBL p=0.875).

3.3mm 조직수준 TiZr 협경임플란트는 혈당 불량 당뇨 환자에서도 4년간 정상혈당군과 동등한 임플란트주위 생물학적 반응을 보여, 수평 골폭 부족 시 증대술 회피를 위한 최소침습 대안으로 예측 가능한 치료 옵션임을 지지한다.

## Key Contributions

- Extends the Diehl 2022 (2-year) dataset to 4 years for the same NDI cohort
- Maintains 100% survival and non-significant MBL differences at 48 months (ΔMBL 0.09 vs 0.15mm)
- Confirms no technical complications at 4-year timepoint
- Notes that MBL trajectory showed no significant progression from 12 months to 48 months
- Identifies tissue-level implant-abutment connection as potentially protective for crestal bone (supracrestal connection, fixed biologic width)

## Methodology

- **Design**: 4-year follow-up of a prospective case-control study
- **N**: 26 patients (11 T2DM, 15 normoglycemic) at 4-year follow-up; originally 32 enrolled
- **Implant**: 3.3mm tissue-level TiZr (Roxolid®, SLActive®, Institut Straumann AG); 29 implants total (13 T2DM, 16 control)
- **T2DM group**: HbA1c >6.5% (mean 7.35 ±0.71 at 4yr), uncontrolled T2DM
- **Control group**: HbA1c ≤6.0%, non-diabetic
- **Primary outcome**: Radiographic MBL (ΔMBL from baseline to 48 months); secondary: PD, CAL, BOP, PBI, recession
- **Assessment timepoints**: T1=24 months, T2=48 months
- **Examiner**: Single independent calibrated investigator (A.B.) for clinical; M.S.Y. for radiographic (ImageJ2)
- **Statistics**: Student's t-test; Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality test; α=0.05

## Key Results

| Parameter | T2DM | Control | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival at 48 months | 100% | 100% | — |
| ΔMBL T2 (mm, mean ±SD) | 0.09 ±0.89 | 0.15 ±0.83 | 0.875 (NS) |
| PD at T2 (mm) | 2.3 ±0.77 | 2.3 ±0.88 | 0.987 (NS) |
| PD at T1 (mm) | 2.4 ±0.55 | 2.3 ±0.71 | 0.959 (NS) |
| Recession T2 (mm) | 0.91 ±0.94 | 0.73 ±0.96 | 0.880 (NS) |
| BOP (% per group) | 45% | 60% | 0.144 (NS) |
| PBI (mean ±SD) | 0.7 ±1.01 | 0.7 ±0.79 | 0.998 (NS) |
| Technical complications | None | None | — |

- One patient in T2DM group lost a non-study 3.3×8mm implant at 24 months (not counted in primary analysis)
- Mean HbA1c at follow-up increased to 7.35±0.71 due to dropout bias toward better-controlled patients exiting
- No further MBL progression from 12-month data to 4-year T2: stable crestal bone level plateau

## Limitations

- Small sample (26 patients); power analysis indicates ≥1000 patients needed for significance
- Significant dropout in T2DM group (16→11; HbA1c selection bias toward higher-HbA1c completers)
- Pilot study design: no a-priori sample size calculation
- Heterogeneous restoration type (screw- vs cement-retained) may introduce MBL variation, though prior literature shows no significant MBL differences
- TL implant-abutment design — results may not generalise to bone-level implants with platform-switching

## Glossary

- **NDI (Narrow-Diameter Implant)**: Implants with diameter <3.5 mm and >2 mm per Schiegnitz/Al-Nawas classification
- **Roxolid**: Straumann TiZr alloy (15% Zr), higher strength than grade-4 Ti, enables 3.3mm diameter
- **SLActive**: Straumann hydrophilic sandblasted acid-etched surface; accelerates early osseointegration
- **ΔMBL**: Change in marginal bone level from baseline to follow-up, measured radiographically
- **TL (Tissue Level)**: Implant with transmucosal collar; abutment connection placed supracrestally, avoiding crestal bone remodeling around the connection
