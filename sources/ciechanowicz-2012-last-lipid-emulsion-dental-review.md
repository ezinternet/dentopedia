## Three-line Summary

Dental-targeted review (Br Dent J 2012;212(5):237-41) on local anesthetic systemic toxicity (LAST) in dentistry and the evidence for intravenous lipid emulsion as rescue therapy, written when awareness of ILE therapy was just emerging among dental practitioners.

Covers amide LA cardiac toxicity pharmacology, high-risk patient identification, early signs/symptoms, preventive measures, and initial LAST management with ILE; notes that LAST accounts for a high proportion of dental office mortalities, and that LA-induced cardiac arrest is particularly resistant to standard resuscitation.

Abstract-only; a foundational early dental-specific LAST + ILE review predating current ASRA/AHA guidelines.

## 세줄요약

치과 특화 LAST + ILE 리뷰 (Br Dent J 2012, 237-41) — ILE 인지도가 막 형성되던 시기, 치과의사를 위한 LAST 병인·예방·초기관리 지침.

아미드 국소마취제 심근 독성 약리, 고위험 환자 인식, 전구증상, 예방, ILE 구제치료 근거; LAST는 치과 진료실 사망의 주요 원인이며 LA 유발 심정지는 표준 심폐소생술에 저항적임.

abstract-only; 현행 ASRA/AHA 가이드라인 이전의 기초 참고문헌.
