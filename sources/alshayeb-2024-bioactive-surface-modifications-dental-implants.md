## Why Ingested

This is the general-purpose SR+MA our surface-treatment synthesis was missing: it pools 9 in-vivo animal studies of bioactive surface modifications (collagen, bioactive peptides, BMP-2 combinations, rhBMP-2/PLGA) against unmodified titanium controls and quantifies the BIC gain directly (MD=7.29, 95% CI 2.94–11.65), giving [[overviews/implant-surface-comparison]] a pooled-effect anchor for the "bioactive coating → BIC" claim that previously rested on single animal studies like [[implants/surface/yamada-2012-nanopolymorphic-hydroxyapatite-titanium-implant]] and [[implants/surface/bonfante-2012-calcium-phosphate-coatings-plasma-spray]]. The paper's own Discussion explicitly cross-references Lopez-Valverde et al.'s biomolecule-coating SR/MA (a sibling paper in this same ingest batch) — both converge on BMP-based coatings as the most favorable, though Lopez-Valverde reports far higher heterogeneity (I²=99%) than this review's moderate I²=50%/47%.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review + meta-analysis (RevMan 5.4.1, random-effects) of 9 in-vivo animal studies (rat/dog/pig/rabbit, 421 screened → 9 included) comparing bioactive-surface-modified titanium dental implants (collagen, bioactive peptides, BMP-2/HA, rhBMP-2/PLGA) against unmodified titanium controls over a 30-day observation window.
Bioactive surface modification significantly improved both bone-to-implant contact (BIC: MD=7.29, 95% CI 2.94–11.65, I²=50%, Z=3.28, p=0.001) and implant longevity (MD=7.52, 95% CI 3.18–11.85, I²=47%, Z=3.40, p=0.0007) versus unmodified surfaces.
Collagen-based coatings consistently promoted early bone integration and BMP-2 combinations were most effective for osseointegration, but the evidence base is animal-only with a 30-day follow-up ceiling, so human long-term translation remains unvalidated.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Investigations 2024;28(11):592
- **DOI**: 10.1007/s00784-024-05958-y
- **Institution**: multi-institutional (authors span UK, Italy — Marrapodi/Cicciù/Minervini; Elfadil/Abutayyem/Shqaidef dental faculties)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First quantitative pooled meta-analysis (not just narrative) of bioactive surface modification vs unmodified titanium across both BIC and implant longevity outcomes in a unified PECO framework
- Identifies collagen-based coatings as consistently favorable for early bone integration across independent research groups (Bae 2018, Cho W 2021)
- Identifies BMP-2 combinations (with HA/HAP) as effective for activating osseointegration (Pang 2021), while flagging protein-delivery dosage/repeat-application challenges vs gene-delivery alternatives (Yoo 2015)
- Direct cross-study comparison against Lopez-Valverde et al.'s biomolecule-coating SR/MA — both converge on BMP favorability, but this review's heterogeneity (I²=50%/47%) is far lower than Lopez-Valverde's I²=99%

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA (PRISMA-guided, PECO framework), SYRCLE risk-of-bias tool (adapted for in-vivo animal studies)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar
- **n**: 473 records → 421 after dedup → 375 sought full-text → 9 studies included (Bae 2018, Barros 2009, Cardoso 2017, Cecconi 2014, Cho 2019, Cho W 2021, Lutz 2010, Pang 2021, Yoo 2015)
- **Outcomes**: primary — osseointegration (BIC, bone volume density/BVD, histomorphometry); secondary — implant longevity (survival beyond 30 days, a rodent-lifespan-anchored milestone)
- **Statistics**: RevMan 5.4.1, random-effects model, forest plots with MD and 95% CI

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | MD | 95% CI | Tau² | Chi² (df) | I² | Z | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BIC | 7.29 | 2.94–11.65 | 18.57 | 16.08 (8) | 50% | 3.28 | 0.001 |
| Longevity (30-day) | 7.52 | 3.18–11.85 | 17.28 | 14.95 (8) | 47% | 3.40 | 0.0007 |

Included studies span rat tibia (Bae, 84wk), dog mandible (Barros, 8wk), pig parietal bone (Cardoso, 4/8wk), rabbit femur (Cecconi, 7wk), rabbit tibia (Cho, 2wk), dog jaw (Cho W, 8wk), pig forehead (Lutz, 2/4wk), rabbit tibia (Pang, 4wk; BMP-2+HA), rabbit tibia (Yoo, 3/7wk; rhBMP-2/PLGA). Bias assessment (SYRCLE-adapted): Barros, Lutz, Pang = low overall bias; Bae, Cardoso, Cho, Yoo = moderate; Cecconi, Cho W = low overall but high in specific domains (introduction/ethics, introduction/outcome measures respectively).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Moderate heterogeneity (I²=50% BIC, 47% longevity) reflects variability in study design, surface-modification technique, and animal model
- Small sample sizes in several included studies limit robustness/generalizability
- 30-day follow-up ceiling does not capture long-term bioactive-coating performance
- Measurement/reporting-standard variation across studies complicated data standardization for pooling
- Animal-only evidence base — authors explicitly flag the need for human clinical trials before translation
- Heterogeneous mix of bioactive agents (collagen, peptides, BMP-2 combinations) makes agent-specific efficacy conclusions harder to isolate despite the overall pooled significance

## 6. Related Work
- Lopez-Valverde et al. (biomolecule-based bioactive surfaces SR/MA, cited in-text, sibling ingest — not typed-edged per batch rule): both find BMP-based coatings most favorable; this review's heterogeneity (I²=50%/47%) is far lower than Lopez-Valverde's I²=99%
- Meng et al. (narrative review of biologically active implant surfaces): consistent with this review's BMP-2 early-healing findings
- Kligman et al., Han et al. (broader implant-surface-modification reviews): complementary — cover physical/chemical/biological techniques and biofilm-attachment angles this review does not focus on
- alshayeb-2024 vs [[implants/surface/yamada-2012-nanopolymorphic-hydroxyapatite-titanium-implant]]: yamada is a single animal study (nano-HA coating, rat femur) showing BIC gain confined to ≤50 µm; alshayeb's pooled MD=7.29 gives independent multi-study quantitative support to the same "bioactive coating raises BIC" direction, via different (non-HA-only) bioactive agents
- alshayeb-2024 vs [[implants/surface/bonfante-2012-calcium-phosphate-coatings-plasma-spray]]: bonfante compares two bioactive CaP coating *sub-types* (PSHA vs ACP) and finds BIC equivalence between them — a narrower, non-conflicting question (coating-type-within-bioactive) distinct from alshayeb's bioactive-vs-non-bioactive comparison

## 7. Glossary
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: histomorphometric percentage of implant surface in direct contact with bone, primary osseointegration outcome measure
- **BVD (Bone Volume Density)**: proportion of bone volume within a defined peri-implant region
- **PECO**: Population, Exposure, Comparator, Outcome — the review's structured eligibility framework (animal analogue of PICO)
- **SYRCLE Risk of Bias tool**: bias-assessment instrument originally developed for animal experimentation, adapted here for in-vivo bias appraisal
- **rhBMP-2/PLGA**: recombinant human bone morphogenetic protein-2 delivered via poly(lactic-co-glycolic acid) carrier scaffold
- **Implant longevity (this review's definition)**: survival beyond 30 days post-placement, a milestone anchored to rodent lifespan norms (18–24 month median lifespan)
