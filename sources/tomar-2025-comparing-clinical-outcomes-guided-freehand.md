## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA + PROSPERO) of 13 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Google Scholar, Cochrane; Jan 2000–Jul 2025) comparing guided (static or dynamic) vs freehand dental implant placement on accuracy, marginal bone loss (MBL), survival, and complications; AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal, descriptive synthesis (no new meta-analysis).

Guided/computer-assisted placement showed better accuracy than freehand (SMD for coronal deviation −0.97; 95% CI −2.27 to −0.32; P<.01); MBL and implant survival were comparable between techniques (RR 1.02; 95% CI 0.98–1.06; P=.28), both remaining high, while complication rates were low with a non-significant trend toward lower iatrogenic risk with guided techniques (RR 0.88; 95% CI 0.74–1.04; P=.11).

Guided surgery improves placement accuracy and may reduce surgical risk in complex cases, but freehand placement remains reliable with comparable long-term outcomes in experienced hands — selection should weigh case complexity, clinician expertise, and resources; **abstract-only, full text not retrieved (paywalled)**.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(Umbrella Review, PRISMA·PROSPERO 등록) — SR/MA 13편 종합(PubMed·Scopus·Embase·Google Scholar·Cochrane, 2000년 1월~2025년 7월), 가이드(정적·동적) 대 프리핸드(Freehand) 임플란트 식립을 정확도·변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)·생존율·합병증으로 비교, AMSTAR-2 품질평가·기술적 종합(신규 메타분석 없음).

가이드/컴퓨터보조 식립이 프리핸드보다 정확도 우수(관상부 편차 표준화평균차, Standardized Mean Difference, SMD −0.97; 95% 신뢰구간 −2.27~−0.32; P<.01); MBL·임플란트 생존율은 기법 간 유의차 없음(위험비, Risk Ratio, RR 1.02; 95% CI 0.98–1.06; P=.28)이며 둘 다 높은 수준 유지, 합병증률은 낮고 가이드 기법에서 의인성 위험이 낮아지는 경향(RR 0.88; 95% CI 0.74–1.04; P=.11)이 있으나 유의하지 않음.

가이드 수술은 식립 정확도를 개선하고 복잡 증례에서 수술 위험을 줄일 수 있으나, 숙련된 술자의 프리핸드 식립도 장기 결과가 비슷하게 신뢰할 만함 — 증례 난이도·술자 숙련도·가용 자원을 고려해 선택; **초록만 확보(abstract-only), 전문 미확보(paywall)**.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Umbrella-level synthesis of 13 SRs/MAs comparing guided vs freehand implant placement, spanning accuracy, MBL, survival, and complications in one PRISMA/PROSPERO-registered review.
- Reports a coronal-deviation accuracy advantage for guided/computer-assisted surgery (SMD −0.97) while explicitly finding MBL and survival statistically comparable between guided and freehand techniques (RR 1.02, P=.28).
- Frames selection between guided and freehand as a function of case complexity, clinician expertise, and resource availability rather than a uniform recommendation for either technique.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (JBI-style summarizing-systematic-reviews methodology), PRISMA 2020 reporting, PROSPERO-registered.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library.
- **n**: 13 included systematic reviews (individual review-level n of studies/patients/implants not extractable from the abstract).
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR-2.
- **Synthesis**: Effect sizes and statistical outcomes extracted directly from the included meta-analyses; results synthesized descriptively without conducting a new pooled meta-analysis.
- **Outcomes**: Implant placement accuracy (coronal/apical/angular deviation), marginal bone loss, implant survival, complications.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingestion (paywalled); individual-review data, AMSTAR-2 scores, heterogeneity (I²), and forest plots not available.
- Umbrella review inherits limitations of its 13 constituent SRs (primary-study overlap across reviews is a known umbrella-review risk not detailed in the abstract).
- **Data-integrity note**: the reported coronal-deviation SMD (−0.97, 95% CI −2.27 to −0.32, P<.01) is numerically identical to the pooled coronal-deviation statistic reported by [[digital-workflow/mehta-2025-accuracy-assessment-robot-assisted-dental]], a different umbrella review (different DOI, different comparison — robot-assisted vs comparators) published four months earlier in the same journal. Flagged for future verification once full text becomes available; treat this specific figure with caution pending clarification.
- Authors call for future work to reconcile which patient/case factors (complexity, clinician expertise, resources) should drive the guided-vs-freehand decision — this remains descriptive/qualitative in the abstract, not quantified.

## 6. Related Work

- shirani-2026: SR+MA (9 RCTs) finding no significant CAIS-vs-FHIP difference in overall MBL/survival — this umbrella review's comparable finding (RR 1.02, P=.28) reinforces that conclusion at a higher evidence tier.
- mehta-2025: Umbrella review of robot-assisted implant accuracy reporting a pooled coronal-deviation SMD identical to this paper's guided-vs-freehand coronal SMD — flagged as a data-integrity question above.
- schiavon-2025 / nava-2026: Accuracy-domain NMAs establishing that guided surgery is more accurate than freehand; this umbrella review corroborates the accuracy direction while adding that accuracy does not extend to MBL/survival superiority.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAIS**: Computer-Assisted Implant Surgery (static or dynamic navigation-guided implant placement)
- **FHIP / Freehand**: Freehand Implant Placement, without a surgical guide or navigation system
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss
- **SMD**: Standardized Mean Difference
- **RR**: Risk Ratio
- **AMSTAR-2**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews, version 2
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews
