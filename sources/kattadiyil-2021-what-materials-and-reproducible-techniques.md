## Three-line Summary

Best Evidence Consensus Statement (American College of Prosthodontists, J Prosthodont 2021;30(S1):34-42) evaluating centric-relation (CR) recording techniques and materials in dentate and partially dentate populations.

From >3500 initial hits, limiting to systematic reviews, randomized controlled studies, meta-analyses and clinical trials selected 291 articles for further analysis; the abstract reports no pooled effect estimates.

Chin-point guidance, bimanual manipulation, power centric, Gothic arch tracing, leaf gauge and anterior deprogramming can be comparably precise and clinically accurate; operator experience is critical, polyvinyl siloxane/polyether perform best, and virtual capture needs further study.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames the clinical question as two focus questions: (1) which techniques are used and how reproducible they are for recording CR in dentate and partially dentate populations, and (2) what effect different recording materials have on CR reproducibility.
- States that six techniques — chin-point guidance, bimanual manipulation, power centric, Gothic arch tracing, leaf gauge, and anterior deprogramming devices — **can all be comparable in precision and clinical accuracy** with respect to clinical relevance.
- Elevates **practitioner experience and familiarity with a given technique** as critical to accuracy when recording CR.
- Identifies **polyvinyl siloxane and polyether** as consistently better performers across the broad range of recording-material studies.
- Flags **virtual capture of CR** as a potentially comparable recording medium that nonetheless requires further clinical study.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Consensus-statement process built on a keyword literature search rather than a new clinical experiment.
- Initial search keywords: CR, interocclusal records, CR accuracy, CR reproducibility, and CR technique.
- Search restricted to Systematic Reviews, Randomized Controlled Studies, Meta-analyses, and Clinical Trials.
- No detail on consensus voting/Delphi rounds, number of reviewers, quality-appraisal instruments, or data-extraction procedure is available in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Initial search produced **more than 3500 articles**.
- After restriction to systematic reviews, randomized controlled studies, meta-analyses and clinical trials, **291 articles** were selected for further analysis.
- **Techniques**: chin point guidance, bimanual manipulation, power centric, Gothic arch tracing, leaf gauge and anterior deprogramming devices "can all be comparable in precision and clinical accuracy in regards to clinical relevance."
- **Operator factor**: practitioner experience and familiarity with a particular technique is critical for accuracy.
- **Materials**: polyvinyl siloxane and polyether "consistently performed better in the broad range of studies on recording materials."
- **Digital**: virtual capture of CR could serve as a comparable recording medium but requires further clinical study.
- No quantitative effect sizes, precision values, or material-performance magnitudes are reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **abstract-only — full text not retrieved**: only the PubMed abstract was available (no PMCID), so the evidence-appraisal method, consensus process, and any quantitative comparisons in the full paper are not verifiable here.
- The stated focus questions cover **dentate and partially dentate** populations; edentulous patients are not the stated population, limiting direct transfer to complete-denture prosthodontics.

## 7. Glossary

- **Centric relation (CR)**: maxillomandibular reference position used as a reproducible prosthetic/occlusal reference; here the position being recorded.
- **Interocclusal record**: registration of the jaw relation between upper and lower arches, made with a recording material.
- **Bimanual manipulation**: two-handed guided technique for manipulating the mandible into CR.
- **Chin-point guidance**: guided technique using posterior pressure at the chin to seat the mandible into CR.
- **Power centric**: a guidance technique using a posteriorly directed force to record CR.
- **Gothic arch tracing**: graphic method whose arrowhead apex marks the CR position.
- **Leaf gauge**: an anterior deprogramming/interocclusal device used to disengage posterior teeth and facilitate CR registration.
- **Anterior deprogramming device**: appliance that removes posterior proprioceptive guidance to permit a repeatable CR record.
- **Polyvinyl siloxane (PVS)**: elastomeric impression/recording material reported here as consistently performing well.
- **Polyether**: elastomeric recording material reported here as consistently performing well.
- **Virtual capture**: digital acquisition of the CR record; described as comparable but requiring further clinical study.
