## Why Ingested

The `implants/mbl` folder asks what drives early crestal bone loss, and this Oh et al. 2002 review ranks six hypothesized causes. It is the earliest source in the wiki that sets out the competing etiologies (surgical trauma, occlusal overload, peri-implantitis, microgap, biologic width, crest module), and later MBL papers should be read against it. Related wiki pages: [[implants/mbl/mbl]], [[overviews/implant-failure-mbl-risk-factors-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Review (Journal of Periodontology, 2002) of six hypothesized causes of early crestal bone loss around osseointegrated endosteal implants — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The authors judge biologic-width reformation, a microgap at or below the crest, occlusal overload and crest module the most likely causes, while surgical trauma and peri-implantitis may also contribute.

Causation is unresolved in the abstract; the authors call for randomized trials comparing the plausible factors, and no effect sizes are given.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Structured list of six early-bone-loss etiologies.
- Ranking of the most plausible causes based on the literature at the time.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review of six candidate factors; no systematic search reported in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Qualitative ranking; the abstract cites typical bone loss of ≤0.2 mm per year after the first year, without its own study data.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative, 2002; subsequent MBL literature has moved on (see the wiki's MBL pages).
- Full text not retrieved.
- Requests randomized comparisons of each factor.

## 7. Glossary

- **Biologic width**: soft-tissue attachment dimension above the bone. **Microgap**: space between implant and abutment at the crest.
