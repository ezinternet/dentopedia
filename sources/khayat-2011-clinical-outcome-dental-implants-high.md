## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study (48 patients, 66 Zimmer Tapered Screw-Vent 4.5 mm implants, non-submerged healing) comparing marginal bone levels of implants placed at maximum insertion torque (MIT) >70 Ncm (n=42; mean 110.6, range 70.8–176 Ncm) versus 30–50 Ncm (n=9; mean 37.1 Ncm), recorded electronically.

All implants were clinically stable after 2–3 months; mean marginal bone resorption was 0.72 mm (high torque) vs 1.03 mm (control) at loading and 1.24 mm vs 1.09 mm at 1 year, with no significant between-group difference in bone stability or implant success.

High torque up to 176 Ncm did not prevent osseointegration in the short term, but the evidence is weak: a 9-implant control arm, torque groups formed post hoc (not assigned), one implant system, and only 1 year of follow-up — too short to see the multi-year crestal harm signal reported elsewhere.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상연구 (환자 48명, Zimmer Tapered Screw-Vent 직경 4.5 mm 임플란트 66개, 비매몰 치유): 전자식으로 기록한 최대 삽입 토크 (Maximum Insertion Torque, MIT) >70 Ncm군(42개; 평균 110.6, 범위 70.8–176 Ncm)과 30–50 Ncm 대조군(9개; 평균 37.1 Ncm)의 변연골 수준 비교.

2–3개월 치유 후 전 임플란트 임상적으로 안정; 평균 변연골 흡수 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)는 부하 시 고토크 0.72 mm vs 대조 1.03 mm, 1년 후 1.24 mm vs 1.09 mm — 골 안정성·임플란트 성공률 모두 군간 유의차 없음.

최대 176 Ncm의 고삽입토크 (High Insertion Torque, HIT)도 단기 골유착 (Osseointegration)을 막지 않았으나 근거는 약하다: 대조군 9개, 토크군은 사후 분류(배정 아님), 단일 시스템, 1년 추적 — 다른 연구가 보고한 수년 단위 치조정 해악 신호를 보기엔 너무 짧다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Human clinical data at the **upper extreme of reported insertion torque**: 42 implants >70 Ncm, mean 110.6 Ncm, maximum 176 Ncm — well above the ≥50 Ncm cutoff used by later SR/MA and RCT work
- Directly tests the osteocyte-necrosis / microcirculation-disturbance hypothesis clinically: all implants osseointegrated after 2–3 months of non-submerged healing
- Reports group-by-timepoint MBL (loading and 1 year), showing no significant between-group difference in bone stability or success rate
- Objective torque capture (electronic device, Tohnichi STC200CN) rather than surgeon-estimated ratchet values

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective clinical study; torque groups defined by the recorded MIT, i.e., **post hoc classification, not randomized or assigned**
- **n**: 48 patients / 66 implants; analysed groups: control 9 implants (MIT 30–50 Ncm), experimental 42 implants (MIT >70 Ncm). The remaining 15 implants are not assigned to either group in the abstract (presumably the 50–70 Ncm band — not stated)
- **Implant**: Zimmer Tapered Screw-Vent, 4.5 mm diameter (tapered, placed to achieve primary stability in slightly underprepared sites per the background rationale)
- **Sites**: MeSH lists maxilla and mandible, partially edentulous jaws; per-arch breakdown not reported in abstract
- **Healing**: non-submerged, 2–3 months before loading
- **Outcomes**: clinical stability at loading; marginal bone levels (radiographic) at loading and 1 year after loading; implant success rate

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | n implants | MIT mean (range) | MBL at loading, mm (SD) | MBL at 1 year, mm (SD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Control (low torque) | 9 | 37.1 Ncm (30–50) | 1.03 (0.44) | 1.09 (0.62) |
| Experimental (high torque) | 42 | 110.6 Ncm (70.8–176) | 0.72 (0.56) | 1.24 (0.75) |

- All implants clinically stable after 2–3 months non-submerged healing
- No significant between-group difference in bone stability or implant success rate (exact p-values and success-rate figures not given in abstract)
- **Derived from the reported group means (not an author-reported or tested quantity):** loading → 1-year change was ~0.52 mm in the high-torque group vs ~0.06 mm in the control group. The high-torque group started with less bone loss at loading and ended with numerically more at 1 year. With n=9 controls and SDs of 0.4–0.75 mm this is compatible with noise, but its direction matches the post-loading crestal-harm trajectory reported by longer-follow-up studies.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Small, unbalanced groups** — control arm only 9 implants vs 42 experimental; underpowered to detect a between-group MBL difference of a few tenths of a millimetre
- **No randomization / no assignment of torque** — MIT is an outcome of site bone density and preparation, so the high-torque group is likely confounded by denser (likely more mandibular/cortical) sites; torque effect cannot be separated from site effect
- **Single implant system** (tapered Zimmer TSV, one diameter) — generalizability to other macrogeometries unknown
- **1-year follow-up only** — too short to capture the multi-year crestal remodeling differences reported at 3 years
- **15 of 66 implants unaccounted for** in the two-group comparison (abstract does not say what happened to them)
- **Abstract-only ingest** — exact p-values, success criteria, per-arch data, dropout, and patient-level clustering handling unavailable

## 6. Related Work
- Lemos 2020 SR+MA [[wiki/implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]]: pooled ≥50 vs <50 Ncm null (MD 0.15 mm) — Khayat is very likely one of its 6 constituent studies (not verifiable from Lemos's abstract); Khayat supplies the extreme-range primary data behind that pooled null
- Trisi 2011 [[wiki/implants/isq/trisi-2011-high-low-implant-torque-histology-sheep]]: sheep histology — no necrosis at 110 Ncm; Khayat independently reaches the consistent clinical conclusion at a nearly identical mean torque (110.6 Ncm) in humans
- Marconcini 2018 [[wiki/implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]]: RCT, ≥50 Ncm → more MBL/recession at 3 years — tension with Khayat's 1-year null; Khayat's own numeric trajectory points the same direction as Marconcini but is not significant
- Aldahlawi 2018 [[wiki/implants/isq/aldahlawi-2018-implant-torque-crestal-bone-remodeling]]: retrospective, >55 Ncm → greater MBL — another harm-side study against which Khayat's null should be read
- Faot 2019 [[wiki/implants/isq/faot-2019-insertion-torque-mandibular-overdenture]]: ≥45 Ncm best survival in narrow implants — the "beneficial/safe" side of the literature alongside Khayat

## 7. Glossary
- **MIT (Maximum Insertion Torque, 최대 삽입 토크)**: peak rotational resistance recorded during implant seating, Ncm
- **IT (Insertion Torque, 삽입 토크)**: torque during implant insertion; "high IT" cutoffs vary (≥50 Ncm in Lemos/Marconcini; >70 Ncm here)
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골 소실)**: radiographic crestal bone resorption around the implant neck
- **Non-submerged healing (비매몰 치유)**: one-stage protocol, implant/healing abutment exposed to the oral cavity during healing
- **Tapered Screw-Vent (TSV)**: Zimmer tapered, threaded implant design; tapered implants in underprepared sites generate higher compression and torque
- **Osteocyte necrosis hypothesis (골세포 괴사 가설)**: proposed mechanism by which excessive compression disturbs microcirculation, killing osteocytes and triggering resorption
