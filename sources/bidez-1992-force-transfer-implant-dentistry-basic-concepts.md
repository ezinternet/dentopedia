## Why Ingested

The implant biomechanics pages in the wiki need a primer on force, moment and stress-strain concepts. This Bidez and Misch 1992 primer is the basic-concept source that the later design and overload sources assume, so it is ingested as the foundational biomechanics reference for `implants`.

## Three-line Summary

Biomechanics primer (Journal of Oral Implantology, 1992) on force transfer from dental implants to surrounding tissues — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The primer covers forces and their components, moments, force-transfer mechanisms, impact, and stress-strain relationships, and links them to implant longevity and restorative planning.

The abstract is a conceptual primer and presents no numeric data or clinical outcomes.
