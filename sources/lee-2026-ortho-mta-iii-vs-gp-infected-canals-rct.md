## Three-line Summary

Parallel-arm RCT, 120 teeth with infected root canals and apical periodontitis, 30-month median follow-up — comparing Ortho MTA III (bioceramic sealer) vs gutta-percha with AH Plus for orthograde obturation.

Success rates were comparable between Ortho MTA III and GP+AH Plus under both loose (88.24% vs 83.02%) and strict (64.71% vs 58.49%) criteria; no statistically significant difference; age, larger periapical lesion, and higher PAI score predicted failure.

Single-center study; wide gap between loose and strict success criteria (88% vs 65%) indicates ongoing incomplete healing in many cases; longer follow-up needed.

## 세줄요약

평행군 RCT, 감염근관 120치 (근단 주위염 동반), 30개월 중앙 추적 — Ortho MTA III (바이오세라믹 실러) vs GP+AH Plus 근관 폐쇄 비교.

완화 기준 성공률 Ortho MTA III 88.24% vs GP+AH Plus 83.02%; 엄격 기준 64.71% vs 58.49%; 두 방법 간 유의차 없음; 고령·큰 근단 병소·높은 PAI가 실패 예측인자.

단일 기관; 완화/엄격 기준 간 큰 격차 (88% vs 65%)는 많은 증례에서 불완전 치유 진행 중임을 시사 — 장기 추적 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct RCT comparison of Ortho MTA III (bioceramic sealer) vs GP+AH Plus for infected canal obturation with 30-month median follow-up
- Establishes comparable success rates between the two approaches (no statistically significant difference)
- Identifies age, periapical lesion size, and PAI score as significant predictors of treatment failure
- Provides long-term data (30-month median) beyond most short-term obturation studies

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: RCT (parallel-arm)
- Population: 120 teeth with infected root canals + apical periodontitis
- Intervention: Ortho MTA III orthograde obturation
- Control: GP + AH Plus (conventional lateral condensation)
- Follow-up: 30-month median follow-up
- Outcomes: Healing success (loose vs strict criteria), predictor variables for failure
- Statistics: Survival analysis; predictor analysis (age, periapical lesion size, PAI score)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Success rate (loose criteria): 88.24% (Ortho MTA III) vs 83.02% (GP+AH Plus) — no significant difference
- Success rate (strict criteria): 64.71% vs 58.49% — no significant difference
- Predictors of failure: older age, larger periapical lesion size, higher PAI score
- No significant difference between the two obturation methods on success

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-center study; blinding of operators may be limited given different material handling properties
- PAI score and lesion size as confounders not stratified in randomization
- Loose vs strict criteria gap (88% vs 65%) suggests ongoing incomplete healing in many cases; longer follow-up needed

## 6. Related Work
- GP+AH Plus is the current conventional gold-standard comparator in obturation RCTs
- Bioceramic sealers have gained momentum as alternatives; this adds long-term RCT evidence

## 7. Glossary
- Ortho MTA III: calcium silicate/bioceramic-based root canal sealer
- PAI score: Periapical Index score (radiographic grading of periapical lesion severity)
- Loose vs strict criteria: different threshold definitions of healing success on follow-up radiographs
