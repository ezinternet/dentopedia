## Why Ingested

ISO 6872 was updated in 2024 to replace water immersion with steam for LTD testing. This study directly compares the two protocols on the same 3Y-TZP, quantifying the difference in t→m transformation depth, monoclinic content, and flexural strength — critical for interpreting future LTD literature under the new standard. Extends methodology baseline for [[dental-materials/zirconia/koenig-2021-ltd-monolithic-zirconia-prospective]] and related studies.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (150 3Y-TZP discs, uniaxial pressing, 3 groups: immediate, steam-aged 134°C/10h ISO 6872:2024, water-immersed 134°C/10h): water immersion produced significantly MORE t→m transformation (37% monoclinic) vs steam (24%), deeper degraded layer, and reduced translucency.

Water-immersion group also showed significantly HIGHER characteristic flexural strength vs steam — consistent with transformation toughening from greater surface monoclinic content.

Conclusion: the ISO 6872:2024 switch from water to steam produces a less severe LTD test; clinicians and researchers comparing studies across the standard change must account for this systematic difference.

## 세줄요약

150개 3Y-TZP 디스크, 3군(즉시/증기에이징134°C/10h ISO6872:2024/수침지에이징134°C/10h): 수침지군이 증기군보다 단사정 비율 높음(37% vs 24%), 열화층 더 깊고 투명도 감소.

그러나 수침지군이 특성굴곡강도도 더 높음 — 더 많은 t→m 전환에 의한 변태강화.

ISO 6872:2024 변경(수침지→증기)이 LTD 심각도를 낮추므로, 개정 전후 연구 비교 시 체계적 차이 고려 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First direct comparison of ISO 6872:2024 steam vs traditional water immersion for 3Y-TZP LTD testing
- Shows water immersion is more aggressive: 37% vs 24% monoclinic, deeper degraded layer by cross-sectional SEM
- Demonstrates reduced translucency (TP) in water-immersed group — optical degradation alongside phase change
- Provides Weibull statistics for both protocols: characteristic strength and modulus

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 3Y-TZP (Zpex, Tosoh): 150 disc specimens, uniaxial pressing → density >99%
- Groups: Zpex-I (immediate control), Zpex-St (steam, 134°C/2.2 bar/10h per ISO 6872:2024), Zpex-Iw (water immersion, 134°C/2.2 bar/10h)
- Density measurement, XRD (monoclinic phase), SEM (grain size, cross-sectional degraded layer depth)
- Translucency parameter (TP) and contrast ratio (CR)
- Biaxial flexural strength + Weibull analysis
- 1-way ANOVA + Tukey's test for TP, CR, grain size

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Monoclinic Content | Degraded Layer | TP | Characteristic FS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zpex-I (immediate) | ~0% (tetragonal dominant) | — | Highest | Baseline |
| Zpex-St (steam) | 24% | Shallower | No significant difference from Zpex-I | Lower than Zpex-Iw |
| Zpex-Iw (water) | 37% | Deeper | Significantly reduced | Highest |

- Zpex-Iw: significantly larger grain size (SEM) vs Zpex-I; Zpex-St showed no significant grain size difference
- Water immersion's higher FS paradox = transformation toughening from greater monoclinic surface layer
- No significant differences in CR between groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single zirconia brand (Zpex/Tosoh); applicability to 4Y/5Y-PSZ formulations unclear
- 10h aging: does not reflect long-term clinical kinetics
- No mechanical fatigue or wear component
