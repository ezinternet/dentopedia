## Why Ingested

Dennis Tarnow (NYU) 공저; 심미부위 단일치 즉시식립+즉시 프로비저널의 가장 긴 추적(최대 12년) 감염 소켓 데이터. 급성·만성·비감염 3군 비교 — 평균 7년 추적에서 군간 생존율 차이 없음(p=0.8). [[wiki/immediate-implant/munoz-camara-2020-immediate-implants-acute-periapical-infected]]의 단기(1년) 결과를 장기로 연장하는 근거.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=127 patients, 143 implants, mean 7-year follow-up range 2-12 years); esthetic zone single-tooth immediate implants (maxillary anterior/premolar); 3 groups: non-infected (47), acute infection (56), chronic infection (40); flapless + graft + immediate provisional.

Survival: non-infected 97.8%, acute 96.4%, chronic 95%; overall 96.5% (5 total failures); no significant difference between groups (p=0.8).

Immediate single-tooth implant placement with immediate provisionalization is viable in both infected and non-infected esthetic zone sites; survival comparable across infection status at up to 12 years.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Longest follow-up (2-12 years, mean 7 years) for infected-site immediate implant in the esthetic zone
- Tarnow co-authorship — high-credibility NYU group
- Confirmed NS survival difference across infection severity groups (p=0.8) at 7 years
- Evaluated both periodontal and endodontic infections including acute abscess/fistula (Group 2)
- Reported pink esthetic score (PES) and marginal bone loss alongside survival — full clinical picture
- Protocol: flapless + particulate graft + resorbable membrane (when buccal plate compromised) + screw-retained provisional OOO

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort
- **n**: 127 patients, 143 single-tooth implants
  - Group 1 (non-infected): 47 implants — healthy perio + endo
  - Group 2 (acute): 56 implants — perio/endo abscess or fistula
  - Group 3 (chronic): 40 implants — perio pocket or periapical lesion without acute signs
- **Region**: Maxillary central incisor, lateral incisor, canine, premolar (esthetic zone)
- **Protocol**: Flapless extraction, thorough debridement, immediate implant, particulate graft, resorbable membrane (if buccal plate compromised), screw-retained provisional (out of occlusion)
- **Follow-up**: Mean 7 years (range 2-12 years)
- **Outcomes**: Survival, MBL, recession, PD, gingival index, pink esthetic score

## 7. Glossary

- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: Assessment of peri-implant soft tissue appearance (papilla, tissue level, contour, color, texture)
- **Out of occlusion (OOO)**: Provisional restoration with no occlusal contact — protects osseointegration during healing
- **Esthetic zone**: Maxillary anterior and premolar region (visible in smile)
