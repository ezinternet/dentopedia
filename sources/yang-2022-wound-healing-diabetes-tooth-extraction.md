## Why Ingested

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Frontiers in Endocrinology 2022) synthesizing mechanisms and clinical interventions for delayed tooth extraction socket (TES) healing in diabetic patients — covers physiological, inflammatory/immune, endocrine/neural mechanisms and miRNA regulation.

Hyperglycemia impairs TES healing via: reduced TGFB/VEGF/BMP-4 expression, elevated MMP-9, dysregulated M1/M2 macrophage polarization, impaired neutrophil function, AGE-mediated collagen cross-linking, deficient neuropeptides; bone remodeling 4–8 weeks post-extraction is particularly vulnerable; healing delay is most evident at day 7 vs. non-diabetic but well-controlled DM often catches up.

Topical PRGF/L-PRF/A-PRF and HA are the best-supported clinical approaches for improving early TES closure in T2DM; most interventions still lack large-scale RCTs; well-controlled DM patients generally heal comparably to non-diabetic after extraction.

## 세줄요약

당뇨 환자의 발치와 지연 치유 기전과 임상 중재를 종합한 내러티브 리뷰 (Frontiers in Endocrinology 2022) — 생리적·염증/면역·내분비·신경 기전 및 miRNA 조절 포함.

고혈당은 TGFB/VEGF/BMP-4 발현 감소, MMP-9 상승, M1/M2 대식세포 극성화 이상, 호중구 기능 저하, AGE 매개 콜라겐 교차결합, 신경펩타이드 결핍을 통해 발치와 치유를 저해하며; 골 개형(4–8주)이 특히 취약하고; 치유 지연은 발치 7일째 가장 두드러지나 잘 조절된 당뇨에서는 대부분 따라잡는 패턴.

국소 PRGF/L-PRF/A-PRF 및 히알루론산이 T2DM의 조기 발치와 폐쇄를 개선하는 근거가 가장 강하고; 대부분 중재는 아직 동물 실험 단계이며 대규모 RCT가 부족; 잘 조절된 당뇨 환자는 비당뇨 환자와 비슷한 예후.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates multi-axis pathogenesis of diabetic TES healing: physiological (AGE, MMP, BMP, VEGF), inflammation/immune (M1/M2 polarization, neutrophils, TNF-α), endocrine/neural (PTH, osteoblast pyroptosis, neuropeptides), miRNA layers.
- Provides summary table of clinical trials on TES healing interventions in diabetes — PRGF, L-PRF, A-PRF, HA, maxillary complete denture (MICD) with study designs and outcomes.
- Clarifies that well-controlled DM (Goss et al.) shows no statistically significant difference in healing rate vs. non-DM; healing delay is primarily in poorly controlled patients and most evident at day 7.
- Identifies BMP-4 as the most under-expressed bone-regulating gene in T2DM blood (gene ontology analysis) — mechanistic link to impaired MSC osteogenic differentiation.
- Flags low-level laser therapy (808 nm) and HA as promising non-surgical adjuncts with clinical evidence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Narrative literature review (non-systematic); searched PubMed and related databases.
- **Scope**: Mechanisms (physiology, inflammation, endocrine, neural, miRNA) + clinical interventions (growth factors, BMPs, PTH, stem cells, synthetic drugs, natural products, HA, LLLT, denture).
- **Included**: Both human clinical trials and animal/in vitro mechanistic studies synthesized narratively.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Healing delay pattern**: Most evident at day 7 post-extraction; osteogenic tissue proliferates and bone matures 4–8 weeks post-extraction.
- **Well-controlled DM**: Goss et al. found no significant healing difference in T1DM or T2DM vs. non-diabetic when well controlled; bone healing duration similar in diabetic and normal individuals in some studies.
- **PRGF (clinical)**: Split-mouth study (34 T1DM patients) — PRGF reduced residual socket volume and improved Healing Index days 3–14 by accelerating epithelialization and tissue maturation.
- **L-PRF (clinical)**: Enhanced bone density (p=0.007); L-PRF + HA improved mucosal healing and reduced alveolar osteitis in mandibular third molar extraction.
- **HA (clinical)**: 0.8% HA in 30 poorly controlled T2DM patients improved wound healing especially on first days after extraction.
- **LLLT**: 808 nm laser reduced inflammatory infiltration and increased angiogenesis in rat models.
- **BMP-4**: Most under-expressed gene in T2DM blood among 11 differentially expressed genes; critical for MSC osteogenic differentiation.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative (not systematic): susceptible to selection bias; no AMSTAR or GRADE quality assessment.
- Majority of mechanistic interventions remain animal/in vitro with uncertain clinical translation.
- No meta-analysis; study heterogeneity in clinical trial table (varied DM control level, follow-up duration, outcome measures).
- Authors call for well-designed large-scale multi-centre RCTs for interventional wound healing in diabetics.

## 7. Glossary

- **TES**: Tooth Extraction Socket
- **AGE**: Advanced Glycation End-products — non-enzymatic glycosylation products that cross-link collagen and impair bone mechanics
- **M1/M2 macrophage polarization**: pro-inflammatory (M1) vs. anti-inflammatory/reparative (M2) phenotypes; dysregulated toward M1 in T2DM
- **PRGF**: Plasma Rich in Growth Factor (type of platelet concentrate, Anitua protocol)
- **L-PRF**: Leukocyte- and Platelet-Rich Fibrin (second-generation platelet concentrate)
- **MICD**: Maxillary Immediate Complete Denture — proposed as mechanical socket protector
