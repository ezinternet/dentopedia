## Three-line Summary

Case report plus literature review on early implant failure after transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE): a 4.8 mm x 10 mm standard implant placed after TSFE loosened suddenly 6 weeks after surgery.

The authors group candidate risk factors into patient-related, anatomical, and operation- and implant-related factors, and within the study's limits name graft particles lodged between the implant surface and the socket as a direct risk factor; fracture of the buccal or palatal cortical plate during site preparation or insertion is a further consideration.

They recommend careful socket cleaning during TSFE and discourage loose particulate graft material; the evidence is a single case plus a review, taken from the abstract only, with no effect sizes or failure rates available.

## 세줄요약

경치조 상악동거상술 (transcrestal sinus floor elevation, TSFE) 후 조기 임플란트 실패에 대한 증례보고와 문헌고찰: TSFE 후 직경 4.8 mm, 길이 10 mm 표준 임플란트를 식립하고 6주 뒤 갑자기 동요가 관찰됨.

위험요인을 환자 관련, 해부학적, 수술 및 임플란트 관련 요인으로 분류했으며, 연구 한계 내에서 임플란트 표면과 식립와 사이에 낀 입자형 골이식재를 직접적 위험요인으로 보았고 부위 형성 및 식립 중 협측·구개측 피질골 골절 가능성도 추가 고려사항으로 제시함.

TSFE 중 식립와 세척을 강조하고 느슨한 입자형 이식재 사용을 지양할 것을 권고; 단일 증례와 문헌고찰이며 초록만 확인하여 효과 크기나 실패율은 알 수 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames the gap: few studies address risk factors for early implant failure specifically associated with TSFE.
- Reports one failed case: standard implant 4.8 mm diameter x 10 mm length placed after TSFE, sudden loosening 6 weeks after the initial surgery.
- Classifies candidate risk factors as patient-related, anatomical factors of the operative area, and operation- and implant-related factors.
- Proposes graft particles between the implant surface and the socket as a direct risk factor, and cortical plate fracture (buccal or palatal) as another plausible cause of loss.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: single case report plus literature review (narrative, as described in the abstract); no search strategy, databases, or inclusion criteria are given in the abstract.
- **Patient/implant (n)**: 1 patient, 1 implant described.
- **Procedure**: TSFE followed by placement of a 4.8 mm x 10 mm standard implant; graft type and osteotome versus other technique are not stated in the abstract.
- **Early failure definition**: not formally defined; the failure event reported is loosening at 6 weeks after the initial surgery.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case plus review; the abstract gives no pooled failure rate, odds ratios or confidence intervals, so the risk-factor ranking cannot be quantified.
- Abstract-only ingest: review methods, the list of reviewed studies, and the specific patient, anatomical and implant factors are not verifiable.
- The authors themselves hedge ("within the current study's limitations"); the graft-particle mechanism is a proposed explanation, not a demonstrated cause.
- Authors call for further study of these factors and of cortical plate fracture.

## 7. Glossary

- **TSFE (Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation, 경치조 상악동거상술)**: elevation of the sinus membrane through the alveolar crest osteotomy, usually in insufficient posterior maxillary bone height.
- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: loss of the implant before functional loading; in this case, loosening at 6 weeks.
- **Cortical plate (피질골판)**: buccal or palatal outer bone wall of the alveolar ridge.
