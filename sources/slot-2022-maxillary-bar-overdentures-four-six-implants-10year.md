## Why Ingested
상악 피개의치 지지 임플란트 수(4개 vs 6개) 비교의 10년 RCT 데이터. [[complete-denture/carames-2025-four-vs-six-implant]] 및 [[complete-denture/difrancesco-2021-maxillary-overdentures-four-six-implants-sr-ma]]의 4 vs 6 임플란트 논쟁에 장기 RCT 근거를 추가한다.

## One-line Summary
10-year RCT (n=46 completers): maxillary bar overdentures on 4 vs. 6 posterior implants showed comparable survival, bone loss, and patient satisfaction; 4 implants sufficient in augmented posterior maxilla.

## 한줄요약
10년 RCT(n=46 완료): 후방 상악 4임플란트 vs 6임플란트 바 오버덴처 — 생존율·변연골소실·환자만족도 모두 동등; 골증대 상악에서 4임플란트로 충분.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research, 2022;33:1147–1156
- **Institution**: University Medical Center Groningen, Netherlands
- **Funding**: Institut Straumann AG, Basel (COI: implant manufacturer)
- **Registration**: NTR9729
- **Follow-up**: 10 years post-loading

## 2. Key Contributions
- Longest RCT (10-year) comparing 4 vs. 6 implants for maxillary bar overdentures
- Confirms 4-implant configuration as non-inferior to 6-implant in augmented posterior maxilla
- High implant survival in both groups despite augmented bone base (atrophic maxilla + iliac crest bone graft)
- Patient satisfaction significantly improved vs. baseline with NO difference between groups
- 6-implant group required more new overdentures (12 vs. 7) despite comparable clinical parameters

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: RCT (two-arm parallel)
- **Population**: 66 edentulous patients with functional maxillary denture complaints + insufficient bone
- **Procedure**: Bone augmentation (iliac crest) → randomization to 4 or 6 posterior implants → bar overdenture after 3 months osseointegration
- **Implant system**: Straumann
- **Outcomes**: Implant survival, overdenture survival, clinical scores, peri-implant bone height changes (periapical radiographs), patient satisfaction (VAS)
- **Completers**: 46/66 (70%) at 10 years

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant survival**:
  - 4-implant group: 100%
  - 6-implant group: 96.7%
- **New overdentures made** (wear): 4-implant=7, 6-implant=12
- **Marginal bone loss from baseline**:
  - 4-implant: 0.41 ± 0.37 mm
  - 6-implant: 0.70 ± 1.07 mm (not statistically different)
- **Clinical parameters**: no significant differences (probing depths, bleeding, plaque)
- **Patient satisfaction**: significantly improved from baseline in both groups; no difference between groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 30% dropout (66→46) over 10 years; potential survivor bias
- Funding by Straumann (implant manufacturer COI)
- All patients had augmented bone (atrophic maxilla + iliac graft) — may not generalize to patients with native bone
- Bar overdenture design limits generalizability to other attachment types (locator, magnet)

## 6. Related Work
- Di Francesco 2021 (SR+MA): 4 implants minimum recommended for maxillary overdentures
- Caramês 2025 (4 vs 6 full-arch): reinforces equivalence at prosthetic level
- Ng 2026 (SR+MA implant-assisted maxillary): broader systematic context

## 7. Glossary
- **Bar overdenture**: Implant-retained overdenture connected by a bar superstructure (splinting implants)
- **Augmented maxilla**: Atrophic maxilla that received iliac crest bone graft before implant placement
- **NTR9729**: Netherlands Trial Register identifier
