## Why Ingested
Anycheck IST(임플란트 안정성 검사)를 Osstell ISQ와 비교한 최신(2026) 통제 실험실 연구로, SmartPeg 없이도 ICC>0.90 재현성 및 r>0.85 상관을 보임. [[implants/isq/bavetta-2024-isq-osstell-osseo-device-comparison]] 의 디바이스 비교군을 확장하며, [[overviews/isq-loading-threshold]] 임상 도구 선택 근거를 보강한다.

## One-line Summary
Controlled laboratory study comparing Anycheck IST and Osstell ISQ across hard/normal/soft bone models (n=7 hygienists, 3 bone types), showing both devices achieve ICC>0.90 and r>0.85 correlation, independent of examiner experience.

## 한줄요약
통제 실험실 연구: Anycheck IST vs Osstell ISQ를 경도·보통·연조 3종 골질 모형에서 비교, 두 기기 모두 ICC>0.90·상호 상관 r>0.85, 검사자 경험 수준 무관하게 일치.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study to compare IST vs ISQ inter-examiner reliability across 3 bone quality types simultaneously
- Both devices achieve ICC>0.90 — clinically acceptable excellence in all bone quality categories
- Strong IST–ISQ correlation (r>0.85) regardless of bone density
- Examiner experience level (dental hygienists, varying) does not significantly affect measurement consistency
- IST practical advantages: no SmartPeg required, no abutment manipulation, simplified protocol

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Controlled laboratory study
- Examiners: 7 dental hygienists with varying experience levels
- Bone quality models: hard, normal, soft (standardized implant models)
- Devices compared:
  - Osstell ISQ (Osstell, Integration Diagnostics AB, Gothenburg, Sweden)
  - Anycheck IST (Implant Stability Test, Neobiotech Co., Ltd., Seoul, Republic of Korea)
- IST principle: measures energy dissipation after controlled impact (damping/percussion-based)
- Outcome measures: ICC, Pearson r, inter-examiner agreement
- Statistical significance: p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Inter-examiner reliability ICC: >0.90 for both IST and ISQ across all 3 bone quality types
- IST–ISQ correlation: r>0.85 (strong positive) across all bone density conditions
- Experience level effect: not statistically significant for either device
- Bone quality types correctly differentiated by both devices

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Laboratory (in-vitro) only — no clinical patient data
- Standardized models may not capture full clinical variability
- Hygienists only — clinician (dentist) examiner data not included
- No head-to-head stability tracking over time (single measurement session)
- IST measurement principle (energy dissipation) mechanistically different from RFA — clinical equivalence in marginal cases not fully characterized

## 6. Related Work
- Previous Lee et al. study: ISQ–IST correlation r=0.981 (p<0.01); healing abutment diameter not significant
- Lee et al. (2nd): both RFA and DCA show similar trends across bone densities and implant lengths
- Periotest and AMA reported significant correlations with ISQ in prior literature

## 7. Glossary
- IST (Implant Stability Test): Anycheck device by Neobiotech; percussion/damping-based, no SmartPeg needed
- ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient): Osstell 0–100 scale, RFA-based
- ICC (Intraclass Correlation Coefficient): reliability measure; ICC>0.90 = excellent
- DCA (Damping Capacity Analysis): IST measurement principle — energy dissipation after impact
- AMA (Acoustic Modal Analysis): vibration-based alternative to RFA
