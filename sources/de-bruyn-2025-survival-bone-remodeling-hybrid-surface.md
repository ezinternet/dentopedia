## Why Ingested

2026-09-30 smoking/early-failure surveillance. The wiki lacked any source reporting smoking-related early failure alongside a healing-protocol comparison; this cohort supplies a small, directionally consistent smoker signal next to [[implants/survival/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]] and [[implants/survival/wahlberg-2025-multicenter-early-implant-failures-part-2-patient]], but it contains only one-stage healing-abutment protocols and no interaction analysis, so it does not fill the submerged-versus-non-submerged gap.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective practice-based cohort (one surgeon, Netherlands; 2019-2023): 101 single hybrid-surface (machined coronal, MSc) implants, all one-stage with a healing abutment, placed by flap in healed bone (n=49), flapless (n=28) or immediately after extraction (n=24); 12-62 months follow-up.

Six implants (5.9%) failed before loading and none after; 3/10 implants in smokers failed (30%) versus 3/91 in non-smokers (3.3%), while by protocol 3/49, 1/28 and 2/24 failed (not significant); bone remodeling was 0.74 mm at loading and 0.93 mm at final recall, unaffected by protocol or smoking (p=0.27).

The smoker figure rests on 3 events in 10 smokers and no smoking-by-protocol breakdown or interaction test is reported, so it cannot show whether smoking risk differs by healing protocol; no submerged (two-stage) arm exists.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First report on MSc (machined-coronal hybrid surface, Southern Implants) single implants in a consecutively treated, unselected practice population.
- Compares three one-stage protocols (all with a healing abutment): flap in healed bone, freehand flapless, and immediate placement.
- Reports early failure by smoking status (3/10 vs 3/91) and bone remodeling by protocol, smoking, gender and implant type.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective, non-randomized, single-surgeon practice register; protocol chosen by surgeon/patient judgment; no power calculation.
- **Cohort**: 625 MSc implants placed 2019-2023; overdenture (234) and multi-implant fixed (168) excluded; 103 single implants excluded (maintained by referring dentist); 120 single implants remained; 11 dropouts and 8 immediately loaded excluded, leaving 101.
- **Protocols (all one-stage, healing abutment at surgery)**: Flap Healed + HA n=49 (full-thickness flap, healed bone); Flapless + HA n=28 (freehand, non-guided, soft tissue perforated by drills, crest not exposed); Immediate Placement + HA n=24 (after extraction). Immediate loading excluded. No submerged (two-stage, buried) arm.
- **Outcomes**: pre-loading failure; bone level on periapical radiographs (baseline, loading, last recall), 95 implants analyzed.
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis (protocol, implant type), Mann-Whitney U (gender, smoking), paired comparisons over time; p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 101 implants (41 mandible, 60 maxilla; 52 molar, 31 premolar, 18 anterior; 46 female, 55 male; median age 59).
- Early failure 6/101 (5.9%), all before loading; no late failures.
- **Smoking**: 3/10 smokers failed (30%) vs 3/91 non-smokers (3.3%). The text writes "3/91 implants in smokers ... 3/10 implants placed in smokers", an evident typo; 91 = 101-10 identifies the 3/91 group as non-smokers, and the abstract confirms.
- **By protocol (survival stated in Discussion)**: flapless 96.4% (1/28 failed), flap 93.9% (3/49), immediate 91.7% (2/24); not significantly different; counts derived from stated percentages, which sum to the 6 failures.
- No smoking-by-protocol failure breakdown is reported and no interaction test was performed.
- Bone loss: 0.74 mm at loading, 0.93 mm at final recall (p=0.068); no difference by implant type (p=0.994/0.510), protocol (p=0.544/0.1; 0.603 after loading), gender (p=0.557) or smoking (p=0.273). 54% showed bone gain after loading; 31% lost up to 1 mm; 4% lost more than 2 mm (abstract says 3% above 3 mm; Discussion cites 3/96).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Smokers n=10 (3 events): any smoking estimate is very unstable; the authors themselves state too few smokers for unequivocal conclusions.
- Retrospective, non-randomized, single surgeon; flapless placed only in good bone (selection bias); no CBCT.
- Non-standardized radiographs; peri-implantitis and soft-tissue data not retrievable.
- Internal inconsistencies: abstract 5% vs 5.9% failure; 3% (>3 mm) vs 4% (>2 mm); "irrespective of smoking status" in the abstract conclusion sits beside 30% vs 3.3%. Brief numbers were read from the paper text, not the screening summary.
- Tables/figures not available in the retrieved text.

## 7. Glossary

- **MSc**: machined surface coronal, hybrid implant with minimally rough neck and moderately rough body.
- **HA**: healing abutment (one-stage, non-submerged protocol).
- **IIP**: immediate implant placement.
- **Biologic width**: soft-tissue attachment dimension driving early crestal remodeling.
