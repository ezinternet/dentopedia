## Why Ingested

Orthodontic tooth movement (OTM) is driven by the periodontal/bone remodeling cascade that pages like [[orthodontics/tsolakis-2023-orthodontic-tooth-movement-molecular-narrative]] and [[orthodontics/jeon-2021-orthodontic-tooth-movement-mechanisms-animal]] describe at the cellular/molecular level; this review extends that mechanism story into a *chairside monitoring* modality — the specific GCF biomarkers (PGE2, TNF-α, IL-1β, RANKL/OPG, ALP, MMPs, osteocalcin, NTx) whose GCF levels track pressure- vs tension-side remodeling and could be sampled to monitor OTM. It also connects to GCF-as-diagnostic-fluid work in periodontics ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/donertas-2026-gbt-subgingival-debridement-gcf-biomarkers]]).

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (19 studies, Aug 2015–Aug 2024) mapping the changes in gingival crevicular fluid (GCF) biomarkers — inflammatory mediators (PGE2, TNF-α, IL-1β, RANKL/OPG), enzymes (ALP, MMP-1/2/3/8/9/13), and bone-turnover markers (osteocalcin, N-telopeptide) — during orthodontic tooth movement across both pressure and tension sides.

Key findings include: MMP-9 rises as early as 4 h and persists throughout tooth movement; the RANKL/OPG ratio governs osteoclastogenesis (RANKL↑ at pressure, OPG↑ at tension); ALP peaks around day 14 (formation marker); and different appliance systems (lingual vs labial, self-ligating vs conventional, Twin Block vs clear) produce distinct biomarker profiles.

Non-invasive chairside GCF sampling (absorbent paper strip + ELISA) could theoretically monitor tooth movement in real time to shorten treatment and reduce adverse effects, but the evidence base is small (n=19), non-systematic, and current assays are too laborious for routine clinical use.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰(19편, 2015.8–2024.8): 교정 치아이동(Orthodontic Tooth Movement, OTM) 동안 치은열구액(Gingival Crevicular Fluid, GCF) 내 염증매개체(PGE2, TNF-α, IL-1β), 골재형성 표지자(RANKL/OPG 비, ALP, MMP-1/2/3/8/9/13, 오스테오칼신, N-telopeptide)가 압박측·긴장측에서 시간 의존적으로 변하며, MMP-9는 4시간 내 상승·장기 지속, ALP는 14일경 정점을 보임.

장치 종류(설측 vs 순측, 자가결찰 vs 일반, Twin Block vs 투명교정)에 따라 GCF 표지자 프로파일이 달라지고, 척도별 변화 패턴이 압박측(RANKL↑, OPG↓)과 긴장측(OPG↑)으로 뚜렷이 구분됨.

흡수지(Absorbent Paper Strip) + ELISA 기반 GCF 비침습 채취로 OTM을 실시간 모니터링해 치료기간 단축·부작용 회피가 가능하다는 관점이나, 19편의 소규모 비체계적 근거로 현재 임상 적용은 제한적.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates a decade of GCF biomarker evidence into a single map of *which molecule signals what* during orthodontic tooth movement (OTM), split by pressure vs tension side.
- Frames GCF sampling as a non-invasive, repeatable, chairside investigative method to monitor OTM biology in real time — with the stated clinical payoff of shortening treatment and reducing adverse effects (root resorption, bone thinning).
- Organizes biomarkers into three functional classes: **inflammatory mediators**, **enzymes**, and **metabolic products / bone-turnover markers**.
- Reviews and compares GCF harvesting methods (gingival lavage, capillary microtubes, absorbent paper strips) and quantification (electronic transducer + ELISA).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review, not a meta-analysis — no pooled effect sizes. Conceptual architecture:

1. **Tissue restructuring during OTM** — orthodontic force compresses PDL/alveolar bone on one side (bone resorption) and stretches it on the other (bone formation). Strong force → blood-flow cut-off → hyalinization → delayed osteoclast recruitment (tooth movement in 7–14 d); mild force → osteoclast recruitment → movement in ~2 d.
2. **GCF origin and nature** — derived from the gingival blood-vessel plexus in the gingival chorion; transudate in health, inflammatory exudate in pathology; composition varies with periodontal status and remodeling.
3. **Biomarker inventory** by class (see Results), each tied to a role in the bone-remodeling / RANKL-OPG axis.
4. **Sampling methods** and quantification.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Inflammatory mediators**
- **PGE2** — stimulates bone resorption; elevated on both pressure and tension sides during OTM. Compressively stimulated PDL cells produce more activated FAK and PGE2. Limited data during CAOT/PAOO accelerated techniques.
- **TNF-α** — pro-inflammatory, stimulates osteoclasts. Rises at 1 h and 24 h after force in both pre-adjusted edgewise (PEA) and self-ligating (SL) appliances; by 168 h TNF-α falls in PEA but stays elevated in SL.
- **IL-1β / TNF-α** — immediate rise at 1 h, peak at 24 h, supporting inflammation's role in *initial* tooth movement.
- **IL-1α, IL-1β, IL-2, IL-6, IL-8, TNF-α** — higher with fixed **lingual** than labial appliance → lingual exerts greater mechanical stress on periodontium.
- **RANKL / OPG axis** — RANKL + M-CSF drive osteoclastogenesis; OPG (from osteoblasts and B lymphocytes) inhibits RANKL/RANK. **RANKL/OPG ratio** is the major determinant of bone resorption: in the compression zone RANKL ↑ and OPG ↓ (ratio ↑, osteoclastogenesis favored); in the tension zone OPG ↑ (RANKL inhibited). Invisalign maxillary molar distalization (initial force ~1 N) raises IL-1b + RANKL at pressure sites and TGF-1b + OPN at tension sites.

**Enzymes**
- **ALP (alkaline phosphatase)** — marker of bone *formation*; high GCF ALP during OTM. Increases by day 14, more in tension than pressure zone, higher with conventional brackets than self-ligating. Twin Block > Clear Block/Forsus for ALP activity at 1/3/6 months.
- **MMPs** — MMP-1, -2, -3, -8, -9, -13 all rise on both pressure and tension sides. MMP-3 peaks ~24 h (p38 MAPK pathway). **MMP-9** = sensitive marker of periodontal inflammation, appears as early as 4 h and stays high throughout OTM. MMP-13 affects osteoclast activity/resorption. MMP level proposed as an early marker of active tooth movement.

**Metabolic products / bone-turnover markers**
- **Chondroitin sulfate** — increases across loading; no significant difference between 120 g and 180 g force → stronger force not needed.
- **Pentraxin-3 (PTX-3, TSG-14)** — rises after appliance activation, peaks at 24 h, returns to baseline after ~2 weeks in children / ~1 week in adults → involved in initial periodontal remodeling and aseptic inflammation.
- **Osteocalcin (OC)** — bone-formation/turnover marker; GCF levels highest between day 7–14, significant rise on days 14 and 21; serum OC associated with alveolar bone *density* loss.
- **N-telopeptide (NTx)** — bone-*resorption* marker specific to bone, osteoclast-mediated. GCF NTx rises gradually from day 7 to day 21, significant on days 14 and 21, maximum on day 21; useful for systemic osteoclast activity, e.g. with mandibular protrusive appliances.

**GCF harvesting methods**
- Gingival lavage — collects cells/proteins but dilution factor not precisely determinable.
- Capillary microtubes — precise, undiluted, site-specific, but slow (>30 min) and can cause tissue damage.
- **Absorbent paper strips** — most used; insert 30–60 s; fast, easy, no tissue damage; blood/saliva-contaminated samples discarded. Volume best measured by electronic transducer; biomarkers assayed by **ELISA**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small number of included studies (n=19); narrative (non-systematic), no quantitative synthesis.
- Bone-turnover markers still not widely implemented in clinical orthodontic practice.
- GCF biomarker levels are confounded by gingival inflammation, occlusal forces, and poor oral hygiene — must be controlled to read biomarkers as orthodontic-effect indicators.
- Biomarker determination is laborious and expensive; authors advocate developing chairside/in-office point-of-care tests.

## 6. Related Work

- Cellular/molecular basis of OTM and force biology — pressure/tension-side remodeling, RANKL/OPG.
- GCF as a diagnostic fluid in periodontal monitoring (biomarker shifts with gingivitis → periodontitis).

## 7. Glossary

- **GCF (Gingival Crevicular Fluid)** — fluid from the gingival blood-vessel plexus seeping into the gingival sulcus; transudate in health, inflammatory exudate in disease.
- **OTM (Orthodontic Tooth Movement)** — tooth displacement from applied orthodontic force via periodontal/bone remodeling.
- **RANKL / OPG** — Receptor Activator of NF-κB Ligand and its decoy receptor Osteoprotegerin; their ratio governs osteoclastogenesis/bone resorption.
- **ALP** — Alkaline phosphatase; enzyme marking bone formation/mineralization.
- **MMPs** — Matrix metalloproteinases; ECM-degrading enzymes involved in remodeling and inflammation.
- **PGE2** — Prostaglandin E2; inflammatory mediator promoting bone resorption.
- **OC (Osteocalcin)** — non-collagenous bone-matrix protein; bone-formation/turnover marker.
- **NTx (N-telopeptide)** — amino-terminal telopeptide of type I collagen; bone-resorption marker.
- **PTX-3 (Pentraxin-3)** — TNF-induced acute-phase glycoprotein marking aseptic inflammation.
- **PEA / SL** — Pre-adjusted Edgewise Appliance / Self-Ligating bracket systems.
- **CAOT / PAOO** — Corticotomy-Assisted Orthodontic Therapy / Periodontally Accelerated Osteogenic Orthodontics.
- **ELISA** — Enzyme-Linked ImmunoSorbent Assay; standard method to quantify GCF biomarkers.
