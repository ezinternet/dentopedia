## Why Ingested

First stratified RCT directly comparing ∅3.3 mm versus ∅4.1 mm TiZr implants within HbA1c-stratified hyperglycaemic cohorts, filling a gap in dimensionally-stratified diabetes implant evidence; establishes that HbA1c ≥7% carries ~7× higher odds of clinically relevant MBL regardless of implant diameter.

→ [[implants/survival/shahi-2026-implant-outcomes-diabetes-mellitus-sr]]

## Three-line Summary

Stratified RCT (n=83 patients, 166 implants) comparing ∅3.3 mm versus ∅4.1 mm hydrophilic titanium-zirconium (TiZr) implants in hyperglycaemic subjects (HbA1c 5.8–<7% and ≥7%) supporting mandibular distal-extension fixed dental prostheses, 12-month follow-up, India (CTRI/2017/08/009315).

Implant survival 100%, no peri-implantitis; MBL did not differ between diameters (3.3mm: 0.27mm vs 4.1mm: 0.27mm, p=0.078); however HbA1c ≥7% conferred ~7× higher odds of MBL ≥0.5mm; secondary ISQ was significantly higher with 4.1mm (79.6 vs 76.1, p<0.001) while early healing (Healing Index) was better with 3.3mm (p=0.005).

Diameter choice does not independently drive bone loss in hyperglycaemic patients, but glycaemic control is the dominant modifiable risk for MBL; chewing ability improved significantly from baseline irrespective of diameter or HbA1c category.

## 세줄요약

층화무작위대조시험 (Stratified RCT, n=83명, 166개 임플란트) — HbA1c 5.8–<7% 및 ≥7%로 층화 후 ∅3.3mm vs ∅4.1mm 친수성 티타늄-지르코늄 (Titanium-Zirconium, TiZr) 임플란트로 무작위배정, 하악 원심연장 고정성의치 지지, 12개월 추적.

임플란트 생존율 (Implant Survival Rate) 100%, 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 없음; 직경별 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 차이 없음(3.3mm 0.27mm vs 4.1mm 0.27mm, p=0.078); 단, HbA1c ≥7%는 MBL ≥0.5mm 교차비 (Odds Ratio) 약 7배 증가; 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 4.1mm에서 유의하게 높고 (79.6 vs 76.1, p<0.001) 초기치유는 3.3mm가 우수 (p=0.005).

당뇨 환자에서 임플란트 직경은 골소실의 독립적 결정인자가 아니며, 혈당조절 수준이 MBL의 주요 가변 위험인자; 저작능력 (Chewing Ability)은 직경·HbA1c와 무관하게 기저치 대비 유의하게 향상.

## Key Contributions

- First stratified RCT isolating diameter effect (3.3mm vs 4.1mm TiZr) within HbA1c-stratified cohorts
- Quantifies HbA1c ≥7% as ~7× multiplier for clinically relevant MBL (≥0.5mm) at 12 months
- Demonstrates ISQ advantage for wider diameter (4.1mm ISQ 79.6 vs 76.1) but superior early healing for narrow (3.3mm Healing Index, p=0.005)
- 100% survival and no peri-implantitis at 12 months in both HbA1c strata
- Patient-reported chewing ability improved for all groups — clinical benefit independent of glycaemic status

## Methodology

- **Design**: Stratified RCT; stratification by HbA1c (<7% vs ≥7%), then 1:1 randomisation to ∅3.3mm or ∅4.1mm TiZr implant
- **N**: 88 enrolled; 83 patients (166 implants) evaluated at 12 months
- **Implant**: Hydrophilic TiZr (Roxolid-class), mandibular distal-extension two-implant-supported multi-unit FDP
- **Primary outcome**: MBL change at 12 months post-prosthesis delivery
- **Secondary outcomes**: ISQ/RFA, peri-implant indices, Healing Index, patient-reported outcomes (chewing ability, PRO)
- **Setting**: Oral Rehabilitation Center, Bangalore, India
- **Blinding/allocation**: Stratified randomisation within HbA1c strata

## Key Results

| Outcome | ∅3.3mm | ∅4.1mm | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| MBL at 12 months (median, IQR) | 0.27mm (0.07–0.68) | 0.27mm (−0.04 to 0.54) | 0.078 (NS) |
| ISQ (mean) | 76.1 | 79.6 | <0.001 |
| Healing Index (early) | Better | — | 0.005 |
| Survival | 100% | 100% | — |
| Peri-implantitis | None | None | — |

- **HbA1c effect**: HbA1c ≥7% → ~7× higher odds MBL ≥0.5mm vs HbA1c <7%
- **Chewing ability**: Significantly improved from baseline in all groups (p<0.0001)

## Limitations

- 12-month follow-up only — MBL and ISQ divergence over longer timeframes not captured
- Single center (Bangalore) — generalisation to other populations limited
- Abstract-only access; full methodology details (randomisation sequence, blinding) require full paper

## Glossary

- **TiZr (Titanium-Zirconium)**: Binary alloy (Roxolid-class) with higher strength than grade-4 titanium, enabling narrow-diameter implants
- **Healing Index**: Soft-tissue wound-healing index scored clinically at early post-surgical visits
- **Stratified RCT**: Randomisation performed within HbA1c strata to ensure balanced glycaemic distribution across diameter groups
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: Dimensionless 1–100 scale from resonance frequency analysis (RFA)
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: Radiographic change in crestal bone height from baseline
