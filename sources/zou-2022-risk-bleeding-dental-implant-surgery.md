## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 7 studies (PubMed, Embase, CENTRAL) comparing bleeding after dental implant placement with continued oral anticoagulant (OAC) or antiplatelet (AP) therapy vs controls, or OAC vs AP.

Pooled OAC vs control: no significant difference in bleeding (RR 1.81, 95% CI 0.70-4.63; I2 = 14%); VKA subgroup borderline (RR 3.42, 95% CI 1.00-11.67; P = .05), DOAC subgroup not elevated (RR 1.67, 95% CI 0.49-5.70).

Continuing OAC for implant surgery does not significantly increase bleeding when local haemostatic measures are used; the VKA signal and the OAC-vs-AP comparison rest on very few studies (abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Implant-specific pooled estimate for continued OAC therapy vs controls.
- Indirect VKA vs DOAC contrast; limited OAC vs AP comparison.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PubMed, Embase and CENTRAL searched for studies comparing bleeding outcomes after implant placement between OAC/AP therapy and controls, or OAC vs AP. Seven studies included; pooled relative risks (RR) with heterogeneity (I2).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- OAC vs control: RR 1.81 (95% CI 0.70-4.63; I2 = 14%, P = .22).
- VKA: RR 3.42 (95% CI 1.00-11.67; I2 = 23%, P = .05). DOAC: RR 1.67 (95% CI 0.49-5.70; I2 = 0%, P = .41).
- OAC vs AP: RR 0.08 (95% CI 0.01-0.76; I2 = 0%, P = .03), described by the authors as "limited data" suggesting higher bleeding with OAC than AP.
- Conclusion: continuing OAC does not increase bleeding provided local haemostatic measures are used.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest: study designs, definitions of bleeding, implant numbers, bias assessment and local haemostatic protocols are not available.
- Only 7 studies; subgroup CIs wide. VKA result is borderline (lower CI bound 1.00).
- The OAC vs AP RR of 0.08 is reported in the abstract alongside the statement that OAC carries more bleeding; the direction of the ratio is not defined in the abstract (which arm is the reference), so the number should not be interpreted without the full text.
- VKA vs DOAC is an indirect comparison.
