## Why Ingested

Only paper found in the 2026-10-21 surveillance that applies progressive loading to a severely atrophic edentulous mandible with 17–20-year follow-up, a different question from the crown-level crestal-bone RCTs ([[implants/loading-protocol/ghoveizi-2013-a-radiographic-comparison-of-progressive]]). It also gives a human, long-term counterpart to the animal progressive-load data in [[occlusion/podaropoulos-2016-bone-reactions-progressive-static-load-dogs]]. Evidence weight is low (3 patients) and must be read that way.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series of 3 women (64–76 years) with extremely atrophic edentulous mandibles; 13 implants (4–5 per patient, bicortical, between the mental foramina) were immediately loaded with a fixed prosthesis, then the distal cantilever was lengthened stepwise (≈4 → 7–8 → 11–12 mm) over years; follow-up 17, 19 and 20 years.

Mandibular body height 1 cm distal to the last implant rose from 7.0 ± 3.9 mm to 11.0 ± 3.2 mm (third quadrant) and from 8.1 ± 4.4 mm to 11.2 ± 4.4 mm (fourth quadrant), i.e., mean gains of 4.0 ± 0.7 and 3.1 ± 1.0 mm; a mandibular canal roof re-formed in 2 patients and thickened in the third.

The authors attribute the vertical bone gain to progressive load, but with no control, n = 3, co-interventions (PRGF, biological drilling, bicortical anchorage) and CBCT measurement error, this is hypothesis-generating only.

## 세줄요약

극심한 하악 골위축을 가진 무치악 여성 3명(64–76세)의 후향적 증례 연구: 이공 사이에 임플란트 4–5개(총 13개, 이중피질 고정)를 식립하고 즉시 부하한 뒤, 원심 캔틸레버를 수년에 걸쳐 단계적으로(약 4 → 7–8 → 11–12 mm) 늘렸으며 추적은 17·19·20년.

마지막 임플란트 원심 1 cm 지점의 하악체 높이가 제3사분면 7.0 ± 3.9 → 11.0 ± 3.2 mm, 제4사분면 8.1 ± 4.4 → 11.2 ± 4.4 mm로 증가(평균 증가 4.0 ± 0.7, 3.1 ± 1.0 mm); 2명에서 하치조신경관 상부 골이 새로 형성되었고 1명은 두꺼워졌다.

저자들은 점진적 부하가 수직 골 증가를 유도했다고 해석하나, 대조군 없음·n = 3·병용 처치(PRGF 등)·CBCT 측정 오차로 가설 생성 수준에 그친다.

## 1. Document Information

- Frontiers in Dental Medicine 2024;5:1465137; PMID 39917668; PMCID PMC11797772.
- Design: retrospective case series, 3 patients, 13 implants; follow-up 17, 19, 20 years.
- Implant system in the paper: BTI (Vitoria, Spain); PRGF used at placement.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Describes a staged-cantilever progressive-loading prosthesis concept for severely atrophic mandibles: immediate loading with minimal cantilever, cantilever lengthened as CBCT shows mandibular body growth.
- Reports vertical growth of the mandibular body distal to the implants and re-formation of a bony roof over the inferior alveolar nerve in 2 of 3 patients.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Patients: 3 women, 64/69/76 years; hypertension in two, type II diabetes (diet-controlled) in one, hypercholesterolemia in one; non-smokers; complete denture wearers.
- Surgery: 4–5 implants between the foramina, bicortically stabilized in basal cortical bone; low-speed (125 rpm) drilling without irrigation; PRGF on implant surface; insertion at 25 Ncm then torque wrench.
- Prosthesis: intermediate abutments; immediate fixed provisional resin on metal bar with minimal or no cantilever; later fixed screw-retained resin prosthesis with progressive cantilever: stage 1 at 3 months after immediate loading, stage 2 on evidence of bone growth, stage 3 progression of growth; definitive metal-reinforced resin screw-retained prosthesis.
- Follow-up: every 6 months with panoramic radiographs; CBCT when thickening seen and at each prosthesis change; height measured 1 cm posterior to the last implant (BTI scan software).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Third quadrant | Fourth quadrant |
|---|---|---|
| Mandibular body height at baseline (1 cm distal to last implant) | 7.0 ± 3.9 mm | 8.1 ± 4.4 mm |
| Height at last follow-up | 11.0 ± 3.2 mm | 11.2 ± 4.4 mm |
| Mean vertical gain | 4.0 ± 0.7 mm | 3.1 ± 1.0 mm |
| Cantilever stage 1 / 2 / 3 | 4.1 ± 0.9 / 6.5 ± 1.3 / 11.2 ± 3.2 mm | 4.1 ± 1.67 / 8.1 ± 2.6 / 12.3 ± 5.1 mm |

- Residual height at the implant sites: 7.8 ± 2.7 mm (abstract).
- Stage 2 reached at 2, 3 and 5 years and stage 3 at 8, 5 and 7 years in the three patients.
- Canal roof: new 1–2 mm bone above the nerve in 2 patients; in the third, bone above the nerve increased from 1 mm to 2.5 mm (text) — a figure legend says 1 to 3 mm.
- Implant sizes: diameter 3.5–4.5 mm, length 5.5–10 mm.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- n = 3, retrospective, no control group, no patient-reported outcomes (authors).
- Authors' stated limits: small sample, retrospective design, CBCT beam-hardening and 3D-reconstruction error near implants.
- Internal inconsistencies noted on reading: mean insertion torque reported as 68 ± 12 Ncm with a stated range of 45–60 Ncm; the Results sentence says "completely edentulous maxilla" although all cases are mandibles; canal-roof gain 2.5 mm (text) vs 3 mm (figure legend). Individual implant survival and marginal bone loss are described as study variables but not reported in the text read.
- Co-interventions (PRGF, biological drilling, bicortical anchoring, immediate loading) and natural bone behavior under implant support cannot be separated from the effect of progressive cantilever loading.
- The authors recommend selecting patients and expert surgeons because mandibular fracture is a risk in such atrophy.

## 7. Glossary

- Progressive loading (here): staged lengthening of the distal cantilever to raise mechanical stimulus on the atrophic mandible.
- Bicortical stabilization: implant apex engaged in the basal cortical plate.
- PRGF: plasma rich in growth factors, autologous platelet-derived preparation used to irrigate the implant surface.
