## One-line Summary
SR+MA (Clin Oral Investig 2026; 45 studies): propolis-containing formulations reduce plaque index vs negative control (SMD −1.11, 95%CI −1.52 to −0.70) and microbial counts (SMD −1.45) — no significant difference from CHX; very low GRADE certainty.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA specifically examining propolis for dental caries prevention with meta-analyzed plaque data
- PI vs negative control: SMD −1.11 (95%CI −1.52 to −0.70, p<0.01) — clinically meaningful reduction
- PI vs CHX: SMD 0.46 (95%CI −0.04 to 0.95, p=0.07) — not statistically inferior to chlorhexidine
- Microbial counts vs negative control: SMD −1.45 (95%CI −2.43 to −0.48, p=0.004)
- Microbial counts vs CHX: SMD 0.38 (p=0.13) — comparable
- Direct caries outcome data: insufficient for meta-analysis in most studies

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA; PRISMA 2020
- Databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, Ovid MEDLINE, Cochrane; no language/date restrictions
- Search date: December 11, 2025
- Eligibility: RCTs; propolis formulations (dentifrice, mouthwash, tablets, etc.) vs negative control or CHX; caries-related outcomes
- 1919 records identified → 45 studies included (qualitative); meta-analysis for PI and microbial counts
- Statistical: random-effects models, SMD; RoB 2 tool; GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Comparison | Outcome | SMD (95%CI) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Propolis vs negative control | Plaque index | −1.11 (−1.52 to −0.70) | <0.01 |
| Propolis vs CHX | Plaque index | 0.46 (−0.04 to 0.95) | 0.07 |
| Propolis vs negative control | Microbial counts | −1.45 (−2.43 to −0.48) | 0.004 |
| Propolis vs CHX | Microbial counts | 0.38 (−0.11 to 0.87) | 0.13 |

- Direct caries indices: rarely assessed, could not be meta-analyzed
- GRADE certainty: very low (high RoB, inconsistency, indirectness)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No meta-analysis possible for direct caries outcomes (primary goal)
- Very low GRADE certainty
- High risk of bias across studies
- Standardized propolis concentration and formulation lacking across studies
- Long-term caries prevention data absent

## 6. Related Work

- Jiang 2026: curcumin vs chlorhexidine SR+MA — natural vs standard comparison parallel

## 7. Glossary
- Propolis: bee-derived resinous substance with antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties
- PCF: Propolis-Containing Formulation (dentifrice, mouthwash, tablet)
- SMD: Standardized Mean Difference — effect size measure for meta-analysis
- RoB 2: Risk of Bias 2 tool for RCTs (Cochrane)
