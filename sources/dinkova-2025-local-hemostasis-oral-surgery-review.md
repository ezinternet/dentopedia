## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (51 studies, 1990–2023) of biomaterial-based and surgical approaches to local hemostasis in oral surgery, covering traditional absorbable agents, autologous platelet concentrates (PRP/L-PRF), tranexamic acid, fibrin sealants, and topical thrombin.

Oxidized cellulose/gelatin sponges controlled bleeding in >85% of standard cases; tranexamic acid mouthwash reduced postoperative bleeding 50–60%; fibrin sealants achieved 70–90% reduction in high-risk patients; topical tranexamic acid reduced hemorrhagic events ~80% in anticoagulated patients without thromboembolic risk increase.

Baseline postoperative bleeding occurs in 4–6% of all oral surgery cases and 9–12% of anticoagulated/antiplatelet patients; agent selection should be risk-stratified, as no single hemostatic agent is optimal for all patients, and standardized protocols for emerging nanomaterial-based agents are lacking.

## 세줄요약

구강외과에서 국소지혈제 사용에 대한 narrative review(51편, 1990–2023) — 전통 흡수성 지혈재, 자가혈소판 농축물(PRP/L-PRF), 트라넥삼산(TXA), 피브린 실런트, 국소 트롬빈을 망라.

산화셀룰로오스/젤라틴 스펀지는 표준 증례 >85% 지혈; 트라넥삼산 양치는 술후 출혈 50–60% 감소; 피브린 실런트는 고위험 환자 70–90% 감소; 국소 트라넥삼산은 항응고 환자 출혈 사건 ~80% 감소(혈전색전 위험 증가 없음).

술후 출혈 기저 발생률은 전체 구강외과 4–6%, 항응고·항혈소판 환자 9–12%; 단일 지혈제로 모든 환자에 최적이 아니므로 위험도 기반 약제 선택이 필요하며, 나노재료 기반 신흥 지혈제의 표준 프로토콜은 아직 없음.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Agent | Efficacy |
|---|---|
| Oxidized cellulose / gelatin sponge | Bleeding control >85% standard cases |
| PRP / L-PRF (Autologous Platelet Concentrates) | Reduce bleeding time 30–50%; enhance soft tissue healing |
| Tranexamic acid mouthwash | Reduces postoperative bleeding 50–60% |
| Fibrin sealants | 70–90% bleeding reduction in high-risk patients |
| Topical tranexamic acid | ↓80% hemorrhagic events in anticoagulated patients (no thromboembolic risk increase) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Limited comparative studies in medically compromised populations
- Gaps in long-term outcomes and cost-effectiveness
- No standardized protocols for emerging agents (nanomaterials)
- Future: high-quality RCTs across diverse patient groups; integrated clinical guidelines
