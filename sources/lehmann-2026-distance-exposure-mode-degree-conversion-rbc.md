## Why Ingested

The wiki's curing-mode evidence so far is anchored in dual-cure cement chemistry ([[resin-bonding/li-2025-curing-modes-dual-cure-dentin]]: light-curing vs self-curing of dual-cure resin cements at dentin). This paper extends the "curing mode/exposure" question to direct resin-based composites and, critically, adds the LED curing-light DEVICE parameters (tip-to-surface distance, exposure protocol: standard/soft-start/pulse) that Li 2025 does not address — a genuine light-curing-unit operational-parameter study rather than a material-chemistry study.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro preliminary study (n=81 specimens, 27 groups: 3 composites × 3 exposure modes × 3 distances) using Raman spectroscopy to measure degree of conversion (DC): material type and distance significantly affected top-surface DC (p<0.001, p=0.049), while material type and exposure mode significantly affected bottom-surface DC (p<0.001, p=0.032);

flowable composite had the highest DC (top 74%, bottom >60%), bulk-fill the lowest (bottom <45%, below the ~55% clinically suggested minimum).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study (per authors) to combine curing distance, exposure mode, AND material type into one factorial design, rather than testing distance or mode in isolation.
- Follow-up to the authors' own prior photometer study — this paper moves from measuring light irradiance at a sensor surface to measuring actual degree of conversion (DC) within composite specimens via Raman spectroscopy, at both top and bottom surfaces.
- Demonstrates that the significant factors differ by depth: distance matters at the top surface, exposure mode matters at the bottom surface — a depth-dependent dissociation not previously characterized together.
- Flags bulk-fill composite bottom-surface DC (<45%) as falling below the ~55% clinically suggested minimum threshold, with clinical relevance for thick-increment posterior restorations.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: in vitro factorial study, 27 experimental groups = 3 RBCs × 3 exposure modes × 3 distances, n=3 specimens per group (81 specimens total, single operator).
- Materials: Evetric (nanohybrid, anterior), Filtek One Bulk Fill (posterior bulk-fill), G-aenial Universal Flo (flowable, multipurpose).
- Specimens: 4 mm diameter × 2 mm height, light-cured 20 s with a Woodpecker LED.H curing light (Guilin Woodpecker Medical Instrument Co.).
- Exposure modes: (A) Standard — full power 1000 mW/cm²; (B) Soft-start — ramps from weak to peak power over 5 s; (C) Pulse — oscillates 850–1000 mW/cm² in 2 s intervals.
- Distances: 0, 2, 4 mm between LCU tip and composite surface, set with spacer rings; irradiance verified with a Bluephase Meter II photometer.
- Post-cure storage: light-proof containers, distilled/deionized water, 37 ± 1 °C, 24 h before analysis.
- DC measurement: Raman spectroscopy (Labram HR 800, He-Ne laser 632.8 nm), ratio of aliphatic C=C band (1639 cm⁻¹, polymerization-sensitive) to aromatic C=C band (1609 cm⁻¹, internal standard); spectra deconvoluted in Fityk 1.3.1 (Lorentzian peak-shape).
- Statistics: three-way ANOVA with interaction terms, run separately for top and bottom surfaces (factors: material [3], exposure mode [3], distance [3]); Tukey post hoc; alpha = 0.05; Statistica 13.3.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Top surface: material type p<0.001 (highly significant); distance p=0.049 (significant); exposure mode p=0.391 (not significant). No significant 2-way or 3-way interactions.
- Bottom surface: material type p<0.001 (highly significant); exposure mode p=0.032 (significant); distance p=0.068 (not significant). No significant interactions.
- DC was consistently higher at top than bottom across all conditions, materials, and modes.
- Flowable (G-aenial Universal Flo): highest DC — top 74%, bottom >60%.
- Bulk-fill (Filtek One Bulk Fill): lowest DC — top >55%, bottom <45% (below the ~55% suggested clinical minimum at the bottom).
- Nanohybrid (Evetric): intermediate results.
- Soft-start mode: best at top surface (69% top, 56% bottom), only marginally behind standard mode at bottom (standard: 67% top, 57% bottom).
- Increasing distance 0→4 mm still gave adequate top-surface DC (~70% at 2 mm, ~60% at 4 mm).
- Pulse mode: acceptable top-surface polymerization even at increased distance, but overall the lowest DC values among the three modes — authors suggest cautious use in deep cavities with vital pulp (reduced pulp-overheating risk trade-off).
- Prior (companion) photometer study by the same authors: distance most strongly reduced irradiance in soft-start mode (985→884 mW/cm² from 2→4 mm) vs standard (996→906) and pulse (993→893) — but that study measured light energy at a sensor, not actual composite DC, motivating this study.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Author-stated limitations:
- In vitro design cannot fully replicate the oral environment (operator technique, saliva, pH, temperature variation).
- Only 2 mm and 4 mm curing distances tested, though clinical posterior cavity depths may exceed 4 mm.
- Small, preliminary/pilot sample size (n=3 per group), no a priori power analysis — limits statistical power and generalizability; normality/variance testing has limited reliability at this n, so results should be read as exploratory.
- Radiant exposure was not directly quantified at each distance/mode combination; only a single increment thickness (2 mm) was assessed.
- No rigid surface during curing — the flexible Mylar strip may have allowed oxygen diffusion, creating a partially inhibited zone affecting top-surface conversion in areas of poor contact.
- Physicochemical/mechanical properties (microhardness, flexural strength) were not measured or correlated with DC.
- Potential bias in Raman spectral fitting parameters cannot be excluded; no thermal measurements or aging protocols were performed.

Future work proposed: larger, power-calculated sample sizes; thicker (e.g., 4 mm) bulk-fill increments; direct measurement of delivered radiant exposure; correlation of DC with mechanical/thermal properties; aging protocols.

## 7. Glossary

- **DC (Degree of Conversion)**: percentage of C=C double bonds converted to single bonds during polymerization; core polymerization-quality metric.
- **RBC**: Resin-Based Composite.
- **LCU**: Light-Curing Unit.
- **Soft-start mode**: LED exposure protocol that ramps irradiance from low to peak power over several seconds (here, 5 s) to reduce shrinkage stress.
- **Pulse mode**: LED exposure protocol oscillating irradiance between two power levels at fixed intervals (here, 850–1000 mW/cm² every 2 s).
- **Raman spectroscopy (DC assay)**: vibrational spectroscopy method quantifying DC via the ratio of the aliphatic C=C (1639 cm⁻¹, reactive) to aromatic C=C (1609 cm⁻¹, internal standard) Raman bands.
- **Irradiance**: light intensity delivered by the curing unit, measured in mW/cm².
