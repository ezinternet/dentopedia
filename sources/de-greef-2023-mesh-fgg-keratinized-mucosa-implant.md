## Why Ingested

Novel mesh-FGG technique for peri-implant keratinized mucosa augmentation — directly extends the clinical repertoire covered by [[implants/soft-tissue/oh-2024-keratinized-mucosa-augmentation-functioning-implants-sr-ma]] and provides a technique variant relevant to sites where conventional FGG cannot cover large recipient areas with a single harvest.

## Three-line Summary

A mesh free gingival graft (mesh-FGG) combined with an apically positioned flap was applied in two patients to increase keratinized mucosa width, vestibule depth, and soft-tissue thickness around implants. The mesh scoring of the conventional FGG allows physical expansion of the graft, covering larger recipient sites from a smaller palatal harvest. At 4-month follow-up both sites healed uneventfully with good esthetic integration and minimal graft demarcation.

## 세줄요약

메쉬 유리치은이식 (Mesh Free Gingival Graft, Mesh-FGG)을 치근단변위판막 (Apically Positioned Flap, APF)과 병용해 임플란트 주위 각화점막 폭(Keratinized Mucosa Width, KMW)·전정 깊이·연조직 두께를 증가시킨 증례 2례. 기존 유리치은이식 (Free Gingival Graft, FGG)에 격자 절개를 가해 채취량 대비 수여부 피개 면적을 확장하는 신기법으로, 공여부 이환율(donor morbidity) 감소가 기대된다. 4개월 관찰로 치유 양호·색상 통합 우수가 확인됐으나 장기 효과 검증을 위한 무작위 대조 임상시험이 필요하다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Case Reports (n=2)
- **Journal**: Clinical Advances in Periodontics, 2023;14(3):157-164
- **Institution**: Private Practice, Merchtem, Belgium; KU Leuven & University Hospitals Leuven (Periodontology)
- **Follow-up**: ~4 months (preliminary)
- **Keywords**: autogenous grafts, autografts, periodontal, soft tissue grafting

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Novel mesh-FGG technique**: Conventional FGG harvested from the palate, then scored in a mesh/lattice pattern (analogous to split-thickness skin mesh grafts in burn surgery) to physically expand its surface area before placement.
2. **Expanded recipient coverage**: Mesh expansion allows a single smaller palatal harvest to cover a significantly larger edentulous recipient site — directly reducing donor-site morbidity.
3. **APF combination**: Mesh-FGG paired with apically positioned flap (APF) to deepen the vestibule simultaneously while augmenting KM width.
4. **Esthetic outcome**: Both cases showed good color integration and minimal graft demarcation at 4 months — a commonly cited disadvantage of conventional FGG.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective case series, n=2 healthy adults
- **Sites**: Posterior mandible (Subject 1), anterior mandible (Subject 2)
- **Indication**: Large edentulous alveolar ridge with shallow vestibule and minimal KM after implant placement
- **Technique**: APF to reposition the mucogingival junction apically → mesh-FGG secured to the periosteal recipient bed
- **Mesh preparation**: Conventional FGG harvested from the palate, then scored with a scalpel in a lattice pattern to allow stretching and expansion (exact expansion ratio not reported in abstract)
- **Outcome measures**: Vestibule depth, soft-tissue thickness, KM width (clinical), esthetics (color disparity, graft demarcation), patient-reported outcomes
- **Follow-up**: Approximately 4 months

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Healing | Uneventful in both cases |
| KM width | Increased (quantitative data not specified in abstract) |
| Vestibule depth | Increased |
| Soft-tissue thickness | Increased |
| Color integration | Good; minimal color disparity and graft demarcation |
| Patient-reported discomfort | None reported as accessory discomfort |

**Principal finding (authors' statement)**: Mesh-FGG allowed expansion of harvested graft length, resulting in coverage of large recipient sites, increase in KM height, and good aesthetic integration.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **n=2 only** — strictly preliminary; no control group, no statistical analysis possible
- **4-month follow-up** — long-term stability of KM gain, bone level, and esthetics unknown
- **No quantitative mesh expansion ratio** reported
- **Authors' call**: Randomized controlled clinical studies comparing mesh-FGG vs. conventional FGG and other techniques (CTG, XCM, etc.) required to assess long-term efficacy for soft-tissue thickness, KMW, and patient-reported outcomes

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Mesh-FGG | Free gingival graft scored in a lattice/mesh pattern to expand its surface coverage area |
| APF | Apically positioned flap — mucosal flap displaced apically to deepen the vestibule and expose periosteum as a recipient bed |
| KM / KMW | Keratinized mucosa / keratinized mucosa width — band of non-movable, keratinized epithelium around teeth/implants |
| Donor morbidity | Morbidity at the palatal harvest site (pain, bleeding, healing) — reduced when a smaller harvest covers a larger area via mesh expansion |
| Graft demarcation | Visible color/texture boundary between grafted tissue and surrounding native mucosa — esthetic concern with conventional FGG |
