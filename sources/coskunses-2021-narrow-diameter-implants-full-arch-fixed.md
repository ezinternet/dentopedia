## Why Ingested

상악 풀아치 계획에서 심한 수평 골위축 증례에서 협소직경 임플란트(Narrow Diameter Implant, NDI) 사용 가능성이 대두됨. 기존 wiki에 NDI 풀아치 임상 데이터가 없었으며, 이 연구는 NDI(3.3 mm Ti-Zr)와 표준직경 혼용 풀아치에서 2년 MBL·생존율 데이터를 제공함. [[wiki/implants/mehta-2021-tilted-axial-implants-edentulous-maxilla-sr-ma]] 보완.

## Three-line Summary

2-year prospective-design clinical study at Kocaeli University (28 patients, 37 jaws, 179 Straumann Roxolid BLT implants: 67 NDI 3.3 mm + 97×4.1 mm + 15×4.8 mm, immediate loading 78%); axial and tilted implants mixed.

Overall implant CSR 99.4%; NDI CSR 98.5%; mean MBL at 1yr 0.51 mm, at 2yr 0.73 mm; 6-implant-supported prostheses had significantly lower MBL than 4-implant at 1yr (0.35 vs 0.63 mm, P=0.006); NDI MBL at 2yr 1.02 mm vs 4.1 mm diameter 0.44 mm (P=0.035 at 2yr).

NDIs are viable in full-arch rehabilitation when ridge width is insufficient, but MBL is significantly higher at 2yr; 6 implants distribute load better than 4; night guard is mandatory for bruxers.

## 세줄요약

코카엘리대 2년 임상 연구(28명, 37악궁, Straumann Roxolid BLT 179개: NDI 3.3 mm 67개 + 4.1 mm 97개 + 4.8 mm 15개; 즉시 부하 78%).

전체 CSR(누적생존율, Cumulative Survival Rate) 99.4%, NDI 98.5%; 1년 평균 MBL 0.51 mm, 2년 0.73 mm; 6 임플란트 지지 보철이 4개 지지보다 MBL 유의하게 낮음(0.35 vs 0.63 mm, P=0.006); 2년째 NDI MBL 1.02 mm — 표준직경(4.1 mm) 0.44 mm 대비 유의하게 높음(P=0.035).

골위축 심한 증례에서 NDI는 골이식 회피 옵션이나 2년 MBL이 표준직경보다 높음 — 임플란트 수 증가(6개) 및 야간 마우스가드가 MBL 감소에 기여; 장기 RCT 추가 필요.

## 1. Document Information

- **Study type**: Prospective-design retrospective clinical study
- **Follow-up**: 6–24 months (mean 15.8±6.3 months)
- **Sample**: 28 patients, 37 jaws (19 mandible + 18 maxilla), 179 implants
- **Journal**: International Journal of Implant Dentistry, 2021

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study specifically evaluating 3.3 mm Ti-Zr NDIs in fixed full-arch rehabilitation
- Demonstrates 4 vs 6 implant number effect: 6-implant significantly better MBL at 1yr
- Shows immediate vs late loading produces equivalent MBL outcomes (clinically important for protocol selection)
- Mandible > maxilla MBL (attributed to higher % of 4-implant mandible cases vs 6-implant maxilla cases)

## 3. Methodology

- Inclusion: edentulous or teeth with hopeless prognosis, ≥18 yrs, medically able, acceptable hygiene
- Minimum 4 implants per jaw; distal tilting (17–30°) with 30° MUA when needed to reach 1st molar region
- Immediate loading criteria: insertion torque ≥35 Ncm AND ISQ ≥65
- Definitive prosthesis at 4–6 months: titanium or CoCr framework + ceramic or composite resin
- Night guard provided to all patients
- MBL measured by ImageJ on paralleling technique periapical radiographs

## 4. Key Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Immediate loading rate | 130/179 implants (73%) |
| Lost implants | 1 (3.3 mm mandible, 3 months) |
| Overall CSR (2yr) | 99.4% |
| NDI CSR (2yr) | 98.5% |
| Mean MBL 1yr | 0.51±0.51 mm |
| Mean MBL 2yr | 0.73±0.66 mm |
| Maxilla MBL 2yr | 0.43±0.32 mm |
| Mandible MBL 2yr | 0.90±0.74 mm |
| 4-implant vs 6-implant MBL 1yr | 0.63 vs 0.35 mm (P=0.006) |
| NDI vs 4.1mm MBL 2yr | 1.02 vs 0.44 mm (P=0.035) |
| Provisional prosthesis fracture | 16.1% (5/31 prostheses) |
| Definitive prosthesis fracture | 0% |
| Ceramic chipping | 5.4% |

## 5. Limitations

- Short follow-up (mean 15.8 months; only subset reached 24 months)
- Non-randomized; convenience sample
- No separate subgroup analysis by jaw/implant number for NDIs at 2yr (inadequate sample)

## 6. Related Work

- Patzelt et al. SR: tilted=axial MBL (no difference) — consistent
- Tallarico et al.: similar 4 vs 6 implant outcomes at 5yr — partially consistent
- Brånemark 1995: 4 vs 6 implants similar survival but 4 had more complications — consistent direction

## 7. Glossary

- **NDI**: Narrow Diameter Implant (≤3.5 mm)
- **Ti-Zr**: Titanium-zirconium alloy (Roxolid® Straumann) — higher fatigue strength than Grade IV Ti
- **MUA**: Multi-Unit Abutment
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient (RFA measurement)
