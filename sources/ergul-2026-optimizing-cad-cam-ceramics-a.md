## Why Ingested

Multi-material in vitro study comparing polishing vs glazing on three CAD/CAM ceramics (translucent zirconia, lithium disilicate, zirconia-reinforced lithium silicate) after simulated chairside grinding and coffee thermocycling. Directly relevant to chairside adjustment finishing decisions across material types. Extends the existing zirconia polishing/glazing evidence base with material-type comparisons and stain-aging interactions.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/ergul-2026-optimizing-cad-cam-ceramics-a]]

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (BMC Oral Health 2026; n = 120 specimens, 1 mm discs; LDC = IPS e.max CAD HT A2, ZLC = Celtra Duo HT A2, TrZ = Cercon HT A2; n = 20 per subgroup) comparing glazing vs polishing after standardized grinding (20 s, red-band diamond bur at 20,000 rpm) and 10,000 coffee thermocycles (5–55 °C), assessing surface roughness (Ra, Rz by profilometer), color stability (ΔE₀₀ by CIEDE2000), and translucency parameter (TP by colorimeter).

After grinding, ZLC had the highest Ra (2.59 μm) > LDC (2.00 μm) > TrZ (1.08 μm); polishing produced significantly lower Ra in TrZ (TrZ-P vs TrZ-G: p < 0.05) while glazing produced equivalent or lower roughness in ZLC/LDC; thermocycling increased Ra in all glazed groups but not in TrZ-P or ZLC-P; ZLC-P had the highest ΔE₀₀ (median 1.84) barely below the acceptability threshold (2.23), while LDC-G had the lowest; TP decreased significantly in all groups except LDC-P after thermocycling.

Glazing is the preferred finishing method for glass-ceramics (LDC, ZLC) for color stability and roughness, while mechanical polishing produces superior surface quality and stability for translucent zirconia; all groups remained clinically acceptable for color change and translucency loss.

## 세줄요약

시험관내 (in vitro) 연구 (BMC Oral Health 2026; n = 120 디스크형 시편 1 mm; LDC = IPS e.max CAD HT A2, ZLC = Celtra Duo HT A2, TrZ = Cercon HT A2; 군당 n = 20): 표준화 연삭(20 s, 적색밴드 다이아몬드 버, 20,000 rpm) 후 유약 처리 (glazing) vs 기계적 연마 (polishing), 커피 열순환 (thermocycling) 10,000회(5–55 °C)를 거쳐 표면거칠기 (Ra, Rz), 색안정성 (ΔE₀₀, CIEDE2000), 반투명도 파라미터 (TP)를 평가.

연삭 후 Ra: ZLC(2.59 μm) > LDC(2.00 μm) > TrZ(1.08 μm); TrZ에서는 연마군(TrZ-P) Ra가 유약군(TrZ-G)보다 유의하게 낮았고, ZLC·LDC는 유약군이 동등하거나 낮음; 열순환 후 모든 유약군에서 Ra 증가, TrZ-P·ZLC-P는 변화 없음; ZLC-P ΔE₀₀ 중앙값 1.84로 임상 허용역치(2.23) 미만; 열순환 후 LDC-P를 제외한 전 군에서 TP 유의미하게 감소.

글라스 세라믹(LDC, ZLC)에는 유약 처리가, 반투명 지르코니아(TrZ)에는 기계적 연마가 색안정성·표면거칠기 측면에서 우수; 모든 군의 색변화·반투명도 변화는 임상적 허용 범위 내.

## Key Contributions

1. First multifactorial study combining material type, finishing protocol, and coffee thermocycling effects on roughness, color, and translucency in a single design
2. Demonstrates material-specific finishing recommendations: glazing for glass-ceramics, polishing for translucent zirconia
3. Polishing maintains more stable Ra after thermocycling vs glazing (glazed surfaces roughen; polished zirconia does not)
4. All ΔE₀₀ values remained within clinical acceptability threshold (< 2.23) despite thermocycling
5. Thermocycling-induced TP decreases remained within clinical acceptability (ΔTP < 4.43) for all groups

## Methodology

**Grinding**: All specimens standardized with red-band diamond bur (50 μm grit) at 20,000 rpm for 20 s unidirectional, using KaVo EWL 4990 handpiece; new bur every 10 specimens.

**Glazing**: Material-specific glazes applied (IPS Ivocolor for LDC; Celtra Universal Overglaze for ZLC; Dentsply Sirona Universal Overglaze for TrZ); all fired per manufacturer protocol.

**Polishing kits**: LDC → Optrafine (Ivoclar Vivadent, 3-step); ZLC → StarTec (Edenta, 2-step); TrZ → EVE Diacera (EVE Ernst Vetter, 2-step).

**Thermocycling**: 10,000 cycles, 5–55 °C, dwell 10 s, transfer 5 s; coffee solution (Nescafe Classic 1 g/100 mL) in both baths.

**Measurements**: Contact profilometer (Perthometer M2, Mahr); tristimulus colorimeter (Konica Minolta CR-321); SEM for surface morphology.

**Statistics**: One-way ANOVA, repeated-measures ANOVA, Kruskal–Wallis, Duncan post-hoc; α = 0.05.

## Key Results

**Surface roughness after grinding (Ra, μm):**
- ZLC: 2.59 ± 0.29 (highest; p < 0.001 vs LDC and TrZ)
- LDC: 2.00 ± 0.30
- TrZ: 1.08 ± 0.17 (lowest; zirconia hardness ~1485 HV vs ZLC ~700 HV, LDC ~580 HV explains difference)

**Post-finishing Ra (pre-thermocycling):**
- TrZ-P had significantly lower Ra than TrZ-G (polishing superior for zirconia)
- ZLC-G and LDC-G showed lower Ra than polished counterparts (not significant)

**After thermocycling Ra changes:**
- Ra increased significantly in all glazed groups (p = 0.025 overall)
- TrZ-P and ZLC-P showed no significant Ra change (stable polished surfaces)

**Color stability (ΔE₀₀, CIEDE2000):**
| Group | ΔE₀₀ Median | Clinically acceptable |
|---|---|---|
| LDC-G | 0.23 | ✓ (lowest) |
| TrZ-P | 0.25 | ✓ |
| TrZ-G | 0.67 | ✓ |
| ZLC-G | 0.79 | ✓ |
| LDC-P | 0.78 | ✓ |
| ZLC-P | 1.84 | ✓ (but perceptible; threshold 1.25) |

**Translucency (TP, pre-thermocycling):**
- LDC highest TP; TrZ lowest TP
- Thermocycling significantly decreased TP in ZLC-G, ZLC-P, TrZ-G, TrZ-P, LDC-G (not LDC-P)
- LDC-G: ΔTP = 2.22 (exceeded perceptibility threshold 1.33 but < acceptability limit 4.43)

## Limitations

- Both surfaces of disc specimens exposed to staining solution (not one-sided as clinical scenario)
- Only coffee tested; other staining agents (red wine, tea) not included
- No dynamic occlusal loading simulation
- SEM was qualitative only; no post-aging SEM
- Disc specimens (flat) may not fully represent complex restoration geometries

## Glossary

- **Ra**: arithmetic mean surface roughness (μm) — average deviation of surface from mean line
- **Rz**: mean peak-to-valley height (μm) — average over five sampling lengths
- **ΔE₀₀**: color difference by CIEDE2000 formula; perceptibility threshold = 1.25; acceptability threshold = 2.23
- **TP (Translucency Parameter)**: color difference between black and white backgrounds; perceptibility ΔTP > 1.33; acceptability ΔTP > 4.43
- **LDC**: lithium disilicate ceramic (리튬 이규산염 세라믹)
- **ZLC**: zirconia-reinforced lithium silicate ceramic (지르코니아 강화 리튬 규산염 세라믹) ~10% ZrO₂ content
- **TrZ**: translucent zirconia (반투명 지르코니아); 1485 HV hardness
- **t→m phase transformation**: tetragonal-to-monoclinic zirconia phase change induced by thermocycling, increasing light scattering at grain boundaries and reducing TP
