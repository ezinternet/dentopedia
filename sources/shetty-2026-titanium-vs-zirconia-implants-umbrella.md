## Why Ingested

기존 [[overviews/zirconia-implant-clinical-outcomes]]는 지르코니아 임플란트 단독 성과(Mohseni 2024 SR+MA, Pieralli 2017)만 다루고 티타늄과의 **직접 비교** umbrella review는 부재했다. 본 논문(Shetty 2026)은 6편 SR(2014–2023)을 종합한 overview of reviews로 생존율·MBL·PES 등에서 Ti > Zr 방향성을 보고해, [[implants/mohseni-2024-clinical-outcomes-zirconia-implants]]의 "설계만 좋으면 10년 티타늄과 동등" 결론과 긴장 관계를 형성한다 — 이 비교축을 overview에 보강하기 위해 인제스트.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (overview of reviews) synthesizing 6 systematic reviews (2014–2023, AMSTAR-2 mixed quality) comparing titanium (Ti) vs zirconia (Zr) dental implants on survival, success, marginal bone loss (MBL), probing depth, plaque index, bleeding on probing, pink esthetic score (PES), and osseointegration.

Ti showed higher survival (92.6–100% vs Zr 87.5–93.3%) and success rates in most included SRs (Elnayef 2017: Zr ~89% higher failure risk; Duan 2023 meta-analysis: survival RR 0.91, success RR 0.87 favoring Ti); MBL, PPD, PI, and BoP were largely comparable or mixed across reviews; Zr showed better esthetics in some reviews (PES) and faster early (2-week) bone apposition, reversing by 4 weeks.

Qualitative narrative synthesis only (no quantitative pooling across the six SRs, no overlap/CCA analysis reported); conclusions should be read alongside [[implants/mohseni-2024-clinical-outcomes-zirconia-implants]], whose larger, more recent SR+MA attributes most zirconia failure to modifiable design factors (two-piece, drill-prepared coronal) rather than the material itself.

## 세줄요약

Umbrella review (overview of reviews) — 2014–2023년 SR 6편(AMSTAR-2 품질 혼재)을 종합해 티타늄 vs 지르코니아 임플란트를 생존율·성공률·변연골소실(MBL)·탐침깊이·치태지수·출혈지수·핑크심미점수(PES)·골유착 측면에서 비교.

티타늄이 대부분 SR에서 생존율(92.6–100% vs 지르코니아 87.5–93.3%)·성공률 우위(Elnayef 2017: 지르코니아 실패위험 약 89% 높음; Duan 2023 메타분석 생존 RR 0.91, 성공 RR 0.87 티타늄 우세); MBL·탐침깊이·치태지수·출혈지수는 대체로 비슷하거나 결과 혼재; 지르코니아는 일부 SR에서 심미(PES) 우위 및 2주차 초기 골접촉 우세(4주차엔 역전).

6편 SR 간 정량 통합(메타분석)이나 중복도(CCA) 분석 없는 정성적 서술 종합에 그침 — 더 크고 최신인 [[implants/mohseni-2024-clinical-outcomes-zirconia-implants]] SR+MA는 실패 원인을 재료 자체보다 2-piece·드릴가공 등 수정가능한 설계 요인으로 귀속시키므로 함께 해석 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First identified umbrella review directly comparing Ti vs Zr implant clinical outcomes head-to-head (existing wiki zirconia-implant papers characterize Zr largely on its own terms).
- Consolidates 6 SRs (2014–2023, 3,364 records screened → 6 included) with AMSTAR-2 methodological quality grading (4 high, 1 moderate, 1 low).
- Reports per-outcome directional counts (how many of the 6 SRs favored Ti vs Zr vs equal) rather than a single pooled effect — useful as a qualitative counting synthesis distinct from Mohseni 2024's formal meta-analysis.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
PRISMA-guided overview of systematic reviews. PICOS: adults with ≥2 implants (Ti, Ti-Zr hybrid, or Zr); intervention = veneered/monolithic Zr implants; comparator = Ti implants; outcomes = survival, BoP, MBL, PPD, osseointegration (primary), PI, PES, failure reasons (secondary); study period 2014–March 2023. Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar. Included only SRs (with/without MA) of ≥6 months follow-up comparing Ti and Zr implants; excluded in vitro/animal SRs and non-SR primary study types. Screening: 3,364 records → 1,231 duplicates removed → 1,795 excluded at title/abstract → 16 assessed for eligibility (out of 280 reported at a later stage — numbers as reported in the source, some internal inconsistency in the funnel) → 6 SRs included. Two independent reviewers, third-reviewer arbitration. AMSTAR-2 used for quality appraisal. Data synthesis descriptive/qualitative only.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Survival**: Ti > Zr in 5/6 SRs (Zr 87.5–93.3% vs Ti 92.6–100%); Duan 2023 meta-analysis RR 0.91 (95% CI 0.82–1.02, p=0.10, I²=0%) favoring Ti numerically (NS); Elnayef 2017: Zr ~89% higher failure risk vs Ti.
- **Success**: Ti > Zr in 2/4 SRs, equal in 2/4; Duan 2023 meta-analysis RR 0.87 (95% CI 0.78–0.98, p=0.03, I²=0%) — statistically significant Ti advantage.
- **MBL**: mixed — 2/4 SRs found Zr worse, 2/4 found equal (Ti −1.17 to −0.125 mm; Ti-Zr hybrid −0.6 to −0.32 mm; Zr −1.38 to −0.25 mm).
- **PPD/PI/BoP**: mostly equal or mixed direction across the 2 SRs reporting each.
- **PES**: 1 SR favored Zr, 1 favored Ti.
- **Osseointegration**: 1 SR (Sivaraman 2018) — comparable overall; Zr higher bone-to-implant contact at 2 weeks (54–55% vs 42–52%), Ti higher at 4 weeks (68–91% vs 62–80%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Purely narrative/qualitative synthesis — no quantitative pooling across the six included SRs, unlike Mohseni 2024's direct meta-analysis.
- No corrected covered area (overlap) analysis reported for the six SRs' primary-study overlap.
- Only 6 SRs included (small evidence base), heterogeneous outcome definitions and follow-up windows (6–80 months across primary studies).
- Does not stratify Zr failure by design (one-piece vs two-piece) or chairside modification the way Mohseni 2024 does — a key confound, since Mohseni attributes most Zr failure to two-piece/drill-prepared designs rather than the ceramic itself.
- AMSTAR-2 quality of included SRs ranged low-to-high; one SR (Sivaraman 2018) rated low quality.

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- **Ti**: titanium; **Zr**: zirconia/zirconium dioxide implant.
- **AMSTAR-2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews, version 2 — methodological quality appraisal tool for SRs.
- **PES**: Pink Esthetic Score — soft-tissue esthetic index around implants.
- **BoP / PPD / PI / MBL**: bleeding on probing / probing pocket depth / plaque index / marginal bone loss.
- **RR (risk ratio) / OR (odds ratio)**: effect-size measures used in the pooled sub-analyses (Duan 2023, Elnayef 2017) cited within this overview.
