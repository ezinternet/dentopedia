## Why Ingested

Bridges the NSAID-osseointegration question to the specific case of chronic low-dose (cardioprotective) aspirin: it quantifies that this dose transiently delays early bone-to-implant contact but fully recovers by 28 days. Cross-links the NSAID-implant review [[wiki/drug/analgesics/etikala-2019-nsaids-periodontal-implant-therapy-review]] and the antiplatelet aspirin cluster [[wiki/drug/anticoagulants/patrono-2024-low-dose-aspirin-prevention-atherosclerotic]].

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (32 male Wistar rats, machined-surface Ti implant in tibia): four groups received 42 days of pre-surgical daily gavage of saline vs low-dose aspirin (6.75 mg/kg ≈ human 75-80 mg), then were assessed at 7 or 28 days post-implant by histomorphometry (BIC, BABT).

Aspirin significantly reduced bone-to-implant contact at 7 days (62.8% vs 82.7%, p<0.05) but the difference vanished by 28 days (91.9% vs 89.9%, NS); bone area between threads followed the same transient pattern.

Low-dose aspirin causes only a discrete, self-limiting inhibition of the early phase of osseointegration and does not contraindicate implants; caution is warranted mainly for aggressive early loading.

## 세줄요약

동물 연구 (수컷 Wistar 랫 32마리, 경골에 기계가공 표면 티타늄 임플란트): 4개 군에 이식 42일 전부터 생리식염수 vs 저용량 아스피린 (Low-dose Aspirin, 6.75 mg/kg ≈ 인체 75-80 mg)을 매일 경구 투여하고 이식 후 7일·28일에 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)으로 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)과 나사산 사이 골면적 (Bone Area Between Threads, BABT)을 측정했다.

아스피린은 7일째 BIC를 유의하게 낮췄으나 (62.8% vs 82.7%, p<0.05) 28일째에는 차이가 사라졌고 (91.9% vs 89.9%, 유의차 없음), BABT도 동일한 일시적 패턴을 보였다.

저용량 아스피린은 골유착 (Osseointegration) 초기 단계만 경미하게·일시적으로 억제할 뿐 임플란트 금기가 아니며, 주의는 주로 공격적인 조기 하중 (Early Loading)에 국한된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Characterizes, for the first time at the specifically cardioprotective LOW dose (not the analgesic/anti-inflammatory dose), how chronic aspirin affects peri-implant bone formation.
- Uses a clinically relevant chronic-exposure model: dosing starts 42 days BEFORE implant surgery, mimicking a patient already on long-term low-dose aspirin.
- Separates early (7-day) from late (28-day) healing, revealing that the aspirin effect is a transient early-phase deficit rather than a permanent impairment.
- Provides quantitative histomorphometric endpoints (BIC, BABT) usable for clinical risk framing and for chairside loading decisions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 32 male Wistar rats randomized to four groups (n=8): CG-7, AG-7 (control vs aspirin, 7 days) and CG-28, AG-28 (control vs aspirin, 28 days). ARRIVE guidelines followed.
- Drug: aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) 6.75 mg/kg once daily by oral gavage; dose obtained from human 75-80 mg via FDA body-surface-area animal-human conversion. Controls received saline vehicle.
- Exposure timeline: 42 days of pre-surgical gavage to reach steady state, continued for 7 or 28 days after implant placement.
- Implant: machined (turned) commercially pure titanium, 1.5 mm x 8.0 mm, installed in the tibia.
- Outcomes (undecalcified histology + histomorphometry): bone-to-implant contact (BIC, % of implant perimeter in bone contact) and bone area between threads (BABT, % of inter-thread area filled with bone).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Group | Aspirin | Control | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BIC | 7 days | AG-7 62.8% ± 17.1 | CG-7 82.7% ± 15.2 | p < 0.05 (reduced) |
| BIC | 28 days | AG-28 91.9% ± 5.4 | CG-28 89.9% ± 9.7 | NS (recovered) |
| BABT | 7 days | AG-7 70.9% ± 15.2 | controls higher | trend lower |
| BABT | 28 days | AG-28 95.4% ± 3.7 | controls comparable | recovered |

- Both BIC and BABT were higher at 28 days than at 7 days within every group (normal peri-implant bone maturation).
- The inhibitory effect of low-dose aspirin was confined to the early (7-day) stage and no longer detectable at the late (28-day) stage.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only a machined (turned) implant surface was tested; modern moderately-rough surfaces osseointegrate faster and may not show the same early deficit.
- Only two healing intervals (7 and 28 days) were sampled — the exact recovery point between them is unknown.
- Rat tibia differs from human jaw bone in density, loading and remodeling rate; biomechanical (removal-torque) and direct molecular endpoints were not measured, so the mechanistic account is inferential.

## 7. Glossary

- BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact): percentage of the implant surface/perimeter in direct contact with bone; primary index of osseointegration.
- BABT (Bone Area Between Threads): fraction of the area between implant threads occupied by bone; indexes peri-implant bone quantity.
- PGE2 (Prostaglandin E2): COX-derived lipid mediator driving early vasodilation and angiogenesis in new bone; suppressed by aspirin.
- BMP-2 (Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2): osteogenic growth factor promoting osteoblast differentiation and bone formation.
- CBFA-1 (Core-Binding Factor Alpha-1 / Runx2): master transcription factor for osteoblast differentiation.
- VEGF (Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor): angiogenic factor supporting vascularization of healing bone.
- RANKL (Receptor Activator of Nuclear factor Kappa-B Ligand): osteoclastogenesis signal; aspirin suppresses osteoclast formation via this pathway.
