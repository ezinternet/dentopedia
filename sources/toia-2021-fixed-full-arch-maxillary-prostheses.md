## Three-line Summary

Multicentre RCT, 56 edentulous patients (280 implants) across three centres, randomized to 4-implant (4-I) or 6-implant (6-I) groups supported by a titanium CAD/CAM milled framework fixed complete denture (FCD) in the maxilla, followed for 3 years.

Implant survival was 100% (4-I) and 99% (6-I); marginal bone level (MBL) change was not significantly different between groups over 3 years despite a statistically significant baseline-to-3-year MBL difference (4-I: 0.30±0.50→0.24±0.38 mm; 6-I: 0.14±0.32→0.12±0.26 mm); technical/biological complication rates were low (1.6%/6.0%) but prosthetic complications (mainly tooth chipping/fracture) affected 47.2% of FCDs.

Four implants are a feasible, more cost-effective alternative to six for maxillary full-arch fixed rehabilitation when bone augmentation is to be avoided, but clinicians should counsel patients on the likelihood of prosthetic maintenance visits.

## 세줄요약

다기관 RCT, 무치악 환자 56명(임플란트 280개)을 4-임플란트군과 6-임플란트군(티타늄 CAD/CAM 밀링 프레임워크 상악 전악 고정성 보철)으로 무작위배정해 3년간 추적.

임플란트 생존율은 4-I군 100%, 6-I군 99%였고 3년간 변연골수준(Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화는 두 군 간 유의한 차이가 없었으나(4-I: 0.30±0.50→0.24±0.38mm, 6-I: 0.14±0.32→0.12±0.26mm) 기저치·3년 시점 절대 MBL은 유의하게 달랐고, 기술적·생물학적 합병증률은 낮았지만(1.6%/6.0%) 보철 합병증(주로 인공치 파절/칩핑)은 전체 보철물의 47.2%에서 발생.

4개 임플란트는 골이식을 피하고 비용효율을 우선할 때 실행 가능한 대안이나, 임상가는 환자에게 보철 유지관리 내원 가능성을 사전에 설명해야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the few multicentre (3-centre) RCTs directly randomizing implant number (4 vs 6) for maxillary full-arch fixed prostheses using a single standardized prosthetic design (titanium CAD/CAM milled framework FCD), reducing confounding from framework material/design that plagues retrospective full-arch comparisons.
- Provides 3-year MBL, implant survival, clinical parameter, and complication-rate data as an early-outcome companion to a subsequent 5-year follow-up of the same cohort (Toia et al. 2025).
- Directly informs the cost-effectiveness vs biological-safety trade-off in the 4-implant ("All-on-4"-type) vs 6-implant treatment-planning decision for the edentulous maxilla.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, multicentre (3 centres), randomized controlled trial.
- **Population**: 56 edentulous patients receiving maxillary implant-supported FCDs; 280 implants placed in total, allocated to 4-implant (4-I) or 6-implant (6-I) treatment groups.
- **Intervention**: Fixed complete denture (FCD) supported by a titanium CAD/CAM milled framework; groups differed only in number of supporting implants (4 vs 6).
- **Outcomes assessed**: Primary outcome — marginal bone level (MBL) change from baseline to 3 years. Secondary — implant survival, clinical periodontal/peri-implant parameters at each follow-up visit, technical complications, biological complications, prosthetic complications.
- **Follow-up**: Baseline, 1 year, 3 years (radiographic MBL measured at these timepoints at minimum).
- **Statistical approach**: Analysis accounted for clustering effects (implants nested within patients), consistent with cluster-adjusted methods appropriate for multi-implant-per-patient full-arch RCT data.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | 4-Implant (4-I) | 6-Implant (6-I) |
|---|---|---|
| Implant survival | 100% | 99% |
| MBL at baseline | 0.30 ± 0.50 mm | 0.14 ± 0.32 mm |
| MBL at 1 year | 0.24 ± 0.31 mm | 0.16 ± 0.35 mm |
| MBL at 3 years | 0.24 ± 0.38 mm | 0.12 ± 0.26 mm |

- MBL change over the 3-year follow-up was **not** significantly different between the 4-I and 6-I groups when clustering effects were considered, though the absolute MBL values differed significantly between groups at baseline and at 3 years.
- No significant between-group differences in clinical parameters at any timepoint or across visits.
- Technical complication rate: 1.6%; biological complication rate: 6.0%.
- Prosthetic complications (predominantly chipping/fracture of prosthetic teeth) affected 25 of 53 evaluable FCDs (47.2%) — the dominant complication category by far.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Extracted PDF is an abstract-level webpage capture; granular per-group statistical tables (p-values, confidence intervals, regression models) beyond the abstract were not available in the extracted text and should be re-checked against the original PDF/journal supplementary tables (Table S1–S4) if finer detail is needed.
- 3-year follow-up is a relatively early timepoint for full-arch implant prosthodontics; the companion 5-year report (Toia et al. 2025, same cohort) should be consulted for longer-term stability of the non-significant MBL/survival findings.
- High prosthetic (tooth chipping/fracture) complication rate (47.2%) is a notable practical burden not fully explored mechanistically in the abstract-level text — framework/material or occlusal-scheme contributors would need the full-text Discussion.
- Sample size (56 patients / 280 implants across 3 centres) is modest for definitively ruling out a clinically meaningful difference in MBL between 4-I and 6-I groups; authors report statistical clustering-adjustment but a formal power calculation for the MBL primary endpoint was not evident from the abstract text.

## 6. Related Work

- Companion 5-year follow-up of the same trial cohort: Toia et al. 2025, "Fixed Full-Arch Maxillary Prostheses Supported by Four Versus Six Implants: 5-Year Results of a Multicenter Randomized Clinical Trial."

## 7. Glossary

- **FCD (Fixed Complete Denture)**: Implant-supported fixed full-arch prosthesis replacing all teeth in an edentulous arch.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: Radiographically measured distance from a reference point (e.g., implant shoulder) to the first bone-to-implant contact; used as a proxy for peri-implant bone loss/stability over time.
- **CAD/CAM milled framework**: Computer-aided design/computer-aided manufacturing milled (subtractive) titanium substructure for the prosthesis, as opposed to cast frameworks.
- **4-I / 6-I**: Shorthand for the four-implant and six-implant treatment groups in this RCT.
