## Three-line Summary

Randomized clinical trial (34 patients, 80 platform-switching posterior implants) testing supracrestal tissue height (STH ≤2 vs >2 mm) × crestal/subcrestal placement on marginal bone level change (ΔMBC).

Mean STH was 1.65 mm in group A vs 3.11 mm in group B; significantly more mesial ΔMBC occurred in A-I (thin STH + crestal) than B-I at T0–T1 (6 months), whereas ΔMBC did not differ by placement depth when STH >2 mm.

Abstract-only ingest (publisher preview): STH >2 mm protects marginal bone early regardless of depth and subcrestal placement is advised when STH is insufficient; exact ΔMBC values were not retrievable.

## 세줄요약

무작위 대조 임상시험 (환자 34명, 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching) 후방 임플란트 80개): 초치조정 조직 높이(STH, Supracrestal Tissue Height; ≤2mm vs >2mm) × 식립 위치(치조정 vs 하방)에 따른 변연골 수준 변화(ΔMBC) 비교.

평균 STH는 A군 1.65mm, B군 3.11mm; T0–T1(6개월)에 A-I군(얇은 STH+치조정)이 B-I군보다 근심측 ΔMBC가 유의하게 컸고, STH >2mm에서는 식립 위치 간 ΔMBC 차이가 유의하지 않음.

초록 전용 인제스트(출판사 미리보기): STH >2mm는 식립 깊이와 무관하게 초기 변연골을 보호하며 STH 부족 시 하방 식립이 유리 — 정확한 ΔMBC 수치는 미확보.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tests STH as a modifier of ΔMBC in platform-switching posterior implants placed crestally vs subcrestally (null hypothesis: STH has no effect).
- Two main groups by STH (A ≤2 mm, B >2 mm) crossed with two placement levels (I crestal, II subcrestal) → A-I, A-II, B-I, B-II.
- First study, per the authors, to report subcrestal placement depth vs marginal bone level change separately for mesial and distal aspects.
- Reports a clinically actionable threshold: STH >2 mm for early marginal bone stabilization regardless of placement depth.
- Suggests subcrestal placement as a rescue strategy when STH is insufficient, preventing bone loss beyond the implant shoulder.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial (parallel groups); single-center (Kutahya Health Sciences University ethics approval).
- **n**: 34 patients, 80 implants.
- **Groups**: STH ≤2 mm (A) vs STH >2 mm (B); crestal (I) vs subcrestal (II) placement → A-I, A-II, B-I, B-II.
- **Measurements**: STH and implant placement depth measured intraoperatively from mesial and distal aspects.
- **Outcomes**: Mesial and distal peri-implant marginal bone levels on periapical radiographs at T0 (immediately), T1 (6 months), T2 (9 months), T3 (12 months) after functional loading; ΔMBC = difference between marginal bone levels.
- **Context variables**: Keratinized mucosa width (KMW) reported; subcrestal placement depth compared between A-II and B-II.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Mean STH: group A = 1.65 mm; group B = 3.11 mm.
- Subcrestal placement depth (mesial and distal): no statistically significant difference between A-II and B-II (p > 0.05).
- T0–T1 (6 months): statistically significantly more mesial ΔMBC in A-I than in B-I (thin STH + crestal vs adequate STH + crestal).
- In the STH >2 mm group: ΔMBC difference between crestally and subcrestally placed implants was not statistically significant.
- Discussion wording: a lower ΔMBC value was associated with an STH above 3 mm (paper's own phrasing; pre-specified grouping used a 2 mm cut-off).
- Conclusion: STH >2 mm is necessary to preserve marginal bone, especially early, regardless of placement depth; subcrestal placement is beneficial when STH is insufficient.
- Exact ΔMBC millimeter values, per-group tables (Table 1, Table 2), and later timepoint (T2, T3) numbers were not present in the retrieved preview — abstract-only.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only source (publisher landing/preview page): full Results tables and exact ΔMBC magnitudes unavailable; no effect sizes or confidence intervals retrievable.
- Authors state larger samples and longer follow-up are needed to evaluate STH and placement depth effects on ΔMBC.
- Follow-up reported to 12 months (T3) after functional loading; the PDF preview only gives a significant result at T0–T1.
- The paper uses both a >2 mm (grouping/conclusion) and an >3 mm (discussion) phrasing for the protective STH threshold; both are reproduced verbatim rather than reconciled.
- Single-center trial with 34 patients / 80 implants.

## 7. Glossary

- **STH**: Supracrestal tissue height (초치조정 조직 높이) — vertical dimension of peri-implant soft tissue; grouping factor here (≤2 mm vs >2 mm).
- **ΔMBC**: Marginal bone level change (변연골 수준 변화) — difference between marginal bone levels measured on periapical radiographs.
- **PS**: Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭) — abutment narrowed relative to implant platform.
- **Crestal (I)**: implant shoulder at the alveolar crest (치조정 식립).
- **Subcrestal (II)**: implant shoulder below the alveolar crest (하방 식립).
- **KMW**: Keratinized mucosa width (각화점막 폭) — reported context variable in this trial.
