## Why Ingested

The existing platform-switching SR+MAs in this wiki ([[implants/mbl/di-girolamo-2016-platform-switching-matching-sr-ma]] and [[implants/mbl/strietzel-2015-platform-switching-mbl-sr-ma]]) focus specifically on platform-switching design comparisons; this Kumar 2021 meta-analysis provides a complementary pooled estimate of absolute 1-year marginal bone loss (0.56 mm) across loading protocols, offering a baseline MBL benchmark independent of connection design that reinforces the acceptability threshold discussion anchored in Albrektsson criteria.

## Three-line Summary

Meta-analysis of 5 prospective studies (525 implants) evaluated marginal bone level (변연골 수준, MBL) changes at 1 year after implant loading.

Pooled mean marginal bone loss (변연골 소실, MBL) was 0.56 mm (Z=7.48, p=0.01); significant heterogeneity was present (tau=0.03, p=0.041, I²=99.8%).

Mean 1-year MBL of 0.56 mm is within acceptable criteria (<1.5 mm per Albrektsson), but the extreme heterogeneity (I²=99.8%) limits generalizability and highlights the need for standardized reporting protocols.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Established a pooled 1-year marginal bone loss (변연골 소실, MBL) estimate of **0.56 mm** across multiple loading protocols.
- Confirmed that 1-year MBL within Albrektsson's success criteria (<1.5 mm first year, <0.2 mm annually thereafter) is achievable across diverse implant systems.
- Highlighted the platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭) benefit: PS implants showed 0.01–0.95 mm MBL vs ~1.67 mm in conventional controls.
- Identified significant heterogeneity (이질성) (I²=99.8%) as a major limitation necessitating standardized MBL reporting.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- MEDLINE literature search: 1980–2018.
- Inclusion criteria: randomized controlled trials (무작위 대조 임상시험, RCT) or prospective studies, minimum 1-year follow-up, marginal bone level data reported.
- 5 studies included; 525 implants total.
- Statistical model: random-effects meta-analysis; Z-test and p-values; heterogeneity assessed via I², tau², and Cochran Q (p=0.041).
- Pooled estimate: weighted mean MBL.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Pooled 1-year MBL | 0.56 mm |
| Z-statistic | 7.48 |
| p-value (pooled effect) | 0.01 |
| I² (heterogeneity) | 99.8% |
| tau (heterogeneity) | 0.03 |
| Cochran Q p-value | 0.041 |
| Platform switching MBL range | 0.01–0.95 mm |
| Control (conventional) MBL | ~1.67 mm |

- Albrektsson success threshold: <1.5 mm 1st year, <0.2 mm/year thereafter.
- Most MBL occurred in the first year after implant loading.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 5 studies included — small evidence base.
- Extreme heterogeneity (I²=99.8%) severely limits meta-analytic conclusions.
- Small number of implants per study.
- Potential publication bias not formally assessed.
- Limited to English-language publications.
- No subgroup analysis by implant system, loading protocol type, or patient factors.
