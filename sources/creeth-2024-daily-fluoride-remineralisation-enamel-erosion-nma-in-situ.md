This NMA qualifies the stannous-superiority claim of [[dental-erosion/gomez-2026-anti-erosive-dentifrices-umbrella-review]] under a remineralisation-weighted protocol. → [[dental-erosion/creeth-2024-daily-fluoride-remineralisation-enamel-erosion-nma-in-situ]]

## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis (NMA) of 14 single-protocol, crossover in situ studies (22 treatments; subject-level data; mixed model; healthy adults wearing palatal appliances with eroded bovine enamel, single product use, 4 h intra-oral remineralisation, then a second acid challenge; outcomes surface microhardness recovery, SMHR, and acid resistance ratio, ARR). All 14 studies were sponsored and funded by Haleon (formerly GSK Consumer Healthcare), and three of five authors are affiliated with Haleon; the set is the sponsor's own complete in situ series, not a systematic review.

Fluoride showed a clear dose-response: 1150 ppm F as NaF vs fluoride-free placebo gave SMHR +8.8% (SE 0.6%; 33.0% vs 24.2%) and ARR +0.27 (SE 0.03; 0.43 vs 0.15) across 9 mutual studies (both p < 0.001); remineralisation rose linearly (+0.7% SMHR per 100 ppm F, 95% CI 0.6-0.9) whereas acid resistance plateaued near 1000 ppm F; a fluoride mouthwash after toothpaste added SMHR +2.4% (SE 1.1%, p = 0.043, 3 studies) but ARR +0.08 (p = 0.164, not significant); monofluorophosphate (MFP) toothpaste was inferior to free-fluoride NaF (SMHR -8.9%, p < 0.001).

Polyvalent metal ions (stannous, zinc) and short-chain polyphosphates sharply reduced fluoride-induced remineralisation (SnF2-zinc paste SMHR 8.8% lower than the NaF benchmark, p = 0.001, ARR equivalent), and the stannous-zinc system gave no net gain in overall protection (RER) over well-formulated NaF; findings are single-sponsor, single-model, single-exposure surface-hardness data and do not establish clinical erosive tooth wear (ETW) outcomes.

## 세줄요약

14개 단일 프로토콜 교차설계 in situ 연구 (22개 처치, 대상자 수준 데이터, 혼합모형; 구개 장치에 부착한 침식 소 법랑질을 건강한 성인이 착용, 제품 1회 사용 후 4시간 구강내 재광화, 이후 2차 산 공격; 결과변수 표면미세경도 회복률 SMHR, 산저항비 ARR)의 네트워크 메타분석(NMA)이다. 14편 전부 Haleon (전 GSK Consumer Healthcare)이 후원·재정 지원했고 저자 5명 중 3명이 Haleon 소속이며, 이 연구 세트는 후원사 자체의 in situ 시리즈 전체로 체계적 문헌고찰이 아니다.

불소는 명확한 용량-반응을 보였다: 1150 ppm F (NaF) 대 무불소 위약은 SMHR +8.8% (SE 0.6%; 33.0% 대 24.2%), ARR +0.27 (SE 0.03; 0.43 대 0.15) (9편 공통, 모두 p < 0.001); 재광화는 100 ppm F당 SMHR +0.7% (95% CI 0.6-0.9)로 선형 증가한 반면 산저항은 약 1000 ppm F에서 정체; 불소 가글은 치약 후 추가 시 SMHR +2.4% (SE 1.1%, p = 0.043, 3편) 증가했으나 ARR +0.08은 유의하지 않았고 (p = 0.164), 모노플루오로포스페이트 (MFP) 치약은 유리불소 NaF보다 열등했다 (SMHR -8.9%, p < 0.001).

다가 금속이온 (주석, 아연)과 단쇄 폴리인산은 불소 유도 재광화를 크게 저해했고 (SnF2-아연 치약은 NaF 기준 대비 SMHR 8.8% 낮음, p = 0.001; ARR은 동등), 주석-아연 시스템은 잘 설계된 NaF 대비 전체 보호효과 (RER)에서 순이득이 없었다; 단일 후원사·단일 모델·1회 노출 표면경도 데이터이므로 임상 침식성 치아마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) 결과를 입증하지 않는다.

**Funding and affiliation (industry).** All included studies were sponsored and funded by Haleon (formerly GSK Consumer Healthcare). PubMed lists Creeth, Smith and Franks at Haleon PLC (Weybridge, UK) and Hara and Zero at the Indiana University Oral Health Research Institute, where 13 of the 14 studies were run. The studies mostly tested the sponsor's marketed products (Pronamel, Aquafresh) against competitor products. A separate conflict-of-interest statement was not present in the retrieved text.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pools the sponsor's whole in situ erosion series (22 treatments) on one protocol to estimate fluoride concentration, salt, vehicle and ingredient effects.
- Shows remineralisation (SMHR) is linear in fluoride concentration while acid resistance (ARR) plateaus near 1000 ppm F.
- Quantifies ingredient interference: polyvalent metal ions and polyphosphates reduce fluoride-driven remineralisation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Single-centre, randomised, examiner- and analyst-blind, multi-period crossover in situ studies (13 at Indiana University, 1 in the UK). Adults 18 or older in good health wore maxillary palatal appliances with up to 8 bovine enamel specimens. Sequence: baseline microhardness (B), ex vivo acid challenge (E1), 25 s brushing with 1.5 g paste plus 60 s (95 s in the last two studies) swish, 4 h intra-oral remineralisation (R), second acid challenge (E2); Knoop microhardness. Outcomes: SMHR = [(E1-R)/(E1-B)] x 100; ARR = 1 - [(E2-R)/(E1-B)]; RER (relative erosion resistance) combines the two. Mixed model on subject-period data, treatment as fixed effect, random effects for study, period, subject (Kenward-Roger df). Not a systematic review; no registration or risk-of-bias tool.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 1150 ppm F (NaF, non-SLS) vs placebo: SMHR +8.8% (SE 0.6; 33.0 vs 24.2%), ARR +0.27 (SE 0.03; 0.43 vs 0.15), RER +36.2% (SE 3.1); all p < 0.001; per-study increments SMHR 6-12%, ARR 0.15-0.47 (9 studies).
- Dose-response: SMHR +0.7% per 100 ppm F (95% CI 0.6-0.9), no non-linearity (p = 0.427); ARR non-linear (p = 0.029), plateau about 1007 ppm; RER plateau about 1078 ppm.
- MFP/SLS (1450 ppm) vs NaF non-SLS: SMHR -8.9% (SE 1.9, p < 0.001); ARR difference not significant (0.08, p = 0.406).
- Mouthwash after toothpaste: SMHR +2.4% (SE 1.1, p = 0.043; 3 studies); ARR +0.08 (SE 0.05, p = 0.164). Mouthwash-only treatments ranked above any toothpaste-only treatment; best treatment was the highest-dose toothpaste plus mouthwash.
- Short-chain polyphosphate pastes: SMHR 12.1% (C3D110) and 9.0% (PPH110) lower than NaF non-SLS (p < 0.001); ARR 0.15 lower (p = 0.099).
- SnF2-zinc (CPS110) vs NaF non-SLS: SMHR 8.8% lower (SE 1.7, p = 0.001); ARR difference 0.02 (p = 0.831); RER overall negative per authors. The SnF2-zinc-hexametaphosphate paste had the lowest SMHR but the highest ARR of the toothpastes.
- SLS: F/SLS/phosphate/carbomer paste 3.3% lower SMHR than NaF non-SLS (p = 0.006; 4 head-to-head studies); the direct SLS comparison was underpowered (SMHR 2.4%, p = 0.382).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Authors state: NMA is exploratory; poor connectivity for some products (e.g. Aquafresh) and high between-study variability, especially for ARR; all studies share one sponsor and protocol, restricting generalisability; single product exposure and surface microhardness do not measure surface loss or long-term effects; appliance not worn overnight; no abrasive challenge; bovine enamel with immature pellicle. Authors note the stannous results contrast with profilometry-based in situ studies where stannous outperformed NaF. Ingredient attribution is confounded in multi-ingredient products. Future: enamel-loss endpoint; SnF2 vs NaF comparison at 1000-1500 ppm F.
