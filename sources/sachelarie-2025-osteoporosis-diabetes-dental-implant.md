## Why Ingested
골다공증(Osteoporosis)과 T2DM에서의 임플란트 안정성(ISQ)을 12개월간 직접 비교한 파일럿 연구. [[implants/isq/diehl-2022-narrow-diameter-implant-stability-hyperglycemic]] 및 [[implants/survival/guadarrama-bello-2026-bone-healing-implants-diabetes-osteoporosis-review]]의 서술 비교에 실제 ISQ 수치를 제공함.

## One-line Summary
Prospective pilot cohort (n=50; 21 T2DM, 29 osteoporosis) showing lower ISQ in T2DM vs osteoporosis at 6 months (62.10 vs 67.04, p=0.02) and 12 months (65.79 vs 68.93), with both conditions improving over time through distinct osseointegration pathways.

## 한줄요약
T2DM 21명 vs 골다공증 29명 전향 파일럿: 6개월 ISQ 당뇨 62.10 vs 골다공증 67.04 (p=0.02), 12개월에 두 군 모두 개선되나 당뇨 군 Periotest 호전폭 더 유의.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective pilot cohort (n=50: 21 T2DM, 29 osteoporosis) comparing implant stability by ISQ and Periotest over 12 months.

ISQ was lower in T2DM at 6 months (62.10 vs 67.04, p=0.0215) and the gap narrowed by 12 months (65.79 vs 68.93); both groups improved, and Periotest change at 12 months was more pronounced in the diabetic group (p=0.0001).

Both conditions can reach acceptable stability but diabetic implants stabilise later; the small single-centre pilot without blinding or HbA1c data needs confirmation.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 안정성을 12개월간 ISQ와 Periotest로 비교한 전향적 파일럿 코호트 (50명: 제2형 당뇨 21명, 골다공증 29명)다.

6개월 ISQ는 당뇨군이 더 낮았고 (62.10 대 67.04, p=0.0215) 12개월에는 차이가 줄었으며 (65.79 대 68.93), 두 군 모두 호전되었고 12개월 Periotest 변화는 당뇨군에서 더 뚜렷했다 (p=0.0001).

두 질환 모두 허용 가능한 안정성에 도달할 수 있으나 당뇨군은 안정화가 늦다; 소규모 단일기관 파일럿이며 눈가림과 HbA1c 자료가 없어 확인이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct head-to-head ISQ comparison between T2DM and osteoporosis in same follow-up protocol
- 12-month longitudinal data: both conditions show significant ISQ improvement
- Periotest M used in parallel with Osstell ISQ — dual measurement approach
- Identifies distinct osseointegration trajectories: diabetes delays ISQ but eventual improvement possible
- Both conditions amenable to implant therapy with appropriate management

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective cohort (pilot)
- **Population**: 50 patients — 21 T2DM, 29 osteoporosis; implants placed at single center
- **Instruments**: Osstell ISQ + Periotest M at 6 and 12 months post-placement
- **Statistics**: Between-group comparisons at each time point; within-group time comparisons; α = 0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**ISQ (Osstell):**
| Time | Osteoporosis | T2DM | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 months | 67.04 ± 5.42 | 62.10 ± 5.46 | 0.0215 |
| 12 months | 68.93 ± 4.83 | 65.79 ± 3.87 | — |

**Periotest M (lower = more stable):**
| Time | Osteoporosis | T2DM | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 months | -3.34 ± 1.59 | -2.81 ± 1.44 | 0.0001 |

- Both groups showed significant ISQ improvement from 6 to 12 months
- T2DM group showed more significant reduction in implant mobility (Periotest) at 12 months
- ISQ gap between groups narrowed from 4.94 at 6mo to 3.14 at 12mo — diabetes group catching up

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=50 total, 21 DM); underpowered for subgroup analyses
- Pilot study designation — results require confirmation
- No glycemic control data (HbA1c not reported)
- 12-month follow-up only — long-term failure rates unknown
- Single center, no blinding

## 7. Glossary
- **Periotest M**: Electromechanical percussion device measuring implant mobility
- **Osstell ISQ**: Resonance frequency analysis device (0–100 scale)
- **Primary stability**: Mechanical stability immediately post-insertion
- **Secondary stability**: Biological osseointegration-derived stability
