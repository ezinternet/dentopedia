## Why Ingested

MID(최소개입치의학)의 **효능 근거가 아니라 실제 이행률**을 재는 기준점(base-line) 논문. 위키에는 MID 무엇이 효과적인지(SDF·Hall Technique·선택적 제거 등)를 다룬 [[caries/banihani-2021-minimal-intervention-primary-teeth-carious-umbrella-review]]가 있지만, "치과의사가 그 근거를 실제로 얼마나 따르는가"는 별도 축이다. 이 논문은 일본 JDPBRN 치과의사 206명의 근거 일치도(concordance)를 10개 임상 문항으로 정량화해 평균 60%를 보고하며, 영어 논문을 자주 읽는 치과의사만 일치도가 유의하게 높았다(OR 2.45) — 이 위키 자체의 존재 이유(RAG로 근거 접근성 낮추기)와 직결되는 근거. [[caries/caries]] 카테고리 개요의 MID 섹션에 practice-gap 축을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional web survey of 297 JDPBRN-affiliated Japanese dentists (206 responded, 69%; Jan–Nov 2017) using 10 clinical questions adapted from the US National Dental PBRN's Practice Impact Questionnaire, each response coded concordant or not with published minimal intervention dentistry (MID) evidence.

Mean and median overall concordance were both 60% (SD 18, IQR 50–75%); area means were primary caries 68%, deep caries 53%, restoration 54%; lowest items were use of magnification 33%, repair of defective composite with cementum-dentinal margins 48%, and caries risk assessment 51%.

Multiple logistic regression (n=197): female gender OR 2.33 (95% CI 1.01–5.39), clinic in a government-ordinance-designated city OR 2.01 (1.02–3.96), and frequent use of English-language scientific journal articles OR 2.45 (1.08–5.59) were associated with higher-than-median concordance (predictive accuracy 72.6%).

## 세줄요약

일본 JDPBRN(일본 치과 practice-based research network) 소속 치과의사 297명 대상 웹 설문(응답 206명, 응답률 69%; 2017년 1–11월) — 미국 National Dental PBRN의 Practice Impact Questionnaire에서 따온 10개 임상 문항으로 최소개입치의학(Minimal Intervention Dentistry, MID) 근거 일치도를 측정.

전체 일치도 평균·중앙값 모두 60% (SD 18, IQR 50–75%); 영역별 평균은 1차 우식 진단·치료 68%, 심부 우식 53%, 수복 54%; 최저 항목은 확대경(magnification) 사용 33%, 시멘트-상아질(cementum-dentinal) 변연 결손 레진 수복선의 수리 48%, 우식위험도평가 (Caries Risk Assessment, CRA) 51%.

다중 로지스틱 회귀(n=197): 여성 치과의사 OR 2.33 (95% CI 1.01–5.39), 정령지정도시 개원 OR 2.01 (1.02–3.96), 영어 과학논문 빈용 OR 2.45 (1.08–5.59)가 중앙값(60%) 이상 일치도와 유의 연관 — 모형 예측 정확도 72.6%.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Cross-sectional web-based questionnaire survey (practice-based research network study)
- **Journal**: Journal of Dentistry, Vol 102, Article 103469, November 2020 (online 2020; NIH author manuscript nihms-1634038)
- **Population**: 297 dentists affiliated with the Dental Practice-based Research Network Japan (JDPBRN), covering Japan's seven major regions; 206 analyzed (response rate 69%)
- **Instrument**: 10 clinical questions/scenarios on MID selected from the 12-item US National Dental PBRN Practice Impact Questionnaire (third molar extraction and defective amalgam restoration excluded — amalgam no longer covered by Japanese National Health Insurance)
- **Funding**: JSPS KAKENHI JP16K11866; NIH U19-DE-22516, U19-DE-28717

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to quantify the evidence-practice gap (EPG) specifically for MID across 10 caries-related clinical decisions in Japan, using the same instrument family as the US National Dental PBRN (direct comparability).
- Establishes a benchmark overall concordance of 60% for Japanese dentists — "medium" adherence — against the US practitioner figure of 62% (Norton et al.), VCU faculty 75%, VCU graduating students 67%.
- Identifies three modifiable/non-modifiable dentist characteristics associated with higher concordance, the most actionable being frequent use of English-language scientific journals (OR 2.45).
- Provides item-level concordance rates exposing where the gap concentrates: risk assessment, magnification use, deep-caries excavation strategy, defective-restoration repair vs replace, and proximal restoration thresholds.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cross-sectional survey, January–November 2017, web-delivered questionnaire (Japanese translation by four dentists/clinical epidemiologists); IRB-approved, informed consent obtained.
- **Concordance coding**: Each of 10 responses coded consistent ('1') or inconsistent ('0') with published evidence; overall concordance = % consistent. Three clinical areas: primary caries diagnosis/treatment (Q1–5), deep caries diagnosis/treatment (Q6–7), restoration diagnosis/treatment (Q8–10).
- **Primary analysis**: Multiple logistic regression with concordance dichotomized at the median (≥60% = high); linear regression and ordinal logistic (terciles) run as sensitivity analyses with substantively similar results.
- **Predictors**: individual characteristics (years since graduation, gender, specialty practice), practice characteristics (city population ≥700,000 designated city vs other, type of practice, busyness), evidence-source preference (internet, non-academic journal, textbook, Japanese-language journals, English-language journals, clinical practice guidelines; Frequently/Occasionally = higher frequency).
- **Software**: IBM SPSS Statistics 23; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Overall**: mean = median = 60% (SD 18, IQR 50–75%). Area means: primary caries 68%, deep caries 53%, restoration 54%.
- **Item-level concordance** (consistent responses): air drying for diagnosis 77% (159/206); occlusal 1 (brown discoloration, some fissures, no cavitation → non-invasive) 97% (200/206); occlusal 2 (most fissures) 82% (168/206); caries risk assessment 51% (106/206); magnification ≥80% of time 33% (67/206); deep caries 1 (indirect pulp cap ≥25% of time) 52% (107/206); deep caries 2 (stop decay near pulp horn) 54% (112/206); defective composite enamel margins → repair 60% (119/200); cementum-dentinal margins → repair 48% (96/200); proximal lesion depth into dentin before restoring 53% (109/206).
- **Regression** (n=197): female gender OR 2.33 (95% CI 1.01–5.39, p=0.048); government-ordinance-designated city OR 2.01 (1.02–3.96, p=0.043); English-language scientific journals OR 2.45 (1.08–5.59, p=0.033); model predictive accuracy 72.6%. Years since graduation, specialty, busyness, internet/textbook/Japanese-journal/guideline use all non-significant.
- **International benchmarks**: US National Dental PBRN practitioners 62% (2009–2010, 12-item version); VCU faculty 75%, graduating students 67%. Japan notably lower on caries risk assessment (US 85% vs JP 51%), magnification (US 57% vs JP 33%), cementum-dentinal margin repair (US 67% vs JP 48%) — authors note individual CRA is not reimbursed under Japanese National Health Insurance.
- **Evidence-source prevalence** (higher frequency): textbook 95%, internet 91%, non-academic journals 66%, Japanese journals 61%, CPGs 58%, English journals only 32%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Non-random sampling (JDPBRN volunteers) — possible sampling bias.
- Self-reported practice — recall/social-desirability bias possible.
- Cross-sectional design — no causal inference between characteristics and concordance.
- Authors' stated next steps: share results with participants as an educational intervention and measure impact; exploratory qualitative studies on EPG mechanisms.

## 6. Related Work

- Norton WE et al. (2014) JADA — origin of the 12-item instrument; US National Dental PBRN mean concordance 62%.
- Chiang HK, Best AM, Sarrett DC (2017) — same questionnaire at Virginia Commonwealth University: faculty 75%, students 67%.
- Kakudate N et al. (2019) J Dent — deep occlusal caries treatment patterns in the same sample; endodontic-related procedures remained most frequent for possible mild pulpitis.
- FDI World Dental Federation (2017) policy statement endorsing MID as contemporary caries management.

## 7. Glossary

- **EPG (evidence-practice gap)**: 격차 — 발표된 근거와 실제 임상 행동 사이의 불일치.
- **MID (Minimal Intervention Dentistry)**: 최소개입치의학 — 건전 치질 최대 보존·평생 치아 기능 유지를 지향하는 우식 관리 철학 (조기 발견·재광물화·최소침습 수복·수리).
- **JDPBRN**: Dental Practice-based Research Network Japan — 일본 7대 권역 치과의원·진료 기반 임상연구 네트워크.
- **Concordance**: 일치도 — 개별 응답을 문헌 근거와 일치('1')/불일치('0')로 코딩한 뒤 계산한 일치 응답 비율(%).
- **Indirect pulp cap**: 간접치수커버링 — 심부 우식 제거 중 치수 노출을 피하기 위해 근관치료 없이 남긴 우식 위에 약물·수복물을 놓는 처치.
- **Government-ordinance-designated city**: 정령지정도시 — 인구 70만 이상 일본 대도시 (본 연구의 도시 인구 변수).
