## Three-line Summary

Historical (retrospective) observational study at the Mashhad Faculty of Dentistry implant department, Iran (March 2018 to 2020): 983 implants (56.5% in women, mean age 49.34 ± 13.67 years, mean follow-up 13.95 ± 5.88 months); early failure was defined as osseointegration failure (mobility and/or radiographic findings) within about 3-4 months of surgery, before prosthetic loading.

Early failure occurred in 42 implants (4.3%). Surgical incidents (inferior alveolar nerve exposure, implant fixture fracture; p=0.005) and implant loading time (p<0.001) were significantly associated with early failure, whereas sex (p=0.238), age (p=0.263), systemic disease (p=0.388), smoking (p=0.316), manufacturer (p=0.066), surface (p=0.886), jaw (mandible 4.7% vs maxilla 3.8%, p=0.511), implant dimensions and grafting (p=0.351) were not.

Only univariate chi-square/Fisher/Mann-Whitney tests were reported (no effect sizes or multivariable model, number of patients not stated, tables absent from the retrieved text), only 2.2% of implants were in smokers, and the shorter follow-up in failed implants was itself significant (p<0.001), so null findings and the loading-time association cannot be read causally.

## 세줄요약

이란 마슈하드 치과대학 임플란트과에서 2018년 3월~2020년에 시행한 후향적(historical) 관찰 연구: 임플란트 983개(여성 56.5%, 평균 연령 49.34 ± 13.67세, 평균 추적 13.95 ± 5.88개월); 조기 실패 (early failure)는 수술 후 약 3~4개월 이내, 보철 하중 전에 동요도 및/또는 방사선 소견으로 확인된 골유착 실패로 정의했다.

조기 실패는 42개(4.3%)였다. 수술 중 사건(하치조신경 노출, 임플란트 고정체 파절; p=0.005)과 임플란트 하중 시기 (loading time; p<0.001)가 조기 실패와 유의하게 연관되었고, 성별(p=0.238), 연령(p=0.263), 전신질환(p=0.388), 흡연(p=0.316), 제조사(p=0.066), 표면(p=0.886), 악골(하악 4.7% 대 상악 3.8%, p=0.511), 임플란트 크기, 골이식(p=0.351)은 유의하지 않았다.

단변량 검정(카이제곱/Fisher/Mann-Whitney)만 보고되어 효과 크기나 다변량 모델이 없고, 환자 수가 기재되지 않았으며, 흡연자가 임플란트의 2.2%뿐이고, 실패군의 추적 기간이 유의하게 짧아(p<0.001) 비유의 결과와 하중 시기 연관성을 인과로 해석할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Early failure rate 4.3% (42 of 983 implants) in a 2-year Iranian university clinic series; described as the first epidemiological implant-failure study in Iran.
- Significant univariate associations: surgical incidents (IAN exposure, fixture fracture; p=0.005) and loading time (p<0.001).
- No significant association with sex, age, systemic disease, smoking, manufacturer, surface, implant dimensions, level type, site or grafting.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: historical observational study, March 2018 to 2020, Mashhad; STROBE; ethics approval from Mashhad University of Medical Sciences; consent per Helsinki; census sampling.
- **Population**: 983 implants (555 in women, 428 in men), mean age 49.34 ± 13.67 years (18-93); 217 implants (22.1%) in patients with systemic disease; 22 (2.2%) in smokers. Number of patients not reported. Mean follow-up 13.95 ± 5.88 months (1-38).
- **Early failure definition**: failure to establish osseointegration of biological origin (e.g. peri-implantitis) appearing within 3-4 months after surgery, diagnosed by recorded mobility and radiographs.
- **Variables**: demographics, systemic disease, smoking, manufacturer, surface, bone/tissue level, site, length, diameter, grafting, surgical incidents, loading time.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 22; chi-square, Fisher exact, Mann-Whitney; p<=0.05.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective archived data; bone quality/quantity, sinus-floor proximity and hormonal imbalance not examined (authors).
- Univariate tests only; no effect sizes; patients not counted; tables absent from retrieved text so the loading-time direction is not numerically verifiable.
- Few smokers (2.2%), so null findings are underpowered.
- Early window stated as 3-5 months in the Introduction and 3-4 months in Methods.
- The conclusion text says the results suggest a relationship between surgical incidents and loading time and early failure; the Discussion also contains an unrelated probiotics/postbiotic passage.

## 7. Glossary

- **Early failure (조기 실패)**: loss of osseointegration before prosthetic loading, here within about 3-4 months of surgery.
- **IAN exposure (하치조신경 노출)**: inferior alveolar nerve exposed during implant surgery.
- **Loading time (하중 시기)**: immediate versus delayed loading.
- **DIF (Dental Implant Failure)**: the paper's abbreviation for dental implant failure.
