## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD420251032758) of systematic reviews examining bruxism as a risk factor for implant-supported prosthesis failure, screening 2395 records to identify 8 eligible systematic reviews (5 with meta-analysis), published January 1994–April 2025.

Quality varied widely (2 SRs with perfect AMSTAR scores, 1 with a perfect Glenny et al score); more recent SRs and meta-analyses consistently found bruxism to be an important risk factor, with one meta-analysis reporting an odds ratio of 4.68 for implant failure in bruxers vs non-bruxers.

Clinical implication: bruxism should be treated as a significant risk factor for implant-supported prosthesis failure warranting pre-treatment screening and management; key limitation is substantial heterogeneity across the underlying systematic reviews and inconsistent bruxism-diagnosis criteria among primary studies.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review specifically assessing the methodological quality of systematic reviews on bruxism and implant-supported prosthesis failure (prior umbrella reviews on implant failure did not focus on bruxism as the sole exposure).
- Applies dual quality-appraisal frameworks (AMSTAR + Glenny et al checklist) to the 8 included SRs, exposing wide variance in methodological rigor despite converging conclusions.
- Synthesizes across 5 meta-analyses to report a pooled directional signal (bruxers at higher failure risk than non-bruxers), with one MA reporting OR 4.68.
- Registered a priori in PROSPERO (CRD420251032758), strengthening the umbrella review's own methodological credibility.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews)
- **Databases**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus; supplemented by reference-list hand-searching
- **Date range**: January 1994–April 2025
- **n**: 2395 initial records → 50 full-text screened → 8 systematic reviews included (5 with meta-analysis)
- **Quality tools**: AMSTAR (measurement tool for SR methodological quality); Glenny et al checklist (dentistry-specific SR appraisal)
- **Outcomes**: Implant-supported prosthesis failure/complication rate stratified by bruxism status; SR/MA methodological quality scores

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 8/50 screened full-text SRs met eligibility; 5 of the 8 included meta-analysis.
- Considerable heterogeneity among the 8 included SRs (populations, bruxism-diagnosis criteria, outcome definitions).
- 2 SRs achieved perfect AMSTAR scores; 1 SR achieved a perfect Glenny et al score (not necessarily the same SR).
- More recent SRs/meta-analyses more consistently identified bruxism as an important risk factor for implant failure than older ones.
- One meta-analysis reported OR = 4.68 for implant-supported prosthesis failure in bruxers vs non-bruxers.
- Bottom line: "Bruxism plays an important role in the failure of implant-supported prostheses."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only retrieval for this ingest (see Retrieval note above) — the full paper's evidence table (SR-by-SR AMSTAR/Glenny breakdown, individual MA effect sizes beyond the OR 4.68 headline) is not captured here; re-extract from full text if institutional access becomes available.
- Umbrella-review-of-umbrella-review limitation inherent to all such syntheses: quality ceiling is bounded by the weakest included primary SR; heterogeneity in bruxism-diagnosis criteria (self-report vs EMG/PSG vs clinical wear signs) across primary studies propagates upward and is not resolved by this umbrella review.
- Authors themselves note heterogeneity among included SRs as a key caveat to the pooled directional conclusion.

## 7. Glossary
- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews on the same topic, rather than primary studies directly.
- **AMSTAR**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews — a validated instrument scoring the methodological quality of systematic reviews.
- **Glenny et al checklist**: A dentistry-specific checklist for appraising the quality of systematic reviews in oral health research.
- **PROSPERO**: International prospective register of systematic reviews, used for a priori protocol registration.
- **Odds ratio (OR)**: A measure of association; OR 4.68 here means implant failure was 4.68 times more likely among bruxers than non-bruxers in the cited meta-analysis.
