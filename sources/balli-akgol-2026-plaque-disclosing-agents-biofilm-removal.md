## Why Ingested

치태착색제(plaque-disclosing agent, PDA)를 표준 구강위생교육 단독 대비 **직접 무작위 비교**한 시험으로, [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/sooampon-2026-dc-red-33-erythrosine-plaque-disclosure-rct]]가 "어떤 착색제를 쓰는가"(색소 선택)를 다룬 것과 달리 "착색제를 추가하는 것이 애초에 이득인가"라는 축의 음성 근거를 제공한다. [[overviews/oral-hygiene-instruction-overview]]가 기본 권고로 삼는 '개별 착색제 기반 TBI'의 적용 범위를 고기준 숙련군으로 한정(refine)하는 결과다.

## Three-line Summary

Single-center parallel-group RCT (1:1, ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06080672; n=124 fourth-year dental students, Istanbul Medipol University) testing whether adding a plaque-disclosing agent (Mira 2-tone) before Modified-Bass brushing improves plaque/gingival outcomes versus standardized instruction alone.

The primary outcome — between-group difference in ΔPlaque Index (baseline→week 1) — was not significant (0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24), and ΔPI (post-brushing, week 1, month 1) and ΔGingival Index (week 1, month 1) likewise showed no significant between-group differences, although both arms improved significantly within-group.

In a high-baseline-proficiency student population the disclosing agent conferred no measurable advantage over instruction alone (ceiling effect); the authors conclude benefit may only appear in lay populations with lower oral-health literacy, and the 1-month horizon plus unblinded participants limit interpretation.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 평행군 무작위 임상시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, 1:1, NCT06080672; n=124, 이스탄불 메디폴대학교 4학년 치대생)으로, Modified Bass 칫솔질 전 치태착색제(plaque-disclosing agent, PDA; Mira 2-tone) 추가가 표준 구강위생교육 단독 대비 치태·치은 지표를 개선하는지 평가.

1차 결과인 기저선→1주 치태지수 변화량(ΔPI)의 군간 차이는 유의하지 않았고(0.09, 95% CI −0.05~0.24), 칫솔질 직후·1주·1개월의 ΔPI와 1주·1개월의 ΔGI도 모두 군간 차이 없음(다만 양군 모두 군내 개선은 유의, Friedman p<0.001).

구강위생 숙련도가 이미 높은 치대생 집단에서는 착색제의 부가 이득이 관찰되지 않았으며(천장효과, ceiling effect), 이득은 구강건강 문해력이 낮은 일반인에서 기대해야 함; 1개월이라는 짧은 추적과 참가자 비눈가림이 핵심 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (per the authors) single-center RCT to test whether **adding a plaque-disclosing agent to standardized instruction alone** changes plaque/gingival outcomes in a **high-baseline-proficiency** population (fourth-year dental students).
- Reports an **honest negative primary result**: the prespecified between-group difference in ΔPI (baseline→week 1) was not significant, and all secondary between-group ΔPI/ΔGI comparisons were null.
- Provides the concrete effect estimates and 95% CIs for the null between-group changes (e.g. ΔPI week 1 = 0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24; ΔGI week 1 = 0.04, 95% CI −0.14 to 0.21).
- Frames the null result as a **ceiling effect** (already-proficient students) and redirects the clinical question toward lay populations with lower oral-health literacy.
- Discloses a **protocol deviation** (prespecified OHI-S not collected) and residual risk of detection bias, and explicitly discusses the **Hawthorne effect** as a confounder of the within-group improvements.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Single-center, interventional, prospective, parallel-group randomized controlled trial, 1:1 allocation; no stratification.
- **Setting / period**: Istanbul Medipol University; October 2023 – January 2024.
- **Population**: 124 fourth-year dental students (from the Preventive Dentistry course), age 20–30 y; convenience sampling. 204 screened, 80 excluded (46 smokers, 15 orthodontic, 3 pregnant/breastfeeding, 16 declined). No losses to follow-up; all 124 analyzed (control n=62, test n=62).
- **Randomization / concealment**: computer-generated sequence (GraphPad QuickCalcs); sequentially numbered opaque sealed envelopes (SNOSE) prepared by an independent researcher (M.B.); allocation opened by N.Ü./B.B.A. immediately before the intervention.
- **Intervention**: Both arms received identical standardized Modified-Bass instruction (video + model), were told to brush twice daily ~2 min and perform interdental cleaning once daily, and were supplied the same toothbrush (Banat Deep Clean) and toothpaste (Colgate Total 12). Test arm additionally had teeth stained with **Mira 2-tone Solution (Hager & Werken, Duisburg, Germany)** before brushing and the stained areas shown in a mirror. No home disclosing agent prescribed.
- **Blinding**: Outcome assessor (N.A., 8 y periodontology experience) blinded — measurements performed only **after triple water rinsing** to remove residual dye; assessor had no access to the allocation list.
- **Outcomes**: Primary = between-group difference in **ΔPI baseline→week 1**. Secondary = ΔPI baseline→post-brushing, ΔPI baseline→month 1, ΔGI baseline→week 1, ΔGI baseline→month 1. PI (Silness & Löe 1964) and GI (Löe & Silness 1963) recorded on 4 surfaces (MB, midB, DB, midL) of all teeth except third molars with a Williams probe; DMFT at baseline.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 22; Kolmogorov–Smirnov normality; Mann–Whitney U / Student t for between-group, Friedman + Bonferroni-adjusted Wilcoxon for within-group; univariate ANCOVA (group fixed; sex and baseline self-reported gum bleeding as covariates) with EMMEANS; α=0.05. Sample size 124 assumed 70%→90% plaque removal, power 80%, α 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Participants**: 55 male (44.4%) / 69 female (55.6%); mean age 23.13 ± 1.47 y. Groups comparable at baseline except self-reported gum bleeding — Group 2 **higher** (37.1% vs 16.1%; p=0.015).
- **Baseline indices** (mean ± SD [median]): GI G1 0.95 ± 0.44 (0.97), G2 0.82 ± 0.46 (0.86); PI G1 0.59 ± 0.32 (0.53), G2 0.63 ± 0.41 (0.54); DMFT 6.02 ± 4.15 vs 5.92 ± 4.76.
- **Primary endpoint (ΔPI baseline→week 1)**: G1 +0.04 ± 0.38 (0.03) vs G2 −0.05 ± 0.45 (−0.02); between-group Δ **0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24 — not significant**.
- **ΔGI baseline→week 1**: G1 −0.40 ± 0.48 (−0.46) vs G2 −0.44 ± 0.50 (−0.37); between-group Δ **0.04, 95% CI −0.14 to 0.21 — not significant**.
- **Observed index scores (Mann–Whitney p)**: PI baseline 0.59±0.32 vs 0.63±0.41 (p=0.942); PI post-brushing 0.48±1.53 vs 0.23±0.29 (p=0.435); PI week 1 0.63±0.43 vs 0.66±0.73 (p=0.595); PI month 1 0.55±0.33 vs 0.58±0.40 (p=0.863). GI baseline 0.95±0.44 vs 0.82±0.46 (p=0.129); GI week 1 0.55±0.49 vs 0.38±0.37 (p=0.053); GI month 1 0.58±0.40 vs 0.50±0.40 (p=0.215). All between-group score comparisons non-significant.
- **Within-group change (supporting)**: GI fell significantly from baseline to week 1 in both arms, then partially rebounded by month 1 (remaining below baseline); PI dropped immediately post-brushing in both arms (p<0.01) and rose again toward week 1 and month 1. Friedman within-group p<0.001 for both groups.
- **ANCOVA (adjusted for sex + baseline gum bleeding)**: Group 1 showed higher adjusted GI at baseline (p=0.047) and week 1 (p=0.009), but not at month 1 (p=0.142); PI showed no adjusted between-group difference at any time point (all p>0.05). (These are cross-sectional adjusted score differences, not a differential treatment effect — baseline was already imbalanced.)
- **Hypothesis outcome**: The authors state the study hypothesis (PDA enhances plaque removal and oral health) was **rejected**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Homogeneous, high-proficiency sample** (dental students only) → probable **ceiling effect**; limited generalizability to lay populations.
- **Short 1-month follow-up** — cannot capture long-term behavior change; increases susceptibility to the **Hawthorne effect** (repeated measurement itself motivating behavior).
- **Residual detection bias** cannot be fully excluded if any minimal staining persisted or participants revealed allocation.
- **Protocol deviation**: prespecified Green & Vermillion OHI-S not collected (time constraints); authors state the registry will be updated.
- **Baseline imbalance** in self-reported gum bleeding (Group 2 higher) required ANCOVA; the adjusted GI differences are not change-based and should not be read as a treatment effect.
- **Future work**: more diverse/lay populations, real-world settings, larger samples, objective plaque measures (e.g. digital quantification), and psychological determinants (motivation, self-efficacy, perceived susceptibility).

## 6. Related Work

- **Sooampon 2026 (D&C Red No. 33 vs erythrosine RCT)** — sibling axis: compares *which* disclosing dye; found percentage plaque-index reduction equivalent across agents. Ballı Akgöl 2026 instead asks whether adding *any* disclosing agent beats instruction alone, and found no incremental benefit in proficient users.
- **Choi 2013 / Choi 2017 (Korean domestic SR)** — repeated disclosing-agent-based OHI beats lectures/model instruction (PHP +24.0 vs +2.8). Ballı Akgöl's null result qualifies that default by population (already-proficient vs low-literacy).
- **Peng et al.** — reported PI/GI increases at 3 months with biofilm-disclosing tablets and no reinforcement; consistent with this study's rebound-by-month-1 pattern.
- **Marini et al.** — in contrast, found significant PI reductions after repeated chairside motivation with a plaque indicator at 3 months; discrepancy attributed to differing populations, methods, and motivation intensity.
- **Boyd** — disclosing solution for motivation was more effective than plaque-control instructions alone; **Acharya et al.** and **Peng et al.** found disclosing agents no more effective than control — a mixed literature this trial adds to on the null side.
- **Fasoulas et al. (SR)** — disclosing agents combined with OHI improve self-performed plaque control; this trial suggests short-term use without reinforcement may yield limited benefit.
- **Kim et al., Buischi et al., Kawanishi et al., Hoskin et al.** — disclosing agents as dental-education/visual-feedback tools; framed as educational aids rather than efficacy boosters.

## 7. Glossary

- **PDA**: Plaque-disclosing agent — dye (solution/tablet/swab) that stains dental biofilm to make plaque visible (here Mira 2-tone Solution, Hager & Werken).
- **PI**: Plaque Index (Silness & Löe, 1964) — 0–3 score of soft deposits at the gingival margin; averaged across 4 surfaces/tooth.
- **GI**: Gingival Index (Löe & Silness, 1963) — 0–3 score of gingival inflammation/bleeding.
- **DMFT**: Decayed, Missing, Filled Teeth index — cumulative caries experience.
- **ΔPI / ΔGI**: Between-group difference in the **change** from baseline (primary analysis metric), as opposed to the raw index score at a time point.
- **Modified Bass technique**: Toothbrushing method with bristles at ~45° to the gingival margin and small vibratory strokes; the standardized instruction given to both arms.
- **SNOSE**: Sequentially Numbered Opaque Sealed Envelopes — allocation-concealment mechanism used here.
- **Hawthorne effect**: Behavior change (improved hygiene) caused by awareness of being observed/measured, a confounder of the within-group improvements.
- **Ceiling effect**: Already-near-maximal performance at baseline, leaving little room for an intervention to show added benefit.
- **OHI-S**: Simplified Oral Hygiene Index (Green & Vermillion) — prespecified but not collected (protocol deviation).
- **ANCOVA / EMMEANS**: Analysis of covariance with estimated marginal means — used to adjust between-group comparisons for sex and baseline gum bleeding.
