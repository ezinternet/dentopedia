## Why Ingested

The cracked-tooth cluster quotes tooth-level VRF prevalence from narrative reviews ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]]: 4-32% of root-filled teeth; [[wiki/cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]]: 2-25%) and the consensus statement ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]]) reports a post-COVID-19 rise, but the wiki held no primary dataset giving a patient-level VRF prevalence or the COVID-period comparison. This retrospective chart/letter review supplies both, and explicitly tests the "VRF epidemic" premise that has been used to justify minimally invasive endodontic protocols.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective records review of one university endodontics program (UEP; 21,156 patients, 2011-Mar 2022) and one private endodontics practice (PP; 7,209 treatment letters, 2006-Mar 2022) estimating vertical root fracture (VRF) prevalence and its change before vs during COVID-19.

Overall VRF prevalence was 1.80% (UEP), 2.62% (PP) and 2.01% combined; the combined total rose from 1.72% pre-COVID-19 to 3.82% during COVID-19 (p < 0.0001), but the rise in confirmed-positive cases was smaller (combined 0.65% to 1.01%, p = 0.0052; PP alone not significant, p = 0.07) than the rise in merely suspected cases (combined 0.99% to 2.31%).

VRF is uncommon at patient level and not an "epidemic", yet suspected cases far outnumber visually confirmed ones, so visual confirmation should precede an extraction recommendation; the keyword-search retrospective design, shorter COVID window and no causal data limit the COVID interpretation.

## 세줄요약

후향적 기록 조사 — 대학 근관치료 프로그램 (University Endodontics Program, UEP; 환자 21,156명, 2011~2022.3)과 개인 근관치료 의원 (Private Practice, PP; 진료 서신 7,209건, 2006~2022.3)에서 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 유병률과 COVID-19 전후 변화를 평가.

전체 VRF 유병률은 UEP 1.80%, PP 2.62%, 합산 2.01%였고, 합산 유병률은 COVID-19 이전 1.72%에서 COVID-19 기간 3.82%로 증가 (p < 0.0001); 다만 확진 (confirmed positive) 증가는 합산 0.65%→1.01% (p = 0.0052, PP 단독은 비유의 p = 0.07)로 의심 (suspected) 증가 (합산 0.99%→2.31%)보다 작았음.

VRF는 환자 단위로는 흔하지 않아 "유행 (epidemic)"이라 할 수 없으나 의심 사례가 확진보다 훨씬 많으므로 발치 권고 전 육안 확인이 필요; 키워드 검색 기반 후향 설계, 짧은 COVID 구간, 인과 자료 부재로 COVID 해석에는 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Patient-level VRF prevalence from two endodontic settings (academic 1.80% vs private 2.62%; combined 2.01%), a different denominator from the tooth-level "root-filled teeth" figures in the reviews.
- First pre-COVID-19 vs COVID-19 comparison in this dataset: combined total 1.72% to 3.82% (p < 0.0001).
- Separates suspected from visually/radiographically/surgically confirmed VRF and shows that suspected cases account for most of the apparent rise.
- Reports that crown-originating cracks and split teeth were frequently mislabeled as VRF in the records.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective electronic-record (Axium notes, UEP) and treatment-letter (PP) review; one-tailed z-test, 95% confidence.
- **Periods**: UEP Jan 2011-Mar 2022 (pre-COVID-19 to Dec 2019; COVID-19 Jan 2020-Mar 2022); PP 2006-Mar 2022 (pre-COVID-19 2006-2019).
- **n**: UEP 21,156 patients (60,733 notes reviewed); PP 7,209 letters (1,018 in the COVID-19 period); combined 28,365.
- **Case finding**: keyword search ("vertical root fracture", "VRF", "fracture", "longitudinal ...", "vertical", etc.) with manual exclusion of "warm vertical", "vertical release"; duplicates (same patient and tooth) removed.
- **Categories**: suspected (patient elected endodontic treatment, extraction or monitoring); confirmed positive (clear fracture on periapical radiograph [PP only], visual confirmation after gingival reflection with methylene blue, or surgical confirmation by flap/transillumination); confirmed negative. Fracture seen only after extraction did not count as confirmation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | UEP | PP | Combined |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall VRF prevalence | 1.80% | 2.62% | 2.01% |
| Suspected (all years) | 0.93% | 1.87% | - |
| Confirmed positive (all years) | 0.69% | 0.72% | - |
| Suspected, pre-COVID-19 to COVID-19 | 0.78% to 1.91% (p < 0.0001) | 1.62% to 3.44% (p < 0.0001) | 0.99% to 2.31% (significant) |
| Confirmed positive, pre-COVID-19 to COVID-19 | 0.65% to 0.99% (p = 0.0202) | 0.66% to 1.08% (p = 0.0708, ns) | 0.65% to 1.01% (p = 0.0052) |
| Total (suspected + confirmed) | - | - | 1.72% to 3.82% (p < 0.0001) |

- Authors' explanation for the PP vs UEP gap: more retreatment cases in private practice, longer data window, UEP digital radiography only from 2011. COVID-19 rise attributed (speculatively) to pandemic stress/masticatory load and deferred care.
- Source-data inconsistencies reproduced as written (see deviations log): abstract says "increasing from 2.1% to 3.82%" while Results give 1.72% to 3.82% with the "2.1% increase" being a percentage-point difference; UEP notes reviewed are 60,733 in Results and 60,773 in Discussion; PP confirmed-positive p-value is 0.0721 in the abstract but 0.0708 (positive) and 0.0721 (negative) in the text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Keyword-search retrieval of free-text notes; misspellings or alternative terms could be missed; one note can cover several teeth (deflates prevalence) and one patient can have several VRFs (inflates it).
- "Suspected" may include endo-perio lesions with PDL-route sinus tracts; no follow-up of whether suspected cases were treated, extracted or monitored.
- UEP radiographic confirmation unavailable; few CBCT scans; no minimally invasive access/preparation was used, so the effect of those techniques on prevalence is untested.
- COVID-19 period (about 27 months) much shorter than the pre-COVID-19 period; association only, no causal data. Authors call for a prospective design.

## 7. Glossary
- **Vertical root fracture (VRF)**: fracture extending along the long axis of the root; complete if it reaches both proximal sides, incomplete if one side only.
- **Suspected VRF**: clinician suspicion without visual, surgical or radiographic confirmation; the patient proceeded to endodontic treatment, extraction or monitoring.
- **Confirmed positive**: fracture seen radiographically, visually (gingival reflection + methylene blue) or surgically (flap/transillumination).
- **UEP / PP**: University Endodontics Program / Private Endodontics Practice (the two study settings).
- **Prevalence (patient-level)**: VRF cases divided by patients (UEP) or treatment letters (PP), not by root-filled teeth.
