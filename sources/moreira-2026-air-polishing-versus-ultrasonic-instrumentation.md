Adds a head-to-head RCT of two mechanical nonsurgical modalities (erythritol air polishing vs ultrasonic instrumentation, both with systemic metronidazole) for peri-implantitis, complementing the adjunct-ranking network meta-analysis [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/bai-2024-seven-nonsurgical-methods-peri-implantitis-network]] and the multi-arm glycine air-abrasion trial [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/eraydin-tufek-2026-nonsurgical-peri-implantitis-multiarm-rct]]. ## Three-line Summary

Randomized clinical trial comparing nonsurgical peri-implantitis management with erythritol air polishing versus ultrasonic instrumentation, each combined with systemic metronidazole, integrating clinical and patient-centered outcomes over 12 months (sample size not reported in the abstract).

Ultrasonic instrumentation reduced probing depth and bleeding on probing throughout the 12-month period (abstract does not report effect sizes).

Ultrasonic instrumentation appeared favorable for clinical outcomes, but this rests on an abstract-only reading — group sizes, effect magnitudes, and patient-reported results must be checked in the full text before use.

## 세줄요약

환자 중심 결과와 임상 결과를 함께 본 무작위 임상시험(Randomized Clinical Trial, RCT)으로, 임플란트주위염 (peri-implantitis)의 비외과적 치료에서 에리스리톨 에어폴리싱 (air polishing)과 초음파 기구 사용 (ultrasonic instrumentation)을 각각 전신 메트로니다졸 (systemic metronidazole)과 병용해 12개월간 비교했다 (초록에 표본 수 미기재).

초음파 기구 사용군에서 12개월 내내 탐침 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD)와 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)이 감소했다 (초록에 효과 크기 미기재).

임상 지표에서는 초음파 기구가 유리해 보이나 초록만 읽은 결과이므로, 군별 인원·효과 크기·환자 보고 결과는 전문 확인 후 사용해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Head-to-head RCT of erythritol air polishing versus ultrasonic instrumentation for nonsurgical peri-implantitis treatment, both with systemic metronidazole.
- Reports clinical and patient-centered outcomes together.
- Ultrasonic instrumentation reduced probing depth and bleeding on probing throughout 12 months.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial (per title); 12-month follow-up.
- **Interventions**: Air polishing with erythritol vs ultrasonic instrumentation, each combined with systemic metronidazole.
- **n**: Not reported in the abstract.
- **Outcomes**: Probing depth, bleeding on probing, and patient-centered outcomes (specific instruments not named in the abstract).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Ultrasonic instrumentation reduced probing depth and bleeding on probing throughout the 12-month period.
- No numeric values, p-values, or patient-centered outcome results are given in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only ingest: sample size, effect sizes, and patient-centered results are unavailable.
- Both arms received systemic metronidazole, so the effect of the mechanical modality alone cannot be separated from the antibiotic.

## 6. Related Work

- Eraydin-Tufek 2026: multi-arm RCT including glycine air abrasion.

## 7. Glossary
- **Peri-implantitis**: inflammatory disease of tissues around an implant with progressive bone loss.
- **Erythritol air polishing**: low-abrasive powder-air-water jet used to disrupt biofilm.
