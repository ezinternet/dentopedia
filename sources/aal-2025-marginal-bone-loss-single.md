서브머지드 vs 난서브머지드 서베일런스 sweep 중 발견. 기존 [[implants/mbl/verma-2024-comparison-bone-loss-submerged-nonsubmerged]]가 단일 치아 임플란트를 다룬 반면, 이 RCT는 단일 임플란트 하악 피개의치(SIRMO) 맥락에서 두 치유 프로토콜과 두 가지 어태치먼트를 2년간 비교한 최초의 RCT로, [[overviews/implant-placement-protocol-submerged-one-stage-one-piece-overview]] evidence map에 직접 추가 가능.

## One-line Summary

RCT (n=80 edentulous; 4-arm: submerged/non-submerged × ball/CM-LOC; 2-year follow-up, mandibular SIRMO): no statistically significant MBL difference between healing protocols or attachment types, but non-submerged numerically favored (0.27 vs 0.43 mm).

## 한줄요약

무작위 대조 시험 (n=80 무치악 환자, 4군: 매몰형/비매몰형 × 볼/CM-LOC, 하악 단일 임플란트 피개의치, 2년): 치유 프로토콜 및 어태치먼트 종류 모두 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 유의 차이 없음, 비매몰형 수치적 우위 (0.27 vs 0.43 mm).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT specifically designed to compare submerged (S) vs non-submerged (N) healing protocols AND attachment type (ball vs CM-LOC) simultaneously in SIRMO
- 2-year MBL outcomes within physiological limits for all 4 groups (≤0.49 mm)
- Non-submerged healing numerically favored but not statistically superior; both protocols clinically acceptable
- Age confound identified: BS group significantly older (62.4 vs 57.7 yr), which may partly explain its higher MBL
- "Form follows function" proposed to explain NS advantage: early functional loading → lamellar bone formation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Study design: 2-stage randomization RCT; Ethical approval Cairo University (June 2016); SPIRIT-compliant; Declaration of Helsinki
- Population: 80 completely edentulous patients, age 50–69 yr, HbA1c ≤8, ISQ ≥60, insertion torque ≥30 Ncm
- Implant: Tapered Screw-Vent Zimmer Biomet, 3.7×10 mm, mandibular midline
- Stage 1 randomization: S (cover screw, 3-month healing) vs N (healing abutment)
- Stage 2 randomization: Ball (Zimmer) vs CM-LOC (PEKK matrix, Cendres & Metaux)
- MBL measurement: standardized digital periapical radiographs at baseline, 12, 24 months; long-cone paralleling technique; XCP Rinn bite block; DIGORA software
- Statistics: Mann-Whitney U, Kruskal-Wallis, Wilcoxon signed rank; χ²; ICC for intra-observer reliability
- Follow-up: 80 enrolled → 71 received attachment → 55 at 2 years (6 implant failures during healing, 4 drop-outs, 4 additional drop-outs by 24 months)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | 2-yr MBL (mm, mean ± SD) | p vs comparator |
|---|---|---|
| Ball (B) | 0.28 ± 0.44 | p=0.3 vs CM-LOC |
| CM-LOC (C) | 0.38 ± 0.46 | — |
| Non-submerged (N) | 0.27 ± 0.39 | p=0.6 vs Submerged |
| Submerged (S) | 0.43 ± 0.51 | — |
| BN (best subgroup) | 0.01 ± 0.45 yr-2 | — |
| BS (worst subgroup) | 0.49 ± 0.48 (2yr total) | p=0.021* |

*BS significant vs baseline after 2yr (time effect); significant age difference in BS group.

- Year 1 MBL: Ball 0.21±0.38 mm (p=0.018), CM-LOC 0.23±0.28 mm (p<0.001) — both significant vs baseline
- MBL physiological reference: <1.5mm year 1, <0.2mm/yr thereafter (Albrektsson criteria)
- ICC for measurements: 0.991–0.999 (excellent intra-observer reliability)
- Implant failure during healing: 6 total (2 N group, 4 S group)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- More males (56) than females (23) — sex-imbalanced sample
- Significant age difference between S and N subgroups within ball group (62.4 vs 57.7 yr), potentially confounding BS MBL
- No blinding possible (attachment type visible)
- Follow-up 2 years — longer-term data needed (5+ years)
- Single center (Cairo University); results may not generalize
- No systemic disease subgroup analysis (HbA1c cut-off 8 included controlled diabetics)
- SIRMO-specific: findings may not transfer to 2-implant overdentures or fixed restorations

## 6. Related Work

- Alsabeeha 2011: ball vs locator in 2-implant overdenture, no difference (aligned with this RCT)
- Coutinho 2022: 1.46 mm MBL at 5yr in SIRMO with ball (longer-term perspective)
- Liddelow 2007, 2010: immediate loading SIRMO with ball — physiological MBL
- Padmanabhan SR (SIRMO systematic review): high success rates, cost-effective

## 7. Glossary

- SIRMO: Single implant-retained mandibular overdenture
- CM-LOC: Cendres & Metaux locator-like attachment with PEKK matrix
- PEKK: Polyetherketoneketone — high-performance polymer with shock-absorption
- BS/CS/BN/CN: Ball-Submerged / CM-LOC-Submerged / Ball-Non-submerged / CM-LOC-Non-submerged
- MBL: 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss)
