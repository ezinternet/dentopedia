## Why Ingested

Early (pre-prosthetic, within 6 months) implant failure cohort stratified by age band with CBCT bone density, ISQ and postoperative antibiotic use, adding an age-centred, Chinese hospital dataset (n = 1621 subjects) to the early-failure literature and a counterpoint to cohorts where systemic/age factors were not significant. → [[implants/survival/nyland-2024-risk-factors-for-early-dental]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center cohort (Affiliated Stomatological Hospital of Chongqing Medical University, China; implants treated March 2019-March 2024) of 1621 generally healthy adults aged 20-80 years (ASA I-II) in four age bands, with CBCT bone density, resonance-frequency ISQ, and early implant failure (loss within 6 months of surgery) as outcomes.

Early failure was 14.56% in the 66-80 group versus 4.99% (20-35), 1.07% (36-50) and 0.64% (51-65), and postoperative infection was 22.78% versus 7.34%, 5.76% and 3.51%; in the multivariate model, age 66-80 (vs 36-50: OR 9.235, 95% CI 3.208-26.587), posterior maxilla (OR 8.085), posterior mandible (OR 5.409) and periodontal disease (OR 1.885) raised risk while postoperative antibiotic use lowered it (OR 0.481, 95% CI 0.269-0.860); AUC was 0.823.

Age and bone density were strongly correlated (r = -0.85) so their separate effects cannot be cleanly isolated, HU values from CBCT are relative only, the youngest band had a higher failure rate than the two middle bands (not explained), and the retrospective design, clustering of multiple implants per patient and the antibiotic association (observational) limit causal reading.

## 세줄요약

중국 충칭의과대학 부속 치과병원에서 2019년 3월-2024년 3월 임플란트 치료를 받은 ASA I-II 건강 성인 1621명(20-80세, 4개 연령군)의 후향적 단일기관 코호트로, CBCT 골밀도, 공명주파수분석 (resonance frequency analysis, RFA) 기반 임플란트 안정성 지수 (implant stability quotient, ISQ), 수술 후 6개월 이내 조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure)를 평가했다.

조기 실패율은 66-80세군 14.56%로 20-35세 4.99%, 36-50세 1.07%, 51-65세 0.64%보다 높았고 술후 감염도 22.78% 대 7.34%, 5.76%, 3.51%였으며, 다변량 분석에서 66-80세(36-50세 대비 OR 9.235, 95% CI 3.208-26.587), 상악 구치부 (OR 8.085), 하악 구치부 (OR 5.409), 치주질환 (OR 1.885)은 위험을 높이고 술후 항생제 사용은 낮췄다 (OR 0.481, 95% CI 0.269-0.860); AUC 0.823.

나이와 골밀도가 강하게 상관(r = -0.85)해 각각의 독립 효과를 분리하기 어렵고, CBCT HU는 상대값일 뿐이며, 가장 어린 군의 실패율이 중간 두 군보다 높은 점은 설명되지 않았고, 후향적 설계·환자 내 다중 임플란트 군집·항생제 효과의 관찰연구 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Four age bands (20-35, 36-50, 51-65, 66-80 years) compared on CBCT bone density (HU), ISQ and early failure in one hospital cohort of 1621 subjects.
- Early failure 14.56% in 66-80 versus 4.99%, 1.07% and 0.64% in younger bands; infection 22.78% versus 7.34%, 5.76% and 3.51%.
- Multivariate model with age band, implant site, periodontal disease and postoperative antibiotic use as independent factors; AUC 0.823, Nagelkerke R2 0.21.
- OHIP-14 improved significantly in younger patients but not in the 66-80 group.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, single center, IRB approved, informed consent obtained. Treatment period March 2019 to March 2024.
- **Population**: age 20-80 years, ASA I-II. Excluded: ASA III or higher/uncontrolled systemic disease, bisphosphonate therapy or radiotherapy, heavy smoking (>10 cigarettes/day), systemic diseases affecting bone healing, edentulous opposing jaw or severe bruxism, pregnancy/breastfeeding. Total 1621 subjects; the full text does not state the implant count (the PubMed abstract reports 1,821 implants).
- **Surgery**: standardized two-stage (submerged) protocol by the same team; restorations after 3-6 months healing, cement-retained custom abutment prostheses by a single prosthodontist.
- **Early implant failure definition**: implant loss within the initial 6 months after surgery (healing period), confirmed clinically (mobility, pain, lack of osseointegration) and radiographically. Follow-up 1, 2, 3 and 6 months; survival time treated as discrete.
- **Infection definition**: redness, swelling, discharge, pain or radiographic peri-implant bone loss within 6 months.
- **Measurements**: CBCT (Planmeca ProMax 3D Mid) HU at coronal and apical points; Osstell ISQ immediately and at 1, 2, 3, 6 months; OHIP-14 at baseline, 1, 3, 6 months. Four calibrated investigators.
- **Covariates**: age band, sex, diabetes, osteoporosis, hypertension, implant location, reason for implantation, immediate vs delayed timing, length, diameter, postoperative antibiotics (mainly amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily, or clindamycin 300 mg three times daily if penicillin-allergic, usually 5-7 days).
- **Statistics**: SPSS 22; repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni, chi-square/ANOVA across bands, Kaplan-Meier with log-rank, univariate then multivariate logistic regression (OR, 95% CI), ROC/AUC, Nagelkerke R2, VIF, interaction testing.

- Bone density rose over time in all groups, with the smallest gain in the 66-80 group; the 51-65 group had the highest ISQ at 3 and 6 months.
- Kaplan-Meier: 66-80 group had reduced survival (log-rank p < .001).
- OHIP-14: change of -11.74 in younger patients (p < .001) versus -2.14 in 66-80 (p = 0.111).
- Smoking, diabetes and osteoporosis were significant only in univariate analysis; they dropped out of the multivariate model.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective single-center; selection bias and limited generalizability (authors).
- CBCT not validated for absolute HU; values are relative indicators only (authors).
- Multiple implants per patient treated as independent; no GEE or mixed model (authors).
- Patient adherence to postoperative care not assessed (authors).
- Age and bone density were highly collinear (r = -0.85), so the age effect and the bone density effect cannot be separated; the authors conclude on both together.
- Reviewer notes on the text: the 20-35 band failure rate (4.99%) exceeded the 36-50 and 51-65 bands and is not explained; per-group denominators and numbers of failures are not given in the text; the bone density values (1.50 vs 1.90 "HU") are implausible as HU and appear to be on another scale; the ISQ value is ambiguous (labelled post-op) and its time point is not clear; the infection and antibiotic association could be confounded by indication. The PubMed abstract attributes an OR of 0.481 to "age" and an OR of 1.89 to low bone density, whereas the full text assigns 0.481 to postoperative antibiotic use and reports no bone density OR in the model; the full text was used here.
- No analysis of implant surface, torque, or surgeon factors; implants buried and loaded at different times.
- Authors call for multicenter prospective validation.

## 7. Glossary

- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: here, implant loss within 6 months after surgery, before or around loading.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수)**: RFA-derived stability value, 1-100.
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis, 공명주파수분석)**: vibration-based stability measurement (Osstell).
- **HU (Hounsfield Unit, 호운스필드 단위)**: CT density scale; CBCT values are not calibrated absolutely.
- **OHIP-14 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14, 구강건강 영향 프로파일)**: 14-item quality-of-life questionnaire, score 0-56.
- **ASA (American Society of Anesthesiologists physical status, 미국마취과학회 신체등급)**: I-II = healthy or mild systemic disease.
