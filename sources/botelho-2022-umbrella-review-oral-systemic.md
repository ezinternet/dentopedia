## Why Ingested

전신질환-치과치료 연관성을 다룬 개별 SR/MA는 위키에 다수 있으나([[periodontics/dolcezza-2024-rheumatoid-arthritis-periodontal-disease-sr-ma]] 등), 이들을 evidence-grading 기준으로 통합 평가한 엄브렐라 리뷰가 없었다. 본 논문(Botelho 2022, Nature Communications)은 293편 SR/MA·855개 비교를 AMSTAR2 방법론적 질 + fail-safe number로 평가해 28개 NCD와 구강질환의 강한 연관성을 등급화한 landmark 논문으로, 개별 질환별 근거를 상위 evidence map에 배치하는 기준점 역할을 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews) synthesizing 293 meta-analyses (855 comparisons) on the bidirectional association between oral diseases and systemic noncommunicable diseases (NCDs), registered PROSPERO CRD42022300740.

28 NCDs (including 5 cancer types, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, rheumatoid arthritis, IBD, PCOS, obesity, asthma) showed strong evidence of association with oral disease (mainly periodontitis); only 10.3% of included meta-analyses had high/moderate AMSTAR2 methodological quality, yet fail-safe-number analysis indicated 97.5% of suggestive-to-strong associations are unlikely to be overturned by future studies.

Clinical implication: periodontal disease is the dominant driver of oral-systemic associations (cancer, CVD, diabetes, RA, IBD, PCOS, NAFLD, MRONJ, OSA); evidence for causality remains largely observational/associational, and only periodontal treatment→systemic inflammation and endodontic treatment→CVD reached "strong" intervention-level evidence.

## 세줄요약

293편의 메타분석(855개 비교)을 종합한 엄브렐라 리뷰(체계적 문헌고찰의 체계적 문헌고찰)로, 구강질환과 전신 비전염성질환(Noncommunicable Disease, NCD)의 양방향 연관성을 평가함 (PROSPERO CRD42022300740 등록).

임상적 의미: 치주질환이 구강-전신 연관성의 주요 동인이며(암·심혈관질환·당뇨·류마티스관절염·염증성장질환·다낭성난소증후군·비알콜성지방간·약물관련턱뼈괴사·수면무호흡), 대부분 근거는 관찰연구 수준이고 개입(치료) 근거는 치주치료→전신염증, 근관치료→심혈관질환 두 건만 강한 근거에 도달함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest-scale umbrella review to date on the oral–systemic health link: 293 SR/MA, 855 meta-analytic comparisons, five databases searched to Dec 2021.
- Introduces a four-tier evidence grading (strong / highly suggestive / suggestive / weak) based on Papadimitriou et al. criteria (case count, p-value threshold, heterogeneity, prediction interval, small-study/excess-significance bias).
- Applies Rosenberg's fail-safe number (FSN) to quantify how robust each significant association is to future null studies — median FSN 51 (range 1–87,973) across suggestive-to-strong associations.
- Separately grades (a) oral disease as exposure/outcome of NCDs, and (b) oral treatment (periodontal/endodontic/mandibular advancement) as an intervention affecting NCD outcomes.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search**: PubMed, Cochrane, EMBASE, Web of Science, LILACS to Dec 2021; no language/year restriction; grey literature + manual reference search.
- **Inclusion**: SR with meta-analysis, human studies, assessing oral–systemic association.
- **Exclusion**: SR without meta-analysis, binary results without controls, missing heterogeneity/effect data, umbrella reviews of umbrella reviews, plus post-hoc exclusions (commentaries, country-restricted SRs, animal studies, etc.).
- **Screening**: 14,861 records → 6976 after dedup → 726 full-text reviewed → 293 included (κ=0.91 full-text screening).
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR 2 (κ=0.84), categorized High/Moderate/Low/Critically Low.
- **Statistics**: R v4.03; Meta-Essentials for FSN (binary: OR/RR/HR/IRR; continuous: MD/SMD/WMD).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 855 comparisons: 592 (69.2%) nominally significant; 66 (7.7%) strong, 157 (18.4%) highly suggestive, 55 (6.4%) suggestive, remainder weak.
- 66 strong-evidence associations: 35 oral-disease-as-exposure, 19 oral-disease-as-outcome, 2 oral-treatment-as-intervention (periodontal Tx→systemic inflammation; endodontic Tx→CVD).
- Periodontal disease strongly associated (as exposure) with: cancer (n=9 MAs), CVD (n=5), diabetes mellitus (n=3), APOs (n=2), lower longevity (n=2), neurodegeneration (n=3), PCOS, psoriasis, altered MCH, serum CRP (n=1 each).
- 14 NCDs strongly associated as risk factors *for* oral disease: depression, mental disorders, RA, ankylosing spondylitis, IBD, NAFLD, PCOS, MRONJ, H. pylori, OSA, stroke, obesity, diabetes (denture stomatitis), asthma, special-needs conditions.
- Methodological quality: only 6.5% high + 3.7% moderate AMSTAR2; 71.4% critically low.
- FSN: significant in 97.5% of suggestive-to-strong meta-analyses is unlikely to change with future studies (FSN > included study count).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Predominantly observational-study meta-analyses; low proportion of prospective/RCT evidence → association, not proven causality.
- Substantial heterogeneity in oral-disease case definitions (DMFT index limitations, variable periodontitis/gingivitis case definitions across studies).
- Unmeasured confounding largely handled via subgroup/meta-regression only, not adjusted effect-size pooling.
- Short intervention follow-up in oral-treatment trials limits detection of delayed systemic effects.
- Authors call for: adherence to reporting guidelines, adjusted-effect-size reporting, longer follow-up intervention trials, and broader representation from developing-country research.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review of systematic reviews/meta-analyses, used to synthesize and grade the overall strength of evidence across a research field.
- **AMSTAR 2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews (version 2) — methodological quality appraisal instrument for SRs of both randomized and non-randomized studies.
- **Fail-safe number (FSN)**: The number of additional null-result studies that would be needed to reverse a statistically significant meta-analytic finding (Rosenberg's method used here).
- **NCD**: Noncommunicable disease (e.g., diabetes, cardiovascular disease, cancer) — chronic disease not transmitted person-to-person.
- **Periodontal Medicine**: The research field studying causal/associative links between periodontal disease and systemic health.
