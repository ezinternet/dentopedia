## Why Ingested

## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (43 studies — 3 RCTs + 40 observational, pub3): association between topical fluoride use in young children and dental fluorosis is very uncertain; evidence very low certainty throughout; cannot draw firm conclusions on which practices increase fluorosis risk.

## 한줄요약
코크란 SR (43편, pub3): 소아의 국소 불소제 (Topical Fluoride) 사용과 치아 불소증 (Dental Fluorosis) 간 연관성은 근거 매우 불확실 — 명확한 결론 도출 불가.

## Three-line Summary

Wong et al. (2024) published the third update (pub3) of the Cochrane systematic review on topical fluoride as a cause of dental fluorosis in children, synthesising 43 studies (3 RCTs and 40 observational studies) comparing various topical fluoride practices in children under 6 years—the critical period for permanent tooth mineralization. The five comparison groups examined age at toothbrushing initiation, brushing frequency, toothpaste amount, fluoride concentration, and other topical fluoride exposures (varnishes, gels, mouth rinses).

The overall certainty of evidence was rated as very low (GRADE) throughout all comparisons, with the vast majority of studies at high risk of bias due to observational designs, uncontrolled confounding, and recall bias for retrospective fluoride use reporting. No clear association between any specific topical fluoride practice and increased dental fluorosis risk could be established with confidence; for example, starting toothbrushing at ≤12 months versus >12 months showed RR 0.98 (95% CI 0.81–1.18, very low certainty).

The clinical significance of this very low certainty baseline is not that topical fluorides are safe with respect to fluorosis risk, but that existing evidence is too heterogeneous and confounded to generate definitive guidance on specific practices. This uncertainty coexists with the established association between community water fluoridation and fluorosis (Iheozor-Ejiofor 2024), framing topical fluoride application protocols for children as an area where practice continues to be driven by clinical consensus and precautionary principles rather than definitive trial evidence.

## 세줄요약

웡 등(2024)은 소아 치아 불소증(Dental Fluorosis) 원인으로서의 국소 불소제를 평가한 코크란 SR 3판(pub3)으로 43편(RCT 3편+관찰 연구 40편)을 종합했다. 치솔질 시작 연령, 빈도, 치약 사용량, 불소 농도, 불소 바니시/젤 등 5개 비교군을 대상으로 했다.

전체 근거 확신도는 모든 비교에서 매우 낮음(GRADE)이었다. 절대다수 연구가 비무작위 설계, 비통제 교란변수, 회상 편향으로 고위험 비뚤림을 보였다. 어떤 특정 국소 불소 처방도 불소증 위험 증가와 명확한 연관성을 확립하지 못했다(예: 치솔질 시작 ≤12개월 vs >12개월 RR 0.98, 95% CI 0.81–1.18, 매우 낮은 확신도).

이 매우 낮은 확신도 기준선의 의미: 국소 불소제가 불소증 위험 없이 안전하다는 것이 아니라, 현재 근거가 특정 실천에 대한 명확한 지침을 제시하기에 너무 이질적이고 혼재되어 있다는 것이다. 소아 국소 불소 처방 프로토콜은 여전히 임상 합의와 예방 원칙에 의존한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Systematic review of topical fluoride-fluorosis relationship: 43 studies across 5 comparison groups
- Population: children under 6 years (critical period for permanent tooth mineralization)
- Covers all topical fluoride forms: toothpaste, mouth rinses, gels, foams, varnishes
- Establishes very low certainty as the baseline for all current evidence in this area
- Cannot definitively determine whether age of start, frequency, or amount of fluoride toothpaste affects fluorosis risk

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 43 studies: 3 RCTs, 4 cohort, 10 case-control, 26 cross-sectional
- Risk of bias: 3 RCTs, 1 cohort, 1 case-control, 6 cross-sectional at "some concerns"; rest at HIGH risk
- Databases: Cochrane Oral Health Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, 3 others + 2 trial registers; search July 2022
- Outcomes: percentage prevalence of dental fluorosis in permanent teeth
- Certainty: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Comparison 1 — Age at toothbrushing start:
- Starting ≤12 months vs >12 months: RR 0.98 (95% CI 0.81–1.18); VERY LOW certainty
- Starting before vs after 24 months: RR 0.83 (0.61–1.13); VERY LOW certainty

Comparison 2 — Frequency of brushing:
- <2x/day vs ≥2x/day: VERY LOW certainty; no clear association established

Comparison 3 — Amount of toothpaste:
- >pea-sized amount: possible association; VERY LOW certainty

Comparisons 4–5 — Fluoride concentration; other topical fluorides:
- VERY LOW certainty throughout

OVERALL: All evidence very uncertain. Cannot determine which practices increase or decrease fluorosis risk.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Vast majority of studies at high risk of bias (observational designs, confounding)
- Fluorosis assessment varied across studies
- Recall bias for toothpaste use behaviours in retrospective studies
- Blinding of outcome assessors often unclear

## 7. Glossary
- 치아 불소증 (Dental Fluorosis): 치아 형성기 과잉 불소 섭취에 의한 에나멜 외관 변화(백반~갈색 착색, 심할 경우 함몰)
- 임계기 (Critical Period): 영구치 무기화 단계로 불소증 가장 취약한 시기 (대략 출생~8세)
- 불소 치약 (Fluoride Toothpaste): 1,000–1,500 ppm 불소 함유 치약; 소아용은 500 ppm 이하 권장
