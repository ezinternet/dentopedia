## Why Ingested
소아 국소 불소 사용과 불소증 위험 연관성을 평가한 Cochrane SR pub3 (2024). 기존 [[caries/iheozor-ejiofor-2024-water-fluoridation-prevention-dental]] 수돗물 불소화 근거와 함께 불소증 원인 체계 완성. 소아 불소 치약 처방 및 예방치료 프로토콜 기반 근거.

## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (43 studies — 3 RCTs + 40 observational, pub3): association between topical fluoride use in young children and dental fluorosis is very uncertain; evidence very low certainty throughout; cannot draw firm conclusions on which practices increase fluorosis risk.

## 한줄요약
코크란 SR (43편, pub3): 소아의 국소 불소제 (Topical Fluoride) 사용과 치아 불소증 (Dental Fluorosis) 간 연관성은 근거 매우 불확실 — 명확한 결론 도출 불가.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2024
- DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD007693.pub3
- PMID: 38899538 | PMC: PMC11187792
- Evidence current to: 28 July 2022
- Previous versions: pub1 (2010), pub2 (2014)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Systematic review of topical fluoride-fluorosis relationship: 43 studies across 5 comparison groups
- Population: children under 6 years (critical period for permanent tooth mineralization)
- Covers all topical fluoride forms: toothpaste, mouth rinses, gels, foams, varnishes
- Establishes very low certainty as the baseline for all current evidence in this area
- Cannot definitively determine whether age of start, frequency, or amount of fluoride toothpaste affects fluorosis risk

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 43 studies: 3 RCTs, 4 cohort, 10 case-control, 26 cross-sectional
- Risk of bias: 3 RCTs, 1 cohort, 1 case-control, 6 cross-sectional at "some concerns"; rest at HIGH risk
- Databases: Cochrane Oral Health Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, 3 others + 2 trial registers; search July 2022
- Outcomes: percentage prevalence of dental fluorosis in permanent teeth
- Certainty: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Comparison 1 — Age at toothbrushing start:
- Starting ≤12 months vs >12 months: RR 0.98 (95% CI 0.81–1.18); VERY LOW certainty
- Starting before vs after 24 months: RR 0.83 (0.61–1.13); VERY LOW certainty

Comparison 2 — Frequency of brushing:
- <2x/day vs ≥2x/day: VERY LOW certainty; no clear association established

Comparison 3 — Amount of toothpaste:
- >pea-sized amount: possible association; VERY LOW certainty

Comparisons 4–5 — Fluoride concentration; other topical fluorides:
- VERY LOW certainty throughout

OVERALL: All evidence very uncertain. Cannot determine which practices increase or decrease fluorosis risk.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Vast majority of studies at high risk of bias (observational designs, confounding)
- Fluorosis assessment varied across studies
- Recall bias for toothpaste use behaviours in retrospective studies
- Blinding of outcome assessors often unclear

## 7. Glossary
- 치아 불소증 (Dental Fluorosis): 치아 형성기 과잉 불소 섭취에 의한 에나멜 외관 변화(백반~갈색 착색, 심할 경우 함몰)
- 임계기 (Critical Period): 영구치 무기화 단계로 불소증 가장 취약한 시기 (대략 출생~8세)
- 불소 치약 (Fluoride Toothpaste): 1,000–1,500 ppm 불소 함유 치약; 소아용은 500 ppm 이하 권장
