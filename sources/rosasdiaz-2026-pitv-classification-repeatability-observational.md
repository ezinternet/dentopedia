## Why Ingested
점진적 삽입 토크값 (Progressive Insertion Torque Value, PITV)에 기반한 새로운 1차 안정성 분류 체계의 반복도·재현성을 검증 — [[implants/isq/rosasdiaz-2024-insertion-compression-primary-stability]] 및 [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]]에서 다루는 IT 분류 및 적재 프로토콜 결정에 임상적 도구를 추가.

## One-line Summary
Analytical observational study (n=1,250 torque-curve interpretations, 25 dentists): PITV-based primary stability classification showed near-perfect intra- and inter-examiner agreement (k=0.82–0.95), with moderate predictive discrimination (AUC=0.69).

## 한줄요약
관찰 연구 (n=1250 토크 곡선, 치과의사 25명): PITV 기반 1차 안정성 분류 체계가 우수한 검사자 내·간 일치도(k=0.82–0.95) 및 보통 수준 예측 변별도(AUC=0.69) 달성.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Presents and validates a PITV-based classification for primary stability: Type I (High ≥50 Ncm), Type II (Moderate 30–<50 Ncm), Type III (Low <30 Ncm)
- Types I and II have 4 subtypes (A–D) based on curve trajectory; Type III has 6 subtypes (A–F)
- Demonstrates almost-perfect reliability: k=0.82–0.95 intraexaminer; k=0.81–0.89 interexaminer
- Calibrated 25 dentists (general + specialist) using torque-curve interpretation training
- Identifies moderate predictive validity (AUC=0.69, 95%CI: 0.56–0.81)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Analytical observational study — reliability and validity analysis
- Sample: 1,250 implant torque-curve interpretations by 25 dentists (general + specialists)
- Classification: 3 types by final IT; subtypes A–F by torque-curve trajectory pattern
- Agreement: Cohen's κ (intraexaminer), Fleiss' κ (interexaminer), PABAK
- Predictive validity: ROC analysis (AUC, 95% CI), calibration (intercept, slope), Brier score
- Training: standardized interpretation training provided to all examiners

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Intraexaminer agreement: general dentists k=0.82, specialists k=0.95 (almost perfect)
- Interexaminer agreement: general dentists k=0.81, specialists k=0.89 (almost perfect)
- Overall agreement: k=0.84 (p<0.001)
- AUC=0.69 (95%CI: 0.56–0.81): moderate discrimination
- Classification thresholds: High ≥50 Ncm, Moderate 30–<50 Ncm, Low <30 Ncm

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Torque-curve interpretations — not validated against clinical outcomes (ISQ, bone loss, survival)
- AUC=0.69 is moderate; predictive validity limited
- Single institution, Peruvian population, selected implant systems — external validity unclear
- Training may need standardization across different training levels/countries

## 7. Glossary
- **PITV (Progressive Insertion Torque Value)**: torque measured progressively during implant insertion via digital torque meter, plotted as curve from bone entry to final crestal position
- **PABAK**: Prevalence- and Bias-Adjusted Kappa — adjusts Cohen's κ for prevalence imbalance
- **Brier score**: mean squared error between predicted probabilities and actual outcomes (calibration)
- **Cohen's κ / Fleiss' κ**: intra/inter-rater reliability coefficients (0=chance, 1=perfect agreement)
