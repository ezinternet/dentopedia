## Why Ingested

CBCT is the imaging backbone of implant planning, and the wiki's [[digital-workflow/fouda-2025-accuracy-digital-workflow-implant-fullarch]] page relies on guided-surgery accuracy without a dedicated imaging-indication source. This ICOI consensus (Benavides 2012) supplies the expert-panel position on when CBCT is justified and what it can and cannot answer. It was ingested from the Misch-author PubMed sweep (consensus-type papers first).

## Three-line Summary

Consensus statement (ICOI panel; systematic PubMed review of CBCT in implant dentistry, Jan 2000–Jul 2011; diagnostics, planning, surgical guidance, postimplant evaluation) — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The literature supports CBCT for implant planning, especially linear measurement, 3-D ridge topography, proximity to vital structures and surgical-guide fabrication; CBCT-derived bone density, CBCT-aided navigation and postimplant artifacts were flagged as needing further research.

Panel recommendations: justify each CBCT on individual need with benefit exceeding risk, take history and examination first, use CBCT when conventional radiography cannot show the 3-D anatomy, use the smallest field of view, and interpret the entire volume.

합의문 (ICOI 패널; 2000년 1월–2011년 7월 CBCT의 치과 임플란트 문헌 체계적 검토; 진단·계획·수술 가이드·식립 후 평가) — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

문헌은 특히 선형 계측, 3차원 치조제 지형, 중요 구조물과의 근접성, 수술 가이드 제작에 CBCT를 지지; CBCT 기반 골밀도 측정, CBCT 보조 항법, 식립 후 아티팩트는 추가 연구 필요로 표시.

패널 권고: 개별 필요에 따라 정당화(이득이 위험을 상회), 병력·임상검사 선행, 통상 방사선으로 3차원 해부를 못 볼 때 CBCT 사용, 최소 시야(FOV), 전체 볼륨 판독.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Organizes the CBCT-in-implantology literature into four domains: diagnostics, implant planning, surgical guidance, postimplant evaluation.
- States explicit use indications and a justification rule for each CBCT scan (abstract-level summary).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Systematic PubMed search of CBCT and implant dentistry studies (2000–2011), with presentations by invited panelists at an ICOI meeting in Seoul (Oct 2011). Synthesis is narrative/consensus; the abstract does not report a formal evidence grading.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Supported uses (abstract): linear measurements, 3-D ridge topography, proximity to vital anatomy, surgical guide fabrication. Unresolved (abstract): CBCT bone density, CBCT-aided navigation, postimplant artifacts. No numeric effect sizes are given in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Full text not retrieved; grading of individual studies and the panel's voting process are not visible here.
- Literature cutoff is July 2011; newer CBCT, AI and guided-workflow evidence is not covered.
- Future work named in the abstract: CBCT bone-density validation, navigation, artifact reduction.
