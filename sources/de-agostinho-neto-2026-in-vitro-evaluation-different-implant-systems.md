## Why Ingested

The wiki holds [[implants/kim-2019-double-spiral-condensing-screw-implant]] (HaeNaem, KR 10-2304707 B1), whose whole engineering premise is that prior 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD) burs densify the side wall strongly but the osteotomy floor weakly (d2 ≪ d1) — hence the lower compression dome (하부압착돔). That premise has been an **unmeasured inventor assertion** in this wiki: no held paper reports densification by region. This paper is the first we hold that assesses bone microarchitecture separately at **cervical, body, and apical** regions by micro-CT, across conventional drilling / OD (VERSAH) / bone expander (MAXIMUS).

It is also ingested as a deliberate counterweight. Our OD cluster leans positive — [[implants/versah-protocols/de-lima-2026-osseodensification-vs-conventional-drilling-exvivo]] and [[implants/versah-protocols/barbera-millan-2021-primary-stability-low-density-osseodensification]] both found significant OD torque advantages. This paper, in the same class of model, found **none**, and its own framing ("threshold of utility" in extremely porous bone) is a scope limit worth holding explicitly rather than a result to discount.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study on fresh low-density bovine ribs comparing three osteotomy systems for a SIN Strong SW CM 3.5 × 8.5 mm implant — conventional drilling (SIN, control), osseodensification (VERSAH/Densah burs), and bone expander (MAXIMUS) — with micro-CT bone surface/volume ratio measured separately at cervical, body, and apical regions (n=5/group) and peak insertion torque measured with a digital torque wrench (n=8/group).

Nothing reached significance: no differences between cervical/body/apical regions, none between systems on microarchitecture, and none on insertion torque (SIN 35 ± 21.5, VERSAH 43.2 ± 27.1, MAXIMUS 59.6 ± 28.5 N·cm) — though the means ordered MAXIMUS > VERSAH > SIN, a ~70% spread from lowest to highest.

The authors read this as densification approaching a "threshold of utility" in extremely porous bone where trabecular biology, not drill choice, sets primary stability; but with n=5–8/group and SDs of 21–28 N·cm against a 24.6 N·cm mean gap, the study is very likely underpowered — this is a failure to detect a difference, not evidence that none exists.

## 세줄요약

신선 저밀도 소 늑골 in vitro 연구: SIN Strong SW CM 3.5 × 8.5 mm 임플란트용 골삭제 시스템 3종 — 기존 드릴링(SIN, 대조군), 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD; VERSAH/Densah bur), 골확장기 (Bone Expander; MAXIMUS) — 를 비교하고, 미세단층촬영 (micro-CT)으로 **경부·체부·근단부**를 나눠 골 표면적/부피비를 측정(군당 n=5) + 디지털 토크렌치로 최대 식립 토크 (Insertion Torque, IT) 측정(군당 n=8).

유의차 전무: 경부/체부/근단부 간 차이 없음, 시스템 간 미세구조 차이 없음, 식립 토크도 차이 없음 (SIN 35 ± 21.5, VERSAH 43.2 ± 27.1, MAXIMUS 59.6 ± 28.5 N·cm) — 다만 평균 순서는 MAXIMUS > VERSAH > SIN으로 최저–최고 약 70% 차이.

저자는 이를 극도로 다공성인 골에서 골밀도화가 "유용성의 한계점 (threshold of utility)"에 도달해 드릴 선택보다 해면골 생물학이 1차 안정성을 지배하는 것으로 해석하나, 군당 n=5–8에 표준편차 21–28 N·cm가 평균 격차 24.6 N·cm를 덮는 구조 — **검정력 부족**이 유력하다. "차이를 못 찾았다"이지 "차이가 없다"가 아니다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First regional (cervical / body / apical) micro-CT comparison of OD vs conventional vs expander that the wiki holds.** Directly addresses whether OD's densification is regionally biased — the premise underlying the HaeNaem lower-compression-dome patent.
- **A published null in the OD literature**, which is dominated by positive reports; the authors explicitly position it against "optimistic studies" and propose a **"threshold of utility"** — densification's benefit may vanish where bone is extremely porous, because there is too little trabecular substance to compact.
- **Three-arm head-to-head** including a bone expander (MAXIMUS, Brazilian) alongside Densah/VERSAH — expanders are usually compared only against conventional drilling, not against OD in the same experiment.
- Reframes the clinical lever: in extremely low-density bone, **implant macrogeometry, loading protocol, and grafting** may matter more than drill choice.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In vitro / bench, three-arm parallel comparison. Null hypothesis: no difference in primary stability between systems (and the null was not rejected).
- **Substrate**: Fresh bovine rib — chosen deliberately for its **low-density** characteristics.
- **Implant**: SIN Strong SW CM 3.5 × 8.5 mm (CM 11.5, 16°), placed **at bone level** in every group.
- **Groups / protocols**:
  - *SIN (control)*: manufacturer sequence — 2.0 mm spear drill → 2.0 mm twist drill → 3.0 mm conical drill; 1200 rpm initial, 800 rpm subsequent; clockwise; irrigated. (Note: final 3.0 mm osteotomy for a 3.5 mm implant — i.e. the control is itself undersized.)
  - *VERSAH (OD)*: 1.7 mm pilot clockwise (cutting) → VT1525 (2.0 mm) → VT2535 (3.0 mm) counterclockwise (densifying); 800 rpm. Only two densifying burs — a short sequence.
  - *MAXIMUS (expander)*: 1.3 mm pilot → progressive bone reamers up to indicated size (ALO34.TI); 800 rpm; clockwise.
- **n**: **Inconsistently reported.** The abstract states "n = 5 per group" globally; Methods specify n=5 milling operations per system for micro-CT and **n=8 per technique** for insertion torque.
- **Micro-CT**: Skyscan 1172; 8 μm isotropic voxel; 92 kV / 120 mA; 180° rotation; 0.5 mm Al filter. Analysed at three axial points per osteotomy (described as "axial, middle and apical" in Methods, "cervical, body, apical" elsewhere), masked to 500 μm from the osteotomy centre. **Outcome = bone surface area / volume ratio** — a microarchitecture proxy, *not* a direct bone mineral density measurement.
- **Torque**: INSTRUTHERM TQ-680 digital torque wrench; peak insertion torque.
- **Statistics**: Shapiro–Wilk normality → one-way ANOVA → Tukey multiple comparison, α = 0.05; GraphPad Prism 10.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Insertion torque (peak, N·cm)** — all comparisons non-significant:

| Group | Technique | Insertion torque | vs SIN |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIN | Conventional drilling (control) | 35 ± 21.5 | — |
| VERSAH | Osseodensification (Densah burs) | 43.2 ± 27.1 | +23% (NS) |
| MAXIMUS | Bone expander | 59.6 ± 28.5 | +70% (NS) |

**Bone microarchitecture (surface/volume ratio)**: no significant differences between cervical, body, or apical regions, and none between milling techniques in any region. The only descriptive signal: SIN showed a *tendency* toward a higher surface/volume ratio vs VERSAH and MAXIMUS, particularly cervically — i.e. the numerical trend on microarchitecture runs **opposite** to the torque trend.

**★ Two reporting problems that must travel with these numbers:**

1. **The abstract's inequality is written backwards.** It reports "no statistically significant differences … (p < 0.05)" — twice. For a null result this must be **p > 0.05**. The Discussion writes it correctly ("…MAXIMUS: 59.6 ± 28.5 N cm; p > 0.05"), which confirms the abstract is the error, not the analysis. (Caveat: the PMC JATS extraction strips italic statistical symbols, so the retrieved text shows "(< 0.05)" and "(= 0.05)" — the leading `p` / `α` is inferred from context and from the Discussion's correctly-signed counterpart.)
2. **No power analysis, and the design looks underpowered.** With n=5 (micro-CT) / n=8 (torque) per group and SDs of 21.5–28.5 N·cm, an observed 24.6 N·cm SIN→MAXIMUS gap (a ~70% relative difference) still fails to separate. A difference of that size mattering clinically — 35 N·cm sits barely above the 30 N·cm immediate-loading threshold the paper itself cites, while 59.6 N·cm sits comfortably above it — being statistically undetectable is the signature of insufficient n, not of absent effect. Compare [[implants/versah-protocols/de-lima-2026-osseodensification-vs-conventional-drilling-exvivo]], which used a paired design with n=16 and power = 0.80 and *did* separate its groups.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Underpowered; no a priori power calculation.** See above. The null hypothesis was "not rejected", which the paper's Conclusion then narrates as similarity — the classic absence-of-evidence conflation.
- **Unpaired design.** Each osteotomy sits in a different rib region; bovine rib density varies along and between ribs, so between-specimen variance is folded into the group SDs. [[implants/versah-protocols/de-lima-2026-osseodensification-vs-conventional-drilling-exvivo]] avoids exactly this by putting all three techniques in the same block.
- **Outcome is a proxy.** Bone surface/volume ratio is a microarchitecture descriptor, not bone mineral density or bone-to-implant contact (BIC). A densification effect could exist and not show in S/V — indeed, compaction could plausibly *lower* S/V. The paper does not discuss the construct validity of this choice, which weakens the regional (apical vs cervical) reading considerably.
- **n reported inconsistently** between abstract (n=5 per group) and Methods (n=5 micro-CT, n=8 torque).
- **Implants placed at bone level, not the manufacturer-recommended 1.5–2.0 mm subcrestal.** The authors acknowledge this and defend it as internal-validity standardisation (avoids crestal cortical friction inflating torque) — a reasonable call that nonetheless moves the result away from clinical conditions.
- **Short VERSAH sequence** (1.7 mm pilot → 2.0 → 3.0 mm; only two densifying burs), and no report of the pumping/pecking motion or irrigation regime for the OD arm that the Densah protocol depends on. An under-executed OD protocol is an alternative explanation for the null that the paper does not consider.
- **Bovine rib ≠ human maxilla.** No cortical plate architecture comparable to the posterior maxilla; no healing, no remodelling, no secondary stability.
- **Bench torque only** — no ISQ/RFA, no BIC, no histomorphometry, no follow-up. Torque and ISQ are known to dissociate in this exact model (see de-lima 2026), so a single-metric null is thinner than it looks.
- **Future work** (authors): combine OD with interventions that raise local bone strength; measure secondary stability and remodelling, not just primary.

## 6. Related Work

- **de-lima-2026** (ex vivo, bovine rib, paired, n=16, power 0.80): the closest comparable design the wiki holds and it reached the **opposite** conclusion — OD significantly beat conventional drilling on insertion torque (77.6 vs 61.3 N·cm, p=0.007). Same substrate class, conflicting bottom line. Note also that de-lima's absolute torques run ~2× higher across all arms (61–95 vs 35–60 N·cm), pointing at substantive differences in block preparation, implant (Biomorse 4×10 vs SIN 3.5×8.5), and rpm (1000 vs 800).
- **barbera-millan-2021** (in vitro, low-density porcine tibia, n=55/group): explicitly cited in this paper's own Discussion; found OD significantly superior to conventional **under-drilling** — the harder comparator — on both torque (21.7 vs 8.9 N·cm, p<0.001) and ISQ (69.8 vs 65.2, p=0.001). Directly conflicting finding in the same low-density-bench class.
- **rittipakorn-2025** (cadaveric human tibia, D3/D4, 40 implants): the closest match in *result* — clockwise OD trended higher but non-significant on both ISQ (67.5 vs 62.9, p=0.077) and torque (34.0 vs 29.5 N·cm, p=0.052), and the authors likewise attributed the non-significance to sample size. Independent replication of "OD numerically ahead, statistically undetectable, underpowered, in low-density bone".
- **kim-2019** (HaeNaem patent, KR 10-2304707 B1): asserts that prior OD burs densify laterally (d1) far more than apically (d2), motivating the lower compression dome. This paper supplies the wiki's first regional measurement — and finds no cervical/apical gradient for VERSAH. Because the study detects no densification effect at all, it cannot test the gradient claim on its own terms; it qualifies the scenario in which the patent's premise bites rather than refuting it.
- **huwais-2017** (foundational OD bench study, porcine tibia): found significant torque increase and ~3× BIC with OD; ISQ NS. Not directly engaged by this paper, and the substrate's density characterisation differs, so the relationship is left in prose rather than typed.
- **marzorati-2026** (SR+MA, 555 patients / 685 implants, human in vivo) and **althobaiti-2023** (SR): both find a real OD advantage (marzorati: IT 45.75 vs 38.00 N·cm, P<0.001; ISQ MD 3.24). A single underpowered bovine-rib null does not weigh against pooled human in vivo evidence, and the populations barely overlap — noted in prose, deliberately not typed as a contradiction.
- **Hindi & Bede (2020)** and the ovine/posterior-maxilla studies cited in the Discussion are not currently held in the wiki.

## 7. Glossary

- **골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD)**: osteotomy preparation in which a multi-fluted tapered bur run counterclockwise compacts bone outward against the osteotomy wall instead of excising it (autografting compaction). Commercial instance: Densah burs (Versah LLC).
- **골확장기 (Bone Expander)**: instrument that widens the osteotomy by progressive lateral displacement of bone, run clockwise; MAXIMUS (Maximus Produtos Odontológicos, Brazil) is the kit tested here. Mechanistically related to OD but not identical — expansion without the counterclockwise compaction face.
- **1차 안정성 (Primary Stability)**: mechanical anchorage of the implant in bone at placement, before any biological healing; commonly proxied by insertion torque.
- **식립 토크 (Insertion Torque, IT)**: peak rotational resistance during implant placement, N·cm. >30 N·cm is the threshold this paper cites for immediate-loading suitability; ≥45 N·cm is the comfortable immediate-provisionalization range used elsewhere in the wiki.
- **골 표면적/부피비 (Bone surface/volume ratio, BS/BV)**: micro-CT microarchitecture descriptor — trabecular surface area per unit bone volume. Higher values indicate finer/more-dissected trabecular structure. **Not** a density measure.
- **미세단층촬영 (micro-Computed Tomography, micro-CT)**: high-resolution 3D X-ray imaging; here 8 μm isotropic voxel on a Skyscan 1172.
- **유용성의 한계점 (Threshold of Utility)**: this paper's coinage — the point at which bone is so porous that there is insufficient trabecular substance for densification to compact, so the choice of osteotomy instrument stops mattering.
- **검정력 (Statistical Power)**: probability of detecting a true effect. Not reported here; with n=5–8 and SDs comparable to the group means, power against the observed 24.6 N·cm gap is low — hence "no difference found" ≠ "no difference exists".
