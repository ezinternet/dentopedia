## Why Ingested

기존 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/fonseca-planells-2026-clear-aligner-maxillary-expansion-growing]] SR+MA는 대부분 후향적 연구를 풀링해 CA가 conventional expander 대비 구치간 확장이 유의하게 부족하다(−1.77mm)고 결론 내렸다. 본 논문(Kim 2026)은 그 SR+MA에 포함되지 않은 **전향적 다기관 비열등성(non-inferiority) 코호트**로, 성장기 환자에서 CA 기반 확장을 SME와 직접 비교한 드문 설계다 — 위키의 CAT 문헌 대부분이 성인·발치 중심이었던 것과 달리 소아/성장기 확장 적응증으로 scope를 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective multicenter cohort, 48 growing patients (mean age 9.3±1.7y; CA group n=24, SME group n=24) with maxillary transverse deficiency, comparing clear-aligner (Invisalign) vs slow maxillary expander (SME) arch expansion at pretreatment (T0), post-expansion (T1), and post-retention (T2, ≥3 months).

Predefined non-inferiority analysis (margin 1.6 mm) found CA was not inferior to SME on the primary outcome (intermolar transverse expansion); CA showed significantly greater canine cusp-tip expansion at both T0–T1 and T0–T2 (regression β=0.898, P=0.017 and β=1.483, P=0.015), while SME showed a nonsignificant trend toward greater bodily (vs. tipping) molar movement; age was a negative predictor of canine-region expansion.

Both appliances produced clinically significant, largely stable transverse gains through retention, but the CA group showed significant buccal molar tipping relapse (ΔMI −7.1±9.8°, P=0.030) not seen in SME, and the non-randomized design (patient-preference allocation) plus Invisalign-Korea funding are notable limitations.

## 세줄요약

전향적 다기관 코호트 연구, 상악 횡적 부족(MTD) 성장기 환자 48명(평균연령 9.3±1.7세; 투명교정군 24명, 완속상악확장장치(SME)군 24명) 대상, 치료전(T0)·확장후(T1)·유지후(T2, 3개월 이상) 3시점에서 투명교정(Invisalign) vs SME 확장 비교.

사전 설정 비열등성 마진(1.6mm) 기준 1차 결과지표(구치간 횡적 확장)에서 투명교정이 SME 대비 비열등; 견치 첨두부 확장은 투명교정군이 두 시점 모두 유의하게 더 컸음(회귀 β=0.898, P=0.017 / β=1.483, P=0.015), SME는 치체이동(bodily movement) 경향이 다소 우세했으나 유의하지 않음; 연령은 견치부 확장의 음의 예측인자.

두 장치 모두 임상적으로 유의한 확장을 유지했으나 투명교정군에서만 구치 협측경사 재발이 유의(ΔMI −7.1±9.8°, P=0.030); 비무작위(환자 선호 배정) 설계와 Invisalign Korea 연구비 지원은 핵심 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the few **prospective, multicenter** (not retrospective single-center) comparisons of CA vs conventional slow expander (SME) specifically in growing/mixed-dentition patients with MTD.
- Uses a **predefined non-inferiority framework** (margin 1.6 mm, informed by prior expansion-variability literature) rather than a simple superiority test — methodologically more rigorous than most prior CA-expansion comparisons.
- Adds **long-term retention (T2, ≥3 months, up to 25 months)** data — most prior aligner-expansion studies report only immediate post-expansion outcomes; retention/stability data for CA-based expansion were previously scarce.
- Identifies a **CA-specific advantage at the canine region** (greater cusp-tip expansion vs SME) not previously highlighted, and flags **age as a negative predictor** of canine-region expansion — a novel covariate finding.
- Documents **significant buccal molar tipping relapse unique to the CA group** during retention (ΔMI −7.1°, P=0.030), an important stability caveat despite overall non-inferiority.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: prospective multicenter cohort study (Asan Medical Center + one private orthodontic clinic, Seoul, Korea), Sept 2022–Sept 2024 data collection.
- Population: 50 enrolled, 48 analyzed (1 lost to follow-up per group). CA group n=24 (13F, mean age 9.8±1.4y); SME group n=24 (11F, mean age 8.8±1.8y).
- Inclusion: MTD indicated for expansion; mixed or early permanent dentition; Cervical Vertebral Maturation Stage ≤ IV. Exclusion: prior orthodontic expansion, craniofacial syndromes/cleft, poor oral hygiene/dental health contraindicating treatment.
- Allocation: **non-randomized**, by patient/guardian preference after informed discussion of CA vs SME differences — a key limitation acknowledged by authors.
- CA protocol: Invisalign® system (Align Technology), ClinCheck®-planned; deciduous canines, deciduous/premolars, and first molars expanded ~0.2 mm/stage (0.1 mm/side) using contralateral teeth as reciprocal anchorage; optimized expansion-support attachments; 20–22h/day wear; aligner change every 7–14 days.
- SME protocol: Hyrax expander (bonded acrylic, university hospital) or Haas-type expander (banded, private practice) per site's standard protocol; jackscrew activated ¼ turn twice weekly (~0.5 mm/week) until posterior crossbite corrected; passive retention 2–6 months.
- Outcome measurements: iTero® intraoral scans at T0 (pretreatment), T1 (post-expansion), T2 (post-retention, ≥3 months). Crown-level (CL3–CL6) and gingival-level (GL3–GL6) transverse widths, arch perimeter (APE), molar buccolingual inclination (MI, Handelman method), and gingival-to-crown (G-C) ratio (bodily vs. tipping movement indicator).
- Reliability: single examiner (JYK), ICC >0.95 intra-examiner, >0.93 inter-examiner (20 randomly selected patients, second examiner YJK).
- Statistics: SPSS 22.0; Shapiro-Wilk normality; paired t-tests (within-group), independent t-tests (between-group); multiple linear regression (Δ = T1−T0 or T2−T0 as DV, group + age + sex as covariates); sample size 23/group (non-inferiority margin 1.6 mm, SD 1.9 mm, one-sided α=0.025, power 80%); subgroup Mann-Whitney U test comparing ≤6mo vs >6mo retention follow-up.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Duration: expansion phase (T0–T1) 8.9±3.4 months (CA) vs 6.3±6.1 months (SME); retention (T1–T2) 8.8±5.0 vs 8.3±5.7 months; total observation 17.3±5.7 vs 13.8±7.8 months — no significant between-group difference.
- Both groups: significant transverse gains at all crown/gingival regions and arch perimeter at T0–T1 (all P<0.001), largely maintained at T0–T2 except gingival-level canine width (GL3: CA 0.3±2.9 mm vs SME 1.0±1.4 mm relapse toward baseline) and arch perimeter (regressed toward pretreatment).
- Primary outcome (intermolar expansion, molar region CL6/GL6): **no significant intergroup difference** at T0–T1 or T0–T2 → supports non-inferiority conclusion.
- Canine cusp-tip expansion (ΔCL3): CA significantly greater than SME — regression β=0.898 (P=0.017) at T0–T1 and β=1.483 (P=0.015) at T0–T2; age (Age_T0) was a significant negative predictor of ΔCL3 in both intervals (β=−0.383 to −0.549, P≤0.003).
- Molar inclination (MI, buccolingual tipping): CA group showed significant buccal tipping over T0–T2 (ΔMI −7.1±9.8°, P=0.030) while SME did not (ΔMI −1.0±10.9°, P=0.759); intergroup difference not statistically significant (P=0.177) but regression showed a borderline trend (β=−7.859, P=0.085).
- G-C ratio at first molar (bodily vs tipping indicator) trended lower (more tipping) in CA at T0–T2 (0.6 vs 1.0 in SME) but not statistically significant (P=0.259).
- Subgroup analysis (≤6mo vs >6mo retention follow-up): no significant differences in transverse changes or MI, suggesting retention-duration variability (3–25 months) did not materially bias stability conclusions — though subgroup n was small.
- Overall conclusion: within the predefined 1.6 mm non-inferiority margin, CA-based expansion was **not inferior** to SME for growing patients with mild-to-moderate MTD.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Non-randomized** — allocation by patient/guardian preference, introducing potential selection bias (author-acknowledged).
- Multicenter design with potential center-level clustering not fully modeled (no design-effect inflation applied; authors suggest future mixed-effects modeling with center as random effect).
- SME group combined **two different appliance designs** (bonded Hyrax vs banded Haas-type) at the two sites, introducing possible biomechanical heterogeneity (though cited literature suggests comparable outcomes between designs).
- No RME (rapid maxillary expansion) arm — comparison limited to CA vs SME only; direct CA vs RME long-term stability comparison flagged as a future research need.
- Wide variability in retention follow-up duration (3–25 months) and small subgroup sizes limit confidence in long-term stability conclusions.
- Large SDs and borderline significance for MI change warrant a larger sample to confirm tipping/relapse findings with adequate power.
- Funding from Invisalign® Korea is a disclosed potential conflict of interest, though authors state no role in study design/analysis/writing.

## 6. Related Work

- Bruni et al. (RCT, Invisalign First vs RME) — no significant difference in intercanine width/palatal surface area/volume, but significantly lower gingival-level intermolar gain (more buccal tipping) in the CA group — partially consistent with this study's MI relapse finding.
- Pamukçu et al. — no significant difference in intercanine/intermolar widths between CA and removable acrylic expanders, though acrylic expanders produced greater palatal surface area/volume gains; less buccal tipping with Invisalign First.
- Zhou & Guo — bodily buccal expansion efficiency of Invisalign averaged 36.35% for maxillary first molars; recommended sufficient buccal root torque.
- Lu et al., Wang et al. — reported Invisalign First producing *less* buccal tipping than RME/SME (contrasting with Bruni et al. and with the tipping trend observed in this study).
- Zhou et al. (meta-analysis, 2014) — effectiveness of non-surgical maxillary expansion generally; cited for SME/RME stability context.
- See also wiki [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/fonseca-planells-2026-clear-aligner-maxillary-expansion-growing]] (SR+MA, mostly retrospective studies) which found CA significantly *inferior* on intermolar/palatal-volume expansion — this prospective non-inferiority RCT-like cohort nuances that conclusion for the intermolar/molar region specifically (no significant difference found here), while still showing a CA-specific tipping/relapse pattern in molars and a novel CA advantage in the canine region.

## 7. Glossary

- **MTD** — Maxillary Transverse Deficiency
- **CA** — Clear Aligner(s) (Invisalign®)
- **SME** — Slow Maxillary Expander (conventional, e.g., Hyrax/Haas-type)
- **RME** — Rapid Maxillary Expansion
- **T0/T1/T2** — Pretreatment / Post-expansion / Post-retention (≥3 months) timepoints
- **CL3–CL6** — Crown-level transverse width at deciduous canine (3), deciduous/premolars (4,5), first molar (6)
- **GL3–GL6** — Gingival-level transverse width at same tooth positions
- **APE** — Arch perimeter
- **MI** — Molar (buccolingual) Inclination angle (Handelman method)
- **G-C ratio** — Gingival-to-crown expansion ratio; indicates bodily movement (ratio near 1) vs. tipping (ratio <1)
- **Non-inferiority margin (Δ)** — Pre-specified maximum clinically acceptable difference (1.6 mm here) below which the experimental treatment is considered "not inferior"
- **CVM** — Cervical Vertebral Maturation (skeletal maturity staging)
- **ICC** — Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (measurement reliability)
