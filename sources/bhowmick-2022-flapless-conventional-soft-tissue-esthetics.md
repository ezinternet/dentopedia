## Why Ingested

Adds direct soft tissue esthetic outcome data (VAS scores, emergence profile) for flapless vs conventional flap implant placement — a dimension not covered in [[implants/loading-protocol/ravindran-2010-flapless-implant-surgery-soft-tissue]], which compared loading protocols rather than surgical access. Reinforces [[implants/chrcanovic-2014-flapless-vs-conventional-implant-surgery-ma]] (MA) with prospective split-mouth clinical data specifically on gingival indices and esthetic scores.

## Three-line Summary

Line 1: Prospective split-mouth study (10 patients, 20 Osstem SSIII implants) compared soft tissue response and esthetics between flapless and conventional flap technique using titanium collar implants over ~12 months.
Line 2: No significant difference in gingival index (GI), plaque index (PI), or modified sulcular bleeding index (mSBI); probing depth significantly lower in flapless group at 3 months (p<0.05); VAS esthetic score higher for flapless (7.62 vs 6.85, p=0.05).
Line 3: Flapless technique yields better soft tissue adaptation, esthetic outcomes, and patient comfort due to reduced trauma and faster healing, though small sample limits generalizability.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 전향적 분리구강 연구(10명, 20개 임플란트)에서 티타늄 칼라 임플란트를 무판막 술식과 기존 판막 술식으로 식립하여 연조직 반응 및 심미성을 비교했습니다.
줄2: 치은지수(GI), 플라크지수(PI), 변형 열구출혈지수(mSBI)는 차이 없었으나, 탐침 깊이는 3개월째 무판막군에서 유의하게 낮았고(p<0.05), VAS 심미 점수도 무판막군에서 높았습니다(7.62 vs 6.85, p=0.05).
줄3: 무판막 술식이 연조직 적응·심미적 결과·환자 편안함에서 우수하나 소표본(n=10) 단기 연구로 일반화에 한계가 있습니다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Pharmaceutical and Bioallied Sciences (JPBS), 2022;14(Suppl):S514
- **DOI**: null (no DOI assigned; dedup by title + first author)
- **PMID**: not retrieved
- **Study design**: Prospective split-mouth clinical study
- **N**: 10 patients, 20 two-piece one-stage implants (Osstem SSIII)
- **Follow-up**: ~12 months (6 weeks, 3 months, 1 month post-prosthesis, 3 months post-prosthesis)
- **Institution**: Multiple centers, India

## 2. Key Contributions

- Head-to-head comparison of flapless vs conventional open-flap implant placement on peri-implant soft tissue indices and patient-perceived esthetics in the same patient (split-mouth design)
- Quantified esthetic advantage of flapless technique using VAS (Visual Analog Scale, 시각적 상사 척도)
- Demonstrated superior soft tissue contour and emergence profile (출현 윤곽) preservation with flapless approach
- Prospective design controls for patient-level confounders via split-mouth allocation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective split-mouth; each patient received one flapless and one conventional open-flap implant on contralateral sides
- **Implant**: Osstem SSIII two-piece, one-stage titanium collar implant
- **Parameters measured**: GI (gingival index, 치은지수), PI (plaque index, 플라크 지수), mSBI (modified sulcular bleeding index, 변형 열구출혈지수), probing depth, VAS esthetic score
- **Assessment time points**: 6 weeks, 3 months, 1 month after prosthesis delivery, 3 months after prosthesis delivery
- **Statistical analysis**: Comparative analysis of paired outcomes; p<0.05 threshold

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Flapless | Conventional Flap | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gingival Index (GI) | No significant difference | No significant difference | NS |
| Plaque Index (PI) | No significant difference | No significant difference | NS |
| mSBI | No significant difference | No significant difference | NS |
| Probing depth (3 mo) | Significantly lower | — | p<0.05 |
| VAS esthetic score | 7.62 | 6.85 | p=0.05 |

- Soft tissue contour and emergence profile were subjectively better in the flapless group
- Both techniques showed acceptable peri-implant soft tissue health throughout follow-up
- No implant failures or adverse events reported in either group

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n=10 patients, 20 implants) — severely limits statistical power and generalizability
- Short follow-up (~12 months) — long-term stability of soft tissue outcomes unknown
- Single-center study — operator-dependent technique variation not controlled
- No blinding — VAS assessment potentially subject to bias
- No long-term bone level (marginal bone loss) data reported
