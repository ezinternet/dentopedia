## Why Ingested

Protamine sulfate reversal of enoxaparin beyond 12 h (21.5 h) with therapeutic anti-Xa assay; case demonstrating renal accumulation. → [[drug/anticoagulants/lauer-2019-protamine-sulfate-for-the-reversal]]

## Three-line Summary

A case report of a hemodynamically unstable patient with enoxaparin-induced abdominal wall hematoma, last enoxaparin dose 21.5 h prior, presenting with therapeutic anti-Xa of 0.8 IU/mL; protamine sulfate 50 mg IV (0.5 mg per 1 mg enoxaparin) was administered. Renal dysfunction can accumulate enoxaparin and prolong therapeutic anti-Xa assays beyond 12 h. Future reversal dosing should be guided by the initial anti-Xa assay.

## 세줄요약

Enoxaparin 마지막 투여 21.5시간 후 발생한 복벽 혈종 환자에 대해 항-Xa 0.8 IU/mL로 protamine sulfate 50 mg IV(1 mg enoxaparin당 0.5 mg)를 투여한 사례 보고. 신기능 저하 시 enoxaparin 축적되어 12시간 이상 항-Xa가 치료 수준으로 유지될 수 있음. 향후 protamine 용량 결정 시 초기 항-Xa 검사를 기준으로 삼아야 함.

- Outcome: reversal of therapeutic anticoagulation alongside resuscitation.

## 7. Glossary

- Anti-Xa (anti-factor Xa assay): laboratory measure of low-molecular-weight heparin activity.
- Protamine sulfate: antidote reversing heparin/enoxaparin anticoagulation.
- Enoxaparin: low-molecular-weight heparin anticoagulant.
- Abdominal wall hematoma: bleeding into abdominal wall.
- Renal dysfunction: impaired kidney function causing drug accumulation.
