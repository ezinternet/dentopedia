## Why Ingested

기존 [[prosthetic-materials/contrepois-2013-marginal-adaptation-ceramic-crowns-sr]]는 세라믹 크라운 단일 SR(54편, 1994–2012)로 이질성 때문에 메타분석·시스템 순위화가 불가능했다고 결론지었다. 본 umbrella review(Dudley 2025)는 그 이후 발표된 17편의 SR(301건 in vitro 측정)을 통계적으로 통합(Welch test, 다중회귀)해 crown type·재료·제작법이 변연 간극에 유의한 영향이 없음을 정량적으로 재확인하고, 대신 측정 기구(impression replica가 과대평가)가 진짜 교란 변수임을 새로 밝혀 기존 SR의 "이질성 때문에 결론 불가" 한계를 부분적으로 극복한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (17 systematic reviews, 301 in vitro crown assessments) quantitatively synthesizing determinants of marginal gap in single crowns before cementation, using Welch ANOVA, Games-Howell post hoc, and robust-clustered multiple regression.

Mean marginal gap was 79.4 ± 22.8 µm (range 34.5–125.5 µm); crown type (F=0.37, P=.775), fabrication material (F=0.48, P=.784), and fabrication method (conventional 88.0 ± 35.0 µm vs CAD-CAM 80.3 ± 35.0 µm, F=1.00, P=.319) showed no significant effect, but the impression-replica measurement instrument significantly overvalued gaps versus microscopy (β=37.86, P<.001).

The contemporary acceptable in vitro marginal gap limit is confirmed at 120 µm; clinicians and researchers should treat crown type/material/fabrication method as largely interchangeable for marginal fit, while recognizing that impression-replica-derived gap values run systematically high compared with optical methods.

## 세줄요약

우산연구(Umbrella Review, systematic review 17편, in vitro 크라운 측정 301건) — 합착 전 단일 크라운의 변연 간극(Marginal Gap) 결정 요인을 Welch ANOVA·Games-Howell 사후검정·클러스터 강건표준오차 다중회귀로 정량 통합.

평균 변연 간극 79.4 ± 22.8 µm(범위 34.5–125.5 µm); 크라운 종류(F=0.37, P=.775)·제작 재료(F=0.48, P=.784)·제작법(기존법 88.0 ± 35.0 µm vs CAD-CAM 80.3 ± 35.0 µm, F=1.00, P=.319) 모두 유의한 영향 없음, 그러나 인상 레플리카(Impression Replica) 측정 기구가 현미경 대비 유의하게 간극을 과대평가(β=37.86, P<.001).

현대 임상 허용 변연 간극 기준선은 120 µm로 재확인; 크라운 종류·재료·제작법은 변연 적합도 측면에서 상호 교체 가능하나, 인상 레플리카로 측정한 값은 광학적 방법보다 체계적으로 높게 나올 수 있음에 유의.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review to quantitatively pool systematic reviews of in vitro single-crown marginal gap studies (rather than qualitatively narrate them), using Welch ANOVA, Games-Howell post hoc, and robust-clustered multiple regression.
- Demonstrates that crown type (ceramic single crown, 1-piece ceramic endodontic crown, metal-ceramic, metal), fabrication material (zirconia, lithium disilicate, zirconia-reinforced lithium silicate, resin hybrid, cobalt chromium, etc.), and fabrication method (conventional vs CAD-CAM) do not significantly affect marginal gap.
- Isolates measurement instrument as the dominant confounder: impression replica measurement significantly overvalues marginal gap compared with microscopy (β=37.86, P<.001), explaining much of the reported variability across the field.
- Reaffirms the historically accepted 120 µm marginal gap threshold (McLean & von Fraunhofer) as the contemporary acceptable in vitro limit, based on pooled evidence (mean 79.4 ± 22.8 µm, all crown materials within limit).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review of systematic reviews of in vitro studies (PRISMA-guided), quantitative synthesis (not a formal meta-analysis of individual patient/specimen data).
- **Databases**: EBSCOHost (Dentistry & Oral Sciences Source), Scopus, PubMed, Web of Science, Cochrane; search conducted June 2025, no publication-date restriction, English language.
- **n**: 82 records screened → 38 full-text assessed → 17 systematic reviews included, covering 301 in vitro crown assessments; 17 additional systematic reviews were excluded for mixing in vivo/in vitro data inseparably.
- **Quality appraisal**: Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) 11-domain Critical Appraisal Checklist for Systematic Reviews, scored by 2 independent reviewers (J.D., T.H.F.); interreviewer agreement 0.84 (κ=0.63); mean score 82.7% (low risk), 1 study high risk.
- **Statistics**: Welch test for unequal variances + Games-Howell post hoc (crown type, fabrication material, tooth type, measurement instrument); multiple regression adjusted with robust clustered standard errors, dummy variables, and Wald robust model-level test (α=.05).
- **Outcomes**: Marginal gap (µm) as a function of crown type, crown fabrication material, measurement instrument, abutment tooth material, tooth type, and fabrication method (conventional vs CAD-CAM).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Mean marginal gap across 301 in vitro assessments: 79.4 ± 22.8 µm (range 34.5–125.5 µm, median 82.7 µm).
- Crown type: no significant effect (F=0.37, P=.775).
- Crown fabrication material: no significant effect (F=0.48, P=.784); resin hybrid materials showed the greatest variability.
- Measurement instrument: significant effect (Welch ANOVA F=4.32, P=.001); impression replica overvalued gaps vs microscopy (regression β=37.86, P<.001).
- Fabrication method: conventional 88.0 ± 35.0/35.1 µm vs CAD-CAM 80.3 ± 35.0/35.1 µm — no significant difference (F=1.00, P=.319).
- Standardized-metadata subset (4 studies, median 24 crowns/study, median 12 measurements/crown) used for the instrument/tooth-type/abutment/material analysis.
- Conclusion: contemporary acceptable limit for in vitro crown marginal gap = 120 µm (all crown materials fell within this limit).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Umbrella-review-of-umbrella-data design inherits heterogeneity from the 17 constituent SRs (differing measurement instruments, sample sizes, and reporting formats); only 4 of 17 had standardized/extractable metadata for the full multivariable analysis.
- In vitro-only scope (all in vivo/mixed-method SRs were excluded), so findings may not directly generalize to clinical (in-mouth) marginal gap behavior.
- Reliance on aggregate/study-level data (not individual specimen-level data) limits statistical power and the ability to fully adjust for nested covariates despite robust clustered SE.
- Publication bias assessment was rated unclear in most of the 17 included SRs per JBI domain D9.
- Future research directions: standardized measurement protocols (e.g., micro-CT) and specimen-level data pooling to enable formal meta-analysis.

## 6. Related Work
- contrepois-2013: single systematic review of ceramic crown marginal adaptation (1994–2012) that this umbrella review's search excluded as mixed in vivo/in vitro; its "no ceramic system ranks superior" conclusion is echoed and statistically confirmed here.
- sengottaiyan-2025: systematic review isolating tooth-preparation design (finish line, taper, occlusal reduction) as a marginal/internal-gap determinant; complementary variable to those tested in this umbrella review.
- omar-2026: in vitro study showing milled crowns have smaller marginal gaps than 3D-printed crowns, consistent with the "Mosaddad 2025" included review in this umbrella review's Table 2.

## 7. Glossary
- **Marginal gap**: The measured distance between the axial wall of the prepared tooth and the intaglio (inner) surface of the restoration at the margin, before cementation.
- **Umbrella review**: A review that synthesizes findings across multiple existing systematic reviews on a topic, rather than individual primary studies.
- **JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist**: An 11-domain Joanna Briggs Institute tool for assessing methodological quality/risk of bias of systematic reviews.
- **Welch test / Games-Howell post hoc**: Statistical tests for comparing group means under unequal variances, used here instead of standard ANOVA/Tukey because of heterogeneous SR-level data.
- **CAD-CAM**: Computer-aided design and computer-aided manufacturing; digital intraoral scanning + milling/printing fabrication workflow, contrasted with conventional impression + casting/pressing.
