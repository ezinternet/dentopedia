## Why Ingested

Directly addresses the clinical decision of whether to reglaze or polish after intraoral occlusal adjustment of monolithic zirconia restorations. Provides in-vitro evidence on three key properties—translucency, wettability, and flexural strength—across two 3Y-TZP and two 5Y-PSZ materials compared under glazed vs. polished conditions. High relevance to chairside clinical protocol.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/monteiro-2026-effect-of-occlusal-adjustment-and]]

## Three-line Summary (EN)

This in-vitro study compared glazed versus polished-after-occlusal-adjustment surface conditions across four monolithic zirconia types (two 3Y-TZP, two 5Y-PSZ) for translucency parameter (TP), contact angle, and biaxial flexural strength. Polishing maintained TP for all materials (not statistically different from glaze), increased contact angle (reduced wettability) in all groups, and produced material-specific flexural strength outcomes: increased in Cercon hT (~60% gain, 576→856 MPa), decreased in Lava Plus (578→229 MPa), and preserved in Prettau 4 Anterior and Cercon xT. Polishing after occlusal adjustment is clinically viable for three of four materials tested, eliminating the need for laboratory reglazing in most cases.

## 세줄요약 (KO)

4종 단일구조 지르코니아(3Y-TZP 2종·5Y-PSZ 2종)를 유약 처리 상태와 교합조정 후 연마 상태로 비교하여 투명도 (Translucency Parameter, TP), 접촉각, 굴곡강도를 평가한 체외 실험이다. 연마 후 TP는 모든 군에서 유약 처리와 통계적으로 유의한 차이 없이 유지되었고, 접촉각은 모든 군에서 증가(소수성↑)하였으며, 굴곡강도 (Flexural Strength)는 Cercon hT에서 약 60% 상승(576→856 MPa), Lava Plus에서 감소(578→229 MPa), Prettau 4 Anterior와 Cercon xT에서 보존되었다. 임상적으로 대부분의 단일구조 지르코니아 수복물은 재유약 처리 없이 구내 연마로 충분하며, 재내원 없이 같은 날 합착이 가능하다.

## Key Contributions

1. **Polishing preserves translucency**: Post-adjustment intraoral polishing maintained TP comparable to glaze across all four zirconia types — translucency is not sacrificed by polishing.
2. **Material-dependent flexural strength response**: The effect of polishing on flexural strength is strongly material-dependent (significant interaction p<0.001). 3Y-TZP Cercon hT gained ~60% strength (transformation toughening dominant); 3Y-TZP Lava Plus lost strength (surface defects dominant); 5Y-PSZ Prettau 4A and Cercon xT were preserved.
3. **Polishing increases contact angle universally**: Polished surfaces showed higher contact angles (more hydrophobic) than glazed surfaces across all materials, consistent with rougher micro-topography after polishing.
4. **Clinical viability for 3 of 4 materials**: Polishing after adjustment is a valid same-appointment alternative to lab reglazing for PA, hT, and xT; Lava Plus showed significant strength reduction and warrants caution.

## Methodology

- **Materials**: Prettau 4 Anterior (PA, 5Y-PSZ, Zirkonzahn), Lava Plus (LP, 3Y-TZP, 3M ESPE), Cercon hT (hT, 3Y-TZP, Dentsply Sirona), Cercon xT (xT, 5Y-PSZ, Dentsply Sirona)
- **Specimens**: Disk-shaped, Ø12×1 mm; sintered per manufacturer protocol
- **Glaze**: Prettau Plus glaze (80±20 μm), vacuum-fired at 750°C
- **Occlusal adjustment simulation**: Medium (90–160 μm) then fine-grained (38–45 μm) diamond burs at high speed
- **Polishing**: Zir Gloss intraoral polishing rubber kit (Jota) at low speed; glaze thickness reduced ~10–20 μm
- **Translucency Parameter (TP)**: Spectrophotometer (Konica Minolta CM-3700d), CIEDE2000, D65/8°
- **Contact angle**: Goniometer DSA25 (Krüss), sessile drop method, Young-Laplace fit
- **Flexural strength**: Piston-on-three-balls biaxial test, ISO 6872/2008, universal testing machine
- **Statistics**: Two-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD, α=0.05; Shapiro-Wilk normality; Minitab v17

## Key Results

### Translucency Parameter
| Group | Glazed TP | Polished TP | Sig. diff? |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA (5Y-PSZ) | 4.2 | 3.9 | No |
| LP (3Y-TZP) | 3.8 | 5.0 | No |
| hT (3Y-TZP) | 6.4 | 6.3 | No |
| xT (5Y-PSZ) | 6.9 | 6.6 | No |

- xT and hT had significantly higher TP than LP and PA (cubic phase effect)
- Surface condition (glaze vs. polish) did NOT significantly affect TP (p=0.723 main effect); interaction was significant (p=0.042) but no group showed significant within-material change

### Contact Angle
| Group | Glazed (°) | Polished (°) |
|---|---|---|
| PA | 21.8 | 31.1 |
| LP | 22.0 | 37.6 |
| hT | 13.4 | 39.6 |
| xT | 19.7 | 43.7 |

- All groups: polished > glazed (p<0.001)
- Surface condition was the dominant factor; polishing uniformly increased contact angle

### Flexural Strength (MPa)
| Group | Glazed | Polished | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA | 410.4 | 333.1 | Preserved (ns) |
| LP | 577.5 | 228.8 | Decreased ↓ |
| hT | 576.5 | 856.0 | Increased ↑↑ |
| xT | 384.5 | 287.1 | Preserved (ns) |

- hT increase attributed to transformation toughening (martensitic) > surface defects
- LP decrease attributed to surface defects > compression zone from phase transformation
- PA and xT: polishing preserved strength (no significant change)

## Limitations

1. Flat geometric disks used, not anatomical crowns; clinical geometry is more complex
2. No thermocycling or mechanical aging protocol; long-term performance not assessed
3. Single glaze material (Prettau Plus) applied to all zirconia types — not system-matched glazes
4. Intraoral factors (saliva, temperature, operator variability) not simulated
5. High coefficient of variation in xT polished group (60%) suggests heterogeneous defect population

## Glossary

- **TP (Translucency Parameter)**: Color difference (ΔE CIEDE2000) between measurements on black and white backgrounds; higher = more translucent
- **3Y-TZP**: 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal; predominantly tetragonal phase; high strength
- **5Y-PSZ**: 5 mol% yttria partially stabilized zirconia; higher cubic phase fraction; more translucent but lower strength
- **Martensitic transformation / transformation toughening**: Stress-induced tetragonal → monoclinic phase transformation creating compressive stresses that resist crack propagation
- **Contact angle**: Angle of water droplet on surface; higher = more hydrophobic; glazed surfaces are more hydrophilic (lower contact angle)
- **Piston-on-three-balls (biaxial flexural test)**: ISO 6872 standardized method for measuring ceramic flexural strength
