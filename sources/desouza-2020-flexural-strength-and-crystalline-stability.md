## Why Ingested

Serves the adjustment→phase-transformation/strength axis of the 2026-11-12 `zirconia-occlusal-adjustment` surveillance topic: it quantifies how much monoclinic-phase gain and strength change a clinically realistic diamond-bur grind produces, and whether a standard rubber-polish sequence recovers the surface — the core "how much adjustment is tolerable" question. It also supplies the second half of that axis, showing that the strength gain from adjustment-induced transformation toughening is erased once the same specimens meet hydrothermal aging, which reframes the post-adjustment polishing protocol as a durability question rather than a roughness question — compare [[dental-materials/zirconia/sarabi-2024-grinding-ltd-flexural-strength-zirconia]] and [[dental-materials/zirconia/mohammadi-bassir-2017-grinding-overglazing-polishing-zirconia]].

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (90 Ø12 × 1 mm discs, alumina-free 3Y-TZP "Vipi Block Zirconn Translucent", 3 surface treatments × 3 aging conditions, n = 10): as-sintered vs diamond-bur grind vs grind + rubber polish, each at baseline, autoclave (134 °C / 2.2 kgf·cm⁻² / 5 h) or thermocycling (200,000 cycles, 5/55 °C).

Grinding raised both roughness and biaxial flexural strength (Grind 1078 ± 235 MPa, Grind + Polish 1129 ± 139 MPa vs As Sintered 775 ± 190 MPa, p < 0.001); aging lowered strength regardless of surface treatment (Baseline 1149 ± 232 MPa → Autoclave 939 ± 211 → Thermocycling 916 ± 230, p < 0.001), and polishing never restored the as-sintered smoothness.

Clinical implication: adjustment-induced transformation toughening transiently protects the ground surface, but it is not a substitute for a fine-grain bur + polishing protocol — grinding with medium-grain burs left defects that standard polishing could not remove, and under LTD every surface treatment lost strength.

## 세줄요약

in-vitro (Ø12 × 1 mm 원판 90개, 알루미나 무첨가 3Y-TZP "Vipi Block Zirconn Translucent", 표면처리 3군 × 에이징 3군, 각 n = 10): 소결 직후 / 다이아몬드 버 연삭 / 연삭 + 러버 폴리싱 × 무에이징·오토클레이브(134 °C, 2.2 kgf·cm⁻², 5시간)·열사이클링(200,000회, 5/55 °C).

연삭은 표면거칠기 (surface roughness, Ra)와 이축 굴곡강도 (biaxial flexural strength)를 함께 높였고 (연삭 1078 ± 235 MPa, 연삭+폴리싱 1129 ± 139 MPa vs 소결 직후 775 ± 190 MPa, p < 0.001), 에이징은 표면처리와 무관하게 강도를 떨어뜨렸다 (무에이징 1149 ± 232 MPa → 오토클레이브 939 ± 211 → 열사이클링 916 ± 230, p < 0.001). 폴리싱 후에도 소결 직후의 매끄러움에는 도달하지 못했다.

임상적 의미: 교합조정으로 유발된 변태강화 (transformation toughening)는 일시적 방어일 뿐이며, 중립도 다이아몬드 버가 남긴 결함은 표준 폴리싱으로 완전히 제거되지 않는다 — 세립 버 + 폴리싱 프로토콜을 대체할 수 없고, 저온열화 (low-temperature degradation, LTD) 하에서는 모든 표면처리군이 강도를 잃었다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Separates the two competing consequences of chairside adjustment on the same specimens: **defect introduction** (roughness ↑) and **transformation toughening** (monoclinic phase ↑ → compressive stress → strength ↑). At baseline the toughening term wins — ground groups were *stronger* than the untouched as-sintered surface despite being rougher.
- Shows grind + polish does not restore as-sintered smoothness: defects from medium-grain diamond burs at low speed survived the standard rubber-polish sequence (SEM, Fig. 5).
- Demonstrates that autoclave aging and 200,000-cycle thermocycling produce equivalent LTD-driven strength loss — thermocycling is not merely a bonding-test aging method, it also drives t→m transformation and subsurface micro-defect growth.
- Reports the reversal of the baseline strength ranking after aging: the adjustment "benefit" is consumed by LTD, so surface treatment does not protect against long-term strength loss.
- Finds no statistically significant roughness–strength correlation (p > 0.05 in all conditions), i.e. roughness alone is a poor predictor of zirconia strength once phase transformation is at play.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Specimen prep: 90 discs cut from pre-sintered blocks, finished with 1200-grit SiC paper, ultrasonically cleaned in isopropyl alcohol, sintered at 1450 °C for 2 h (8 °C/min ramp).
- Surface treatment (n = 30 each): **As Sintered** (no treatment), **Grind** (diamond bur), **Grind + Polish** (diamond bur + sequential rubber points). Single calibrated operator, low-speed handpiece at 8,000–10,000 rpm, oscillating movements under digital pressure until a marker line was fully removed; diamond burs replaced every 5 specimens. This mimics fitting-surface and post-cementation occlusal adjustment.
- Aging (n = 10 each): **Baseline** (no aging); **Autoclave** 134 °C, 2.2 kgf/cm², 5 h (≈ 21 years at 37 °C); **Thermocycling** 200,000 cycles, 5 °C / 55 °C, 15 s dwell (≈ 20 years in service).
- Outcomes: (1) Ra by profilometer (5 µm diamond tip, 2.5 mm traverse at 0.5 mm/s, 0.25 mm cut-off, three readings 45° apart); (2) biaxial flexural strength, piston-on-three-ball, treated surface in tension, immersed in artificial saliva at 37 °C, 1.7 mm piston, 1 mm/min, ISO 6872 equations; (3) monoclinic volume fraction by XRD (CuKα, 25–35° 2θ, 0.03° step, 1 s/step, Toraya–Garvie-type equations); (4) SEM (500×, 1000×, 5000×) for topography.
- Statistics: two-way ANOVA (surface treatment × aging) + Tukey (α = 0.05); Spearman correlation for roughness vs strength.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Biaxial flexural strength — pooled means (SD), MPa; different letters differ significantly:

| Factor | Level | Strength (MPa) |
|---|---|---|
| Surface treatment | As Sintered | 775 (190) B |
| Surface treatment | Grind | 1078 (235) A |
| Surface treatment | Grind + Polish | 1129 (139) A |
| Aging | Baseline | 1149 (232) A |
| Aging | Autoclave | 939 (211) B |
| Aging | Thermocycling | 916 (230) B |

- ANOVA: surface treatment p < 0.001; aging p < 0.001; surface × aging interaction p = 0.239 (not significant) — i.e. the aging penalty was similar in all three surface conditions.
- Roughness: surface treatment p < 0.001, aging p = 0.025, interaction p < 0.001. Grind > Grind + Polish > As Sintered; autoclave aging raised Ra above baseline. Per-group Ra values appear only in Fig. 1 and Table 2 (not machine-readable in the text layer).
- Grind + Polish was statistically indistinguishable from Grind for strength (both "A") — polishing did not cost strength, but it did not recover the pristine surface either.
- Correlation (Spearman, strength vs Ra): weak positive across all groups, strong positive within baseline-only groups, but not significant in any condition (p > 0.05) — confounding by phase transformation.
- XRD: monoclinic peak at 28° 2θ appeared in all ground/polished groups (mechanical stress) and after both aging methods (LTD); the tetragonal peak at 30° 2θ broadened in surface-treated groups, indicating lattice plastic deformation. Monoclinic fraction rose after aging in the As Sintered group while surface-treated groups were already high at baseline and stayed similarly high after aging. Numeric Vm values are only in Table 2 (image), not in the text layer.
- SEM: grinding created directional surface defects with lateral projections; rubber polishing markedly reduced defect length and depth; aging produced no visible topographical change in either modality.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single brand/composition (alumina-free 3Y-TZP); the authors explicitly note that cubic-containing translucent zirconias (4Y/5Y-PSZ) are both less transformation-toughenable and less LTD-susceptible, so these results should not be generalized to them.
- XRD has limited depth penetration, so the subsurface transformed layer that actually governs strength loss was not quantified — surface Vm alone does not explain the strength drop.
- Only one grinding instrument grade (medium diamond) and one polishing sequence; no fine/extra-fine bur arm despite the paper's own recommendation to prefer them.
- Accelerated aging (5 h autoclave, 200,000 cycles) substitutes for, but does not reproduce, intraoral loading, saliva chemistry and toothbrushing.
- No fatigue/cyclic loading, no bond-strength testing — the authors flag the effect of LTD-induced surface degradation on zirconia bonding as an open question.
- Monoclinic fractions and per-group roughness/strength means live in figures/tables that are not in the extracted text; only pooled means are citable from the text layer.

## 7. Glossary

- **LTD (저온열화, Low-Temperature Degradation)**: 수분·열 존재 시 t→m 상전환이 표면에서 시작해 입계로 물이 침투하며 심부로 진행되는 현상. 미세균열 → Ra 증가 → 강도 저하.
- **Transformation toughening (변태강화)**: 정방정 (tetragonal) → 단사정 (monoclinic) 전환 시 3–5% 체적팽창이 국소 압축응력을 만들어 균열 진행을 억제하는 기전.
- **Monoclinic fraction (Vm, 단사정 부피분율)**: XRD (111)m / (111)m 대 (101)t 피크 강도비로 계산한 상전환 정도.
- **Biaxial flexural strength (이축 굴곡강도)**: piston-on-three-ball 시험으로 ISO 6872 식에 따라 산출한 파절강도 — 3점 굴곡과 달리 방향성이 없어 디스크 시편에 적합.
- **Ra (산술평균 표면거칠기, arithmetic mean roughness)**: 촉침식 profilometer로 측정한 중심선 평균 거칠기.
- **3Y-TZP**: 3 mol% 이트리아 안정화 정방정 지르코니아 다결정 — 가장 전통적인 치과용 지르코니아, 변태강화 활성이 높고 LTD 취약.
