## Why Ingested
This systematic review with meta-analysis directly addresses the critical clinical question of whether clear aligners can reliably close extraction spaces — a common clinical scenario where predictability failures have significant consequences for treatment planning decisions.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review with meta-analysis of 14 studies (510 participants) evaluating clear aligner therapy (CAT) for space closure after premolar extraction (SCaPE), published through November 2024.
CAT significantly underperformed its own predictions: anterior teeth retraction fell short (SPD = -0.87), with more lingual tipping (SPD = 1.09) and extrusion (SPD = 0.88) than planned; canine distal tipping (SPD = 1.42) and first molar mesial tipping (SPD = 1.68) exceeded predictions — evidence certainty very low to low (GRADE).
Clear aligners are likely not effective or predictable for space closure after premolar extraction; fixed appliances may be superior for bodily tooth movement and anchorage control in extraction cases.

## 세줄요약
발치 후 공간 폐쇄를 위한 투명교정장치 치료(CAT)를 평가한 14편(510명) 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석(2024년 11월까지 검색).
CAT는 전치 후방 이동(SPD=-0.87, 예측 미달), 전치 설측 경사(SPD=1.09)·정출(SPD=0.88) 예측 초과, 견치 원심 경사(SPD=1.42)·제1대구치 근심 경사(SPD=1.68) 예측 초과로 자체 예측치 대비 유의하게 미흡; GRADE 근거 수준 매우 낮음~낮음.
투명교정장치는 발치 공간 폐쇄에 효과적·예측 가능하지 않을 가능성이 높으며, 치체이동 및 고정원 조절 측면에서 고정식 장치가 우월할 수 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Quantified the gap between predicted and achieved tooth movements in CAT for space closure after premolar extraction (SCaPE)
- Meta-analysis using Standardized Paired Difference (SPD) with 95% Prediction Interval — a rigorous approach for paired pre-post data
- Identified systematic patterns of failure: lingual tipping and extrusion of anterior teeth, uncontrolled tipping of canines and molars
- Assessed anchorage control as a key limitation of CAT in extraction cases
- Compared CAT vs fixed appliances in clinical trials (limited evidence available)
- Applied GRADE to rate certainty of evidence

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library (through November 12, 2024)
- **Included study designs**: Pre-post studies, RCTs, non-randomized clinical trials
- **Reviewers**: 3 independent reviewers for data extraction
- **Risk of bias**: ROB-2 (RCTs) and ROBINS-I (non-randomized studies)
- **Meta-analysis**: Compared predicted vs achieved maxillary tooth movements (Invisalign® system studies)
- **Effect size**: Standardized Paired Difference (SPD) with 95% Prediction Interval (PI) and 95% Confidence Interval (CI)
- **Evidence certainty**: GRADE
- **Included studies**: 14 studies (510 participants); 4 low risk of bias, 10 high risk of bias

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
### Predicted vs Achieved — Anterior Teeth Retraction
- **Anterior teeth retraction**: SPD = -0.87 (95% CI: -1.15 to -0.60; p < 0.001; n=4 studies) — CAT significantly LESS effective than predicted

### Anterior Teeth Unintended Movements
- **Lingual tipping of anterior teeth**: SPD = 1.09 (95% CI: 0.46 to 1.73; p = 0.001; n=3) — significantly MORE tipping than predicted
- **Extrusion of anterior teeth**: SPD = 0.88 (95% CI: 0.47 to 1.29; p < 0.001; n=4) — significantly MORE extrusion than predicted

### Anchorage Loss (Unintended Posterior Movement)
- **Canine distal tipping**: SPD = 1.42 (95% CI: 0.35 to 2.51; p = 0.009; n=3) — significantly MORE than predicted
- **First molar mesial tipping (anchorage loss)**: SPD = 1.68 (95% CI: 1.17 to 2.20; p < 0.001; n=3) — significantly MORE than predicted

### CAT vs Fixed Appliances
- Clinical trial comparisons provide **limited evidence** — insufficient data for definitive meta-analysis

### Evidence Quality (GRADE)
- Very low to low certainty across outcomes

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 10 of 14 studies had high risk of bias
- Most studies used only the Invisalign® system — limited generalizability to other aligner brands
- No standardized definition of "successful" space closure across studies
- Most studies lacked long-term follow-up (stability/relapse data)
- Heterogeneity in extraction patterns (first vs second premolars), malocclusion types, and auxiliary mechanics used
- Need for RCTs comparing CAT with and without extraction-specific auxiliaries (attachments, elastics, TADs)

## 6. Related Work
- Positioned against broader clear aligner predictability literature showing systematic underperformance for complex movements (torque, extrusion, large translations)
- References Invisalign® ClinCheck® software predictions as the comparator for predicted vs achieved analysis
- Relates to anchorage control literature in extraction orthodontics (importance of posterior anchorage in first premolar extraction mechanics)

## 7. Glossary
- **SCaPE**: Space Closure after Premolar Extraction
- **SPD (Standardized Paired Difference)**: Effect size for paired pre-post comparisons, analogous to Cohen's d for paired data
- **95% PI (Prediction Interval)**: Range expected to contain the true effect in a new similar study (wider than CI, reflects between-study heterogeneity)
- **Bodily movement (치체이동)**: Translation of the entire tooth including root apex, as opposed to tipping
- **Anchorage loss (고정원 소실)**: Unintended mesial movement of posterior teeth during space closure
- **Lingual tipping (설측 경사)**: Retroclination of anterior teeth with apex remaining in place
- **TADs (Temporary Anchorage Devices)**: Mini-screws used for skeletal anchorage to prevent anchorage loss
- **ROB-2**: Revised Cochrane Risk of Bias tool for RCTs
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk of Bias in Non-randomized Studies of Interventions
