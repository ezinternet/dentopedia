## Why Ingested

Two existing pages ([[prosthetic-materials/velez-2020-implant-connection-abutment-design-screw]] on implant-abutment misfit, and the synthesis [[overviews/implant-prosthesis-misfit-connection-mbl-overview]]) already flag "excess-cement control" as a lever for peri-implant marginal bone loss (MBL) — the overview's Kim 2022 branch notes SCRP (screw-and-cement-retained prosthesis) was protective against MBL "attributed to extraoral cementation allowing complete excess-cement removal," but no ingested paper actually quantifies *how much* cement is left behind as a function of margin depth or cement type. This in-vitro study (Gehrke 2019) supplies exactly that missing mechanistic link: it directly measures undetected cement remnant area on CAD/CAM molar abutments at 0/1/2/3 mm submucosal margin depths with two luting materials (zinc oxide non-eugenol vs methacrylate), giving the overview's "excess-cement control matters" claim a concrete evidence base and an actionable threshold (do not exceed 1.5 mm subgingival margin depth in proximal/oral regions).

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study, 20 titanium CAD/CAM molar implant abutments with convex emergence profile, margin positions simulated at 0/1/2/3 mm below a silicone gingival mask, zirconia crowns cemented with zinc oxide non-eugenol (Temp Bond NE) or methacrylate (Panavia V5) cement, cement remnant area quantified by digital-photograph planimetry.

Cement remnants were found at every margin depth and nearly every abutment surface, increasing as the margin was placed more submucosally; lingual surfaces were significantly more prone to residue with Panavia V5 at 3 mm depth (p=0.0291); basal (undercut) abutment surface residue was significantly lower with Panavia V5 (4.9%±3.7%) than Temp Bond NE (8.6%±5.5%, p=0.007), with no significant difference between cements on lateral surfaces.

Clinical implication: CAD/CAM molar abutment margins should be placed as coronally as possible; if subgingival placement is unavoidable for esthetics, margin depth should not exceed 1.5 mm in proximal/oral regions to limit undetected cement excess, a known driver of peri-implant inflammation and MBL — key limitation is the in-vitro silicone-mask model cannot fully replicate the peri-implant sulcus or clinical cementation conditions.

## 세줄요약

시험관내 (in-vitro) 연구, 티타늄 CAD/CAM 구치부 임플란트 어버트먼트 20개, 실리콘 치은 마스크로 변연 (margin) 위치를 점막하 0/1/2/3mm로 재현, 지르코니아 크라운을 산화아연비유지놀 (Temp Bond NE) 또는 메타크릴레이트 (Panavia V5) 시멘트로 접착 후 잔류 시멘트 면적을 디지털 사진 평면측정법으로 정량.

변연이 점막하로 깊어질수록 모든 깊이·거의 모든 표면에서 잔류 시멘트가 검출됐고, 설측 (lingual) 면은 Panavia V5 3mm 깊이에서 유의하게 더 취약했으며 (p=0.0291), 기저부 (basal) 표면 잔류율은 Panavia V5 (4.9%±3.7%)가 Temp Bond NE (8.6%±5.5%)보다 유의하게 낮았음 (p=0.007), 측면 표면에서는 두 시멘트 간 유의차 없음.

임상적 의미: CAD/CAM 구치부 어버트먼트 변연은 최대한 치은연상 (coronal)에 위치시켜야 하며, 심미상 불가피하게 점막하로 위치시킬 경우 근원심·설측 (proximal/oral) 영역에서 1.5mm를 초과하지 않아야 미검출 잔류 시멘트 — 임플란트주위염·변연골소실 (MBL)의 알려진 위험인자 — 를 줄일 수 있음; 핵심 한계는 실리콘 마스크 모델이 실제 임플란트주위 열구·임상 접착 조건을 완전히 재현하지 못한다는 점.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies undetected cement remnant area on **CAD/CAM custom molar abutments** (not stock/prefabricated abutments, which prior literature had focused on), across a range of controlled submucosal margin depths (0, 1, 2, 3 mm).
- Directly compares two commonly used luting materials — zinc oxide non-eugenol (Temp Bond NE, provisional) vs methacrylate (Panavia V5, definitive resin) — for their propensity to leave detectable residue, both at lateral abutment surfaces and at the difficult-to-clean basal (undercut, convex emergence profile) surface.
- Confirms that cement remnants occur not only at the crown-abutment margin but also underneath the convex abutment body itself, an area inaccessible to conventional cleaning instruments (scaler, super-floss).
- Provides an actionable clinical threshold: do not exceed 1.5 mm subgingival margin depth in proximal/oral regions when a supragingival/epigingival margin is not esthetically feasible.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 20 titanium CAD/CAM implant abutments (BEGO Medical GmbH) with internal taper connection/internal hex, convex emergence profile, virtually designed (Implant Studio, 3Shape) at margin depths of 0, 1, 2, and 3 mm below a simulated mucosal margin.
- Peri-implant soft tissue simulated with silicone (polyvinyl siloxane) gingival masks (Gingifast), replicated for each of the 4 margin depths, based on a 3D-printed duplicate of a clinical master cast.
- 20 corresponding monolithic zirconia crowns (Zirlux) designed/milled (Cerec 3D) with occlusal access openings (to protect/retrieve the abutment screw after cementation).
- Two cementation trials on the same abutments (sequential, with thorough interim cleaning/decontamination between trials): Trial 1 — Temp Bond NE (zinc oxide non-eugenol); Trial 2 — Panavia V5 (methacrylate resin cement).
- Cementation performed by one experienced clinician; cement excess removed by a second, blinded investigator using a steel scaler and super-floss, simulating real clinical cleanup.
- Cement remnant quantification: Linkevicius et al.'s computerized planimetric method — standardized digital macro photographs of all 4 quadrants (mesial/buccal/distal/lingual) per specimen, cement-covered area vs total surface area measured in Adobe Photoshop (lasso tool + histogram pixel count). Intra-examiner calibration agreement coefficient 0.96.
- Statistics: Mann-Whitney-U and Kruskal-Wallis H tests, significance at p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Cement remnants detected at essentially every margin depth and abutment surface investigated, for both cements.
- Temp Bond NE: undetected residue on lateral surfaces trended upward from 0→2 mm margin depth, slight decrease at 3 mm; no depth-vs-frequency association reached significance overall (p=0.1520) or per-surface (mesial p=0.1106, distal p=0.0581, buccal p=0.061, lingual p=0.1312); basal-surface residue not significantly associated with margin depth (p=0.336).
- Panavia V5: no significant depth association at buccal (p=0.0860), mesial (p=0.0922), distal (p=0.9679), basal (p=0.9846); **lingual surface significant at 3 mm depth (p=0.0291)**.
- Cement-type comparison: basal surface residue significantly lower with Panavia V5 (4.9%±3.7%) vs Temp Bond NE (8.6%±5.5%, p=0.007); lateral surface sum not significantly different between cements (p=0.398).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro model; small sample size (n=20 abutments per cement trial, but same 20 abutments reused across both trials, sequentially).
- Silicone gingival mask cannot fully replicate the biological/mechanical properties of the peri-implant sulcus.
- Cement application amount not standardized (protocol intentionally mimicked real clinical variability rather than dosing precisely) — authors suggest a micro-brush or automatic pipette for more standardized future studies.
- 2D digital-photograph planimetry may underestimate true cement volume compared to a 3D method like SEM; cement volume/weight were not directly measured.
- The single statistically significant finding (lingual, Panavia V5, 3 mm, p=0.0291) is explicitly flagged by the authors as needing cautious interpretation given the small sample.
- Findings cannot be directly extrapolated to clinical outcomes (peri-implant inflammation/MBL) — this study measures cement residue only, not downstream biological consequences.

## 6. Related Work

- Cited prior stock-abutment literature (Agar et al. and others) establishing that deeper crown-abutment margins impair cement-excess removal — this study extends that finding to CAD/CAM custom molar abutments specifically.
- Pietruski et al. (cited in Discussion) found that CAD-planned abutment shoulder position vs actual clinical/mucosal position mismatched in 20% of 257 abutments, recommending a slightly deeper-than-planned margin for soft-tissue stability — creating a direct clinical tension against this paper's "keep margins coronal" recommendation that must be balanced case-by-case.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAD/CAM abutment**: Computer-aided-designed and -manufactured custom implant abutment, allowing individualized submucosal contour and margin position vs prefabricated stock abutments.
- **Margin location / crown-abutment margin**: The junction line between the abutment and the crown, positioned supra-, epi-, or sub-mucosally relative to the peri-implant soft tissue.
- **Cement remnant / residual cement excess**: Luting cement not fully removed after cementation and cleanup, retained in the peri-implant sulcus or on the abutment/crown surface.
- **Planimetric method**: A quantitative image-analysis technique measuring the proportion of a surface area covered by a feature (here, cement) from calibrated digital photographs.
- **Zinc oxide non-eugenol cement**: A provisional/temporary luting cement (e.g., Temp Bond NE) intended for retrievable restorations.
- **Methacrylate resin cement**: A definitive adhesive resin luting cement (e.g., Panavia V5) offering stronger, less soluble bonding than provisional cements.
- **Basal abutment surface**: The undercut region beneath a convex-profile abutment, inaccessible to conventional cleaning instruments after cementation.
