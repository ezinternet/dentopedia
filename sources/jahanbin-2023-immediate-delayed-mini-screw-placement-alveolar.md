## Why Ingested
This canine split-mouth study evaluates unloaded mini-screw placement (immediate vs delayed, 6 weeks post-extraction) for alveolar ridge preservation after maxillary lateral incisor extraction, showing improved bone height/density/histologic bone formation; it extends evidence in [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/araujo-2003-dynamics-bone-tissue-formation-extraction-sites]] and related ridge preservation literature by testing a mechanical/anchorage-device approach rather than graft/membrane alone.

## Three-line Summary
(Line 1: Split-mouth canine study (12 beagles), bilateral maxillary lateral incisor extraction; immediate (n=6) vs delayed (6 wk post-extraction, n=6) unilateral mini-screw placement, evaluated ~11 wk after insertion via CBCT + histology.)
(Line 2: Immediate placement preserved vertical bone height better vs control (12.1±1.4 vs 11.7±1.3 mm, p=0.02); both groups showed increased bone density around screws (especially delayed at 0-1 mm) and significantly higher osteoid/bone formation vs controls (p<0.05).)
(Line 3: Unloaded mini-screws enhance bone formation/density and immediate placement better preserves vertical height; width unchanged, inflammation not increased — simple cost-effective ridge preservation candidate needing human validation.)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct comparison of immediate vs delayed unloaded mini-screw placement in extraction sockets for ridge preservation (maxilla, canine).
- Demonstrates immediate placement superior for vertical bone height preservation; delayed placement yields greater bone density gains around screws.
- Histomorphometry shows significantly increased osteoid formation on screw sides in both groups vs minimal formation on controls.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Split-mouth, 12 beagles (male), bilateral maxillary lateral incisor extraction.
- **Groups**: Immediate (screw ~3–4 mm apical to crest immediately post-extraction) vs delayed (6 weeks post-extraction), n=6 each (4 lost to screw loosening).
- **Intervention**: Unloaded titanium mini-screws (1.5 mm × 6.5 mm, Jeil). 
- **Outcomes**: CBCT bone height/width/density (0,1,2 mm apical to screw entry) and histology (inflammation, osteoid formation score 1-3, osteoid extension area).
- **Timeline**: Sacrificed ~11 weeks post-screw insertion (mean ~82 days immediate, ~79 days delayed).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Height**: All samples +0.3±0.3 mm on screw side (p=0.014); immediate +0.4±0.3 mm vs control (p=0.023), delayed n.s.; difference immediate>delayed (p=0.029).
- **Width**: No significant effect in either group.
- **Density (Hu)**: Screw side higher at 0 mm overall (1411.6±175.9 vs 1276.7±175.9, p<0.001); difference larger delayed (183.3±86.3) vs immediate (86.5±50.5, p=0.025). At 1 mm higher overall (p=0.001), significant in delayed only. At 2 mm no difference.
- **Histology**: Bone formation score higher on screw side vs control in both groups (immediate 2.7±0.5 vs 0, p=0.023; delayed 2.5±0.8 vs 0.2±0.4, p=0.034); osteoid extension greater on screw side (immediate 4.27±0.161 vs 0.037±0.008 mm^2, p<0.001; delayed 6.086±0.803 vs 0.182±0.125 mm^2, p=0.033). No significant between-group difference in formation/inflammation.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Canine maxilla model; translational relevance to humans needs validation.
- Only male dogs; unloaded screws (no orthodontic force).
- Some screw loosening (4/12) led to exclusion; follow-up ~11 weeks may miss longer-term remodeling.
- Width effects may need longer observation.

## 7. Glossary
- **ARP (alveolar ridge preservation)**: Procedures to limit post-extraction dimensional changes of the alveolar ridge.
- **Mini-screw (temporary anchorage device)**: Small titanium screw used for orthodontic anchorage; here tested unloaded for ridge preservation.
- **CBCT (cone-beam computed tomography)**: 3D radiographic technique used to assess bone height/width/density.
- **Osteoid**: Unmineralized bone matrix; histologic marker of new bone formation.
