## Three-line Summary

Pilot RCT (n=30, 28 completed 3yr): monolithic zirconia RBFPD inlay-retained (IR) vs wing-retained (WR) for posterior tooth replacement — 3yr IR survival 100%, WR 93.3%; no significant difference (P=0.317)

Success IR 72.7% vs WR 86.7% (P=0.379, NS) — one WR debonding at 3mo; wing design preferred for intact abutment teeth due to lower invasiveness

Cement protocol: tribochemical silica coating + silanization + self-etching resin cement (APA equivalent)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First pilot RCT directly comparing IR vs WR design for posterior zirconia RBFPD
- 3yr IR survival 100% vs WR 93.3% — statistically non-significant difference
- Despite higher numerical success for WR (86.7%), clinical significance limited by small n
- Wing retainer preferred for abutment teeth that are largely defect-free (less invasive)
- Inlay retainer viable when abutment already has a restoration requiring replacement
- Cement: Rocatec tribochemical coating + Clearfil Ceramic Primer + Panavia 21

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Prospective pilot RCT, 30 patients (28 completed 3yr), mandibular second premolar/first/second molar replacement
- IR group: n=15 (mean age 56.4yr, 66.7% male)
- WR group: n=15 (mean age 45.9yr, 46.7% male)
- Material: monolithic zirconia (Cercon ht, DeguDent)
- Cementation: ED Primer + Panavia 21 after Rocatec silica coating + silanization
- Follow-up: 1wk, 3mo, 6mo, 1yr, 2yr, 3yr
- Statistics: Kaplan-Meier, log-rank test, Fisher's exact test (α=0.05)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | IR | WR | P value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3yr failure-free survival | 100% | 93.3% | 0.317 NS |
| 3yr success | 72.7% | 86.7% | 0.379 NS |

- 1 failure: WR RBFPD unilateral debonding at 3mo
- 1 withdrawal: patient with intact IR RBFPD (health reasons)
- Success criteria: FDI criteria scores

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Pilot study: underpowered for definitive conclusions
- Heterogeneous replacement sites (premolar + molars)
- Different age/sex distribution between groups (not matched)
- 3yr short-to-medium term; 5yr+ data needed
- Single material/cement system
