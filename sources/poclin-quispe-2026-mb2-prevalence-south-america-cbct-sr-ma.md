## One-line Summary
SR+MA (Front Dent Med 2026): 23 CBCT studies, South America; pooled MB2 prevalence 59%; maxillary first molars 66%, second molars 41%.

## 1. Document Information

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA on MB2 prevalence specifically for South American populations using CBCT
- Pooled estimate: 59% overall; separates first molar (66%) from second molar (41%)
- Adds population-specific CBCT data to global MB2 prevalence literature
- Higher prevalence in first vs second molars — important for clinical anticipation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA
- n: 23 CBCT studies from South American populations
- Outcome: MB2 canal prevalence (%) in maxillary molars
- Subgroups: maxillary first molars vs second molars
- Detection method: CBCT (standardized diagnostic modality across studies)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall pooled MB2 prevalence: 59%
- Maxillary first molars: 66%
- Maxillary second molars: 41%
- Regional range across South American studies likely shows heterogeneity

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- South American population may not be generalizable globally
- CBCT slice thickness/resolution varies across studies
- Patient selection bias possible (referred cases may skew toward complex anatomy)

## 6. Related Work
- MB2 prevalence in Saudi Arabia: alnowailaty-2022 (CBCT-based, Middle Eastern population)
- MB2 detection rates correlate with methodology: CBCT > microscope > loupe > clinical exploration

## 7. Glossary
- MB2: second mesiobuccal canal of maxillary molars
- CBCT: Cone Beam Computed Tomography — imaging gold standard for MB2 detection
- Prevalence: proportion of teeth/patients with MB2 canal identified
