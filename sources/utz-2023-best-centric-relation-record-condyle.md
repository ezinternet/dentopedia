## Why Ingested

Centric-relation (CR) recording is the most error-sensitive step of jaw-relation determination, and [[wiki/overviews/gothic-arch-jaw-relation-recording-overview]] argues that accuracy is reference-dependent with no anatomic gold standard — this study puts that thesis to a direct test by asking whether any single CR record is "best" across 81 subjects and 6 methods. It independently reinforces the sub-millimetre envelope and reference-dependent ranking of [[wiki/complete-denture/jasz-2024-centric-relation-methods-digital-mandibular-motion]] and extends the CR reproducibility framework of [[wiki/complete-denture/de-moraes-melo-neto-2021-centric-relation-condylar-reproducibility-sr]]. ## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional study (81 stomatognathically healthy, fully dentate subjects; 4 licensed clinicians; 6 centric-relation (CR) record types) compared the condylar positions produced by different CR registration methods.

Median spatial distance between the central bearing point (CBP) registration on the tip of the Gothic arch and all other CR records was 0.73 ± 0.24 mm (range 0–3.19 mm); median spatial values among the different CR records were 0.48–0.79 mm, almost all registration positions were slightly but significantly different, yet reproducibilities and SDs were similar.

No single CR record is universally "best" — unrefined wax-wafer registration with bimanual manipulation performed best for precision and time requirements, whereas CBP registration was best with regard to condylar position.

어떤 단일 CR 기록법도 절대적으로 최적은 아니다 — 정밀도·시간 면에서는 양수조작(Bimanual manipulation)을 병용한 미가공 왁스웨이퍼(wax wafer)가, 과두위 포착에서는 CBP 기록이 가장 우수했다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Direct test of the "best CR record" question** — six CR record types compared within the same cohort on both the registration position they produce and their reproducibility.
2. **Central bearing point (CBP) as an explicit condylar-position reference** — mandibular casts were mounted with a CBP registration on the tip of the Gothic arch, giving a defined reference against which the other CR records' condylar positions were measured.
3. **Reproducibility separated from position** — reproducibilities and SDs between registrations were similar across methods while the registered positions differed, so "best" splits by criterion (precision/time versus condylar position) rather than collapsing to one ranking.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: cross-sectional, repeated measures; 81 stomatognathically healthy, fully dentate subjects.
- **Operators**: 4 licensed clinicians recorded the condylar positions in CR.
- **Interventions**: 6 different types of CR records, each registered three times; mean values from the three registrations per method were calculated and comparative calculations made.
- **Mounting**: maxillary casts mounted in articulators after individual facebow transfer; mandibular casts mounted with a central bearing point (CBP) registration on the tip of the Gothic arch.
- **Outcomes**: spatial distances (mm) between the condylar positions yielded by the different CR records, plus reproducibility/SD.
- **Source limitation**: only the PubMed abstract was available; detailed statistics, power calculation, and full method/results text are not accessible.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **CBP versus the other CR records**: median spatial distance of the condylar positions was **0.73 ± 0.24 mm** (range **0 to 3.19 mm**).
- **Between different CR records**: median spatial values ranged **0.48 to 0.79 mm**.
- **Statistical separation**: positions of almost all types of registration were **slightly different from each other**.
- **Reproducibility**: reproducibilities and SDs between registrations were **similar** across methods.
- **Best for precision and time**: **unrefined wax-wafer registration using bimanual manipulation**.
- **Best for condylar position**: **CBP registration**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only — full text not retrieved**; no PMCID. Only the PubMed abstract was available, so methodology details, statistical tests, and the complete results/discussion are unavailable. No full-text details have been fabricated for this page.
- **Fully dentate, stomatognathically healthy cohort** — generalization to edentulous or TMD patients, where CR recording is most clinically critical, is not established.
- **"Best" is criterion-dependent**: the paper shows a method wins on precision/time while another wins on condylar position; it does not yield a single superiority ranking.
- **CBP as the condylar-position reference**: the abstract does not identify an independent anatomic gold standard, so "best for condylar position" is relative to the CBP mounting reference.

## 7. Glossary

- **Centric Relation (CR, 중심위)**: maxillomandibular reference position in which the condyles sit anterior-superior against the posterior slopes of the articular eminences, independent of tooth contact.
- **Central Bearing Point (CBP, 중심지점)**: the central contact point of the Gothic-arch tracing at which the registrating stylus/needle converges; used here to mount the mandibular cast.
- **Gothic arch tracing (고딕아치 트레이싱)**: arrow-point graphic registration whose apex marks the retruded/centric mandibular position.
- **Bimanual manipulation (양수조작)**: operator-guided seating of the condyles using both hands to obtain a reproducible CR.
- **Wax wafer (왁스웨이퍼)**: an interocclusal wax registration medium; "unrefined" here means used without additional reinforcement/trimming.
- **Reproducibility (재현성)**: agreement among repeated registrations by the same or different operators — distinct from agreement with a reference position.
- **Standard Deviation (SD, 표준편차)**: dispersion statistic reported alongside the median spatial distances.
