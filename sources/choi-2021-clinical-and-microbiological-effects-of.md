## Why Ingested

Requested on 2026-11-25 as part of a literature batch relevant to Dongkook's Minoclin (2% minocycline HCl periodontal ointment); Periocline is the same drug, strength and dosage-form class, and this is a Korean-institution RCT of that ointment in the supportive periodontal therapy (maintenance) phase, a setting the wiki's local-minocycline evidence does not cover. It reinforces the null adjunct result of [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/zainuddin-2024-effectiveness-of-conventional-periodontal-treatment]] and qualifies the modest pooled benefit in [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/soysa-2025-the-long-term-efficacy-of]] (mostly active-treatment SRP populations) for already-maintained patients; see also the deep-site-only effect in [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/arnett-2023-effect-of-scaling-and-root]].

## Three-line Summary

Pilot parallel-group RCT (Gangneung-Wonju National University Dental Hospital, Korea, April-October 2018; 24 adults with chronic periodontitis who had already been in supportive periodontal therapy (SPT) for more than 1 year, with at least 4 sites of PD of 5 mm or more and BoP above 25%; 12 per arm randomized, 12 test and 10 control analyzed) comparing whole-mouth subgingival debridement plus a single application of 2% minocycline HCl microcapsule gel (Periocline, 20 mg per subject) versus debridement plus saline irrigation, with clinical and real-time PCR assessment at baseline, 1 and 3 months.

Both arms improved from baseline and did not differ: mean PPD 3.55 to 3.37 to 3.42 mm (control) vs 3.35 to 3.27 to 3.36 mm (test), between-group p = 0.476 at 1 month and 0.685 at 3 months; CAL 5.26 to 4.74 mm vs 4.85 to 4.65 mm at 3 months (p = 0.810); BoP 41.03% to 24.98% vs 43.38% to 23.71% at 3 months (p = 0.794); P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum ratios did not change significantly, with only a non-significant tendency to fall in the test arm and rise in the control arm at 1 month.

In maintenance-phase patients the benefit came from the mechanical debridement itself, and a single minocycline ointment application added nothing detectable at 3 months; with 10-12 patients per arm, no sample-size justification, no described allocation concealment or blinding, and only 3 months of follow-up, this shows "no difference detected", not equivalence.

## 세줄요약

소규모 예비 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 한국 강릉원주대학교 치과병원, 2018년 4-10월): 치주 유지관리 치료 (Supportive Periodontal Therapy, SPT)를 1년 이상 받은 만성 치주염 성인 24명 (치주낭 깊이 5 mm 이상 부위 4곳 이상, 탐침출혈 25% 초과; 군당 12명 배정, 시험군 12명·대조군 10명 분석)을 전악 치주낭 내 디브리드먼트 + 2% 미노사이클린 염산염 마이크로캡슐 겔 (Periocline, 1인당 20 mg) 1회 적용군과 디브리드먼트 + 생리식염수 세척군으로 나누어 기저, 1개월, 3개월에 임상·실시간 PCR 평가.

두 군 모두 기저치 대비 개선되었고 군간 차이는 없었다: 평균 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 3.55에서 3.37, 3.42 mm (대조) vs 3.35에서 3.27, 3.36 mm (시험), 군간 p = 0.476 (1개월)·0.685 (3개월); 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 3개월 4.74 vs 4.65 mm (p = 0.810); 탐침출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BoP) 3개월 24.98% vs 23.71% (p = 0.794); 포르피로모나스 진지발리스 (Porphyromonas gingivalis)·푸조박테리움 뉴클레아툼 (Fusobacterium nucleatum) 비율은 유의한 변화가 없었고, 1개월에 시험군은 감소·대조군은 증가하는 비유의적 경향만 보였다.

유지관리 환자에서 개선은 기계적 디브리드먼트 자체에서 왔고 미노사이클린 연고 1회 적용의 추가 효과는 3개월 시점에 확인되지 않았다; 군당 10-12명, 표본수 산출 근거 없음, 배정은닉·눈가림 미기술, 추적 3개월이므로 "차이를 못 찾음"이지 동등성 입증이 아니다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Periodontal & Implant Science 2021 Feb;51(1):53-62
- **DOI**: 10.5051/jpis.2002720136 (PMID 33634615, PMC7920842)
- **Institution**: Department of Periodontology and Department of Microbiology and Immunology, Research Institute of Oral Sciences, Gangneung-Wonju National University College of Dentistry, Korea
- **Ethics**: IRB 2017-020, Helsinki-compliant, written informed consent

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tests a single application of 2% minocycline HCl microcapsule gel (Periocline, Sunstar) as an adjunct to whole-mouth subgingival debridement in patients who had already received SPT for more than 1 year, a population largely absent from the local-antibiotic literature.
- Adds relative-quantification real-time PCR (P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum as a ratio to total 16S rRNA gene copies) to the clinical readout.
- Reports no significant clinical or microbiological added effect of the ointment at 1 or 3 months, and a clinically relevant improvement from debridement alone in maintenance-phase patients.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: pilot, randomized, parallel-group, two-arm study (random sequence generation, allocation concealment and blinding are not described in the text); single examiner for all clinical parameters; April-October 2018.
- **Population**: 24 enrolled (16 men, 8 women; age 20-65); at least 15 natural teeth; SPT for more than 1 year for chronic periodontitis; at least 4 sites with PD above 5 mm; BoP above 25%. Excluded: systemic disease (e.g., uncontrolled diabetes), caries, antibiotics within 3 months, pregnancy or breast-feeding, heavy smokers, minocycline allergy.
- **Arms**: test (n=12) = SPT + 2% minocycline HCl microcapsule gel (Periocline, 0.5 g pack = 20 mg minocycline HCl per subject) placed into pockets/sulci of the entire dentition after subgingival debridement; control (n=12) = SPT + saline irrigation. Single application at baseline; no brushing, flossing, rinsing or eating for at least 2 hours afterwards; same toothpaste and toothbrush supplied to all.
- **Outcomes**: PPD and CAL (6 sites per tooth, manual Marquis probe), BoP (2 sites per tooth), plaque index (Loe-Silness), proportion of sites PD 5 mm or more, and BoP-positive sites among them; subgingival paper-strip samples pooled from 4 teeth with pockets of 5 mm or more, TaqMan real-time PCR for P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum expressed relative to total bacteria (delta-Ct, universal 16S primers). Time points: baseline, 1 and 3 months.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 21; Friedman test with Wilcoxon post hoc and Bonferroni correction (P < 0.017) for change over time; Mann-Whitney test for pairwise comparisons; data as mean ± SD; limited analysis because pilot.

Two control patients dropped out (analyzed: 12 test, 10 control). Baseline groups did not differ. Values are control vs test, mean ± SD.

- Within each group, PPD fell significantly at 1 month (P < 0.05) and rose slightly by 3 months; per the Table 3 superscripts, the 3-month PPD carries the same letter as baseline (i.e., not distinguishable from baseline), whereas CAL, BoP, sites PD 5 mm or more, and PD 5 mm or more with BoP stayed significantly improved at 3 months (BoP P < 0.001). No significant 1-month versus 3-month change in any parameter.
- Microbiology: baseline P. gingivalis 0.01%±0.02% (test) vs 0.01%±0.01% (control); F. nucleatum 0.15%±0.15% vs 0.19%±0.17%. No significant change over time or between groups for either species; at 1 month the ratios tended to decrease in the test arm and increase in the control arm (P > 0.05). Numeric 1- and 3-month ratios are shown only in figures, not in the text.
- Authors' conclusion: improvement came from mechanical debridement; the added effect of local minocycline was not significant.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Very small pilot (10-12 analyzed per arm), no sample-size calculation, 3-month follow-up; absence of significance cannot be read as equivalence.
- Randomization method, allocation concealment, and blinding of examiner/patients are not described; single examiner (calibration by one investigator) and a saline-irrigation control that is not a true placebo vehicle.
- Single application only; patients were stable, long-term SPT attendees, so ceiling/floor effects (baseline mean PPD 3.35-3.55 mm, only about 9-12% of sites at 5 mm or more) could mask a deep-site effect, a point the authors raise themselves (effect of debridement "too obvious"). Whole-mouth averages dilute site-specific effects; no deep-site-only (PD 5 mm or more) change analysis between arms was reported beyond site proportions.
- Statistics as described: Mann-Whitney used for within-group time-point comparisons; no multiplicity handling across the multiple clinical outcomes besides the Bonferroni post hoc; microbiology pooled 4 sites per patient and reported only as relative ratios with very low baseline P. gingivalis (about 0.01%).
- Plaque index was measured but its results are not reported in the text, and the baseline demographic table lacks column headers in the retrieved text, so PI values are not extracted here.
- The authors interpret the non-significant microbial tendency as a possible antimicrobial effect; this is speculative.

## 6. Related Work

- Zainuddin 2024 (held): pilot RCT, local minocycline gel vs tetracycline fiber vs SRP alone, no between-group difference at 3 months; same direction as this study.
- Soysa 2025 (held): SR+MA of tetracycline-class local adjuncts, modest PPD/CAL gains; this study is a maintenance-phase null result against that pooled effect.
- Arnett 2023 (held): SRP + minocycline microspheres, whole-mouth null but a small deep-site-only gain.
- Estrin 2026 (held): PRF with minocycline vs Arestin, no between-arm difference.
- Cited by the authors: Timmerman et al. (repeated minocycline gel in maintenance, no clinical benefit), Bogren et al. (local doxycycline in SPT, short-term benefit not kept at 1 year), Nakagawa et al. (minocycline ointment in recurrent periodontitis), Matesanz-Perez et al. (PPD 0.40 mm, CAL 0.31 mm).

## 7. Glossary

- **SPT (supportive periodontal therapy)**: periodic recall care after active periodontal treatment, including re-examination, supra/subgingival debridement, and oral hygiene reinforcement.
- **Periocline**: 2% minocycline HCl microcapsule ointment for subgingival application (Sunstar).
- **BoP**: bleeding on probing.
- **delta-Ct relative quantification**: expressing a target species' gene copies relative to total 16S rRNA gene copies in the sample.
