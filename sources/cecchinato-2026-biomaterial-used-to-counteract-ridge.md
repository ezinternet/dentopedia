## Why Ingested
Multicenter RCT (n=42, 4 Italian clinics) directly testing DBBM-C efficacy for ridge preservation after extraction of **two adjacent teeth** — a clinically common but under-studied scenario distinct from single-tooth ARP studies. Reinforces and extends the dimensional evidence base summarized in [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/jung-2013-radiographic-evaluation-different-techniques-ridge]] and the CNMA framework from [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/zhang-2026-component-network-meta-analysis-scaffold]].

## One-line Summary
Multicenter RCT (n=42, 6 months): DBBM-C + resorbable membrane reduced horizontal ridge width loss from 57.7% to 23.0% and buccal vertical loss from 3.0mm to 1.5mm after extraction of two adjacent teeth.

## 한줄요약
다중기관 RCT (n=42, 6개월): 인접 2개 발치 후 DBBM-C + 흡수성 차폐막으로 수평 치조제 폭 감소를 57.7%→23.0%, 협측 수직 감소를 3.0mm→1.5mm로 줄임.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First multicenter RCT specifically for DBBM-C ARP following **adjacent tooth extractions** (not single sockets)
- STL e-models from stone casts as dimensional measurement method (not CBCT — reduces radiation)
- Quantifies both horizontal (57.7% → 23.0%) and vertical (buccal −3.0→−1.5mm; lingual −2.2→−1.3mm) preservation with p<0.001 for all primary endpoints
- Private specialist multicenter design increases external validity for specialist practices
- 6-month timepoint captures most dimensional change; aligns with typical implant placement timing

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Prospective multicenter RCT
- Sites: 4 Italian private specialist clinics
- Patients: 42 patients (21 Test, 21 Control), each requiring extraction of 2 adjacent teeth
- Test intervention: DBBM-C (Bio-Oss Collagen 250mg blocks, Geistlich) + resorbable collagen membrane
- Control: Natural healing without biomaterial
- Measurement: STL digital models from stone casts (not CBCT)
- Timepoints: Baseline (pre-extraction), 6 months post-extraction
- Outcome measures:
  - Primary: Horizontal ridge width % change at 3mm below crest (B-L)
  - Secondary: Vertical buccal and lingual ridge changes (mm)
- Exclusion criteria: Severe socket defects, systemic contraindications
- Statistical: t-test or Wilcoxon for within-group; Mann–Whitney U for between-group

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Measure | Control | Test | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal width reduction (3mm) | 57.7% | 23.0% | <0.001 |
| Buccal vertical loss | −3.0 mm | −1.5 mm | <0.001 |
| Lingual vertical loss | −2.2 mm | −1.3 mm | <0.001 |

Both groups showed dimensional change; Test group preserved significantly more volume at all measurement points.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 6-month follow-up only — no long-term implant survival data
- No histological biopsy — no information on graft resorption or new bone formation
- Stone cast STL models: cannot measure alveolar crest height precisely; subject to impression/cast error vs CBCT
- Only specialist private clinic settings — may not translate to general practice
- No severe socket defects included — results apply to intact or mild defect sockets only
- Adjacent tooth scenario specific — may not apply to isolated single extractions or molar-only extractions
- Future: Long-term RCT with implant placement outcomes, histological analysis, CBCT measurement

## 6. Related Work
- Jung 2013 (J Periodontol): DBBM-C vs natural healing ARP RCT, n=40 — established horizontal dimensional preservation; Cecchinato 2026 confirms in multicenter design for adjacent extractions
- Tomasi et al. (multiple publications): Similar multicenter ARP design with e-model methodology using same STL technique
- Zhang 2026 (CNMA, 31 RCTs): DBBM + barrier membrane combination still lacks statistical significance in network model — Cecchinato's direct RCT shows clear within-trial benefit
- Araujo & Lindhe (2005, J Clin Periodontol): Bundle bone resorption basic science underpinning dimensional changes post-extraction

## 7. Glossary
- **DBBM-C**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral with 10% Collagen (이종골 탈단백화 소 뼈 광물 + 콜라겐 10%) — Bio-Oss Collagen (Geistlich)
- **STL e-model**: Stereolithography digital model from stone cast scan; used for 3D ridge dimensional analysis
- **B-L**: Bucco-Lingual dimension
- **Horizontal reduction %**: Percentage change in B-L width at 3mm below crest between baseline and 6 months
