## One-line Summary
Narrative review (Int J Mol Sci 2026): PDRN acts as A2A receptor agonist + nucleotide salvage substrate; reduces cartilage degradation in OA, supports tendon repair, shows potential in radiculopathy — mechanism framework applicable to dental regeneration contexts.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Consolidated mechanistic framework for PDRN: A2A receptor agonism + nucleotide salvage pathway
- Osteoarthritis: PDRN reduces cartilage degradation + promotes chondrogenic stem cell differentiation
- Tendinopathies (Achilles, rotator cuff): tissue repair support, documented in preclinical + clinical evidence
- Spinal radiculopathy: preliminary non-steroidal alternative when corticosteroids contraindicated
- Safety: favorable preliminary profiles across studies; no serious adverse events reported
- Limitation: dosing protocols not standardized — large-scale trials needed

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: narrative review
- Scope: musculoskeletal disorders — osteoarthritis, tendinopathies, spinal radiculopathy
- Focus: mechanism of action + preclinical and clinical evidence synthesis
- Not a systematic review; no formal inclusion criteria

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- PDRN mechanism:
  1. A2A receptor (A2AR) agonist → anti-inflammatory (NF-κB suppression, TNF-α ↓, IL-1β ↓)
  2. Nucleotide salvage pathway substrate → DNA synthesis support + cell proliferation
  3. VEGF stimulation → angiogenesis → tissue revascularization
  4. Collagen synthesis promotion → extracellular matrix repair
- Osteoarthritis: cartilage degradation ↓, chondrogenic differentiation ↑
- Tendinopathy: tissue repair promotion confirmed in Achilles + rotator cuff models
- Radiculopathy: preliminary pain reduction; no direct corticosteroid comparison RCT yet

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative review — not systematic; selection bias possible
- Dosing: no standardized protocol across musculoskeletal indications
- Robust comparative RCTs against established treatments (corticosteroids, PRP) lacking
- Long-term safety data limited

## 6. Related Work
- Dental PDRN mechanism parallel: same A2A/VEGF/collagen pathway applies to socket healing, peri-implant regeneration, soft tissue augmentation
- Ku 2025: PDRN in dentistry narrative review (dental-specific synthesis)
- Jeon 2026: PDRN effect on osteoblast differentiation and osteoclastogenesis (dental bone biology)

## 7. Glossary
- A2AR: Adenosine A2A Receptor — GPCR; activation suppresses inflammation via cAMP elevation
- Nucleotide salvage pathway: metabolic route recycling purines/pyrimidines for DNA synthesis
- VEGF: Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor — key angiogenesis mediator
- PDRN: Polydeoxyribonucleotide — salmon-derived DNA fragments, MW 50–1500 kDa
- Chondrogenesis: differentiation of stem cells into chondrocytes (cartilage cells)
