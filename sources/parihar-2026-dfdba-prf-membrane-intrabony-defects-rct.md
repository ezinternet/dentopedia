## Why Ingested

DFDBA(demineralized freeze-dried bone allograft) 단독 대비 PRF(platelet-rich fibrin) 병용의 골내결손(intrabony defect) 개별 RCT — 기존 [[wiki/periodontics/miron-2024-autogenous-platelet-concentrates-intrabony-defects]] (55 RCT SR+MA, "골이식재+PRF가 골이식재 단독보다 추가 유의 개선")의 결론을 그래프트 종류를 DFDBA로 한정한 split-mouth RCT 수준에서 재확인하는 개별 근거. n=15 defect pair의 작은 규모이지만 6개월 PPD(p=0.01)·CAL(p=0.04) 유의 개선을 보고해 SR+MA의 "graft+PRF > graft alone" 하위 결론을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized, double-blinded, split-mouth RCT (n=15 patients / 15 paired intrabony defect sites per group, Stage III/IV Grade B/C periodontitis) comparing DFDBA alone (control) vs DFDBA + PRF membrane (test), followed 6 months.

At 6 months, DFDBA+PRF showed significantly greater PPD reduction (3.47±0.61 mm vs 3.91±0.75 mm, p=0.01, Cohen's d=0.64 moderate) and CAL gain (final CAL 3.24±0.73 mm vs 4.20±0.76 mm, p=0.04, Cohen's d=1.29 large); plaque and gingival index showed no significant intergroup difference at any timepoint.

Both protocols produced significant within-group improvement in all parameters (p<0.001); the PRF adjunct benefit is real but modest in magnitude, and the study is limited by small sample size, 6-month follow-up only, and no histologic confirmation of true regeneration vs repair.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: National Journal of Maxillofacial Surgery (Banaras Hindu University, India), published online 2026-06-03.
- **DOI**: 10.4103/njms.njms_55_25; **PMID**: 42422776; **PMCID**: PMC13342730.
- **Design**: Randomized, double-blinded, split-mouth (parallel-arm-within-patient) controlled clinical trial; CTRI No. CTRI/2024/03/063595; ethical clearance no. Dean/2023/EC/6084.
- **Setting**: Single institution outpatient periodontics unit, April 2024–January 2025.
- **Population**: 15 systemically healthy patients (8 M, 7 F; mean age 40±2.2 y) with Stage III or IV, Grade B or C periodontitis (2017 World Workshop classification), each with a pair of contralateral infrabony defects (PD ≥5 mm, radiographic vertical defect depth ≥4 mm, post-Phase I plaque/GI ≤1). Three patients excluded pre-randomization; no attrition during follow-up.
- **Groups**: Group A — DFDBA alone (control, 15 sites); Group B — DFDBA + PRF membrane (test, 15 sites). Randomization by sealed-envelope lottery; two blinded examiners recorded outcomes; operating surgeon (single calibrated clinician) did not record outcomes.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Individual split-mouth RCT directly testing PRF membrane as an adjunct to DFDBA specifically (rather than to OFD alone or to bone graft generically), in Stage III/IV periodontitis defects.
- Reports both statistical significance and effect size (Cohen's d) for PPD (moderate, d=0.64) and CAL (large, d=1.29) — useful granularity beyond the pooled MDs in the Miron 2024 SR+MA.
- Confirms PRF's benefit is confined to hard/soft-tissue regenerative parameters (PPD, CAL) and does not extend to inflammatory/hygiene indices (PI, GI) when both arms receive rigorous plaque control.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRF prepared per Choukroun low-speed centrifugation concept: 10 mL venous blood, no anticoagulant, centrifuged at 3000 rpm x 10 min (Velab VE-4000, fixed-angle rotor 45°, radius 10.6 cm); PRF gel compressed in a PRF box to obtain a membrane.
- Surgical protocol: full-thickness mucoperiosteal flap, curette debridement under saline irrigation, DFDBA placed in defect (both groups), PRF membrane placed over graft (test group only), primary closure with 3-0 resorbable vicryl, interrupted sutures.
- Outcome timepoints: baseline, 3 months, 6 months. Primary outcomes: CAL gain, PPD reduction. Secondary: PI, GI.
- Statistics: Mann-Whitney U (between-group), Friedman test (within-group), Bonferroni correction; sample size (15/group) from G*Power (α=5%, power=95%) based on Agarwal 2016 data.
- Note: the Interventions paragraph of the published text momentarily mislabels which group is "test" vs "control" (an apparent editorial inconsistency), but the Methods (group definitions), Table 1 numeric data, and Abstract are internally consistent and agree that Group B = DFDBA+PRF achieved the superior 6-month PPD/CAL outcomes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Group A (DFDBA alone) 6mo | Group B (DFDBA+PRF) 6mo | Intergroup p | Effect size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PPD (mm) | 3.91±0.75 | 3.47±0.61 | 0.01 | Cohen's d=0.64 (moderate; 95% CI −0.09 to 1.38) |
| CAL (mm, final) | 4.20±0.76 | 3.24±0.73 | 0.04 | Cohen's d=1.29 (large; 95% CI 0.50 to 2.07) |
| GI | 1.38±0.60 | 1.47±0.60 | 0.322 (NS) | — |
| PI | 1.61±0.55 | 1.58±0.49 | 0.887 (NS) | — |

- Both groups improved significantly from baseline across all four parameters (intragroup p<0.001 each).
- Baseline PPD/CAL were comparable between groups (p>0.05), supporting valid randomization.
- Intergroup PPD difference not yet significant at 3 months (p=0.068) — the PRF advantage emerges/consolidates specifically by 6 months.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (15 paired defects/group); limits external validity.
- Short 6-month follow-up only; authors explicitly call for ≥12-month follow-up to confirm durability.
- No histologic evaluation or advanced imaging — clinical probing/CAL measurements cannot distinguish true periodontal regeneration (new cementum/PDL/bone) from long-junctional-epithelium repair.
- Single-center, single-surgeon design; smokers and medically compromised patients excluded (generalizability limited to healthy, non-smoking, well-plaque-controlled patients).
- Three pre-randomization exclusions noted but reasons not detailed in the retrieved text.

## 7. Glossary

- **DFDBA** — demineralized freeze-dried bone allograft; osteoinductive bone graft material exposing embedded BMPs (BMP-2, -4, -7) via demineralization.
- **PRF** — platelet-rich fibrin; second-generation autologous platelet concentrate (Choukroun 2001), a 3D fibrin scaffold with platelets/leukocytes for sustained growth-factor release.
- **PPD** — probing pocket depth (gingival margin to base of pocket).
- **CAL** — clinical attachment level (CEJ to base of pocket).
- **PI / GI** — plaque index / gingival index.
- **OFD** — open flap debridement.
- **Low-speed centrifugation concept** — PRF preparation protocol (lower g-force/longer time) associated with improved cellular content and homogeneity vs original Choukroun protocol.
