## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo push-out bond strength (POBS) and ATR-FTIR study using 60 extracted single-rooted human mandibular premolars, comparing two all-in-one root canal irrigants — Dual Rinse HEDP (etidronate + NaOCl) and Triton (PBTC/citric-acid-based) — against a saline control, on dentin bonded with AH Plus (epoxy resin) or CeraSeal (bioceramic) sealer.

Dual Rinse HEDP produced the highest POBS for both AH Plus (8.98±0.2 MPa) and CeraSeal (11.36±0.5 MPa), versus Triton (7.20±0.4 / 5.85±0.5 MPa) and lowest with saline (3.56±0.3 / 2.39±0.3 MPa) (all P<0.001); FTIR showed Dual Rinse HEDP preserved higher posttreatment amide III:phosphate and carbonate:phosphate ratios than Triton, indicating less organic/inorganic dentin degradation.

Dual Rinse HEDP is the preferable root-conditioning irrigant before either epoxy-resin or bioceramic sealer placement, improving dislodgement resistance versus Triton or saline; findings are ex vivo and need clinical/longitudinal validation.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First head-to-head comparison of **Dual Rinse HEDP** vs **Triton** (both marketed all-in-one, single-irrigant "continuous chelation" systems) for push-out bond strength against **both** an epoxy-resin sealer (AH Plus) and a hydraulic calcium-silicate/bioceramic sealer (CeraSeal).
- Provides ATR-FTIR mechanistic data (amide III:phosphate and carbonate:phosphate ratios) correlating irrigant chemistry with organic/inorganic dentin preservation and downstream bond strength.
- Reports fractographic failure-mode analysis (adhesive/cohesive/mixed) linking irrigant-conditioned dentin surface quality to bonding mechanism per sealer type.
- Establishes that Triton, despite documented smear-layer-removal/antibacterial efficacy, underperforms Dual Rinse HEDP for sealer dislodgement resistance — a bonding property not previously tested for Triton.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Sample**: 60 noncarious extracted human mandibular single-rooted premolars (straight roots, curvature <30°), single canal/mature apex confirmed radiographically and microscopically; apices sealed, roots embedded in polyvinylsiloxane (closed-end system simulation).
- **Instrumentation**: ProTaper Universal to F3; canal taper removed with Peeso reamers #1–2.
- **Groups (n=20 each)**: (1) Dual Rinse HEDP (0.9 g etidronate capsule + 10 mL 3% NaOCl); (2) Triton (two-component automix: PBTC <15%, citric acid <2%, NaOCl <10%, surfactants/polyacrylate/etc.); (3) saline control. Standardized volumes/timing (2 mL between instrument changes ×1 min, 5 mL final flush ×1 min), 30-gauge side-vented needle, 1 mm short of working length; 5 mL distilled-water rinse after.
- **POBS testing**: each irrigant group subdivided n=10 by sealer (AH Plus vs CeraSeal); teeth sectioned into 2.0±0.1 mm middle-third dentin slices; sealers set 48 h at 100% humidity; push-out via universal testing machine (Instron), 1 mm/min crosshead speed, 0.7 mm stainless-steel plungers; POBS (MPa) = Force / (2πrh).
- **Fractography**: stereomicroscopy at 40× classifying adhesive/cohesive/mixed failure.
- **ATR-FTIR**: separate 15 dentin blocks from 8 extracted maxillary incisors, n=5/irrigant group; pre/post-treatment spectra (irrigant exposure 10 mL, 30 min, ultrasonic agitation) via diamond ATR; amide III:phosphate and carbonate:phosphate band-area ratios computed; room temperature standardized at 23°C.
- **Stats**: SPSS v25, Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality, one-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD, α=0.05. Sample size via G*Power (95% CI, 80% power).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **POBS — AH Plus**: Dual Rinse HEDP 8.98±0.2 MPa > Triton 7.20±0.4 MPa > saline 3.56±0.3 MPa (P<0.001).
- **POBS — CeraSeal**: Dual Rinse HEDP 11.36±0.5 MPa > Triton 5.85±0.5 MPa > saline 2.39±0.3 MPa (P<0.001).
- **Fractography — AH Plus**: Dual Rinse HEDP 70% cohesive/30% mixed (best bonding signature); Triton 50% cohesive/30% adhesive/20% mixed; saline 60% adhesive/20% cohesive/20% mixed (worst bonding signature).
- **Fractography — CeraSeal**: Dual Rinse HEDP 60% cohesive/40% mixed; Triton 60% mixed/40% cohesive; saline 60% mixed/40% adhesive.
- **FTIR amide III:phosphate**: pretreatment higher with Dual Rinse HEDP (16.4±4.0) vs Triton (11.6±1.2, P=0.039) and saline (11.4±2.0, P=0.031); posttreatment saline (28.4±4.0) markedly higher (i.e., less protected) than Dual Rinse HEDP (12.7±3.4, P<0.001) and Triton (8.6±0.6, P<0.001); Dual Rinse HEDP vs Triton posttreatment NS (P=0.131).
- **FTIR carbonate:phosphate**: pretreatment lower in Triton (25.6±2.7) vs Dual Rinse HEDP (45.8±6.7, P<0.001) and saline (37.1±5.9, P=0.015); posttreatment higher in Dual Rinse HEDP (39.6±3.4) vs Triton (28.1±3.5, P<0.001) and saline (32.7±2.8, P=0.016).
- **Interpretation**: Dual Rinse HEDP's milder chelation preserves more organic (collagen/amide) and inorganic (mineral) dentin content than Triton's stronger citric-acid/higher-NaOCl formulation, correlating with its higher POBS for both sealer chemistries.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Ex vivo methodology: POBS may not translate directly to clinical performance given differences in irrigation technique, dentin variability, canal anatomy, obturation technique, and in vivo moisture/loading conditions.
- FTIR used a 30-minute irrigant exposure (longer than the clinically mimetic POBS protocol) to reliably resolve spectral shifts — a study-design compromise, not a clinical protocol recommendation.
- Contrasts with at least one prior study reporting Dual Rinse HEDP had lower POBS with a different CaSi sealer (TotalFill BC Sealer), suggesting sealer-brand-specific effects; authors call for further studies across sealer types/irrigation protocols and longer-term clinical follow-up.

## 6. Related Work

- Contrasted with a prior study finding Dual Rinse HEDP had *lower* POBS with TotalFill BC Sealer (a different CaSi sealer) — authors attribute the discrepancy to CaSi-sealer type, irrigation technique/volume, and obturation technique differences.
- Builds on prior literature establishing Dual Rinse HEDP's antibacterial/tissue-dissolving efficacy comparable to NaOCl and its bonding-enhancement effect for sealers/cements to radicular dentin.
- Builds on prior literature establishing Triton's efficacy for smear-layer/debris removal and antibacterial action, while newly testing its previously uninvestigated effect on sealer bond strength.

## 7. Glossary

- **Dual Rinse HEDP (DR HEDP)** — commercial all-in-one irrigant: 0.9 g etidronate (HEDP) capsule dissolved in 10 mL NaOCl (0.5–5.25%), enabling "continuous chelation" — a single solution for the entire chemomechanical preparation.
- **Triton** — two-component (A+B) automix all-in-one irrigant containing PBTC, citric acid, NaOCl, surfactants, and polyacrylate.
- **Push-Out Bond Strength (POBS, 밀어내기 결합강도)** — force per unit adhesion area required to dislodge a sealer/filling material from a dentin slice, measured via universal testing machine.
- **AH Plus** — epoxy resin-based root canal sealer bonding via covalent linkage to exposed dentin collagen amino groups.
- **CeraSeal** — premixed hydraulic calcium silicate (bioceramic) sealer forming a mineral infiltration zone / cement tags with dentin.
- **ATR-FTIR** — attenuated total reflectance Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy, used here to quantify amide III:phosphate (organic/collagen) and carbonate:phosphate (inorganic/mineral) band-area ratios before/after irrigant exposure.
- **Continuous Chelation (지속적 킬레이션)** — single-irrigant strategy combining disinfection and smear-layer chelation throughout instrumentation, avoiding sequential NaOCl-then-EDTA steps.
