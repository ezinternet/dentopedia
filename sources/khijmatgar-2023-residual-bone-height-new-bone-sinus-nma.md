## Why Ingested

기존 [[sinus-lift/lateral/trimmel-2021-relative-performance-various-biomaterials]]는 34개 무작위 대조 시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)을 단일 네트워크로 순위화했지만 잔존골높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH)를 층화 변수로 쓰지 않았고, [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]]는 생존율만 봤다. 본 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA; 67 RCTs, 1955명, 2405개 상악동)은 RBH 4 mm를 기준으로 네트워크를 분리해 "이식재 순위가 RBH에 따라 달라진다"는 조건부 결론을 제시하므로, 이식재 선택을 RBH로 조건화하는 근거 축을 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022331993; PubMed/Scopus/Cochrane/WoS to 31 Dec 2022) of 67 RCTs — 1955 patients, 2405 lateral maxillary sinus augmentations — comparing autogenous bone (Auto), xenograft (XG), allograft (AG), alloplast (AP), bioactive agents (Bio), hyaluronic acid (HA) and their combinations for histomorphometric new bone formation, split by residual bone height (RBH) <4 mm vs ≥4 mm.

For RBH <4 mm the top SUCRA ranks were XG+AG (treatment effect 8.97, 95% CI −3.60 to 21.56), XG+AP (1.93) and Auto/SH-Auto with alloplasts last; for RBH ≥4 mm they were Auto (9.86, 95% CI 12.71 to −15.06 as printed), Bio+XG (2.01) and XG+Auto (1.86) with allograft last — the only statistically significant inconsistency loop was XG/AP/Bio+AP.

No single biomaterial wins at both RBH strata, and GRADE certainty was low for every material because all confidence intervals were wide, so the RBH-dependent ranking is a hypothesis-generating signal rather than a protocol-grade recommendation.

## 세줄요약

네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA; PROSPERO CRD42022331993; PubMed·Scopus·Cochrane·Web of Science, 2022-12-31까지): 무작위 대조 시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 67편, 환자 1955명, 측방 상악동거상술 2405건에서 자가골 (Autogenous bone, Auto)·이종골 (Xenograft, XG)·동종골 (Allograft, AG)·합성골 (Alloplast, AP)·생물활성제 (Bioactive agents, Bio)·히알루론산 (Hyaluronic Acid, HA) 및 조합을 조직형태계측 신생골 (New Bone Formation, NBF) 비율로 비교하고 잔존골높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 4 mm 기준으로 층화.

RBH <4 mm에서는 누적순위곡선하면적 (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking, SUCRA) 1–3위가 XG+AG (치료효과 8.97, 95% CI −3.60–21.56)·XG+AP (1.93)·Auto/SH-Auto이고 합성골 단독이 최하위; RBH ≥4 mm에서는 Auto (9.86, 95% CI 12.71 to −15.06, 인쇄 그대로)·Bio+XG (2.01)·XG+Auto (1.86)이며 동종골 단독이 최하위; 유의한 비일관성 (Inconsistency Factor, IF) 루프는 XG·AP·Bio+AP 하나뿐.

두 RBH 층 모두에서 우위인 이식재는 없고 모든 재료의 근거수준 (GRADE)이 낮음(신뢰구간이 모두 넓음)이라, RBH 의존적 순위는 프로토콜 수준 권고가 아니라 가설 생성 신호로 읽어야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First NMA to **condition the graft-material ranking on RBH** — two separate networks (<4 mm and ≥4 mm) rather than one pooled ranking, testing the hypothesis that the optimal material depends on residual bone height.
- Largest included evidence base of the sinus-graft NMAs ingested so far: **67 RCTs, 1955 patients, 2405 sinus augmentation procedures** (vs 34 RCTs / 842 augmentations in Trimmel 2021).
- Provides **SUCRA + predictive interval (PrI) + inconsistency factor (IF)** triple reading for each RBH stratum, explicitly separating "ranked best now" (SUCRA) from "likely to replicate" (PrI).
- Reports the RBH-dependent winners: **XG+AG** and **XG+AP** for <4 mm; **Auto**, **Bio+XG**, **XG+Auto** for ≥4 mm — with the last-ranked material flipping too (AP for <4 mm, AG for ≥4 mm).
- Documents a single statistically significant inconsistency loop (**XG–AP–Bio+AP**) and states that all loops within <4 mm RBH are consistent (lower CI limit at zero).
- Applies GRADE and reports the honest ceiling: **low certainty for all biomaterials**, driven by uniformly wide 95% CIs.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Design and registration**
- SR + NMA; PROSPERO CRD42022331993; PRISMA reporting.

**Search and selection**
- PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Central, Web of Science; no language or year limits; hand search of major OMFS/implant journals plus reference lists of included RCTs and relevant SRs.
- Boolean: `(((maxillary sinus) OR (sinus lift)) ... OR (sinus lift procedure))) AND (((histomorphometric) OR (histomorphometric analysis)) OR (bone histomorphometry))`.
- Two independent reviewers; Cohen's kappa 0.92 (title/abstract) and 0.93 (full text); third reviewer for disagreements.

**PICO**
- **P**: patients requiring sinus lift, any RBH, no age/sex limits.
- **I**: sinus lift with ≥1 biomaterial.
- **C**: self-healing / no material, or any material other than the intervention material.
- **O (primary)**: NBF% by histomorphometry. **O (secondary)**: adverse events/complications.
- Healing period at biopsy ≥2 months.

**Exclusion criteria** (verbatim intent): narrative reviews/letters/opinions/book chapters/case reports/conference abstracts; duplicates; in vitro and in vivo animal studies; studies using the same biomaterial in both arms.

**Data handling**
- Extracted by one reviewer, validated by a second.
- **RBH stratification**: data split at the **4 mm mean-RBH threshold**, with separate NMAs run for <4 mm and ≥4 mm — the core structural decision of the paper.
- Studies lacking mean/SD were resolved by contacting authors; if unresolved, the study was excluded.

**Statistics**
- NMA via `metan` commands in **STATA v17.0** (frequentist).
- Network geometry plots (nodes = treatments, edge thickness = number of direct comparisons), contribution plots.
- Consistency via closed-loop inconsistency factor (IF); significant when the lower CI limit does not reach zero.
- Ranking via **SUCRA**; **predictive intervals (PrI)** for replication likelihood; **MDS** for dissimilarity of rankings.
- Heterogeneity: Higgins I² (low <50%, high >50%); Moses–Shapiro–Littenberg regression and subgroup analyses if high.
- Publication bias: funnel plot and Deek's asymmetry test; two-sided tests, α=0.05.
- Certainty: **GRADE**.

**Risk of bias**
- Cochrane Handbook criteria: randomisation method, allocation concealment, blinding of outcome assessors, completeness of outcome reporting, reasons for withdrawal, other biases (sample-size calculation, inclusion/exclusion definitions, group comparability) — scored adequate/inadequate/unclear.
- Performance bias (blinding of surgeon/patient) deliberately **not** scored, because graft material cannot be masked in sinus surgery.
- Overall: low (all adequate) / moderate (≥1 unclear) / high (≥1 inadequate) risk of bias.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**PRISMA flow**

| Stage | n |
|---|---|
| Records identified (databases + other sources) | 707 |
| Duplicates removed | 109 |
| Screened by title/abstract | 598 |
| Excluded at title/abstract | 266 |
| Full texts assessed / selected for qualitative analysis | 84 |
| Excluded before quantitative analysis | 17 (multiple reasons per study) |
| **Included in NMA** | **67** |
| Patients | 1955 |
| Sinus augmentation procedures | 2405 |

Reasons for the 17 exclusions: same biomaterial in test and control (13 studies cited: [20,25,26,27,31,32,39,42,49,53,55,73,90] — e.g. phototherapy on/off, antrostomy vs crestal biopsies in the same patient, monophasic vs biphasic alloplast both mapped to "AP", autograft biopsies at different healing times); RBH not reported so uncategorisable (4: [52,53,54,57]); not randomised (3: [20,52,54]); vertical course of regeneration without per-material results (1: [21]).

**Network nodes** (materials pooled into classes, combinations allowed)

| Code | Class | Constituents as defined by the authors |
|---|---|---|
| Auto | autogenous bone | autograft |
| XG | xenograft | bovine, equine, porcine bone ± collagen |
| AG | allograft | — |
| AP | alloplast | bioactive glass, hydroxyapatite, β-TCP, PLGA |
| Bio | bioactive agents | rhBMP-2, rhGDF-5, mesenchymal stem cells, enamel matrix derivative, autologous platelet concentrates |
| HA | hyaluronic acid | — |
| XG+AG, XG+AP, XG+Auto, Bio+XG, Bio+AP, Auto+AP, … | combinations | two or more of the above |

**Healing time at biopsy**: mean **6.6 ± 2.6 months** (range 2–15 months); **6 months** was the most frequent (used in 52 studies).

**Network geometry (density)**
- **<4 mm RBH**: highest sample counts for XG, AP, Auto, Bio+XG; most frequent comparisons XG–AP, XG–XG+AP, AG–Auto.
- **≥4 mm RBH**: most frequent comparisons Bio+AP and XG–Auto+AP.

**Inconsistency (IF)**
- All loops **within** the <4 mm network: lower CI limit at zero → **not significant**.
- The loop **XG–AP–Bio+AP** (entire-loop analysis): **statistically significant inconsistency**.
- No loop formed for XG+AG or XG+Auto → no significant IF, i.e. no statistical difference in effect sizes between studies involving those combinations.

**Ranking and effect estimates (as reported)**

| RBH stratum | Rank | Material | Treatment effect (95% CI, as printed) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <4 mm | 1 | XG+AG | 8.97 (−3.60, 21.56) | best SUCRA; best PrI |
| <4 mm | 2 | XG+AP | 1.93 (5.88, −9.59) | best PrI alongside XG+AG |
| <4 mm | 3 | Auto / SH-Auto | not reported numerically | SUCRA third |
| <4 mm | last | AP alone | — | last SUCRA |
| ≥4 mm | 1 | Auto | 9.86 (12.71, −15.06) | best SUCRA; best PrI |
| ≥4 mm | 2 | Bio+XG | 2.01 (7.74, −13.17) | best PrI alongside Auto |
| ≥4 mm | 3 | XG+Auto | 1.86 (6.88, −11.64) | — |
| ≥4 mm | last | AG alone | — | last SUCRA |

> The CI bounds above are reproduced **verbatim from the txt**, including the inconsistent ordering (lower bound printed above the upper bound for most rows). The paper's prose states XG+AG "performed 8.97 times better compared to other biomaterials", which is not a defensible reading of a difference in NBF percentage — treat the effect column as a network estimate with a wide, zero-crossing interval.

**Predictive intervals (PrI)** — materials most likely to perform best in *future* studies:
- <4 mm: **XG+AG**, **XG+AP**
- ≥4 mm: **Auto**, **Bio+XG**

**Certainty of evidence (GRADE)**
- **Low for all biomaterials**, because 95% CIs of direct, indirect and network estimates were all wide. The authors state moderate/high certainty would have required narrower CIs.

- The per-study characteristics table (Table 1) and the SUCRA/network figures (Figures 2–6) are referenced but **not reproduced in the retrievable txt** — the numbers above are the in-text values only.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

**Limitations stated by the authors**
- SUCRA rankings are sensitive to the number of outcomes, material cost, clinician familiarity/handling, the ranking algorithm, and apparent differences between treatments.
- Healing time before biopsy varied widely (**2–15 months**); the authors chose **not** to split by healing time to avoid further data fragmentation — so the ranking mixes early and late remodelling.
- Low certainty for all materials (wide CIs across direct, indirect and network evidence).
- Histomorphometric data on residual biomaterial % and connective tissue % were too rarely reported, so NMA could **only** be run for NBF — the ranking cannot be cross-checked against graft resorption or soft-tissue replacement.

**Additional limitations visible in the retrieved text**
- **Class pooling erases within-class heterogeneity**: bovine/equine/porcine xenografts ± collagen collapse into "XG"; bioactive glass, HA, β-TCP and PLGA collapse into "AP" — one of the excluded studies was rejected precisely because monophasic vs biphasic alloplasts map to the same node, which is the same problem at a coarser grain.
- **Cross-class inconsistency**: the one significant loop (XG–AP–Bio+AP) sits exactly where the ranking claims matter, so the network is not fully coherent at the top of the ranking.
- **RBH dichotomy at 4 mm** is a mean-value split, not a per-sinus measurement; studies with heterogeneous RBH are forced into one stratum, and the threshold itself is a design choice (the Introduction separately uses 6 mm and 10 mm as clinical decision points for transcrestal vs lateral approach).
- **Database inconsistency**: abstract lists three databases, Methods four.
- **Secondary outcome under-reported**: adverse events were collected but no pooled complication analysis is presented in the results text.
- **No implant-level endpoints** (survival, ISQ, MBL) in the analysis; NBF% is a surrogate.

**Open questions / future work implied**
- Whether an RBH-conditioned material choice changes implant survival — the outcome that actually drives the clinical decision.
- Trials stratified (not retrospectively dichotomised) by RBH, with a standardised biopsy time and reporting of residual graft and connective tissue percentages so all three histomorphometric compartments can be meta-analysed.
- Whether the XG+AG (<4 mm) and Auto (≥4 mm) signals survive with narrower CIs and greater certainty.

## 6. Related Work

- **Trimmel et al. 2021** (Bayesian NMA, 34 RCTs, 842 augmentations, 28 biomaterial subgroups, SUCRA for NB%) — same question without RBH stratification; found bovine + BMC and bovine + PRP on top and autologous bone alone only 12th (57%). Khijmatgar's ≥4 mm network instead puts Auto first, so the two NMAs disagree on the position of autogenous bone.
- **Del Fabbro et al. 2025** (SR of 49 RCTs, 1722 augmentations) — implant survival 84.6–100% with no difference among DBBM, autogenous ± DBBM and synthetic HA/β-TCP; supplies the survival axis that this NMA does not measure.
- **Stacchi et al. 2018** (cited as [104] in the discussion) — reported a **negative correlation between sinus width and new bone formation** and found RBH did **not** influence NBF, a direct counter-observation to the premise of this NMA.
- **A prior meta-analysis cited as [12]** — NBF increases by approximately **2% per mm** of RBH; this paper's dichotomised design tests material × RBH interaction rather than a continuous RBH slope.
- **Chao et al. 2010 meta-regression** ([13]) — 12 lateral-window studies, 406 patients, 1644 implants; implant survival rises from ~1 to 5 mm initial bone height and plateaus >5 mm — the implant-level analogue of an RBH effect.
- **Alkandari et al. 2025** (10 RCTs, 328 patients) — head-to-head BCP vs DBBM: BCP +3.48% NBF and −8.41% residual graft; the pairwise detail that this NMA's "AP vs XG" node collapses.

## 7. Glossary

- **NMA (Network Meta-Analysis)**: simultaneous comparison of ≥3 treatments using direct and indirect evidence, allowing a ranking even where no head-to-head trial exists.
- **RBH (Residual Bone Height)**: vertical height of native alveolar bone below the sinus floor before augmentation; here dichotomised at 4 mm to create two separate networks.
- **NBF (New Bone Formation)**: percentage of newly formed bone in a biopsy specimen, measured histomorphometrically — the primary outcome.
- **SUCRA (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking curve)**: 0–100% summary of the probability that a treatment is the best; a ranking aid, not an effect size.
- **PrI (Predictive Interval)**: interval expected to contain the effect of a *future* study; wider than a CI and used here to judge replicability.
- **IF (Inconsistency Factor)**: disagreement between direct and indirect evidence within a closed loop; significant when the lower CI limit excludes zero.
- **GRADE**: framework grading certainty of a body of evidence (high/moderate/low/very low), driven by risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias.
- **XG / AG / AP / Bio / Auto / HA**: xenograft / allograft / alloplast / bioactive agents / autogenous bone / hyaluronic acid — the network's treatment classes.
