## Why Ingested

This SR synthesizes the sparse in vitro evidence on bonding clear aligner attachments specifically to aesthetic crown materials (lithium disilicate, monolithic zirconia, 3D-printed resin, bis-acryl composite). The topic is directly relevant to adult patients undergoing clear aligner therapy (CAT) who present with aesthetic crown restorations — an increasingly common clinical scenario as adult orthodontic caseloads grow. No existing wiki page covers crown-surface bonding in the CAT context; the closest pages ([[orthodontics/clear-aligner/jedlinski-2023-attachments-orthodontic-aligner-comprehensive-sr]] and [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/frias-2026-material-properties-of-composite]]) focus on attachment design/efficacy and composite material properties on natural-tooth enamel, leaving the crown-material bonding gap unaddressed.

## Three-line Summary

A PRISMA-compliant SR of 9 in vitro studies (2015–2025) investigated surface treatments and bonding agents for clear aligner attachments on aesthetic crown materials (lithium disilicate, monolithic zirconia, 3D-printed resin, bis-acryl composite). Hydrofluoric acid (HFA) etching with Filtek Z350 composite consistently produced the highest shear bond strength (전단결합강도, SBS) on lithium disilicate and zirconia; however, narrative synthesis without meta-analysis was required due to substantial heterogeneity among studies. Higher surface roughness (표면거칠기, Ra) did not linearly predict higher bond strength, underscoring the multifactorial nature of adhesion.

## 세줄요약

투명교정장치 부착체(clear aligner attachment)를 심미 보철물(리튬 디실리케이트·모놀리식 지르코니아·3D 프린팅 레진·비스아크릴 복합재)에 접합하는 방법을 평가한 PRISMA 체계 문헌고찰(SR) — 9편 실험실연구(2015–2025). 불산(hydrofluoric acid, HFA) 에칭 + Filtek Z350 복합레진 조합이 리튬 디실리케이트(lithium disilicate)와 모놀리식 지르코니아(monolithic zirconia) 모두에서 가장 높은 전단결합강도(shear bond strength, SBS)를 보였다. 표면거칠기(surface roughness, Ra)가 높다고 결합강도가 선형적으로 높아지지 않으며, 이질성으로 인해 메타분석은 불가능하여 서술적 합성에 그쳤다.

## Summary

This systematic review addresses a clinically significant gap: how to bond clear aligner attachments to the increasing number of aesthetic crown materials placed in adult orthodontic patients. The PICO question focused on surface roughening procedures and bonding agents as interventions, with shear bond strength (SBS) as the primary outcome and surface roughness as the secondary outcome.

Nine in vitro studies (n=9) covering four crown material types met inclusion criteria:
- **Lithium disilicate** (IPS e.max CAD/Press): 2 studies (Alsaud 2022, Fayyadh 2024)
- **Monolithic zirconia**: 3 studies (Alhunayni 2025, Nitasnoraset 2024, Arslan 2023) + 1 (Nguyen 2025 zirconia crowns)
- **Both LD and MZ compared**: 1 study (Çokakoğlu 2024)
- **3D-printed definitive resin**: 1 study (Criollo-Barrios 2025)
- **Bis-acryl composite**: 1 study (Shahin 2024)

Risk of bias (QUIN tool): 7 of 9 studies = low risk (66–83%); 2 = medium risk (Alhunayni, Nguyen ~66%).

Key clinical conclusion: hydrofluoric acid etching for 1 min + Filtek Z350 composite = optimal protocol for lithium disilicate and monolithic zirconia. Sandblasting (air abrasion with 50 µm AlO2) is a viable clinical alternative given that HFA's corrosive nature requires rubber dam isolation.

## Key Contributions

1. **First SR** exclusively targeting clear aligner attachment bonding to aesthetic crown materials (not natural enamel)
2. **HFA vs sandblasting** hierarchy quantified: on LD and MZ, HFA consistently outperforms or matches sandblasting for SBS; sandblasting produces higher Ra on LD but lower Ra on MZ vs HFA
3. **Surface roughness ≠ bond strength** (non-linear): excessive roughness may impede adhesive penetration, create voids, concentrate stress — Jennings' finding confirmed across SR
4. **Packable Filtek Z350 > flowable composites** on both LD and MZ consistently
5. **3D-printed crown with prefabricated attachment** identified as promising multidisciplinary alternative (pending further in vitro/clinical validation)
6. **Narrative synthesis only** — meta-analysis precluded by heterogeneity in HFA concentrations (4%, 9.5%, 9.6%), sandblasting particle size (50 vs 110 µm), thermocycling variability, bonding agents, and composites

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review, PRISMA 2020 guidelines
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD420251174624
- **Search period**: 2015–2025
- **Databases**: Scopus, PubMed, Web of Science (no grey literature)
- **Language restriction**: English only
- **Screening tool**: Rayyan; reference management: Zotero
- **Dual independent screening**: AK + CS; third reviewer (MR) for disagreements
- **Risk of bias**: QUIN tool for dental studies
- **Synthesis**: Narrative (heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis)
- **Included**: 9 of 260 articles after duplicate removal and screening
- **Excluded from meta-analysis**: All — in vitro designs with substantial protocol heterogeneity
- **PICO**: P = aesthetic crown materials; I = surface roughening + bonding agents; C = untreated/no treatment; O = SBS and Ra

## Results

### Shear Bond Strength — By Material

**Lithium Disilicate:**
- Alsaud 2022 (Saudi Arabia, n=120): Air abrasion + Assure Universal Bond + Filtek Z350 = best (21.80 ± 3.86 MPa); PhA + SBU + Z350 flowable = worst (0.96 ± 1.86 MPa); AUB > SBU overall
- Fayyadh 2024 (Egypt, n=80): HFA + Filtek Z350 > HFA + flowable (16.43 ± 2.09 MPa) > air abrasion (15.00 ± 2.55 MPa) > Er,Cr:YSGG > phosphoric acid (4.421 MPa)

**Monolithic Zirconia:**
- Alhunayni 2025 (Egypt, n=66): HFA highest; sandblasting (10.59 ± 2.76 MPa) > laser (8.33 ± 1.37 MPa) with Z350; SBU used throughout
- Nitasnoraset 2024 (Thailand, n=50): All treated with 9.6% HFA; silane + Superbond C&B = highest (33.25 ± 4.57 MPa); bonding agent alone = lowest (4.55 ± 0.99 MPa)
- Arslan 2023 (Turkey, n=102): Filtek Z250 + silane + diamond bur > flowable + AA 110µm (2.22 MPa); flowable + diamond bur = worst (1.58 MPa)
- Nguyen 2025 (Vietnam, n=108): 30s sandblasting + Universal/MDP primer = highest; silane at all durations < MDP/Universal; silane + 10s sandblasting = lowest

**Mixed (LD + MZ):**
- Çokakoğlu 2024 (Turkey, n=40): MZ + AA + Monobond Plus = highest (14.20 ± 6.26 MPa); LD + HFA + Monobond Plus = lowest (6.02 ± 2.19 MPa)

**3D-Printed Definitive Resin:**
- Criollo-Barrios 2025 (Mexico, n=72): AA + Single Bond Universal = best (14.84 ± 2.24 MPa); AA + TXT = 10.36 ± 1.76 MPa; control = lowest

**Bis-Acryl Composite:**
- Shahin 2024 (Saudi Arabia, n=120): NTPT = highest; Er:YAG (9.68 ± 2.03 MPa) and sandblasting (9.15 ± 3.29 MPa) ↑; carbide/diamond burs = lowest

### Surface Roughness (5/9 studies)

- **LD (Alsaud)**: AA = highest Ra (1.20 ± 0.30 µm); HFA > PhA; no treatment = lowest (0.24 ± 0.08 µm)
- **LD (Fayyadh)**: Sandblasting = highest Ra (1.253 ± 0.12 µm); PhA = lowest Ra (0.399 ± 0.08 µm)
- **MZ (Alhunayni)**: HFA = highest Ra (0.82 µm) > AA (0.67) > laser (0.63)
- **Zirconia (Nguyen)**: Sandblasting 30s > 20s > 10s
- **3D-resin (Criollo-Barrios)**: AA = highest (2.21 ± 0.30 µm); HFA = 0.81 ± 0.20 µm; control = lowest (0.07 ± 0.03 µm)

**Key finding**: Ra ↑ does not = SBS ↑ (non-linear); adhesion governed by roughness + surface energy + wettability + chemical bonding + adhesive properties together.
