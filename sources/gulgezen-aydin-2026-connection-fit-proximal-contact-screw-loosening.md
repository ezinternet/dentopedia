## Why Ingested

Existing abutment-screw pages cover preload mechanics, coatings and connection geometry, and the proximal-contact-loss pages cover prevalence and risk factors, but neither side links contact loss to a mechanical consequence for the screw. This in vitro study tests both a horizontal connection misfit and a reduced proximal contact in one factorial design, extending [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/velez-2020-implant-connection-abutment-design-screw]] (vertical marginal misfit vs torque) and giving a screw-side consequence for the contact loss documented in [[implants/kim-2026-proximal-contact-loss-implants-10years]].

## Three-line Summary

In vitro 2x2 factorial study (n=40 internal-hex implants in stainless steel molar models, 10 per group; standard vs 25 µm horizontally reduced hex abutment x standard vs 15 µm-per-side reduced proximal contact on monolithic zirconia crowns) after thermocycling plus 720,000 chewing cycles at 100 N.

Removal torque value (RTV) fell stepwise from control 26.82 ± 0.70 N·cm to reduced-contact only 25.51 ± 0.52, reduced-fit only 24.78 ± 0.89, and both 21.70 ± 0.99 N·cm; all groups differed (P < .05) with a significant fit x contact interaction (P = .001), and misfit had the larger main effect.

Both horizontal misfit and loose proximal contacts accelerate preload loss, but no screw actually loosened in any group; single implant system, internal hex only, rigid steel model and no baseline RTV limit generalization.

## 세줄요약

체외 2x2 요인 연구(내부 육각 임플란트 40개를 상악 우측 제1대구치 스테인리스 모델에 식립, 군당 10개; 표준 vs 25 µm 수평 축소 육각 지대주 x 표준 vs 편측 15 µm 축소 인접면 접촉의 모노리식 지르코니아 크라운), 온도 순환과 100 N 저작 720,000회 후 평가.

제거 토크값(Removal Torque Value, RTV)은 대조군 26.82 ± 0.70 N·cm에서 접촉 축소 25.51 ± 0.52, 적합 축소 24.78 ± 0.89, 둘 다 축소 21.70 ± 0.99 N·cm로 단계적으로 감소; 전 군 간 유의차(P < .05), 적합 x 접촉 교호작용 유의(P = .001), 지대주 적합도의 주효과가 더 컸다.

수평 부적합과 느슨한 인접면 접촉 모두 예압 손실을 가속하지만 어느 군에서도 나사 풀림 자체는 발생하지 않았으며, 단일 임플란트 시스템·내부 육각만·강성 강철 모델·기저 RTV 미측정이 일반화를 제한한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Factorial test of horizontal implant-abutment misfit and proximal contact tightness on abutment screw RTV in cement-retained single crowns; the authors state evidence on proximal contact and screw loosening in single implants was limited.
- Shows the two factors add and interact: the combined group lost the most RTV.
- Reports that horizontal misfit (25 µm narrower hex diagonal) outweighed a 30 µm total proximal contact reduction (partial eta squared .790 vs .679).
- Argues for annual proximal contact checks and screw-stability assessment at follow-up.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro, randomized allocation of abutments and crowns (random number generator), 2x2 factorial, two-way ANOVA with Bonferroni (alpha .05).
- **Sample size**: G*Power (alpha .05, power .95, effect size 0.848 from Rutkunas et al.) gave a minimum of 5 per group; 10 per group used (n=40).
- **Model**: CNC stainless steel maxilla segment (missing right first molar, partial second premolar and second molar crowns); implants (Oxy, 4.5 x 10 mm, non-surface-treated internal hex) bonded with dimethacrylate adhesive; model dimensional deviation 0.2-0.6%.
- **Abutments**: standard vs supplier-made abutments with hex diagonal 25 µm narrower, otherwise identical; uncoated Ti-6Al-4V screw, 30 N·cm with digital torque meter, re-torqued after 10 minutes by a single operator; initial RTV not measured.
- **Crowns**: milled monolithic 3Y-TZP zirconia; marginal gap 0 µm, internal gap 30 µm; contacts reduced 15 µm mesially and distally (checked with 15 µm metal film and 16 µm articulating film); cemented with a UDMA-based temporary implant cement under 50 N.
- **Aging**: 720,000 cycles, 100 N, 1.6 Hz, with thermocycling 5-55 °C; RTV then measured with the torque meter.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Group (abutment fit / proximal contact) | RTV mean ± SD (N·cm), n=10 |
|---|---|
| Control (standard / standard) | 26.82 ± 0.70 |
| SR (standard / reduced) | 25.51 ± 0.52 |
| RS (reduced / standard) | 24.78 ± 0.89 |
| RR (reduced / reduced) | 21.70 ± 0.99 |

- Overall model F(3,36) = 74.507, P < .001, eta squared .861. Abutment fit F(1,36) = 135.083 (partial eta squared .790); proximal contact F(1,36) = 76.071 (.679); interaction F(1,36) = 12.366, P = .001 (.256).
- Bonferroni mean differences (all P < .001, SE 0.36): RR vs control -5.12; reduced vs standard abutment -3.81 within reduced-contact crowns and -2.04 within standard-contact crowns; reduced vs standard contact -3.08 within reduced abutments and -1.31 within standard abutments.
- No screw loosening occurred in any group despite the RTV decrease.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro; rigid stainless steel model does not reproduce bone elasticity or osseointegration.
- One internal-hex, single-screw, cement-retained system and one zirconia material; authors note conical connections may behave differently.
- Baseline RTV was not measured, so "torque loss" is inferred from group comparison with the control.
- One operator, no torque guide fixture, and no inter-rater reliability for the operator-dependent contact assessment.
- Authors note no published torque value defines loss of connection stability, and no consensus exists on how to define or measure misfit; they call for tolerance limits and metal-alloy restorations in future work.

## 6. Related Work
- Velez 2020: vertical marginal misfit vs torque; this study adds horizontal misfit.
- Kim 2026 and the proximal-contact-loss cluster: prevalence and risk factors of contact loss; this study supplies a mechanical consequence.
- Rutkunas et al.: source of the effect size used for sample size (not assessed here).

## 7. Glossary
- **RTV**: removal torque value, the torque needed to loosen the abutment screw after loading.
- **Horizontal misfit**: lateral clearance between the abutment hex and implant internal hex.
- **Proximal contact tightness**: how snugly the crown touches adjacent teeth, assessed with thin films.
- **Settling effect**: preload loss from flattening of micro-roughness at mating surfaces.
