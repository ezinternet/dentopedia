## Why Ingested

The alcohol pages in this wiki were clinical association studies with no biological bridge — why heavy drinking should impair an implant was asserted, not shown. This rat experiment is the direct histomorphometric demonstration that *duration* of alcohol intake maps onto *bone-to-implant contact*, and it is the mechanistic anchor for the "heavy, chronic, not occasional" framing in [[implants/survival/boroomand-2026-alcohol-consumption-dental-implant-failure-sr-ma]].

→ [[implants/survival/de-deco-2015-extended-alcohol-intake-osseointegration-rats]]

## Three-line Summary

Animal experiment in female rats (initially 35, final n = 27; 3 months old at start) randomised to no alcohol or 3, 4, 5, or 6 months of alcohol intake, with a dental implant placed in the right femur and histomorphometry one month later.

Longer alcohol exposure produced progressively lower percentages of direct bone-to-implant contact, with the deficit greatest in the apical region; serum calcium was unchanged but serum phosphate rose.

The experiment links duration of drinking to the quantity of bone actually in contact with the implant surface — the biological plausibility behind the clinical dose-response, at the cost of an animal model with a non-loaded femoral implant.

## 세줄요약

동물실험 (암컷 래트, 시작 35마리·최종 n = 27, 3개월령 시작): 무음주 대조군과 3·4·5·6개월 음주군으로 나누어 우측 대퇴골에 임플란트를 식립하고 1개월 뒤 조직형태계측.

음주 기간이 길수록 직접 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)이 감소했고 결손은 치근단부에서 가장 컸으며, 혈청 칼슘은 불변·인산은 상승.

음주 기간과 실제 골접촉량을 연결한 기전 근거 — 다만 하중이 걸리지 않는 대퇴골 모델이라는 한계를 안고 임상 용량-반응의 생물학적 개연성을 뒷받침함.

- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Implantology 2015;41(3):e44–e49

- **Design**: controlled animal experiment (rat)
- **Institution**: not stated in the abstract-level record available
- **Ethics**: not reported in the available text

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Duration, not just exposure.** Five groups differing only in *how long* alcohol was consumed (0, 3, 4, 5, 6 months) isolate chronicity as the variable — which maps onto the clinical finding that heavy/chronic drinkers, not social drinkers, carry the implant risk.
- **Quantifies the tissue-level outcome.** Percentage of direct bone-implant contact by histomorphometry is the hardest available measure of "did bone actually form against the surface".
- **Regional pattern.** The deficit was largest apically, a detail that matters when interpreting marginal bone loss data in humans.
- **Dissociates mineral homeostasis from the local effect.** Calcium unchanged while phosphate rose suggests the mechanism is not simple systemic calcium depletion.

## 3. Methodology

- **Animals**: 35 female rats, 3 months old at enrolment; final analysed n = 27.
- **Groups**: control (no alcohol) plus 3, 4, 5, and 6 months of alcohol consumption; solid food ad libitum in all groups.
- **Intervention**: at 8 months of age all animals received a dental implant in the right femur.
- **Endpoint**: euthanasia one month after implant placement; histomorphometric quantification of the percentage of direct bone-implant contact.
- **Serology**: serum calcium and phosphate measured.
- **Analysis**: group comparison of BIC percentage (regional analysis including the apical portion) plus body-weight and caloric-intake comparisons.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Groups consuming alcohol for longer periods showed **decreasing percentages of direct bone-implant contact** — a monotonic direction with exposure duration.
- The difference was **larger in the apical region** of the implant.
- Serum **calcium was unaffected**; serum **phosphate was raised**.
- Alcohol consumption **increased caloric intake** but also **increased weight loss** — i.e. the alcohol groups ate more yet weighed less.
- Eight of the 35 animals were not available at final analysis (n = 27); the abstract does not break the losses down by group.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Non-loaded femoral implant**: no occlusal load, no oral environment, no plaque biofilm — the model isolates bone healing, it does not reproduce peri-implantitis.
- Animal bone turnover and rat lifespan compress the exposure window; "6 months of alcohol" is a large fraction of a rat's adult life and does not translate into a human dose.
- The alcohol dose in grams per kilogram is not stated in the abstract-level record, so the exposure cannot be scaled to human intake.
- Group-level losses (8/35) are not attributed, leaving differential attrition as an open question.
- Histomorphometry is a two-dimensional section-based measure; three-dimensional BIC may differ.

## 7. Glossary

- **BIC (bone-to-implant contact)**: percentage of the implant surface in direct contact with bone on histologic section — the standard osseointegration metric in animal studies.
- **Histomorphometry**: quantitative measurement of tissue structure on stained sections.
- **Apical region**: the portion of the implant furthest from the platform; here the site of the largest alcohol-related deficit.
- **Osseointegration**: direct structural and functional connection between living bone and the implant surface.
- **ad libitum**: free access to food and water.
