## Why Ingested

This paper evaluates surface roughness and phase transformation behavior of (Y, Nb)-TZP (niobium-doped yttria-stabilized zirconia) chairside blocks after sequential grinding and polishing, relevant to clinical finishing protocols for this emerging fully-sintered chairside material.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/yoo-2022-analysis-of-surface-characteristics-of]]

## Three-line Summary

A chairside (Y, Nb)-TZP fully-sintered zirconia block showed significantly higher surface roughness and monoclinic phase content than conventional 3Y-TZP in as-delivered and ground states, but polishing with a conventional 2-step zirconia kit reduced roughness to equivalent levels. Phase transformation (tetragonal → monoclinic) was detected in every surface treatment step for the chairside material but not in the lab-side 3Y-TZP. Conventional sequential polishing is clinically recommended before delivering (Y, Nb)-TZP restorations in the chairside setting.

## 세줄요약

(Y, Nb)-TZP 완전소결 체어사이드 블록은 연삭(grinding) 후 표면조도(Ra)와 단사정(monoclinic) 상 함량이 기존 3Y-TZP 랩사이드 군보다 유의하게 높았으나, 2단계 지르코니아 연마킷 사용 후 두 군 간 조도 차이가 소실되었다. 체어사이드 군에서는 연삭 단계마다 상전이(tetragonal→monoclinic)가 관찰된 반면 랩사이드 군은 나타나지 않았다. 임상 적용 시 고속 다이아몬드 버 삭제 후 반드시 순차 연마를 시행할 것을 권고한다.

## Key Contributions

1. First documentation of surface finishing characteristics for (Y, Nb)-TZP (Perfit-FS; Vatech MCIS) fully-sintered chairside zirconia blocks
2. Demonstrated that as-delivered milled (Y, Nb)-TZP surfaces are clinically unacceptable due to grain exposure and elevated Ra
3. Showed monoclinic phase transformation occurs in (Y, Nb)-TZP from the as-milled state onward — unlike conventional 3Y-TZP — but polishing significantly reduces M phase content
4. Established that conventional 2-step zirconia polishing (J1/J2) achieves Ra equivalent to polished 3Y-TZP, validating the same clinical protocol for this new material

## Methodology

- **Specimens**: 40 zirconia crowns (n=20 per group): CS group milled from (Y, Nb)-TZP fully-sintered block (Perfit-FS) using 4-axis mill; LS group from 3Y-TZP (Perfit-HT) using 5-axis mill with post-milling sintering
- **Crown design**: Full-contour monolithic anterior crown, maxillary central incisor typodont, incisal third flattened for surface analysis
- **Surface treatment sequence**: as-delivered (AD) → blue bur grinding → red bur → yellow bur → J1 polisher → J2 polisher (5 steps total)
- **Surface analysis**:
  - Confocal laser scanning microscopy (CLSM, LSM 800, Zeiss) with Gaussian filtering (25 µm cutoff) per ISO 4287
  - X-ray diffraction (XRD, Bruker D8 Advance) at 27–32° for monoclinic (M) phase content (Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya method)
  - FE-SEM (Thermo Fisher Apreo S) at ×1,000 and ×10,000
- **Statistics**: RM-ANOVA with Greenhouse-Geisser correction, Dunnett's post-hoc, independent t-test; n=20 per group based on power analysis (effect size=2, β=0.80, α=0.95)

## Key Results

- **Surface roughness (Ra)**: Polishing significantly reduced Ra in both groups (P < .001). CS group showed significantly higher Ra than LS in AD and ground states, but no significant difference after J1/J2 polishing
- **Phase transformation**: LS group: no M phase detected at any step. CS group: M phase present at every treatment step including AD; significant reduction after polishing (RM-ANOVA F=282.75, P < .001)
- **SEM/CLSM findings**: Grain exposure (5–10 µm grains) visible in CS group throughout AD and grinding states; markedly reduced after J2 polishing
- **Clinical implication**: Delivering (Y, Nb)-TZP in as-milled or high-speed-ground state is not recommended; full sequential polishing is required

## Limitations

- In vitro design; clinical polishing variables (operator technique, cooling, angulation) not fully replicable
- Cannot directly compare Ra values to other published studies due to crown geometry vs flat-specimen designs
- Mechanical properties (flexural strength, hardness) post-polishing not assessed; previous data on as-milled specimens only
- Six-month follow-up clinical data limited; long-term evaluation lacking

## Glossary

- **(Y, Nb)-TZP**: Niobium oxide-containing yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal — fully sintered, reduced hardness (8.5 GPa), improved chairside machinability
- **Ra (Average surface roughness)**: Mean arithmetic deviation of surface profile per ISO 4287; clinical threshold ~0.2 µm for ceramic restorations
- **Monoclinic (M) phase / Phase transformation**: Tetragonal-to-monoclinic martensitic transformation under mechanical stress; associated with volume expansion (~4%); may be protective stress shield or crack initiation site
- **Vm**: Volume fraction of monoclinic phase calculated by Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya XRD peak-height method
- **LTD (Low-Temperature Degradation)**: Hydrothermal aging phenomenon in Y-TZP causing surface monoclinic phase accumulation; (Y, Nb)-TZP claimed to have high LTD resistance
- **CS (Chairside)**: Same-day in-office CAD-CAM milling and delivery workflow
- **LS (Labside)**: Conventional dental laboratory fabrication with post-milling sintering
