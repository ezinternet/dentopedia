## Why Ingested

Every alcohol page in this wiki until now answered "does drinking raise the risk?" — none answered "does stopping help?". This murine experiment is the only held evidence that tests the reversible arm: ethanol was withdrawn at the moment of surgery, and osseointegration returned to control levels. That is the experimental basis for a pre-operative abstinence conversation, and it is why [[implants/survival/de-deco-2015-extended-alcohol-intake-osseointegration-rats]] had to be read alongside it.

→ [[implants/survival/doring-2025-postoperative-abstinence-restores-osseointegration]]

## Three-line Summary

Murine experiment (adult mice; regular water vs 10–15% ethanol in drinking water for 3 months) with a load-bearing porous titanium implant placed in the proximal tibia and continuous ethanol versus ethanol stopped immediately after surgery.

Continuous ethanol reduced peri-implant bone formation (histology and micro-CT) and weakened the bone-implant interface on pull-out testing, whereas postoperative abstinence restored both to control levels.

Ethanol-induced osseointegration failure was reversible when exposure ceased at surgery — direct animal evidence for a perioperative abstinence strategy, though in an orthopaedic rather than dental model.

## 세줄요약

뮤린 실험 (성체 마우스, 일반 식수 대 10–15% 에탄올 식수 3개월): 근위 경골에 하중 부하형 다공성 티타늄 임플란트를 식립하고, 수술 후에도 음주 지속 vs 수술 즉시 중단을 비교.

음주 지속군은 조직학·미세 전산화단층촬영 (micro-CT)에서 임플란트 주위 골형성 감소, 생역학 인발시험에서 골-임플란트 계면 강도 저하를 보였고, 수술 후 금주군은 두 지표 모두 대조군 수준으로 회복.

수술 시점에 음주를 중단하면 에탄올로 인한 골유착 장애가 가역적이었다는 직접 동물 근거 — 단, 치과가 아닌 정형외과 모델이라는 한계를 안고 술전·술후 금주 전략을 뒷받침함.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery, American Volume 2025;107(24):2774–2779
- **DOI**: 10.2106/JBJS.25.00517
- **Design**: controlled animal experiment (mouse), three-arm
- **Model**: load-bearing porous titanium implant, proximal tibia (orthopaedic, not dental)
- **Institution/authors**: orthopaedic research groups (Hospital for Special Surgery / Medical University of Vienna affiliations per author list); ethics approval not detailed in the abstract-level record

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Tests reversibility, not just causation.** The abstinence arm is the design feature no other held alcohol study has: ethanol withdrawn at implantation, and both histological and biomechanical outcomes return to control.
- **Two independent outcome modalities agree.** Micro-CT and histology both show reduced peri-implant bone formation in the continuous-ethanol arm; pull-out testing independently shows a weaker interface.
- **Load-bearing implant.** Unlike many small-animal dental models, the tibial implant is loaded, so the interface is tested mechanically rather than only morphologically.
- **Names the clinical question.** The authors explicitly call for clinical studies of pre- and postoperative abstinence — the gap this wiki's clinical pages cannot yet fill.

## 3. Methodology

- **Animals**: adult mice, previously developed murine tibial implant model.
- **Exposure**: regular drinking water (control) or 10–15% ethanol in drinking water for **3 months before surgery**.
- **Implant**: load-bearing, porous titanium implant in the proximal tibia.
- **Arms**: (1) control, (2) ethanol continued until euthanasia at 4 weeks post-implantation, (3) ethanol **stopped immediately post-implantation** (abstinence).
- **Outcomes**: micro-computed tomography, biomechanical pull-out testing, histological analysis of peri-implant bone formation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Continuous ethanol**: significantly reduced peri-implant bone formation on both histology and micro-CT versus controls; weaker bone-implant interface on pull-out testing.
- **Postoperative abstinence**: peri-implant bone formation and interface strength **restored to levels similar to controls**.
- Timing: 3 months of pre-implant ethanol exposure, 4 weeks of post-implant follow-up.
- Ethanol concentration: 10–15% in drinking water (a fixed range, not a measured blood level).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Orthopaedic model.** Tibial implant in a mouse, not an oral implant: no plaque biofilm, no transmucosal seal, no peri-implantitis dimension. The finding transfers as *bone-healing biology*, not as a dental protocol.
- **No dose-response.** One ethanol concentration band (10–15%) and one duration (3 months); the experiment cannot say how much drinking is too much, nor how long abstinence must precede surgery.
- **Abstinence began at surgery, not before it.** The clinically interesting window — days or weeks of pre-operative abstinence — is untested here.
- Rodent bone turnover is far faster than human; a 4-week healing window is not a human 3–6 month healing window.
- The authors themselves state that clinical studies are needed before translating to patients.

## 7. Glossary

- **Osseointegration**: direct structural and functional union between bone and implant surface.
- **Micro-CT**: three-dimensional X-ray imaging at micrometre resolution, used to quantify peri-implant bone.
- **Pull-out test**: biomechanical test measuring the force needed to extract the implant — a proxy for interface strength.
- **Peri-implant bone formation**: new bone deposited around the implant after placement.
- **Abstinence arm**: the experimental group in which ethanol was withdrawn at a defined timepoint (here, immediately post-implantation).
