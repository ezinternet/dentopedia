## One-line Summary
In silico genomic analysis (n=258 Streptococcus IE isolates, 1935–2021): no oral streptococci carried amoxicillin resistance genes; azithromycin resistance 24.88%; doxycycline resistance 16.59% — primary IE prophylaxis regimen remains genomically sound, but penicillin-allergy alternatives are at risk.

## 한줄요약
In silico 게놈 분석(IE 유래 연쇄구균 258균주): 아목시실린 내성 유전자 없음; 아지스로마이신 24.88%·독시사이클린 16.59% 내성 — 1차 IE 예방 요법은 안전, 페니실린 알러지 대체 약제가 위험.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Journal of Antimicrobial Chemotherapy 2026;81(10)
- DOI: 10.1093/jac/dkag320 | PMID: 42747901
- Setting: Universidade Federal Fluminense (UFF) + FIOCRUZ, Brazil
- Databases: BioSample + RefSeq (NCBI); ResFinder for resistance prediction

## 2. Key Contributions
- First in silico analysis of antibiotic resistance in oral Streptococcus isolates from IE patients specifically targeting dental prophylaxis antibiotics
- Amoxicillin: 0% resistance among oral streptococcal IE isolates — primary regimen genomically sound
- Azithromycin (penicillin-allergy alternative): 24.88% predicted resistance — significant clinical concern
- Doxycycline (alternative): 16.59% resistance — also concerning
- Tn6331 transposon identified in 7 isolates: confers multi-class resistance (aminoglycosides, macrolides, lincosamides, streptogramins, tetracyclines)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: in silico genomic retrospective analysis
- Source: NCBI BioSample and RefSeq databases
- n=258 Streptococcus assemblies; 79.46% classified as oral streptococci
- Date range: 1935–2021; 12 countries
- Resistance prediction: ResFinder software (gene-based resistance prediction)
- Analysis: resistance gene prevalence across antibiotic classes and prophylaxis agents

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Antibiotic | Resistance gene prevalence | IE prophylaxis role |
|---|---|---|
| Amoxicillin | 0% | Primary regimen |
| Azithromycin | 24.88% | Penicillin-allergy alternative |
| Doxycycline | 16.59% | Penicillin-allergy alternative |

- Total: 38.37% of 258 assemblies carried resistance to at least one antimicrobial
- Tn6331 transposon (7 isolates): multi-class resistance potential
- 258 isolates from 12 countries; 1935–2021 span

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In silico only — ResFinder predicts genotypic resistance, not phenotypic/clinical resistance
- Historical isolates (1935–2021) may not represent current resistance landscape
- Not all oral streptococcal species equally represented
- Does not account for new IE prophylaxis guidelines or evolving resistance patterns post-2021
- Phenotypic validation needed before direct clinical extrapolation

## 7. Glossary
- IE: Infective Endocarditis (감염성 심내막염)
- ResFinder: bioinformatics tool for identifying resistance genes in genomic sequences
- Tn6331: transposon conferring multi-class antibiotic resistance
- In silico: computational analysis using databases and algorithms (not lab experiments)
- Resistome: complete set of antibiotic resistance genes in a genome or community
