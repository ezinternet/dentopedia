## Why Ingested

Directly addresses the user's clinical question: does CBCT Hounsfield Unit (HU) value predict implant primary stability (ISQ) after socket preservation? This is the only paper found that measures CBCT HU at 4 months post-ARP and correlates it with both ISQ and histomorphometric % new bone formation. Complements [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/abdulkarim-2021-bone-graft-cbct-bone-density-pilot]] (which showed HU unreliable after xenograft ARP) — Ivanova used FDBA/PRF (allograft-type), consistent with Abdulkarim's finding that allograft does not break the HU–quality correlation.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=30 maxillary patients, Medical University of Plovdiv): socket preservation with FDBA or PRF → CBCT HU at 4 months → ISQ (Osstell) + trephine histomorphometry at implant placement.

CBCT HU significantly correlated with ISQ along the vestibulo-oral axis (r=0.392, p=0.032) and mesiodistal axis (r=0.407, p=0.026); HU also strongly correlated with % newly formed bone on histomorphometry (r=0.776, p<0.001).

After FDBA or PRF socket preservation, CBCT bone density at 4 months is a significant but moderate predictor of implant primary stability, and a strong predictor of histomorphometric bone regeneration quality.

## Key Contributions

- First (and rare) study to simultaneously measure CBCT HU, ISQ, and histomorphometry in the **same** post-ARP implant sites
- Established that CBCT HU at 4 months post-FDBA/PRF socket preservation predicts ISQ at implant placement — supports HU as a clinical readiness proxy in allograft/PRF ARP
- Strong HU↔histomorphometry correlation (r=0.776) validates that CBCT captures real biological bone regeneration, not just residual graft opacity — specific to FDBA/PRF which resorb fully
- Moderate (not strong) HU↔ISQ correlation (r≈0.40) — HU predicts direction and range of ISQ, but other factors (implant design, drill protocol, cortical engagement) also contribute
- Consistent with Abdulkarim 2021 in which allograft sites maintained HU–quality correlation while xenograft sites did not — FDBA is allograft-class

## Methodology

- Design: prospective cohort
- n: 30 patients, 30 maxillary implants; sites from second premolar to second premolar
- Socket preservation: FDBA (freeze-dried bone allograft) OR PRF; details of protocol not specified in abstract
- Timing: CBCT at 4 months post-socket preservation → implant placement
- CBCT: bone density in HU at the planned implant osteotomy site
- Histomorphometry: 30 trephine core samples harvested at implant osteotomy; analyzed with ImageJ for % newly formed bone
- Primary stability: ISQ measured immediately post-implant with Osstell Idx
- Statistics: correlation analysis (Pearson or Spearman not specified)
- Limitations: small n (30), single institution, graft material (FDBA vs PRF) not analyzed separately, no control group, abstract-only available

## Key Results

| Correlation | r | p |
|---|---|---|
| CBCT HU ↔ ISQ (vestibulo-oral axis) | 0.392 | 0.032 |
| CBCT HU ↔ ISQ (mesiodistal axis) | 0.407 | 0.026 |
| CBCT HU ↔ % newly formed bone (histomorphometry) | 0.776 | <0.001 |

**Interpretation**: HU–ISQ is a moderate positive correlation (r≈0.40); HU–histomorphometry is strong (r=0.776). CBCT HU reliably reflects true bone formation (biology), and this translates into a moderate but significant predictor of primary stability.

## Limitations

- Abstract-only — surgical protocol details, graft proportions, ISQ values, and HU ranges not reported
- n=30 — underpowered for subgroup analysis (FDBA vs PRF)
- Single institution, single region (maxilla only; second premolar to second premolar)
- ISQ measured immediately post-placement — secondary stability (osseointegration) not assessed
- Correlation magnitude (r=0.39–0.41) indicates HU explains ~16% of ISQ variance — HU is useful but insufficient alone

## Glossary

- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수)**: 0–100 scale from resonance frequency analysis (RFA); >65 = high stability for loading; measured immediately post-placement
- **Osstell Idx**: device for ISQ measurement via resonance frequency analysis
- **FDBA (Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, 동결건조골 동종이식재)**: human-derived, freeze-dried allograft; resorbs and is replaced by native bone → post-healing HU reflects native bone
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, 혈소판풍부섬유소)**: autologous blood-derived fibrin matrix; not a radiopaque material → post-ARP CBCT HU reflects native bone regeneration directly
- **Histomorphometry (조직계측학)**: quantitative histological analysis of bone architecture; % newly formed bone = gold standard for bone quality assessment
- **Trephine core**: cylindrical bone sample harvested at implant osteotomy site; sacrifices ~1–2mm diameter core for histology
