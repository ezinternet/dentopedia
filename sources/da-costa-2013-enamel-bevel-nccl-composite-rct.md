## Three-line Summary

Within-patient clinical trial (randomization not stated in the abstract; classified as rct) in 42 adults (more than 20 teeth in occlusion, at least two NCCLs each; 84 restorations) comparing non-beveled cavities with a short enamel bevel (45 degrees, 1-2 mm) restored with a two-step etch-and-rinse adhesive (ExciTE) and resin composite (4 Seasons), evaluated at baseline, 6 and 12 months with modified USPHS criteria.

The 12-month retention rate was 91% (95% CI 77-97%) with bevel versus 88% (95% CI 73-95%) without bevel, a non-significant difference, and no other USPHS criterion differed between groups (Fisher test, p > 0.05); no significant change across recall times within either group (McNemar test, p > 0.05).

Enamel beveling does not appear to be necessary for retention of composite restorations in NCCLs restored with a two-step etch-and-rinse system, but follow-up is only 12 months, the sample is small, and this abstract-only record cannot be checked for allocation details or generalized to self-etch or universal adhesives.

## 세줄요약

환자 내 비교 임상시험 (초록에 무작위 배정 명시 없음, RCT로 분류): 성인 42명 (교합 치아 20개 초과, 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 2개 이상), 수복물 84개를 법랑질 사면 없음 (대조)과 짧은 사면 (45도, 길이 1-2 mm)으로 나누어 2단계 산부식-수세 접착제 (Etch-and-Rinse Adhesive)와 복합레진으로 수복하고, 수정 미국공중보건국 (USPHS) 기준으로 기저·6개월·12개월에 평가.

12개월 유지율 (Retention Rate)은 사면군 91% (95% 신뢰구간 77-97%), 비사면군 88% (73-95%)로 유의한 차이가 없었고, 다른 평가 항목에서도 군 간 차이가 없었다 (Fisher 검정, p > 0.05); 각 군 내에서 회복 시점 간 변화도 유의하지 않았다 (McNemar 검정, p > 0.05).

2단계 산부식-수세 접착제를 쓰는 NCCL 복합레진 수복에서는 법랑질 사면 형성이 필수는 아닐 수 있다; 다만 추적 12개월·소규모 표본이며, 초록만 확보한 기록 (전문 미확보)이라 배정·눈가림 세부와 자가산부식·유니버설 접착제로의 일반화는 확인할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct clinical comparison of beveled versus non-beveled enamel margins in NCCL composite restorations, a question the abstract describes as scarce and controversial in clinical studies despite many laboratory advantages.
- Reports 12-month retention with confidence intervals (91% vs 88%) showing no clinically relevant difference.
- Clinical significance statement: beveling enamel margins may not be necessary for NCCL restorations with two-step etch-and-rinse systems.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: clinical trial with two cavity-preparation groups within the same patients (non-beveled control vs short bevel); abstract does not state randomization, allocation concealment or blinding.
- **n**: 42 patients, 84 restorations
- **Intervention**: short bevel (45 degrees, 1-2 mm) with diamond bur vs no bevel; ExciTE (2-step etch-and-rinse) plus 4 Seasons composite (Ivoclar Vivadent)
- **Outcomes**: modified USPHS criteria at baseline, 6 and 12 months; retention as primary reported outcome
- **Statistics**: Fisher test between groups; McNemar test across recalls within a group

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Beveled | Non-beveled |
|---|---|---|
| 12-month retention (95% CI) | 91% (77-97%) | 88% (73-95%) |
| Other USPHS criteria | no significant difference between groups (p > 0.05) | |
| Change across recalls within group | not significant (McNemar, p > 0.05) | |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 12 months of follow-up; NCCL retention losses accumulate over longer periods.
- Small sample (42 patients, 84 restorations); wide confidence intervals overlapping substantially, so a modest difference cannot be excluded.
- One adhesive class (2-step etch-and-rinse) and one composite; results may not transfer to self-etch or universal adhesives.
- Abstract-only record: randomization method, allocation concealment, blinding and lesion characteristics could not be assessed.

## 6. Related Work
- Mahn 2015 meta-analysis: enamel beveling had no influence on clinical outcome across 81 prospective trials.
- Lührs 2020 RCT: dentin pretreatment and cavity preparation in NCCL restorations.
- Prose-only siblings (ingested in parallel): Schroeder 2015 (enamel bevel and cervical composite retention, SR/MA), Baratieri 2003 (resin viscosity and enamel bevel, Class V, 3-year RCT), Heintze 2010 (clinical performance of cervical restorations, MA), Melo 2026 (Class V bevel and universal adhesive bond strength).

## 7. Glossary
- **NCCL**: non-carious cervical lesion
- **Enamel bevel**: angled finish of the enamel cavosurface margin intended to increase etchable enamel surface
- **USPHS criteria**: modified U.S. Public Health Service clinical rating system for restorations
