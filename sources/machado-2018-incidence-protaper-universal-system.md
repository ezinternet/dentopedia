## Why Ingested
Retrospective clinical fracture incidence data for ProTaper Universal in postgraduate hands; rare large-n (1031 teeth, 2355 canals) real-world study showing arch (mandibular 66.7%) and root-third (apical 84.4%) effects. Extends [[wiki/endodontics/shaping]] file-separation evidence base.

## Three-line Summary
Retrospective chart review of 1031 teeth (2355 canals) treated by postgraduate students using ProTaper Universal over 5 years at a Brazilian reference center.
Overall fracture incidence was 4.4% per tooth and 1.9% per canal; mandibular arch had significantly more fractures (66.7% vs 33.3%, P<0.01) and apical third accounted for 84.4% of fractures.
Mandibular molars showed highest rates (8.8–9.6%); operator experience (once-monthly postgraduate training) was the key driver vs prior lower rates in full-time residency settings.

## 세줄요약
후향적 차트 리뷰로 브라질 레퍼런스 센터에서 5년간 ProTaper Universal로 치료된 1031개 치아(2355개 근관)를 분석함.
전체 파절률은 치아당 4.4%, 근관당 1.9%였으며, 하악궁(66.7% vs 33.3%, P<0.01)과 치근 첨단부(84.4%)에서 유의하게 더 많이 발생함.
하악 제1·2대구치가 가장 높았으나(8.8~9.6%), 미국 전일제 레지던트 대비 브라질 월 1회 수련생의 낮은 숙련도가 주요 원인으로 지목됨.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: European Endodontic Journal 2018;3(2):87-92
- **DOI**: 10.14744/eej.2018.30592
- **Institution**: Universidade Estadual de Londrina (UEL), Paraná, Brazil

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest single-system retrospective fracture incidence study for ProTaper Universal (1031 teeth, 2355 canals)
- Quantified arch effect: mandibular 66.7% of fractures vs maxillary 33.3% (P<0.01)
- Quantified root-third effect: apical 84.4% vs middle 15.6% (P<0.01); zero coronal-third fractures
- Identified postgraduate operator inexperience as primary factor for elevated rates vs literature

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective clinical study (chart/radiograph review)
- **n**: 1031 teeth, 2355 canals; 45 fractures
- **Period**: March 2009 – March 2014
- **Operators**: Postgraduate endodontic students (once/month clinical sessions)
- **Protocol**: Coronal access → SX → Gates Glidden → manual glide path #15 → S1/S2 → F1–F5; X-Smart motor, 250 rpm, manufacturer torques; max 4 uses/instrument; complex canals single-use
- **Statistics**: Fisher's exact test, α=0.01

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design with inherent selection/recording bias
- Single center, Brazilian postgraduate students treating once/month (limited generalizability to full-time specialists)
- ProTaper Universal only (conventional NiTi, not heat-treated)
- Operator experience variable not fully controlled or quantified
- No comparison group with different file systems or operator levels

## 6. Related Work
- Wolcott et al. (2006): 2.4% fracture rate (full-time US residents)
- Di Fiore et al. (2010): 0.41% fracture rate (full-time US residents)
- Pirani et al. (2011): 5.3% in retreatments
- Yared et al. (2016): Single-file systems lower fracture rates

## 7. Glossary
- **NiTi**: Nickel-titanium alloy used for rotary endodontic instruments
- **Cyclic fatigue**: Failure mode from repeated bending in curved canals
- **Torsional stress**: Failure mode from instrument locking while motor continues rotation
- **ProTaper Universal**: Conventional (non-heat-treated) NiTi rotary file system with progressive taper design
