## Why Ingested

[[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]]'s comparison table cites this paper as the platform-switching (PS) study with the **largest horizontal mismatch tested** in its whole cluster — a 4mm abutment on a 6mm implant platform, i.e. 1.0mm per-side offset, roughly double the 0.25–0.45mm range seen in sibling studies (Hürzeler 2007, Cappiello 2008, Rodríguez-Ciurana 2009). Ingesting it independently sources that high-end data point rather than relying on Gupta's secondhand table entry — and in doing so surfaces a discrepancy worth flagging (see Limitations): the abstract retrieved here reports only an aggregate bone-resorption figure (0.78 ± 0.36mm), not the mesial/distal split (M 0.57mm, D 1.01mm) that Gupta's table attributes to this paper.

## Three-line Summary

Proof-of-concept case series (9 patients, 10 immediately placed and immediately loaded maxillary implants in extraction sockets, 18–36 month follow-up) testing platform switching at a 1.0mm per-side offset (6mm implant platform, 4mm transmucosal abutment).

Radiographic bone resorption was 0.78 ± 0.36mm (significantly lower than a 1.7mm reference value, P ≤ .005); soft tissue outcomes were favorable — mean buccal margin gain 0.2mm, mean papilla height gain 0.25mm, PPD averaging 2.8mm with no site exceeding 3mm.

Small, uncontrolled case series (no platform-matched comparator arm) at the largest offset magnitude in this wiki's PS cluster; abstract-only ingest (no PMC full text) so the mesial/distal bone-loss breakdown reported secondhand by [[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]] could not be independently confirmed here.

Proof-of-concept 증례군 연구(환자 9명, 발치 후 즉시 상악 임플란트 10개, 즉시부하, 추적 18–36개월) — 6mm 임플란트 플랫폼에 4mm 임시 지대주를 연결해 편측 1.0mm 오프셋의 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS)을 시험.

방사선학적 골 흡수량 0.78 ± 0.36mm(기준값 1.7mm 대비 유의하게 낮음, P ≤ .005); 연조직 결과도 양호 — 협측 변연 평균 0.2mm 이득, 유두 높이 평균 0.25mm 이득, 탐침깊이(PPD) 평균 2.8mm(전 부위 3mm 이하).

대조군(플랫폼 매칭) 없는 소규모 증례군이며 이 위키 PS 클러스터 중 가장 큰 오프셋 값을 가짐; 초록만 확보(PMC 전문 없음)되어 [[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]]가 간접 인용한 근원/원심 분리값은 이번 소싱으로 독립 확인하지 못함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Reports a 1.0mm per-side horizontal implant-abutment mismatch — the largest platform-switching (PS) offset among the studies tabulated in Gupta 2019's comparative review.
- Combines two interventions in one small case series: immediate implant placement into fresh extraction sockets AND platform switching, with immediate non-functional loading.
- Tracks soft tissue outcomes (buccal margin position, papilla height, PPD) alongside bone resorption — a multi-domain peri-implant tissue-preservation readout, not bone-only.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: prospective, uncontrolled case series ("proof-of-concept study"; MeSH: Pilot Projects; Clinical Trial)
- **Databases**: n/a (not a review)
- **n**: 9 patients, 10 implant sites, maxillae, non-compromised bone
- **Outcomes**: radiographic crestal bone height (mesial/distal, image-analysis software), pocket probing depth (PPD), gingival recession, papilla height; follow-up every 6 months from prosthesis insertion (baseline), mean follow-up 22 months (range 18–36)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Bone resorption: 0.78 ± 0.36mm (aggregate figure reported in the abstract), significantly lower than a 1.7mm reference value (P ≤ .005).
- PPD: average 2.8mm, no site exceeding 3mm.
- Buccal margin: mean **gain** of 0.2mm (i.e., no recession on average).
- Papilla height: mean gain of 0.25mm.
- All 10 implants clinically osseointegrated at follow-up.
- **Not independently confirmed here**: the mesial 0.57mm / distal 1.01mm split cited by [[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]]'s Table 1 — the abstract retrieved via PubMed MCP gives only the aggregate 0.78 ± 0.36mm value; the per-surface breakdown would require the full-text results table, which was not retrievable (no PMC full text for this article).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No platform-matched (PM) control arm — cannot isolate the PS effect from the immediate-placement/immediate-loading effect in this design.
- Very small sample (n=9 patients, 10 sites), single-arch (maxilla only), single center.
- Abstract-only source (no PMC full text) — Methodology and Results here are limited to what the abstract states; table-level detail (mesial/distal split, per-patient data) is not available to this wiki.
- DOI unknown — not returned by PubMed metadata for this record.

## 6. Related Work
- Gupta 2019 SR: cites this paper's 1.0mm per-side offset as the largest tested in its 9-study comparison table, with a mesial/distal bone-resorption split not independently confirmable from this abstract.
- Hürzeler 2007 (cited within Gupta 2019, not separately ingested): 0.45mm per-side offset, proposed as the "sufficient" threshold — this paper's 1.0mm offset is roughly double that, offering an upper-bound data point on the offset-magnitude question.
- Lazzara & Rasperini 2006: the origin/naming paper for platform switching; Rasperini is a shared co-author with this 2007 paper.

## 7. Glossary
- **Platform Switching (PS)**: using a prosthetic abutment narrower in diameter than the implant's coronal platform, shifting the implant-abutment junction inward from the implant's outer edge.
- **Per-side offset/mismatch**: half the difference between implant-platform diameter and abutment diameter (here, (6mm − 4mm)/2 = 1.0mm).
- **Proof-of-concept study**: an early-stage, typically uncontrolled study intended to demonstrate feasibility of a clinical concept rather than establish comparative efficacy.
- **Pocket Probing Depth (PPD)**: distance from the gingival margin to the base of the peri-implant/periodontal sulcus, measured with a periodontal probe.
