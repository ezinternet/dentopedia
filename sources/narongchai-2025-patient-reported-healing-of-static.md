## Why Ingested

The wiki held no randomized patient-outcome data on static computer-assisted (guided) lateral window osteotomy — the earlier lateral-approach pages cover anatomy, grafting and membrane perforation, but not whether a guide changes what the patient actually feels while healing, and the one guided-surgery page we hold is transcrestal/robotic. This RCT closes that gap with PROMs plus objective operative time, and it also supplies a directly quotable time benchmark for guided vs freehand windows.

→ [[sinus-lift/lateral/alalshaikh-2024-membrane-perforation-risk-factors-lateral-sinus-sr-ma]]

## Three-line Summary

Double-arm randomized controlled trial (n = 40 patients, 20 conventional freehand sinus floor augmentation vs 20 static computer-assisted sinus floor augmentation, SCA-SFA) in posterior maxilla with residual bone height 1–5 mm, recording patient-reported healing on 0–10 VAS daily for days 1–7 and at day 14, plus complications, wound healing, operative time and operator guide ratings.

Patient-reported pain, bleeding, bruising and sinus symptoms did not differ between groups — the only PROM difference was higher swelling in SCA-SFA on day 2 (VAS 6.85 ± 2.80 vs 4.95 ± 2.85, p = 0.04) — while the guide significantly shortened lateral window osteotomy (11.43 ± 4.75 vs 18.56 ± 12.17 min, p = 0.022) and total surgery (56.24 ± 16.01 vs 69.89 ± 20.08 min, p = 0.023).

Guided surgery buys time, not comfort: it should be chosen when operative time matters and cost does not, and guide designs should be trimmed superiorly to avoid the extra flap reflection that likely caused the greater early swelling.

## 세줄요약

이중군 무작위배정 임상시험 (n = 40; 통상적 자유수기 상악동거상술 (Freehand Sinus Floor Augmentation, SFA) 20명 vs 정적 컴퓨터지원 상악동거상술 (Static Computer-Assisted Sinus Floor Augmentation, SCA-SFA) 20명), 후방 상악 잔존 치조골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 1–5 mm; 환자보고 결과 측정 (Patient-Reported Outcome Measures, PROMs)을 시각아날로그척도 (Visual Analog Scale, VAS) 0–10으로 술후 1–7일 매일 및 14일에 기록.

통증·출혈·멍·부비동 증상은 군 간 차이 없음 — 유일한 PROM 차이는 술후 2일째 SCA-SFA의 부종 증가 (VAS 6.85 ± 2.80 vs 4.95 ± 2.85, p = 0.04) — 한편 측방창 골절개 시간 (11.43 ± 4.75 vs 18.56 ± 12.17분, p = 0.022)과 총 수술 시간 (56.24 ± 16.01 vs 69.89 ± 20.08분, p = 0.023)은 유의하게 단축.

가이드는 시간을 벌어주지만 환자 편안함을 개선하지는 않는다 — 수술 시간이 중요하고 비용 부담이 크지 않을 때 선택하며, 가이드 상연부를 제거해 추가 판막 거상 필요성을 줄이는 설계 개선이 권고된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First randomized comparison of **patient-reported healing outcomes** between static computer-assisted (s-CAIS/SCA-SFA) and conventional freehand lateral window sinus augmentation — the authors state no such comparison existed previously.
- Demonstrates a **null PROM/complication result** with one exception: swelling was significantly higher with the guide on postoperative day 2 (6.85 vs 4.95 on a 0–10 VAS, p = 0.04) — attributed to the need for additional superior flap elevation and longer vertical releasing incisions to seat a full-frame guide.
- Quantifies the **time benefit**: window osteotomy ~40% faster (11.4 vs 18.6 min) and total surgery ~20% faster (56.2 vs 69.9 min), robust to removing the 17-min guide-adjustment outlier (p = 0.046) and to multivariate adjustment (β = 0.399 for osteotomy time; only operator experience and SCA-SFA predicted total time).
- Provides **operator-efficacy ratings** for the guide (fit excellent 55%, stability excellent 50%, window precision 45% excellent, time-efficiency "good" 45%) and flags that guide misfit requiring extraoral adjustment occurs in 5/20 cases and can erase the time advantage.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized, two-arm, controlled clinical trial (described in the text as "double-blinded"; the authors later acknowledge that neither operators nor patients could be blinded, so examiner/assessor blinding is the realistic interpretation). Allocation by computerized block randomization (block of 4) with sealed opaque envelopes.
- **Setting**: Single center — OMFS Department, Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok; patients enrolled February 2023–February 2024.
- **n**: 40 patients / 40 sites (20 SFA control, 20 SCA-SFA test); no dropouts. Sample size from G*Power using day-2 pain data from Scarano et al. (38.67 ± 6.40 vs 29.33 ± 7.33), effect size 1.39, α 0.05, power 0.95 → minimum 30, inflated 30% to 40.
- **Inclusion**: ASA I–II, ≥ 21 years, requesting 1–2 implants in the posterior maxilla, residual bone height 1–5 mm, non-smoker or < 10 cigarettes/day, no sinus membrane pathology/rhinosinusitis, written consent. **Exclusions**: contraindications to implant/augmentation, oral bisphosphonates, need for simultaneous soft-tissue augmentation.
- **Intervention**: CBCT (Dentiiscan, Nectec) planning in all patients; SCA-SFA additionally had an intraoral scan (Trios 3) fused with DICOM in coDiagnostiX, a guide with a lateral-window osteotomy frame designed and smoothed (Meshmixer), printed in dental resin (Straumann CARES P10+), autoclaved, and seated on ≥ 4 teeth and/or teeth + bone. Freehand sites used a periodontal probe plus premeasured CBCT dimensions. Both arms: full-thickness mucoperiosteal flap, rotary or piezo osteotomy, Schneiderian membrane elevation, deproteinized bovine bone graft, collagen membrane over the window, tension-free 4/0 absorbable primary closure.
- **Operators**: 2 certified specialists (> 100 unsupervised sinus procedures) and 2 OMFS residents (< 20 supervised procedures), randomly distributed across arms; all surgeries video-recorded and timed by one independent observer.
- **Primary outcomes**: patient-reported healing on 10-cm VAS (0–10) daily days 1–7 and day 14 — bleeding, swelling, pain, bruising; sinus symptoms (nosebleed, nasal congestion, rhinorrhea, hyposmia, sinus pressure); daily analgesic consumption (yes/no and tablet count). Instrument adopted from a previously validated PROMs questionnaire.
- **Secondary outcomes**: intraoperative complications (membrane perforation, excessive bleeding, root exposure, flap tearing) and postoperative complications at weeks 2 and 4 (dehiscence, palpation pain, swelling/sulcus obliteration, sinusitis, suppuration, paresthesia, graft loss, pathological fracture, sequestrum); specialist-assessed wound healing (blinded examiner, Cohen's κ = 0.83); duration of window osteotomy, membrane placement and total surgery (implant-placement time subtracted); operator Likert assessment of guide efficacy (fit, stability, window precision, time-efficiency, vision/control).
- **Statistics**: SPSS v29 — independent t-tests (continuous), chi-square exact tests (categorical), repeated-measures ANOVA for within/between-group time effects, univariate + stepwise multivariate linear regression (predictors: group, quadrant, implant number, operator experience), Spearman correlation for PROM associations; α = 0.05, no multiplicity adjustment (acknowledged limitation).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Baseline (well balanced, all p > 0.05)**: age 56.15 ± 11.81 (SFA) vs 61.45 ± 11.69 (SCA-SFA), p = 0.162; residual bone height 3.53 ± 1.48 vs 3.57 ± 1.33 mm, p = 0.928; window width 9.9 ± 1.74 vs 10.47 ± 3.38 mm; window height 8.15 ± 1.46 vs 8.07 ± 1.77 mm; simultaneous implant placement 45% in both arms; specialists performed 25% vs 35% of cases.

**Patient-reported outcomes** — no significant between-group differences for pain, bleeding, bruising, nosebleed, rhinorrhea, nasal congestion, hyposmia or sinus pressure at any time point.

|---|---|---|---|
| Pain, day 1 / day 2 | 3.10 ± 3.13 / 2.87 ± 2.52 | 4.08 ± 2.06 / 3.36 ± 1.96 | n.s. |
| Bleeding, day 1 | 2.20 ± 2.76 | 2.37 ± 2.47 | n.s. |
| Bruising, day 1 | 2.11 ± 2.55 | 1.71 ± 2.27 | n.s. |
| **Swelling, day 1** | 4.70 ± 2.65 | 6.15 ± 2.45 | — |
| **Swelling, day 2** | **4.95 ± 2.85** | **6.85 ± 2.80** | **0.04** |
| Sinus pressure, day 1 | 0.93 ± 1.92 | 1.05 ± 1.92 | n.s. |
| Hyposmia, day 1 | 0.33 ± 0.78 | 0.31 ± 0.77 | n.s. |

- **Time effect**: all VAS scores declined significantly over 2 weeks (all p < 0.05) except hyposmia (p = 0.058); **no group × time interaction** for any variable (all p > 0.05).
- **Analgesic use**: freehand patients used significantly less on day 1 (75% vs 100%, p = 0.024) and day 6 (5% vs 30%, p = 0.046); other days and mean tablet counts did not differ.
- **Symptom–healing concordance**: patients rated by the examiner as having uneventful healing reported better self-reported outcomes on days 3, 5 and 6 (p = 0.029, 0.034, 0.029).

**Complications**: no major intraoperative events; **6/40 (15%) minor Schneiderian membrane perforations** (3 SFA, 3 SCA-SFA; text describes them as "> 10 mm" yet "minor"), all repaired with a collagen membrane and the procedure completed as planned; no postoperative CBCT required. Postoperatively: flap dehiscence 2/20 (SFA), partial graft loss 1/20 (SFA), maxillary sinusitis with pus discharge 1/20 (SCA-SFA, diagnosed at 2 weeks). Specialist-assessed wound healing (flap closure, swelling, redness, dehiscence, suppuration) showed no between-group difference at 2 or 4 weeks.

- SCA-SFA correlated with faster window osteotomy (r = 0.368) and faster total surgery (r = 0.36). Guide adjustment: none needed in 15/20; 3 cases 1–2 min; 2 outliers (17 min / 1046 s and 9 min / 563 s). Excluding the 1046-s outlier, osteotomy + adjustment remained faster (p = 0.046).
- **Regression**: osteotomy-time model R² = 0.228, adj R² = 0.186 (p = 0.008) — SCA-SFA β = 0.399 (p = 0.009) and Quadrant 1 vs 2 β = 0.305 (p = 0.0043). Total-time model: only operator experience (β = 0.358, p = 0.017; specialists ≈ 15 min faster than residents) and SCA-SFA (β = 0.321, p = 0.0031; ≈ 12 min reduction) were significant, explaining ~18% of variance.
- **Operator ratings (% excellent / good / fair / poor)**: fit 55/20/15/10; stability 50/40/5/5; window precision 45/40/10/5; time-efficiency 40/45/15/0; vision and control 35/40/15/10.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Many outcomes tested at multiple time points with no multiplicity adjustment → inflated type-1 error risk; conversely, correcting would have reduced power to detect true associations.
- Four operators with heterogeneous experience in both sinus surgery and static CAIS; neither operators nor patients could be blinded (the "double-blind" label in the methods is inconsistent with the discussion), so a novelty effect cannot be excluded.
- n = 40 in a single center; anatomy varied widely (residual bone height 1–5 mm), and dichotomous Y/N assessment of dehiscence may over-report minor deviations.
- Blinded to the patient experience only in the narrow PROM sense: satisfaction with care, oral health-related quality of life, functional limitation and perceived value were not measured — flagged as the next step.
- 2-week/4-week follow-up only: no implant survival, graft consolidation or histomorphometric endpoints.

## 6. Related Work

- **Scarano et al. (cited [20])**: source of the sample-size pain data (day-2 VAS 38.67 ± 6.40 vs 29.33 ± 7.33) and a comparator total surgical duration (31.6 ± 18.0 min) with a different flap design.
- **Cho et al. (cited [24])**: reported increased postoperative swelling with longer vertical releasing incisions — the mechanism proposed here for the day-2 swelling difference.
- **Zaniol et al. (cited [25])**: showed a low window osteotomy position reduces mucoperiosteal flap detachment, supporting the authors' suggestion to trim the guide's superior outline.
- **Baldini et al. (cited [30])**: expert-operator lateral sinus lift in 42.62 ± 6.67 min through a 6 × 6 mm window — this trial's windows were larger (~10 × 8 mm).
- **Systematic review on lateral wall sinus floor elevation (cited [26])**: confirms peak pain at day 1 with gradual decline, matching this cohort's trajectory.

## 7. Glossary

- **SCA-SFA**: static computer-assisted sinus floor augmentation — a 3D-printed, tooth/bone-supported surgical guide that transfers the CBCT-planned lateral window position (and implant position) to the patient.
- **s-CAIS**: static computer-assisted implant surgery; the umbrella technology used for the guided arm.
- **SFA**: conventional freehand sinus floor augmentation (control arm) — window located intraoperatively with a periodontal probe and premeasured CBCT dimensions.
- **PROMs**: patient-reported outcome measures; here a validated questionnaire scoring bleeding, swelling, pain, bruising and sinus symptoms on a 10-cm VAS.
- **VAS (0–10)**: visual analog scale; 0 = none, 10 = extremely severe.
- **RBH**: residual bone height — 1–5 mm here, i.e. lateral-approach territory.
- **Cohen's κ**: inter-/intra-rater agreement statistic for the examiner's photographic wound-healing assessment (0.83).
- **TCTR20230427005**: Thai Clinical Trials Registry identifier.
