## Why Ingested
근관치료 성공률 향상 서베일런스. 치주-근관 복합 병소(PEL)에서 치료 순서(근관 우선 vs 치주 우선)가 성공률·임상 부착 수준에 미치는 영향을 16개 연구 1,468 케이스로 종합한 SR+MA. 기존 위키에 PEL 전용 페이지가 없어 지식 공백을 채움. 해당 증거는 [[endodontics/endodontics]] 카테고리에 편입.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA (16 studies, 1,468 cases) on combined periodontal-endodontic lesions: endodontic-first treatment significantly improved probing depth (MD -1.07 mm), CAL gain (MD 0.99 mm), and tooth survival/success (RR 1.22) vs periodontal-first or periodontal-only approaches.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (16개 연구, 1,468케이스): 치주-근관 복합 병소에서 근관 우선 치료 순서가 치주 우선 대비 탐침 깊이(-1.07mm), 부착 수준(+0.99mm), 치아 생존/성공률(RR 1.22) 모두 유의하게 우수.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 16 trials (10 RCTs, 6 non-randomized) comparing treatment sequence in combined periodontal-endodontic lesions (PELs), searching English and Chinese databases plus grey literature from inception to May 2025.

An endodontic-first sequence was superior on all three endpoints — probing depth reduction MD −1.07 mm (95% CI −1.49 to −0.65, p<0.0001, I²=98.4%), clinical attachment level gain MD +0.99 mm (95% CI 0.61–1.37, p<0.0001, I²=91.5%), and tooth survival RR 1.22 (95% CI 1.08–1.37, p=0.001).

The direction of effect is consistent and clinically actionable, but extreme heterogeneity (I² 91–98%), publication bias for probing depth (Egger's p=0.003) and a study pool concentrated in China and India mean the magnitude — roughly 1 mm — should not be quoted to patients as a precise expectation.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (SR+MA): 치주-치수 복합병소 (Combined Periodontal-Endodontic Lesions, PELs)의 치료 순서를 비교한 16편(무작위대조시험 10편, 비무작위 6편)을 영어·중국어 데이터베이스와 회색문헌에서 2025년 5월까지 수집.

**근관치료 우선(endodontic-first)** 순서가 세 지표 모두에서 우월했다 — 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 감소 평균차 MD −1.07 mm (95% CI −1.49~−0.65, p<0.0001, I²=98.4%), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득 MD +0.99 mm (95% CI 0.61~1.37, p<0.0001, I²=91.5%), 치아 생존 상대위험 RR 1.22 (95% CI 1.08~1.37, p=0.001).

효과의 **방향**은 일관되고 임상 적용이 가능하지만, 극심한 이질성(I² 91~98%)·PD의 출판편향(Egger p=0.003)·중국과 인도에 편중된 연구 풀 때문에 **약 1 mm라는 크기 자체를 환자에게 확정 수치로 제시해선 안 된다.**

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: International Dental Journal, Volume 76, Issue 1, Pages 104021, 2025
- DOI: 10.1016/j.identj.2025.104021
- PMC: PMC12681557
- Databases searched: MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus + Chinese databases + grey literature (inception to May 2025)
- 10 RCTs + 6 nRCTs included

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA with broad inclusion (English + Chinese databases) for treatment sequence in PELs
- Endodontic-first approach significantly superior across PD, CAL, and survival metrics
- Subgroup: Chinese studies reported larger effects (PD MD -1.39mm, CAL MD 1.35mm) with low intra-subgroup heterogeneity
- Region, follow-up duration, and regenerative materials identified as significant heterogeneity sources
- Publication bias detected for PD outcomes (Egger's p=0.003) but not CAL

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA (PRISMA guidelines)
- Population: Adult patients with combined periodontal-endodontic lesions (PELs)
- Interventions compared: (1) Root canal treatment first → then periodontal therapy; (2) Periodontal therapy first → then RCT; (3) Periodontal therapy only
- Outcomes: Probing depth (PD) reduction, clinical attachment level (CAL) gain, tooth survival/success rate
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2.0 (RCTs); ROBINS-I (nRCTs)
- Statistical analysis: Mean difference (MD) with 95% CI; random-effects model; Benjamini-Hochberg correction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Primary outcomes (endodontic-first vs periodontal-first/only):**
- Probing depth reduction: MD = **-1.07 mm** (95% CI: -1.49 to -0.65; p<0.0001; I²=98.4%)
- CAL gain: MD = **+0.99 mm** (95% CI: 0.61-1.37; p<0.0001; I²=91.5%)
- Tooth survival/success rate: RR = **1.22** (95% CI: 1.08-1.37; p=0.001)

**Subgroup analysis:**
- Chinese studies: PD MD = -1.39 mm, CAL MD = 1.35 mm (lower intra-subgroup I²)
- Regenerative materials and longer follow-up associated with better outcomes

**Heterogeneity:** Very high for PD (I²=98.4%) and CAL (I²=91.5%) — limits direct clinical application.

**Publication bias:** Significant for PD (Egger's p=0.003); not significant for CAL (p=0.733).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Very high heterogeneity across PD and CAL outcomes — precludes definitive conclusions
- Lesion classification systems varied across studies (true combined PEL vs secondary involvement)
- Most studies from China/India — generalizability limited
- Publication bias detected for PD outcomes
- Future need: well-designed multicenter RCTs with standardized PEL classification, longer follow-up

## 7. Glossary
- PEL: Periodontal-endodontic lesion — combined pathology involving both pulp and periodontium
- EPL: Endo-perio lesion (alternate terminology for PEL)
- PD: Probing depth — distance from gingival margin to base of pocket
- CAL: Clinical attachment level — distance from CEJ to base of pocket
- nRCT: Non-randomized controlled trial
- ROBINS-I: Risk of bias tool for non-randomized intervention studies
