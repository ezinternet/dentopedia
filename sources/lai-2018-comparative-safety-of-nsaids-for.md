## Why Ingested
From a 2026-11-09 surveillance triggered by a chairside question: a patient vomited after amoxicillin/clavulanate + loxoprofen + mosapride, and the wiki held no loxoprofen-specific GI safety evidence (only the celecoxib umbrella review). This cohort supplies loxoprofen-vs-diclofenac serious-GI-event data and extends → [[drug/analgesics/beaudart-2025-celecoxib-safety-musculoskeletal-sr-ma]] with observational evidence on Asia-Pacific NSAIDs.

## Three-line Summary
Multi-database, distributed-network retrospective cohort study (common data model) of new users of diclofenac, loxoprofen, mefenamic acid, or celecoxib in Australia, Hong Kong, Japan, Korea, and Taiwan (9,879 Japan; 70,492 Taiwan; 263,741 Korea; 246 Hong Kong; 44,013 Australia).
Versus diclofenac, loxoprofen had a lower gastrointestinal (GI) hospitalization risk in Korea (HR 0.37, 95% CI 0.25-0.54) but not in Japan (HR 1.65, 95% CI 0.47-5.78); mefenamic acid was lower in Taiwan (HR 0.45, 95% CI 0.26-0.78) and Korea (HR 0.11, 95% CI 0.05-0.27) but not Hong Kong (HR 2.16, 95% CI 0.28-16.87).
Loxoprofen looks no worse than diclofenac for serious GI events in Korean data, but the effect was not reproduced in Japan (wide CI) and the abstract reports hospitalization only, not mild GI symptoms such as nausea or vomiting; abstract-only, so confounding handling cannot be appraised.

## 세줄요약
다국가 분산 네트워크 공통자료모델 후향 코호트 연구로, 호주·홍콩·일본·한국·대만에서 디클로페낙·록소프로펜·메페남산·셀레콕시브 신규 사용자를 비교 (일본 9,879명, 대만 70,492명, 한국 263,741명, 홍콩 246명, 호주 44,013명).
디클로페낙 대비 록소프로펜의 위장관 입원 위험은 한국에서 낮았으나 (HR 0.37, 95% CI 0.25-0.54) 일본에서는 유의하지 않았고 (HR 1.65, 95% CI 0.47-5.78), 메페남산은 대만 (HR 0.45)과 한국 (HR 0.11)에서 낮았다.
한국 자료에서 록소프로펜은 중증 위장관 사건 위험이 디클로페낙보다 높지 않아 보이나, 일본에서는 재현되지 않았고 (넓은 신뢰구간) 초록은 입원만 다루어 오심·구토 같은 경미한 증상은 알 수 없으며, 초록만 확보해 교란 처리는 평가 불가.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf 2018;27(11):1223-1230
- **DOI**: 10.1002/pds.4663
- **PMID**: 30232832
- **Institution**: Multi-institution Asia-Pacific collaboration (not stated in abstract)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Loxoprofen and mefenamic acid GI-hospitalization risk versus diclofenac in five Asia-Pacific jurisdictions.
- Country-level heterogeneity of the loxoprofen finding.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, distributed network, common data model
- **n**: 9,879 Japan; 70,492 Taiwan; 263,741 Korea; 246 Hong Kong; 44,013 Australia
- **Outcomes**: First hospitalization for GI event (Cox proportional hazards)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Incidence per 1000 person-years: Japan 25.6, Taiwan 32.8, Korea 11.5, Hong Kong 484.5, Australia 35.6.
- Loxoprofen vs diclofenac: Korea HR 0.37 (0.25-0.54); Japan HR 1.65 (0.47-5.78).
- Mefenamic acid vs diclofenac: Taiwan HR 0.45 (0.26-0.78); Korea HR 0.11 (0.05-0.27); Hong Kong HR 2.16 (0.28-16.87).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract only; confounding adjustment and celecoxib results not available.
- Outcome is hospitalization, so minor GI symptoms are not captured; Hong Kong and Japan estimates have wide CIs.

## 6. Related Work
- beaudart-2025: celecoxib GI safety umbrella review (RCT-based).

## 7. Glossary
- **HR**: hazard ratio from Cox proportional hazards model.
- **Common data model**: shared data structure allowing distributed multi-database analysis without pooling patient-level data.
