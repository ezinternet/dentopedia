## Three-line Summary

O'Malley et al. (2026) published a Cochrane systematic review (pub2) applying the Behaviour Change Technique (BCT) taxonomy (Michie et al. 2013) to assess behavioural interventions for improving oral hygiene in adults with periodontal diseases—the first completed Cochrane review in this area, synthesising 25 RCTs involving 1,422 adults across both periodontitis and gingivitis subgroups. Due to data heterogeneity across diverse BCT cluster types, exposures, outcome measures, and follow-up periods, Synthesis Without Meta-analysis (SWiM) was used rather than meta-analysis.

The overall certainty of evidence was rated as very low (GRADE) throughout all outcome domains—bleeding on probing (BOP), gingival index (GI), plaque index, and self-reported behaviour—for both periodontitis and gingivitis subgroups. Results were inconsistent: some studies showed benefit, others showed no difference, and the inability to blind participants to behavioural interventions created inherent performance bias across all 25 trials. This very low certainty baseline means no conclusion can currently be drawn about the effectiveness of any specific behavioural intervention for periodontal oral hygiene improvement.

The clinical significance of this finding is not that behavioural interventions are ineffective, but that existing RCT evidence is too heterogeneous and underpowered to support definitive claims. The review identifies 14 BCT cluster labels used across the literature and calls for future research with standardised intervention descriptions, outcome measures, and longer follow-up. This baseline complements the existing motivational interviewing (MI) literature for periodontal patients by establishing the broader behavioural context.

## 세줄요약

오말리 등(2026)은 행동 변화 기법(Behaviour Change Technique, BCT) 분류 체계를 적용해 치주질환 성인의 구강위생 행동 중재를 평가한 최초의 코크란 체계적 문헌 고찰(pub2)을 발표했다. RCT 25편(1,422명)을 포함했으며, 치주염 및 치은염 하위군 모두를 다뤘다. 중재·결과 지표·추적 기간의 이질성으로 메타분석 대신 메타분석 없는 종합(Synthesis Without Meta-analysis, SWiM)을 사용했다.

전체 근거의 확신도는 모든 결과 영역(출혈지수·치은지수·플라크·자기보고 행동)에서 매우 낮음(GRADE)으로 평가됐다. 일부 연구는 편익을 보였으나 결과가 불일치했으며, 행동 중재 특성상 참여자 맹검이 불가능해 전 연구에 수행 편향이 내재했다.

이 결과는 행동 중재가 효과없다는 의미가 아니라, 현재 근거가 특정 중재의 효과를 단정하기에 너무 이질적이고 소규모임을 의미한다. 14개 BCT 클러스터가 식별됐으며, 향후 연구에서 중재 기술·결과 지표·추적 기간의 표준화가 필요하다. 이 근거 기반은 치주 환자 동기부여 면접(Motivational Interviewing, MI) 문헌의 광범위한 행동적 맥락을 제공한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First completed Cochrane SR applying BCT taxonomy (Michie et al. 2013) to oral hygiene behaviour change in periodontal disease
- Separates evidence for periodontitis (active treatment vs supportive) and gingivitis subgroups
- Identifies 14 BCT cluster labels used across interventions
- Synthesis Without Meta-analysis (SWiM) used due to data heterogeneity
- Establishes very low certainty as the baseline for all current evidence in this area

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 25 RCTs, 1422 adults (19 periodontitis-only, 5 gingivitis-only, 1 mixed)
- Follow-up: 3 weeks to 12 months
- Databases: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, 3 others; latest search 3 July 2024
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 1 tool
- Synthesis: SWiM (Synthesis Without Meta-analysis) due to data nature
- Certainty: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Certainty: VERY LOW throughout (all outcomes)

Periodontitis (17 studies, 892 participants):
- BOP: inconsistent results (some better, some no difference vs active control)
- GI: inconsistent
- Plaque: 1 benefit, 2 no difference
- Self-reported behaviour: mixed benefit for interdental cleaning; no difference for toothbrushing frequency

Gingivitis (6 studies, 958 participants):
- Gingival bleeding: 3 studies found reduction; 1 cluster-RCT benefit for BOP <20% at baseline; evidence very uncertain

GRADE downgrade reasons: risk of bias (unblinded), inconsistency, imprecision (small samples, wide CIs)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Participants could not be blinded to behavioural intervention → performance bias
- Studies designed differently → heterogeneity prevents meta-analysis
- Very small samples in most studies
- No data for CAL or adverse effects found at time of search
- Future research should focus on interventions with plausible behavioural mechanisms

- 행동 변화 기법 (Behaviour Change Technique, BCT): 행동을 목표 방향으로 변화시키기 위해 설계된 체계적 활동
