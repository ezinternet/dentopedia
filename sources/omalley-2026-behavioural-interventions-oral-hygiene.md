## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (25 RCTs, 1422 adults, pub2): behavioural interventions for oral hygiene in periodontitis/gingivitis — very low certainty evidence throughout; insufficient to conclude on effectiveness.

## 한줄요약
코크란 SR (RCT 25편, 1422명): 치주질환 성인의 구강위생 행동 중재 (Behavioural Intervention) — 전체 근거의 확신도 매우 낮음, 효과 결론 도출 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First completed Cochrane SR applying BCT taxonomy (Michie et al. 2013) to oral hygiene behaviour change in periodontal disease
- Separates evidence for periodontitis (active treatment vs supportive) and gingivitis subgroups
- Identifies 14 BCT cluster labels used across interventions
- Synthesis Without Meta-analysis (SWiM) used due to data heterogeneity
- Establishes very low certainty as the baseline for all current evidence in this area

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 25 RCTs, 1422 adults (19 periodontitis-only, 5 gingivitis-only, 1 mixed)
- Follow-up: 3 weeks to 12 months
- Databases: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, 3 others; latest search 3 July 2024
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 1 tool
- Synthesis: SWiM (Synthesis Without Meta-analysis) due to data nature
- Certainty: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Certainty: VERY LOW throughout (all outcomes)

Periodontitis (17 studies, 892 participants):
- BOP: inconsistent results (some better, some no difference vs active control)
- GI: inconsistent
- Plaque: 1 benefit, 2 no difference
- Self-reported behaviour: mixed benefit for interdental cleaning; no difference for toothbrushing frequency

Gingivitis (6 studies, 958 participants):
- Gingival bleeding: 3 studies found reduction; 1 cluster-RCT benefit for BOP <20% at baseline; evidence very uncertain

GRADE downgrade reasons: risk of bias (unblinded), inconsistency, imprecision (small samples, wide CIs)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Participants could not be blinded to behavioural intervention → performance bias
- Studies designed differently → heterogeneity prevents meta-analysis
- Very small samples in most studies
- No data for CAL or adverse effects found at time of search
- Future research should focus on interventions with plausible behavioural mechanisms

## 7. Glossary
- 행동 변화 기법 (Behaviour Change Technique, BCT): 행동을 목표 방향으로 변화시키기 위해 설계된 체계적 활동
- SWiM: Synthesis Without Meta-analysis — 이질성이 높아 메타분석 불가 시 사용하는 서술적 종합 방법
- 지지 치주치료 (Supportive Periodontal Treatment, SPT): 활성 치료 완료 후 지속적 유지 단계
