## Why Ingested
테이퍼형 칼날 나사산 임플란트(Anyridge)에서 삽입 토크(IT)와 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ)의 상관관계를 임상적으로 평가 — [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] 및 [[implants/isq/rosasdiaz-2024-insertion-compression-primary-stability]]에서 다루는 "IT는 ISQ를 예측하지 못한다"는 핵심 명제를 임상 데이터로 보강.

## Three-line Summary
Multicenter prospective study (n=75 Anyridge tapered knife-edge implants, 3 IT groups): IT–ISQ correlation is significant only in the moderate-torque group (30–50 Ncm, ρ=0.481) and absent at both low and high extremes.
High-torque group (>50 Ncm, mean 68.2 Ncm) yielded no additional ISQ gain (78.0 vs 75.6 in the moderate group) and produced all 2 implant failures (IT 77–80 Ncm) and the majority of screw-loosening events.
For tapered knife-edge designs, exceeding ~50 Ncm increases risk without stability benefit — supporting an implant-design-specific upper torque ceiling rather than a universal "more torque is better" rule.

## 세줄요약
다기관 전향 연구 (n=75, Anyridge 테이퍼 칼날나사): IT–ISQ 상관관계는 중간 토크군(30–50 Ncm, ρ=0.481)에서만 유의, 저·고토크 극단에서는 소실.
고토크군(>50 Ncm, 평균 68.2 Ncm)은 ISQ 추가 이득 없음(78.0 vs 중간군 75.6); 전체 임플란트 실패 2개(IT 77–80 Ncm) 전부 이 군; 나사 풀림도 고토크군에 집중.
테이퍼 칼날나사 설계에서 ~50 Ncm 초과 토크는 안정성 이득 없이 위험만 증가 — 임플란트 설계별 토크 상한 개념 지지.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates IT-ISQ correlation is significant overall (Spearman ρ=0.461, p=0.0001) but breaks down at extremes (low and high torque groups individually not significant)
- Establishes that ISQ benefit plateaus around 50 Ncm for tapered knife-edge implants
- Both implant failures (2.7%) occurred in high torque group (IT 77–80 Ncm) — supports upper torque limit concept
- Provides implant-specific data for Anyridge (Megagen), a knife-edge thread tapered design
- Clinically: ISQ and IT may be positively correlated up to ~50 Ncm; beyond this, excess torque confers risk without stability benefit

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Multicenter prospective study (Good Clinical Practice, Declaration of Helsinki)
- n=75 identical implants (Anyridge, Megagen) — tapered, knife-edge thread (TL41, CS34)
- Groups: Low (<30 Ncm, n=25), Medium (30<IT<50, n=25), High (>50 Ncm, n=25)
- IT measured by surgical unit with torque control + integrated RFA module (Implantmed, W&H)
- ISQ recorded at time of surgery; follow-up at 3 months (loading) and 1 year
- Statistics: Kruskal-Wallis, Bonferroni-corrected Mann-Whitney U, Spearman Rho (α=0.05)
- Ethical approval: Comitato Etico Regione Calabria; NCT03222219

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- IT values: Low 18.8±6.0 Ncm, Medium 41.2±7.2 Ncm, High 68.2±12.1 Ncm
- ISQ values: Low 71.8±6.6, Medium 75.6±9.2, High 78.0±6.4
- ISQ significantly different only between Low vs High groups (pairwise, p=0.003)
- Spearman ρ overall: 0.461 (p=0.0001); Medium group: 0.481 (p=0.015); Low: 0.342 (NS); High: 0.121 (NS)
- 2 implant failures (2.7%): both in High torque group (IT 80 Ncm + ISQ 79; IT 77 Ncm + ISQ 77) at 3-month follow-up; both mandibular
- No other biological/mechanical complications; screw loosening in 3 (2 High, 1 Medium)
- 38 maxillary, 37 mandibular; groups balanced by age/sex

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single implant system (Anyridge) — results may not generalize to other tapered designs
- Groups defined post-hoc by IT values — no randomization of torque level
- Missing radiographic follow-up data (bone loss) — no marginal bone outcome reported
- Short follow-up (1 year); 3-month loading assessment
- No stratification by arch (maxilla vs mandible) for ISQ analysis

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- **Anyridge (Megagen)**: tapered implant with knife-edge thread design — wider engagement per turn
- **Knife-edge thread**: thread cross-section with sharp apical edge — designed for bone condensation
- **Spearman Rho (ρ)**: non-parametric rank correlation coefficient
- **Kruskal-Wallis**: non-parametric ANOVA equivalent for 3+ independent groups
- **NCT03222219**: ClinicalTrials.gov registration number
