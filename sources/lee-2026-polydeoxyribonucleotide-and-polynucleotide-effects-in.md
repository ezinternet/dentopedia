## Three-line Summary

Systematic review without meta-analysis of 38 studies (28 clinical, 10 preclinical) on polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN)/polynucleotide (PN) injection for musculoskeletal and related neurological pain, identified from 13,267 records across 10 databases (published 2010–2026).

Pain (visual analog scale, VAS / numeric rating scale, NRS) and function (Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index, WOMAC / maximum mouth opening, MMO) improved within groups, but between-group significance varied by anatomical site and comparator — especially hyaluronic acid (HA) — and GRADE certainty for pain was low to very low in every controlled comparison.

Not a dental trial — the temporomandibular joint (TMJ) is one of several sites; heterogeneous designs plus incomplete and inconsistent adverse-event reporting preclude meta-analysis and limit generalizability (24 of 28 clinical studies from Korea, all 10 preclinical from Korea).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review to integrate **both** PDRN and PN — agents previously conflated or reviewed singly — across multiple musculoskeletal sites, searching 10 databases (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) with no language or year restriction.
- Explicitly separates PDRN (low molecular weight 50–1,500 kDa) from PN (high molecular weight >1,500 kDa) and links preclinical mechanisms (아데노신 A2A 수용체 (Adenosine A2A Receptor, A2AR)–cAMP–PKA–CREB signaling; M1 suppression/M2 activation; VEGF, FGF, TGF-β1 upregulation; caspase-3/9 inhibition) to clinical functional outcomes.
- Applies design-stratified synthesis (controlled comparative / uncontrolled single-arm / case reports and series), formally refusing to treat within-group baseline improvement as a between-group treatment effect — a key safeguard for a literature dominated by small and single-arm studies.
- Documents site-specific heterogeneity, comparator-dependent effects (HA, corticosteroid, saline, physiotherapy, hypertonic dextrose), and the incomplete/inconsistent adverse-event reporting; TMJ evidence is confined to 3 studies with inconsistent between-group significance.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review with qualitative synthesis only — a meta-analysis was not performed because studies were clinically and methodologically heterogeneous.
- **Databases (10)**: Ovid-Medline, Ovid-EMBASE, Ovid-AMED, Cochrane Library (English); China National Knowledge Infrastructure / CNKI (Chinese); CiNii (Japanese); KoreaMed, KMBASE, OASIS, Science On (Korean). Search date March 2026, no language/year limits; terms: "Polydeoxyribonucleotides", "Polynucleotides", "PDRN", "Placentex".
- **Eligibility**: randomized controlled and clinical studies; adults ≥18 with musculoskeletal or musculoskeletal-related neurological pain; comparator unrestricted (HA, corticosteroids, placebo). Preclinical: randomized/non-randomized animal models of musculoskeletal or related neurological pain; injection route required.
- **Selection/extraction**: two authors (SHL, YJL) independently screened and extracted, with third-author consensus for disagreements.
- **Risk of bias / certainty**: Cochrane RoB 2 (clinical RCTs), ROBINS-I (non-randomized comparative), NHLBI before-after tool, JBI case-series and case-report checklists, GRADE (pain certainty per controlled comparison), SYRCLE (preclinical studies).
- **Outcomes**: pain (VAS, NRS), function (WOMAC, MMO), quality of life (EQ-5D); preclinical molecular, histological, and biomechanical endpoints.
- **Flow**: 13,267 records → 10,023 after deduplication → 114 full-text reviewed → 38 included (28 clinical, 10 preclinical).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Search funnel (verified from text).** MEDLINE 9,790; EMBASE 2,979; AMED 1; Cochrane 175; CNKI 100; CiNii 30; KoreaMed 52; KMBASE 6; OASIS 7; Science On 127 = 13,267 records. After deduplication 10,023 screened, 9,909 excluded, 114 full-text assessed, 76 excluded, 38 included (28 clinical + 10 preclinical), published 2010–2026.

**Site distribution (clinical, n = 28).** Knee 11 (knee osteoarthritis 9, pes anserine bursitis 1, ACL/LCL tear 1); shoulder 4; TMJ 3 (TMJ osteoarthrosis 2, temporomandibular disorder 1); wrist 3; ankle/foot 3; elbow 2; spine 2. HA served as control in 7 knee studies. Preclinical (n = 10): shoulder rotator cuff tear 4, lumbar models 3 (sciatica, lumbar foraminal stenosis, neuropathic pain), Achilles tendon injury 3.

**TMJ-specific findings (the dental-adjacent slice, 3 studies only).**
- Choi et al. 2026 (retrospective chart review, TMDs, n = 66; PDRN 30 / 10% hypertonic dextrose 36): VAS 4.70 ± 1.78 → 0.98 ± 1.21, significant within-group reduction (p < 0.001) but **no significant between-group difference** vs dextrose (final VAS p = 0.936). MMO improvement also not significant vs control.
- Jang et al. 2025 (retrospective case series, TMJ-OA, n = 111, single-arm ultrasound-guided pericapsular PDRN): NRS 4.36 ± 2.35 → 1.48 ± 1.86; acute ΔNRS −3.74 vs chronic −2.64; MMO improved without a control comparison.
- Cenzato et al. 2024 (RCT, TMJ osteoarthrosis, n = 60, PN + HA pericapsular vs self-delivered physiotherapy): VAS 4.8 ± 2.87 → 2.80 ± 2.78 (−41.7% at T3, p = 0.006), greater decrease than physiotherapy at 12 weeks (p < 0.0001), but baseline VAS imbalance (p < 0.001). MMO improvement was nonsignificant while left–right asymmetry improved significantly.

**Selected non-TMJ comparators.** Knee: Moon et al. 2023 PN vs HA/cross-linked HA, VAS −41.0 (p < 0.001), significantly better than HA (Δ −17.6, adjusted p = 0.048) and cross-linked HA (Δ −22.4, adjusted p = 0.016); Yoon et al. 2019 PDRN + HA vs HA, VAS 71.27 ± 5.24 → 38.13 ± 4.47 at 6 months, Δ −33.14 vs control Δ −27.86 (p = 0.0001) with significant WOMAC difference; Dallari et al. 2020 PN + HA vs HA, WOMAC between-group NS (T3 p = 0.46, T4 p = 0.14, T5 p = 0.46); Kim T. W. et al. 2023 PN vs HMWHA, PN −27.4 mm at 16 weeks (−54.0%, p < 0.001) but NS vs HMWHA (p = 0.296). Ankle/foot: Kim and Chung 2015 PDRN vs saline placebo, significant only at 12 weeks (3.7 vs 6.3, p < 0.001; NS at 4 weeks); Lee et al. 2020 PDRN inferior to corticosteroid at 2 weeks (p = 0.010) and 6 weeks (p = 0.016), NS at 6 months (p = 0.523).

**Certainty and bias.** GRADE for pain was **low to very low in all 7 controlled comparisons**, none reaching moderate or high — downgraded for risk of bias, imprecision (single small trial), and inconsistency across knee OA trials. RoB 2 of 10 clinical RCTs: 1 low risk, 2 high risk, the remainder "some concerns." SYRCLE of 10 preclinical studies: baseline homogeneity low risk in all; sequence generation, allocation concealment, random housing, and investigator blinding mostly "unclear"; outcome-assessor blinding low risk in 6/10.

**Geography and safety.** 24/28 (86%) clinical studies from Korea and 4/28 (14%) from Italy; all 10 preclinical studies from Korea. No serious adverse events directly attributable to PDRN/PN were reported, but assessment and reporting were incomplete and inconsistent — e.g., no AE reporting at all in TMJ (n = 2), elbow (n = 2), shoulder (n = 2), wrist (n = 1); Kim W. H. et al. 2023 reported 1 mild and 2 moderate AEs with no serious events; Lim et al. 2016 reported an NSAID-associated effect; most preclinical studies did not report AEs separately.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Substantial heterogeneity across disease site, intervention mode (monotherapy/combination, ultrasound guidance), comparator (HA, corticosteroid, placebo, exercise, dextrose), and outcomes precluded quantitative meta-analysis.
- Marked geographic concentration (Korea and Italy only) limits generalizability.
- Limited evidence quality: RoB 2 rated only 1/10 clinical RCTs low risk; SYRCLE ratings largely "unclear" for sequence generation, allocation concealment, and blinding.
- Clinical evidence level lagged the preclinical mechanistic evidence; many knee studies used the active comparator HA, and randomized trials were largely confined to knee and foot while shoulder/wrist/spine data came mainly from case reports or small series.
- Safety evidence asymmetry: no serious attributable AEs reported, but incomplete/inconsistent AE reporting and mostly absent preclinical AE outcomes prevent a firm safety conclusion.
- Within-group improvement must not be read as a between-group effect; randomized, retrospective, and single-arm studies cannot be weighted equally.
- Authors' future agenda: large rigorously designed RCTs with described allocation concealment, blinded outcome assessors and prospective registration; head-to-head PDRN vs PN trials to define molecular-weight-based indications by site (PDRN for tendon/ligament, PN for weight-bearing joints) with CONSORT-standardized dosing/routes; longitudinal ultrasound/MRI structural outcomes plus EQ-5D; factorial studies of combinations (ESWT, microcurrent, atelocollagen); multinational studies beyond Korea and Italy.

## 7. Glossary

- **PDRN**: Polydeoxyribonucleotide — low-molecular-weight (50–1,500 kDa) salmonid germ-cell DNA fragment; A2AR-mediated anti-inflammatory/regenerative agent.
- **PN**: Polynucleotide — high-molecular-weight (>1,500 kDa) DNA chains forming a three-dimensional scaffold; used for cushioning/viscosupplementation in weight-bearing joints.
- **A2AR**: Adenosine A2A receptor (아데노신 A2A 수용체) — cell-surface receptor mediating PDRN's anti-inflammatory and regenerative signaling.
- **cAMP–PKA–CREB**: intracellular signaling cascade downstream of A2AR, demonstrated at protein level in Achilles tendon and partially in sciatic nerve injury models.
- **VEGF / FGF / TGF-β1**: vascular endothelial growth factor / fibroblast growth factor / transforming growth factor-β1 — angiogenic and matrix-remodeling growth factors upregulated by PDRN/PN.
- **COL-1**: type I collagen — densely arranged at the tendon–bone junction after PDRN/PN in preclinical models.
- **M1 / M2 macrophages**: inflammatory (M1) vs regenerative (M2) phenotypes; PDRN/PN suppress M1 and activate M2.
- **caspase-3/9**: executioner caspases inhibited by PDRN/PN, yielding anti-apoptotic effects in tenocytes.
- **HA**: hyaluronic acid (히알루론산) — viscosupplementation comparator in 7 knee studies.
- **MMO**: maximum mouth opening (최대 개구량) — TMJ functional outcome.
- **WOMAC**: Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index — knee pain/function outcome.
- **VAS / NRS**: visual analog scale / numeric rating scale — pain outcomes.
- **EQ-5D**: EuroQol 5-Dimension quality-of-life measure.
- **GRADE / RoB 2 / SYRCLE**: certainty-of-evidence, clinical RCT risk-of-bias, and animal-study risk-of-bias tools, respectively.
- **Load-to-failure**: biomechanical measure of tendon–bone attachment strength, used as a proxy for re-rupture risk.
