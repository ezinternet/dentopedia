## Why Ingested

Mined as a reference from [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]] — that narrative review discusses GBR graft/membrane stabilization principles but relies on secondary citations for the magnitude of intraoperative graft displacement. This ex vivo study provides primary, quantitative 3D data (CBCT + intraoral-scan superimposition) on how much particulate xenograft moves during primary wound closure, and shows that four-pin membrane fixation — not defect morphology or soft-tissue phenotype — is the variable that significantly reduces that displacement. It directly supports and quantifies the review's GBR stabilization claims.

## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo preclinical study, n=60 horizontal bone augmentation (HBA) procedures on 30 fresh pig hemimandibles, comparing partially contained vs. contained defect morphology and collagen-membrane fixation with vs. without four pins.

Graft material displacement (GMD) at the implant platform was substantial regardless of defect morphology (PCD −28.3% vs. CD −28.6%, p≥0.068), but membrane pin-fixation significantly reduced GMD (−15.8% with pins vs. −38.1% without pins, p<0.001), with the greatest apico-lateral loss in the central-crestal zone.

Clinicians should over-contour graft material to compensate for an expected ~15–40% crestal collapse during primary wound closure, and four-pin membrane fixation is an effective, low-cost method to reduce that collapse and improve procedural predictability; limitation: ex vivo pig-mandible model without dynamic postoperative loading/healing.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: The Effect of Defect Morphology and Membrane Fixation on 3D Graft Material Displacement During Primary Wound Closure in Horizontal Bone Augmentation—An Ex Vivo Study
- **Authors**: Raabe C, Cafferata EA, Zhou W, et al.
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research, 2025
- **PMID**: 41194314 | **PMCID**: PMC12975682 | **DOI**: [10.1111/clr.70072](https://doi.org/10.1111/clr.70072)
- **Study type**: Ex vivo preclinical experimental study (2×2 factorial design), pig hemimandible model
- **Institution**: Department of Oral Surgery and Implantology, Goethe University, Carolinum, Frankfurt am Main, Germany

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to present 3D volumetric (intraoral-scan-based) data on the *directionality* of graft material displacement (GMD) during primary wound closure in horizontal bone augmentation.
- Directly compares two defect morphologies (partially contained defect, PCD, simulating late implant placement with extensive resorption; vs. contained defect, CD, simulating early implant placement with 3-wall configuration) — finding no significant difference in GMD between them.
- Quantifies the magnitude of GMD reduction achievable with four-pin membrane fixation vs. no fixation, both at the implant platform (CBCT cross-sectional) and across the full grafted footprint (intraoral-scan volumetric/9-section grid).
- Tests and rules out local soft-tissue phenotype (keratinized mucosa width, flap thickness at 3/6/9 mm, flap advancement) as a confounder of GMD.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Model**: 30 fresh hemi-mandibles from 30-week-old pigs (butcher-sourced; no ethics approval required per ARRIVE 2.0 exempt ex vivo tissue). Each hemimandible received 2 sequential HBA procedures (60 total), randomized for defect morphology and membrane fixation, yielding 4 equal groups (n=15 each): PCD−Pins, PCD+Pins, CD−Pins, CD+Pins.
- **Surgical protocol**: Standardized trapezoidal full-thickness flap with sulcular + 2 vertical releasing incisions; modified periosteal releasing incision for tension-free advancement; standardized 144 mm³ bone defects at 2nd premolar sites; SPI Element 4.0×12.5mm implant placed 1mm within bony envelope; particulate xenograft (BioOss) soaked in radiocontrast, over-contoured 2mm (GMT_0=3mm); collagen membrane (BioGide) applied ± 4 fixation pins (Frios Membrane Tacks).
- **Closure**: Blinded clinician performed standardized closure (1 horizontal mattress + 9 interrupted sutures), verified flap tension ≤0.1N via spring dynamometer.
- **Imaging/measurement**: CBCT before (CBCT_1) and after (CBCT_2) wound closure — graft material thickness (GMT) measured at 1mm increments from implant platform to 8mm apical (L0–L8). Intraoral scans (IOS_1 open-wound, IOS_2 re-opened-wound after suture removal and flap reflection) superimposed via best-fit algorithm on adjacent-tooth surfaces; 12×12mm ROI subdivided into a 3×3 grid (nine 4×4mm subsections) for volumetric displacement.
- **Statistics**: Sample size for Cohen's f=0.75, one-way ANOVA 4 groups, α=0.05, 90% power → planned 60 procedures (exceeded both the base 30 and pairwise-comparison-adjusted 42 requirement). Mann-Whitney U / t-test for 2-group; ANOVA/Kruskal-Wallis with Tukey's/Dunn's-Bonferroni post hoc for >2 groups; regression for soft-tissue predictors vs. GMD. ICC=0.91 for intra-rater CBCT reliability. R v4.2.3.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Defect morphology (PCD vs CD)**: no significant difference in GMD at any CBCT level (L0–L8, p≥0.068) or any of the 9 IOS sections (p≥0.086). At L0 (implant platform): PCD −28.3%±28.2% vs CD −28.6%±33.2%.
- **Membrane fixation (Pins vs no Pins)**: significantly less GMD with pins at L0–L2 (p<0.001 at L0/L1, p=0.007 at L2); no significant difference at L3–L8 (p≥0.141). At L0: Pins −15.8%±25.6% vs no Pins −38.1%±27.4%.
- **Interaction**: within-morphology pin effect significant at L0 only — CD+Pins vs CD−Pins −13.2% vs −39.8% (p=0.016); PCD+Pins vs PCD−Pins −18.3% vs −36.3% (p=0.046).
- **Volumetric (IOS) analysis**: GMD occurred apico-laterally, greatest in central-crestal section, then mesial/distal crestal; volume gains in mid/apical sections. Pins significantly reduced GMD in all 9 sections except apical-distal (p≤0.040); within-morphology crestal-section pin effect significant (p≤0.031).
- **Soft tissue**: mean KMW 7.6±1.1mm, FT3 1.2±0.3mm, FT6 1.1±0.4mm, FT9 0.8±0.2mm, flap advancement 8.7±1.0mm — none significantly associated with GMD (p≥0.240).
- **Graft compression mechanism**: GMT_1 (after membrane, before closure) was 2.9mm (no-pin) vs 2.2mm (pin) — pin placement itself compresses the graft via membrane tension; final GMT_2 converged to 1.7–1.9mm across all groups regardless of pinning, meaning flap closure compresses ungrafted-fixed sites equivalently. Practical implication: expect ~1mm net crestal graft-thickness loss from a 3mm starting thickness in all groups; pins shift the timing of compression earlier (before closure) allowing intraoperative reshaping.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Standardized mandibular defect model does not capture full clinical defect diversity; maxillary sites may behave differently due to gravitational effects.
- Ex vivo design lacks dynamic postoperative factors (muscular activity, mechanical loading, tissue swelling, biologic healing) that could further influence GMD in vivo.
- Possible slight positional shift of graft material during wound reopening for IOS_2 acquisition, potentially biasing non-pin-fixation sites toward overestimated displacement.
- Two sequential procedures per hemimandible may introduce intra-subject correlation, violating full statistical independence.
- Authors call for future study of GMD in defects with a vertical component, and for clinical (in vivo) confirmation of GMD's effect on long-term graft integration/stability.

## 6. Related Work

- Builds on the same group's prior work on flap-advancement technique and GMD (Raabe et al., cited internally) — no confounding effect of soft-tissue phenotype was found in either study.
- Compares against Mir-Mari et al. and Naenni et al. (ex vivo pig-model GMD literature; reported ranges −23% to −65%).
- Contrasts with a micro-CT animal study (Park et al.) showing membrane fixation in contained defects did not change *final* ridge volume — this study similarly found comparable post-closure GMT_2 across groups despite different fixation, reconciling the two findings via the graft-compression-timing mechanism above.

## 7. Glossary

- **GBR** — Guided Bone Regeneration: barrier-membrane-based principle for horizontal/vertical bone augmentation.
- **HBA** — Horizontal Bone Augmentation.
- **GMD** — Graft Material Displacement, the outcome measure of this study (mm and % change in graft thickness/volume).
- **PCD** — Partially Contained Defect (divergent bone walls on all sides; simulates late implant placement with extensive resorption).
- **CD** — Contained Defect (3-wall configuration with vertical stop at base; simulates early implant placement).
- **GMT** — Graft Material Thickness (GMT_0 = initial/pre-membrane; GMT_1 = after membrane, before closure; GMT_2 = after primary wound closure).
- **KMW** — Keratinized Mucosa Width.
- **FT** — Flap Thickness (measured at 3/6/9mm apical to gingival margin).
- **IOS** — Intraoral Scan.
- **ICC** — Intra-class Correlation Coefficient.
