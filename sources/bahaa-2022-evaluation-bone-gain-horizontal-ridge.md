## Why Ingested

This is the only 4-arm head-to-head RCT the wiki holds comparing plain periosteal releasing incision (PRI) against three named alternatives — DFI, MPRI, CALF — on a hard clinical/radiographic bone-gain endpoint, directly quantifying what [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]]'s systematic review only classified taxonomically (advancement strata, no comparative outcome data). It also supplies the largest-sample, bone-gain-outcome evidence for CALF's superiority that [[suture-wound-closure/attia-2023-coronally-advanced-lingual-flap-titanium-mesh]] (n=14, mesh-exposure outcome only) could not test.

## Three-line Summary

RCT (Int J Oral Maxillofac Surg 2022;52(3):379-387; Cairo University, Egypt; n=40 partially edentulous mandible patients, 4 groups of 10) comparing four flap-advancement techniques for titanium-mesh horizontal GBR — periosteal releasing incision (PRI), double flap incision (DFI), modified periosteal releasing incision (MPRI), coronally advanced lingual flap (CALF) — on clinical and radiographic bone gain.

CALF achieved the highest mean clinical bone gain (4.12 ± 1.37 mm) and radiographic bone gain (3.54 ± 1.65 mm); PRI had the lowest of both (2.60 ± 1.36 mm clinical, 2.06 ± 1.11 mm radiographic); both differences were significant across the four groups (P < 0.001). Flap advancement correlated positively with radiographic bone gain (P = 0.003) and inversely with mesh exposure area/percentage vs clinical bone gain (P = 0.022 / 0.017).

Among the four techniques tested, plain PRI is the weakest performer for bone gain and CALF the strongest; abstract-only ingestion — the specific numeric bone-gain values for DFI and MPRI (the two middle groups) are not disclosed in the abstract, only the CALF-highest/PRI-lowest extremes.

## 세줄요약

RCT (Int J Oral Maxillofac Surg 2022;52(3):379-387; 카이로 대학교, 이집트; 하악 부분무치악 환자 n=40, 4개 군 각 10명)으로 티타늄 메시 수평 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)에서 4가지 피판전진술식 — 골막이완절개 (Periosteal Releasing Incision, PRI), 이중피판절개 (Double Flap Incision, DFI), 변형 골막이완절개 (Modified Periosteal Releasing Incision, MPRI), 관상측 전진 설측 피판 (Coronally Advanced Lingual Flap, CALF) — 을 임상적·방사선학적 골증대량으로 비교.

CALF군이 임상적 골증대(4.12±1.37mm)·방사선학적 골증대(3.54±1.65mm) 모두 최고, PRI군이 임상적(2.60±1.36mm)·방사선학적(2.06±1.11mm) 모두 최저였으며 4군 간 차이는 두 지표 모두 유의(P<0.001). 피판전진량은 방사선학적 골증대와 양의 상관(P=0.003), 메시노출 면적·비율은 임상적 골증대와 음의 상관(P=0.022/0.017).

4개 기법 중 단순 PRI가 골증대 면에서 가장 약하고 CALF가 가장 강함; 초록만 확보하여 DFI·MPRI(중간 두 군)의 구체적 수치는 초록에 없고 CALF 최고/PRI 최저 극값만 확인 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions

- The only 4-arm **head-to-head RCT** in the wiki directly comparing PRI, DFI, MPRI, and CALF against each other on the same bone-gain outcome in the same trial — every other flap-advancement page in the wiki tests at most 2 techniques (or 1 technique vs a taxonomy/no-treatment control).
- Establishes **bone gain** (clinical and radiographic) as the comparative outcome, distinct from the exposure-rate outcome ([[suture-wound-closure/attia-2023-coronally-advanced-lingual-flap-titanium-mesh]]) and the advancement/morbidity outcome ([[suture-wound-closure/ogata-2013-comparison-double-flap-incision-periosteal]]) used by the narrower 2-arm trials in this cluster.
- Provides correlational evidence linking flap-advancement magnitude, mesh exposure (area and %), pain, and swelling to bone-gain outcomes — a multivariate view none of the single-technique papers in this category offer.
- Ranks plain PRI as the **weakest** of the four techniques tested and CALF as the **strongest**, giving a comparative ordering that [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]]'s taxonomic framework does not provide.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial (RCT), single-center (Cairo University).
- **Population**: 40 patients with a partially edentulous mandible requiring horizontal ridge augmentation with titanium mesh.
- **Groups**: 4 arms, randomly allocated, n=10 each — PRI, DFI, MPRI, CALF.
- **Primary outcome**: clinical bone gain (mm).
- **Secondary outcome**: radiographic bone gain (mm).
- **Additional analysis**: correlations between clinical/radiographic bone gain, flap advancement, mesh exposure area, mesh exposure percentage, pain, and swelling.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Clinical bone gain** (primary outcome, mm): CALF highest (4.12 ± 1.37) > [DFI, MPRI — values not disclosed in abstract] > PRI lowest (2.60 ± 1.36). Significant difference among groups, P < 0.001.

**Radiographic bone gain** (mm): CALF highest (3.54 ± 1.65) > [DFI, MPRI — values not disclosed] > PRI lowest (2.06 ± 1.11). Significant difference among groups, P < 0.001.

**Correlations**:
| Variables | Direction | P value |
|---|---|---|
| Flap advancement ↔ radiographic bone gain | positive | 0.003 |
| Swelling ↔ pain | positive | 0.007 |
| Flap advancement ↔ swelling | inverse | 0.049 |
| Mesh exposure area ↔ clinical bone gain | inverse | 0.022 |
| Mesh exposure percentage ↔ clinical bone gain | inverse | 0.017 |

**Bottom line**: CALF produced the greatest clinical and radiographic bone gain of the four techniques tested; plain PRI produced the least. Greater mesh exposure (area or %) tracked with less clinical bone gain — consistent with the exposure-harms-regeneration relationship reported by [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]] (≈6× bone-gain reduction at exposed vs non-exposed sites) and with CALF's exposure-elimination finding in [[suture-wound-closure/attia-2023-coronally-advanced-lingual-flap-titanium-mesh]].

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Small per-group n** (10 patients/group, 40 total) — limited power for pairwise comparisons among the middle-ranked techniques (DFI vs MPRI).
- **Single-center** (Cairo University) — generalizability to other populations/surgical teams untested.
- **Abstract-only ingestion**: the specific numeric clinical/radiographic bone-gain values for DFI and MPRI are NOT disclosed in the abstract — only the CALF-highest and PRI-lowest extremes with overall omnibus P-values are reported. This gap is explicit and not reconstructed; a future full-text retrieval (if the paywall is crossed) should backfill the DFI/MPRI numbers and any post-hoc pairwise comparisons.
- No data on long-term implant survival, marginal bone loss after loading, or esthetic outcomes — the trial's endpoint is augmentation-phase bone gain only.

## 6. Related Work

- Bahaa 2022 vs [[suture-wound-closure/attia-2023-coronally-advanced-lingual-flap-titanium-mesh]]: Attia (n=14, CALF vs no-CALF) established CALF eliminates titanium-mesh exposure (0% vs 83.3%, P<.0001) using exposure as the outcome; Bahaa extends this to a 4-arm bone-gain comparison and confirms CALF's superiority on a different, harder endpoint.
- Bahaa 2022 vs [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]]: Plonka's SR classifies advancement techniques by required-advancement stratum (PRI contributes +5.5 mm) but performs no meta-analysis and reports no comparative bone-gain data; Bahaa supplies the empirical, comparative bone-gain evidence the taxonomy lacked.
- Bahaa 2022 vs [[suture-wound-closure/ogata-2013-comparison-double-flap-incision-periosteal]]: Ogata (n=23) found DFI outperforms PRI on advancement magnitude and morbidity (pain/swelling/bleeding VAS); Bahaa's DFI arm sits within a larger 4-technique comparison using bone gain rather than advancement/morbidity as the outcome.
- Ogata GT, Griffin TJ, Ko AC, Hur Y (the same 2013 DFI/PRI trial) is also cited by Bahaa's technique set — both papers share the DFI arm, though Ogata's population is Seibert Class III vertical/horizontal defects vs Bahaa's horizontal titanium-mesh augmentation.
- Not yet wikilinked (no page in the wiki at time of writing, prose-only): a companion modified-PRI (MPRI) paper by Hur and colleagues, a wide-PRI variant (Kim et al. 2015), and a laser-vs-scalpel PRI RCT (Shahnaz et al. 2017) — all part of the same parallel-ingest batch as this page.
- [[suture-wound-closure/raabe-2025-impact-two-flap-advancement-techniques]] tests MPRI (one of Bahaa's four arms) but in an ex vivo porcine model on graft-material displacement, not human clinical/radiographic bone gain — related by shared technique, not by outcome; no typed edge forced given the different model, outcome, and chronology (Raabe postdates Bahaa).

## 7. Glossary

- **PRI (periosteal releasing incision)**: a horizontal incision through the periosteum at the base of a reflected mucoperiosteal flap that releases its inelastic attachment to bone, allowing coronal advancement for tension-free primary closure. The reference/conventional technique in this trial; performed weakest on bone gain.
- **DFI (double flap incision)**: a flap-advancement technique using two incision layers to advance the flap; compared here as an alternative to plain PRI (see also [[suture-wound-closure/ogata-2013-comparison-double-flap-incision-periosteal]]).
- **MPRI (modified periosteal releasing incision)**: a modification of the conventional PRI technique; tested here as a distinct arm from plain PRI (see also [[suture-wound-closure/raabe-2025-impact-two-flap-advancement-techniques]], which tests MPRI in an ex vivo model).
- **CALF (coronally advanced lingual flap)**: a technique advancing the lingual flap coronally (rather than relying on buccal advancement alone) to achieve primary closure; highest bone gain in this trial (see also [[suture-wound-closure/attia-2023-coronally-advanced-lingual-flap-titanium-mesh]]).
- **Titanium mesh (Ti-mesh)**: a rigid, space-maintaining barrier used in guided bone regeneration for horizontal/vertical ridge augmentation; prone to mucosal exposure if flap closure is under tension.
- **Clinical bone gain**: bone-width/height gain measured directly at surgical re-entry or clinical assessment (primary outcome of this trial).
- **Radiographic bone gain**: bone gain measured via radiographic imaging (secondary outcome of this trial).
