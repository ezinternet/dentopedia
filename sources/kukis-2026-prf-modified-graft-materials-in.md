## Why Ingested

기존 [[sinus-lift/lateral/almutairi-2025-lprf-dbbm-sinus-augmentation-sr-ma]]는 L-PRF + DBBM 조합만, [[sinus-lift/lateral/zhang-2025-platelet-bone-enhancers-dbbm-sinus]]는 DBBM 단일 이식재만 다뤘는데, 본 SR+MA(Kukis 2026, 4 RCTs)는 동종골(Allograft) 대조군과 H-PRF·T-PRF까지 포함해 측방 상악동거상술의 조직형태계측 결과를 다시 종합하고, 신생골 증가(Hedges' g 0.61)와 잔류이식재 감소(g −0.65)를 처음으로 이식재 종류를 넘어 pooled 추정한 페이지다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + random-effects meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420261301066; PubMed/Cochrane/Google Scholar, March 2026, last 10 years) of 4 randomized clinical studies comparing PRF-modified allograft or xenograft with the same graft alone in lateral maxillary sinus augmentation (healing 4–6 months).

PRF significantly increased newly formed bone (Hedges' g 0.61, 95% CI 0.15–1.08, p=0.01, I²=17%) and reduced residual graft material (g −0.65, 95% CI −1.15 to −0.14, p=0.01, I²=8%), with no significant effect on connective tissue/marrow spaces (g −0.14, p=0.65, I²=55%) or radiographic bone height (g −0.39, p=0.15, I²=37%).

Only 3 of 4 trials favoured PRF histologically (Nizam found equal new bone), the absolute new-bone gain ranged from ~0.1% to 11.2%, and no trial reported implant-level outcomes — PRF looks like a supportive biologic adjunct for early graft remodelling, not a substitute for the graft or a proven clinical benefit.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420261301066; PubMed·Cochrane Library·Google Scholar, 2026년 3월, 최근 10년): 측방 상악동거상술에서 혈소판풍부피브린(Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)을 동종골(Allograft) 또는 이종골(Xenograft)에 병용한 4개 무작위 임상연구(치유 4–6개월)를 종합.

PRF 병용군에서 신생골(New Bone, NB) 유의 증가(헤지스 g 0.61, 95% CI 0.15–1.08, p=0.01, I²=17%) 및 잔류이식재(Residual Graft) 유의 감소(g −0.65, 95% CI −1.15 to −0.14, p=0.01, I²=8%); 결합조직·골수강(g −0.14, p=0.65)과 방사선학적 골높이(g −0.39, p=0.15)는 유의차 없음.

조직학적으로 PRF가 우세했던 것은 4편 중 3편뿐(Nizam은 신생골 동등)이고 신생골 절대 차이는 약 0.1–11.2%에 그쳤으며 임플란트 수준 결과는 전무 — PRF는 초기 이식재 리모델링을 돕는 보조 생물학적 제제이지 이식재 대체나 임상적 우위의 근거는 아님.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pools histomorphometric outcomes across **both** allograft and xenograft comparisons and across multiple PRF protocols (L-PRF, H-PRF, T-PRF) — the explicit gap left by Almutairi et al. 2025 (L-PRF + DBBM only) and by older L-PRF-only reviews.
- Quantifies the effect as Hedges' g in a random-effects model: new bone 0.61 (p=0.01) and residual graft −0.65 (p=0.01), with low heterogeneity (I²=17% and 8%).
- Demonstrates a dissociation between histology and radiography: no significant pooled effect on connective tissue/marrow spaces or radiographic bone height, i.e. PRF appears to accelerate early cellular bone formation without a measurable dimensional change at 4–6 months.
- Makes the PRF-subtype problem explicit: pooling L-PRF, H-PRF and T-PRF (different centrifugation, tube material, fibrin density) is itself declared a limitation that may drive the moderate heterogeneity in some outcomes.
- States plainly that no included trial reported implant-level endpoints (insertion torque, ISQ, survival), so the histological advantage cannot yet be translated into clinical benefit.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420261301066)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar — identical Boolean string across all three; searched independently and in duplicate by two reviewers; reference lists hand-screened
- **Search string**: ('platelet-rich fibrin' OR 'PRF' OR 'L-PRF' OR 'H-PRF' OR 'T-PRF') AND ('sinus augmentation' OR 'lateral window sinus augmentation') AND ('histomorphometry' OR 'histology' OR 'bone regeneration' OR 'bone volume') AND ('bone graft' OR 'allograft' OR 'xenograft' OR 'DBBM')
- **Limits**: English only; published within the last 10 years; no country/publication-status restriction
- **Inclusion**: RCTs, split-mouth randomized studies, controlled clinical trials, single-blinded and observational/retrospective studies that directly compared graft alone vs graft + PRF in lateral MSA and reported histologic/histomorphometric outcomes
- **Exclusion**: animal/in vitro studies, case reports, narrative reviews, meta-analyses; crestal (transalveolar) technique only; no histologic/histomorphometric evaluation; PRF without a bone graft; grafts other than allograft/xenograft; non-English or unavailable full text
- **n**: 4 randomized clinical studies (studies [25]–[28]); cohort sizes given per trial — 20 patients (Shiezadeh), 21 of 26 sinuses analysed (Nizam), 10 patients (Eken), 26 histological sections (Reis); no pooled patient total is reported
- **Outcomes**: primary — newly formed bone %, residual graft %, marrow/connective tissue %; secondary — radiographic bone height, graft volume, bone density, micro-CT bone volume fraction
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB 2 (5 domains; low +, medium −, high ×), visualised with Robvis
- **Statistics**: Hedges' g with 95% CI, random-effects model; Cochran's Q and I² (0–40% low, 30–60% moderate, 50–90% substantial, 75–100% considerable); fixed-effects used only if heterogeneity low and non-significant; α=0.05; forest plots; publication bias not assessed (k=4)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Study selection (PRISMA flow)**: 718 records identified → 138 duplicates removed → 580 title/abstract screened → 35 full texts assessed → 29 excluded (wrong study type/secondary literature k=8; not lateral window n=2; intervention not PRF n=3; no eligible comparator n=7; no histologic/histomorphometric outcome n=4; outside 10-year window n=4; wrong graft material n=3) → **4 included**.

**Risk of bias**: 3 studies [25,27,28] low risk; 1 study [26] (Nizam) some concerns — incomplete reporting of randomisation and uncertainty about outcome measurement/reporting.

**Pooled effects (random-effects, Hedges' g)**

| Outcome | g | 95% CI | p | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newly formed bone | 0.61 | 0.15 to 1.08 | 0.01 (Z=2.58) | 17% |
| Residual graft material | −0.65 | −1.15 to −0.14 | 0.01 (Z=−2.52) | 8% |
| Connective tissue/marrow spaces | −0.14 | −0.77 to 0.48 | 0.65 (NS) | 55% |
| Radiographic bone height | −0.39 | −0.92 to 0.14 | 0.15 (NS) | 37% |

**Radiographic / micro-CT**
- Reis: baseline residual bone height 2.1 ± 0.9 vs 2.6 ± 0.6 mm (p=0.418); micro-CT bone volume fraction significantly higher with H-PRF (4485 ± 1469 vs 2562 ± 1271), material volume % significantly higher in DBBM-only.
- Shiezadeh: sinus bone width 6.24 ± 1.07 vs 6.25 ± 1.27 mm; bone height 2.74 ± 0.88 vs 2.72 ± 0.92 mm — all NS.
- Nizam: augmented sinus bone height 13.60 ± 1.09 vs 13.53 ± 1.20 mm — NS.
- Eken: **DBBM-only significantly higher** total bone height (14.25 ± 1.65 vs 10.64 ± 3.96 mm), bone gain (11.47 ± 2.14 vs 7.33 ± 3.75 mm), graft volume (1519.39 ± 432.61 vs 989.89 ± 523.07 mm³) and density (492.77 ± 117.35 vs 192.09 ± 127.90 HU).

**Interpretation stated by the authors**: PRF enhances early biological healing/graft remodelling rather than radiographic bone dimensions; absolute new-bone differences spanned ~0.1% (Nizam) to 11.2% (Eken); the present result contrasts with prior meta-analyses reporting no benefit (Almutairi 2025 [20]; a bovine-xenograft PRF review [36]); low I² with k=4 should not be read as proof of homogeneity.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- k=4 studies: precision and robustness of pooled estimates are limited; publication bias not formally assessed (funnel plot/Egger's require many more studies), so unpublished null trials cannot be excluded.
- Graft type and PRF use are **not disentangled** — allograft in Shiezadeh vs DBBM in the other three, so the pooled g reflects PRF within varying graft contexts, not a graft-independent PRF effect.
- PRF protocols pooled despite differing centrifugation/tube material (L-PRF, H-PRF, T-PRF) and resulting fibrin density and growth-factor release kinetics.
- Healing periods narrow (4–6 months) and histomorphometric protocols varied in biopsy site/parameters.
- English-language restriction → possible language bias.
- No implant-level endpoints (insertion torque, ISQ, survival) and no long-term (>5 y) graft stability data; authors call for larger RCTs with standardised PRF protocols.

## 6. Related Work

- Almutairi et al. 2025 [20]: L-PRF + DBBM-only meta-analysis; cited by Kukis as reporting no significant differences in new bone, residual graft or soft-tissue area — the direct scope gap this review claims to close.
- Prior PRF sinus reviews [19]: largely restricted to L-PRF and predating H-PRF/T-PRF.
- Bovine xenograft + PRF systematic review [36]: no significant histomorphometric or radiographic benefit.
- Giordano et al. [37]: 5-year stability of bone gain after sinus lift; Trombelli et al. [38]: multiple factors in long-term peri-implant marginal bone loss — cited to frame the untested long-term question.
- Choukroun et al. [13] (PRF, 2000) and the 2006 introduction of PRF into sinus augmentation [31]: biological rationale (PDGF, TGF-β, IGF-1, VEGF; BMP-2/Runx2 upregulation).

## 7. Glossary

- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: second-generation autologous platelet concentrate; centrifuged blood without anticoagulant, three-dimensional fibrin scaffold with sustained growth-factor release.
- **L-PRF / H-PRF / T-PRF**: leukocyte-PRF (glass tubes, classic protocol) / horizontal-centrifugation PRF (smoother, more evenly distributed cell layer) / titanium-tube PRF (thicker, denser fibrin architecture).
- **DBBM (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral)**: porous carbonate apatite xenograft with high osteoconductivity but low bone-regeneration capacity.
- **Hedges' g**: standardised mean difference corrected for small-sample bias; here 0.61 and −0.65 are read as small-to-moderate effects.
- **I²**: share of variability across studies attributable to heterogeneity rather than chance.
- **RoB 2**: Cochrane risk-of-bias tool, five domains, rated low / some concerns / high.
