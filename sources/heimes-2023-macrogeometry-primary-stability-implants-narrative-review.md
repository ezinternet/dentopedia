## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Int J Implant Dent 2023; PubMed/Embase/Cochrane search) synthesizing how implant macrogeometry — diameter, taper/conicity, thread design, and surface roughness — determines primary stability.

Larger diameter and conical/tapered shape increase bone-contact area and mechanical interlocking; roughened surface and optimized thread geometry further enhance bone engagement; these parameters are key levers in low-density bone sites.

Macrogeometry effects are difficult to isolate from site preparation and bone quality, and direct head-to-head comparisons between macrogeometric variants remain scarce — limiting definitive prescriptive guidance.

## 세줄요약

서술적 고찰(Int J Implant Dent 2023; PubMed/Embase/Cochrane) — 임플란트 macrogeometry(직경, 테이퍼, 나사 설계, 표면 거칠기)가 일차안정성을 결정하는 핵심 인자임을 종합.

큰 직경·원추형이 골접촉 면적과 기계적 맞물림을 높이고, 거친 표면과 나사 형상이 추가로 골 결합을 강화하며, 저밀도골에서 특히 중요한 가변 요소임.

macrogeometry 효과는 술기·골질과 분리가 어렵고, 변수 간 직접 비교 연구가 부족해 최적 설계에 대한 처방적 근거는 아직 제한적임.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Organizes macro-geometric determinants of primary stability into one frame.
- Links diameter, taper/conicity, thread geometry, and surface roughness to bone-contact area.
- Situates macrogeometry as a controllable lever for low-density bone.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Narrative review; literature search via PubMed, Embase, Cochrane Library.
- Studies screened/selected, quality assessed, data narratively synthesized.
- No meta-analysis (narrative).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Larger diameter and conical shape increase contact area → higher primary stability.
- Roughened surface and thread design further enhance bone engagement.
- Primary stability framed as prerequisite for osseointegration; influenced by implant design among other factors.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative (not systematic) — selection and synthesis not formalized.
- Macrogeometry effects entangled with site preparation and bone quality.
- Few head-to-head macrogeometry comparisons.

## 6. Related Work
- Macrodesign–ISQ studies (Stoilov 2023, Shiffler 2016); tapered-vs-cylindrical (Nandini 2022).

## 7. Glossary
- Macrogeometry: macroscopic implant design (diameter, taper, thread).
- Primary stability: mechanical stability at placement.
