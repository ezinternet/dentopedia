## Why Ingested
First prospective RCT using **3D volumetric CT sinus analysis** to quantify how DBBM-based ARP prevents maxillary sinus pneumatization after posterior extraction. Bridges [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/jung-2013-radiographic-evaluation-different-techniques-ridge]] ARP evidence base with the sinus augmentation decision threshold literature covered in [[overviews/sinus-lift-graft-material-prf-overview]]. Clinically actionable: reduces lateral 상악동 거상 (Sinus Floor Elevation, SFE) need from 89% → ~42%.

## One-line Summary
Randomized controlled pilot (n=28, 4 months): DBBM granules or DBBM-C block ARP after posterior maxillary extraction prevented ridge height loss, reduced sinus pneumatization (sinus volume −3.7% vs control +3.9%, p=0.03), and cut lateral SFE need from 89% to ~42%.

## 한줄요약
상악 후방 발치 랜덤화 대조 파일럿 (n=28, 4개월): DBBM 입자 또는 DBBM-C 블록 치조제보존술이 치조골 높이 손실 방지, 상악동 공기화 감소(-3.7% vs +3.9%, p=0.03), 측방 상악동 거상 필요를 89%→~42%로 감소시킴.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First prospective RCT to use 3D volumetric CT analysis to show ARP **prevents sinus pneumatization** after posterior maxillary extraction
- Quantifies reduction in need for lateral SFE: 89% (control) → 42.8% (DBBM granules) → 40% (DBBM-C block)
- Demonstrates equivalence of DBBM granules vs DBBM-C block for sinus protection
- Provides 3D sinus volume change data (Control +3.9% vs Test 1 −3.7%, p=0.03)
- Clinically actionable: ARP as a **prophylactic strategy** to avoid sinus augmentation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Randomized controlled pilot study
- Patients: 28 patients, 28 posterior maxillary extraction sites (n≈9–10 per group)
- Groups:
  - Control: Unassisted healing (blood clot only)
  - Test 1: DBBM granules 1–2mm (Bio-Oss, Geistlich) + collagen membrane
  - Test 2: DBBM-C block (Bio-Oss Collagen, Geistlich) + collagen membrane
- Imaging: CBCT at baseline and 4 months; 3D volumetric software analysis
- Primary outcomes: Ridge height change, sinus volume change, residual bone height (RBH)
- Secondary outcome: Proportion requiring lateral SFE (defined as RBH <8mm)
- Follow-up: 4 months post-extraction (pre-implant timepoint)
- Statistical: ANOVA + post-hoc for between-group; paired t-test for within-group

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Measure | Control | Test 1 (DBBM) | Test 2 (DBBM-C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-ridge height change | −2.7±0.9mm (−39.7%) | +0.9mm | +1.0mm |
| Sinus volume change | +3.9% | −3.7%* | −2.1% |
| Residual bone height | 7.9mm | 10.4mm | 10.3mm |
| Lateral SFE needed (RBH<8mm) | 89% | 42.8% | 40% |

*p=0.03 vs Control for sinus volume change (Test 1); both Test groups significantly better than Control for ridge height preservation.

Test 1 vs Test 2: No statistically significant difference in any outcome.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Pilot study: n≈9–10 per group — underpowered for definitive conclusions
- 4-month only follow-up — does not capture implant placement or survival outcomes
- Geistlich-funded: potential commercial bias
- No histological confirmation of graft incorporation quality
- Australian specialist setting — may differ in community practice
- Membrane technique not fully standardized across sites
- Future: Powered RCT with implant placement outcomes; comparison with transcrestal SFE as alternative

## 6. Related Work
- Jung 2013 (J Periodontol, n=40): Established DBBM-C ARP dimensional evidence; Lam 2024 extends to sinus pneumatization outcomes
- Lund et al. / Araújo et al.: Posterior maxilla ARP dimensional studies (horizontal focus, not sinus volume)
- Jensen & Terheyden (2009): Classical lateral SFE, residual bone height thresholds; Lam 2024 quantifies how ARP shifts patients below/above 8mm threshold
- Esposito 2014 (Cochrane): Short implants vs SFE comparison; ARP preventing SFE need adds a third pathway

## 7. Glossary
- **ARP**: Alveolar Ridge Preservation (치조제 보존술)
- **SFE**: Sinus Floor Elevation (상악동 거상, Sinus Lift)
- **RBH**: Residual Bone Height (잔존 치조골 높이)
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral (탈단백화 이종골)
- **DBBM-C**: DBBM with 10% collagen (Bio-Oss Collagen)
- **Pneumatization**: Post-extraction expansion of sinus into the former tooth socket space (공기화)
