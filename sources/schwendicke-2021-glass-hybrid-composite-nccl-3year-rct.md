## Why Ingested

Requested as part of a sweep on cervical (NCCL) resin restoration retention loss ("서비컬 레진 탈락"). This is the 3-year report of the NCT02631161 trial comparing glass hybrid (GH) and resin composite (RC) in sclerotic NCCLs placed without mechanical cavity preparation; it independently reinforces the GH-vs-RC equivalence seen in [[resin-bonding/koc-vural-2024-glass-hybrid-composite-bruxist-nccl-60m]] and [[resin-bonding/meral-2025-gic-resin-composite-systemic-disease-nccl-48m]], and adds the only cost data in that comparison. → [[resin-bonding/schwendicke-2021-glass-hybrid-composite-nccl-3year-rct]]

## Three-line Summary

Cluster-randomized trial (NCT02631161; 88 patients aged 50-70, 175 sclerotic NCCLs; 83 glass hybrid vs 92 resin composite restorations placed without mechanical cavity preparation), mean follow-up 36 months (range 31-55) — abstract-only ingest.

Total retention loss in 17 GH vs 19 RC restorations (5 additional partial GH losses); survival not significantly different (p = 0.396, multi-level Cox); FDI quality did not differ except surface luster (RC better, p < 0.001); total cost per restoration 41.72 EUR (GH) vs 51.60 EUR (RC), p < 0.001.

Survival was equivalent but GH was cheaper both initially and long-term, so RC is cost-effective only for payers willing to pay a large premium for minimal survival gains; only the abstract was available, so lesion-level and failure-mode detail is not captured.

클러스터 무작위배정 임상시험(NCT02631161; 50-70세 환자 88명, 경화성 비우식성 치경부 병소 (sclerotic non-carious cervical lesion, sNCCL) 175개; 기계적 와동 형성 없이 유리 혼합형 (Glass Hybrid, GH) 83개 vs 복합레진 (Resin Composite, RC) 92개 수복), 평균 추적 36개월(31-55개월) — 초록만 수집.

완전 탈락(retention loss)은 GH 17개 vs RC 19개(GH 부분 탈락 5개 별도), 생존율 차이 유의하지 않음(p = 0.396, 다수준 Cox 회귀); FDI 평가는 표면 광택(RC 우수, p < 0.001) 외 차이 없음; 수복당 총비용 GH 41.72유로 vs RC 51.60유로(p < 0.001).

생존율은 동등했으나 GH가 초기·장기 비용 모두 낮아, RC는 미미한 생존 이득에 큰 추가 비용을 낼 의향이 있는 지불자에서만 비용효과적; 초록만 확보해 병소 단위·실패 양상 상세는 반영하지 못함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- 3-year (mean 36 months) comparison of GH (EQUIA Forte Fil/Coat) vs RC (OptiBond FL/Filtek Supreme XTE) in sclerotic NCCLs restored without mechanical cavity preparation.
- Survival (retention) not significantly different between GH and RC.
- Payer-perspective costing: GH significantly cheaper initially and over follow-up.
- Report of the 3-year follow-up of the trial whose 18-month interim report is Göstemeyer 2021.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cluster-randomized trial (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02631161), as stated in the abstract.
- **n**: 88 patients (50-70 years), 175 sclerotic NCCLs; 43 patients/83 restorations GH, 45 patients/92 restorations RC.
- **Interventions**: GH = EQUIA Forte Fil + EQUIA Forte Coat; RC = OptiBond FL + Filtek Supreme XTE; no mechanical cavity preparation.
- **Follow-up**: mean 36 months (min/max 31/55), variable because of the COVID-19 lockdown; quality re-evaluated at 1, 18 and 36 months (FDI criteria).
- **Outcomes**: survival (multi-level Cox regression), FDI restoration quality, costs from German payer perspective (micro-costing for initial treatment; statutory insurance fee-item catalogues for follow-up; compared by GLM).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest; no failure-mode breakdown, hazard ratios, or lesion-level covariates are available here.
- Variable follow-up (31-55 months) because of the COVID-19 lockdown.
- Sclerotic NCCLs in patients aged 50-70, no mechanical preparation: results may not transfer to prepared cavities or other lesion types.
- Costs are from a German statutory-insurance payer perspective and may not transfer to other systems.

## 7. Glossary

- **Glass hybrid (GH)**: highly viscous glass-ionomer restorative with a nano-filled resin coating (EQUIA Forte Fil + Coat).
- **sNCCL**: sclerotic non-carious cervical lesion.
- **FDI criteria**: World Dental Federation criteria for clinical evaluation of direct restorations.
- **Micro-costing**: bottom-up cost estimation from time recordings and hourly costs.
- **Multi-level Cox regression**: survival model accounting for clustering of restorations within patients.
