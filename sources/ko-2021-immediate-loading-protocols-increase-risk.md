## Three-line Summary
Single-center RCT enrolling 72 partially edentulous patients (187 implants) compared immediate loading (IL) versus 3-month delayed loading (DL) following fully guided implant surgery, with IL eligibility gated at IT >20 Ncm and ISQ >65.
At 1-year follow-up, IL showed significantly lower survival in both ITT (83.4% implant-level) and per-protocol (90.2%) analyses versus 100% DL survival (all p<0.01), while marginal bone loss remained below 0.1 mm in both groups with no intergroup difference.
Even when standard primary stability thresholds are met, fully guided surgery does not mitigate the higher failure risk of immediate loading; delayed loading should remain the preferred protocol for guide-assisted cases until further long-term evidence is available.

## One-line Summary

RCT (n=72 patients, 187 implants) found fully guided surgery + immediate loading yielded significantly lower 1-year survival (83.4% implant-level ITT) vs. 3-month delayed loading (100%), with no difference in marginal bone loss.

## 한줄요약

완전 가이드 수술로 식립한 임플란트에서 즉시로딩은 3개월 지연로딩 대비 유의하게 낮은 1년 생존율을 보였으며(임플란트 수준 ITT 83.4% vs 100%), 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 두 군 모두 0.1 mm 미만으로 차이 없는 RCT.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT to test IL vs. DL specifically in **fully guided (template-guided) implant surgery** in partially edentulous patients
- Demonstrates that even with pre-set eligibility gates (IT >20 Ncm AND ISQ >65), IL in the guided-surgery context increases failure risk significantly
- Provides both ITT and PP survival analyses, strengthening internal validity
- Shows MBL is not sensitive to loading timing (both groups <0.1 mm at 1 year)
- Establishes that 10% of IL-assigned patients (4/36) failed the IL eligibility criteria, highlighting variability even in guide-positioned implants

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized controlled trial, 1-year follow-up
- **Population**: 72 patients (partially edentulous jaws); IL n=36 (93 implants), DL n=26 (94 implants)
- **Intervention (IL)**: Prefabricated provisional prosthesis delivered immediately; prerequisite — IT >20 Ncm AND ISQ >65. 4 IL-assigned patients did not meet criteria → excluded from IL delivery (78 implants, 32 patients actually received IL)
- **Control (DL)**: Conventional 3-month healing; all 94 implants loaded after 3 months
- **Primary outcome**: 1-year implant survival rate (ITT and per-protocol analyses)
- **Secondary outcome**: Marginal bone loss (MBL) by cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT)
- **Statistical threshold**: p < 0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | IL (ITT) | IL (PP) | DL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patient-level survival | 69.4% | 78.1% | 100% |
| Implant-level survival | 83.4% | 90.2% | 100% |
| MBL | <0.1 mm | <0.1 mm | <0.1 mm |

- All IL vs. DL survival differences: p < 0.01
- MBL intergroup difference: p > 0.05 (not significant)
- DL: 100% survival at both patient and implant levels at 1 year

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single center (Yonsei University); sample size modest (72 patients)
- IL group criteria (IT >20 Ncm + ISQ >65) are relatively low thresholds; stricter criteria might reduce failures
- Fully guided surgery introduces cumulative positional error that may compound under immediate loading; this mechanism was not directly measured
- 1-year follow-up only; long-term (3–5 year) data needed
- No subgroup analysis by jaw location (anterior vs. posterior), bone density, or implant dimensions

## 7. Glossary
- **IL**: Immediate Loading — provisional prosthesis placed within 48 h of implant surgery
- **DL**: Delayed Loading — prosthesis placed after ≥3 months healing
- **ITT**: Intention-to-Treat analysis — all randomized patients included regardless of protocol compliance
- **PP**: Per-Protocol analysis — only patients who received the assigned intervention
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient — resonance frequency analysis (RFA) measure of implant stability (scale 1–100)
- **IT**: Insertion Torque — rotational resistance at final seating (Ncm)
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss — periimplant crestal bone change measured by CBCT
- **Fully guided surgery**: Static surgical guide covering all drilling and implant placement steps (guide-to-implant contact)
