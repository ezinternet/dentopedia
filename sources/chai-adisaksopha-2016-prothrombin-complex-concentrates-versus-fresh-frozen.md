## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 13 studies (5 randomised, 8 observational; searched to December 2015) comparing prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC) with fresh frozen plasma (FFP), each given with vitamin K, for urgent warfarin reversal in major bleeding or urgent surgery.
PCC reduced all-cause mortality (OR 0.56, 95% CI 0.37–0.84), achieved INR normalization more often (OR 10.80) and 6.5 h faster, and reduced volume overload (OR 0.27), without more thromboembolism (OR 0.91, NS); haemostasis OR 2.00 was NS.
Supports PCC over FFP as the factor-replacement partner to vitamin K; the mortality benefit rests largely on observational data and was not reproduced in RCT-only analyses.

## 세줄요약

주요 출혈 또는 응급 수술 시 와파린 (Warfarin) 긴급 역전에서 비타민 K와 함께 투여한 프로트롬빈 복합체 농축물 (Prothrombin Complex Concentrate, PCC) 과 신선동결혈장 (Fresh Frozen Plasma, FFP) 을 비교한 SR+MA (13편 — 무작위 5, 관찰 8; 2015년 12월까지).
PCC가 전체 사망 감소 (OR 0.56, 95% CI 0.37–0.84), 국제정상화비율 (International Normalized Ratio, INR) 정상화 증가 (OR 10.80)·6.5시간 단축, 용적 과부하 감소 (OR 0.27); 혈전색전증 차이 없음 (OR 0.91), 지혈 달성 OR 2.00은 유의하지 않음.
비타민 K와 짝지을 인자 보충제로 FFP보다 PCC를 지지하나, 사망 이득은 주로 관찰연구에 기대며 RCT만 본 분석에서는 재현되지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames urgent warfarin reversal as **vitamin K + coagulation factor replacement (PCC or FFP)**.
- Pools randomised and observational studies on clinically relevant outcomes (mortality, haemostasis, volume overload, thromboembolism), not only INR.
- Reports a significant mortality reduction with PCC.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Library to December 2015
- **n**: 13 studies (5 randomised, 8 observational); patient count not given in abstract
- **Population**: warfarin patients with major bleeding or needing urgent surgery
- **Outcomes**: all-cause mortality, haemostasis, INR normalization, time to correction, RBC transfusion, volume overload, thromboembolism

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | PCC vs FFP |
|---|---|
| All-cause mortality | OR 0.56 (95% CI 0.37–0.84), p=0.006 |
| Haemostasis achieved | OR 2.00 (0.85–4.68), NS |
| INR normalization | OR 10.80 (6.12–19.07) |
| Time to INR correction | MD −6.50 h (−9.75 to −3.24) |
| RBC transfusion | OR 0.88 (0.53–1.43), NS |
| Volume overload | OR 0.27 (0.13–0.58) |
| Thromboembolism | OR 0.91 (0.44–1.89), NS |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract only — risk-of-bias detail, patient numbers and heterogeneity not available here.
- Mixed randomised and observational data; the mortality signal is vulnerable to confounding by indication.
- Medical/surgical major bleeding population; no dental data.

## 7. Glossary

- **PCC**: prothrombin complex concentrate — concentrated vitamin K-dependent factors.
- **FFP**: fresh frozen plasma.
- **Volume overload (TACO)**: circulatory overload from large-volume transfusion.
- **Confounding by indication**: sicker patients receive a different treatment, biasing observational comparisons.
