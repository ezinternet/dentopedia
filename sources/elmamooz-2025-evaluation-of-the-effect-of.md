## Why Ingested

Directly extends the polishing-after-grinding literature for monolithic zirconia by isolating the *extent* of chairside adjustment (one vs. two areas, 21.5% vs. 43% surface) as a variable on biaxial flexural strength (BFS) after hydrothermal aging — a gap not addressed by prior polishing-protocol studies.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study of 56 Ceramill ZI monolithic zirconia discs divided into 7 groups (n = 8) testing chairside grinding, polishing, or reglazing over one vs. two surface areas, followed by ISO 13356 autoclave hydrothermal aging (134 °C, 2 bar, 5 h), with biaxial flexural strength (BFS) assessed by the piston-on-three-ball method.
Polishing after grinding achieved the highest BFS (G6: 999.2 ± 81.1 MPa vs. control 688.8 MPa), reglazing after grinding produced the lowest (G4: 528.9 ± 17.5 MPa); both surface treatment (p < 0.0001) and extent of adjustment (p < 0.0001) significantly affected BFS, with wider adjustment areas yielding higher BFS regardless of treatment modality.
Clinically, rubber polishing should follow chairside grinding of monolithic zirconia; reglazing is contraindicated post-adjustment due to its significant strength-reducing effect, and even minimal adjustment area is enough to trigger clinically meaningful strength changes.

## 세줄요약

단일 지르코니아 (Monolithic Zirconia) 56개 디스크를 연삭(Grinding)·연마(Polishing)·재유약(Reglazing) × 조정 면적 1구역/2구역으로 7군 분류 후 ISO 13356 오토클레이브 수열노화(Hydrothermal Aging) 처리 뒤 이축굴곡강도 (Biaxial Flexural Strength, BFS)를 측정하였다.
연마군 2구역(G6)이 BFS 999.2 MPa로 가장 높았고, 재유약 1구역(G4)이 528.9 MPa로 최저; 표면처리 종류(p < 0.0001)와 조정 범위(p < 0.0001) 모두 BFS에 유의한 영향을 미쳤다.
임상적으로 의자 옆 연삭 후 반드시 지르코니아 전용 러버 연마 키트로 마무리해야 하며, 재유약은 강도를 유의하게 감소시키므로 연삭 후 적용을 피해야 한다.

## Key Contributions

- First study to isolate *extent of adjustment area* (21.5% vs. 43% of total surface) as an independent variable on BFS after aging, in addition to surface treatment type
- Demonstrates that polishing with the EVE Diacera two-step diamond rubber system after grinding significantly improves BFS compared with control (999.2 vs. 688.8 MPa)
- Demonstrates that reglazing after grinding significantly reduces BFS below control (528.9 MPa for one-area; 657.4 MPa for two-area)
- Provides clinically representative grinding protocol using fine diamond bur (106 µm, ZF801L) with water coolant under high-speed handpiece
- All groups exceeded 500 MPa after aging, maintaining mechanical adequacy despite treatment effects

## Methodology

- **Design**: In-vitro, 7-group two-way ANOVA experiment
- **N**: 56 disks (n = 8/group), Ø15 × 1.2 ± 0.2 mm per ISO 6872:2024
- **Material**: Ceramill ZI (Amann Girrbach), yttrium-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal (Y-TZP), sintered 1450 °C/2 h
- **Groups**:
  - G1: control (glazed, no adjustment)
  - G2: grinding one area (21.5% surface)
  - G3: grinding + polishing one area
  - G4: grinding + reglazing one area
  - G5: grinding two areas (43% surface)
  - G6: grinding + polishing two areas
  - G7: grinding + reglazing two areas
- **Grinding**: fine diamond bur 106 µm (ZF801L, DiaTessin), high-speed handpiece, water coolant, 1 min sweeping
- **Polishing**: EVE Diacera two-step kit (green medium → orange fine silicone-diamond, 1 min each), low-speed handpiece
- **Reglazing**: Clear Glaze (Kuraray), applied and fired per manufacturer protocol
- **Aging**: ISO 13356:2015, autoclave 134 °C, 2 bar, 5 h (≈ 15–20 years in vivo)
- **BFS testing**: piston-on-three-ball, universal testing machine (ZwickRoell Z050), 1 mm/min; treated surface on tensile side
- **Statistics**: two-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD, α = 0.05

## Key Results

| Group | Treatment | Area | BFS Mean ± SD (MPa) |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Control | — | 688.79 ± 25.22 |
| G2 | Grinding | 1 area | 781.83 ± 35.47 |
| G3 | Grinding + Polishing | 1 area | 898.15 ± 76.93 |
| G4 | Grinding + Reglazing | 1 area | **528.88 ± 17.54** (lowest) |
| G5 | Grinding | 2 areas | 829.50 ± 76.29 |
| G6 | Grinding + Polishing | 2 areas | **999.20 ± 81.13** (highest) |
| G7 | Grinding + Reglazing | 2 areas | 657.36 ± 18.45 |

- Surface treatment effect: p = 0.0001; Extent of adjustment: p = 0.0001; Interaction: p = 0.114 (NS)
- Two-area groups had significantly higher BFS than one-area groups overall (828.7 vs. 736.3 MPa)
- G5 vs. G2 and G5 vs. G3: not statistically significant (p = 0.58, 0.17)
- G7 vs. G1: not statistically significant (p = 0.90)

## Limitations

- Single zirconia brand/formulation (Ceramill ZI) — may not generalize to other Y-TZP compositions
- Cannot fully standardize clinical grinding/polishing pressure
- No cyclic fatigue testing — monotonic strength only
- No surface characterization (XRD, SEM, Raman) to quantify monoclinic phase transformation

## Glossary

- **이축굴곡강도 (Biaxial Flexural Strength, BFS)**: 원형 디스크 중앙에 피스톤으로 하중을 가해 측정하는 굴곡강도 — ISO 6872 피스톤-온-쓰리볼 방법으로 계산
- **수열노화 (Low-Temperature Degradation, LTD)**: 습한 환경에서 지르코니아의 정방정 → 단사정 상전이가 자발적으로 진행되어 강도가 저하되는 현상
- **상전이 인성 강화 (Transformation Toughening)**: 연삭 시 발생하는 t→m 상전이에 의한 약 4% 부피 팽창이 균열 선단 주변에 압축응력층을 형성해 균열 전파를 억제하는 메커니즘
- **재유약 (Reglazing)**: 연삭 후 도재 소부로(Furnace)에서 유리질 유약층을 다시 소성하는 시술 — 미세결함은 충전하지만 반복 소성에 의한 균열 형성과 역상전이로 강도를 저하시킬 수 있음
- **의자 옆 조정 (Chairside Adjustment)**: 임상 현장에서 치과의사가 다이아몬드 버로 직접 지르코니아 보철물을 삭제·수정하는 행위
- **지르코니아 전용 연마 키트 (Zirconia Polishing Kit)**: 다이아몬드 입자 함침 실리콘 러버 버로 구성된 2단계 연마 시스템 — EVE Diacera 등
