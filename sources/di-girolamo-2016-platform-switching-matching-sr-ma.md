## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis (15 RCTs; 1,439 implants, 642 patients; English-language studies with ≥12 months follow-up post-loading) comparing marginal bone level changes around platform-switching (PS) versus platform-matching (PM) implants.

Platform-switching implants showed consistently and significantly lower marginal bone loss (MBL) compared to platform-matching implants across most included studies, with no statistically significant differences in implant failure rates between the two configurations.

Platform switching is an effective technique to limit crestal bone resorption around implants; however, heterogeneity among included studies necessitates cautious interpretation of pooled results.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석(15편 RCT; 1,439개 임플란트, 642명; 부하 후 ≥12개월 추적 영어 문헌): 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트와 플랫폼 매칭(Platform Matching, PM) 임플란트의 변연골 수준 변화를 비교.

PS 임플란트는 대부분의 포함 연구에서 PM 대비 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)이 유의하게 낮았으며, 임플란트 실패율에는 두 구성 간 통계적으로 유의한 차이가 없었음.

플랫폼 스위칭은 임플란트 주변 치조골 흡수를 효과적으로 제한하는 술식으로 확인되나, 포함 연구 간 이질성이 높아 통합 결과 해석 시 주의가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 15 RCTs included; 1439 implants, 642 patients
- PS implants: consistently lower MBL vs PM across most studies
- No significant differences in implant survival/failure rates
- Platform switching effective technique to prevent marginal bone resorption
- Heterogeneity of included studies requires cautious interpretation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Systematic review + meta-analysis. English-language RCTs only. Inclusion: ≥1 PS vs PM group, ≥12 months follow-up post-loading, ≥10 implants, quantitative MBL data provided. Outcomes: implant failure rates and marginal bone loss.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 15 publications met inclusion criteria (1439 implants, 642 patients)
- Most studies: significantly less MBL around PS implants
- No studies showed significantly worse outcomes for PS vs PM
- Implant failure rates: no statistically significant differences
- Conclusion: PS technique effectively limits crestal bone resorption

## 7. Glossary
- **PS**: Platform switching — use of smaller-diameter abutment on larger implant collar
- **PM**: Platform matching — abutment matches implant platform diameter
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss — radiographic bone level change around implant
- **TL**: Tissue-level implant — transmucosal component integral to implant
- **BL**: Bone-level implant — implant platform at crestal bone level
