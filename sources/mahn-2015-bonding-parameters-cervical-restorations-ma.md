## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective clinical trials of Class V (cervical) restorations with at least 18 months of observation (MEDLINE and SCOPUS): 81 studies, 185 experiments, 47 adhesives, covering composite resin, glass-ionomer cement (GIC), resin-modified glass ionomer (RMGIC) and polyacid-modified resin composite (PMRC), analyzed with a linear mixed model with a random study effect.

At 5 years, on average 12.3% of cervical restorations were lost, 27.9% showed marginal discoloration and 34.6% showed deteriorated marginal integrity (clinical index: 17.4% failures at 5 years, 32.3% at 8 years); rubber-dam isolation combined with roughened dentin gave significantly higher retention than unprepared dentin or no rubber dam (p < 0.05), enamel beveling had no influence, and one-step self-etch adhesives had a significantly worse clinical index than two-step self-etch (p = 0.026) and three-step etch-and-rinse (p = 0.002).

Two-step self-etch or three-step etch-and-rinse adhesives with composite resin are preferable to one-step self-etch adhesives, GIC-based materials and PMRCs for cervical lesions; this is an abstract-only record (full text not retrieved), and the isolation/dentin-roughening findings come from between-experiment comparisons rather than randomized contrasts.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상시험 기반 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis): 관찰기간 18개월 이상의 5급 (Class V) 치경부 수복물 전향적 임상시험 81편·185개 실험·접착제 47종 (복합레진, 글라스아이오노머 시멘트 (Glass-Ionomer Cement, GIC), 레진강화형 글라스아이오노머 (Resin-Modified Glass Ionomer Cement, RMGIC), 폴리애시드 변형 레진 복합체 (Polyacid-Modified Resin Composite, PMRC)), 연구 임의효과를 포함한 선형 혼합모형으로 분석.

5년 시점 평균 12.3%가 탈락 (유지 소실), 27.9%가 변연 변색, 34.6%가 변연 적합도 저하를 보였다 (임상 지수: 5년 실패율 17.4%, 8년 32.3%); 러버댐 (Rubber Dam) 격리와 상아질 조면화를 함께 시행한 군이 상아질 비처리군 또는 러버댐 미사용군보다 유지율이 유의하게 높았고 (p < 0.05), 법랑질 사면 형성 (Beveling)은 영향이 없었으며, 1단계 자가산부식 접착제 (One-step Self-etch Adhesive)는 2단계 자가산부식 (p = 0.026) 및 3단계 산부식-수세 접착제 (Etch-and-Rinse Adhesive, p = 0.002)보다 임상 지수가 유의하게 나빴다.

치경부 병소에는 1단계 자가산부식 접착제·GIC 계열·PMRC보다 2단계 자가산부식 또는 3단계 산부식-수세 접착제와 복합레진 조합이 권장된다; 초록만 확보한 기록 (전문 미확보)이며, 격리·상아질 조면화 결과는 무작위 대비가 아닌 실험 간 비교에서 나온 것이라는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pooled estimates of long-term cervical restoration deterioration: 12.3% retention loss, 27.9% marginal discoloration and 34.6% marginal-integrity deterioration at 5 years.
- Clinical index (composite failure): 17.4% at 5 years and 32.3% at 8 years.
- Identified rubber-dam isolation plus roughened dentin as the operative combination associated with significantly higher retention; enamel beveling had no effect on any outcome.
- Ranked adhesive classes: one-step self-etch performed worst (vs two-step self-etch p = 0.026; vs three-step etch-and-rinse p = 0.002).
- Secondary caries was almost absent, even beyond 8 years of follow-up.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective clinical trials.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE and SCOPUS (English literature).
- **Inclusion**: observation period at least 18 months; reporting of retention, marginal discoloration, marginal integrity and marginal caries; description of operative technique (enamel beveling, dentin roughening, type of isolation).
- **n**: 81 studies, 185 experiments, 47 adhesives.
- **Materials**: composite resin, GIC, RMGIC, PMRC.
- **Model**: linear mixed model log(-log(Y/100)) = beta + alpha log(T) + error, beta = log(lambda) as the summary measure of non-linear deterioration per experiment, with a random study effect.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: full text not retrieved, so per-adhesive estimates, heterogeneity statistics and risk-of-bias assessment are not available here.
- Operative-technique effects (isolation, dentin roughening) are inferred from between-experiment comparisons of non-randomized technique variants (inferred from the model design), so confounding by adhesive and material cannot be excluded.
- Adhesive systems are those of the literature up to 2015 (inferred from publication date); universal adhesives are not covered.

## 7. Glossary
- **Class V restoration**: restoration of a cervical (gingival-third) lesion on the facial or lingual surface.
- **Clinical index**: composite failure measure combining retention, marginal integrity and discoloration.
- **PMRC**: polyacid-modified resin composite (compomer).
- **RMGIC**: resin-modified glass-ionomer cement.
- **Dentin roughening**: mechanical roughening of dentin (e.g., bur) before bonding.
- **Beveling**: preparation of an enamel bevel at the cavity margin.
