## Why Ingested

First pilot prospective study with CKF/HD patients undergoing extraction and no antibiotic prophylaxis — fills a clinical gap alongside [[oral-surgery/fernandes-2015-immunologic-glycemic-postextraction-t2dm]] for immunocompromised patient dental management; shares Gallottini (USP) authorship and addresses analogous antibiotic prophylaxis question.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective pilot cohort with control group (48 chronic kidney failure/hemodialysis patients vs 29 controls; University of São Paulo) evaluating complications after simple tooth extraction without antibiotic prophylaxis, with assessments at days 3, 7, 21, and 60.

No postoperative infections occurred in either group; bleeding exceeding 30 minutes occurred in 18.5% of CKF/HD interventions (controlled with local hemostatics); delayed epithelialization at day 21 was significantly higher in CKF/HD patients (29.9%) vs controls (3.9%; p<0.001), but all sockets healed by day 60.

Dental extractions in CKF/HD patients can be performed safely without antibiotic prophylaxis; post-extraction bleeding is manageable with local hemostatic agents; the prolonged epithelialization timeline resolves by day 60, challenging routine prophylactic antibiotic prescription in this population.

## 세줄요약

예방적 항생제 없이 단순 발치를 시행한 만성 신부전·혈액투석(CKF/HD) 환자(48명) vs 대조군(29명)의 전향적 파일럿 코호트(상파울루대); 술 후 3·7·21·60일 평가.

양군 모두 술후 감염 없음; CKF/HD군 30분 이상 출혈 18.5%(국소 지혈제로 조절); 21일 지연 상피화(delayed epithelialization)는 CKF/HD 29.9% vs 대조 3.9%(p<0.001)로 유의하게 높았으나 60일째 전례 치유.

CKF/HD 환자의 발치는 예방적 항생제 없이도 안전하게 시행 가능하며, 출혈은 국소 지혈 처치로 조절되고 지연 상피화는 60일까지 완전히 해소됨 — 이 집단에서 일상적 항생제 처방의 근거를 재고할 필요.

## One-line Summary

Prospective pilot (48 CKF/HD vs 29 controls): no postoperative infection in either group; bleeding >30min in 18.5% of HD group (controlled with local hemostatics); delayed epithelialization at 21d in 29.9% CKF vs 3.9% control (p<0.001); all sockets healed by day 60.

## 한줄요약

전향적 파일럿 연구(말기 신부전 혈액투석 (Chronic Kidney Failure-Hemodialysis, CKF-HD) 48명 vs 대조군 29명): 양군 모두 술후 감염 없음(항생제 예방 투여 없이); CKF군 출혈 >30분 18.5%(국소 지혈 처치로 조절); 21일째 지연 상피화 CKF 29.9% vs 대조 3.9%(p<0.001); 60일째 전례 치유.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Oral Surgery, Oral Medicine, Oral Pathology, Oral Radiology, Vol. 133(2):174-181, February 2022 (published online 2021)
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.oooo.2021.08.004
- **Institution**: University of São Paulo School of Dentistry
- **Study design**: Prospective cohort with control group (pilot study)
- **n**: 48 CKF/HD (study group) + 29 controls; 87 teeth (SG) and 76 teeth (CG) extracted
- **Follow-up**: 3, 7, 21, and 60 days post-extraction
- **No antibiotic prophylaxis** administered to any participant

## 2. Key Contributions

- No postoperative infectious complications in either CKF/HD or control group (without antibiotic prophylaxis)
- Bleeding >30 minutes: 18.5% of CKF/HD interventions (12 of 65) vs 0% controls — controlled with local hemostatic agents
- Delayed epithelialization at day 21: 29.9% (26/87 teeth) in CKF vs 3.9% (3/37 teeth) in controls (p<0.001)
- All sockets epithelialized by day 60 in both groups with radiographic signs of bone neoformation
- Challenges common practice of prescribing prophylactic antibiotics in CKF/HD patients

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **CKF/HD inclusion**: Patients receiving hemodialysis; simple tooth extractions
- **Control**: Participants without CKF; comparable extractions
- **No prophylactic antibiotics**: Design explicitly tests whether antibiotics are necessary
- **Assessment**: One calibrated dentist; days 3, 7, 21, 60 post-extraction
- **Outcomes**: Hemostasis time, infection (local/distant), epithelialization, alveolar bone deposition
- **Bleeding management**: Local hemostatic agents when bleeding >30 min

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Pilot study; small sample (48 CKF/HD)
- Single center (USP Brazil); potentially not generalizable
- No standardized HbA1c-equivalent measure for kidney disease severity
- No subgroup by dialysis duration, platelet function, or anticoagulant use
- Larger confirmatory studies needed

## 6. Related Work

- Fernandes 2015: Similar prospective case-control (T2DM); same conclusion — no infection without antibiotics
- Mozzati 2013: PRGF in diabetic socket (similar healing endpoint comparison)
- Dinkova 2025: Local hemostasis in oral surgery — broader review applicable to bleeding management in CKF

## 7. Glossary

- **CKF (Chronic Kidney Failure, 만성 신부전)**: irreversible progressive loss of renal function requiring dialysis or transplant
- **HD (Hemodialysis, 혈액투석)**: extracorporeal blood purification; creates bleeding risk via anticoagulants and platelet dysfunction
- **Delayed epithelialization (지연 상피화)**: incomplete socket mucosa closure beyond expected timeframe (21 days)
- **BMD-CKF (골무기질대사장애)**: bone mineral disorder in CKF — affects calcium/phosphate/PTH; impact on socket healing unclear
