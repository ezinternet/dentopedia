## Why Ingested

The wiki holds the innervation map of the retrodiscal tissue (bilaminar zone) via [[wiki/tmj/alfaro-2025-sensory-fiber-types-mouse-tmj]] but nothing on how that tissue changes measurably in disc-displacement patients. This cross-sectional T2-mapping study quantifies retrodiscal-tissue degeneration in vivo and — the novel point — shows the *contralateral, clinically unaffected* joint already carries a biochemical signal, extending the bilateral-unit concept in [[wiki/tmj/zheng-2023-unilateral-chewing-tmj-structure-tmd-review]] and adding an imaging-biomarker angle to the disc-displacement management picture in [[wiki/tmj/sa-2024-conservative-invasive-tmd-disc-displacement-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional 1.5T MRI T2-mapping study of 65 unilateral anterior disc displacement (uADD) patients (15 with reduction, 50 without) vs 17 healthy volunteers, measuring T2 relaxation time of the articular disc and retrodiscal tissue (bilaminar zone + superior + inferior attachment ROIs). Retrodiscal-tissue T2 was elevated on both the affected side (41.6 ± 7.1 ms) and the *unaffected contralateral* side (37.8 ± 5.8 ms) vs volunteers (34.4 ± 3.2 ms, p < 0.001); disc displacement without reduction (uADDWOR) drove almost all of the effect (affected 43.3 ms), while the disc itself changed only on the affected side (31.5 vs 28.9 ms). In a subgroup with disc displacement but no joint effusion/osteoarthritis/bone-marrow edema (n = 8), no T2 difference vs volunteers was seen — displacement alone is a weak driver; concomitant degenerative MR findings dominate the biochemical signal.

## 세줄요약

편측 전방 원판 변위 (unilateral anterior disc displacement, uADD) 환자 65명(정복성 15, 비정복성 50)과 정상지원자 17명을 1.5T MRI T2 매핑 (T2 mapping)으로 비교한 단면연구로, 관절원판과 후방조직 (retrodiscal tissue, 이중판대 (bilaminar zone)·상방·하방 부착부 ROI)의 T2 이완시간을 측정했다. 후방조직 T2는 환측(41.6 ± 7.1 ms)뿐 아니라 증상이 없는 반대측(37.8 ± 5.8 ms)에서도 정상지원자(34.4 ± 3.2 ms)보다 유의하게 길었고(p < 0.001), 이 효과는 거의 전적으로 비정복성 원판 변위 (ADDwoR) 군에서 나왔다. 관절 삼출·골관절염·골수부종이 없는 원판 변위 하위군(n = 8)에서는 정상지원자와 T2 차이가 없어 — 원판 변위 단독은 약한 유발인자이고, 동반된 퇴행성 MRI 소견이 생화학적 신호를 지배한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First quantitative T2 comparison between affected and unaffected sides** of the articular disc and retrodiscal tissue in unilateral disc displacement — prior work only compared TMD patients vs volunteers, not within-patient sides.
2. **Contralateral (clinically unaffected) retrodiscal tissue already shows elevated T2** in uADDWOR patients — biochemical evidence the two TMJs behave as one mechanical unit and that "unilateral" disc displacement is rarely biochemically unilateral.
3. **Retrodiscal tissue is the more sensitive biomarker than the disc** — the retrodisc showed a contralateral effect the disc did not, attributed to its high vascularity, easy stretch, and elasticity loss making it responsive to fluid-content change.
4. **Displacement alone is a weak T2 driver** — the no-JE/OA/BE subgroup showed no T2 elevation; joint effusion, osteoarthritis, and bone-marrow edema account for most of the signal.
5. **Reduction status matters** — uADDWOR had markedly higher T2 than uADDWR across disc and retrodiscal tissue, consistent with ADDWOR's established association with degeneration.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Population**: From 260 consecutive TMD patients (2009–2016), 65 met uADD criteria (NorSup disc one side + ADDwR or ADDwoR the other); partial displacement (PADDwR/PADDwoR) excluded. 55 F / 10 M, median age 43 (14–77). Controls: 17 volunteers (5 F / 12 M, median age 26), bilateral normal disc position confirmed by MRI (34 joints).
- **Scanner**: 1.5T Signa HDxt (GE Healthcare) with TMJ surface coil. Diagnostic protocol: oblique sagittal + coronal FSE-PDWI, fat-suppressed FSE-T2WI, closed and open mouth.
- **T2 mapping**: oblique sagittal FSE, closed mouth, 8 echo times (8.9–71.3 ms), 5 min 22 s per joint; T2 computed with Functool software.
- **ROIs** (2 blinded oral-maxillofacial radiologists, 20 y and 12 y experience): 1 disc ROI + 3 retrodiscal-tissue ROIs (**bilaminar zone, superior attachment, inferior attachment**). Reliability: disc intra-rater CV 1.1–4.7%, inter-rater ICC 0.862–0.891; retrodiscal tissue CV 1.5–5.5%, ICC 0.818.
- **MR feature grading**: disc position (Tasaki), joint effusion (Larheim, 4 grades), osteoarthritis (Kirk, osteophyte/erosion +/–), bone-marrow edema/osteonecrosis (Larheim).
- **Statistics**: linear regression with generalized estimating equations (GEE) for within-patient correlation; multivariable adjustment for age and sex; p < 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Articular disc T2 (ms)

| Group | Volunteers | Unaffected side | Affected side |
|---|---|---|---|
| All uADD (n=65) | 29.3 ± 3.8 | 28.9 ± 4.5 | 31.5 ± 3.8 (vs unaffected p<0.001; vs healthy p=0.014) |
| uADDWR (n=15) | 29.3 ± 3.8 | 28.5 ± 5.5 | 29.6 ± 4.9 (ns) |
| uADDWOR (n=50) | 29.3 ± 3.8 | 29.0 ± 4.1 | 32.0 ± 4.4 (p<0.001 vs unaffected) |
| No JE/OA/BE (n=8) | 29.3 ± 3.8 | 29.5 ± 6.1 | 29.3 ± 2.6 (ns) |

### Retrodiscal tissue T2 (ms)

| Group | Volunteers | Unaffected side | Affected side |
|---|---|---|---|
| All uADD (n=65) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 37.8 ± 5.8 (p<0.001 vs healthy) | 41.6 ± 7.1 (p<0.001 vs unaffected & healthy) |
| uADDWR (n=15) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 33.9 ± 3.8 | 35.8 ± 4.4 |
| uADDWOR (n=50) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 38.9 ± 5.8 (p<0.001 vs healthy & vs uADDWR unaffected) | 43.3 ± 6.8 (p<0.001 vs all other groups) |
| No JE/OA/BE (n=8) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 35.5 ± 3.9 | 34.9 ± 4.5 (ns) |

- After age/sex adjustment, the affected-vs-unaffected retrodiscal difference held (p<0.001); the unaffected-vs-volunteer difference lost significance in the all-uADD and no-JE/OA/BE analyses but the core uADDWOR findings were unchanged.
- Longer T2 = more mobile water / disrupted collagen alignment = degeneration; ADDWOR consistently highest.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- 1.5T scanner (3.0T preferred for small-joint TMJ imaging, better SNR); fixed 8-echo vendor protocol.
- Age/sex imbalance: patients older and more female; controls younger and more male (partially handled by multivariable adjustment).
- Cross-sectional — no data on symptom onset/duration or disease chronicity; cannot establish that retrodiscal T2 change precedes disc change (only inferred).
- Retrospective convenience sample; small no-JE/OA/BE subgroup (n=8) underpowered.
- No histological correlation of T2 values.

## 7. Glossary

- **Bilaminar zone**: the retrodiscal tissue proper — a two-layered elastic/collagenous structure posterior to the articular disc (superior lamina to the temporal bone, inferior lamina to the condyle) with loose, highly vascular, innervated connective tissue between; the "retrodiscal pad" or "posterior attachment"
- **Retrodiscal tissue (RDT) / retrodisc**: umbrella term for the bilaminar zone plus its superior and inferior attachments
- **T2 value / T2 relaxation time**: quantitative MR parameter (ms) from multi-echo spin-echo; rises with free/mobile water content and with loss of collagen-fiber organization — a marker of early degeneration before morphology changes
- **uADD**: unilateral anterior disc displacement; **uADDWR** with reduction (disc recaptures on opening), **uADDWOR** without reduction (disc stays displaced, "closed lock")
- **JE / OA / BE**: joint effusion / osteoarthritis / bone-marrow edema-osteonecrosis — the concomitant MR findings that, more than displacement itself, drive T2 elevation
- **GEE (generalized estimating equations)**: regression method accounting for correlation between the two joints of the same patient
