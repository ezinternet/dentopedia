## Three-line Summary

This critical review surveys current evidence on adhesive approaches for restoring endodontically treated teeth (ETT) with and without extensive coronal tissue loss, examining partial versus full coverage, ferrule effect, post type influence on catastrophic failures, and postless alternatives including endocrowns and postless build-ups.

Preservation of remaining tooth structure is paramount; the presence of a 1.5-2 mm ferrule improves fracture resistance, while partial restorations show comparable survival to full crowns (94-100% vs 91.9-100%), and randomized trials with PFM crowns demonstrate post placement improves retention but adhesive approaches with bonded ceramics may render posts unnecessary.

Clinical evidence remains contradictory: in vitro studies favor postless approaches with bonded ceramic crowns to reduce catastrophic failures, but RCTs using PFM crowns show higher success with posts (84% cast vs 95% fiber post over 4 years), highlighting that restoration type (cemented vs bonded) critically influences outcomes and more clinical studies on modern adhesive postless techniques are needed.

## 세줄요약

본 종설은 근관치료된 치아 (Endodontically Treated Teeth, ETT)의 접착 수복에 관한 현재 지식을 조사하였으며, 부분 피개 대 완전 피개, 페룰 효과, 포스트 종류가 치명적 파절에 미치는 영향, 엔도크라운 및 포스트 없는 코어 축성 (postless build-up)과 같은 무포스트 대안을 검토하였다.

잔존 치아 구조의 보존이 가장 중요하며, 1.5-2 mm 페룰의 존재는 파절 저항성을 향상시키고, 부분 수복물은 완전관과 유사한 생존율을 보였으며 (94-100% 대 91.9-100%), PFM 크라운을 사용한 무작위 임상시험에서는 포스트 식립이 유지력을 향상시켰으나 접착성 세라믹을 사용한 접근법에서는 포스트가 불필요할 수 있음을 시사하였다.

임상 근거는 여전히 상반된데, 시험관 연구는 접착성 세라믹 크라운과 무포스트 접근법이 치명적 파절을 감소시킨다고 보는 반면, PFM 크라운을 사용한 RCT는 포스트 식립이 더 높은 성공률을 보였으며 (4년간 주조포스트 84% 대 섬유포스트 95%), 이는 수복물 유형 (시멘트형 대 접착형)이 결과에 결정적 영향을 미침을 강조하며, 현대 접착 무포스트 기법에 관한 추가 임상 연구가 필요함을 시사한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Preservation of coronal tooth structure is paramount for ETT survival; 1.5-2 mm ferrule significantly improves fracture resistance and lowers impact of post-and-core systems.
- Partial adhesive restorations (direct composite, indirect onlays) show comparable survival to full crowns (94-100% vs 91.9-100%) for ETT with minimal to moderate tissue loss, challenging the need for full crown preparation.
- Post placement does not improve fatigue resistance of posterior teeth with ferrule and is associated with more catastrophic root fractures in vitro; postless approaches show more favorable failure modes.
- Endocrowns represent a promising conservative alternative for posterior ETT, with 90-95% survival in short-term reports and up to 99% success over 44.7 months for lithium disilicate.
- Clinical evidence is contradictory: RCTs with PFM crowns show posts improve survival (84% cast vs 95% fiber post at 4 years; up to 50% crown dislodgment without posts), but studies with bonded ceramic crowns favor postless approaches.
- Restoration type (cemented PFM vs bonded all-ceramic) critically influences stress distribution and outcomes; bonded restorations may eliminate the need for posts when proper adhesion (IDS) is applied.
- Immediate dentin sealing (IDS) is essential for optimizing bond strength in postless approaches; all postless studies showing high success used IDS protocols.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Critical narrative review surveying current knowledge on adhesive approaches for ETT restoration.
- Scope: Examines partial vs full coverage, ferrule effect, post type and catastrophic failures, and postless alternatives (endocrowns, postless build-ups).
- Evidence synthesis: Draws from in vitro studies, finite element analyses, prospective and retrospective clinical studies, randomized clinical trials, systematic reviews, and meta-analyses; no formal systematic search or PRISMA protocol described.
- Included evidence: References cover laboratory studies, computer simulations, clinical trials (RCTs, cohort studies), and systematic reviews/meta-analyses published primarily 1989-2018.
- Outcomes assessed: Fracture resistance, survival rates, failure modes (catastrophic vs repairable), fatigue resistance, retention, stress distribution.
- Statistical considerations: Reports findings from included studies with p-values, confidence intervals, I², and heterogeneity assessments as originally reported; no new meta-analysis performed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Partial restorations vs full crowns —** Pooled survival rates: crowns 94-100%, resin composite 91.9-100% (no significant difference, heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis)
- **Ferrule effect —** Systematic review: 1.5-2 mm ferrule positively affects fracture resistance; meta-analysis: no significant difference in failure risk with/without ferrule (lack of clinical data, only 2 studies included)
- **Post type comparison (4-year RCT) —** Cast posts: 84% success; glass-fiber posts: 95% success; root fractures and crown dislodgment only in cast post group
- **Post vs no-post (PFM crowns, RCTs) —** Postless build-ups: up to 50% crown dislodgment; post placement improved retention and survival
- **Endocrowns —** Short-term survival: 90-95% (posterior teeth); 44.7-month follow-up: lithium disilicate 99.0%, polymer-infiltrated ceramic 89.9% success
- **Systematic review on posts (14 studies) —** 10/14 studies showed no positive effect of post placement; post advocated only when no cavity wall present
- **Meta-analysis (13 in vitro) —** Cast posts significantly higher fracture resistance than glass-fiber posts; root fracture rates similar between metal and fiber posts (7 RCTs + 7 cohort studies)
- **Failure modes —** Metal posts: higher root fracture risk; fiber posts: higher post/crown/core loss (more repairable)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Lack of high-quality clinical trials comparing adhesive crowns bonded to postless build-ups vs post-and-core build-ups with modern adhesive techniques
- Heterogeneity of clinical studies prevents meta-analysis for partial vs full coverage comparisons
- Most in vitro studies use bonded all-ceramic crowns, while clinical RCTs predominantly use cemented PFM crowns — direct comparison limited
- No clinical trial evidence for anterior endocrowns (only finite element and one in vitro study)
- Short follow-up periods in most endocrown studies; long-term data lacking
- Evidence on post necessity remains inconclusive; current systematic reviews limited by small number of included studies (e.g., ferrule meta-analysis only 2 studies)
- Need for randomized clinical trials specifically evaluating adhesive postless techniques with bonded ceramic restorations to confirm in vitro findings

## 6. Related Work

- Juloski et al. 2012 — Systematic review confirming 1.5-2 mm ferrule positively affects fracture resistance and lowers impact of post-and-core systems
- Magne et al. 2017 — In vitro study showing ferrule effect dominates over fiber post use when restoring endodontically treated incisors
- Lazari et al. 2018 — In vitro study on survival of extensively damaged incisors restored with different post-and-core materials; postless approaches showed more favorable failure modes
- Skupien et al. 2016 — First RCT including all tooth types and severely broken-down teeth comparing PFM crowns vs composite restorations on ETT (annual failure rate 0.92%, no difference between types)
- Ferrari et al. 2012, Guldener et al. 2017 — RCTs showing PFM crowns with posts have better survival than postless build-ups (crown dislodgment common without posts)
- Belleflamme et al. 2017 — 10-year retrospective study of endocrowns: 99.0% success for lithium disilicate, 89.9% for polymer-infiltrated ceramic at 44.7 months
- Figueiredo et al. 2015 — Meta-analysis: metal posts result in more root fractures than fiber posts
- Naumann et al. 2018 — Systematic review concluding no unequivocal evidence to support or reject posts even in no-ferrule situations

## 7. Glossary

- **ETT (Endodontically Treated Teeth)**: 근관치료된 치아
- **Ferrule**: 치관부 수직 잔존 치아 구조물 (보통 1.5-2 mm 높이), 크라운 변연부에서 치근을 감싸는 치질
- **PFM (Porcelain Fused to Metal)**: 도재-금속 융합 크라운
- **IDS (Immediate Dentin Sealing)**: 즉시 상아질 봉쇄술 — 간접 수복물의 접착 강도를 높이기 위한 기법
- **Endocrown**: 치수강을 크라운 자체의 연장으로 사용하는 포스트 없는 수복 기법 (monobloc)
- **Catastrophic failure**: 치아/치근 파절로 발치가 필요한 치명적 실패
- **Monobloc**: 크라운과 코어 축성이 단일 구조물로 결합된 수복물 (예: endocrown)
- **Postless build-up**: 근관 내 포스트 없이 잔존 치관부에만 접착성 레진 코어를 축성하는 기법
