## Why Ingested

전해질 세정(electrolytic cleaning)은 임플란트 표면 오염 제거를 위한 비화학적·비기계적 보조 수단으로 임상에 도입되고 있으나, 재건적 수술에서의 추가 효과는 불분명했음. 기존 [[implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2025-surface-decontamination-reconstructive-peri-implantitis]] 등 표면 오염 제거 RCT와 직접 비교 가능한 negative RCT로, 임상 결정에 중요한 음성 근거를 제공.

## One-line Summary

RCT (Clin Oral Implants Res 2026): 44 implants with peri-implantitis intra-bony defects; adjunctive electrolytic cleaning (ElectroMed) on top of titanium brush + GBR provided no significant additional clinical, radiographic, or patient-reported benefit over titanium brush + GBR alone at 12 months.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First adequately powered RCT to isolate the adjunctive effect of electrolytic cleaning during reconstructive peri-implantitis surgery
- Demonstrates titanium brush alone + GBR achieves favorable outcomes at 12 months (clinical/radiographic/PRO)
- Electrolytic cleaning showed no added benefit; post-surgical complications occurred exclusively in test group
- Primary composite success rate numerically higher in test group (40% vs 27%) but not significant (p=0.41), showing the adjunct does not reliably improve outcomes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Study type: Single-center parallel-arm RCT
- Participants: 40 patients, 44 implants with peri-implantitis-associated intra-bony defects
- Control: Rotating titanium brush decontamination + xenogeneic bone substitute + resorbable collagen membrane
- Test: Rotating titanium brush + adjunctive electrolytic cleaning (ElectroMed device) + xenogeneic bone substitute + resorbable collagen membrane
- Follow-up: 6 months and 12 months
- Primary outcome: Composite (implant survival + complete absence of BoP/SoP + PPD ≤5mm + REC ≤1mm) at 12 months
- Secondary: PPD, BoP/SoP, REC, keratinized mucosa, radiographic MBL, PROs

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Primary composite (12 months):**
- Control: 27.3% met composite outcome
- Test: 40.0% met composite outcome
- p = 0.41 — not significant

**PPD reduction (mean ± SD):**
- Control: 4.5 ± 2.6 mm
- Test: 3.8 ± 2.3 mm
- No significant intergroup difference

**MBL gain (mean ± SD):**
- Control: 1.9 ± 2.4 mm
- Test: 2.1 ± 2.0 mm
- No significant intergroup difference

**Implant survival:** 100% in both groups at 12 months

**Post-surgical complications:** Occurred only in test group (electrolytic cleaning arm)

**PROs:** No significant intergroup differences

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center study; limited sample size (44 implants)
- 12-month follow-up only; longer-term effects unknown
- Operator-dependent use of electrolytic cleaning device; learning curve not controlled
- GBR augmentation performed in both groups — results specific to reconstructive (not resective) approach
- Device-specific findings (ElectroMed) may not generalize to other electrolytic systems

## 7. Glossary

- **ElectroMed**: Electrolytic cleaning device using galvanic current to decontaminate implant surfaces
- **GBR**: Guided bone regeneration — xenogeneic bone substitute + resorbable collagen membrane
- **BoP/SoP**: Bleeding on probing / Suppuration on probing
- **Composite outcome**: All four criteria must be met simultaneously (survival + BoP/SoP absent + PPD ≤5mm + REC ≤1mm)
- **PROs**: Patient-reported outcomes
