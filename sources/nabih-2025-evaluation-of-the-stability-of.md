## Three-line Summary
Double-centred, double-blinded randomized controlled trial (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; n=22, one maxillary single-tooth site each, D3/D4 bone, buccolingual width ≥4 mm) comparing the 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD) technique (Versah Densah burs, counterclockwise 800–1200 rpm) against the HaeNaem expander kit ((주)해냄, Pyeongtaek, Korea; 2.6/3.2/3.6 mm, 50 rpm + ratchet wrench).

Implant stability showed no significant intergroup difference at baseline (75.27 vs 78.64, P=0.2247) or 3 months (73.10 vs 73.00, P=0.9770), but a significant 6-month difference in favour of OD (79.60 vs 72.10, P=0.0268); ridge width at 2, 4 and 8 mm levels favoured OD at 6 months (P=0.0002/0.0004/0.0096); pain was equivalent; 3 implants failed (87% retention).

OD appears effective for expanding narrow low-quality ridges and improving implant stability with minimal postoperative pain, but the groups differed significantly in preoperative crestal ridge width and follow-up was short (6 months) — the stability advantage may reflect baseline ridge-width disparity; authors suggest expanders may suit moderate density (D2–D3) with adequate width.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First held RCT directly comparing OD against ridge expanders for implant placement in D3/D4 low-density bone — the OD-vs-expansion axis is new to the wiki (held OD evidence compares against standard drilling).
- Documents the HaeNaem expander kit ((주)해냄, Pyeongtaek-si, Korea) in a prospective double-blinded control arm — one of the first non-retracted clinical uses of a HaeNaem product held in the wiki (OD arm used Versah LLC Densah burs; implants were Roott/TRATE AG).
- Reports secondary-stability superiority for OD at 6 months (ISQ 79.60±6.36 vs 72.10±7.50, P=0.0268) over expanders, while primary stability (75.27 vs 78.64, P=0.2247) and 3-month stability (73.10 vs 73.00, P=0.9770) were equivalent.
- Quantifies ridge-width gain via CBCT at three vertical levels (2, 4, 8 mm) from the implant platform, showing OD favoured at all levels at 6 months (2 mm: 7.73 vs 5.47, P=0.0002; 4 mm: 8.41 vs 6.65, P=0.0004; 8 mm: 8.85 vs 7.19, P=0.0096); claims to be the first study assessing ridge width at multiple vertical levels.
- Adds prospective pain (VAS; baseline 3.73 vs 4.82, P=0.1480, ≈0 by 1 week in both) and 87% retention data (2 expander-group failures at 3 months, 1 OD-group failure post-loading).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: double-centred, prospective, double-blinded (participants + outcome evaluator), randomized, parallel, two-arm clinical trial (RCT).
- **n**: 22 patients (11 per group), one missing upper tooth each.
- **Bone quality**: D3 (350–850 HU) or D4 (150–350 HU); residual buccolingual bone width ≥4 mm; interocclusal space 8–10 mm.
- **Test group (OD)**: sequential Densah burs (Versah, LLC, Jackson, Michigan, USA), counterclockwise, 800–1200 rpm, sized to ridge width.
- **Control group (expanders)**: HaeNaem expander kit ((주)해냄, 194 Jisan-ro 175beon-gil, Jinwi-myeon, Pyeongtaek-si, Gyeonggi-do, Korea) — bone expanders of increasing diameter (2.6, 3.2, 3.6) mm at 50 rpm with electric handpiece, completing with ratchet wrench; instruments inserted in intervals to let bone expand.
- **Implants**: Roott dental implants (TRATE AG, Bahnhofstrasse 16, 6037 Root, Switzerland).
- **Outcomes**: primary = implant stability (primary stability at placement; secondary stability at 3 and 6 months, via Osstell ISQ); secondary = pain outcome (PO) on 10 cm VAS at surgery day and 1 week; ridge width on CBCT (90 kVp, 12 mA, 5×8 cm FOV, 1 mm slice) at 2, 4, 8 mm from the implant platform pre-op, at baseline (immediately post-op), 3 and 6 months.
- **Allocation**: computer-generated (randomizer.org), 1:1, sequentially numbered opaque sealed envelopes opened by the supervisor after local anaesthesia; principal investigator not blinded.
- **Statistics**: Shapiro–Wilk/Kolmogorov–Smirnov normality; independent t-tests between groups; repeated-measures ANOVA with Tukey post hoc + Bonferroni within groups; two-tailed, α=0.05; MedCalc v22.
- **Recruitment**: September 2022 – January 2024, Cairo University & Future University in Egypt.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Implant stability (ISQ, Table 2)**:
  - Baseline: OD 75.27 ± 4.15 vs expanders 78.64 ± 7.88; difference 3.36 (95% CI −2.23 to 8.96); **P=0.2247** (NS).
  - 3 months: OD 73.10 ± 7.94 vs 73.00 ± 7.73; difference −0.10 (95% CI −7.26 to 7.06); **P=0.9770** (NS).
  - 6 months: OD 79.60 ± 6.36 vs 72.10 ± 7.50; difference −7.50 (95% CI −14.03 to −0.96); **P=0.0268*** favouring OD.
  - Intragroup over time: OD P=0.053 (NS), expanders P=0.166 (NS).
- **Pain (VAS, Table 3)**: baseline OD 3.73 ± 1.35 vs expanders 4.82 ± 1.99; difference 1.09 (95% CI −0.42 to 2.60); **P=0.1480** (NS). 1 week: 0.00 ± 0.00 in both groups (P=1.0000). Intragroup pain fell significantly in both groups (P<0.0001).
- **Ridge width at 2 mm (Table 4)** (mm):
  - Preoperative: OD 6.63 ± 0.93 vs 5.60 ± 0.96, **P=0.0193*** (groups differed at baseline).
  - Baseline: 7.74 ± 0.91 vs 6.76 ± 0.92, **P=0.0213***; 3 months: 7.64 ± 0.85 vs 5.78 ± 0.98, **P=0.0003***; 6 months: 7.73 ± 1.18 vs 5.47 ± 0.87, **P=0.0002*** — all favouring OD.
- **Ridge width at 4 mm (Table 5)** (mm):
  - Preoperative: 7.40 ± 0.95 vs 6.89 ± 1.01, P=0.2346 (NS); baseline: 8.83 ± 1.12 vs 7.56 ± 0.76, **P=0.0055***; 3 months: 8.72 ± 1.15 vs 6.73 ± 0.64, **P=0.0002***; 6 months: 8.41 ± 1.00 vs 6.65 ± 0.68, **P=0.0004*** — favouring OD.
- **Ridge width at 8 mm (Table 6)** (mm):
  - Preoperative: 7.88 ± 1.34 vs 7.34 ± 0.97, P=0.2942 (NS); baseline: 9.35 ± 1.44 vs 7.93 ± 0.64, **P=0.0072***; 3 months: 9.26 ± 1.43 vs 7.50 ± 0.74, **P=0.0029***; 6 months: 8.85 ± 1.53 vs 7.19 ± 0.77, **P=0.0096*** — favouring OD.
  - Intragroup: 2/4 mm — OD maintained expansions to 6 months while expanders returned to ~preoperative width by 3–6 months; 8 mm — OD gain was significant at baseline/3 months only.
- **Retention**: 3 implants failed in 6 months — 2 in the expander group (at 3 months, implant mobility; two additional D4 cases excluded after buccal cortex fracture during expander use) and 1 in the OD group (post-loading, before 6 months) — an 87% retention rate.
- **Baseline characteristics**: mean age 35.00 ± 8.7 years; groups comparable in age (P=0.22) and sex (P=0.67). OD group 5M/6F; expanders 4M/7F.
- **Discussion anchor**: Aboelnaga et al. [17] reported similar 6-month ISQ superiority for OD (86.40 ± 3.50) over expansion (69.30 ± 2.58).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Significant preoperative difference in crestal ridge width at 2 mm between groups (6.63 vs 5.60 mm, P=0.0193) — OD group started wider, confounding both the ridge-width and possibly stability comparisons; authors flag this and call for stratification/statistical adjustment in future work.
- Short follow-up (6 months); no long-term crestal bone stability or survival data.
- Sample size small (n=22); powered at 80% for the expander-secondary-stability mean from a prior study.
- Pain was the only patient-reported outcome — oedema, function, and satisfaction not assessed.
- Failures (2 expander 3-month mobility, 1 OD post-loading) plus 2 excluded D4 buccal-cortex fractures during expander use were attributed by the authors to high insertion torque and/or systemic factors (e.g. vitamin D deficiency) rather than technique alone.
- Principal investigator was not blinded to the treatment protocol (procedures inherently differ).
- Factors outside the osteotomy technique (implant diameter 3.8/4.2 mm, length 10/12 mm) varied by site; expander thread design may be excessive for D4 bone.

## 6. Related Work
- mello-machado-2021: double-blind RCT of OD vs standard undersized drilling in Misch D3/D4 — found higher insertion torque but equivalent ISQ and survival with OD; nabih-2025 extends this to the OD-vs-ridge-expander comparator (and here finds a 6-month ISQ advantage for OD).
- koutouzis-2019: multicentre retrospective on OD for alveolar ridge expansion — the expansion-by-density mechanism nabih-2025 tests prospectively; koutouzis reported ~2.83 mm ridge-width gain and 92.8% survival with OD alone on narrow ridges.
- manekar-2022: systematic review of modern ridge split/expansion devices — places the HaeNaem expander comparator used here in the wider device literature.
- kim-2019 (double-spiral condensing screw patent, HaeNaem (주)해냄): inventor-asserted densifying screw from the same Korean manufacturer whose expander kit was used in the control arm here — this RCT is its first non-retracted clinical companion.
- aboelnaga et al. [17] (cited in the paper, not held): reported 6-month ISQ OD 86.40 ± 3.50 vs expansion 69.30 ± 2.58; basis for this trial's sample-size calculation and its main discussion anchor.
- changrani-2024 (retracted): prior clinical study of the HaeNaem Zero Bone Loss Kit; retracted, non-citable — prose mention only, no typed edge.

## 7. Glossary
- **골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD)**: rotary instrumentation in the non-cutting (counterclockwise) direction intended to compact bone trabeculae against the osteotomy wall while autografting bone particles.
- **치조제 확장기 (Ridge Expander)**: sequential condensing screws/broaches of increasing diameter that widen a narrow ridge by lateral bone compression rather than cutting. This trial's control used the HaeNaem expander kit ((주)해냄, Pyeongtaek-si, Korea; 2.6/3.2/3.6 mm diameters).
- **임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)**: Osstell resonance-frequency-derived unit (0–100) of implant stability; higher = more stable.
- **일차 안정성 (Primary stability)**: mechanical anchorage at placement; **이차 안정성 (Secondary stability)**: stability at follow-up after biologic osseointegration.
- **D3/D4 bone**: (Hounsfield units — HU) D3 = 350–850 HU, D4 = 150–350 HU (as defined in the paper).
- **시각통증척도 (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS)**: 10 cm pain scale, 0 = no pain, 10 = worst pain.
