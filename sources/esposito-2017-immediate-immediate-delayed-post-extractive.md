## Why Ingested

Part of the implant-iip-loading surveillance batch, found by manual PDF discovery. 1-year post-loading maturation of the same 210-patient trial reported at 4 months in [[wiki/immediate-implant/felice-2016-immediate-immediate-delayed-post-extractive]] — ingested together so the two follow-up horizons of one trial stay linked rather than read as independent studies.

## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=210, 70 per arm) randomising single post-extraction implant sites to immediate placement, immediate-delayed placement (6 weeks), or delayed placement (4 months), reporting outcomes at 1 year post-loading (same cohort as the 4-month interim report).

Implant failure: 4/70 (6%) immediate, 4/70 (6.2%) immediate-delayed, 1/70 (1.6%) delayed (P=.369, NS, unchanged from the 4-month data — no new failures in the interim); marginal bone loss -0.25/-0.29/-0.31mm (P=.015); PES 12.52/12.49/11.78 (P<.001), same direction as at 4 months.

At 1 year, the trade-off pattern first seen at 4 months persists essentially unchanged: no statistically significant difference in failures or complications across the three placement timings, but immediate and immediate-delayed placement retain a statistically significant bone-level and esthetic advantage over delayed placement.

## Key Contributions
- Confirms at 1-year maturity that the 4-month interim trade-off pattern (higher-but-NS failure risk, better bone/esthetics for earlier placement) is stable, not a transient artifact
- No new implant failures occurred between the 4-month and 1-year assessments in any arm

## Methodology
Same trial as the 4-month interim report (see companion source): 210 patients randomised 70/70/70 to immediate, immediate-delayed (6-week), or delayed (4-month) placement, same grafting/loading protocol. This report: 1-year post-loading follow-up.

## Key Results
| Outcome | Immediate (n=70) | Immediate-delayed 6wk (n=70) | Delayed 4mo (n=70) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures | 4 (6%) | 4 (6.2%) | 1 (1.6%) | .369 |
| Complications (patients) | 6 | 6 | 4 | .792 |
| Marginal bone loss (1yr) | -0.25 ± 0.17mm | -0.29 ± 0.14mm | -0.31 ± 0.16mm | .015 |
| PES at 1 year | 12.52 | 12.49 | 11.78 | <.001 |
