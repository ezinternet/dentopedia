## Why Ingested

The wiki had no page on ultra-wide (≥6 mm) implants despite them being the standard rescue option when a narrow/standard fixture fails to gain stability or bone quality is D3/D4 — this 5-year cohort supplies that baseline and shows the failure is concentrated in borderline primary stability → [[implants/short-narrow/witek-2021-surgical-instrumentation-narrow-wide-short-implants]] and [[immediate-implant/checchi-2017-wide-diameter-immediate-post-extractive]].

## Three-line Summary

Five-year retrospective cohort (J Korean Assoc Oral Maxillofac Surg 2023;49:13-20, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital; 78 ultra-wide SuperLine implants, 6 or 7 mm diameter, in 71 patients placed 2008-2010; mean observation 97.8 months).

Survival was 92.3% and success 83.3%, with 13 failures; MBL averaged 0.2 mm at 1 year and 0.54 mm at final observation, and neither MBL nor most variables predicted failure.

The only significant predictors were stability at placement and at second stage — primary ISQ <60 dropped success to 61% (vs 100% at 60-70) and secondary ISQ 71+ raised survival to 100% — supporting ultra-wide implants as a salvage option in posterior poor-quality bone provided adequate primary stability is achieved.

## 세줄요약

5년 후향 코호트 (대한구강악안면외과학회지 2023;49:13-20, 분당서울대; 2008-2010년 식립된 직경 6·7 mm 초광폭 (Ultra-wide) SuperLine 임플란트 78개, 환자 71명, 평균 관찰 97.8개월).

생존율 92.3%, 성공률 83.3% (실패 13개); 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 부하 1년 0.2 mm, 최종 0.54 mm였고 MBL과 대부분 변수는 실패를 예측하지 못함.

유의한 예측인자는 식립 시·2차 수술 시 안정성뿐 — 초기 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) <60에서 성공률 61%(60-70은 100%), 2차 ISQ ≥71에서 생존율 100% → 초광폭 임플란트는 초기 고정만 확보되면 후방부 불량 골질·재식립 대안으로 유효.

- **Study type:** retrospective (5-year cohort)

## Key Contributions
- Establishes 5-year benchmarks for ≥6 mm diameter implants in posterior sites: survival 92.3%, success 83.3%, MBL 0.54 mm at ~8 years.
- Shows diameter (6 vs 7 mm), length, arch, sex, age and adjunctive surgery were all non-predictive — the wide-implant outcome is not a design-parameter story.
- Identifies primary stability as the success determinant (ISQ <60 → 61% success) and secondary stability as the survival determinant (ISQ ≥71 → 100% survival).
- Documents the "unstable start recovered later" pattern: the 6-70 ISQ band reached 100% success and survival.
- Details failure modes: 4 removals for mobility, 2 for radiolucency, 2 for ≥0.2 mm annual MBL, 4 for ≥1.5 mm first-year MBL, 1 for peri-implant inflammation/bleeding.

## Methodology
Retrospective chart and radiograph review of 78 ultra-wide implants placed in 71 patients (39 male, 32 female; mean age 54.2 years) between 2008 and 2010 at a single institution, followed a mean of 97.8 months.

Variables tested against 5-year survival, success and MBL: sex, age, site, diameter, length, adjunctive surgery (sinus lift, GBR, both), primary and secondary ISQ, and reason for choosing an ultra-wide implant (poor bone quality vs failed initial fixation of a ≤5 mm implant vs replacement after failed implant removal).

Statistics: one-way ANOVA and independent-samples t-tests for MBL; Kaplan-Meier for survival/success; comparisons by variable as tabulated.

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean observation | 97.8 months |
| Survival | 92.3% |
| Success | 83.3% |
| Failures | 13 implants |
| MBL at 1 year | 0.2 mm (range 0-2.3) |
| MBL at final observation | 0.54 mm (range 0-4.99) |

**Success by primary ISQ:** <60 → 61% (P = 0.045); 60-70 → 100%; >70 → 91.2%.

**Survival by secondary ISQ:** 60-70 → 87.5%; >70 → 100% (P = 0.036).

**Non-significant for both outcomes:** age group, sex, site (maxilla 81.25% success / mandible 84.78%), diameter (6 mm 82.26%, 7 mm 84.7%), length (8 mm 84.8%, 10 mm 89.47%, 12 mm 42.87%), adjunctive surgery (no surgery 78.6% success vs 92-100% with sinus lift/GBR), and reason for selection (poor bone quality 100% success; failed ≤5 mm implant 88.6%; replacement after failed implant removal 100%).

**MBL:** no variable showed a significant association.

**Indication distribution:** 59 implants chosen for poor bone quality, 3 to replace a ≤5 mm implant that failed to gain stability, the remainder as replacements after failed implant removal.

## Limitations
Retrospective single-centre design with a 2008-2010 cohort predating current surface and thread technology; no control group of standard-diameter implants in matched sites, so the comparison with standard diameters is historical literature rather than internal. Success criteria are study-specific and relatively strict (annual MBL ≥0.2 mm counts as failure), which inflates the gap between 92.3% survival and 83.3% success. ISQ subgroups are small (the 12 mm length group is 7 implants with 42.87% success — an unstable estimate). No multivariable model, so confounding between stability, bone quality and site is unresolved. Laterality and immediate-loading status are not analyzed.

## Glossary
- **Ultra-wide implant:** fixture ≥6 mm in diameter (standard 3.75-5 mm).
- **ISQ:** Implant Stability Quotient.
- **MBL:** Marginal Bone Loss.
- **SLA:** Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched.
- **GBR:** Guided Bone Regeneration.
