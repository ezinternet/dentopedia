## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT, 28 patients (42 implants), Ege University Turkey — comparing osseodensification vs Summers' osteotome for transcrestal sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement, assessing ISQ, insertion torque, MBL, pain, and quality of life.

OD yielded significantly higher ISQ (75.09 vs 67.50, p=0.013), greater insertion torque (32.95 vs 26.25 Ncm, p=0.008), less analgesic consumption, and better OHIP scores vs osteotome; MBL and 100% survival were comparable between groups.

Relatively small sample (n=42 implants) limits generalizability; OD established as superior for primary stability in TSFE context but longer follow-up and larger trials needed.

## 세줄요약

전향적 RCT, 28명 (42개 임플란트), 터키 에게대학 — 치조정접근 상악동 거상 (TSFE) 동시 식립에서 골밀도화 (OD) vs Summers' osteotome 비교, ISQ·IT·MBL·통증·삶의질 평가.

OD가 ISQ (75.09 vs 67.50, p=0.013)·IT (32.95 vs 26.25 Ncm, p=0.008)·진통제 소비·OHIP에서 유의하게 우수; MBL·생존율 동등.

표본 소규모 (42개 임플란트)로 일반화 제한; OD가 TSFE 맥락에서 1차 안정성 우위 확립 — 장기 추적 및 대규모 시험 필요.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: International Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Implants, 2026;41(Suppl 1):s77–s88

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT comparing OD vs osteotome technique specifically for TSFE + simultaneous implant placement
- OD: significantly higher ISQ (MD +7.59, 95%CI 1.68–13.50, p=.013) and IT (MD +6.70 Ncm, 95%CI 1.87–11.53, p=.008)
- OD: significantly less analgesic consumption (MD -1.36, p=.016)
- OD: significantly better OHIP after prosthetic loading (p=.008)
- MBL comparable (p=.459); 100% survival both groups
- Establishes OD as superior primary stability technique for TSFE context

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Prospective randomized clinical trial
- n=28 patients, 42 implants: OD (n=22) vs osteotome (n=20), TSFE + simultaneous implant
- Primary outcome: implant stability (ISQ via Osstell, insertion torque)
- Secondary: MBL, survival, pain (VAS), analgesic consumption, OHIP-14
- Statistics: t-tests, Mann-Whitney U, chi-square, linear mixed-effects, Brunner-Langer models

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=42 implants — relatively small, limits generalizability
- Single center (Ege University, Turkey)
- Follow-up duration not specified for MBL — supplementary outcome
- No sinus membrane perforation rate reported
- Need: multicenter RCT, longer follow-up, subgroup by RBH

## 6. Related Work
- OD for non-sinus sites: higher ISQ than conventional drilling (Marzorati 2026 SR+MA; Tomar 2026 umbrella review)
- Osteotome TSFE (Summers 1994): established technique, balloon/manual tap approaches
- TSFE vs LSFE ISQ: crestal sites often have lower bone density, OD-mediated compaction may compensate

## 7. Glossary
- TSFE: 치조정접근 상악동 거상 (Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation); also called internal/crestal/osteotome technique
- OD: 골밀도화 (Osseodensification) using Densahbur clockwise compaction drills (Versah)
- ISQ: Implant Stability Quotient; IT: Insertion Torque
- OHIP: Oral Health Impact Profile — patient-reported quality of life
- VAS: Visual Analog Scale — pain
