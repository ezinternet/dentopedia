## Why Ingested

근막통증증후군 (Myofascial Pain Syndrome, MPS) 치료에서 안정화 교합장치 (Stabilization Splint, SS)의 효과를 초음파 치료, TheraBite, 근육 운동과 직접 비교한 4군 RCT로, 기존 [[wiki/tmj/zhang-2021-exercise-vs-splint-tmd-sr-ma]] 메타분석의 운동 대 장치 비교를 보완하는 단일 RCT 근거를 추가함.

## Three-line Summary

Single-blind 4-arm RCT (n=80) comparing ultrasound therapy, stabilization splint, TheraBite device, and masticatory muscle exercises for myofascial pain syndrome (MPS) at Damascus University (April–October 2023).

At 4-week endpoint, ultrasound therapy and stabilization splint achieved the greatest VAS pain reduction (VAS ~1.1) and were statistically superior to masticatory muscle exercises (VAS ~4.7; p=0.013), while all groups showed equivalent mandibular mobility improvement by the 5-month follow-up.

Ultrasound therapy and stabilization splint are the most effective short-term treatments for MPS, with masticatory exercises being the least effective for pain relief during the active treatment phase.

## 세줄요약

단맹검 4군 무작위대조시험 (n=80), 다마스쿠스 대학교에서 초음파 치료·안정화 교합장치·TheraBite·저작근 운동을 근막통증증후군 환자에서 비교(2023년 4–10월).

4주 치료 종료 시, 초음파 치료·안정화 교합장치군이 VAS ≈1.1로 가장 큰 통증 감소를 보였고 저작근 운동군(VAS ≈4.7)보다 유의하게 우월(p=0.013); 하악 운동 범위는 5개월 추적 시점에 모든 군에서 동등하게 개선.

MPS 단기 통증 완화에는 초음파 치료 또는 안정화 교합장치가 권장되며, 저작근 운동만으로는 치료 기간 중 통증 조절이 불충분할 수 있음.

## 1. Document Information

- **Full title**: Effectiveness of ultrasound therapy, stabilization splint, TheraBite device, and masticatory muscle exercises in reducing pain and improving mandibular mobility in MPS
- **Journal**: Clinical and Experimental Dental Research (Clin Exp Dent Res)
- **Year**: 2024
- **DOI**: 10.1002/cre2.921
- **PMID**: 38923288 / **PMCID**: PMC11194472
- **Design**: Single-blind, randomized, parallel-group, active-controlled trial (4 arms)
- **n**: 80 patients (20 per group)
- **Duration**: 4-week treatment + follow-up at 2 and 5 months
- **Setting**: Department of Fixed Prosthodontics, Damascus University, Syria

## 2. Key Contributions

- First 4-arm RCT directly comparing ultrasound therapy, stabilization splint, TheraBite passive motion device, and masticatory muscle exercises head-to-head for MPS
- Demonstrates that ultrasound therapy and SS achieve equivalent, superior VAS pain reduction vs. exercises at the 4-week endpoint
- Shows that all four modalities converge to equivalent mandibular mobility at 5-month follow-up, suggesting exercises catch up functionally even if slower for pain
- Provides data that TheraBite performs intermediately for pain (VAS ~2.5 at week 4) and is superior to exercises for lateral mandibular movement

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Randomization**: Simple 1:1:1:1 via online software; ISRCTN20833186
- **Blinding**: Outcome assessors blinded; patients not blinded (nature of treatments precludes this)
- **Interventions**:
  - Group 1 (Ultrasound): 3 sessions/week × 4 weeks
  - Group 2 (SS): Full-coverage maxillary acrylic splint (1.5 mm), 8 h nightly × 4 weeks
  - Group 3 (TheraBite): 10 bites × 10 s hold, 30 s rest, daily × 4 weeks
  - Group 4 (Exercises): Vertical, lateral, protrusive resistance exercises, 2×/day × 4 weeks
- **Outcomes**: VAS (visual analogue scale, 0–10), maximum interincisal opening (MIO, mm), right lateral movement (RLM, mm), left lateral movement (LLM, mm)
- **Time points**: Baseline (t0), weeks 1–2–4, months 2–5
- **Statistics**: Kruskal–Wallis with post-hoc multiple comparisons; IBM SPSS 26

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Pain intensity (VAS)**:
| Timepoint | Ultrasound | SS | TheraBite | Exercises | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 8.7 | 8.0 | 8.0 | 8.4 | 0.206 |
| Week 2 | 2.6 | 2.7 | 3.5 | 5.5 | 0.002* |
| Week 4 | 1.1 | 1.1 | 2.5 | 4.7 | 0.006* |
| Month 2 | 0.6 | 1.1 | 1.9 | 2.9 | 0.085 |
| Month 5 | 0.9 | 0.9 | 1.5 | 2.5 | 0.383 |

At week 4: Ultrasound vs. Exercises p=0.012; SS vs. Exercises p=0.013; TheraBite vs. Exercises p=1.000 (NS); Ultrasound vs. TheraBite p=0.012.

**Mandibular mobility**: No significant between-group differences at any timepoint for MIO. LLM showed significant differences at weeks 2 and 4 (exercises inferior). By month 5, all groups were equivalent.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-blind only (patients aware of treatment allocation)
- Short observation: 5-month follow-up may miss long-term relapse rates
- Single-center study (Damascus University) limits generalizability
- No sham/placebo control — all comparators are active; "control" group designation for SS is nominal
- Sample size calculated for small-to-medium effect; actual effect sizes not reported per group
- Compliance monitored only by self-report checklist
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria details not fully extractable from the PMC text (table content stripped)

## 7. Glossary

- **MPS (Myofascial Pain Syndrome, 근막통증증후군)**: TMD subtype with chronic fascial pain related to trigger points in masticatory muscles
- **SS (Stabilization Splint, 안정화 교합장치)**: Full-coverage occlusal splint that eliminates occlusal interferences; standard conservative TMD device
- **TheraBite**: Passive jaw motion rehabilitation device using intermittent stretching (10 s hold/30 s rest protocol)
- **VAS (Visual Analogue Scale, 시각적 상사 척도)**: 0–10 pain self-report scale
- **MIO (Maximum Interincisal Opening, 최대 개구량)**: Distance between upper and lower incisal edges, mm
- **RLM/LLM (Right/Left Lateral Movement, 우/좌 측방 운동)**: Mandibular lateral excursion in mm
