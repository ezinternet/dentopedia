## Three-line Summary

Multi-center prospective study (n=90 implants, maxillary premolar area; T0 = placement, T1 = 3-month submerged uncovering; Kruskal-Wallis + multivariate regression) measuring buccal and palatal bone thickness changes and their relationship to early marginal bone loss (EMBL).

Mean buccal bone thickness decreased from 2.42±0.64 mm at T0 to 1.92±0.71 mm at T1 (p=0.000); palatal bone decreased from 1.31 to 0.87 mm; vertical resorption was negligible (~0.03–0.04 mm); initial thickness was inversely correlated with horizontal resorption — thinner bone at placement predicted greater loss.

The ≥2 mm buccal bone rule of thumb is supported by this prospective dataset; however, measurements are limited to submerged healing (no loading phase), 3-month follow-up, and manual probe precision (0.5 mm steps), limiting detection of subtle changes.

## 세줄요약

다기관 전향 연구(n=90 임플란트, 상악 소구치 부위; T0=식립, T1=3개월 잠입치유 후 2차 수술; Kruskal-Wallis+다변량 회귀): 협측·구개측 골 두께 변화 및 초기 변연골소실(Early Marginal Bone Loss, EMBL)과의 관계.

협측 골 두께 T0 2.42±0.64 mm → T1 1.92±0.71 mm(p=0.000); 구개측 1.31→0.87 mm; 수직 흡수는 미미(~0.03–0.04 mm); 초기 두께가 수평 흡수와 역상관 — 식립 시 얇을수록 더 많은 소실.

≥2 mm 협측골 규칙이 이 전향 데이터로 지지됨; 단, 잠입치유 단계만(기능하중 없음)·3개월 단기·수동 탐침 0.5 mm 정밀도 한계로 미세 변화 감지 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions
Quantifies the buccal/palatal envelope thickness threshold (~2 mm) required to keep submerged-healing EMBL within negligible range. Adds prospective evidence to the rule of thumb of ≥2 mm buccal bone.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Multi-center prospective. Periodontal probe measurements at T0 (placement) and T1 (3-month uncovering). Kruskal-Wallis + multivariate linear regression on bone changes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Mean buccal thickness 2.42 ± 0.64 mm at T0 → 1.92 ± 0.71 mm at T1 (p=0.000). Palatal 1.31 → 0.87 mm. Vertical resorption non-significant (~0.03–0.04 mm). Initial thickness inversely correlated with horizontal loss.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Submerged healing only (no functional loading); 3-month follow-up; manual probe measurements with 0.5 mm precision.
