## Why Ingested

Fills a gap left by the held reconstructive peri-implantitis RCTs ([[implants/peri-implantitis/soldini-2025-reconstructive-therapy-peri-implantitis-rct]], [[implants/peri-implantitis/hakkers-2026-reconstructive-peri-implantitis-3wall-4wall-rct]]) and the titanium-brush decontamination trial ([[implants/peri-implantitis/park-2025-implantoplasty-vs-titanium-brushes-peri-implantitis-rct]]): none asks whether an adjacent compromised tooth or implant lesion can be treated *in the same procedure* as placement of a new implant, instead of in a staged approach. These two cases supply a 3-year signal for that simultaneous strategy and for the deep 3-wall defect morphology already identified as a favorable prognostic factor in [[implants/peri-implantitis/ramanauskaite-2023-guided-bone-regeneration-periimplantitis-defects-nma]].

## Three-line Summary

Two-case report (Seoul National University Dental Hospital; 3-year follow-up) of implant placed simultaneously with flap debridement and collagenated deproteinized bovine bone mineral grafting of an adjacent compromised site — a residual periodontal intrabony defect (Case 1) or an adjacent peri-implantitis defect (Case 2).

At 3 years both sites were stable with radiographic bone fill of 3.9 mm (tooth #35) and 4.1 mm (implant #16), clinical attachment level gains of 1.7 and 1.8 mm, and the new implants (#36, #15) at −0.7 mm radiographic bone level with PPD ≤3.0 mm and ≤4.0 mm, no recession and no progressive bone loss.

Simultaneous management may avoid staged surgery and reduce morbidity in selected patients with deep 3-wall defects and good plaque control, but two cases without histologic confirmation or a standardized protocol cannot establish generalizability.

## 세줄요약

2증례 보고 (서울대학교 치과병원; 3년 추적관찰): 임플란트 (Implant) 식립과 동시에 인접 손상 부위 — 잔여 치주 골내 결손 (Residual Periodontal Intrabony Defect; 증례 1) 또는 인접 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 골내 결손 (증례 2) — 를 플랩 거상 후 세정하고 교원질 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Collagenated Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral)로 골이식함.

3년 시점에 두 부위 모두 안정적이며 방사선학적 골충전은 3.9 mm (치아 #35), 4.1 mm (임플란트 #16), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득은 각 1.7·1.8 mm; 신규 식립 임플란트 (#36, #15)는 방사선학적 골수준 (Radiographic Bone Level, RBL) −0.7 mm, 탐침깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) ≤3.0·≤4.0 mm, 후퇴 (Recession, REC) 없이 진행성 골소실 없음.

선택된 환자에서 단계적 술식을 줄이고 술 후 불편을 낮출 수 있으나, 2증례·조직학적 확인 부재·술식 비표준화로 일반화된 프로토콜은 확립 불가 — 신중한 증례 선택·세심한 세정·지지요법 (Supportive Periodontal Therapy)이 전제.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Describes a **single-stage** alternative to the conventional staged approach: the adjacent compromised lesion (periodontal or peri-implant) is debrided and grafted during the same operation as new implant placement.
- Two distinct adjacent-lesion scenarios: residual 3-wall intrabony defect on a tooth (Case 1, 10 mm deep) and a Continuous Class 1b 3-wall peri-implant defect spanning the extraction site and the mesial aspect of an existing implant (Case 2, 9 mm deep).
- Implant surface decontamination in Case 2 combined titanium brush, hydrogen peroxide and doxycycline irrigation, with the existing prosthesis retained (transmucosal healing).
- Documents 3-year clinical and radiographic stability at both the treated adjacent sites and the newly placed implants, with comparable bone gain despite the wider defect in Case 2.
- Flap design rationale: crestal incision shifted lingually/palatally with no vertical releasing incisions, full-thickness elevation, keeping the incision away from the defect/graft to reduce dehiscence and graft exposure.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Case report of two patients, approved by institutional review board and written to CARE guidelines; clinical parameters recorded at baseline (pre-surgery) and at 3-year follow-up.
- **Setting**: Seoul National University Dental Hospital, Department of Periodontology, Seoul, Korea.
- **n**: 2 patients / 2 newly placed implants / 2 adjacent compromised sites (one tooth, one existing implant).
- **Case 1**: 70-year-old non-smoking woman, hypertension (ASA-II); hopeless tooth #36 extracted, residual 10 mm 3-wall intrabony defect on the distal of #35 found at 4 months; BL Straumann Ø4.8 × 8 mm implant placed at #36; BioOss-Collagen grafted at both the implant site and the #35 defect; collagen sponge (Teruplug) over the graft; healing abutment Ø6.5 × 4.0 mm; final prosthesis at 5 months.
- **Case 2**: 69-year-old man, ~2/3 pack/day smoker, no systemic disease (ASA-II); hopeless tooth #15 extracted, 9 mm Class 1b 3-wall defect from #15 to the mesial of existing implant #16 at 4 months; #16 decontaminated with titanium brush (TN-Brush), H₂O₂ pellet and doxycycline irrigation; BLT Straumann Ø4.1 × 12 mm implant at #15; BioOss-Collagen graft plus double-layer Bio-Gide membrane; cover screw, healing abutment Ø5.0 × 4.0 mm placed at 3 months; final prosthesis at 5 months with the existing prosthesis on #16 retained.
- **Postoperative medication**: cefdinir 100 mg + acetaminophen 650 mg three times daily (7 days in Case 1, 5 days in Case 2) and 0.2% chlorhexidine rinse twice daily.
- **Outcomes**: PPD, REC, KGW/KMW, vestibular depth, PI/mPI, SBI/mSBI, full-mouth plaque index (FMPI), and radiographic bone level (RBL) at six sites per tooth/implant; bone fill inferred from baseline-to-3-year RBL change.
- **Follow-up**: 3 years in both cases.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Case 1 — adjacent tooth #35 (grafted residual intrabony defect), 3 years**
- Mean PPD reduced by 2.5 mm (5.5 mm → 3.0 mm).
- Mean REC increased by 0.8 mm; KGW/KMW decreased by 2.0 mm; vestibular depth unchanged.
- SBI decreased by 1; PI unchanged; FMPI fell from 25.8% to 16.7% (−9.1 percentage points).
- Mean radiographic bone fill: **3.9 mm**; clinical attachment level gain: **1.7 mm**.

**Case 1 — new implant #36, 3 years**
- PPD ≤3.0 mm (mean 2.5 mm), no recession; KGW/KMW 4.0 mm; vestibular depth 6.0 mm.
- mPI 1, mSBI 0; RBL **−0.7 mm** relative to the implant shoulder (bone coronal to shoulder); no peri-implant bone loss.

**Case 2 — adjacent existing implant #16 (regenerative therapy), 3 years**
- Mean PPD reduced by 1.8 mm (5.3 mm → 3.5 mm), no recession; KGW/KMW increased by 1.0 mm; vestibular depth decreased by 2.0 mm.
- No change in mPI or mSBI; authors report FMPI decreased by 11.62%.
- Mean radiographic bone fill: **4.1 mm**; clinical attachment level gain: **1.8 mm**.

**Case 2 — new implant #15, 3 years**
- PPD ≤4.0 mm (mean 3.3 mm), no recession; KGW/KMW 3.0 mm; vestibular depth 6.0 mm.
- mPI 1, mSBI 1; RBL **−0.7 mm** relative to the implant shoulder; bone levels around #15 and #16 stable.

**Interpretive note from the Discussion** — although Case 2's defect was wider (extending into the new implant site), radiographic bone gains were comparable (3.9 vs 4.1 mm); the authors attribute this to the shared 3-wall configuration providing graft/clot containment. A slight increase in recession on the distal of tooth #14 was noted in Case 2, with no signs of inflammation.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only two cases — generalization is limited.
- No histologic confirmation of true periodontal or peri-implant bone regeneration.
- Surgical approach differed slightly according to adjacent-defect morphology; no standardized protocol can be established.
- Although 3-year outcomes were stable, longer observation and controlled clinical studies are required to confirm long-term predictability.

## 7. Glossary

- **Collagenated deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM-C)**: xenograft granules (BioOss-Collagen) combined with collagen, used for its biocompatibility, slow resorption and volume stability.
- **Class 1b intrabony defect**: a peri-implant defect morphology (Monje classification) bounded by remaining bony walls; here a 3-wall defect extending from the extraction socket to the mesial of the adjacent implant.
- **Transmucosal healing**: healing with the prosthesis left in place, avoiding prosthesis removal and a second-stage reconnection (used in Case 2).
- **Radiographic bone level (RBL)**: distance from the implant platform or tooth CEJ to the crestal bone; in this paper a negative implant RBL means bone coronal to the shoulder, and the baseline-to-follow-up change represents bone gain/fill.
- **Full-mouth plaque index (FMPI)**: proportion of examined tooth surfaces with plaque, used here as the oral-hygiene control marker.
- **Clinical attachment level (CAL)**: attachment gain measured alongside PPD reduction as a regenerative outcome.
