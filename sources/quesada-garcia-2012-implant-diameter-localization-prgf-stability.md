## Why Ingested

The wiki has no prior paper quantifying plasma rich in growth factors (PRGF, 성장인자 농축 혈장)'s effect on Implant Stability Quotient (임플란트 안정성 지수, ISQ) — a gap versus the well-covered PRP/PRF literature elsewhere in `bone-regeneration`/`sinus-lift`. This 235-implant multivariate study also directly clashes with [[implants/isq/shiffler-2016-implant-length-diameter-location-isq]] on whether implant diameter drives ISQ (Quesada: narrow diameter significantly worse, P<.001; Shiffler: diameter not significant) while agreeing with it on the maxilla-worse-than-mandible location effect, and reinforces [[implants/isq/huang-2017-multivariate-regression-isq-prediction]]'s finding that diameter is a general T2-ISQ predictor.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study (n=235 implants, 93 patients, University of Granada Oral Surgery/Implantology Clinic) using multivariate regression (SUDAAN, clustered sampling) to identify patient, implant, and operative predictors of Osstell ISQ at 12 weeks post-placement.

No patient-level variable was associated with 12-week ISQ; among implant variables, narrow diameter (P<.001) and maxillary location (P=.006) predicted worse ISQ; among surgical-technique variables, use of plasma rich in growth factors (PRGF) predicted the best 12-week ISQ (P=.011).

Wider-diameter, mandibular implants humidified with PRGF at placement show the highest secondary stability at 12 weeks — abstract-only source (full text paywalled), so exact effect sizes/coefficients and adjusted model details are not recoverable here.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상 연구(n=235 임플란트, 93명, 그라나다 대학 구강외과·임플란트 클리닉): 다변량 회귀(SUDAAN, 군집 표본)로 식립 12주 후 Osstell 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)의 환자·임플란트·수술 인자를 분석.

환자 변수는 12주 ISQ와 연관 없음; 임플란트 변수 중 좁은 직경(P<.001)·상악 위치(P=.006)가 낮은 ISQ와 연관; 수술 기법 변수 중 성장인자 농축 혈장(Plasma Rich in Growth Factors, PRGF) 사용이 가장 높은 12주 ISQ와 연관(P=.011).

넓은 직경·하악 위치·PRGF 습윤 처리 임플란트가 12주 이차 안정성이 가장 높음 — 초록만 확보(전문 유료화 장벽)되어 정확한 효과크기·보정계수 등 세부 수치는 본 페이지에서 복원 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the earlier multivariate (clustered-sampling) analyses of ISQ at a fixed 12-week secondary-stability timepoint, jointly modeling patient, implant, and operative factors.
- Establishes narrow implant diameter as an independent risk factor for lower 12-week ISQ (P<.001).
- Establishes maxillary localization as an independent risk factor for lower 12-week ISQ (P=.006), consistent with the maxilla's lower cortical bone density.
- Reports plasma rich in growth factors (PRGF) humidification of the implant surface at placement as associated with the best 12-week ISQ values (P=.011) — an early clinical signal for a biologic adjunct on secondary stability specifically (not just early bone healing/BIC).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective clinical cohort study.
- **Setting**: Oral Surgery and Implantology Clinic, University of Granada.
- **n**: 235 dental implants in 93 patients (47 men, 46 women; mean age 52.13 ± 11.23 years, range 27–76).
- **Predictor groups**: patient variables, implant variables (diameter, localization, etc.), operative variables (including PRGF use), and baseline ISQ.
- **Outcome**: ISQ at 12 weeks post-placement, measured by resonance frequency analysis (Osstell system).
- **Statistics**: Multivariate regression model; SUDAAN software used to account for clustered sampling (multiple implants per patient).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- No patient variable (age, sex, etc.) was associated with 12-week ISQ.
- Narrow implant diameter → significantly worse 12-week ISQ (P<.001).
- Maxillary implant localization → significantly worse 12-week ISQ (P=.006) vs mandibular.
- Use of PRGF during surgery → significantly better 12-week ISQ (P=.011), the best-performing surgical-technique variable studied.
- (Effect sizes, adjusted coefficients, and full regression table are behind the publisher paywall and not available from the abstract alone.)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only source**: this summary is built from the published abstract; exact ISQ means/SDs by subgroup, the full multivariate model (adjustment set), and PRGF protocol details (formulation, application timing/volume) are not recoverable without the full text.
- Single-center design (University of Granada) limits generalizability across implant systems/operators.
- The abstract does not report implant systems/brands used, which is a known effect modifier of ISQ (per [[implants/isq/huang-2017-multivariate-regression-isq-prediction]]).
- 12-week single timepoint — trajectory of ISQ change over healing (T1 baseline → 12 weeks) is not detailed beyond the endpoint comparison implied by "baseline ISQ" as a covariate.

## 6. Related Work

- [[implants/isq/shiffler-2016-implant-length-diameter-location-isq]]: retrospective study of length/diameter/location on ISQ — agrees on maxilla-worse-than-mandible location effect, but disagrees on diameter (Shiffler found no diameter effect on ISQ; this study found narrow diameter significantly worse).
- [[implants/isq/huang-2017-multivariate-regression-isq-prediction]]: multivariate regression identifying implant diameter as the only general T2 (pre-restoration) ISQ predictor — directionally consistent (wider diameter → higher ISQ).
- [[implants/isq/javed-2013-primary-stability-osseointegration-factors-influence]]: narrative review framework (bone quality/quantity, implant geometry, surgical technique) — this study empirically quantifies geometry (diameter) and technique (PRGF) effects within that framework.

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: 0–100 scale output of resonance frequency analysis (Osstell), reflecting implant-bone interface rigidity.
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: non-invasive method measuring implant stability via a transducer/SmartPeg attached to the implant/abutment.
- **PRGF (Plasma Rich in Growth Factors)**: autologous platelet concentrate (Anitua technique) distinct from PRP/PRF, used here to humidify the implant surface at placement as a biologic adjunct.
- **Secondary stability**: implant stability derived from biological bone remodeling/osseointegration over the healing period, as opposed to primary (mechanical, at-placement) stability.
- **Clustered sampling**: statistical handling (via SUDAAN) for non-independence when multiple implants are placed in the same patient.
