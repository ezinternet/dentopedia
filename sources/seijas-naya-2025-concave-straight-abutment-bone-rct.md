## Why Ingested

Concave vs. straight abutment 비교 24개월 double-blind RCT로, [[wiki/overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]]의 아부트먼트 형태가 변연골 수준에 미치는 영향 근거를 보강한다. 기존 6개월 데이터(Pérez-Sayáns 2023)의 장기 추적판이며, 아부트먼트 높이와 형태의 교호작용(interaction) — 즉 concave 군에서는 높이 증가가 오히려 골소실을 완화한다는 역방향 효과 — 을 혼합선형회귀(mixed linear regression)로 처음 정량화한 연구.

## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT, 68 implants in 21 patients (straight n=33, concave n=35), IPX 4×10 mm subcrestal, 24-month follow-up, Spain.

At 8 weeks pre-loading, concave abutments showed significantly less MBL than straight (avg −0.18 vs −0.54 mm, p=0.002); at 24 months post-loading, MBG was similar between groups (avg 0.37 vs 0.30 mm, p=0.689), but mixed regression confirmed concave group had significantly less overall MBL (effect sizes 1.948–2.105, p=0.006–0.026) with greater abutment height paradoxically mitigating bone loss in the concave group (interaction p<0.01).

Concave (slim) abutments offer a bone-protective advantage primarily in the critical early pre-loading phase; clinicians should consider concave design especially in esthetic zones, though the magnitude of MBL difference (<0.5 mm) is small and long-term superiority beyond 2 years remains unconfirmed.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 RCT, 임플란트 68개(21명), IPX 4×10 mm 치조골하 식립, 직선형 vs 오목형(Slim) 아부트먼트, 24개월 추적.

8주 시점(보철 전) 오목형 아부트먼트군의 평균 변연골소실(MBL)이 유의하게 적었고(−0.18 vs −0.54 mm, p=0.002), 혼합선형회귀 분석에서 오목형 군의 전 기간 MBL이 유의하게 적음이 확인됨(p=0.006–0.026); 오목형 군에서는 아부트먼트 높이가 높을수록 오히려 골소실이 줄어드는 역방향 교호작용 확인(p<0.01).

오목형 아부트먼트는 보철 전 초기 치유 단계에서 골 보호 효과가 크며, 심미 영역에서 우선 고려할 수 있으나 차이 크기(<0.5 mm)가 작고 2년 이후 장기 데이터는 부재하여 해석에 주의가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Longest published RCT comparing straight vs. concave (slim) abutment shapes on MBL — 24-month follow-up extending the same trial's 6-month data.
2. First quantification of the **height × shape interaction**: increasing abutment height increases MBL in the straight group, but decreases MBL in the concave group (p<0.01 across all models).
3. Confirms that the concave abutment protective effect appears **early** (pre-loading, 8 weeks) and is sustained, though the mesial interaction reaches only borderline significance at 24 months (p=0.072).
4. Generalized Additive Models (GAMs) used in addition to mixed linear regression to visualize the non-parametric effect of abutment height on MBL estimation — a methodological upgrade over earlier analyses.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

### Participants & Setting
- Recruited Feb 2020–Apr 2020 at University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain
- Inclusion: ≥2 missing posterior teeth (1st premolar–2nd molar), maxilla or mandible
- Final sample: 68 implants in 21 patients (9M, 12F); mean age 61.2 ± 9.9 years
- Attrition: 12 implants excluded (6 lost to follow-up, 2 abutment fractures, 4 crown fractures causing occlusal interference)

### Interventions
- **Implant**: IPX hexagonal internal connection (Galimplant), 4 mm diameter × 10 mm length, healed bone (>6 months post-extraction), subcrestal placement ~1 mm below alveolar crest
- **Straight group** (n=33): straight aesthetic antirotational abutments, 2 or 3 mm height
- **Concave group** (n=35): Slim antirotational abutments (concave profile along full length), 2 or 3 mm height
- **Protocol**: One-abutment-one-time; prosthesis placed 8 weeks post-surgery (screw-retained metal-porcelain crowns, 20 N); no fixed partial dentures

### Randomization & Blinding
- Randomized at patient level; intra-patient allocation for implant sites (each patient serves as own control)
- Sequence by independent researcher; sealed opaque envelopes; opened at abutment connection
- Blinded: patients and data analyst (received anonymized group labels); **not blinded**: radiographic evaluator (abutment shapes distinguishable on X-ray)

### Primary Outcome
- MBL / MBG by digital intraoral radiology at T0 (8 weeks, pre-loading) and T24 (24 months post-loading); also T6 (6 months)
- ISQ by resonance frequency analysis (Ostell)

### Statistics
- Mixed linear regression models (IBM SPSS 24.0) with patient as random effect, abutment type and height as fixed effects, interaction terms
- GAMs (R 4.1.1, mgcv package) for non-parametric height effect
- Per-protocol analysis (ITT not performed — stated limitation)
- Inter-examiner reliability: k=0.86 (probing depth), k=0.96 (MBL)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Baseline (TB) and Pre-loading (T0) Bone Levels

| Timepoint | Straight (avg) | Concave (avg) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| TB (baseline) | 1.06 ± 1.01 mm | 0.71 ± 0.57 mm | 0.086 |
| T0 (8 wks, pre-load) | 0.51 ± 0.71 mm | 0.53 ± 0.51 mm | 0.913 |

### MBL at Different Periods

| Period | Straight MBL (avg) | Concave MBL (avg) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline → T0 (pre-load) | −0.54 ± 0.53 mm | −0.18 ± 0.37 mm | **0.002** |
| T0 → T6 (load to 6 mo) | −0.08 ± 0.47 mm | −0.01 ± 0.34 mm | 0.487 |
| T6 → T24 (6–24 months) | +0.38 ± 0.41 mm (MBG) | +0.38 ± 0.68 mm (MBG) | 0.972 |
| Total T0 → T24 | +0.30 ± 0.58 mm (MBG) | +0.37 ± 0.72 mm (MBG) | 0.689 |

**Key finding**: The significant difference in MBL favoring concave abutments is concentrated in the **pre-loading (surgical healing) phase**. Post-loading differences between groups are not statistically significant in simple comparisons; they only emerge as significant via regression modeling that accounts for patient-level variation.

### Mixed Linear Regression (Primary Analysis)

- Concave group coefficient (mesial): **+1.948**, p=0.006; distal: +2.105, p=0.026; average: +2.016, p=0.006 (positive value = less MBL relative to straight)
- Abutment height main effect (straight group): **positive association** with MBL (greater height → more bone loss)
- Concave × height interaction: **negative coefficient** (mesial: −1.058, p=0.001; distal: −1.144, p=0.008; average: −1.094, p=0.001) — in concave group, greater height mitigates bone loss

### Stability & Periodontal Indices
- ISQ at 24 months: straight 70.2 ± 8.7, concave 71.0 ± 9.1 — no significant difference
- Bleeding index, plaque index, probing depth: no significant change or between-group difference

### Adverse Events
- Implant failure: 2 cases (2.6%)
- Abutment fracture: 1 case (0.8%)
- No mucositis, crown loosening, or ceramic chipping

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

1. **Radiographic evaluator not blinded** — abutment shape visible on X-ray; potential detection bias
2. **Per-protocol analysis only** — no ITT analysis; may overestimate treatment effects
3. **Single implant type and connection**: results apply specifically to subcrestal bone-level implants with conical internal connection and platform-switching; not generalizable to other designs
4. **Industry funding**: Nueva Galimplant supplied the implants/abutments — though authors declare no conflicts, this is a common risk-of-bias flag
5. **Small clinical magnitude**: MBL differences <0.5 mm over 24 months; long-term clinical significance uncertain
6. **No >24-month data**: behavior after 2 years (physiologic remodeling, peri-implantitis risk) unknown
7. **Future**: studies with longer follow-up, multiple abutment designs, multi-center designs, ITT analysis

## 6. Related Work

- **Pérez-Sayáns et al. (2023)**: 6-month RCT from same dataset — previously reported early MBL favoring concave abutments; current study is the 24-month extension
- **Bernabeu-Mira et al.**: earlier RCT modifying abutment shape along full length — significant MBL difference favoring narrower abutments
- **Canullo et al. (2020 SR)**: systematic review suggesting abutment design influences hard tissue health; inconclusive for soft tissue
- **Camacho-Alonso et al.**: histological study showing concave abutments increase peri-implant tissue height, improve collagen fiber orientation, and reduce chronic inflammatory cell infiltrate in thick phenotypes
- **Linkevicius et al.**: subcrestal placement reduces early MBL in thin tissue cases — foundational for the subcrestal protocol used in this study

## 7. Glossary

- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골소실)**: reduction in bone height around the implant over time, measured radiographically; negative value convention used in this study (loss = negative)
- **MBG (Marginal Bone Gain, 변연골증가)**: increase in bone height around the implant
- **Concave/Slim abutment (오목형 아부트먼트)**: abutment with a concave (inward-narrowing) profile along its full transmucosal length, creating additional space for soft tissue
- **Straight abutment (직선형 아부트먼트)**: conventional cylindrical abutment with uniform diameter along its transmucosal length
- **One-abutment-one-time (1아부트먼트-1타임)**: protocol of placing the definitive abutment at implant placement and not removing it, to minimize soft tissue trauma
- **Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭)**: using a prosthetic component narrower than the implant platform, associated with reduced MBL
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수)**: resonance frequency analysis output (0–100); higher = more stable
- **GAM (Generalized Additive Model)**: non-parametric regression allowing flexible (non-linear) estimation of covariate effects
- **Mixed linear regression (혼합선형회귀)**: regression model with both fixed (abutment type, height, time) and random (patient) effects — accounts for clustering of implants within patients
- **Subcrestal placement (치조골하 식립)**: implant placed 1 mm below the alveolar crest to ensure biological space
