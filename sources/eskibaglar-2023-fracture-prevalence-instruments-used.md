## Why Ingested
Prospective clinical study (2,168 teeth) quantifying instrument fracture prevalence across dental education levels — fills a gap in real-world fracture rates during training. Reinforces [[endodontics/shaping/eskibaglar-2023-fracture-prevalence-instruments-used]] with data on NiTi reciprocating vs hand file fracture patterns and management decisions by fracture level.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective cohort study at Fırat University (Turkey) evaluating instrument fracture in 2,168 teeth treated by 66 fourth-year, 72 fifth-year, and 16 postgraduate students using K-files and Endoart Expert Gold reciprocating NiTi files.
NiTi reciprocating files fractured at ~3× the rate of hand files (59 vs 20 fractures); overall fracture rate 3.6% (79/2,168), highest in molars (6.9%) and apical third (72.1%); no significant difference by operator education level (p=0.051).
Curvature strongly predicts fracture (moderate 44.3%, severe 38% of fractures); apical fractures mostly left in situ (84.2%), coronal fractures mostly retrieved (66.7%); single-institution student operator cohort limits generalizability.

## 세줄요약
피라트 대학교(튀르키예) 전향적 코호트 연구로, 4학년·5학년·대학원생이 K-파일과 Endoart Expert Gold 왕복 NiTi 파일을 사용해 2,168개 치아 치료 시 기구 파절 발생률을 조사함.
NiTi 왕복 파일이 수기 파일 대비 약 3배 더 많이 파절(59 vs 20); 전체 파절률 3.6%(79/2,168), 대구치(6.9%)와 근단 3분의 1(72.1%)에서 최다; 술자 교육 수준 간 유의한 차이 없음(p=0.051).
만곡도가 파절 강력 예측(중등도 44.3%, 심한 38%); 근단 파절은 주로 방치(84.2%), 치관부는 주로 회수(66.7%); 단일 기관 학생 술자 코호트로 일반화 제한됨.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest prospective dataset (2,168 teeth) on instrument fracture across predoctoral and postgraduate education levels
- Direct comparison of reciprocating NiTi (Endoart Expert Gold) vs hand K-file fracture rates in the same clinical setting
- Fracture management decisions stratified by fracture level (coronal/middle/apical) and canal curvature severity
- Schneider method curvature analysis linked to fracture location and management outcome

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective cohort study (ethical approval 2022/12-29)
- **n**: 2,168 permanent teeth (primary endodontic treatment only; retreatments and primary teeth excluded)
- **Operators**: 66 fourth-year (373 teeth), 72 fifth-year (590 teeth), 16 postgraduate (1,205 teeth) students
- **Instruments**: Hand K-file #10/15/#20 (Mani); Reciprocating NiTi Endoart Expert Gold #25 (İnci Dental) with Endoart WISMY endomotor
- **Protocol**: Postgraduates — manual glide path #10/15 K-file then Endoart Expert Gold #25; Undergraduates — apical prep to #20 K-file then Endoart Expert Gold #25; balanced force technique for manual instrumentation
- **Curvature measurement**: Schneider method on periapical radiographs using AutoCAD; categories: mild (<10°), moderate (≥10–<25°), severe (≥25–<45°), ultra-severe (≥45°)
- **Fracture localization**: Dental microscope (Zumax)
- **Retrieval**: Ultrasonic tips
- **Outcomes**: Fracture incidence by operator level, tooth type, arch, canal, fracture level, curvature; management method (retrieval/bypass/left in situ)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Total fractures | 79 (3.6%) — 59 NiTi reciprocating, 20 hand files |
| Fracture rate by education (p=0.051) | Postgraduate: 3.2% (38/1,205); 4th year: 2.4% (9/373); 5th year: 5.4% (32/590) |
| Fracture rate by arch (p=0.922) | Maxilla: 3.6% (39); Mandible: 3.7% (40) |
| Fracture rate by tooth type (p<0.001) | Incisor: 0.9% (6/654); Premolar: 2.2% (15/677); **Molar: 6.9% (58/837)** |
| Fracture level | Apical: 72.1%; Middle: 19.0%; Coronal: 8.9% |
| NiTi vs hand file fracture | NiTi reciprocating ~3× hand file rate |
| Management by fracture level (p<0.001) | Apical: left 84.2%, bypass 15.8%, retrieval 0%; Middle: left 63.2%, bypass 31.6%, retrieval 5.3%; Coronal: retrieval 66.7%, bypass 33.3%, left 0% |
| Management by curvature (p=0.028) | Mild: bypass 41.7%, left 50%; Moderate: left 65.7%, bypass 28.6%; Severe: left 96.7%, bypass 3.3%; Ultra-severe: left 100% |
| Curvature distribution of fractured cases | Moderate 44.3%, Severe 38%, Mild/Ultra-severe remainder |
| Mean broken fragment length | Incisor 2.42 mm, Premolar 3.4 mm, Molar 3.63 mm |
| Cases referred to specialist clinic | 20 cases from 4th/5th year students |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single institution (Fırat University, Turkey) — limited generalizability
- Student operators with varying experience levels
- Single reciprocating system tested (Endoart Expert Gold, heat-treated)
- No long-term clinical outcome data for retained fragments
- Curvature measured via 2D periapical radiographs (Schneider method) — may underestimate 3D canal anatomy
- No randomization of instrument systems; all operators used the same systems

## 7. Glossary
- **왕복 운동 (Reciprocating motion, REC)**: 회전 방향을 번갈아 가며 회전하는 NiTi 파일 구동 방식
- **슈나이더 법 (Schneider method)**: 근단 방사선 사진상 근관 만곡 각도를 측정하는 표준 기법
- **열처리 NiTi (Heat-treated NiTi, HT-NiTi)**: 열처리로 유연성과 파절 저항성을 높인 니켈-티타늄 합금
- **근단 3분의 1 (Apical third)**: 근관의 근단 방향 1/3 구간
- **글라이드 패스 (Glide path)**: 근관 성형 전 초기 소구경 파일로 확보하는 매끄러운 경로
- **바이패스 (Bypass)**: 파절된 기구 옆으로 새로운 경로를 만들어 근관 성형 완료하는 술식
