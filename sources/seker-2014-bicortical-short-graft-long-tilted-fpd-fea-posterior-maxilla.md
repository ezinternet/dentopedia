## Why Ingested

CT-derived 3D FEA comparing three clinical strategies for the atrophied posterior maxilla in an FPD context: (1) short wide implant + bicortical fixation, (2) sinus-grafted long implant, and (3) 45°-tilted long implant. Directly informs the clinical decision between bicortical short implants and sinus augmentation — a key question this wiki's bicortical evidence base addresses via [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/ng-2018-bicortical-engagement-atrophic-posterior-maxillae]] and [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/malo-2014-bicortical-anchorage-posterior-maxilla-7year]].

## Three-line Summary

3D FEA from CT of atrophic posterior maxilla; 3 treatment scenarios: (1) 6×5 mm short + bicortical FPD, (2) sinus-grafted 11×4 mm long implant FPD, (3) 45°-tilted 11×5 mm long implant FPD; oblique occlusal forces simulating chewing; von Mises and minimum principal stress compared on implants, crestal cortex, sinus cortex, trabecular bone, and graft.
Crestal cortical bone carried the highest compressive stress in all models (max 49.761 MPa); graft material absorbed negligible stress (0.049 MPa — 1,000× less than crestal cortex); short bicortical implant (6×5 mm) showed lowest von Mises on the implant itself (219.63 MPa); 45°-tilted long implant showed highest (499.50 MPa).
Short wide bicortical fixation is the most biomechanically favorable option for atrophied posterior maxilla; graft material has inadequate load-transfer capacity; 45° tilted implants generate the highest implant stress and should be avoided if alternatives exist.

치조정 피질골 최고 압축응력 (49.761 MPa); 이식재 응력흡수 최저 (0.049 MPa, 피질골의 1/1000); 단기 쌍겹질 임플란트 von Mises 최저 (219.63 MPa); 45° 경사 임플란트 최고 (499.50 MPa).
단기 광폭 쌍겹질 고정이 위축 후방 상악 FPD의 생역학적 최선; 이식재는 하중 전달 불량; 45° 경사는 임플란트 응력 급증.

## Key Contributions

- CT-derived model increases anatomical fidelity vs CAD-only studies
- Graft material quantified as load-transfer-incompetent (0.049 MPa vs 49.761 MPa crestal cortex)
- Short bicortical: lowest implant von Mises (219.63 MPa); 45° tilted: highest (499.50 MPa)
- Sinus cortical bone (14.144 MPa) is an effective secondary anchor — bicortical fixation validated
- Trabecular bone absorbs ~4.3 MPa — modest but meaningful secondary support

## Key Results

| Structure | Min principal (compressive) stress |
|---|---|
| Crestal cortical bone | 49.761 MPa (highest) |
| Sinus cortical bone | 14.144 MPa |
| Trabecular bone | 4.347 MPa |
| Graft material | 0.049 MPa (lowest — poor absorption) |

| Model | Implant von Mises |
|---|---|
| Model 1: 6×5 mm short + bicortical | **219.63 MPa (lowest)** |
| Model 2: sinus-grafted 11×4 mm long | Intermediate |
| Model 3: 45°-tilted 11×5 mm long | **499.50 MPa (highest)** |

## Limitations

- Abstract-only; full stress maps and per-model FPD geometry details not available
- Single patient CT anatomy; results may not generalize
- Static oblique load only; dynamic fatigue not modeled
- Graft elastic modulus fixed — actual graft stiffness varies by material and maturation stage
