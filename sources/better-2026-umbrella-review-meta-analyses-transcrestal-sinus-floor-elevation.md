## Why Ingested

PubMed 감시 인제스트(transcrestal sinus lift). 위키에 개별 경치조골 상악동 거상 메타분석(Kadkhodazadeh 2024, Starch-Jensen 2025, Huang 2026)은 있으나 이들을 한 번에 평가한 메타분석의 메타분석(우산 리뷰)은 없었음 — 방법론적 품질(AMSTAR 2)과 중복도(CCA)로 기존 메타분석의 가중치를 재조정하는 상위 레이어. → [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/better-2026-umbrella-review-meta-analyses-transcrestal-sinus-floor-elevation]]

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 30 meta-analyses (2015–April 2026) on transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE): AMSTAR 2 confidence was MODERATE in 9, LOW in 11, CRITICALLY LOW in 10, and none HIGH; pool-level Corrected Covered Area (CCA) was 0.92% but 10.90% in the graftless osteotome cluster.

Implant survival was uniformly high (95–100%) across short implants vs sinus floor elevation, transcrestal vs lateral access, and transcrestal techniques, with no clear survival advantage for any technique, so survival alone cannot rank techniques.

Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV) was reported mainly with mallet-driven osteotome procedures, but events were few and reporting inconsistent, so this is a suggestive signal, not causal proof; the review was not prospectively registered.

## 세줄요약

경치조골 상악동 거상 메타분석 30편(2015~2026.4)을 대상으로 한 우산 리뷰 (umbrella review): AMSTAR 2 신뢰도는 중간 9편·낮음 11편·매우 낮음 10편, 높음 0편이며, 전체 중복도 (Corrected Covered Area, CCA)는 0.92%이나 무이식 골내세공기 (osteotome) 군집에서는 10.90%.

임플란트 생존율은 단축 임플란트 대 상악동 거상, 경치조골 대 측방 접근, 경치조골 기법 간 비교 모두에서 95–100%로 높고 어떤 기법도 생존율 우위가 없어, 생존율만으로는 기법 순위를 매길 수 없음.

양성 돌발성 체위성 현훈 (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo, BPPV)은 주로 망치 타격식 골내세공기 술식에서 보고되었으나 사건 수가 적고 보고가 일관되지 않아 인과가 아닌 시사적 신호이며, 본 리뷰는 사전 등록되지 않았음.

## Summary

Umbrella review of meta-analyses (full text, PMC13511329). 30 meta-analyses on TSFE published 2015–April 2026 from PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus (three-phase search). Reported per Cochrane Handbook Chapter V, JBI and PRISMA 2020; not prospectively registered (protocol finalised after screening began). Narrative synthesis only — no new pooled estimate, because re-pooling would double-count primary studies. Organised around three decisions: short implants vs sinus floor elevation, transcrestal vs lateral access, technique choice within TSFE.

## Methodology

- AMSTAR 2 (16 items, 7 critical) with verbatim per-item evidence; second reviewer checked supporting text and tier; extraction was verified by a second reviewer but not fully independent duplicate extraction.
- Overlap by Corrected Covered Area (Pieper 2014), pool-level and for five thematic clusters.
- Biomechanical (impact vs non-impact) classification used only as an interpretive framework, not a reviewed outcome.

## Results

- **Quality**: 0 HIGH, 9 MODERATE (30.0%), 11 LOW (36.7%), 10 CRITICALLY LOW (33.3%). MODERATE: Ravidà 2019, Lee 2023, Shi S 2023, Kadkhodazadeh 2024, Moraschini 2017, Mester 2023, Yan 2019, Raghoebar 2019, Toledano 2022. Starch-Jensen 2025 was downgraded MODERATE → LOW (single-reviewer signature on Item 9); Kadkhodazadeh 2024 upgraded LOW → MODERATE. Common critical flaws: no a priori protocol (Item 2), no excluded-studies list (Item 7), no publication-bias assessment (Item 15).
- **Overlap**: pool CCA 0.92% (slight); graftless osteotome cluster (7 MAs) 10.90% (high), driven by Nedir 2013/2016 and Si 2013/2016; short-implant vs SFE cluster (8 MAs) 6.8% (moderate).
- **Survival**: 95–100% across all 30 MAs. Moraschini 2017: 97% (1034 implants, graftless TSFE), grafted vs graftless RR 0.55 (95% CI 0.26–1.19). Short implants vs SFE: RR 0.97–1.08, all non-significant (Vetromilla 2021 RR 1.08, 0.61–1.90; Toledano 2022 RR 1.02, 1.00–1.05).
- **Membrane perforation** (11 of 30 MAs): Shi S 2023 RR 0.71 transcrestal vs lateral at RBH ≤6 mm (CI includes 1); Lee 2023 network MA SUCRA 0.94 TSFE-reamer (lowest perforation probability) vs 0.07 lateral-window-rotary; Yan 2019 perforation/infection RR 0.11 (0.02–0.63) favouring short implants; Ye 2021 3.08% in non-grafted osteotome.
- **BPPV** (4 MAs): concentrated in mallet-driven osteotome studies; Kadkhodazadeh 2024 BPPV in 4 of 17 RCTs (3 of 4 osteotome); Starch-Jensen 2025 zero BPPV with non-impact osseodensification vs 1–5 cases with osteotome (p = 0.002); Cobo-Vázquez 2025 overall complications 2.78% (osseodensification) vs 14.32% (osteotome), BPPV 1/24 (4.17%) in osteotome only.
- **PROMs**: one dedicated MA (Menini 2025, LOW); sparse, heterogeneous; less invasive procedures generally favoured.

## Limitations (author-stated)

Not prospectively registered; English only; mostly short/medium-term outcomes; rare events (BPPV, perforation) reported without full denominators; no formal GRADE applied; biomechanical rationale is interpretation, not a finding.
