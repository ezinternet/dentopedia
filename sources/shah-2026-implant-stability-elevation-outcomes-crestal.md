## Why Ingested

Same niche as the existing single-arm n=16 study [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/shalash-2023-crestal-sinus-elevation-densah-oblique]] (oblique sinus floor + RBH 4-7 mm, Densah crestal SFE), but this is the first controlled comparison in that niche: Densah crestal lift alone vs with adjunctive grafting — a graft/no-graft decision angle no current page covers (elevation higher without graft, RFA/stability higher with graft).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective two-arm comparative study (n=60, posterior maxilla, RBH 4-7 mm with oblique sinus floor, Feb 2024-Mar 2025) comparing crestal sinus floor elevation with Densah burs alone (Group I, n=30) vs Densah + adjunctive grafting (Group II, n=30), all with simultaneous implant placement.

Elevation gain was significantly greater without graft (4.22 ± 0.55 vs 3.96 ± 0.29 mm, P=0.042), while RFA implant stability was significantly higher with graft (74.33 ± 4.86 vs 67.76 ± 4.05 ISQ, P<0.001); final seating torque showed no difference (40.33 ± 4.5 vs 38.66 ± 3.9 Ncm, P=0.133).

Densah crestal SFE is a predictable technique for 4-7 mm RBH: grafting buys secondary stability (ISQ >70, favorable for early loading) at a slight elevation cost, but conclusions are limited by short (3-month) follow-up and an unverified randomization claim.

## 세줄요약

잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH) 4-7 mm + 경사진 동저(oblique sinus floor) 후방 상악 60명(남 25/여 35, 근평균 39.2세)을 Densah 버 골밀도화(Osseodensification, OD) 단독군(I)과 Densah + 부가 이식재군(II)으로 나눈 전향적 비교 임상연구(2024.2-2025.3).

거상량은 무이식군(I)이 유의하게 큼(4.22 ± 0.55 vs 3.96 ± 0.29 mm, P=0.042), 공진주파수분석(Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 이식군(II)이 유의하게 높음(74.33 ± 4.86 vs 67.76 ± 4.05, P<0.001), 최종 식립토크는 양군 차이 없음(P=0.133).

RBH 4-7 mm 경사 동저에서 Densah 경치조골 상악동거상(Crestal Sinus Floor Elevation, CSFE)은 예측 가능한 술식 — 이식재는 ISQ>70의 2차 안정성을 얻되 경미한 거상량 손실이 있으며, 3개월 단기추적과 검증되지 않은 무작위배정 주장이 해석에 주의를 요함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Head-to-head graft-vs-no-graft comparison within Densah crestal SFE: no-graft group achieved significantly greater immediate vertical elevation (4.22 vs 3.96 mm, P=0.042), while the grafted group achieved significantly higher RFA implant stability (74.33 vs 67.76 ISQ, P<0.001).
- Largest prospective cohort (n=60) specifically in the oblique-sinus-floor + RBH 4-7 mm niche, extending the single-arm n=16 evidence of Shalash 2023 with a comparator arm.
- Final seating torque was independent of grafting (P=0.133), supporting OD compaction alone as sufficient for primary stability.
- ISQ >70 (achieved by grafted group) cited as favorable threshold for early loading.
- Authors attribute the elevation difference to a "membrane tenting effect" in the graftless group and graft-enhanced secondary stability in the grafted group.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective clinical study; abstract states patients "randomized into two groups," body states "Participants were randomly allocated into two groups (n = 30 each)" — no randomization method (sequence generation), allocation concealment, or blinding described. Treated as prospective comparative rather than verified RCT.
- **Setting**: Single center (Al-Meswak Dental Center, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia)
- **n**: 60 patients (25 men / 41.7%, 35 women / 58.3%); age 20 to >60 yr (mean 39.2 yr; largest subgroup 41-50 yr, 40%)
- **Inclusion**: RBH 4.0-7.0 mm; oblique sinus floor confirmed radiographically; adequate mesiodistal and buccolingual dimensions; ASA I or II
- **Exclusion**: active sinus pathology; uncontrolled systemic conditions (e.g., diabetes mellitus); heavy smokers (>10 cigarettes/day); previous grafting in the intended site
- **Groups**: Group I — crestal SFE with Densah burs, no graft material (n=30, 12M/18F); Group II — crestal SFE with Densah burs + adjunctive grafting (n=30, 13M/17F)
- **Procedure**: pre-operative panoramic radiograph for RBH/sinus-floor planning → local anesthesia, crestal incision, flap reflection → sequential Densah burs in reverse mode (Osseodensification) with controlled sinus membrane elevation → graft introduced in Group II → simultaneous implant placement in both groups → final seating torque recorded at placement; implant stability measured with RFA using the Osstell device → immediate post-operative periapical radiograph
- **Follow-up**: recalls at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months (clinical + radiographic)
- **Measurement caveat**: the exact radiograph/landmarks used to quantify "vertical elevation achieved (mm)" are not specified in the text; CBCT was not used (panoramic + periapical only)
- **Sample size**: separate calculations for the two primary outcomes (final seating torque, vertical elevation); ≥22 cases/group sufficient to detect a 10% difference at α=0.05, power 80%; increased to 30/group for attrition
- **Statistics**: SPSS v29; Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality test; independent samples t-test; P ≤ 0.05 significant

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Short follow-up (3 months) — no long-term implant survival data.
- No histologic evaluation of bone regeneration.
- Randomization claim is not substantiated by any methodological description (no sequence generation, allocation concealment, or blinding); treated as a prospective comparative study.
- Quantitative definition of "elevation achieved" is not explicitly given; method is radiographic (panoramic + periapical), no CBCT.
- No membrane perforation rate reported despite this being a key safety outcome for sinus lift.
- Stated by authors: future studies with longer observation and radiographic bone-gain analysis needed.
- Single center; follow-up/survival confined to early healing phase.

## 7. Glossary
- **Residual Bone Height (RBH)**: Vertical sub-sinus native bone (mm) available before sinus floor elevation; case-selection threshold here is 4.0-7.0 mm.
- **Crestal Sinus Floor Elevation (CSFE) / transcrestal SFE**: Sinus floor elevation accessed through the implant osteotomy at the alveolar crest, without a lateral window.
- **Osseodensification (OD)**: Counterclockwise (reverse-rotation) Densah-bur technique that compacts/densifies trabecular bone while elevating the sinus membrane, rather than excavating the osteotomy.
- **Resonance Frequency Analysis (RFA) / Implant Stability Quotient (ISQ)**: Objective measure of implant stability via the Osstell device; ISQ >70 is cited as favorable for early loading.
- **Final seating torque**: Insertion torque recorded at final implant seating, in Ncm — a primary-stability index.
- **Oblique sinus floor**: Angulated (non-horizontal) sinus floor morphology, a known anatomical challenge for transcrestal elevation.
