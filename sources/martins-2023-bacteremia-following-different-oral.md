## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 64 non-randomized controlled trials (nRCTs) + 25 RCTs quantifying bacteremia incidence, timing, and duration following invasive dental procedures (IDPs) and activities of daily living (ADL) in healthy individuals.

Dental extraction showed the highest bacteremia incidence (62–66%), followed by scaling and root planing (SRP, 44–36%) and oral hygiene procedures (OHP, 27–28%); peak bacteremia occurred within 5 minutes of procedure completion and declined rapidly; toothbrushing (8–26%) and flossing (16%) also caused transient bacteremia.

These quantified risk estimates provide the evidence base for antibiotic prophylaxis guidelines for infective endocarditis and prosthetic joint infection prevention.

## 세줄요약

균혈증(Bacteremia) SR+MA(비무작위 대조시험(nRCT) 64편 + 무작위 대조시험(RCT) 25편): 건강한 개체에서 침습적 치과시술(Invasive Dental Procedures, IDP)·일상생활(ADL) 후 균혈증 발생률·시기·지속시간 정량화.

발치가 가장 높은 균혈증 발생률(62–66%), 이어 치석제거·치근활택(SRP, 44–36%), 구강위생처치(OHP, 27–28%); 균혈증 최고점은 처치 완료 후 5분 이내, 이후 급격 감소; 칫솔질(8–26%)·치실(16%)도 일과성 균혈증 유발.

이 정량화된 위험 추정치는 감염성 심내막염(Infective Endocarditis, IE)·인공관절 감염 예방 항생제 예방투여 지침의 근거를 제공.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First comprehensive SR+MA quantifying bacteremia rates across dental procedures and ADL
- Establishes hierarchy: extraction > SRP > OHP > ADL (flossing, toothbrushing)
- Peak bacteremia within 5 min — supports short prophylaxis window
- ADL (toothbrushing, flossing) produce bacteremia: cumulative lifetime exposure exceeds single dental visit

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 8 databases searched
- Healthy individuals only (no compromised patients)
- Risk of bias: RoB 2.0 for RCTs, ROBINS-I for nRCTs
- Primary outcomes: timing and duration of bacteremia
- Secondary outcome: incidence (proportion with bacteremia ≤5 min post-procedure vs baseline)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Procedure | Bacteremia Incidence |
|---|---|
| Dental extraction | 62–66% |
| Scaling and root planing (SRP) | 44–36% |
| Oral hygiene procedures (OHP) | 27–28% |
| Flossing (ADL) | 16% |
| Toothbrushing (ADL) | 8–26% |

- Peak bacteremia: within 5 min of procedure completion
- Most studies: some concerns (RCTs) or moderate risk of bias (nRCTs)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Most studies had moderate or some-concerns risk of bias
- Healthy individuals only — compromised patients may have different profiles
- Variable bacterial identification methods across studies
- Cannot determine causation between bacteremia and clinical infection

## 6. Related Work and Context
- Foundational to IE prophylaxis guidelines (AHA, ESC, NICE)
- ADL bacteremia finding supports relative-risk counselling over blanket dental procedure restrictions

## 7. Glossary
- **Bacteremia**: Transient presence of bacteria in bloodstream
- **IDP (Invasive Dental Procedure)**: Any dental procedure breaching oral mucosal integrity
- **ADL (Activity of Daily Living)**: Routine oral hygiene activities producing bacteremia
- **IE (Infective Endocarditis)**: Infection of heart valve(s); dental bacteremia one potential portal of entry

## Why Ingested

Quantifies bacteremia rates across dental procedures — foundational reference for antibiotic prophylaxis decision-making in patients at risk for infective endocarditis or prosthetic joint infection.
