## Why Ingested

Clinical-epidemiology counterpart to the bench data already held: [[dental-erosion/barac-2015-erosive-effect-soft-drinks-enamel-profilometry]] and [[dental-erosion/souza-2020-citrus-sweets-enamel-erosion-invitro]] show soft drinks and acidic sweets erode enamel in vitro, and [[dental-erosion/kanzow-2016-etiology-pathogenesis-dental-erosion]] frames extrinsic acid as an etiologic axis. This SR+MA supplies the adolescent prevalence and the pooled association for carbonated drinks. Source: PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3290/j.ohpd.c_2619)).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42023398708) of 24 observational studies (21 cross-sectional, 2 case-control, 1 cohort; 21,541 adolescents, mean age 15.0 years) on dental erosion and carbonated drinks.

Pooled erosion prevalence was 37.6% (95%CI 26.3-49.7); carbonated drink intake above once daily was associated with erosion, OR 1.98 (95%CI 1.42-2.77; 7,785 participants; I2 = 80%).

Only 5 studies were poolable for the OR, exposure definitions differed, and the designs were mostly cross-sectional with self-report, so the association is an observational signal, not a causal estimate.

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA; PRISMA, 관찰연구 24편: 단면연구 21·환자-대조군 2·코호트 1; 청소년 21,541명, 평균 15.0세)으로 탄산음료와 치아침식 (dental erosion)의 관계를 분석.

침식 합산 유병률 37.6% (95%CI 26.3-49.7), 탄산음료 하루 1회 초과 섭취의 침식 오즈비 (Odds Ratio, OR) 1.98 (95%CI 1.42-2.77; 7,785명; I2 80%).

OR 합산에 쓰인 연구는 5편뿐이고 노출 정의가 제각각이며 단면연구·자가보고 위주여서 인과가 아닌 관찰적 연관 신호로 해석해야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Adolescent-specific pooled prevalence of dental erosion: 37.6% (95%CI 26.3-49.7).
- Pooled OR 1.98 for high-frequency (more than once daily, or more than 7 per week) carbonated drink intake.
- Narrows scope to carbonated (sugar-containing, effervescent) beverages rather than all acidic drinks.
- Qualitative tally: 19 of 24 studies (74%) reported a significant association.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, LILACS, SciELO; 2000 to 17 Dec 2023; English/Spanish; PEO framework.
- Included case-control, cross-sectional, cohort; excluded case reports, reviews, in vitro, insufficient data, severity-stratified data.
- Outcome: presence/absence of erosive lesions (BEWE 8 studies, TWI/Smith and Knight 6, O'Sullivan 4, Lussi 3, SEPRS 2, Carlsson 1).
- Risk of bias: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale; 24 studies scoring at least 5 included.
- Prevalence: MedCalc, Freeman-Tukey transform, DerSimonian-Laird random effects. OR: RevMan 5.4.1, Mantel-Haenszel, random effects. Egger test and funnel plot.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 131 records, 101 after de-duplication, 24 included; 21,541 participants; study prevalences 1.4% to 80%.
- Prevalence meta-analysis 37.6% (95%CI 26.3-49.7; the forest-plot caption prints the lower bound as 26.6).
- Association meta-analysis: OR 1.98 (95%CI 1.42-2.77), 7,785 participants, I2 = 80%, p < 0.0001; only 5 studies combinable.
- Study-level examples: Al-Hadi 2013 drinking before bedtime OR 7.8 (3.94-15.42); Shahbaz 2016 more than 8 per week OR 3.87 (1.43-10.49); Marro 2018 more than once daily OR 2.13 (1.38-3.28); Arnadottir 2003 (over 800 ml/day) OR 1.7 (0.5-5.0) not significant; Kirthiga 2015 no association (prevalence 1.4%).
- Quality: 20 studies good, 4 fair; comparability the weakest domain (11 studies). Egger 7.38 (95%CI -14.05 to 28.81; p = 0.479) with an asymmetric funnel plot.
- Modifiers reported in primary studies: night-time intake, slow sipping, holding drink in mouth, milk as protective, xerostomia.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Mostly cross-sectional with self-report; recall bias and no temporal sequence, possible overestimation.
- Exposure defined by frequency in some studies and volume in others; only about 25% of eligible studies met homogeneity criteria for the OR pooling (the authors' figure; 5 of 24 is about 21%).
- I2 = 80%; the authors advise cautious interpretation. Funnel asymmetry acknowledged.
- Sex-stratified analysis impossible because primary studies did not report it.
- Diagnostic indices varied; severity was not analysed.
- Authors call for longitudinal designs, standardised criteria, validated questionnaires.

## 7. Glossary

- Dental erosion: irreversible non-bacterial acid loss of tooth structure.
- BEWE: Basic Erosive Wear Examination, sextant-based 0-3 score.
- PEO: Population, Exposure, Outcome framework.
- NOS: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale for observational study quality.
- Egger test: regression test for funnel-plot asymmetry.
