## Why Ingested

Reference-mined from [[wiki/immediate-implant/alqutaibi-2026-root-analog-dental-implants]] — this Aldebes 2022 trial is the SR's **sole included RCT** (of 28 total included studies: 16 clinical reports, 1 retrospective case series, 7 prospective case series, 2 prospective nonrandomized studies, 1 RCT, 1 retrospective comparative study). Ingesting it directly gives the wiki a primary-source anchor for the SR's highest-evidence-weight data point (root-analogue/anatomic custom zirconia implant, thorny macro-retentions, PFM-vs-composite crown comparison, RoB 2 moderate risk per the SR's appraisal).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-center RCT (n = 20 enrolled, 18 completed all phases) at Damascus University comparing porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) vs indirect composite resin (ICR) crowns on custom-made, thorny-macroretentive, root-analogue anatomic zirconia implants placed immediately (flapless, un-drilled) into fresh premolar extraction sockets.

18-month implant survival was 90% (2/20 early failures before loading, both before crown placement); mean marginal bone resorption (MBR) at 18 months was 0.61–0.77 mm across mesial/distal sites in both crown groups, with no statistically significant difference between PFM and ICR at either 12 or 18 months (all P > 0.05).

Restoration material (PFM vs composite) did not influence peri-implant crestal bone resorption on this root-analogue zirconia implant design — both are viable options — but the trial is small (n = 18 analyzed), non-split-mouth, single-center, and short-term (18 months), so it is hypothesis-generating rather than definitive.

## 세줄요약

다마스쿠스 대학교에서 진행된 전향적 단일기관 RCT(등록 20명, 전체 단계 완료 18개 임플란트)로, 발치 즉시(flapless, 무천공) 전치·소구치 발치와에 식립한 맞춤형 치근형태모사(root-analogue) 가시형 대구치 지르코니아 임플란트에서 도재금속관(PFM) vs 간접복합레진관(ICR) 보철물의 효과를 비교.

18개월 생존율 90%(로딩 전 조기 실패 2/20건), 18개월 평균 변연골흡수(MBR)는 근원심 부위 모두 0.61–0.77mm로 PFM·ICR 두 군 간 12·18개월 시점 모두 유의차 없음(모두 P>0.05).

이 치근형태모사 지르코니아 임플란트에서는 보철재료(PFM vs 복합레진)가 변연골흡수에 영향을 주지 않아 둘 다 viable하나, 표본이 작고(n=18) split-mouth 설계가 아니며 단일기관·단기추적(18개월)이라 확정적이기보다 가설생성적 근거임.

- **Study type**: Prospective, single-center, randomized controlled trial (CONSORT-reported)

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (per authors, and per the citing SR) clinical evaluation of restoration material's effect on marginal bone resorption around custom-made, root-analogue, anatomic zirconia implants.
- Novel implant design: modified, one-piece, root-analogue zirconia implant fabricated from an optical scan of the patient's own extracted premolar, with the buccal aspect minimally reduced (0.5 mm) to preserve buccal cortical bone and multiple thorn-like macro-retentions added to the apical two-thirds of the mesial/distal root surfaces (CAD/CAM-milled from Y-TZP, sandblasted, sintered at 1500°C, autoclaved).
- Placement protocol: flapless, finger-pressure + gentle mallet insertion into a curetted, un-drilled fresh extraction socket; Periotest used to confirm primary stability (negative PTV required).
- Randomized (random-number table) 1:1 allocation of osseointegrated implants to PFM vs ICR (GRADIA PLUS composite) crown restoration at 3-month loading, all cemented with self-cured glass-ionomer cement (GC Fuji I).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective RCT, CONSORT-reported, IRB-approved (Damascus University Research Ethics Committee, Reg. No. 2019-1593), ISRCTN-registered.
- **Inclusion**: good general/oral health; age >18; clear indication for premolar extraction; no acute local inflammation; premolar root length ≥10 mm; Type-2/Type-3 bone density; buccal cortical bone ≥1 mm.
- **Exclusion**: smoking, systemic disease affecting osseointegration (e.g., uncontrolled diabetes), pregnancy, poor oral hygiene, age >60, inadequate bone dimensions.
- **Implant fabrication**: extracted premolar scanned (Ceramill Map 400) → STL processed in exocad Matera → CAD/CAM-milled from Y-TZP zirconia blocks → sandblasted → sintered 1500°C/8h → cleaned/disinfected/autoclaved.
- **Surgical protocol**: chlorhexidine + povidone-iodine antisepsis, 4% articaine LA, flapless socket curettage, flapless finger-pressure + mallet implant insertion (no drilling), Periotest primary-stability check, baseline periapical radiograph. Post-op: amoxicillin/clavulanate 1000/62.5 mg BID×5d, paracetamol 1000 mg TID×2d, povidone-iodine mouthwash BID×1wk, soft diet ×1 month.
- **Randomization**: after 3-month healing, osseointegrated implants randomly allocated (random-number table) to PFM or ICR crown groups.
- **Outcome measurement**: digital periapical radiographs (paralleling technique) at 12 and 18 months; MBR = baseline marginal bone level minus follow-up level, measured mesially and distally, calibrated to implant's known height via Adobe Photoshop CC.
- **Statistics**: sample size via G*Power pilot; SPSS V26; independent t-tests comparing MBR means between groups; α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Enrollment/attrition**: 20 participants enrolled (7 M, 13 F); 2 early implant failures (before loading) → 18 implants (n=18) completed all phases (66.7% female overall).
- **Demographics**: age 21–56 (mean 35.2 ± 10.7); maxillary premolars 83.3% (44.4% first premolar, 55.6% second); implant length 10–14 mm (mean 12.2 ± 1.3); all had negative Periotest values (good primary stability) at placement.
- **Loading**: 18 implants randomized 9 PFM : 9 ICR at 3-month loading.
- **MBR at 12 months**: ICR 0.53 (±0.21) mm mesial / 0.60 (±0.14) mm distal vs PFM 0.67 (±0.16) mm / 0.61 (±0.27) mm — no significant difference (P = 0.91 mesial, P = 0.27 distal).
- **MBR at 18 months**: ICR 0.61 (±0.27) mm / 0.67 (±0.16) mm vs PFM 0.77 (±0.29) mm / 0.77 (±0.27) mm — no significant difference (P = 0.36 mesial, P = 0.25 distal). Overall MBR range across all implants: 0.2–1.2 mm.
- **Survival**: 18/20 = 90% at 18 months; all failures occurred early, before crown loading; no complications (pain, mobility, continuous radiolucency, peri-implant infection) at implant/peri-implant/prosthetic levels through 18 months.
- **Benchmarking**: authors compare favorably to Bradley et al.'s 5-year cohort (94.3% survival, all failures pre-restoration) and to conventional success criteria (≤1–1.5 mm bone loss in year 1).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Not split-mouth — age/gender confounding not eliminated by design (though baseline differences were statistically negligible, P > 0.05).
- Small sample (n=18 analyzed, 9 per crown-material arm) — likely underpowered for the between-group MBR comparison; authors themselves frame results as preliminary.
- Single-center, single surgical team, short follow-up (18 months) — generalizability limited.
- Cases restricted to single-rooted/fused-rooted straight premolars; anatomic RAI feasibility in multi-rooted/curved-root teeth (e.g., molars) not addressed.
- Authors explicitly call for long-term studies assessing this implant type's performance.
- Declared conflict of interest: two authors (A.A., J.A.N.) hold a patent on the "Modified Zirconia Dental Implants" design used in this study (patent no. 6133, Syria) — a relevant bias consideration when weighing the reported 90% survival/favorable MBR results.

## 7. Glossary

- **RAI (Root-Analog Implant)**: custom implant fabricated to replicate the exact external morphology of an extracted tooth root, intended for immediate placement without socket drilling.
- **MBR (Marginal Bone Resorption)**: radiographically measured loss of crestal bone height around an implant, measured from mesial and distal aspects relative to a baseline radiograph.
- **PFM (Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal)**: crown restoration with a cast metal substructure veneered in ceramic; used here as the control/"gold standard" restoration.
- **ICR (Indirect Composite Resin)**: laboratory-fabricated composite resin crown (here, GRADIA PLUS heavy-body paste), lower modulus of elasticity than ceramic/metal.
- **Y-TZP (Yttria-stabilized Tetragonal Zirconia Polycrystal)**: the zirconia ceramic material milled to fabricate the implant body.
- **Periotest (PTV)**: device measuring implant/tooth mobility via a tapping rod; negative values indicate good primary stability.
- **Flapless placement**: implant insertion without raising a mucoperiosteal flap, here combined with no osteotomy drilling (implant seated directly into the curetted extraction socket).
