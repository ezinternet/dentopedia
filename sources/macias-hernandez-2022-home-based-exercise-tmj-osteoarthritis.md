## Why Ingested

TMD 물리치료(physiotherapy) 관련 아시아 저널(Journal of the Korean Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons) 논문을 PMC 풀텍스트로 확보하라는 요청에 따라 검색. 기존 [[tmj/kim-2023-treatment-tmj-osteoarthritis]]·[[tmj/song-2023-understanding-tmj-osteoarthritis-pathophysiology]]가 TMJ 골관절염(OA)의 진단·병태생리를 다루지만 물리치료 프로토콜의 구체적 효과 데이터가 없었고, 기존 [[tmj/zhang-2021-exercise-vs-splint-tmd-sr-ma]]·[[tmj/gonzalez-sanchez-2023-tmj-physiotherapy-treatment-sr]]는 TMD 전반의 운동치료 SR이나 OA 아형에 특화되지 않음. 본 논문(Macías-Hernández 2022)은 임상+방사선 진단 기준을 모두 충족한 TMJ OA 환자만을 대상으로 6개월 가정 운동 프로그램의 통증·기능(Helkimo)·초음파 구조 변화를 정량화해 OA 아형 보존치료 근거를 보강한다.

## One-line Summary

Single-group pre-post experimental study (n=15 patients, 26 TMJs with confirmed clinical+radiographic OA), Mexico; a 6-month home-based exercise program (thermotherapy, intra/extraoral massage, stretching, joint mobilization, strengthening) produced significant reductions in pain (VAS, p=0.001), improved Helkimo functionality index from severe to minimal dysfunction (p=0.001), and increased disc visibility/mouth-opening distance on ultrasound, with minimal NSAID use throughout follow-up.

## 한줄요약

단일군 전후비교 연구(n=15명, 26개 관절, 임상+방사선 확진 TMJ 골관절염), 6개월 가정 운동 프로그램(온열·구강내외 마사지·스트레칭·관절가동·강화운동)으로 통증(VAS, p=0.001)·Helkimo 기능지수(중증→경증, p=0.001)가 유의 개선되고 초음파상 관절원판 관찰률·개구거리도 증가, NSAID 사용은 미미했다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Applies a strict dual diagnostic gate (clinical AND radiographic Research Diagnostic Criteria for TMD Axis I Group IIIb) to isolate a pure TMJ osteoarthritis (OA) cohort, rather than mixed TMD populations used in most exercise-therapy studies.
- Combines three outcome domains in one longitudinal design: pain (VAS at palpation/mouth-opening/rest), function (Helkimo index), and structure (diagnostic ultrasound — disc visibility, osteophytes, synovitis, Doppler, opening distance).
- Fully describes a replicable 5-component home exercise protocol (thermotherapy → intraoral massage → extraoral massage → stretching → free mobilization → strengthening) with an adherence mechanism (diary + weekly phone calls).
- Reports very low rescue-analgesic (acetaminophen) use throughout 6 months, supporting the program as a low-risk, low-cost alternative before invasive TMJ OA interventions.
- Provides longitudinal ultrasound evidence that disc visualization improved (38.5% → 57.7% of joints) alongside functional gains, suggesting possible disc repositioning without direct structural (cartilage echogenicity) change.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: single-group experimental pre-post intervention study (no control group) — confidence tier is `prospective`, not RCT.
- Recruitment: 52 patients with chronic unilateral/bilateral TMJ pain screened via radiographs; 37 excluded for not meeting radiographic OA criteria; final analysis n=15 patients / 26 joints (all female, median age 57y, range 49–62y).
- Inclusion: RDC/TMD Axis I Group IIIb (TMJ OA) — joint pain at rest/opening/movement + crepitus; radiographic classification (joint space reduction, subchondral sclerosis, lateral flattening, condylar surface change, osteophytes) confirmed by two independent observers (oral/maxillofacial surgeon + musculoskeletal radiologist).
- Exclusion: other TMD axes, rheumatic joint disease, malocclusion, occlusal splint use in prior 6 months, prior intra-articular injection/surgery in prior 6 months.
- Intervention: one supervised training session by two physical therapists, then self-administered 3–5×/week for 6 months: (1) 15-min superficial heat, (2) intraoral massage (10 reps), (3) extraoral oil massage + neck massage (10 reps), (4) stretching (mouth-opening + lateral cheek deviation holds, 10s hold/5s rest ×3), (5) free joint mobilization (opening/closing, protrusion/retrusion, laterality, 10 reps each), (6) isometric strengthening (chin-resisted opening, protrusion, lateralization, molar-bite elevation; 10s hold/5s rest ×3).
- Adherence support: exercise diary (self-recorded + signed) + weekly phone check-ins.
- Rescue medication protocol: NSAIDs/glucosamine/chondroitin/collagen stopped 7 days pre-study; acetaminophen up to 1g/6h permitted for VAS >50mm, logged.
- Assessment timepoints: baseline, 1, 3, 6 months — VAS (palpation/opening/rest), Helkimo index, diagnostic ultrasound (echogenicity, disc visibility, Doppler, synovitis, osteophytes, opening distance).
- Statistics: Mann-Whitney U (independent), Wilcoxon (paired), Friedman (within-group over time), chi-square/Fisher's exact (categorical); α=0.05; SPSS v19.0.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Adherence: 4.52±1.23 sessions/week at 3 months, 3.62±0.41 sessions/week at 6 months; zero losses to follow-up.
- Pain (VAS, median): palpation 47.5→48mm, opening 51→49.5mm, rest 60→42.5mm from month-1 to month-6 (p=0.001 overall); largest single-step improvement was in the first month (57.5mm rest-pain reduction, 47.5mm opening-pain reduction from baseline — 67.5–92% reduction of baseline intensity).
- Function (Helkimo index, median total score): baseline 17 (severe dysfunction) → month-1 0.25 (free of TMJ problems) → months 3 and 6, 2 points (minimal dysfunction) (p=0.001).
- Structure (ultrasound): all joints remained linearly homogeneous (no echogenicity change); Doppler signal absent throughout; synovitis absent throughout; osteophytes detected in 2/26 joints (7.7%), unchanged; disc observation rate rose from 38.5% (baseline) to 57.7% (6 months); mouth-opening distance on ultrasound significantly increased by 6 months.
- Baseline radiographic findings (26 joints): joint-space reduction 92.3%, osteophytes 61.5%, subchondral sclerosis 53.8%, lateral flattening 38.5%, condylar surface change 26.9%.
- Rescue analgesic use: 20.0% of patients (19.2% of joints) at month 1; 0% at month 3; 26.7% of patients (23.1% of joints) at month 6 — median 1.25 days of use (range 0.5–2.5), no significant change over follow-up (p=0.12).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No control group — single-arm pre-post design cannot separate exercise effect from natural fluctuation, regression to the mean, or placebo/attention effect; authors explicitly note this means the study is not a clinical trial.
- Small sample (15 patients, 26 joints), partly a consequence of the strict dual clinical+radiographic inclusion criteria; all participants female, limiting generalizability to male TMJ OA patients.
- Authors call for a future adequately powered controlled clinical trial to confirm effect size and improve evidence quality (echoing the Cochrane TMJ-OA review's exclusion of exercise/physiotherapy for insufficient evidence quality).
- Ultrasound could not detect cartilage-level structural change (echogenicity unchanged), so mechanistic interpretation of the disc-visibility improvement (repositioning vs. detection artifact) remains speculative.

## 6. Related Work

- Tuncer et al. (RCT, home PT alone vs. home PT + facility manual therapy, mixed-etiology TMD, 1-month follow-up) — cited as a comparator; manual therapy showed extra benefit for palpation pain, but the present study's OA-specific, 6-month design is not directly comparable.
- Shimada et al. (review of TMJ exercise modalities) — cited to support that passive/active mobilization and postural exercise generally reduce joint/muscle pain, but flags methodological heterogeneity across TMD (sub)diagnoses.
- Systematic review + meta-analysis of manual therapy + exercise across 48 RCTs — cited: manual therapy + exercise combination showed better effect size vs. medication-alone/splints at 1–3 months, consistent with this study's direction of effect.
- Cochrane systematic review on TMJ OA interventions — cited: excluded exercise/physical therapy from its analysis due to insufficient evidence quality in the literature at the time; this paper is offered as data toward filling that gap.
- Calixtre et al. (n=12, mixed TMJ disorders, exercise + manual therapy + mobilization + stretching, 5-week follow-up) — cited as a similar-design comparator with a smaller sample, shorter follow-up, and mixed (non-OA-specific) diagnoses; see also [[tmj/calixtre-2015-manual-therapy-tmd-pain-rom-sr]] already in this wiki.

## 7. Glossary

- **TMJ OA**: Temporomandibular joint osteoarthritis — degenerative joint disease of the TMJ with radiographic changes (joint-space reduction, sclerosis, osteophytes, erosions) plus clinical pain/crepitus.
- **RDC/TMD Axis I Group IIIb**: Research Diagnostic Criteria for Temporomandibular Disorders classification subgroup corresponding to osteoarthritis/osteoarthrosis.
- **Helkimo Index**: Validated composite clinical index scoring TMJ mobility, function, and pain (0 = free of problems; higher = more severe dysfunction).
- **VAS**: Visual Analogue Scale for self-reported pain intensity (0–100mm).
- **MMO**: Maximum mouth opening.
- **Diagnostic (musculoskeletal) ultrasound**: Non-invasive imaging modality used here to assess TMJ echogenicity, disc position/visibility, Doppler flow (synovitis marker), and opening distance.
