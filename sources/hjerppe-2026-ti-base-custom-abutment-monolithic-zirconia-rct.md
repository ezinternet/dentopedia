## Why Ingested

First 3-year RCT comparing standardized Ti-Base vs customized titanium abutment for screw-retained monolithic zirconia crowns — directly addresses clinical workflow decision between stock Ti-Base (simpler, cheaper) and custom abutment for posterior implant single crowns. Extends [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/sterzenbach-2025-hybrid-abutment-crowns-zirconia-titanium-implants]].

## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=51 patients completing 3-year follow-up: Custom n=23, Ti-Base n=28) at University of Zurich; screw-retained monolithic ZrO₂ single crowns on standardized Ti-Base vs customized Ti abutments; primary: MBL change.
No crown losses in either group; MBL changes from baseline to 3 years comparable (p>0.05); 1 minor chipping in Ti-Base group (3.6% technical complication); patient satisfaction 100% Custom vs 92.9% Ti-Base (p>0.05).
Screw-retained monolithic ZrO₂ crowns on both Ti-Base and custom abutments show stable MBL, high survival, and high satisfaction at 3 years — Ti-Base is a clinically acceptable simpler alternative.

## 세줄요약

무작위대조시험(n=51명 완료: Custom 23명, Ti-Base 28명), 취리히대; 표준화 Ti-Base 어버트먼트 vs 맞춤형 Ti 어버트먼트(Customized Titanium Abutment) 위에 나사유지형 모놀리식 ZrO₂ 단관, 기저선·1년·3년 평가.
양군 크라운 소실 없음; 기저선~3년 변연골수준 변화(MBL Change) 비교 가능(p>0.05); Ti-Base군 소치핑 1건(기술적 합병증 3.6%); 환자 만족도 Custom 100% vs Ti-Base 92.9%(p>0.05 NS).
3년 시점 두 어버트먼트 방식 모두 안정적 MBL, 높은 생존율·만족도 — Ti-Base는 맞춤형 어버트먼트의 임상적으로 허용 가능한 대안.

## Key Contributions

- 3-year RCT showing no significant MBL difference between Ti-Base and custom abutment for monolithic ZrO₂ crowns
- Both groups: 0 crown losses; 1 minor chipping only in Ti-Base group (3.6%)
- High patient satisfaction in both groups at 3 years; no major technical complications
- Supports Ti-Base as a cost-effective, protocol-simplified alternative to custom milled abutments

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized controlled clinical trial (interim 3-year analysis)
- **Setting**: University of Zurich
- **n**: 51 patients (Custom n=23, Ti-Base n=28) at 3-year follow-up
- **Intervention**: Posterior implant; screw-retained monolithic ZrO₂ crown on standardized Ti-Base vs customized Ti abutment
- **Outcomes**: MBL change, implant/crown survival, biological/technical complications, PROMs (patient satisfaction)
- **Follow-up**: Baseline, 1 year, 3 years

## Key Results

| Outcome | Custom Ti | Ti-Base |
|---|---|---|
| Crown losses | 0 | 0 |
| MBL change BL→3Y | Comparable (p>0.05) | Comparable (p>0.05) |
| Major technical complications | 0 | 0 |
| Minor chipping | 0 | 1 (3.6%) |
| Patient satisfaction (very satisfied) | 100% | 92.9% (p>0.05) |
| Biological complications | No significant group difference | |

## Limitations

- Interim 3-year analysis; final results pending longer follow-up
- Relatively small per-group sample sizes (n=23 vs n=28)
- Single-center study; generalizability limited
- Full paper data (e.g., PES, detailed bleeding scores) not available from abstract

## Glossary

- **Ti-Base (표준화 티타늄 베이스)**: 공장 규격으로 제작된 임플란트-어버트먼트 연결부; ZrO₂ 크라운을 시멘트로 접착 후 나사유지
- **Customized Ti Abutment (맞춤형 티타늄 어버트먼트)**: CAD/CAM으로 각 케이스에 맞게 개별 가공된 Ti 어버트먼트
- **Screw-Retained Crown (나사유지형 크라운)**: 시멘트 없이 나사로 고정되는 임플란트 크라운; 제거 용이
- **PROMs (Patient-Reported Outcome Measures)**: 환자 자가 보고 결과 지표
