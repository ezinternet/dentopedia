## Why Ingested

[[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/siawasch-2025-autologous-platelet-concentrates-alveolar-ridge]] (SR+MA of APC in ARP, Periodontology 2000 2024)는 임상 결과(치조제 크기, 통증) 전반을 다루지만, **신생 골 형성(vital bone formation)의 조직형태계측(histomorphometry)** 에 특화된 SR+MA는 없었다. Caponio 2023은 RCT 8편·255발치와·10–24주 시점 생검 데이터만으로 메타분석해 혈소판 농축물(Platelet Concentrate, PC)의 골 형성 촉진 효과를 SMD 1.77로 정량화한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review, meta-analysis, and trial sequential analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022340941) of 8 RCTs evaluating histomorphometric new vital bone formation in alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) with platelet concentrates (PCs: L-PRF or P-PRP) vs. spontaneous healing; 255 teeth, 250 patients, 10–24 weeks follow-up.

PCs significantly improved new vital bone formation vs. spontaneous healing (SMD=1.77, 95% CI 1.47–2.06, p<0.001); no significant difference between L-PRF and P-PRP subtypes; TSA confirmed conclusion after 6 studies and RIS reached — evidence conclusive.

No differences between PC types were found (L-PRF SMD=1.75 vs. P-PRP SMD=1.58, ANOVA p=0.71); meta-regression showed trend toward greater bone formation at longer follow-up; study heterogeneity moderate (I²=44%); two studies at high risk of bias.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석·순차적시험분석 (PROSPERO CRD42022340941) — RCT 8편, 255발치와·250명, 혈소판 농축물(Platelet Concentrate, PC: L-PRF 또는 P-PRP) vs. 자연치유의 10–24주 시점 조직형태계측 신생골 형성률 비교.

PC군이 자연치유군 대비 신생 활성골 형성률을 유의하게 높임 (SMD=1.77, 95% CI 1.47–2.06, p<0.001); L-PRF vs. P-PRP 간 유의한 차이 없음; 순차적시험분석에서 6편 추가 후 필요정보량(RIS) 달성 → 결론 확정.

PC 유형 간 차이 없음 (L-PRF SMD=1.75 vs. P-PRP SMD=1.58, p=0.71); 메타회귀에서 추적 기간이 길수록 신생골 형성 증가 경향; 이질성 중등도 (I²=44%); 2편 비뚤림 위험 높음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA focused exclusively on histomorphometric vital bone formation from PCs in ARP (excludes dimensional change and clinical soft-tissue outcomes); answers distinct PICO from dimension-focused SRs.
- TSA analysis confirms sufficient evidence — RIS reached after 6 studies; no additional RCTs needed to confirm PC superiority over spontaneous healing for bone formation.
- Shows no clinically meaningful difference between L-PRF and P-PRP for bone formation — type of PC matters less than applying one.
- Identifies meta-regression finding: bone formation benefit may increase at longer follow-up — supports delayed implant placement timelines.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **PICO**: T18+ adults undergoing atraumatic extraction → ARP with PC alone (L-PRF or P-PRP) vs. blood clot alone → histomorphometric vital bone formation % at ≥10 weeks
- **Exclusion**: Third molar sockets; head/neck RT; bone/immune/uncontrolled DM; PC + other materials; follow-up <10 weeks
- **n**: 8 RCTs (6 parallel-group + 2 split-mouth); 120 L-PRF sockets, 31 P-PRP sockets, 104 controls
- **Sites**: Mostly single-rooted anterior maxilla; 6 studies European, 2 Brazilian, 1 South African
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB 2 — 1 low, 3 some concerns, 2 high risk; 2 split-mouth assessed with extended CONSORT
- **Statistics**: Fixed/random effects by Hedges' g (I²<50% → fixed); TSA with 5%/10% error, 7% MID

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary**: SMD=1.77 (95% CI 1.47–2.06), p<0.001 favoring PCs; fixed effect model (I²=44%)
- **L-PRF subgroup**: SMD=1.75 (1.21–2.28), p<0.001; includes both split-mouth studies, I²=58%
- **P-PRP subgroup**: SMD=1.58 (0.87–2.29), p<0.001; I²=0%
- **L-PRF vs. P-PRP**: ANOVA Q-test p=0.71 — no significant difference
- **Leave-one-out**: Castro et al. removal → SMD=1.65; Du Toit removal → SMD=1.87; range confirms robustness
- **Publication bias**: Trim-and-fill 0 trimmed; Egger p=0.44; safe N=239 (needs 239 negative studies to overturn)
- **TSA**: Cumulative Z-curve crossed monitoring boundary after study 3; RIS reached after study 6 → conclusive

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 2 RCTs used P-PRP; results predominantly L-PRF evidence.
- Heterogeneity in PC preparation protocols (rpm, centrifugation time, rotor radius) — even same-named PC may differ biologically.
- Alveolar ridge dimension change not analyzed (complementary outcome to bone formation).
- No patient-reported outcomes except Anitua et al. (P-PRP: lower pain in first week).
- Cannot determine which PC to use clinically — simplest/cheapest (PRF technique) preferred unless combining with biomaterial (then PRP).

## 7. Glossary

- **Histomorphometry**: Quantitative analysis of bone tissue composition in biopsy specimens (% vital bone, connective tissue, residual graft)
- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference)**: Effect size in SD units; SMD >0.8 = large effect
- **TSA**: Trial Sequential Analysis — adjusts for repeated significance testing and calculates required information size
- **RIS**: Required Information Size — minimum total patients needed to confirm the result
- **L-PRF**: Leukocyte- and Platelet-Rich Fibrin (no anticoagulant, second-gen PC)
- **P-PRP**: Pure Platelet-Rich Plasma (with anticoagulant, first-gen PC)
