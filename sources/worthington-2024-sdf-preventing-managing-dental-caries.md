## Why Ingested
SDF (은 불화 디아민, Silver Diamine Fluoride)에 대한 최초의 완성된 Cochrane SR. 기존 [[caries/banihani-2021-minimal-intervention-primary-teeth-carious-umbrella-review]] MI 접근법 umbrella review와 연계해 비침습 우식 관리 근거 보강. 소아 및 노인에서의 임상 의사결정에 직접 활용.

## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (29 RCTs, 13,036 participants, pub2): SDF probably prevents new root caries vs placebo (moderate certainty); evidence uncertain for most other caries outcomes; staining impact poorly reported.

## 한줄요약
코크란 SR (RCT 29편, 13,036명): 불화 은 (Silver Diamine Fluoride, SDF)은 치근 우식 예방에서 중등도 근거, 나머지 대부분 결과는 근거 매우 불확실; 흑색 착색 영향 미보고.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2024
- DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD012718.pub2
- PMID: 39508296 | PMC: PMC11542151
- Evidence current to: June 2023

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest Cochrane SR on SDF for caries: 29 RCTs, 13,036 participants
- Only moderate-certainty finding: SDF probably prevents new root caries (MD -0.79 surfaces, 95% CI -1.40 to -0.17)
- Establishes very low certainty as the standard for most SDF vs placebo/comparator outcomes
- Covers both primary dentition (children) and permanent dentition (older adults for root caries)
- Identifies critical research gap: staining impact and clustering analysis

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 29 RCTs (12,020 children, 1016 older adults)
- Comparisons: SDF vs placebo/no treatment; different SDF approaches; SDF vs fluoride varnish; SDF vs sealants/resin infiltration; SDF vs ART + GIC
- Outcomes: primary caries prevention (caries increment change), caries arrest, secondary prevention, adverse effects, dental pain/sensitivity, aesthetics
- Databases: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Oral Health Trial Register, 2 clinical trial registers; searched June 2023
- All 29 studies had high risks of bias
- Certainty: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
SDF vs placebo/no treatment (14 studies):
| Outcome | Effect | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| Root caries prevention | MD -0.79 (95% CI -1.40 to -0.17) | Moderate |
| Primary dentition caries prevention | Possibly helps | Very low |
| Coronal permanent dentition prevention | Possibly helps | Very low |
| Primary dentition caries arrest | MD 0.86 (0.39–1.33) | Low |
| Permanent dentition caries arrest | Very uncertain | Very low |
| Adverse effects | Very uncertain | Very low |

SDF vs fluoride varnish (8 studies):
- Primary dentition caries prevention: MD 0.00 (95% CI -0.26 to 0.26) — may be little/no difference (Low)
- Other outcomes: very uncertain

Different SDF approaches (5 studies): Very low certainty — cannot determine if any approach superior.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- All studies had high risks of bias
- SDF stains teeth black/dark brown — blinding impossible → knowledge of treatment may affect oral hygiene
- Most evidence from individual studies; insufficient for pooling in many comparisons
- Staining impact (aesthetics, patient acceptability) poorly reported
- Clustering issues within participants not addressed in most studies

## 7. Glossary
- SDF (불화 은, Silver Diamine Fluoride): 치아에 도포하는 무색/은색 액체; 불소와 은 이온이 우식 억제 작용; 흑색 착색 부작용
- ART (비외상성 수복 치료, Atraumatic Restorative Treatment): 버 없이 손도구로 우식 제거 후 GIC 충전
- GIC (글라스 아이오노머 시멘트, Glass Ionomer Cement): 불소 방출, 화학 결합, 치수자극성 낮은 수복재
- 치근 우식 (Root Caries): 노출된 치근면의 우식 — 노인·건조증 환자에서 호발
