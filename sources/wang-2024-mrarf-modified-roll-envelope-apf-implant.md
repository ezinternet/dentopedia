## Why Ingested

Describes MRARF — a graft-free, combined flap technique for simultaneous keratinized mucosa width and soft tissue thickness augmentation at second-stage implant surgery. Extends the graft-free repertoire catalogued in [[implants/soft-tissue/jung-2021-flap-folding-suture-soft-tissue-implant]] and provides case-level evidence complementing the SR in [[implants/soft-tissue/bassetti-2016-soft-tissue-augmentation-second-stage-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

MRARF combines a modified roll envelope flap with an apically repositioned flap at second-stage implant surgery to simultaneously correct buccal concavity, insufficient keratinized tissue width, and soft tissue thinness.
Four cases treated July 2021–February 2022 all healed uneventfully; harmonious color, texture, and mucogingival junction position matching adjacent tissues were achieved.
The technique is graft-free, minimally invasive, and suitable for implant sites with combined labial/buccal deficiency and reduced keratinized mucosa width.

## 세줄요약

MRARF는 변형 롤 봉투 판막 (Modified Roll Envelope Flap)과 치조정접근 판막 (Apically Repositioned Flap, ARF)을 결합하여 임플란트 2차 수술 시 협측 함몰·각화점막 폭·연조직 두께를 동시에 교정하는 술식이다.
2021–2022년 4증례 모두 합병증 없이 치유되었으며, 인접 치아와 조화로운 색조·질감·치은순행연접부(Mucogingival Junction, MGJ) 위치를 달성하였다.
이식재 없이 최소 침습으로 수직·수평 심미 치은 윤곽 및 충분한 각화점막 폭·두께를 얻을 수 있었다.

- **Type**: Case series (n = 4)
- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Implantology 2024;50(5):507–513
- **Institution**: Department of Periodontology, The Second Affiliated Hospital, College of Medicine, Zhejiang University, Hangzhou, China
- **Keywords**: apical repositioned flap, peri-implant soft tissue phenotype, roll envelope flap, second-stage surgery

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduces MRARF as a combined technique addressing three simultaneous soft-tissue deficiencies at second-stage surgery: (1) buccal contour concavity, (2) inadequate keratinized tissue width, (3) insufficient soft tissue thickness.
- Demonstrates graft-free augmentation without connective tissue graft or free gingival graft.
- Achieves harmonious mucogingival esthetics (color, texture, MGJ position) matching adjacent teeth in all four cases.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Patient selection criteria**:
- Received dental implants + guided bone regeneration (GBR) 6 months prior
- Presenting for second-stage surgery
- Each implant site showed labial/buccal deficiency AND reduced keratinized mucosa width vs. adjacent teeth

**Technique sequence**:
1. Modified roll envelope flap design incorporating the peri-implant tissue
2. Apical repositioning of the flap to increase keratinized tissue width
3. Suture removal at 2 weeks
4. Provisional restoration delivered at 2 weeks
5. Final impression at 6 weeks for definitive implant-supported restoration

**Timeframe**: July 2021 – February 2022 (n = 4 patients)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- All surgical sites healed uneventfully
- No postoperative pain or excessive swelling reported
- Increased width and thickness of keratinized mucosa achieved in all cases
- Harmonious color, texture, and MGJ position matching surrounding tissue and adjacent teeth in all cases
- Patient satisfaction reported as high for final esthetic results
- Satisfactory vertical and horizontal aesthetic gingival contours achieved

*Note: Quantitative measurements (mm change in KM width/thickness) are not reported in the abstract; full text required for numeric outcomes.*

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n = 4); no control group
- Short-term follow-up (outcome assessed at 6-week restoration delivery, longer data not reported in abstract)
- Technique applicability limited to sites with both buccal deficiency and reduced KM width
- Absence of quantified outcome measurements in abstract-level reporting
- Controlled clinical trials needed to compare MRARF with CTG-based or other graft-free approaches

## 7. Glossary

- **MRARF**: Modified Roll Envelope technique combined with Apically Repositioned Flap
- **Roll envelope flap**: A pedicle flap technique that rolls subepithelial connective tissue to augment ridge volume; modified here for peri-implant use
- **Apically repositioned flap (ARF)**: Flap displaced apically to expose more keratinized tissue at the implant neck
- **Peri-implant soft tissue phenotype (PSP)**: Composite descriptor of KM width, soft tissue thickness, and gingival/mucosal architecture around implants
- **Buccal contour concavity**: Alveolar bone/soft tissue deficit on the facial aspect creating an inward profile
- **GBR**: Guided Bone Regeneration — bone augmentation procedure performed 6 months prior in these cases
- **Mucogingival junction (MGJ)**: Boundary between keratinized gingiva and alveolar mucosa
