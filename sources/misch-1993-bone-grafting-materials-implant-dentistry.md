## Why Ingested

The `bone-regeneration` folder needs the classical classification of graft materials by mode of action (osteogenic, osteoinductive, osteoconductive), and this Misch and Dietsh 1993 review is the foundational source for that taxonomy. It is ingested as the reference that later graft-material pages can cite for definitions. Related wiki pages: [[bone-regeneration/bone-regeneration]], [[overviews/bone-graft-material-selection-matrix-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Review (Implant Dentistry, 1993) classifying bone-grafting materials by mode of action (autogenous, allograft, alloplast) and selecting them by defect morphology — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

Autogenous bone is osteogenic, osteoinductive and osteoconductive; allografts are osteoinductive and osteoconductive; alloplasts are osteoconductive only, with dense, macroporous and microporous types. Small defects with four host walls can use alloplasts, while losing three or more walls calls for autogenous bone or a small-pore membrane.

The abstract gives a classification and selection logic without clinical outcome data; newer materials are not covered.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Implant Dentistry, 1993) — 작용 기전 (자가골, 동종골, 합성골) 에 따른 골이식재 분류와 결손 형태에 따른 선택 — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

자가골은 골형성·골유도·골전도, 동종골은 골유도·골전도, 합성골은 골전도만 있으며, 치밀형·대공형·미세공형으로 나뉨. 벽이 4개 남은 작은 결손은 합성골로, 벽이 3개 이상 소실되면 자가골 추가 또는 소공 막이 필요.

초록은 분류와 선택 논리를 제시하며 임상 결과와 최신 재료는 포함하지 않음.
