## Why Ingested

Drug-induced xerostomia in older adults is a recurring question; [[geriatric-dentistry/halasabalu-kalgeri-2026-polypharmacy-oral-health-elderly]] asserts anticholinergic-driven xerostomia mechanistically, and [[oral-medicine/salivary-chemosensory/ramirez-martinez-acitores-2020-antihypertensive-xerostomia-salivary-flow-sr]] found inconclusive evidence for antihypertensives. This systematic review supplies the anticholinergic-specific primary-study evidence (use and burden scales) in people aged 65 or older.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PubMed, CINAHL, Embase to August 2022; PRISMA/CRD) of anticholinergic use or burden versus xerostomia and/or xerophthalmia in people aged 65 or older; 1211 records, 10 studies included (mostly observational, one RCT).

Anticholinergic use was significantly associated with xerostomia in 4 studies; high anticholinergic burden was more closely associated with xerostomia than low/intermediate burden; xerostomia prevalence 10.6-59.2% and xerophthalmia 5.1-34%; the single RCT (ADS reduced by 2 units) showed no salivary flow change at 4 weeks.

Evidence is limited by heterogeneous, mostly non-validated measurement tools and incomplete confounder control; xerophthalmia evidence is too sparse to conclude.

## 세줄요약

65세 이상 노인에서 항콜린 약물 사용·항콜린 부담 (Anticholinergic burden)과 구강건조증 (Xerostomia)·안구건조증 (Xerophthalmia)의 연관성을 본 체계적 문헌고찰 (PubMed, CINAHL, Embase, 2022년 8월까지); 1211편 검색, 10편 포함 (대부분 관찰연구, 무작위대조시험 1편).

항콜린 약물 사용은 4편에서 구강건조증과 유의한 연관; 높은 항콜린 부담에서 연관이 더 뚜렷하고 낮음~중간 부담에서는 불명확; 구강건조증 유병률 10.6-59.2%, 안구건조증 5.1-34%; 유일한 무작위대조시험 (ADS 2점 감소)은 4주 후 침 분비 변화 없음.

검증되지 않은 다양한 측정 도구와 교란변수 통제 미흡이 주요 한계이며, 안구건조증은 연구가 너무 적어 결론 불가.

- Journal: BMC Pharmacology & Toxicology 2022;23(1):94, published 2022-12-21. Source: PubMed (PMID 36539885), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40360-022-00637-8). PMC9769019.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Described as the first review of anticholinergic use/burden and peripheral effects (xerostomia, xerophthalmia).
- Concludes a closer connection between high anticholinergic burden or anticholinergic use and xerostomia; very few data for xerophthalmia.
- Proposes future work to define burden thresholds and use dose-aware scales (e.g., Drug Burden Index).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: systematic review per CRD principles and PRISMA; PICO-based search in PubMed, CINAHL, Embase until August 2022; English/Spanish.
- Inclusion: observational or experimental studies, age 65 or older, relating anticholinergic activity (risk/burden) or therapy to xerostomia or xerophthalmia. Excluded: letters, dissertations, case reports/series, conference abstracts.
- Two reviewers screened; discrepancies resolved by two external reviewers.
- Appraisal: JBI (cross-sectional), Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (cohort/case-control), Cochrane RoB 2 (RCT). No meta-analysis.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Flow: 1211 records, 11 duplicates removed, 1136 excluded at title/abstract, 64 pre-selected, 10 included.
- Design counts and totals are inconsistent within the paper: the abstract reports six cross-sectional, two cohort, one case-control, one RCT and 3535 patients; the full text reports six descriptive, three analytical observational, one trial and 3622 patients (mean age over 75).
- Xerostomia prevalence 10.6-59.2% (seven studies); xerophthalmia 5.1-34% (three studies). Anticholinergic exposure 3.9-100%. Antihistamines most prevalent class in three of five studies reporting classes.
- Scales: ARS (three studies), ADS (two), DBI and ACB (one each in addition to ARS/ADS in one study).
- Four studies: significant association between anticholinergic use and xerostomia; one study: no association with xerophthalmia.
- Burden: positive association with high vs lower or no burden; one study found no association when any burden was compared with none; Rudolph et al. used a compound peripheral-effects variable.
- RCT (median ADS reduced by 2 units): no salivary difference at 4 weeks.
- Quality: two cross-sectional studies adequate (at least 7 of 8 JBI items); cohorts NOS 6 and 5 of 9; case-control NOS 6; RCT low risk of bias.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Few studies, mostly observational, only one RCT; xerophthalmia data sparse.
- Non-validated xerostomia tools (Xerostomia Inventory not used); at least four different questionnaires.
- Several studies had no confounder adjustment; no study addressed head and neck radiotherapy, eye surgery, or Sjögren syndrome.
- Anticholinergic scales differ (ARS 49 drugs vs ADS 117).

## 7. Glossary

- Anticholinergic burden: cumulative effect of one or more drugs with anticholinergic activity.
- ADS / ARS / ACB: Anticholinergic Drug Scale / Anticholinergic Risk Scale / Anticholinergic Cognitive Burden scale.
- DBI: Drug Burden Index, accounts for dose of anticholinergic/sedative drugs.
- Xerostomia: subjective dry mouth, not necessarily hyposalivation.
- Xerophthalmia: inadequate tear production (dry eye).
