## Why Ingested

TMD 보존적 치료 근거군에 저출력레이저치료 (Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT) 단독-효능을 검증한 이중맹검·위약대조 RCT를 추가. 같은 배치로 함께 인제스트되는 [[tmj/nemani-2024-tmj-pain-treatment-modalities-cervical]]가 LLLT를 여러 치료 양식 중 하나로 다룬 multi-arm 설계인 반면, 본 연구(Desai 2021)는 위약 대조군 대비 LLLT만을 격리해 20회기(8주) 프로토콜로 검증한 더 엄격한(rigorous) 이중맹검 설계라 LLLT 특이적 효능 근거를 보강한다. [[tmj/koole-2019-spray-stretch-technique-mouth-opening]] 등 비침습적 물리치료 계열과 함께 TMD 보존치료 스펙트럼의 한 축을 이룬다.

## Three-line Summary

Double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT (n=60 TMD patients, Indian Army Dental Corps setting) comparing 20 sessions of low-level laser therapy (LLLT, applied in closed-mouth and maximum-mouth-opening positions) versus placebo laser over 8 weeks.

Both groups improved in pain, maximum mouth opening, clicking, and mandibular deviation, but the LLLT group showed statistically better treatment outcomes than the placebo group.

LLLT is a viable non-surgical adjunct for TMD symptom management with a favorable safety profile; abstract-only retrieval (PMC full text body was empty for this record) limits access to exact statistical values and laser parameters (wavelength, dose, power).

## 세줄요약

이중맹검·위약대조 RCT (n=60명 측두하악장애 환자, 인도 육군 치과단 세팅): 저출력레이저치료 (Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT, 폐구위·최대개구위 양쪽 조사) 20회기 vs 위약 레이저, 8주간 시행.

두 군 모두 통증·최대개구량·클리킹·하악 편위에서 호전을 보였으나, LLLT 군이 위약군보다 통계적으로 더 나은 치료 결과를 보임.

LLLT는 안전한 비수술적 TMD 보조요법으로 활용 가능; 단, 본 레코드는 PMC 전문 본문이 비어 있어 초록만 확보되었고, 정확한 통계치·레이저 파라미터(파장·용량·출력)는 파악 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Isolates LLLT as the sole variable versus a true placebo (sham laser) arm, in a double-blind design — a more rigorous test of LLLT-specific efficacy than multi-modality comparison trials
- Applies LLLT in both closed-mouth and maximum-mouth-opening positions across 20 sessions over 8 weeks, a relatively intensive dosing protocol
- Assesses four clinically relevant TMD outcome domains together: pain, maximum mouth opening, clicking, and mandibular deviation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
- **Sample**: n=60 patients diagnosed with TMD, randomized into two groups of 30
  - Group I: placebo (sham) laser
  - Group II: active LLLT
- **Intervention**: 20 sessions of LLLT (or placebo) over 8 weeks, applied in both closed-mouth position and maximum mouth opening position
- **Outcome measures**: pain, maximum mouth opening, clicking, mandibular deviation
- Statistical analysis performed on collected outcome data (specific tests/values not available from abstract)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Both groups (placebo and LLLT) showed improvement in pain reduction, maximum mouth opening, and reduction in deviation and clicking
- The LLLT group demonstrated a **better treatment outcome** than the placebo group across these measures
- Exact effect sizes, p-values, and laser parameters (wavelength, power output, energy density) are not recoverable from the abstract alone

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest — PMC full-text body was empty for this record; specific laser device parameters, statistical values, and detailed methodology are not available
- Placebo group also improved, suggesting a nonspecific/regression-to-mean or attention-effect component that the abstract does not quantify against the LLLT-specific gain
- Small single-arm-comparison sample (n=60, 30/group) from a single institutional (military dental) setting; generalizability to broader civilian TMD populations unclear from the abstract

## 7. Glossary

- **TMD** (Temporomandibular Disorders, 측두하악장애): disorders affecting the muscles of mastication, temporomandibular joint, and associated structures
- **LLLT** (Low-Level Laser Therapy, 저출력레이저치료): low-intensity laser application used as a non-surgical, non-thermal therapeutic modality for pain and inflammation
- **Maximum mouth opening** (최대개구량): interincisal distance measured at maximal unassisted mouth opening, a standard TMD functional outcome measure
