## Why Ingested

This case report directly quantifies long-term DBBM resorption in a human sinus graft, showing 96.4% vital bone at 16 years — providing rare in-vivo histological evidence that complements the DBBM overview ([[overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]]) and extends the narrative of residual DBBM stability beyond the 11-year sinus observation noted in [[bone-regeneration/benic-2014-horizontal-bone-augmentation-guided-regeneration]]. The novel clinical application — harvesting that mature sinus bone as an intraoral autograft — has direct implications for GBR decision-making when a previously augmented sinus is present.

## Three-line Summary

This case report describes a novel 유도 골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) technique using bone cores harvested from a maxillary sinus previously augmented with 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 16 years ago.
Histological analysis showed DBBM comprised only 3.6% of total bone volume (1.35 mm³ of 38.83 mm³), while 96.4% was newly-formed vital bone (37.48 mm³); the crushed bone core was used successfully for GBR around a new implant, with stable crestal bone levels at 2-year follow-up.
A previously grafted sinus can serve as a viable intraoral donor site for further bone grafting, as DBBM is largely replaced by new bone over the long term, providing osteogenic potential.

## 세줄요약

본 증례 보고는 16년 전 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)로 상악동 거상술을 시행받은 부위에서 채취한 골 코어를 사용한 새로운 유도 골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 기술을 설명합니다.
채취된 골 코어의 조직학적 분석 결과, DBBM이 전체 골 부피의 3.6%(1.35 mm³/38.83 mm³)만을 차지하고 96.4%(37.48 mm³)는 신생골이었으며, 분쇄 골 코어를 사용한 GBR 후 2년 추적에서 안정적인 변연골 수준이 관찰되었습니다.
이전에 이식된 상악동이 장기간에 걸쳐 DBBM을 신생골로 대체하므로 추가 골이식을 위한 구강내 공여부 역할을 할 수 있음을 시사하나, 증례 수가 1명으로 일반화에 한계가 있습니다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Bone core harvested from a 16-year-old DBBM-augmented sinus used for GBR around a new implant.
- 미세 전산화 단층촬영 (Micro-Computed Tomography, micro-CT) analysis: total bone volume 38.83 mm³.
- New bone formation: 37.48 mm³ (96.4%) of total volume.
- Residual DBBM particles: only 1.35 mm³ (3.6%) of total volume.
- Histology revealed highly vascularized soft tissue, mature and immature bone, and osteoclasts around residual DBBM particles.
- Crushed bone core (1–3 mm fragments) used as autogenous graft material for peri-implant osseous defect GBR.
- Crestal bone levels remained stable throughout 2-year follow-up post-prosthesis.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Case report
- **n**: 1 patient (63-year-old male, severe chronic periodontitis)
- **History**: Bilateral maxillary sinus augmentation (MSA) with DBBM 16 years prior
- **Intervention**: Implant placement + two bone cores harvested from lateral wall of previously augmented sinus using 4.0 mm trephine drill. One core for histology, other crushed (1–3 mm) and used as GBR graft, covered with resorbable collagen membrane.
- **Analysis**: micro-CT (SkyScan 1173) + H-E histology, quantitative volumetric analysis
- **Follow-up**: Clinical and panoramic radiographic follow-up up to 2 years post-prosthesis

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case report; low level of evidence (case-report); findings may not generalize.
- Patient history and 16-year-old graft characteristics may not represent typical cases.
- No comparison group vs standard GBR methods or other donor sites.
- Long-term outcomes need further investigation beyond 2 years.
- Technique requires a previously grafted sinus — rare or inapplicable in many clinical situations.

## 7. Glossary

- **DBBM**: 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral) — xenograft bone substitute for bone augmentation.
- **GBR**: 유도 골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration) — technique using membranes to promote bone regeneration.
- **micro-CT**: 미세 전산화 단층촬영 (Micro-Computed Tomography) — high-resolution 3D imaging for bone structure analysis.
- **SLA**: 샌드블라스트·대립·산부식 (Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched) — implant surface treatment for 골유착 (Osseointegration).
- **MSA**: 상악동 거상술 (Maxillary Sinus Augmentation) — surgical procedure to increase bone volume in the posterior maxilla.
