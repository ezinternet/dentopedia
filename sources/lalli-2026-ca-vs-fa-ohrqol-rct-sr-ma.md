## Why Ingested

Extends the OHRQoL evidence base for clear aligner therapy alongside [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/caldas-2024-pain-ohrqol-satisfaction-aligner]] and [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/baneshi-2024-clear-aligners-malocclusion-sr-ma]]. This is the first SR+MA restricted to RCTs with Trial Sequential Analysis for CA vs FA OHRQoL comparison, providing pooled OHIP-14 domain-level estimates and TSA-adjudicated reliability not available in prior qualitative syntheses.

## One-line Summary

RCT-only SR+MA (2 trials, n=74) comparing CA vs FA on OHIP-14: CA significantly better in psychological discomfort (WMD −1.28, p=0.007) and psychological disability (WMD −1.63, p<0.001); no significant difference in physical, functional, or overall OHRQoL domains; TSA confirms preliminary signal for psychological outcomes.

## 한줄요약

RCT 전용 SR+MA(2편/n=74): CA가 심리적 불편(WMD −1.28, p=0.007) 및 심리적 장애(WMD −1.63, p<0.001)에서 유의하게 우위; 신체·기능·전반 OHRQoL 도메인은 차이 없음; TSA는 심리 영역 조기 신호 확인, 핸디캡 도메인은 임상적 차이 없음을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA restricted exclusively to RCTs comparing CA vs FA OHRQoL using OHIP-14 with pooled weighted mean differences per domain
- Applied Trial Sequential Analysis (TSA) to adjudicate whether cumulative evidence is sufficient — a methodological advance absent from prior qualitative syntheses
- Identified domain-specific CA advantage (psychological domains only); no global OHRQoL superiority
- TSA crossed futility boundary for handicap domain: clinically relevant CA advantage in handicap is unlikely
- GRADE appraisal: low to moderate evidence across all domains; underscores need for further RCTs

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis; PRISMA-compliant; PROSPERO-registered
- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL; up to 2026-03-15; English only
- **Inclusion**: RCTs; Angle Class I malocclusion; permanent dentition; non-extraction; no orthognathic surgery or TADs; OHIP-14 outcome reported; direct CA vs FA comparison
- **Exclusion**: Non-RCT designs; Class II/III; mixed/deciduous dentition; extraction cases; lingual/ceramic/low-friction appliances; systemic disease
- **Included studies**: 2 RCTs (Borsato et al. [Invisalign, 0.022×0.030-inch FA slots, 3M Unitek]; Alhafi et al. [undisclosed CA brand, MBT FA])
- **Sample**: n=74 total (37 CA, 37 FA); mean age 16.56–29.25 years; mild-moderate Class I crowding >4mm
- **Primary outcome**: OHIP-14 change scores T0→T1 (~1 month post-placement)
- **Statistical methods**: Random-effects meta-analysis; weighted mean differences (WMDs) + 95% CI; I² heterogeneity; Cochran Q; TSA (type I error 5%, power 80%, OBF monitoring boundaries, RIS calculated); R metafor package
- **SD imputation**: Wan et al. method for IQR-reported studies; imputed correlation r=0.488 (Spearman estimate)
- **RoB**: Cochrane RoB 2 tool; Borsato = low risk; Alhafi = some concerns (randomization, deviations)
- **Inter-rater reliability**: Cohen's kappa = 0.66 (substantial agreement)

### Overall OHIP-14 trajectories

- Both treatment groups: transient deterioration at T1 (1 month) then progressive improvement toward baseline
- Alhafi et al.: FA T0=17.61→T1=19.94→T4=4.72; CA T0=17.56→T1=22.72→T4=4.83
- Borsato et al.: FA T0=8.16→T1=14.79→T3=8.21; CA T0=9.21→T1=6.47→T3=4.89

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 2 RCTs included (n=74); severely limited statistical power; publication bias assessment not possible
- SD of change scores not directly reported in one study; estimated via Wan et al. + imputed r=0.488
- Substantial heterogeneity in physical domains (I²>80%) despite strict Class I inclusion — residual clinical heterogeneity from aligner brand, attachment protocols, staging, compliance not fully controlled
- Restriction to Class I limits generalizability to Class II/III malocclusions
- Short follow-up (~1 month); no long-term OHRQoL conclusions
- TSA preliminary signals for psychological domains do not confirm definitive evidence; most outcomes underpowered
- Further standardized RCTs with longer follow-up and uniform OHIP-14 reporting needed

## 7. Glossary

- **OHIP-14**: Oral Health Impact Profile – 14 items; 7 domains (functional limitation, physical pain, psychological discomfort, physical disability, psychological disability, social disability, handicap); higher = worse OHRQoL
- **WMD**: Weighted Mean Difference; negative value = CA is better (less deterioration or more improvement)
- **TSA**: Trial Sequential Analysis; controls for cumulative type I/II errors; RIS = Required Information Size
- **O'Brien–Fleming boundary**: Sequential monitoring threshold; Z-curve crossing = preliminary signal
- **Futility boundary**: TSA threshold indicating further evidence is unlikely to change conclusion
- **RIS**: Required Information Size; total N needed for definitive conclusion at specified power
- **RoB 2**: Cochrane Risk of Bias tool version 2 for RCTs
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development, and Evaluation — evidence certainty framework
- **PROSPERO**: CRD420251051003 — international prospective SR registry

## Three-line Summary

- RCT-only SR+MA (2 trials, n=74; PROSPERO CRD420251051003; 5 databases to March 2026) comparing clear aligners versus fixed appliances on OHIP-14 domains in Angle Class I non-extraction patients, applying Trial Sequential Analysis.
- Clear aligners were significantly better only in psychological discomfort (WMD −1.28; p=0.007; I²=0%) and psychological disability (WMD −1.63; p<0.001; I²=0%); no significant difference in physical pain, functional limitation, or overall OHIP-14; TSA crossed the futility boundary for the handicap domain; GRADE low to moderate.
- Clear aligner OHRQoL advantage is domain-specific (psychological only) and early rather than global; patient counseling should emphasize esthetic-related psychological comfort; large standardized RCTs with long-term follow-up are needed.

## 세줄요약

- RCT 전용 SR+MA (2편/n=74; PROSPERO CRD420251051003; 5개 DB 2026년 3월까지): Class I 비발치 환자에서 투명교정장치 vs 고정식의 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL) — OHIP-14 도메인별 연속검증분석 (Trial Sequential Analysis, TSA) 적용.
- CA가 심리적 불편 (Psychological Discomfort) WMD −1.28 (p=0.007; I²=0%), 심리적 장애 (Psychological Disability) WMD −1.63 (p<0.001; I²=0%)에서만 유의 우위; 신체 통증·기능·전반 OHRQoL 차이 없음; TSA 핸디캡 도메인 무용 경계 (Futility Boundary) 통과; GRADE 저-중등도.
- CA OHRQoL 이점은 심리 영역 한정·단기 효과이며 전체적 우위 아님; 환자 상담 시 심미 관련 심리 안정감 중심 설명; 장기 추적 대규모 RCT 필요.
