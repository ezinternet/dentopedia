## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of long-term clinical performance comparing composite resin and ceramic inlays, onlays, and overlays published in Operative Dentistry (2021). Searched PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, and Web of Science through April 2019 for studies with ≥5-year mean follow-up. Analyzed 13 observational studies using Newcastle-Ottawa Scale for quality assessment and applied both fixed and random effects models.

Composite resin inlays, onlays, and overlays achieved 5-year survival rate of 91% with 84% success rate. Ceramic inlays and onlays demonstrated 5-year survival of 90%, declining to 89% at 8 years and 85% at 10 years, with 5-year success of 88% and 10-year success of 77%. Primary failure modes differed by material: resin restorations failed predominantly from secondary caries and endodontic complications, while ceramic restorations failed from fracture and endodontic complications.

Heterogeneous study designs mixing RCTs with observational cohorts limit evidence strength. Overlay clinical data remain limited, and long-term ceramic data beyond 10 years are sparse. Parafunctional habits and grinding showed no direct statistical association with failure. Edentulous cases and multi-surface restorations carried increased failure risk. Both material types demonstrated favorable long-term outcomes supporting evidence-based material selection for indirect restorations.

## 세줄요약

복합레진·세라믹 인레이·온레이·오버레이의 장기 임상 성적 비교 SR+MA (Operative Dentistry 2021). PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science 검색(~2019년 4월), ≥5년 평균 추적 연구. 13편 관찰 연구 분석, Newcastle-Ottawa Scale 품질 평가, 고정·무작위 효과 모델 적용.

복합레진 인레이·온레이·오버레이 5년 생존율 91%, 성공율 84%. 세라믹 인레이·온레이 5년 생존 90%, 8년 89%, 10년 85%, 5년 성공 88%, 10년 성공 77%. 주요 실패 모드: 레진은 이차우식·근관합병증, 세라믹은 파절·근관합병증.

RCT·관찰 혼합으로 설계 이질성 근거 강도 제약. 오버레이 데이터·세라믹 10년+ 장기 데이터 희소. 파라기능·이갈이와 실패 직접 통계 연관 없음. 무수치아·다면 수복은 실패 위험 증가. 양 재료 모두 양호한 장기 성적으로 간접 수복의 근거기반 재료 선택 지지.
