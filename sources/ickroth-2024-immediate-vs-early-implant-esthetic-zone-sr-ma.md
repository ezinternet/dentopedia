## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 RCTs (n=222; IIP: 112, EIP: 110) comparing immediate versus early implant placement for single-tooth replacement in the anterior maxilla or mandible, conducted by the Cosyn group at Ghent University (search to February 2023); primary outcome was vertical midfacial soft tissue change.

Meta-analysis found no significant difference between IIP and EIP in vertical midfacial soft tissue change (MD 0.31 mm, 95% CI [-0.23; 0.86], p=0.260; I²=83%), Pink Esthetic Score (standardized MD 0.92, 95% CI [-0.23; 2.07], p=0.120; I²=89%), implant survival (RR 0.98, 95% CI [0.93, 1.03], p=0.480; I²=0%), or marginal bone level change (MD 0.03 mm, 95% CI [-0.12, 0.17], p=0.700; I²=0%); 4/6 RCTs had high risk of bias.

In low-risk patients with an intact buccal bone wall, IIP and EIP appear equivalent in esthetic and clinical outcomes — but certainty of evidence is rated low due to high/unclear RoB in all 6 included RCTs and limited reporting of patient-reported outcomes.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly compares IIP vs EIP (not the more common IIP vs delayed) in the esthetic zone, restricting inclusion to RCTs only — matching the same evidence scope as Asghar 2023 but with higher methodological authority.
- Formally rates overall certainty of evidence as **low** — a rare and important explicit GRADE-style conclusion in timing-protocol SR+MAs, grounding the "no difference" finding within its uncertainty.
- Identifies high heterogeneity (I²=83–89%) in soft tissue and PES outcomes while simultaneously finding I²=0% for implant survival and marginal bone — a useful signal that soft tissue results are environment-sensitive but hard tissue results are consistent.
- Highlights that "state-of-the-art therapy" (e.g., contour augmentation, connective tissue grafting) was applied in only a minority of included studies, limiting applicability to contemporary protocols.
- Calls for future RCTs to report patient-reported outcomes (PROs), flagging a systematic evidence gap.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis, RCTs only.
- **Databases searched**: PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane; plus manual search.
- **Search date**: up to February 2023.
- **Population**: Patients receiving single-tooth implants in the aesthetic area (anterior maxilla or mandible).
- **Comparison**: IIP vs EIP.
- **Primary outcome**: Vertical midfacial soft tissue change.
- **Secondary outcomes**: Horizontal midfacial soft tissue change, vertical papillary change, Pink Esthetic Score (PES), implant survival, buccal bone thickness, marginal bone level change, patient discomfort, chair time, patient satisfaction.
- **Included studies**: 6 RCTs from 1185 screened records; 222 patients, 222 single implants (IIP: n=112; EIP: n=110); mean patient age 35.6–52.6 years; follow-up 8–24 months.
- **Risk of bias**: 2 RCTs showed "some concerns"; 4 showed "high risk of bias."
- **Meta-analysis subgroup**: 4 studies included for primary outcome meta-analysis; 3 of these restricted to intact buccal bone wall cases.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All 6 included RCTs had unclear or high risk of bias (2 "some concerns," 4 "high") — the primary driver of low evidence certainty.
- High heterogeneity (I²=83–89%) for soft tissue and PES outcomes limits the precision of "no difference" conclusions for those endpoints.
- State-of-the-art contemporary protocols (e.g., immediate contour augmentation, SCTG) used in only a minority of studies — results may underestimate the potential of optimized IIP.
- Follow-up limited to 8–24 months; long-term stability data lacking.
- Patient-reported outcomes systematically underreported across included RCTs.
- Abstract-only ingest: individual study characteristics, detailed ROB domain breakdown, and full methods not extractable.

## 7. Glossary

- **Immediate Implant Placement (IIP, 즉시식립)**: Implant placed at time of tooth extraction into the fresh extraction socket (Type 1 in ITI classification).
- **Early Implant Placement (EIP, 조기식립)**: Implant placed after partial soft-tissue healing (typically 4–8 weeks post-extraction), before complete bone healing (Type 2 in ITI classification).
- **Vertical Midfacial Soft Tissue Change (수직 협측 중앙 연조직 변화)**: Change in the height of the buccal mid-facial soft tissue margin relative to a reference point, the primary esthetic outcome.
- **Pink Esthetic Score (PES, 핑크심미점수)**: Validated scoring index for peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics assessing papillae, tissue level, contour, color, and texture.
- **Marginal Bone Level (MBL, 변연골 수준)**: Radiographically measured alveolar bone crest height adjacent to the implant, a standard peri-implant clinical indicator.
- **Intact Buccal Bone Wall (협측 골벽 보존)**: Presence of an undamaged, continuous labial/buccal alveolar bone plate at the extraction site — a key patient selection criterion for IIP.
- **Risk of Bias (비뚤림 위험)**: Systematic methodological flaw in a study that may distort the true treatment effect.
