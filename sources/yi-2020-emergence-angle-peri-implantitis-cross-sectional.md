Ingesting the original gives the wiki the bone-level vs tissue-level distinction and the mesial+distal splinting finding that later reviews compress.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional analysis of 169 patients / 349 implants (retrospectively included; Seoul National University Dental Hospital) relating prosthetic features — emergence angle (EA), emergence profile (EP), crown/implant ratio (CIR), splinting — to peri-implantitis diagnosed from bone loss and probing depth on 1- and 5-year radiographs.

EA correlated significantly with marginal bone loss (MBL); greater peri-implantitis prevalence appeared with EA ≥30°, convex EP, and a middle implant splinted to both mesial and distal adjacent implants in bone-level implants, while tissue-level implants showed no such correlation and CIR had no significant effect.

Over-contoured prostheses and bilateral splinting are presented as modifiable local risk factors — but this abstract-only record reports no effect sizes or prevalence percentages, so magnitudes remain unverified.

## 세줄요약

후향적 포함·횡단면 분석(서울대학교치과병원, 환자 169명·임플란트 349개): 출현각(Emergence Angle, EA)·출현윤곽(Emergence Profile, EP)·보철물/임플란트 비율(Crown-to-Implant Ratio, CIR)·스플린트 위치를 1년·5년 방사선 사진의 골소실·탐침 깊이로 정의한 임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis)과 분석.

출현각은 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)과 유의한 상관을 보였고, 골수준(Bone-level) 임플란트에서 EA ≥30°·볼록 EP·양측 인접 임플란트와 스플린트된 중간 임플란트에서 유병률이 유의하게 높았으나 조직수준(Tissue-level) 임플란트에서는 유사 상관이 없었으며 CIR은 유의한 영향이 없었다.

과도하게 윤곽을 만든 보철물과 양측 스플린트가 수정 가능한 국소 위험인자로 제시되나, 초록 전용 기록이라 효과크기·유병률 수치가 없어 크기는 확인할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Links prosthetic contour variables — EA, EP, CIR — and prosthesis splinting to peri-implantitis prevalence in a multivariable model (generalized estimating equation) with a Cox regression prediction model.
- Reports a significant EA ↔ MBL correlation and a higher peri-implantitis prevalence at EA ≥30° specifically in bone-level implants.
- Distinguishes bone-level from tissue-level implants: the analogous EA/EP association was not observed in tissue-level implants.
- Reports no significant effect of crown/implant ratio on peri-implantitis prevalence.
- Flags a middle implant splinted to both mesial and distal adjacent implants as higher risk.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cross-sectional analysis of retrospectively included patients (PubMed MeSH: Cross-Sectional Studies; Retrospective Studies); n = 169 patients, 349 implants.
- **Case definition**: Peri-implantitis diagnosed from peri-implant bone loss plus probing depth.
- **Measurements**: Radiographs at 1 and 5 years after prosthesis insertion → MBL, EA, EP, CIR; splinted position recorded.
- **Statistics**: Multivariable generalized estimating equation for feature → prevalence; Cox proportional hazard regression for the final prediction model.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- EA showed a significant correlation with MBL (effect size not stated in the available abstract).
- Statistically greater peri-implantitis prevalence with EA ≥30°, convex EP, and middle-implant splinting to both adjacent implants — in bone-level implants (percentages not stated in the available abstract).
- Tissue-level implants: no similar correlation reported.
- CIR: no significant effect on peri-implantitis prevalence.
- Authors' conclusion: over-contoured implant prosthesis is a critical local confounder for peri-implantitis; bilateral splinting carries higher risk.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only — no full text, so thresholds' derivation, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and the Cox model's covariates cannot be checked here.
- Retrospective inclusion with cross-sectional outcome assessment → association, not causation.
- 1- and 5-year radiographic time points only; no information in the abstract on longer follow-up or confounder residual.
- Retraction check: PubMed article-type list contains no "Retracted Publication" and no correction/comment records (checked 2026-11-25).

## 7. Glossary

- **EA (Emergence Angle)**: angle of the restoration's transmucosal contour relative to the implant long axis.
- **EP (Emergence Profile)**: shape of the restoration as it emerges through the peri-implant soft tissue (convex vs concave/straight).
- **CIR (Crown/Implant Ratio)**: crown height relative to implant length.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: peri-implant crestal bone loss measured on radiographs.
- **GEE (Generalized Estimating Equation)**: regression method accounting for correlation among clustered observations (multiple implants per patient).
