## Why Ingested
흡연이 발치 후 합병증(alveolar osteitis, dry socket)에 미치는 영향을 정량화한 후향적 연구. 기존 [[oral-surgery/marenzi-2015-lprf-postextraction-socket-healing-splitmout]] L-PRF 소켓 치유 연구의 위험인자 배경 근거로 활용.

## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=219 female inmates) found smoking, mandibular third molars, and surgical trauma significantly associated with postoperative complications including alveolar osteitis after dental extractions.

## 한줄요약
후향적 코호트(n=219 여성 수감자)에서 흡연, 하악 제3대구치, 수술적 외상이 발치 후 합병증(치조골염 포함) 발생률과 유의미하게 연관됨.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Oral Surgery, Oral Medicine, Oral Pathology, Oral Radiology, and Endodontology
- Volume 104, Issue 6, December 2007
- DOI: 10.1016/j.tripleo.2007.04.020
- Study period: January 2004–April 2005
- Setting: Federal Correctional Institution, Danbury CT (female institution, ~1,400 inmates)
- Funding/COI: Not reported

## 2. Key Contributions
- Quantified smoking as a significant risk factor for overall postextraction complications (P=0.02)
- Distinguished alveolar osteitis rate from overall complication rate in a general extraction population (not only third molars)
- Leveraged smoke-free policy change (September 2004) as a natural experiment to assess smoking effect
- Demonstrated mandibular third molars carry higher alveolar osteitis risk regardless of smoking status (P=0.02)
- Identified surgical trauma as an independent contributor to both total complications (P=0.05) and dry socket (P=0.01)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Retrospective cohort
- N: 219 subjects; 61.1% smokers, 37.9% nonsmokers; mean age 37.7 years (SD 10.4)
- Period: 8 months pre-ban (Jan–Aug 2004) vs 8 months post-ban (Sep 2004–Apr 2005)
- Extractions: general dental extractions (wide range, not only third molars)
- Outcomes: overall complications (pain, swelling, bleeding, dental revisit) and alveolar osteitis specifically
- Statistics: Chi-square test; significance P<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall complication incidence: 19.6%
- Alveolar osteitis incidence: 5.0%
- Smokers vs nonsmokers overall complications: significant (P=0.02)
- Mandibular third molar vs other extractions for alveolar osteitis: significant (P=0.02) regardless of smoking
- Surgical trauma → total complications P=0.05; alveolar osteitis P=0.01
- Smoking contributed significantly to increased complications among multiple extractions (P=0.03)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design; no randomization
- All-female population; generalizability to males limited
- Prison setting: limited dietary control, stress factors
- Full-text access restricted (abstract + section snippets only in extracted PDF)
- Smoke-free policy compliance not individually verified

## 7. Glossary
- Alveolar osteitis (dry socket): painful condition after tooth extraction caused by failure of blood clot formation or clot dissolution
- Post-extraction bleeding (PEB): continued bleeding >8–12 h after extraction; considered abnormal
