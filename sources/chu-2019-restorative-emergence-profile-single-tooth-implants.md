## Three-line Summary

Narrative review with a clinical decision flowchart (IJPRD, Chu, Kan, Wang et al.) on the subgingival (critical and subcritical) and supragingival contours of the implant restorative emergence profile (IREP) for single-tooth implants in periodontally healthy patients.

Subgingival contours should mimic root and cervical-third crown anatomy where possible, but depend on 3D implant position: a convex facial critical contour is frequently required for a palatally or incisally positioned implant to support the gingival margin, a flat or concave contour is recommended for a facially placed implant, and the subcritical zone may be undercontoured to thicken and stabilize the soft-tissue cuff.

Turns the "concave is better" generalization into a position-conditional rule; expert synthesis without new data.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames the peri-implant seal (connective tissue cuff + junctional epithelium, no Sharpey's fibers) as less resistant to probing and biofilm than periodontium, motivating careful IREP design.
- Divides IREP into subgingival (critical, subcritical) and supragingival contours.
- Decision rules by implant position:
  - Palatal or incisal implant → convex facial critical contour often needed.
  - Facial implant → flat or concave critical contour.
  - No soft-tissue support needed → undercontour subcritical zone.
- Provides a clinical flowchart (content in full text).
