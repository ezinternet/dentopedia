## Why Ingested

Only RCT directly comparing three-way posterior iFDP materials — monolithic ZrO₂ (MZ) vs veneered ZrO₂ (VZ) vs Ti-ceramic (TC) — with 1-year outcomes and 3/5-year follow-ups ongoing. Fills direct-comparison gap noted in [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/pjetursson-2023-implant-fdp-material-design-outcomes-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=49 patients, 63 FDPs) at Brånemark Clinic, Gothenburg; posterior implant-supported partial FDPs randomized to MZ (n=23), VZ (n=20), or TC (n=20); 1-year follow-up (3 and 5 years ongoing).
Prosthetic survival: MZ 100%, VZ 95% (1 framework fracture), TC 95% (1 extensive chipping→replaced); MZ: 0 chipping, VZ chipping 5%, TC chipping 11.1%; MBL change: MZ −0.18 mm, VZ −0.45 mm, TC −0.15 mm (NS between groups).
Monolithic ZrO₂ iFDPs show the lowest technical complication rate at 1 year (zero chipping); veneered ZrO₂ remains vulnerable to framework fracture; Ti-ceramic has the highest chipping rate.

## 세줄요약

무작위대조시험(n=49명, 63개 iFDP), 브로네마크 클리닉 예테보리; 후방 임플란트지지 부분 고정성 보철(Implant-Supported Partial Fixed Dental Prosthesis, iFDP)을 모놀리식 지르코니아(Monolithic Zirconia, MZ) vs 베니어 지르코니아(Veneered Zirconia, VZ) vs 금속도재(Titanium-Ceramic, TC)로 무작위 배정, 1년 추적(3·5년 진행 중).
보철 생존율: MZ 100%, VZ 95%(프레임워크 파절 1건), TC 95%(광범위 칩핑으로 교체 1건); 칩핑(Chipping): MZ 0%, VZ 5%, TC 11.1%; 변연골수준 변화(MBLC): MZ −0.18 mm, VZ −0.45 mm, TC −0.15 mm(군간 통계적 유의차 없음).
1년 시점 단일체 ZrO₂ iFDP가 기술적 합병증 최소(칩핑 0건); 베니어 ZrO₂는 프레임워크 파절 위험 잔존; 장기 추적 결과 대기 중.

## Key Contributions

- First three-arm RCT comparing MZ vs VZ vs TC for posterior implant-supported FDPs
- MZ shows 0% chipping; VZ and TC have chipping rates comparable to prior literature
- VZ framework fracture linked to excessively close implant positioning requiring framework reduction
- Overall implant survival 99.2% at 1 year; MBL changes NS between groups
- MZ screw loosening (1 case, 4.8%) — only MZ technical complication recorded

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective RCT, 1-year (3- and 5-year follow-ups ongoing)
- **Setting**: Brånemark Clinic (public dental health), Gothenburg, Sweden
- **n**: 49 patients, 63 FDPs (MZ=23, VZ=20, TC=20); 59 evaluated at 1-year FU
- **Implants**: Brånemark System MKIII TiUnite + NobelParallel CC TiUnite (Nobel Biocare)
- **FDP design**: Short-span (2–3 units), noncantilevered; all screw-retained at abutment level
- **MZ material**: NobelProcera Zirconia Implant Bridge, Nacera Pearl Multi-Shade (3Y-TZP)
- **VZ material**: Same zirconia framework; veneered with Initial Zr-FS (GC Europe)
- **TC material**: NobelProcera Titanium Implant Bridge Grade 2 + GC Initial Titanium porcelain
- **Primary outcome**: Prosthetic survival + complication rates (MZ and VZ vs TC)
- **Secondary**: Implant survival, MBLC, biological complications

## Key Results

| Outcome | MZ (n=21) | VZ (n=20) | TC (n=18) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prosthetic survival | 100% | 95% (1 framework fx) | 95% (1 extensive chipping) |
| Chipping | 0% | 5% (1) | 11.1% (2) |
| Framework fracture | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Screw loosening | 4.8% (1) | 0 | 0 |
| Implant survival | 97.7% | 100% | 100% |
| MBLC (mean, mm) | −0.18 ± 0.77 | −0.45 ± 1.24 | −0.15 ± 0.77 |
| Peri-implant mucositis | 0% | 10% | 16.7% |

- No statistically significant differences in MBLC or complications between groups (p>0.05)
- VZ framework fracture: implants too close → framework reduced → fracture above mesial abutment

## Limitations

- 1-year follow-up only; 3/5-year data pending (main complication differences expected long-term)
- Sample sized for 5-year complication data (80% power for MZ 2% vs VZ 40%); 1-year NS expected
- Multi-surgeon/prosthodontist design (standardized protocol to mitigate)
- Nobel Biocare funding; potential industry bias
- Imbalance: TC group more mandibular implants

## Glossary

- **MZ (Monolithic Zirconia / 모놀리식 지르코니아)**: 베니어 없이 지르코니아 단일 블록으로 제작
- **VZ (Veneered Zirconia / 베니어 지르코니아)**: 지르코니아 코핑 위에 도재 소성
- **TC (Titanium-Ceramic / 금속도재)**: 티타늄 프레임워크 위에 도재 소성
- **iFDP (Implant-Supported Fixed Dental Prosthesis)**: 임플란트 지지 고정성 치과 보철물
- **MBLC (Marginal Bone Level Change)**: 최종 보철물 장착 시점 대비 변연골수준 변화
