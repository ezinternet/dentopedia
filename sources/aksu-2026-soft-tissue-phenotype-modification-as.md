## Why Ingested

Existing peri-implant mucositis pages cover non-surgical debridement and chemical adjuncts ([[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/mauriello-2026-peri-implant-mucositis-adjunctive-narrative-review]]) while the keratinized mucosa evidence is largely observational ([[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/mahardawi-2023-lack-keratinized-mucosa-peri-implantitis-sr-ma]]). This quasi-randomized trial tests a soft-tissue phenotype modification (free palatal graft) as an interventional adjunct for mucositis, with a mediation analysis on plaque control, filling that gap.

## Three-line Summary

Quasi-randomized clinical trial (43 patients, 55 implants; peri-implant mucositis with keratinized tissue width < 2 mm) comparing free palatal graft (FPG, n = 22) versus no additional intervention (n = 21) one month after oral hygiene instructions and non-surgical treatment.

At 6 months FPG increased keratinized tissue width (MD = 2.36 mm) and tissue thickness (MD = 0.97 mm), and lowered bleeding on probing (BoP) extent (0.75 ± 1.07 vs 1.83 ± 1.20; MD = -1.06; 95% CI -1.67 to -0.44; p = 0.001) with higher treatment success (OR = 8.44); PPD and PISTD did not differ.

Mediation analysis suggested the BoP benefit was largely independent of improved plaque control; the quasi-randomized allocation and abstract-only reading limit certainty.

## 세줄요약

준무작위 임상시험(Quasi-randomized Clinical Trial; 43명, 임플란트 55개; 각화조직 폭(Keratinized Tissue Width, KTW) < 2 mm인 임플란트주위 점막염) — 구강위생교육·비외과적 치료 1개월 후 유리구개이식(Free Palatal Graft, FPG; n = 22) 대 추가 처치 없음(n = 21)을 비교.

6개월 시점에 FPG군은 KTW(평균차 2.36 mm)와 조직 두께(0.97 mm)가 증가했고, 탐침출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BoP) 범위가 0.75 ± 1.07 대 1.83 ± 1.20(평균차 -1.06; 95% CI -1.67 ~ -0.44; p = 0.001)으로 낮았으며 치료 성공률도 높았다(OR = 8.44); 탐침깊이(PPD)와 임플란트주위 연조직 열개(Peri-implant Soft-tissue Dehiscence, PISTD)는 차이가 없었다.

매개분석(Mediation Analysis)상 BoP 개선 효과는 치태 조절 개선과 대체로 독립적이었으나, 준무작위 배정과 초록 수준 정보만 확인된 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Interventional test of soft-tissue phenotype modification (FPG) as an add-on to non-surgical therapy of peri-implant mucositis in sites with KTW < 2 mm.
- Primary outcome (BoP extent at 6 months) favored FPG.
- Mediation analysis addressed whether benefit was explained by improved plaque control (reported as largely independent).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Quasi-randomized clinical trial; allocation one month after oral hygiene instructions and non-surgical treatment.
- **n**: 43 patients, 55 implants (test FPG n = 22; control no additional intervention n = 21).
- **Inclusion**: peri-implant mucositis with KTW < 2 mm.
- **Primary outcome**: BoP extent (bleeding sites per implant) at 6 months.
- **Secondary outcomes**: KTW, tissue thickness, vestibular depth, plaque extent, BoP severity, PPD, PISTD, treatment success.
- **Analysis**: regression-based intergroup comparisons; mediation analysis for plaque control.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome (6 months) | Result (test vs control) |
|---|---|

| BoP severity | MD = -0.50; p < 0.001 |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only reading: randomization method, blinding, attrition, graft technique details, and follow-up beyond 6 months are not available here.
- Quasi-randomized allocation (not true randomization) carries higher allocation-bias risk than an RCT.
- Small sample (43 patients) and 6-month horizon; long-term mucositis recurrence or progression not reported in the abstract.

## 6. Related Work

- Mauriello 2026 narrative review: adjunctive chemical/pharmaceutical therapies for mucositis; this trial tests a surgical tissue-phenotype adjunct instead.
- Mahardawi 2023 SR+MA and Zhang 2025 SR+MA: observational KM associations that this trial tests interventionally.
- Roccuzzo 2025 20-year cohort: long-term FGG protection against peri-implant disease.

## 7. Glossary

- **FPG**: free palatal graft (palatal soft-tissue graft used to increase keratinized tissue).
- **KTW**: keratinized tissue width.
- **BoP**: bleeding on probing; extent = bleeding sites per implant.
- **PISTD**: peri-implant soft-tissue dehiscence.
- **PPD**: probing pocket depth.
