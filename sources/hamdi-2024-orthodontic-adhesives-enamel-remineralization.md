## Why Ingested

교정 브라켓 주위 백반병소(WSL) 예방을 "접착제 자체에 재광화제를 넣는" 재료 관점에서 정리해, WSL 요법 network MA [[caries/xie-2023-white-spot-lesion-therapies-sr-nma]]와 재광화 기술 SR [[caries/malcangi-2023-enamel-remineralization-techniques-sr]]가 다루는 topical agent 접근을 접착제-전달 축으로 확장한다. 또한 재광화 평가법(micro-CT/TMR/PLM/단면경도/FTIR/EDS)의 방법론 프레임을 제공.

## One-line Summary
PRISMA/PROSPERO SR of 24 in vitro studies: orthodontic adhesives loaded with bioactive fillers (bioactive glass, n-HAP, n-ACP, n-AFCP, nCaF2) or fluoride-releasing cements significantly remineralize/anti-demineralize enamel around brackets, but evidence is weak (15/24 high risk of bias, heterogeneous, no meta-analysis).

## 한줄요약
교정 접착제에 생체활성 필러(BAG, n-HAP, n-ACP, n-AFCP, nCaF2)나 불소방출을 넣으면 브라켓 주위 법랑질을 재광화/탈회억제한다는 in vitro 24편 SR — 그러나 24편 중 15편 고비뚤림, 이질성 커 메타분석 불가, 근거 약함.

## 1. Document Information
- BMC Oral Health 2024;24(1):1446. PMC11603835. DOI 10.1186/s12903-024-05237-y.
- Mansoura/Zagazig (Egypt). PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD42024574945. PubMed+Scopus+Web of Science, 2005–2024. 1712 → 62 → 24 in vitro/in situ studies.
- Focus question: "Do orthodontic adhesives remineralize or prevent demineralization of enamel surrounding orthodontic brackets?"

## 2. Key Contributions
- Maps the bioactive-additive landscape for orthodontic adhesives: 13 studies bioactive glass (BAG; some with Ag/Zn/F doping), 2 n-ACP, 1 n-HAP, 1 nCaF2, 1 n-AFCP, 1 ammonium-based methacrylate, + 4 combination systems (MBN+MPC, PEHB+MAE-DB+n-ACP, MPC+DMAHDM+n-ACP, nCaF2+DMAHDM); 7 evaluated commercial adhesives.
- Consolidates the assessment-method toolbox and grades it (below).
- Translates bench findings toward clinic while flagging weak evidence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PECOS eligibility; excluded reviews, clinical trials, in vivo, animal teeth, non-remineralization outcomes, and studies without an ideal cariogenic (demin–remin) challenge.
- Cariogenic challenge: mostly chemical pH-cycling (acetate buffer pH ~4.3–5); 3 biofilm models.
- Assessment techniques classified into: remineralization EXTENT/subsurface (micro-CT [9], PLM [4], cross-sectional hardness [7]); SUPERFICIAL surface (surface hardness [3], DIAGNOdent laser fluorescence [1], digital photography ΔE [1]); CHEMICAL (FTIR [1], EDS [2], XPS [1]); MICROMORPHOLOGY (SEM [7], AFM [1]).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Primary outcome: all 7 cross-sectional-hardness and all 9 micro-CT and all 4 PLM studies showed significant remineralization extent for the additive/experimental adhesives vs control.
- BAG adhesives: 9/13 reported definitive significant remineralization; mechanism = Ca/PO4/Si release → silica-rich layer → HA nucleation (sol-gel BAG preferred for smaller, more reactive particles). n-HAP/n-ACP/n-AFCP/nCaF2 adhesives all showed significant effect (attributed to nano size, high surface area, deep penetration).
- Commercial adhesives with significant effect: Fuji Ortho LC (RMGIC, fluoride release up to 6 mo), Transbond Plus color-change / Transbond Plus SEP + Transbond XT, Quick Cure.
- Ion release (secondary, 8 studies): fluoride release sustained (Fuji Ortho LC 6 mo; nCaF2 adhesive 56 d); Ca/P release up to 42 d (quaternary-ammonium + 40% n-ACP); P release with Ga-doped BAG to 14 d.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 15/24 high risk of bias; 19/24 no sample-size calculation; 14/24 pH-cycling shorter than the ~23–28 d needed to simulate clinical remineralization; frequent lack of blinding; some studies relied on a single technique (e.g., SEM alone) or reported figures/graphs without definitive statistics.
- Ion-release tests too short and used resin discs of non-clinical geometry → over/underestimation.
- Heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis; overall evidence weak. Recommends micro-CT + XRD and/or Raman/FTIR, AFM micromorphology, standardized ≥23 d pH-cycling, blinding, and sample-size calculation in future work.

## 7. Glossary
- **WSL**: white spot lesion (early enamel caries). **BAG**: bioactive glass. **n-HAP/n-ACP/n-AFCP/nCaF2**: nano hydroxyapatite / amorphous calcium phosphate / fluorinated ACP / calcium fluoride.
- **RMGIC**: resin-modified glass ionomer cement (e.g., Fuji Ortho LC), fluoride-releasing.
- **pH-cycling**: alternating demineralization/remineralization to mimic the oral caries process.
- **micro-CT / TMR / PLM**: 3D / 2D spatial remineralization-extent imaging; **DIAGNOdent**: laser-fluorescence surface reading.
