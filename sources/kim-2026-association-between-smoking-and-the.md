## Three-line Summary

Retrospective nationwide cohort study of Korean adults in the National Health Insurance Service-Health Screening Cohort (NHIS-HEALS, implant placement 2016-2019): 23,573 individuals (16,405 non-smokers, 5,381 ex-smokers, 1,787 current smokers); failure was claim-based (reimplantation or removal-surgery procedure codes) and the denominator is persons, not implants.

605 failures occurred (344 non-smokers 2.1%, 182 ex-smokers 3.4%, 79 current smokers 4.4%; 11.56, 16.54, 22.33 per 1,000 person-years); adjusted HR was 1.59 (95% CI 1.20-2.09) for current smokers and 1.16 (0.94-1.45, not significant) for ex-smokers, with significant risk for >10 cigarettes/day (1.31), >10 years (1.27) and >10 pack-years (1.30) but not for lighter or shorter exposure.

Sex-stratified current-smoker HR was 1.48 (1.11-1.97) in males and 3.39 (1.25-9.17) in females; the data give no early-vs-late timing split, no implant-level data (system, bone, surgeon), self-reported smoking and only up to 4 years of follow-up, so this is overall claim-based failure; patient nationality is implied by the Korean national database but not stated as an ethnicity variable.

## 세줄요약

한국 건강보험공단 건강검진코호트 (NHIS-HEALS)에서 2016-2019년 임플란트를 식립한 한국 성인 23,573명(비흡연 16,405, 과거흡연 5,381, 현재흡연 1,787)을 분석한 후향적 전국 코호트이며, 실패는 재식립 또는 제거술 청구코드로 정의한 청구 기반이고 분모는 임플란트가 아니라 환자 수이다.

실패 605건(비흡연 344명 2.1%, 과거흡연 182명 3.4%, 현재흡연 79명 4.4%; 1,000인년당 11.56, 16.54, 22.33); 보정 위험비 (adjusted hazard ratio, HR)는 현재흡연 1.59 (95% CI 1.20-2.09), 과거흡연 1.16 (0.94-1.45, 비유의)이며 하루 10개비 초과 1.31, 10년 초과 1.27, 10갑년 (pack-years) 초과 1.30에서만 유의했다.

성별 층화 시 현재흡연자 HR은 남성 1.48 (1.11-1.97), 여성 3.39 (1.25-9.17)였고, 조기·후기 실패 구분과 임플란트 수준 변수(시스템, 골질, 술자)는 없으며 흡연은 자가보고, 추적은 최대 4년이다; 국적·인종은 변수로 명시되지 않고 한국 국가 데이터베이스라는 설정에서 추정될 뿐이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- 23,573 Korean adults with completed implant treatment; 605 failures (2.6%).
- Current smokers adjusted HR 1.59 (1.20-2.09); ex-smokers 1.16 (0.94-1.45, not significant).
- Dose-response: >10 cigarettes/day 1.31, >10 years 1.27, >10 pack-years 1.30; lighter exposures not significant.
- Female current smokers HR 3.39 (1.25-9.17) vs male 1.48 (1.11-1.97).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Retrospective cohort, NHIS-HEALS 1 Jan 2016 to 31 Dec 2019; index date = implantation (ICD-10 K081 plus procedure codes); health exam within 12 months required.
- Failure = reimplantation (osseointegration failure; reimbursable recipients only) or removal surgery (simple removal; complex removal with trephine bur or removal kit for fixture fracture or potential nerve injury). Claim-based.
- Smoking self-reported at screening; thresholds >10 cigarettes/day, >10 years, >10 pack-years.
- Cox proportional hazards, Kaplan-Meier/log-rank, subgroup by sex; R 4.3.0 and SAS; STROBE; Youngsan University IRB, consent waived.

| Non-smoker | 16,405 | 344 (2.1%) | 11.56 | reference |

- Subgroups vs non-smokers: 10 or fewer/day HR 1.07 (0.74-1.53); >10/day 1.31 (1.06-1.61); >10 years 1.27 (1.04-1.57); >10 pack-years 1.30 (1.05-1.62). Sex: males 1.48 (1.11-1.97), females 3.39 (1.25-9.17).
- Early vs late failure timing is not reported; denominator is persons, not implants; follow-up at most 4 years.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Self-reported smoking; no cotinine.
- No implant-specific variables (bone quality, system, technique, operator).
- Follow-up captures medium-term outcomes; authors call for longer follow-up for late failure.
- Observational, residual confounding.
- Claim-based failure definition; authors suggest treating smoking as a continuous variable.

## 7. Glossary

- **NHIS-HEALS (건강검진코호트)**: Korean National Health Insurance Service health screening cohort.
- **Pack-years (갑년)**: packs per day multiplied by years smoked.
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: relative event rate in a Cox model.
- **Reimplantation / removal surgery (재식립 / 제거술)**: claim procedures defining failure.
