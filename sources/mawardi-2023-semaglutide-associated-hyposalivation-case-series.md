## Why Ingested

GLP-1 수용체작용제(semaglutide/Ozempic·Wegovy)가 체중감량용으로 급증하는데, 위키의 xerogenic-drug 목록에는 이 계열이 없었다. 이 첫 증례보고(3례)는 semaglutide-연관 hyposalivation이라는 새 약물 부작용을 기록해, 기전 리뷰 [[oral-medicine/kim-2023-xerostomia-cellular-targets]](약물성 구강건조 >400종)와 타액-당뇨 지표 [[drug/systemic-disease/maftei-2021-salivary-markers-hba1c-type2-diabetes-extraction]]를 임상 증례로 연결한다. 진료 시 GLP-1 복용력 문진의 근거.

## Three-line Summary

Case series (Medicine 2023, n=3 overweight women, mean 11.3 weeks on semaglutide) reporting the first documented semaglutide (GLP-1 agonist)–associated hyposalivation — severe dry mouth, mean modified Schirmer 9 mL/3 min, Sjögren-negative — reversible on discontinuation or manageable with pilocarpine.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Medicine (Baltimore) 2023
- **Type**: Case series (n=3)
- **DOI**: 10.1097/MD.0000000000036730 · **PMID**: 38206684 · **PMCID**: PMC10754586
- **Setting**: Oral medicine clinic (Saudi Arabia).

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First published report** linking semaglutide (GLP-1 receptor agonist) to hyposalivation/xerostomia — a previously undocumented oral adverse effect.
- Documents a **reversible, dose-duration-related** pattern with objective salivary flow measurement (modified Schirmer test).
- Provides a **management template** (discontinuation vs continue + pilocarpine vs conservative) driven by shared physician–patient decision.
- Recommends **counseling and dental follow-up** for patients starting semaglutide.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Retrospective clinical case series of 3 overweight women presenting to an oral-medicine clinic with xerostomia while on subcutaneous semaglutide 0.5 mg weekly. Unstimulated salivary flow quantified by **modified Schirmer test** (mL at 3 minutes). Systematic exclusion of alternative causes: dehydration, radiation, diabetes, and Sjögren's syndrome (RF, anti-Ro, anti-La, ANA panel all normal; HbA1c 5.9–6.0).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Case | Age | BMI | Duration | Baseline Schirmer | Management | Follow-up Schirmer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 46 | 35 | 12 wk | 10 mL/3 min | Discontinue semaglutide | 30 mL/3 min (4 wk) |
| 2 | 27 | 37 | 16 wk | 9 mL/3 min | Continue + pilocarpine 5 mg/day | 25 mL/3 min (4 wk) |
| 3 | 34 | 35 | 6 wk | 8 mL/3 min | Continue (patient choice) | 27 mL/3 min (16 wk) |

- Mean age 34 (27–46), mean BMI 35.6, mean semaglutide duration 11.3 weeks; mean baseline modified Schirmer **9 mL/3 min** (8–10).
- All Sjögren panels normal; HbA1c 5.9–6.0 (non-diabetic range).
- Reference values cited: stimulated salivary flow 1.5–2 mL/min; unstimulated 0.3–0.4 mL/min; xerostomia prevalence 5.5–46%.
- Onset ~4 weeks after starting semaglutide.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Case series (n=3)** — hypothesis-generating only; no causal proof, no control group.
- Possible confounders: mild dehydration (all within lower-normal water intake), semaglutide-related diarrhea and nasopharyngitis (mouth breathing).
- Case 1 on concurrent methotrexate (no known hyposalivation association per authors' search).
- Authors call for **prospective, larger studies** to confirm; other GLP-1 agonists (liraglutide, dulaglutide, exenatide, etc.) not yet reported with this effect.

## 7. Glossary

- **Semaglutide** — GLP-1 receptor agonist (Ozempic/Wegovy) for type 2 diabetes and weight loss; subcutaneous weekly or oral.
- **GLP-1 receptor agonist** — incretin-mimetic delaying gastric emptying, suppressing appetite; also natriuretic/diuretic.
- **Hyposalivation** — objective reduction of salivary flow (vs xerostomia = subjective dryness).
- **Modified Schirmer test** — strip-based measurement of unstimulated salivary flow (mL over set time).
- **Sjögren panel** — RF, anti-Ro (SSA), anti-La (SSB), ANA used to exclude Sjögren's syndrome.
