## Why Ingested

치과대학생 응급 처치 자기 확신도 + 감성지능 (Emotional Intelligence) 분석 — 기술적 지식·실기 훈련·감성지능이 응급 처치 자신감에 미치는 영향을 동시에 평가한 최초 연구. [[drug/medical-emergencies/olus-2026-code-blue-awareness-dental-students]] (인지도)와 짝을 이뤄 치과대학 교육과정에서의 응급 준비도 다차원 모델을 구성한다.

## One-line Summary

Cross-sectional study (n=100, Brazil) found 82% of dental students felt unprepared for emergencies, with perceived confidence significantly predicted by technical training, practical training, and emotional intelligence scores.

## 한줄요약

단면연구 (n=100, 브라질 치과대학생) — 82%가 응급 처치 미준비 느낌, 자기 확신도 (Perceived Confidence)는 기술 교육·실기 훈련·감성지능 (Emotional Intelligence, EI) 에 의해 유의하게 예측됨.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: European Journal of Dental Education, Vol. 30, Issue 4, pp. 1864–1868 (2026)
- Affiliation: Universidade Estadual da Paraíba (UEPB), Campina Grande/Araruna, Brazil
- Ethics: UEPB HREC, protocol 82491024.8.0000.5187

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study examining emotional intelligence as a predictor of emergency confidence in dental students
- Quantifies the magnitude of unpreparedness: 82% not prepared for emergency management, 91% cannot administer injectable medications
- Demonstrates that EI correlates with diagnostic preparedness (p = 0.04)
- Practical training (not just theoretical) is the main modifiable predictor
- Highlights the psychoemotional dimension of emergency preparedness — purely cognitive training may be insufficient

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Observational, cross-sectional, analytical study
- **Population**: 100 undergraduate dentistry students in clinical courses, UEPB, Brazil
- **Instruments**: Sociodemographic questionnaire + emergency preparation/experience instrument + TEIQue-SF (Trait Emotional Intelligence Questionnaire–Short Form, validated for Brazil)
- **Analysis**: Fisher's exact test, Student's t-test; significance p < 0.05; SPSS v.25.0

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Not prepared to manage emergencies**: 82%
- **Unable to diagnose complications**: 61%
- **Cannot administer injectable medications**: 91%
- **Mean EI score**: 5.06 ± 0.49 (TEIQue-SF scale)
- **Confidence vs. preparedness training**: Significant (p < 0.01) — students with satisfactory training had higher confidence
- **EI vs. diagnostic preparedness**: Students who felt able to diagnose had significantly higher EI (p = 0.04)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single institution (UEPB, Brazil); limited generalizability
- Cross-sectional — cannot determine causality between EI and confidence
- Self-reported confidence only; no objective skill assessment
- Small sample (n=100); EI instrument not specific to dental emergencies
- TEIQue-SF measures trait EI — state EI under real emergency conditions may differ

## 6. Related Work

- olus-2026: Code Blue awareness in Turkish dental students — similar picture of low preparedness
- khattak-2026: BLS knowledge/attitude in practicing dentists (Saudi Arabia)
- cangul-2026: preparedness gaps in practicing dentists, Turkey

## 7. Glossary

- **TEIQue-SF**: Trait Emotional Intelligence Questionnaire–Short Form — self-report measure of emotional self-efficacy
- **Perceived confidence**: Subjective sense of readiness to manage emergencies; mediates training→performance relationship
- **Injectable medication administration**: Epinephrine, glucose, antihistamines — skills dental students often report as barriers to emergency response
