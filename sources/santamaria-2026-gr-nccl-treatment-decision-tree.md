## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (J Periodontol 2026; Medline/PubMed, Embase, BIREME, Google Scholar searched) updating the decision tree for combined defects (CDs) — gingival recession (GR) coexisting with a non-carious cervical lesion (NCCL), reported in approximately 50% of GRs.

NCCL portion classified by Pini-Prato 2010 (A-/A+/B-/B+) and GR portion by Cairo 2011 plus gingival phenotype: A-, A+ and B- NCCLs need root coverage surgery alone; B+ or V-shaped NCCLs need a composite restorative protocol plus root coverage (about 25% of cases are B+ per the plain-language summary).

Graft choice follows phenotype (thin: autogenous graft; thick: graft may be unnecessary; moderate: autogenous or commercial substitute); abstract-only ingest, no pooled numbers — a framework, not outcome evidence.

## 세줄요약

치은퇴축(GR)의 약 50%에 동반되는 비우식성 치경부 병소(NCCL) "복합결손(CD)"의 치료 의사결정 트리를 최신 문헌(연조직 이식 신근거 포함)으로 갱신한 서술적 문헌고찰(J Periodontol 2026).

NCCL은 Pini-Prato 2010(A-/A+/B-/B+), GR은 Cairo 2011 + 치은 표현형으로 분류: A-·A+·B-형은 근면피개술 단독, B+형 또는 V자형은 복합레진 수복 프로토콜 + 근면피개술(쉬운 요약상 B+ 약 25%).

이식재는 표현형 기준(얇음: 자가이식, 두꺼움: 이식 불필요할 수 있음, 중간: 자가 또는 상용 대체재); 초록 기반 인제스트로 정량 결과 없음 — 결과 근거가 아닌 의사결정 프레임.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Defines combined defects (CDs) as coexistence of GR and NCCL on the same tooth, requiring protocols different from GR on an intact root surface.
- Proposes an updated decision tree combining NCCL classification (Pini-Prato et al. 2010) with GR classification (Cairo et al. 2011) and gingival phenotype.
- Core rule: A-, A+, B- NCCL → surgical root coverage only (technique chosen by GR characteristics); B+ or V-shaped NCCL → composite restorative protocol + root coverage procedure.
- Incorporates new soft-tissue graft evidence; phenotype-guided graft selection.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Narrative review; databases: Medline/PubMed, Embase, BIREME, Google Scholar. No systematic protocol, risk-of-bias assessment, or meta-analysis reported in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- ~50% of GRs are associated with NCCLs (abstract).
- Plain-language summary: most cases fall into mild categories (A-, A+, B-; shallow tooth defects) treatable by standard GR procedures alone; about 25% have a more advanced B+ (deep) defect requiring restoration plus gum surgery.
- Phenotype: thin → patient's own tissue graft usually gives best results; thick → graft may not be necessary; moderate → graft of patient tissue or commercial material can improve long-term success.
- Several approaches combine different surgical techniques/graft materials with different NCCL restoration protocols/materials.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: specific techniques, restoration materials, and supporting studies per decision node not verified.
- Briefing figure "75% root coverage alone" is an inference (complement of ~25% B+); the abstract states only "most cases" are A-/A+/B- and "about 25%" are B+. V-shaped lesions are not quantified. Briefing said "CTG preferred" for thin phenotype; abstract says "patient's own tissue" (autogenous) — CTG not named.
- Narrative review — expert framework; evidence strength per branch unknown.
