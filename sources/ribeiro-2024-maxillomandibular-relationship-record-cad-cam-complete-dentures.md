## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR; Arksey and O'Malley + Joanna Briggs Institute protocol; OSF-registered osf.io/rf4xm) of methods for recording the maxillomandibular relationship (MMR) in CAD-CAM complete denture (CECD) digital workflows, searched across MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, and Science Direct.

Of 4779 records screened, only 10 studies were included; the review identified 4 commercially available CAD-CAM denture systems (only the Baltic System and WholeYouNexteeth are individually named in the abstract) and 3 innovative (non-commercial) methods that abbreviate treatment to 2-4 visits, with the Baltic System and all 3 innovative techniques requiring a trial denture step.

Three techniques — 2 of the innovative methods plus WholeYouNexteeth — showed lower difficulty ratings regardless of operator skill, but the review is a descriptive map, not an accuracy comparison, and the authors caution that all techniques remain dependent on the learning curve and the patient's clinical condition.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First scoping review specifically mapping **methods for recording MMR** within CAD-CAM/digital complete-denture (CECD) workflows, rather than the digital CD workflow as a whole.
- Catalogs both **commercially available systems** (4 identified; Baltic System and WholeYouNexteeth individually named) and **innovative, non-commercial methods** (3 identified) as two parallel tracks of MMR-recording innovation.
- Surfaces that trial-denture placement is **inherent** to the Baltic System and to all 3 innovative techniques, and that these approaches are positioned as reducing treatment to 2-4 appointments (versus the more visit-heavy conventional/analog norm).
- Reports a descriptive **difficulty appraisal**: 3 techniques (2 innovative + WholeYouNexteeth) were rated lower difficulty regardless of the clinician's professional skill level — an adoption-relevant but non-validated signal, not a measured performance outcome.
- The screening yield itself is a finding: only 10 of 4779 identified records (~0.2%) met inclusion criteria, indicating a thin clinical evidence base for a commercially active corner of digital prosthodontics.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA-ScR–compliant scoping review, developed per the Arksey and O'Malley framework and the Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) scoping-review protocol; methods pre-registered on the Open Science Framework (osf.io/rf4xm).
- **Focus question**: "What are the different techniques for recording the maxillomandibular relationship in the digital workflow used in CECDs?"
- **Search**: two investigators searched MEDLINE (PubMed), Scopus, and Science Direct independently; 4779 articles identified.
- **Inclusion criteria**: clinical studies and reviews assessing techniques for recording MMR using a digital workflow for the manufacture of CAD-CAM (computer-engineered) complete dentures.
- **Analysis**: descriptive analysis considering study design, manufacturing system, clinical steps, tools used to determine MMR, and the difficulty level of the procedures.
- **Yield**: 10 studies included after screening.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest — full methodology detail (per-study manufacturing-system mapping, individual system/method names beyond Baltic System and WholeYouNexteeth, tool inventory) is not available from PubMed and the journal is not open access.
- Very small included-study set (n=10) relative to the breadth of the digital CD market — limits generalizability and precludes any pooled/statistical comparison (this is explicitly a scoping review, not a systematic review with meta-analysis).
- No accuracy, retention, or patient-reported outcome data are reported in the abstract; "difficulty level" is a descriptive, not validated, appraisal.
- Future: primary comparative/clinical studies quantifying MMR-recording accuracy and patient outcomes across the identified digital methods, which this scoping review sets up but does not itself provide.

## 7. Glossary

- **Maxillomandibular relationship (MMR)**: the spatial jaw-relation record (typically at centric relation) used to orient the maxillary and mandibular casts/scans to each other for denture fabrication.
- **CECD**: computer-engineered (CAD-CAM designed and manufactured) complete denture.
- **Baltic System**: a commercially available CAD-CAM denture system named in the abstract; its MMR-recording procedure inherently includes a trial denture step.
- **WholeYouNexteeth**: a commercially available CAD-CAM denture system named in the abstract; one of the three techniques rated lower difficulty regardless of operator skill.
- **PRISMA-ScR**: PRISMA extension for Scoping Reviews — reporting guideline used for this review's methods and results.
- **Scoping review**: a review type that systematically maps the extent, range, and nature of evidence on a topic without pooling or ranking outcomes (unlike a systematic review with meta-analysis).
