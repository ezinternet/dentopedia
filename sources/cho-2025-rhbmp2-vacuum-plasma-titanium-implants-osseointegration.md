## Why Ingested

사용자가 "임플란트 픽스쳐 진공 플라즈마 1분 처리 효과" PMC 풀텍스트를 요청 — 기존 `implants/surface/plasma`에 이미 [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/kahm-2024-vacuum-plasma-surface-treatment-osseointegration]](60초 VP, null 결과)·[[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/stacchi-2025-vacuum-plasma-early-implant-stability-rct]](50초 VP, 인체 RCT)가 있어, 이번 서베일런스는 그 갭 중 하나인 **진공 플라즈마 + 생체활성 코팅(rhBMP-2) 결합 전략**을 다룬 신규 in vivo 연구를 보강한다. Kahm 2024와 동일 계열 장비(Plasmapp ACTILINK)·동일 기관(부산대) 후속 연구로, kahm의 null 결과와 달리 **rhBMP-2와 결합했을 때 유의한 BIC/ITBD 향상**을 보고해 대비된다.

## Three-line Summary

In vivo beagle mandibular defect study (24 SLA implants, 4 beagles, 8-week healing) comparing untreated (NS), rhBMP-2-only (BS), vacuum plasma-only (PS), and combined plasma+rhBMP-2 (PB) titanium implants, using BIC, inter-thread bone density (ITBD), new bone area (NBA), micro-CT/medical-CT bone volume, and ISQ.

The combined PB group showed significantly higher BIC (83.91% vs 68.81%/70.98% for BS/PS), ITBD (81.32%, significantly higher than all other groups), and NBV by micro-CT (75.00% vs 52.87% for untreated) than the other groups; ISQ showed no significant between-group differences.

Vacuum plasma pretreatment (10 s exposure on companion in-vitro disks; same commercial device used on the in-vivo implants) appears to synergize with low-dose rhBMP-2 by increasing surface energy and improving protein retention/uniform coating, producing histometric osseointegration gains not captured by RFA/ISQ alone.

## 세줄요약

비글 하악 결손 모델(SLA 임플란트 24개, 비글 4마리, 8주 치유) — 무처리(NS)·rhBMP-2 단독(BS)·진공 플라즈마 단독(PS)·플라즈마+rhBMP-2 병용(PB) 4군을 골-임플란트 접촉률(Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)·나사산간 골밀도(Inter-Thread Bone Density, ITBD)·신생골면적(New Bone Area, NBA)·micro-CT/medical CT 골량·임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)로 비교.

병용군(PB)에서 BIC(83.91% vs BS 68.81%/PS 70.98%)·ITBD(81.32%, 전 군 대비 유의하게 높음)·micro-CT 신생골량(75.00% vs 무처리 52.87%)이 유의하게 우수했으나, ISQ는 군간 유의차 없음.

진공 플라즈마 전처리(체외 디스크 실험에서 10초 조사; 동일 상용 장비를 임플란트에도 사용)가 표면에너지 증가·rhBMP-2 결합 균일화를 통해 저용량 rhBMP-2와 시너지를 내는 것으로 보이며, 이는 RFA/ISQ만으로는 포착되지 않는 조직계측 수준의 골유착 향상이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First combination of vacuum plasma pretreatment with low-dose (0.05 mg/mL) rhBMP-2 coating on SLA titanium implants, evaluated in vivo.
- ELISA cumulative-release data show plasma pretreatment reduces rhBMP-2 burst release (42.39 vs 54.03 ng/mL control cumulative supernatant concentration over 21 days) — evidence of improved surface binding, not just faster osteogenic signaling.
- Demonstrates a dissociation between RFA/ISQ (no significant group differences) and histometric/micro-CT osseointegration measures (large, significant PB advantage) — cautions against relying on ISQ alone to detect surface/coating effects.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: animal in-vivo (beagle mandibular defect model) + companion in-vitro (SLA disk) mechanistic work.
- In vitro: SLA disks, plasma irradiation 10 s (ACTILINK Reborn, Plasmapp), ± 20 μg 0.05 mg/mL rhBMP-2; ALP activity, qRT-PCR, SEM, XPS, cumulative-release ELISA.
- In vivo: 24 SLA implants (ADDplant ON, 3.5×8.5 mm), 4 male beagles, 4 groups (NS/BS/PS/PB) randomized per defect; occlusal cylindrical defects with 3 exposed threads; 8-week healing; ISQ (Osstell), medical CT + micro-CT volumetric analysis, Goldner's trichrome histomorphometry (BIC, ITBD, NBA).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (4 beagles, 24 implants), single 8-week timepoint, single rhBMP-2 concentration (0.05 mg/mL).
- Plasma exposure duration for the in-vivo implants themselves is not separately stated (10 s reported only for the in-vitro disk protocol) — a documentation gap noted for future reproducibility.
- ISQ failed to detect the histometrically large PB advantage, echoing prior reports of weak BIC–RFA correlation.

## 6. Related Work

- [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/kahm-2024-vacuum-plasma-surface-treatment-osseointegration]] — same Plasmapp vacuum-plasma device family, SLA implants, but plasma-alone in rabbit femur; found no significant BIC/ISQ gain at 4 weeks (null). This paper's plasma-alone group (PS) similarly shows no significant advantage over rhBMP-2-alone (BS) — consistent with Kahm's null on plasma-alone — but the *combination* with rhBMP-2 (PB) is where significance emerges.
- [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/stacchi-2025-vacuum-plasma-early-implant-stability-rct]] — human split-mouth RCT, ~50 s vacuum plasma, early ISQ advantage at 60/90 days; this paper's ISQ null result (despite large BIC gains) is a useful animal-model counterpoint on the RFA/ISQ vs histometric-outcome dissociation.
- [[overviews/plasma-surface-treatment-dental-implants]] — this paper extends the existing synthesis's "chairside re-activation" thesis by showing plasma's clearest osseointegration benefit may be as a *carrier-surface enhancer* for a bioactive coating, not as a standalone treatment.

## 7. Glossary

- rhBMP-2 = recombinant human bone morphogenetic protein-2 (재조합 인간 골형성단백질-2).
- BIC = bone-to-implant contact (골-임플란트 접촉률).
- ITBD = inter-thread bone density (나사산간 골밀도).
- NBA/NBV = new bone area / new bone volume (신생골 면적/부피).
- ISQ = implant stability quotient (임플란트 안정성 지수), measured by resonance frequency analysis (RFA).
