## Three-line Summary
Mechanistic in vitro (HepG2, RAW 264.7, MC3T3-E1) + in vivo (4-week-old male ICR mice, n=30, 6/group; oral PDRN 20/30/40 mg/kg for 5 weeks) study of systemic PDRN on longitudinal growth — a non-dental cross-domain growth-plate model.
PDRN raised HepG2 IGF-1 mRNA and JAK2/STAT5 phosphorylation (p<0.05), abolished by the A2AR antagonist ZM241385; in mice, 30/40 mg/kg increased nose-to-anus and nose-to-tail length gain, growth-plate thickness and serum IGF-1 while GH and IGFBP-3 stayed unchanged (p<0.05).
Value to this dental wiki is mechanistic only: systemic longitudinal growth must not be extrapolated to alveolar/dental bone regeneration, and oral absorption/pharmacokinetics plus in-vivo A2AR involvement were not verified.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First report that systemic, orally administered PDRN promotes longitudinal bone growth in a growing (juvenile) mouse model — extends prior PDRN work from local tissue repair / in-vitro osteoblast effects to a systemic skeletal-growth endpoint.
- A2AR-dependency demonstrated in HepG2 cells: the selective A2AR antagonist ZM241385 (1 µM, 30 min pretreatment) significantly attenuated PDRN-induced IGF-1 mRNA elevation and JAK2/STAT5 phosphorylation (p<0.05) without cytotoxicity.
- GH-sparing/IGF-1-mediated pattern: serum IGF-1 rose significantly while GH and IGFBP-3 remained unchanged, and hepatic IGF-1 mRNA plus JAK2/STAT5 activation increased in vivo (significant at 40 mg/kg, p<0.05).
- Structural correlates: increased proximal tibial growth-plate thickness and radiolucent cartilage band, with improved tibial/femoral trabecular and cortical microarchitecture and reduced porosity on μCT.
- Bone-remodeling marker modulation: LPS-induced DC-STAMP/TRAP/CatK suppressed in RAW 264.7 osteoclast precursors; ALP, OCN, OPN, RUNX2, OSX, COL1A1, OPG and IGF-1 raised while RANKL lowered in MC3T3-E1 osteoblasts (all p<0.05).
- **Explicit scope limit**: none of these endpoints are dental/alveolar; the paper provides no implant, periodontal, socket-healing or alveolar-bone data.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: two-stage mechanistic study — (i) in-vitro cell-signaling and remodeling-marker assays, (ii) 5-week oral-dosing animal model with longitudinal body-length tracking and terminal μCT/histology.
- **Cell systems**: HepG2 (human hepatocellular carcinoma; hepatic IGF-1/JAK2-STAT5 and A2AR-blockade experiments), RAW 264.7 (murine pre-osteoclastic; LPS 100 ng/mL stimulation), MC3T3-E1 (murine pre-osteoblastic; osteogenic differentiation medium with 1 nM dexamethasone, 10 mM β-glycerophosphate, 50 µg/mL ascorbic acid).
- **A2AR blockade**: ZM241385 1 µM, 30 min pre-incubation before PDRN exposure in HepG2 cells.
- **PDRN preparation**: salmon-milt-derived, purity >80%, 150–400 bp (~97.5–260 kDa), supplied by COSMAX NBT, Inc. (Seongnam, Republic of Korea); dissolved in distilled water at 60 °C and 0.22 µm-filtered.
- **Animals**: 4-week-old male ICR mice (DAMOOL SCIENCE), N=6/group, total N=30, five groups — Normal (distilled water), PDRN 20/30/40 mg/kg, positive control (patented Humulus japonicus extract 100 mg/kg); daily oral gavage for 35 consecutive days; experimental unit = individual mouse. Only males used to avoid estrous-cycle variation.
- **Assays**: MTT viability; qRT-PCR (ΔΔCT, GAPDH reference) for IGF-1, IGFBP-3, BMP-2, DC-STAMP, TRAP, CatK, ALP, OCN, OPN, RUNX2, OSX, COL1A1, OPG, RANKL; Western blot for JAK2/p-JAK2, STAT5/p-STAT5 (in-vivo bands normalized to GAPDH to capture absolute changes); ELISA for serum IGF-1/IGFBP-3; GH ELISA; serum ALT/AST/BUN/CK/LDH/glucose; high-resolution μCT (SkyScan 1276; 70 kV, 200 µA, 0.5 mm Al filter, 180° rotation at 0.5° steps, 10.5 µm isotropic voxels).
- **Statistics**: G*Power a priori power analysis (n=6/group, power >80%, α=0.05); one-way ANOVA + Dunnett post hoc for endpoint and net-gain comparisons; two-way repeated-measures ANOVA for 35-day body weight; data as mean ± SEM (n=3 in vitro, n=6 in vivo); significance p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

All values and directions below are taken from the Results section of the provided full text. Exact effect magnitudes are figure-based and are **not tabulated** in the extracted JATS text, so no numeric magnitudes are asserted here.

**2.1 HepG2 (in vitro)**
- MTT: PDRN had no significant effect on cell viability.
- IGF-1 mRNA: significant dose-dependent increase (p<0.05).
- p-JAK2 and p-STAT5 markedly increased without changing total JAK2/STAT5 protein.
- ZM241385 significantly suppressed PDRN-induced p-JAK2/p-STAT5 and attenuated the IGF-1 mRNA increase (p<0.05), without cytotoxicity; ZM241385 alone had no effect on baseline IGF-1 mRNA.

**2.2 Liver (in vivo)**
- Hepatic IGF-1 mRNA increased, significant specifically at 40 mg/kg vs normal (p<0.05); IGFBP-3 unchanged; positive control affected neither marker significantly.
- Total and phosphorylated JAK2 and STAT5 protein elevated vs normal (p<0.05); unlike the acute in-vitro response, chronic dosing raised total as well as phosphorylated protein, hence GAPDH normalization.

**2.3 Systemic growth and growth plate**
- Nose-to-anus (N-A) and nose-to-tail (N-T) length gains significantly higher in PDRN 30 and 40 mg/kg vs normal (p<0.05); no significant difference between 30 and 40 mg/kg; no significant body-weight change in any group; positive control also increased body length without weight change.
- Serum ALT, AST, BUN, CK, LDH and glucose showed no significant changes.
- Serum IGF-1 significantly elevated (p<0.05); serum IGFBP-3 and GH unchanged.
- Proximal tibial growth-plate thickness and radiolucent band significantly increased (p<0.05); growth-plate IGF-1 and BMP-2 mRNA higher vs normal (p<0.05).

**2.4 μCT bone microarchitecture**
- Tibial length and trabecular BMD significantly increased (p<0.05); BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N and Conn.D significantly higher (p<0.05); Tb.Sp and total porosity significantly reduced (p<0.05).
- Positive control improved several indices but not Tb.Th or Tb.Sp significantly.
- Tibial cortical BMD and femoral BMD in the 40 mg/kg group were markedly higher than normal (no p-value stated in the extracted text).

**2.5 Remodeling markers**
- RAW 264.7: PDRN did not significantly affect viability; LPS induced DC-STAMP, TRAP and CatK mRNA (p<0.05) and PDRN suppressed this dose-dependently (p<0.05).
- MC3T3-E1 (OD conditions): PDRN rescued viability vs OD group (p<0.05); ALP, OCN, OPN, RUNX2, OSX, COL1A1, OPG and IGF-1 expression increased while RANKL decreased (p<0.05).
- Low-dose PDRN + Ca or Mg combination increased IGF-1 mRNA and improved N-A/N-T gains, but no formal interaction/synergy analysis was done.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **CROSS-DOMAIN / NON-DENTAL**: no dental, alveolar, periodontal, implant or socket-healing endpoint exists in this paper — systemic longitudinal growth must not be extrapolated to dental/alveolar bone regeneration on the strength of these data.
- A2AR involvement was tested only in HepG2 cells; it was not directly tested in vivo, and a direct A2AR effect on growth-plate chondrocytes was not determined.
- HepG2 is a hepatocellular carcinoma line and may not represent normal hepatocyte IGF-1 regulation; primary/normal hepatocyte confirmation is needed.
- Oral pharmacokinetics absent: intact absorption vs gastrointestinal degradation to oligonucleotides/adenosine/nucleosides was not measured, so direct hepatic A2AR activation by intact oral PDRN is explicitly called speculative by the authors.
- Serum GH measured at a single time point; no GH pulsatility, GHR expression or downstream GHR signaling, and no direct/genetic GH inhibition — an unchanged basal GH cannot establish a GH-independent/GH-sparing mechanism.
- Single endpoint measurement; the mechanism behind chronic in-vivo elevation of total JAK2/STAT5 remains unexplained.
- Small sample size (n=6/group); larger studies needed to confirm the reported safety/biochemistry profile.
- Male mice only; female sensitivity untested. Mice lack true epiphyseal closure and retain continuous growth-plate activity, so pediatric human translation is uncertain.
- μCT could not resolve proliferative vs hypertrophic growth-plate sub-zones; zonal-marker histology needed.
- Remodeling evidence is marker-expression only, not functional osteoclast formation (e.g., TRAP staining) or mineralized-matrix deposition.
- Ca/Mg combination effects were reported without synergy/dose-response analysis; additivity vs synergy is unestablished.

## 7. Glossary

- **PDRN**: Polydeoxyribonucleotide — DNA-derived purine/pyrimidine polymer (here 150–400 bp, ~97.5–260 kDa from salmon milt), acting via A2AR agonism and nucleotide salvage.
- **A2AR / A2A receptor**: Adenosine A2A receptor; the target blocked by ZM241385 in the HepG2 experiments.
- **IGF-1**: Insulin-like Growth Factor 1 — liver-derived mediator of GH-driven longitudinal growth; elevated here without GH change.
- **GH**: Growth Hormone — upstream endocrine driver of hepatic IGF-1; basal serum level unchanged in this study.
- **JAK2 / STAT5**: Janus kinase 2 / Signal Transducer and Activator of Transcription 5 — intracellular cascade linking GH-receptor activation to IGF-1 transcription.
- **IGFBP-3**: IGF-Binding Protein 3 — major carrier of circulating IGF-1; unchanged in serum and liver here.
- **Growth plate**: epiphyseal cartilaginous region where endochondral ossification drives linear skeletal growth.
- **μCT**: micro-computed tomography; used for trabecular/cortical BMD, BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N, Tb.Sp, Conn.D, porosity, tibial length and growth-plate thickness.
- **Osteoclast markers**: DC-STAMP, TRAP, Cathepsin K — LPS-induced in RAW 264.7 cells and suppressed by PDRN.
- **Osteoblast markers**: ALP, OCN, OPN, RUNX2, OSX, COL1A1, OPG, IGF-1 — increased in MC3T3-E1 cells; RANKL decreased.
- **BMP-2**: Bone Morphogenetic Protein 2 — growth-plate morphogen whose local mRNA rose with PDRN.
- **ZM241385**: selective A2A receptor antagonist used to test A2AR mediation.
- **Positive control (PC)**: patented non-hormonal Humulus japonicus extract (100 mg/kg), orally active reference standard that also raises IGF-1.
