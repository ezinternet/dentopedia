## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional/cohort study at Seoul National University Dental Hospital (implants placed 2005-2015, at least 5 years of follow-up after prosthesis delivery) of sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched (SLA) Dentium implants: 782 implants in 400 patients for survival and 372 implants for marginal bone loss (MBL).

Overall cumulative survival was 96.8% (25 failures; 17 peri-implantitis, 5 mechanical, 3 osseointegration failure); only implant type was independently associated with loss (double-threaded tapered vs dual-thread: HR 2.507, 95% CI 1.058-5.937, p=0.037), while diabetes (HR 1.962, p=0.131) and smoking (HR 2.050, p=0.333) were not significant.

Mean MBL after about 5 years of loading was 1.85±2.31 mm (mesial) and 1.59±2.03 mm (distal), greater in the maxilla and in augmented sites; the paper itself notes the small smoker group and panoramic-radiograph measurement as limits, so the null smoking/diabetes survival findings should not be read as proof of no effect.

## 세줄요약

서울대학교치과병원에서 2005-2015년 식립한 SLA 표면 Dentium 임플란트를 보철 후 5년 이상 추적한 후향적 연구: 생존율 분석 782개 임플란트(환자 400명), 변연골 소실 (marginal bone loss, MBL) 분석 372개.

전체 누적 생존율 (cumulative survival rate) 96.8% (실패 25개: 치주염 17, 기계적 합병증 5, 골유착 실패 3); 이중나사산 테이퍼형 대 이중나사 직선형의 임플란트 소실 위험비 (hazard ratio, HR) 2.507 (95% CI 1.058-5.937, p=0.037)만 독립적으로 유의했고, 당뇨 (HR 1.962, p=0.131)와 흡연 (HR 2.050, p=0.333)은 유의하지 않았다.

보철 후 약 5년 평균 MBL은 근심 1.85±2.31 mm, 원심 1.59±2.03 mm로 상악과 골이식 부위에서 더 컸다; 흡연자 수가 적고 파노라마 방사선으로 측정한 한계가 있어 흡연·당뇨의 비유의 결과를 영향 없음의 증거로 해석하면 안 된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Single-system (Dentium SLA) Korean hospital dataset with at least 5 years of post-prosthesis follow-up: overall implant survival 96.8%, mean time to loss 8.26±3.53 years among failures.
- Reports survival at both implant and patient level; the 25 failed implants are itemized (17 peri-implantitis, 5 mechanical including screw/fixture fracture, 3 osseointegration failure).
- Multivariate Cox: only implant type was significant (tapered double-threaded design HR 2.507, 95% CI 1.058-5.937, p=0.037); authors attribute this partly to the tapered design being introduced later (shorter mean survival time 7.51±2.85 vs 8.84±3.87 years).
- MBL after about 5 years: 1.85±2.31 mm mesial, 1.59±2.03 mm distal; maxilla, bone augmentation, younger age, narrower diameter, and splinted prostheses were linked to greater MBL in regression.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cross-sectional study (STROBE), IRB No. ERI22024 at SNUDH, informed consent waived; single center.
- **Period/population**: Implant treatment January 2005 - December 2015; inclusion required at least 5 years of follow-up after prosthesis installation with sufficient records and final-prosthesis radiographs. Survival analysis: 782 implants in 400 patients (mean age 66.22 years, range 20-85). MBL analysis: 372 implants.
- **Implants**: SLA-surface Dentium implants: Implantium (straight, dual-thread with neck micro-threads, 446 implants) and Superline (tapered double-threaded body, 336 implants).
- **Variables**: sex, age, diabetes mellitus (on anti-diabetic drugs), smoking, location, jaw, implant type, diameter, length, staged surgery, immediate placement, bone augmentation, prosthesis unit.
- **Failure definition**: removal of the implant for any reason seen on panoramic radiograph (early failure before functional loading excluded in the variable definition); bone loss without mobility with implant retained counted as peri-implantitis, not failure. Peri-implantitis: bleeding/suppuration plus radiographic bone loss of 3 mm or more versus post-delivery radiograph.
- **MBL**: panoramic radiographs, implant-abutment junction to most coronal bone-to-implant contact, measured by two examiners on PACS, calibrated to fixture length; implants failing within 5 years of loading or not measurable were excluded.
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier with log-rank; univariate Cox then the 6 most significant factors into multivariate backward-stepwise Cox; Mann-Whitney/Kruskal-Wallis and stepwise multiple regression for MBL; SPSS 25; p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Overall cumulative survival | 96.8% (25 failed of 782) |
| Failure causes | peri-implantitis 17, mechanical 5, osseointegration failure 3 |
| Survival without / with DM | 97.3% / 94.4% (7 of 25 failures in DM, 125 DM implants) |
| Survival non-smoker / smoker | 96.9% / 95.1% (41 smoker implants, 2 failures) |
| Tapered vs dual-thread (multivariate) | HR 2.507, 95% CI 1.058-5.937, p=0.037 |
| DM univariate (patient level) | HR 1.962, p=0.131 |
| Smoking univariate (patient level) | HR 2.050, p=0.333 |
| Narrow / standard / wide diameter survival | 99.1% / 95.6% / 98.8% (not significant) |
| Short (7-8 mm) / standard length survival | 96.3% / 96.9% |
| Mean MBL (mesial / distal) | 1.85±2.31 mm / 1.59±2.03 mm |

- MBL by jaw: maxilla 2.21±2.47 (mesial) and 1.99±2.29 (distal) vs mandible 1.27±1.92 and 0.96±1.30 mm (p=0.000 both sides).
- Stepwise regression: age over 60 lower mesial MBL (p=0.001); wide diameter lower mesial MBL (p=0.014); DM higher distal MBL (p=0.013); bone augmentation higher distal MBL (p=0.004); splinted prosthesis higher mesial MBL (p=0.032); maxilla higher MBL (p=0.000 both sides).
- All other univariate survival variables (sex, age, location, jaw, length, staged surgery, immediate placement, bone augmentation, prosthesis type) were not significant.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective single-center design; no operator effect analysis (stated by the authors).
- MBL measured on panoramic radiographs, not digital intraoral images (authors cite lower accuracy); only 372 of the cohort assessable.
- Only 25 failure events: few smokers (41 implants) and wide confidence intervals (e.g. smoking HR 95% CI 0.479-8.782); authors attribute null smoking findings to small numbers and uncontrolled factors.
- Survival is not success; authors recommend an indicator of biological complications.
- Internal inconsistencies in the retrieved text: Figure 1 caption says 728 implants identified whereas Results state 782; sex rows in Table 1 give implant counts (410/372) though the text labels them men and women; the text lists 4 variables entering the multivariate model though 6 are stated; the text calls DM and smoking "significant" in univariate analysis while reporting p>0.05; the text gives survival with/without DM as 97.3% and 94.4% in an order that is the reverse of the table wording. Interpret numbers from the tables.
- Tapered vs straight comparison confounded by system era and different follow-up time.

## 7. Glossary

- **SLA (sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched)**: common roughened titanium implant surface.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골 소실)**: radiographic crestal bone level change from prosthesis delivery.
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: relative event rate in Cox model.
- **Dual-thread design / double-threaded tapered body design**: Implantium (straight with neck micro-threads) vs Superline (tapered) implant types.
