## Why Ingested

사용자가 요청한 PMC 전문 논문으로, 미성숙 영구치의 개방 근단공(open apex) 관리에서 White MTA의 역할을 정량화한 SR+MA다. 기존 [[endodontics/regenerative/panda-2022-regenerative-apexification-immature-teeth-sr-ma]]가 REP/apexification 전반의 성공률을 다뤘다면, 본 논문(Li 2026)은 WMTA를 관교(coronal barrier)로 특정해 방사선학적 성공(pooled 0.99)과 PC-vs-BC scaffold 비교, WMTA 대 대체 bioceramic 간 변색 위험(58.33% vs 7.69%, P=.01)까지 별도로 분리해 정량화한 점에서 기존 지식을 세분화·보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD420261337704) of 15 studies (4 RCTs, 2 cohorts, 9 case reports/series) on White MTA (WMTA) and alternative bioceramics in regenerative endodontic procedures (REP) and apexification for immature permanent teeth, finding pooled radiographic success of 0.99 (95% CI 0.96-1.00, very low certainty), a non-significant trend favouring platelet concentrates (PC) over blood clot (BC) scaffolds (OR 3.56, 95% CI 0.87-14.52), and significantly higher tooth discolouration with WMTA versus alternative bioceramics (58.33% vs 7.69%, P=.01).

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: International Dental Journal
- Publication date: 2026-05-20
- DOI: 10.1016/j.identj.2026.109611; PMID 42160814; PMCID PMC13217591
- Protocol registered in PROSPERO (CRD420261337704), following PRISMA and Cochrane Handbook methodology
- Funding/conflicts not stated in the extracted full text

## 2. Key Contributions

- Isolates WMTA's effect specifically as a **coronal barrier** in REP/apexification, distinct from prior broader syntheses (e.g., Abdellatif et al.'s 30-study review of WMTA and other bioactive materials, reporting an overall 91.20% success rate without separating WMTA's coronal-barrier-specific role).
- Quantifies radiographic success as a pooled proportion (0.99, 95% CI 0.96-1.00) across 6 studies/112 observations, with subgroup analysis by scaffold combination (PRP+MTA, PRF+MTA).
- Directly compares platelet concentrate (PC: PRP/PRF/PP) versus blood clot (BC) scaffolds on radiographic success (OR 3.56, 95% CI 0.87-14.52) — trend favouring PC, not statistically significant.
- Directly compares WMTA versus alternative bioceramics (Biodentine, Totalfill bioceramic putty) on clinical success and, critically, on tooth discolouration risk (58.33% vs 7.69%, P=.01).
- Applies GRADE certainty assessment per outcome, revealing that despite high point-estimate success rates, evidence certainty ranges from very low (radiographic success, PC vs BC) to moderate (WMTA vs alternative bioceramics clinical success).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis per PRISMA and Cochrane Handbook; PROSPERO-registered.
- **Search**: PubMed/MEDLINE (primary), plus Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, database inception to September 1, 2025, no language restriction.
- **Selection**: Studies evaluating WMTA in pulp regeneration for immature/necrotic permanent teeth — RCTs and prospective cohorts prioritized; non-RCTs and case reports/series included given limited RCT availability. Included WMTA as apical plug (apexification) or coronal barrier in REP with scaffolds (PRP, PRF, PP, BC).
- **Risk of bias**: RoB2 for RCTs; ROBINS-I for non-RCTs; case report guidelines for case series.
- **Statistics**: Random-effects model for pooled proportions and odds ratios (95% CI); Freeman-Tukey double arcsine transformation and generalised linear mixed models for boundary-value proportions; subgroups stratified by scaffold type (PRP+WMTA, PRF+WMTA, PP+WMTA, apexification+WMTA); heterogeneity via τ², I², Cochran's Q; certainty via GRADE.
- **Study flow**: 229 records identified (183 primary databases + 46 secondary) → 32 duplicates removed → 197 screened → 164 excluded at title/abstract → 33 full-text assessed → 18 excluded (13 unsuitable design, 5 reviews/meta-analyses) → 15 studies included (4 RCTs, 2 cohort studies, 9 case reports/series), predominantly from India, Egypt, and Turkey.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Radiographic success** (6 studies, 112 observations, 110 events): pooled proportion 0.99 (95% CI 0.96-1.00, Freeman-Tukey); 0.96 (95% CI 0.90-0.99, GLMM). No heterogeneity (τ²<0.0001, I²=0.00%, Q=0.01, P=1.00). Subgroups: PRP+MTA 0.98 (95% CI 0.87-1.00); PRF+MTA 0.98 (95% CI 0.88-1.00); no subgroup difference (P=1.00).
- **PC vs BC scaffold on radiographic success** (4 studies, 145 observations): pooled OR 3.56 (95% CI 0.87-14.52), favouring PC but CI crosses null. No heterogeneity (I²=0.00%, P=.52). Subgroup: PRP+MTA significant (OR 7.49, 95% CI 1.16-48.22); PRF+MTA and PP+MTA not significant; subgroup-difference test not significant (P=.43).
- **WMTA vs alternative bioceramics, clinical success** (2 studies, 67 observations, 63 events): alternative bioceramics 100.00% vs WMTA, OR 0.18 (95% CI 0.02-1.68), not significant. No heterogeneity (I²=0.00%, χ²=0.13, P=.71).
- **Tooth discolouration**: Aly et al. 58.33% (WMTA) vs 7.69% (Biodentine), P=.01 (significant); Bartaw et al. 14.30% (WMTA) vs 0.00% (Totalfill), not significant.
- **Pulp sensibility**: 60%-86% positive-response rates across studies (Nageh et al. 60.00% at 12 months P<.0001; Alagl et al. 76.70% at 5 months; Ulusoy et al. 86.00% overall, no significant group differences).
- **Root development**: PRP group showed significantly greater root length increase vs BC (Alagl et al., P=.004); conversely BC group showed significantly greater increases in radiographic root area/canal area than most PC groups (Ulusoy et al., P<.05) — i.e., PC favoured root lengthening, BC favoured dentinal wall/canal area thickening.
- No heterogeneity in any meta-analysis (all I²=0.0%). Funnel plots symmetrical (no evident publication bias).
- **GRADE certainty**: radiographic success — very low; PC vs BC — very low; WMTA vs alternative bioceramics clinical success — moderate (downgraded only for imprecision).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Author-stated: (1) limited number and modest sample sizes restrict statistical power/generalisability; (2) predominantly short-to-medium-term follow-up (12-36 months) may miss late complications (periapical pathology, resorption); (3) possible publication bias toward positive outcomes; (4) overall low certainty of evidence (GRADE) for most outcomes.
- Additional limitations identified: majority of included studies (9/15) are case reports/series, inherently prone to selection bias; non-RCTs consistently showed serious overall risk of bias (ROBINS-I) due to non-randomised design; PC vs BC comparison downgraded for indirectness reflecting heterogeneous PC preparation protocols across studies.
- Future work: authors call for high-quality RCTs with standardised protocols to strengthen certainty, particularly for scaffold-type comparisons and long-term discolouration/root-maturation outcomes.

## 6. Related Work

- Abdellatif et al. — prior synthesis of 30 studies on WMTA and other bioactive materials in pulp regeneration for permanent teeth in paediatric patients, reporting 91.20% overall success rate but not isolating WMTA's coronal-barrier-specific role (the gap this paper addresses).
- Bartaw et al. — RCT comparing Totalfill bioceramic putty vs WMTA in necrotic immature maxillary incisors (ages 8-14), showing comparable clinical success with reduced discolouration for Totalfill.
- Aly et al. — RCT comparing Biodentine and WMTA as direct pulp capping/coronal barrier agents, informing the discolouration and root-length comparisons.
- Kahler et al. — systematic work evaluating success against AAE standards, but without isolating WMTA's independent contribution.
- Ulusoy et al. — case series evaluating PRP/PRF/PP vs BC scaffolds, contributing root development and pulp sensibility data.

## 7. Glossary

- **WMTA** — White Mineral Trioxide Aggregate; bioceramic material used as an apical plug (apexification) or coronal barrier (REP).
- **REP** — Regenerative Endodontic Procedure.
- **PC** — Platelet concentrates (encompasses PRP, PRF, PP).
- **PRP** — Platelet-Rich Plasma.
- **PRF** — Platelet-Rich Fibrin.
- **PP** — Platelet-Poor Plasma.
- **BC** — Blood clot (scaffold).
- **RoB2 / ROBINS-I** — Cochrane risk-of-bias tools for randomised (RoB2) and non-randomised (ROBINS-I) studies.
- **GRADE** — Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluation; framework for rating certainty of evidence.
- **SCAP** — Stem cells of the apical papilla.
- **AAE** — American Association of Endodontists.
- **PROSPERO** — International prospective register of systematic reviews.
