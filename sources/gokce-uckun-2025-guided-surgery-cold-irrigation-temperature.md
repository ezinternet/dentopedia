## Why Ingested

First ex vivo study to isolate irrigation temperature (24°C vs 10°C) as a dedicated variable in guided implant surgery thermal safety, directly answering whether cold irrigation provides a measurable advantage over room-temperature irrigation. The counter-intuitive finding — low-speed drilling without irrigation does NOT reduce thermal risk and can exceed the +10°C critical threshold — challenges a common clinical assumption and adds a clinically actionable data point to the `implants/osteotomy-thermal` evidence cluster alongside [[wiki/implants/osteotomy-thermal/tur-2025-scais-cip-guided-conventional-drill-diameter-heat]].

## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo study (Sakarya/Istanbul Universities; 40 sheep iliac crest specimens, 192 implant sites) comparing intraosseous temperature across three guided-surgery protocols: high-speed (900 rpm) with 24°C irrigation, low-speed (300–600 rpm) without irrigation, and high-speed (900 rpm) with 10°C irrigation, using K-type thermocouples at 1 mm from the osteotomy site.

Cold 10°C irrigation (Group C) was the only protocol to significantly reduce mean temperature versus both alternatives (28 ± 3°C vs 30.5 ± 4°C and 31 ± 5°C, p=0.001); low-speed drilling without irrigation showed no thermal advantage over high-speed with room-temperature irrigation and caused 8/80 specimens to exceed the +10°C threshold at 600 rpm.

Guided surgery generated consistently higher temperatures than conventional technique; wider bur diameter and greater depth both independently increased temperature regardless of protocol, but cold irrigation effectively attenuated the diameter effect at shallow depths.

## 세줄요약

사카리아/이스탄불대학교 체외 연구(양 장골능 시편 40개, 골이식 부위 192개) — 유도 식립 3개 프로토콜(A: 고속 900 rpm + 24°C 관개, B: 저속 300–600 rpm 무관개, C: 고속 900 rpm + 10°C 관개) 비교; K형 열전대(골절개부 1 mm 위치) 사용.

10°C 냉각 관개(C군)만이 평균 온도를 양 대조군보다 유의하게 낮췄으며(28 ± 3°C vs 30.5 ± 4°C·31 ± 5°C, p=0.001); 저속 무관개(B군)는 고속 관개(A군) 대비 열적 이점이 없었고 600 rpm에서 8/80 시편이 +10°C 임계값을 초과함.

가이드 수술은 기존 술식보다 일관되게 높은 온도를 나타냈으며, 버 직경 확대 및 깊이 증가는 프로토콜에 관계없이 온도를 독립적으로 높였으나 냉각 관개는 얕은 깊이에서 직경 효과를 효과적으로 감쇠시킴.

## Summary

Ex vivo study (Sakarya/Istanbul Universities) on 40 sheep iliac crest bone specimens (D3, CBCT-confirmed mean 696 HU), 192 implant sites total. Stereolithographic surgical guide fabricated with Real Guide 5.0; K-type thermocouple 1mm from osteotomy site, 100ms sampling.

Three study groups:
- **Group A** (n=80): Guided + conventional, high speed 900 rpm, 24°C irrigation (100 mL/min)
- **Group B** (n=80): Guided + conventional, low speed 300–600 rpm, NO irrigation
- **Group C** (n=36): Guided + conventional, high speed 900 rpm, 10°C irrigation

Four guided implant sizes: 2.9×8.5mm, 2.9×13mm, 3.8×8.5mm, 3.8×13mm; control: 3.5×10mm conventional

Key results:
- Mean temperatures: Group A 30.5 ± 4°C, Group B 31 ± 5°C, Group C 28 ± 3°C (from 24°C baseline)
- Group C significantly lower than A and B (p=0.001 both)
- A vs B: NOT significantly different — low-speed drilling without irrigation offers no thermal advantage
- Group B: 8 specimens exceeded +10°C threshold at 600rpm without irrigation, 5 at 300rpm
- Larger bur diameter → significantly higher temperature in all groups (p=0.001)
- Deeper drilling (13mm > 8.5mm) → higher temperature even in Group C
- Guided surgery consistently higher temperature than conventional technique
- Under 10°C irrigation: wider diameter did not significantly increase temperature at 8.5mm depth

Conclusions: (1) guided surgery is thermally safe under effective external irrigation; (2) 10°C irrigation is the most effective cooling strategy; (3) low-speed drilling without irrigation does NOT reduce thermal risk and may exceed the +10°C critical threshold; (4) wider and deeper = hotter regardless of technique.

Limitations: ex vivo lacks vascularity/body temperature; no standardized cortical/cancellous thickness; single guide design.
