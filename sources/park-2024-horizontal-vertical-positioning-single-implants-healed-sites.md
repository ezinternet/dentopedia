## Why Ingested

인접치 치은 정점 (gingival zenith) 연결선을 기준으로 임플란트 수평·수직 위치를 수치화한 논문으로, zenith gap sweep에서 임플란트 식립 위치와 출현윤곽(cervical crown form)을 직접 잇는 유일한 문헌이다. 출현각도 (Emergence Angle)가 식립 깊이·협설 위치에서 결정된다는 점에서 [[implants/soft-tissue/misch-2025-abutment-height-emergence-angle-peri-implant-bone-loss]]·[[overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]]의 각도/높이 축에 위치 근거를 더한다.

## Three-line Summary

Clinical cast-based study (Loma Linda) of 58 wax patterns on casts of single implants in healed sites without grafting; 10 raters (5 faculty, 5 graduate students) graded cervical crown form good/fair/poor against horizontal and vertical implant position measured from a wire joining adjacent gingival zeniths.

A horizontal distance of 2.0–3.0 mm (zenith line to implant facial surface) gave good cervical contours, with >3.0 mm or <2.0 mm rated fair/poor; a vertical distance of 3.0–4.0 mm (zenith line to platform) was good, and ≤1.0 mm was poor.

Proposes 2–3 mm horizontal and 3–4 mm vertical guidelines from adjacent zeniths, with positions outside them flagging the need for bone grafting — based on subjective assessment of wax-ups, not tissue outcomes.

수평 거리 (정점선–임플란트 순면) 2.0–3.0 mm에서 양호, >3.0 mm·<2.0 mm는 보통/불량; 수직 거리 (정점선–플랫폼) 3.0–4.0 mm 양호, ≤1.0 mm 불량.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Uses the line connecting adjacent teeth's gingival zeniths as a reproducible 3D reference for implant position in healed sites.
- Proposes numeric horizontal (2.0–3.0 mm) and vertical (3.0–4.0 mm) windows for good cervical crown form.
- Frames out-of-window positions as an indication for bone grafting.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 58 wax patterns on casts of single implants placed in healed sites without bone grafting.
- Raters: 5 dental faculty + 5 graduate students; three-level subjective scale.
- Horizontal measure: facial surface of implant ↔ round metal wire connecting adjacent gingival zeniths.
- Vertical measure: wire ↔ implant platform.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Dimension (from adjacent zenith line) | Good | Fair/poor |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (to implant facial surface) | 2.0–3.0 mm | >3.0 mm or <2.0 mm |
| Vertical (to implant platform) | 3.0–4.0 mm | ≤1.0 mm poor |

## 7. Glossary

- **Cervical crown form**: the shape of the crown/abutment in the transmucosal and cervical region (emergence profile).
- **Zenith line (here)**: a wire connecting the gingival zeniths of the teeth adjacent to the implant site.
