## Three-line Summary

Retrospective nationwide cohort of Korean National Health Insurance Service Health Screening Cohort (NHIS-HEALS) claims, January 2016 to December 2019: 44,220 reimbursed implant cases (all with completed second-stage procedure), 40,502 in private dental practices, 2,629 in group dental practices and 1,089 in hospital-based dental clinics (mean age about 71 years); failure was claim-based (re-implantation for osseointegration failure, or simple/complex removal surgery) and no early-versus-late timing split was reported.

Failure events were 1,042 (private), 69 (group) and 14 (hospital-based), i.e. 14.22, 15.00 and 6.70 per 1,000 person-years; hospital-based clinics had lower risk than private practices (crude HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.28-0.81; IPTW-adjusted HR 0.35, 95% CI 0.30-0.41) and than group practices (crude HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.26-0.81; IPTW HR 0.34, 95% CI 0.20-0.58), while private versus group did not differ (IPTW HR 1.03, 95% CI 0.91-1.15).

Within private practices, quartiles of implant volume showed no difference, but the top 10% and top 5% by volume had higher failure (IPTW HR 1.23, 95% CI 1.09-1.38; 1.38, 95% CI 1.23-1.54); the claims data lack bone, implant-system and operator variables, non-reimbursed implants are excluded, and follow-up is short, so the setting effect is observational and residual confounding by case selection cannot be excluded.

## 세줄요약

한국 국민건강보험공단 건강검진코호트 (National Health Insurance Service Health Screening Cohort, NHIS-HEALS) 청구자료(2016년 1월-2019년 12월)를 이용한 전국 후향적 코호트: 급여 임플란트 44,220건(2차 수술 완료 건), 개인의원 40,502, 공동개원 2,629, 병원급 치과 1,089건(평균 연령 약 71세); 실패는 청구 기반(골유착 실패로 인한 재식립, 단순/복합 제거술)이며 조기-후기 구분은 제시되지 않았다.

실패 사건은 개인의원 1,042, 공동개원 69, 병원급 14건(1,000 인년당 14.22, 15.00, 6.70); 병원급 치과는 개인의원 대비(조위험비 0.48, 95% CI 0.28-0.81; 역확률가중 (inverse probability of treatment weighting, IPTW) 보정 HR 0.35, 95% CI 0.30-0.41), 공동개원 대비(조 HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.26-0.81; IPTW HR 0.34, 95% CI 0.20-0.58) 위험이 낮았고, 개인의원과 공동개원은 차이가 없었다(IPTW HR 1.03, 95% CI 0.91-1.15).

개인의원 내 시술량 사분위는 차이가 없었으나 상위 10%·5% 시술량 의원은 실패 위험이 더 높았다(IPTW HR 1.23, 95% CI 1.09-1.38; 1.38, 95% CI 1.23-1.54); 청구자료라 골 상태·임플란트 시스템·술자 변수가 없고 비급여 임플란트가 제외되며 추적이 짧아, 의료기관 유형 효과는 관찰적 연관이며 잔여 교란을 배제할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Described by the authors as the first analysis of implant failure by dental care setting, using nationwide Korean claims data (44,220 cases).
- Hospital-based clinics had lower claim-defined failure than private practices (IPTW HR 0.35, 95% CI 0.30-0.41) and group practices (IPTW HR 0.34, 95% CI 0.20-0.58); private and group practices did not differ.
- Volume-outcome in private practices: no difference by quartile, but top 10% and top 5% volume practices had higher failure (IPTW HR 1.23 and 1.38).
- Failure is defined by reimbursement claims (re-implantation, simple or complex removal); only overall failure is reported, with no early-versus-late timing.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: population-based retrospective cohort, STROBE; IRB of Youngsan University, consent waived.
- **Data source**: Korean NHIS-HEALS (Health Screening Cohort) claims, implants placed from 1 January 2016, follow-up to implant failure, death or 31 December 2019. Reimbursable implant ICD-10 code K081; procedure codes in Table S1 (not in the retrieved text).
- **Exposure**: private dental practice (single dentist), group dental practice (two or more dentists), hospital-based dental clinic (tertiary, general or university hospital); oriental medicine hospitals and public health centers excluded.
- **Population**: 44,220 cases that completed the second-stage procedure.
- **Outcome (claim-based)**: Definition 1, re-implantation after osseointegration failure (reimbursed implants only); Definition 2, removal surgery, simple (failed osseointegration) or complex (non-mobile fixture needing trephine/removal kit, e.g. fracture or nerve concern), reimbursed and non-reimbursed.
- **Analysis**: incidence per 1,000 person-years, Kaplan-Meier with log-rank, stabilized IPTW from logistic propensity scores (age, sex, income, disability, residence, hypertension, diabetes, dyslipidemia, osteoporosis, CCI, smoking, alcohol, exercise, BMI, hemoglobin, GFR; standardized differences <0.1), Cox models, Schoenfeld check, Bonferroni for multiple comparisons; volume-outcome analysis restricted to private practices.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Claims-based outcome: failure is inferred from reimbursement codes for re-implantation or removal surgery, not from clinical or radiographic examination; reimbursed implants only (non-reimbursed placements excluded), so failure is probably underestimated and may be under-captured differently across settings (billing practice, patients not returning).
- No clinical covariates: alveolar bone height/quality, implant system, length, diameter, surgical technique and operator experience were not available; the authors state surgical difficulty could not be assessed. Hospital-based clinics treated sicker patients (CCI >=3 in 49.0% vs 32.8% private), yet had lower failure, so residual confounding in either direction is possible (case selection, referral of complex cases, who returns for follow-up).
- Short follow-up (cohort entry from 2016, end 2019; mean about 1.8 person-years per case computed from Table 2: 73,299.92 / 40,502 for private practices); the authors note reimbursement is recent in Korea and long-term studies are needed. No early-versus-late failure split and no time-since-placement distribution are reported.
- Only 14 events in hospital-based clinics, so the crude HR is imprecise (95% CI 0.28-0.81); the very narrow IPTW CIs (0.30-0.41) reflect the weighted pseudo-population and should be read cautiously.
- Unit of analysis is cases (placements completing stage 2), described as "patients" in the text; whether one person contributes several cases is not clarified.
- In-paper inconsistency: the Results text reports failure as 14.22%, 15.00% and 6.70% "of cases", but these equal the incidence rates per 1,000 person-years in Table 2; the raw proportions from the stated counts are 1,042/40,502 = 2.57%, 69/2,629 = 2.62% and 14/1,089 = 1.29%. Table 2 rates were used here.
- Health-policy interpretation (price competition, clinician fatigue, corporate practice) is the authors' hypothesis, not tested in the data.
- Future work named by the authors: include non-reimbursed cases, detailed clinical variables, long-term follow-up and prospective designs.

## 7. Glossary

- **NHIS-HEALS (National Health Insurance Service Health Screening Cohort, 건강검진코호트)**: Korean national insurance claims linked to health screening data.
- **IPTW (Inverse Probability of Treatment Weighting, 역확률가중)**: propensity-score weighting to balance covariates across groups.
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: relative event rate in a Cox model.
- **CCI (Charlson Comorbidity Index, 찰슨 동반질환 지수)**: comorbidity burden score.
- **Re-implantation / removal surgery (재식립 / 제거술)**: claim codes used as failure definitions.
