## Why Ingested

Existing abutment-screw retightening evidence (5-10 min or immediate retightening restores preload lost to the settling effect) was derived almost entirely on titanium implants without torsional loading; this bench study tests whether that advice holds for a two-piece zirconia implant versus a titanium implant under cyclic torsional loading, which qualifies the retightening-timing conclusion in → [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/varvara-2020-retightening-preload-loss-abutment-screws]].

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (n = 36; titanium Straumann Standard Plus vs zirconia Straumann PURE Ceramic, both with titanium-alloy base and abutment screw; three tightening protocols, n = 6 per cell) subjecting abutment screws tightened to 35 Ncm to 100,000 cycles of cyclic torsional loading at 10% of tightening torque.

Post-loading removal torque (RT-post) was lowest with retightening after 5 min (titanium 27.9 vs 28.9 immediate vs 30.2 Ncm no retightening; zirconia 23.1 vs 24.9 vs 25.4 Ncm), and zirconia implants had lower RT-post than titanium under all protocols (P < 0.05 to P < 0.001), while torque loss percentage (about 15-19%) did not differ between groups.

Under torsional loading retightening did not improve torque maintenance and titanium implant systems retained torque better than zirconia systems; interpretation is limited by differing implant-system designs, a single system pair, n = 6 per group, and a 100,000-cycle (approximately one month) low-load model.

## 세줄요약

체외 연구(n = 36; 티타늄 임플란트 vs 지르코니아 임플란트(Y-TZP), 둘 다 티타늄 합금 베이스·지대주 나사; 조임 프로토콜 3종, 조건당 n = 6)로, 35 Ncm로 조인 지대주 나사에 조임 토크의 10%로 100,000회 반복 비틀림 하중(cyclic torsional loading)을 가했다.

하중 후 제거 토크(RT-post)는 5분 후 재조임군에서 가장 낮았고(티타늄 27.9 vs 즉시 재조임 28.9 vs 재조임 없음 30.2 Ncm; 지르코니아 23.1 vs 24.9 vs 25.4 Ncm), 모든 프로토콜에서 지르코니아가 티타늄보다 낮았으나(P < 0.05~0.001), 토크 손실률(약 15~19%)은 군간 차이가 없었다.

비틀림 하중에서는 재조임이 토크 유지를 개선하지 못했고 티타늄 시스템이 지르코니아 시스템보다 토크를 더 잘 유지했다. 다만 임플란트 시스템 설계 차이, 단일 시스템 쌍, 군당 n = 6, 약 1개월 상당의 저하중 모델이라는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Tests retightening (a recommendation derived mainly from titanium implants and bending-load studies) under cyclic torsional loading in both titanium and zirconia two-piece implants.
- Shows retightening after 5 min gave the lowest post-loading removal torque in both materials; immediate retightening vs no retightening differed only in the titanium group.
- Shows zirconia implants had lower removal torque than titanium before and after loading, with no difference in percentage torque loss.
- Adds SEM and micro-CT (RMS) characterisation of screw wear/deformation: wear on screw heads and threads in all groups; RMS dimensional change smaller in the zirconia group.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro (bench) study, 2 implant systems x 3 tightening protocols.
- **Specimens**: Straumann Standard Plus (commercially pure titanium Grade 4, 4.1 x 10 mm) vs Straumann PURE Ceramic (Y-TZP, 4.1 x 10 mm); both with titanium-aluminum-niobium (TAN) base and abutment screw; milled multilayered zirconia hexagonal-prism superstructure (7 mm) cemented to the base with universal resin cement.
- **Protocols (all to 35 Ncm, digital torque meter)**: A = retightening after 5 min; B = immediate retightening; C = no retightening. n = 6 per group, 36 specimens (a priori power analysis, Cohen's f = 0.723, alpha 0.05, power 0.80).
- **Loading**: custom torsional fatigue machine, load at 10% of specified tightening torque, 10 deg/min, 100,000 reciprocating cycles (set per ISO/TS 13498 and ISO/TR 18130; authors equate to roughly one month of function).
- **Outcomes**: removal torque before (RT-pre; groups A and B only) and after loading (RT-post); torque loss % = (RT-pre - RT-post)/RT-pre x 100; SEM (x50, x150, x300); micro-CT STL superimposition RMS (20.94 um resolution).
- **Statistics**: Welch's t-test with Bonferroni for RT-post; ANOVA/Tukey for RT-pre; Kruskal-Wallis for TL; Mann-Whitney U for RMS (protocol groups pooled for material comparison).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Group | RT-pre (Ncm) | RT-post (Ncm) | TL (%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| TA (Ti, 5 min) | 34.2 +/- 1.0 | 27.9 +/- 0.6 | 18.3 +/- 3.3 |
| TB (Ti, immediate) | 34.9 +/- 0.9 | 28.9 +/- 0.3 | 17.1 +/- 2.3 |
| TC (Ti, none) | - | 30.2 +/- 1.1 | - |
| ZA (Zr, 5 min) | 28.7 +/- 1.1 | 23.1 +/- 1.3 | 19.3 +/- 5.9 |
| ZB (Zr, immediate) | 29.6 +/- 1.8 | 24.9 +/- 1.9 | 15.5 +/- 8.0 |
| ZC (Zr, none) | - | 25.4 +/- 0.5 | - |

- Titanium RT-post: TA < TB and TA < TC (both P < 0.001); TB < TC (P < 0.05).
- Zirconia RT-post: ZA < ZB (P < 0.05) and ZA < ZC (P < 0.001); ZB vs ZC not different.
- Material: zirconia < titanium for every protocol (TA-ZA P < 0.001; TB-ZB P < 0.05; TC-ZC P < 0.001). RT-pre: ZA and ZB lower than TA and TB (P < 0.001); RT-pre did not differ with retightening timing within a material.
- TL: no significant differences between groups; zirconia SD larger (ZB 8.0) than titanium.
- SEM: fine wear marks on screw heads and threads in all groups; no distinct change at the implant body-base interface.
- Micro-CT RMS: 6.3 +/- 6.3 um (titanium) vs 6.1 +/- 2.4 um (zirconia), zirconia significantly smaller (P < 0.05); authors judge a few-micrometre difference clinically negligible.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Titanium and zirconia groups differ in implant-body design, interface and screw/base configuration in addition to implant-body material, so findings reflect the whole clinical system, not intrinsic material properties alone (authors' own caveat).
- Single implant system pair (Straumann), n = 6 per cell, 100,000 cycles at a low load (10% of tightening torque), no thermocycling or wet environment, one operator; the study has no separate limitations section.
- The authors propose future work on tightening protocols that consider loading direction and material properties.

## 6. Related Work
- Sun et al. and Katsuta et al. (cited by authors): source of the tightening-protocol design and the torque-loss formula; earlier reports (refs 31-33) found retightening at 5-10 min or immediately restores screw stability, a result this study did not reproduce under torsion.
- [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/varvara-2020-retightening-preload-loss-abutment-screws]]: retightening-timing study on internal vs external hexagon titanium implants (no cyclic torsion).

## 7. Glossary
- **RT-pre / RT-post**: removal torque before / after cyclic torsional loading.
- **TL**: torque loss percentage between RT-pre and RT-post.
- **Settling effect**: embedment of microscopic surface irregularities under load that reduces preload.
- **Y-TZP**: yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal.
- **RMS**: root mean square of 3D deviation between pre- and post-test micro-CT surfaces.
