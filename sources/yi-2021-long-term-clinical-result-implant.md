## Why Ingested

The only long-term cohort in the wiki's domain that answers what to do *after* an implant has already invaded or touched an adjacent root — the intraoperative moment where most clinicians extract reflexively — and it argues against that reflex → [[implants/joshi-2025-implant-tooth-proximity-sr-ma]] and [[implants/srikanth-2025-treatment-planning-single-tooth-implant]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (Scientific Reports 2021;11:7913, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital; 32 implants with adjacent-root injury in 28 patients, mean follow-up 122.7 months) classifying injury as direct root invasion (group I), root surface contact (group II) or <1 mm proximity (group III).

90.6% of the injured teeth remained functional: 7 of 27 untreated teeth needed root canal treatment (3 later extracted), 19 of 27 (70.4%) stayed vital and asymptomatic, and 5 of 5 previously root-treated teeth functioned without change; implant survival was 96.9% with a single osseointegration failure, and ISQ rose significantly from 64.1 to 73.9 (P = 0.007).

Even direct invasion does not justify immediate extraction of either the tooth or the implant — both can be monitored, with intervention reserved for symptomatic or radiographically deteriorating teeth.

## 세줄요약

후향 코호트 (Scientific Reports 2021;11:7913, 분당서울대; 인접치 손상 임플란트 32개/환자 28명, 평균 추적 122.7개월): 손상 유형을 직접 치근 침범 (group I)·치근 접촉 (group II)·1 mm 미만 근접 (group III)으로 분류.

손상 치아의 90.6%가 기능 유지 — 미치료 27개 중 7개만 근관치료 (Root Canal Treatment, RCT) 필요(3개 추후 발치), 19/27 (70.4%)은 생활력 유지·무증상, 기존 근관치료 5개는 변화 없음; 임플란트 생존율 96.9% (골유착 실패 1개), 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 64.1 → 73.9로 유의 증가 (P=0.007).

직접 침범이어도 치아·임플란트를 즉시 발치할 필요가 없고, 증상·방사선 변화가 있을 때만 개입하는 감시 전략이 타당함.

- **Authors:** Yang-Jin Yi, In-Woo Park, Jeong-Kui Ku, Deuk-Won Jo, Jung-Suk Han, Young-Kyun Kim
- **Journal:** Scientific Reports 2021;11:7913
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41598-021-87062-9
- **Institutions:** Seoul National University Bundang Hospital; Seoul National University School of Dentistry; Gangneung-Wonju National University
- **Study type:** retrospective (cohort of iatrogenic adjacent-tooth injuries)

## Key Contributions
- Provides the only sizeable long-term series (32 teeth, mean 122.7 months, minimum 86) on implant-induced adjacent-root injury, a complication otherwise documented only as 1-3 patient case reports.
- Establishes a usable severity classification — invasion / contact / <1 mm proximity — and shows complication frequency tracks it (group I clearly worse).
- Quantifies outcomes conservatively: 90.6% of injured teeth functional, 19 of 27 untreated injured teeth (70.4%) remaining vital and asymptomatic, 7 requiring RCT and 3 of those extracted after 39 months, 7 years and 14 years.
- Shows previously root-treated adjacent teeth tolerated invasion/contact without deterioration (5 of 5), implying a completed endodontic seal is protective.
- Demonstrates the implant itself also survives: 96.9% survival, one osseointegration failure, and a significant ISQ increase (64.1 → 73.9, P = 0.007) even in invaded cases.

## Methodology
Retrospective review of patients treated at one institution who received implants and sustained adjacent-tooth injury, identified radiographically and by intraoral examination. Injury graded as group I (direct invasion of the root), group II (root surface contact) or group III (<1 mm distance from the root). Clinical and radiographic status (vitality testing, periapical lesions, symptoms, need for root canal treatment, extraction) reviewed periodically; ISQ measured with Osstell where records existed (14 of 32 implants). Paired t-tests for ISQ change, chi-square for tooth complications (α = 0.05).

## Key Results

**Cohort.** 32 implants and adjacent teeth in 28 patients, mean follow-up 122.7 ± 31.7 months (minimum 86 months). Most frequent injury sites: maxillary canines and mandibular second premolars. 4 injured teeth had root curvature; 15 of 32 had unnatural coronal contours (crowns or lost structure) at placement.

**Tooth outcomes**

| Group | Teeth | New RCT | Extraction | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I (invasion) | 10 | 4 (+1 intentional replantation) | 2 | Worst group; all events concentrated here |
| II (contact) | 10 | 1 | 0 | Mostly uneventful |
| III (<1 mm) | 12 | 1 | 1 | One pre-existing apical radiolucency stayed asymptomatic |

- 5 teeth had RCT prior to implant placement: all 5 remained functional without unusual change regardless of invasion depth.
- Of 27 untreated injured teeth, 7 required RCT for symptoms/apical lesion; 3 of those were later extracted (39 months, 7 years, 14 years); 1 was intentionally replanted and later showed external root resorption; 19 of 27 (70.4%) stayed vital with no abnormal response.
- Overall, 90.6% of injured teeth remained functional.

**Implant outcomes.** Survival 96.9% (one osseointegration failure, in group I). ISQ rose from 64.1 ± 12.4 (primary) to 73.9 ± 7.6 (secondary), P = 0.007, paired t-test (n = 14 with complete records).

## Limitations
Retrospective, single centre, and small — 32 teeth spread across three groups of 10/10/12, so group-level comparisons are descriptive rather than powered. Injury severity was classified radiographically after the fact, so the true three-dimensional relationship at placement is uncertain (CBCT was not used). ISQ data existed for only 14 of 32 implants. Adjacent-tooth vitality was assessed clinically without a standardized protocol in all cases, and the decision to perform RCT appears clinician-triggered by symptoms rather than protocolized. There is no untreated-injury comparison group, so the 70.4% vitality figure describes a monitored cohort rather than a natural history. Follow-up spans different implant systems and eras.

## Glossary
- **Group I/II/III:** direct root invasion / root surface contact / <1 mm proximity.
- **RCT:** Root Canal Treatment.
- **ISQ:** Implant Stability Quotient (Osstell RFA).
- **Intentional replantation:** deliberate extraction, extraoral endodontic treatment, and replantation of the tooth.
- **Proximity:** radiographic distance between implant surface and adjacent root <1 mm without contact.
