## Why Ingested

임플란트로 인한 하치조신경(Inferior Alveolar Nerve, IAN) 손상 후 조기 덱사메타손(dexamethasone) 투여가 신경병증성 통증(neuropathic pain)을 예방할 수 있는지를 직접 모델링한 유일한 동물 연구. 기존 [[drug/analgesics]] 카테고리의 코르티코스테로이드 사용 근거를 신경 손상 방지 맥락에서 보강하며, 타이밍의 중요성(early vs. established allodynia)을 실험적으로 뒷받침한다.

## Three-line Summary

Rat model (n=not specified in abstract) of mal-positioned dental implant injuring the inferior alveolar nerve to study dexamethasone analgesic effects on trigeminal neuropathic pain.

Daily dexamethasone (25 or 50 mg/kg i.p.) produced prolonged anti-allodynic effects when given early, but failed to reduce mechanical allodynia once it was already established.

Early corticosteroid intervention is critical after IAN injury; delayed treatment offers no benefit once neuropathic sensitization is established.

## 세줄요약

하치조신경(Inferior Alveolar Nerve, IAN)을 손상시키는 악위치 임플란트 쥐 모델에서 덱사메타손(dexamethasone)의 진통 효과를 평가한 동물 연구.

덱사메타손 25 또는 50 mg/kg 매일 복강 투여 시 조기 투여에서는 지속적 항이질통(anti-allodynic) 효과를 보였으나, 이미 확립된 기계적 이질통(mechanical allodynia)에는 효과 없음.

신경 손상 후 코르티코스테로이드(corticosteroid) 조기 투여가 필수적이며, 신경병증성 감작(neuropathic sensitization)이 확립된 후에는 효과 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First animal model directly simulating implant-induced IAN injury (mal-positioned miniature implant replacing left mandibular 2nd molar)
- Demonstrated bilateral mechanical allodynia, cold and thermal hypersensitivity following IAN injury — evidence of central sensitization / mirror-image pain
- Established a critical timing threshold: dexamethasone prevents but cannot reverse established neuropathic allodynia
- Dose-response demonstrated at 25 and 50 mg/kg i.p. (both effective when given early)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Animal model**: Rat; left mandibular 2nd molar extraction followed by miniature dental implant placement to injure IAN
- **Nociceptive measures**: Air-puff threshold (allodynia), mechanical hyperalgesia, cold and thermal hypersensitivity — assessed ipsilateral and contralateral
- **Treatment**: Dexamethasone 25 mg/kg or 50 mg/kg i.p. daily
- **Protocol arms**: Early treatment (before allodynia established) vs. delayed treatment (after allodynia established)
- **Outcome**: Anti-allodynic effect quantified over designated post-surgical days

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Condition | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Mal-positioned implant | Significant bilateral decrease in air-puff threshold (allodynia ipsi + contra) |
| Early dexamethasone (25 mg/kg) | Prolonged anti-allodynic effect |
| Early dexamethasone (50 mg/kg) | Prolonged anti-allodynic effect |
| Delayed dexamethasone | Failed to reduce established mechanical allodynia |
| Bilateral hypersensitivity | Mechanical hyperalgesia + cold + thermal — all observed bilaterally |

- Bilateral (mirror-image) pain suggests central sensitization, not purely peripheral nerve compression
- Contralateral hypersensitivity is a key novel finding — clinically important for understanding diffuse post-implant pain

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only available (full text paywalled); specific n, exact timepoints, and statistical methods not extractable
- Animal model: rat IAN anatomy differs from human; dose extrapolation not direct
- Mechanism of dexamethasone action not fully elucidated (glucocorticoid receptor / NF-κB / cytokine suppression implied by reference list)
- Does not address long-term outcomes or dose-tapering protocols
- Clinical translation requires human studies on timing of corticosteroid administration post nerve injury

## 7. Glossary

- **이질통 (Allodynia)**: Pain from a stimulus that would not normally provoke pain
- **기계적 과통각 (Mechanical hyperalgesia)**: Exaggerated pain response to mechanical stimuli
- **거울상 통증 (Mirror-image pain)**: Contralateral hypersensitivity following unilateral nerve injury, indicating central sensitization
- **하치조신경 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve, IAN)**: Main sensory nerve of the mandible; the dental implant model here injures it directly
- **중추 감작 (Central sensitization)**: Amplification of neural signaling in the CNS causing hypersensitivity
