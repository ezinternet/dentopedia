## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis of 11 clinical human studies (1,059 identified; search March 2014, no time/language restrictions) comparing immediate nonfunctional loading (INFL) versus immediate functional loading (IFL) on implant failure rates, postoperative infection, and marginal bone loss.

Procedure type did not significantly affect failure risk (RR 0.87, 95% CI 0.44–1.75; P=0.70) or marginal bone loss (MD 0.01 mm, 95% CI −0.04–0.06; P=0.74); postoperative infection could not be pooled for lack of data.

With 7 of 11 studies at high risk of bias and a wide confidence interval, the pooled null result is imprecise — no proven benefit from withholding occlusal contact during immediate loading.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (Systematic Review + Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) — 임상인간 연구 11편 (확인 1,059편, 2014년 3월 검색)을 대상으로 즉시비기능성로딩 (Immediate Nonfunctional Loading, INFL) 대 즉시기능성로딩 (Immediate Functional Loading, IFL)의 임플란트 실패율·수술후감염·변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 비교.

실패율 위험비 (Risk Ratio, RR) 0.87 (95% CI 0.44–1.75, P=0.70)로 유의한 차이 없음, MBL 평균차 (Mean Difference, MD) 0.01 mm (95% CI −0.04–0.06, P=0.74)로 차이 없음; 수술후감염은 데이터 부족으로 메타분석 불가.

11편 중 7편이 편향위험 高 + 넓은 CI로 추정치 불확실 — 즉시로딩 기간 중 교합접촉 제거의 이점은 입증되지 않음 (초록 전용, 전문 미확보).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pooled quantitative test of the INFL vs IFL question: implant failure did not differ significantly (RR 0.87, 95% CI 0.44–1.75, P=0.70) — the first meta-analytic anchor in the wiki for the functional/nonfunctional immediate-loading distinction that [[wiki/implants/loading-protocol/devi-2026-immediate-vs-delayed-implant-loading]] raises as a key decision nuance.
- Marginal bone loss likewise unchanged between loading modes (MD 0.01 mm, 95% CI −0.04–0.06, P=0.74).
- Documents an evidence gap: postoperative infection could not be analyzed at all because included studies did not report the needed data.
- Appraises included-study quality explicitly: 7 of 11 studies at high risk of bias, only 4 at low risk.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA-style electronic search; clinical human studies, randomized or not)
- **Databases**: Electronic search in March 2014 without time or language restrictions (specific databases not named in the abstract)
- **n**: 1,059 studies identified → 11 included (7 high risk of bias, 4 low risk of bias)
- **Outcomes**: implant failure rates and postoperative infection (pooled as risk ratio, RR); marginal bone loss (pooled as mean difference, MD, in mm)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Studies identified / included | 1,059 identified; 11 included (7 high ROB, 4 low ROB) |
| Implant failure, INFL vs IFL | RR 0.87 (95% CI 0.44–1.75), P=0.70 — not significant |
| Marginal bone loss | MD 0.01 mm (95% CI −0.04–0.06), P=0.74 — not significant |
| Postoperative infection | Not analyzed — insufficient data in included studies |
| Search window | March 2014, no time or language restrictions |
| Authors' interpretation | Wide CI demonstrates uncertainty about the effect size; occlusal-loading differences between INFL and IFL "might not affect" survival |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Abstract-only ingest** — full text not retrieved; heterogeneity statistics (I²), per-study estimates, publication-bias tests, and the full search-strategy detail could not be checked.
- Imprecision: the failure RR confidence interval (0.44–1.75) spans a halving to a near-doubling of risk — the null result does not establish equivalence.
- Evidence base is weak: 7 of 11 included studies at high risk of bias; postoperative infection remains unquantifiable until loading studies report it.

## 6. Related Work
- chrcanovic-2016: same first author's retrospective cohort on early implant failure risk factors — smoking/antidepressants, not loading mode.
- esposito-2013 / benic-2014: sibling loading-protocol SRs/Cochrane on the timing axis (immediate vs early vs delayed); this paper isolates the functional vs nonfunctional contrast *within* immediate loading.
- ko-2021: RCT where immediate loading raised failure risk in guided surgery — function-vs-timing disambiguation: that signal concerned timing, not whether the implant was in occlusion.
- chang-2013 / sadowsky-2019 / mojaver-2025: occlusal-overload literature (excessive force axis); this paper addresses presence/absence of function at immediate loading, a distinct exposure.

## 7. Glossary
- **INFL**: Immediate Nonfunctional Loading — implant loaded immediately after placement but kept out of occlusal contact during healing.
- **IFL**: Immediate Functional Loading — implant placed and put into occlusal function immediately.
- **RR**: Risk Ratio — pooled relative risk of an event (implant failure) between groups.
- **MD**: Mean Difference — pooled absolute difference in a continuous outcome (marginal bone loss, mm).
- **ROB**: Risk of Bias — study-level quality appraisal (high vs low).
