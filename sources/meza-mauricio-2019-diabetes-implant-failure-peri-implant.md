## Why Ingested

We already hold two individual SR/SR+MA on diabetes and implants — [[drug/systemic-disease/wagner-2022-diabetes-mellitus-dental-implants-sr]] (controlled DM ≈ healthy, HbA1c <8% threshold) and [[drug/systemic-disease/al-ansari-2022-diabetes-mellitus-dental-implants-sr-ma]] (77.7% higher failure odds overall, but maxilla-localized, MBL widens over time) — but no umbrella-level (review-of-reviews) synthesis with a formal methodological-quality appraisal (AMSTAR 2) of the underlying SR evidence base. This 2019 umbrella review (12 SRs) fills that gap: it independently corroborates the "no significant DM vs non-DM difference in implant failure meta-analyses" finding while flagging that 50% of the constituent reviews were AMSTAR-2 "critically low" quality — a caution that pre-dates and contextualizes both Wagner (2022) and Al-Ansari (2022), and tempers how much weight the wiki's existing diabetes-implant conclusions should carry. Note: a sibling ingest (dambrosio-2023-systemic-diseases-medications-influence) covers systemic disease and implants more broadly; that page did not exist yet at ingest time of this paper, so no wikilink to it is set here, but the topical overlap (systemic disease as an implant-failure modifier) should be cross-checked once that page lands.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (review of reviews) of 12 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (MEDLINE/EMBASE/JBI/Cochrane/PROSPERO, 1990–May 2018) on diabetes mellitus (DM) and dental implant complications, AMSTAR-2 appraised.

Implant survival ranged 83.5–100% and failure 0–14.3% in DM patients; all three meta-analyses on "implant failure" found no statistically significant DM vs non-DM difference, while peri-implantitis risk appeared increased in DM; 50% of included reviews were AMSTAR-2 "critically low" quality, 25% "low", 25% "moderate".

DM as a whole does not appear to significantly raise implant failure risk in the pooled SR evidence, but the true effect of hyperglycemia specifically remains uncertain because most included studies treated DM as a single category rather than stratifying by glycemic control — and the underlying review quality is largely weak.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (at the time) umbrella review specifically aggregating systematic reviews/meta-analyses on DM and implant complications, rather than individual primary studies — a review-of-reviews layer above the individual SRs already held in this wiki.
- Applies AMSTAR 2 formally to grade the methodological quality of all 12 included SRs, exposing that the evidence base underpinning "DM does not significantly raise implant failure" claims is largely weak (50% critically low, 25% low, only 25% moderate; no reviews rated high).
- Consolidates disparate implant survival/failure ranges (83.5–100% survival; 0–14.3% failure) across the 12 SRs into a single reference range for DM patients.
- Separately flags peri-implant disease (peri-implantitis) risk as apparently elevated in DM, distinct from the implant-failure question — an important distinction because most individual meta-analyses conflate different endpoints.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews).
- **Search**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, JBI Database of Systematic Reviews and Implementation Reports, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, PROSPERO register; 1990 through May 2018; MeSH terms plus free-text keywords.
- **Eligibility**: Systematic reviews and meta-analyses investigating associations between DM and implant complications — failure, survival, bone loss, peri-implant diseases, and post-surgical infection.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR 2 (Assessment of Multiple Systematic Reviews Tool, version 2) applied to each of the 12 included reviews, yielding a 4-tier quality classification (high/moderate/low/critically low).
- Abstract does not specify the number of primary studies or patients pooled within the 12 SRs (that level of detail would require full-text extraction, not available in this abstract-only ingest).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **12 systematic reviews** included.
- **Implant survival**: 83.5%–100% range across reviews in DM subjects.
- **Implant failure**: 0%–14.3% range across reviews in DM subjects.
- **Meta-analytic pooling for "implant failure"**: 3 meta-analyses performed this specific pooling; none found a statistically significant difference between diabetic and non-diabetic subjects.
- **Peri-implant disease risk**: apparently increased in DM/hyperglycemia, per the reviews assessed — though the abstract notes this rests on a limited number of systematic reviews.
- **AMSTAR 2 quality distribution**: critically low 50%, low 25%, moderate 25% (0% rated high).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest (full_text: false) — no access to the full methodology detail, PRISMA flow, or the identity/overlap of the 12 constituent reviews; some may overlap in primary-study coverage with reviews already held individually in this wiki (e.g., Wagner 2022, Al-Ansari 2022 — both published after this umbrella review's May 2018 search cutoff, so they are NOT among the 12 included reviews, but represent later evidence this umbrella review could not incorporate).
- Most primary evidence treated DM as a single undifferentiated category (not stratified by glycemic control/HbA1c, DM type, or duration), so the umbrella review itself cannot resolve whether hyperglycemia specifically — as opposed to a DM diagnosis broadly — drives any failure or peri-implantitis signal.
- Half of the underlying SRs were AMSTAR-2 "critically low" quality, meaning the reliability of the pooled conclusions (especially "no significant difference in failure") should be treated cautiously rather than as definitive reassurance.
- Search cutoff of May 2018 means the review does not capture DM-implant evidence published after that date, including the wiki's own more recent, larger, and more granular Wagner (2022) and Al-Ansari (2022) SR/SR+MA.
- No web search was performed to retrieve the SciELO full text per Rule #1; if deeper methodological detail (e.g., list of the 12 constituent reviews, exact peri-implantitis effect sizes) becomes clinically necessary, obtain the PDF directly and re-ingest as a full-text update.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: a systematic review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on the same or related questions, rather than from primary studies directly.
- **AMSTAR 2**: "A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews," version 2 — a 16-item critical appraisal instrument rating the methodological quality of systematic reviews as high, moderate, low, or critically low.
- **Peri-implant disease / peri-implantitis**: inflammatory conditions affecting the tissues around a dental implant; peri-implantitis specifically involves progressive peri-implant bone loss, distinct from implant failure (loss of the implant itself).
- **Hyperglycemia**: elevated blood glucose, the proximate physiological abnormality in diabetes mellitus hypothesized to impair wound healing and osseointegration.
