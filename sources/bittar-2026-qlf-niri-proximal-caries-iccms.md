## Why Ingested

Ingested to address the clinical question of how early proximal caries is detectable on radiographs. Directly tests QLF (QrayPen C) and NIRI (iTero Element 5D) as non-ionizing adjuncts to bitewing radiography for proximal caries at predefined ICCMS radiographic depth thresholds — the first such head-to-head comparison in vivo.

## Three-line Summary

QLF and NIRI were compared against ICCMS bitewing radiography as the reference standard across 297 posterior proximal sites in 66 adults. Both methods showed only moderate AUC (~0.73) for any caries detection (enamel lesions) but high accuracy for dentin-level thresholds (AUC 0.90–0.98). NIRI was statistically superior to QLF at the deeper-dentin threshold (DeLong p=0.036); the two methods showed substantial agreement overall (κ=0.707).

## 세줄요약

양광유발형광 (Quantitative Light-Induced Fluorescence, QLF)과 근적외선영상 (Near-Infrared Imaging, NIRI)을 국제우식분류관리시스템 (International Caries Classification and Management System, ICCMS) 방사선 기준과 비교한 297개 인접면 부위 진단정확도 연구. 초기 법랑질 병소에서는 두 방법 모두 AUC 약 0.73의 보통 수준 정확도를 보인 반면, 상아질 침범 임계값에서는 AUC 0.90–0.98의 높은 정확도를 기록하였다. 더 깊은 상아질 임계값에서는 NIRI가 QLF보다 통계적으로 유의하게 우수하였으며(DeLong p=0.036), 두 방법의 전반적 일치도는 κ=0.707로 상당한 수준이었다.

## Key Contributions

1. First in vivo head-to-head comparison of QLF and NIRI against bitewing radiography using ICCMS-defined thresholds across three clinically meaningful depth stages.
2. Both modalities demonstrate high diagnostic accuracy at dentin-level thresholds (AUC 0.90–0.98), confirming their utility for detection beyond enamel involvement.
3. At the deeper-dentin threshold, NIRI is statistically superior (AUC 0.978 vs 0.961, DeLong p=0.036) — the only threshold where a significant difference was found.
4. QLF exhibits higher specificity for early enamel lesions (88.1% vs 78.2%), while NIRI shows higher sensitivity for deeper dentin (83.3% vs 72.2%).
5. Explicitly flags that bitewing radiography under-detects enamel lesions, making it an imperfect reference standard for the any-caries threshold.
6. Substantial inter-method agreement (Spearman ρ=0.735, κ=0.707) supports clinical interchangeability at most thresholds.

## Methodology

**Population**: 66 adults (mean age 28.1y, ASA I–II). One quadrant per participant; 297 posterior proximal sites analyzed.

**Index tests**:
- QrayPen C (QLF): QS score 0–3 (AIOBIO, Seoul, Korea)
- iTero Element 5D NIRI: NIRI score 0–3 (Align Technology)

**Reference standard**: ICCMS bitewing radiography, R0–R6 categories collapsed to:
- R0 = sound
- R1–R2 = enamel caries
- R3 = outer dentin
- R4–R6 = deeper dentin

**Three pre-specified thresholds**:
1. Any caries: R0 vs R1–R6
2. Dentin involvement: R0–R2 vs R3–R6
3. Radiographically deeper dentin: R0–R3 vs R4–R6

**Site distribution (reference standard)**:
| ICCMS Category | n | % |
|---|---|---|
| Sound (R0) | 202 | 68.0% |
| Enamel (R1–R2) | 62 | 20.9% |
| Outer dentin (R3) | 15 | 5.1% |
| Deeper dentin (R4–R6) | 18 | 6.1% |

**Statistical methods**: ROC analysis, DeLong method for AUC comparison, Spearman correlation, linear-weighted kappa, McNemar test for sensitivity/specificity comparison.

## Key Results

| Threshold | Method | AUC | Sensitivity | Specificity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Any caries | QLF (QS ≥1) | 0.729 | 54.7% | 88.1% |
| Any caries | NIRI (≥1) | 0.727 | 61.1% | 78.2% |
| Dentin involvement | QLF (QS ≥2) | 0.909 | 81.8% | 95.5% |
| Dentin involvement | NIRI (≥2) | 0.896 | 81.8% | 93.9% |
| Deeper dentin | QLF (QS =3) | 0.961 | 72.2% | 96.4% |
| Deeper dentin | NIRI (=3) | 0.978 | 83.3% | 97.1% |

**AUC comparisons (DeLong method)**:
- Any caries: p=0.902 (no significant difference)
- Dentin involvement: p=0.605 (no significant difference)
- Deeper dentin: p=0.036 (NIRI statistically superior)

**Inter-method agreement**: Spearman ρ=0.735 (p<0.001); linear-weighted κ=0.707 (substantial agreement)

## Limitations

- Single-center study; external generalizability uncertain
- Small n for deeper dentin category (n=18), limiting precision of estimates at that threshold
- Bitewing radiography is an imperfect reference standard — explicitly acknowledged to under-detect enamel lesions
- No histologic validation
- Site-level analysis does not account for within-participant clustering (potential underestimation of confidence intervals)
- iTero Element 5D NIRI cannot detect caries progression beyond the dentinoenamel junction (DEJ) per manufacturer's user manual

## Glossary

- **QLF (Quantitative Light-Induced Fluorescence)**: Optical method using violet-blue light to detect demineralization via reduced fluorescence in carious tissue
- **NIRI (Near-Infrared Imaging)**: Imaging modality using 780–1300 nm light; enamel is transparent at these wavelengths, enabling transillumination of proximal surfaces
- **ICCMS**: International Caries Classification and Management System; radiographic staging R0–R6
- **QrayPen C (QS score)**: 0=no lesion, 1=minor, 2=moderate, 3=extensive fluorescence loss
- **iTero Element 5D**: Intraoral scanner with integrated NIRI channel
- **DeLong method**: Non-parametric test for comparing paired AUC values from the same sample
- **DEJ**: Dentinoenamel junction — the boundary NIRI cannot reliably image beyond
