## Why Ingested

아관긴장증 (Trismus) 전용 리뷰 페이지가 위키에 없던 상태에서 최초의 종합 분류·진단 알고리즘 문헌으로 추가. 신경계/비신경계 × 구조적/비구조적 4분류 체계와 단계별 진단 알고리즘, 레드플래그 기반 응급 판별법 제시. 관련 기존 위키 페이지: [[tmj/saini-2024-botulinum-toxin-tmd-sr-ma]], [[tmj/zhu-2020-btx-painful-tmd-sr-ma]], [[tmj/kapos-2020-temporomandibular-disorders-aetiology-diagnosis-review]].

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review proposing a novel 4-quadrant etiological classification (neurological vs. non-neurological × structural vs. non-structural) for trismus, combined with a red-flag-prioritised stepwise diagnostic and management algorithm. Trismus is defined as interincisal distance < 35 mm; etiology spans CNS/PNS lesions, tetanus, TMJ disorders, orofacial infections, post-surgical and post-radiation sequelae, and metabolic disturbances. Treatment follows the underlying cause: NSAIDs/muscle relaxants for acute uncomplicated cases, botulinum toxin type A (BoNT-A) or baclofen for neurological or spastic variants, physical therapy universally, and coronoidectomy/myotomy for refractory structural restriction.

## 세줄요약

아관긴장증 (Trismus) 원인을 신경계/비신경계 × 구조적/비구조적 4분면으로 분류하는 새로운 체계를 제시한 서술적 리뷰. 정상 개구량은 40–60 mm이며 35 mm 미만을 아관긴장으로 정의; 기도 타협·심경부 감염·파상풍·두개내 병변은 즉각 응급 평가가 필요한 레드플래그. 치료는 원인별로 NSAIDs/근이완제(급성 단순형) → 보툴리눔독소A형 (Botulinum Toxin Type A, BoNT-A)/바클로펜(신경계형) → 물리치료(전 케이스 기반) → 관상돌기절제술 (Coronoidectomy)/근절개술(불응성 구조적 제한) 순으로 단계화.

## Key Contributions

1. Novel 4-quadrant etiological classification for trismus (neurological/non-neurological × structural/non-structural) — first such systematic taxonomy in the literature
2. Red-flag-first stepwise diagnostic algorithm distinguishing emergencies (airway, CNS infection, tetanus, intracranial lesion, deep neck space infection) from routine workup
3. Comprehensive treatment hierarchy mapping etiology to intervention, including BoNT-A evidence review and coronoidectomy indications
4. Synthesis of instrumental tools: EMG, trigeminal reflex, jaw tracking, FEES, USS for masseter thickness

## Methodology

- Narrative review (no quantitative synthesis / meta-analysis)
- Authors from neurology (Sapienza University, Rome) and oral/maxillofacial surgery backgrounds (multi-disciplinary)
- Covers neurological, oral-maxillofacial, oncologic, infectious, metabolic, pharmacological, autoimmune, and congenital etiologies
- No PROSPERO registration / PRISMA; expert opinion and case series drive most treatment recommendations

## Key Results

### Diagnostic Threshold

- **Normal interincisal distance**: 40–60 mm
- **Trismus definition**: < 35 mm interincisal distance

### Etiological Classification (Table 1)

| Axis | Structural | Non-structural |
|---|---|---|
| **Neurological — CNS** | TBI/DAI, brain tumours, stroke, MS, CNS infections | Parkinson's/PSP/CBD, status epilepticus, oromandibular dystonia, drug-induced (phenothiazines, metoclopramide, fluoxetine, tricyclics), Wilson's disease, uremic encephalopathy, hemimasticatory spasm, functional disorder, catatonia |
| **Neurological — PNS** | Trigeminal nerve lesions, tumours of masticator space | Tetanus (tetanospasmin blocks GABA/glycine → sustained jaw-closer contraction), heavy metal poisoning (Pb/Hg) |
| **Non-neurological — Structural** | TMJ disorders (internal derangement, osteoarthritis, ankylosis), oral/oropharyngeal/masticator space tumours, trauma, orofacial infections (pericoronitis, periapical abscess, deep fascial space, peritonsillar abscess, osteomyelitis), mandibular/maxillary fractures, post-surgical oedema/haematoma (third molar extraction, implant, orthognathic), radiation-induced fibrosis, burns, autoimmune (RA, scleroderma, temporal arteritis), congenital (trismus pseudo-camptodactyly, arthrogryposis) | — |
| **Non-neurological — Non-structural** | — | Hypocalcaemia, hypomagnesaemia, malignant hyperthermia (succinylcholine/halothane → masseter spasm), drug-induced (etomidate), muscle contractures, myofascial pain syndrome |

### Red Flags (Immediate Workup Required)

1. Airway compromise
2. Deep neck space infections
3. Tetanus
4. CNS infections
5. Intracranial lesions

### Stepwise Diagnostic Algorithm

1. Interincisal distance measurement (< 35 mm confirms trismus)
2. Clinical history: onset/duration/progression, prior trauma, infections, dental procedures, neurological Sx, systemic diseases, medication/vaccination (tetanus) history
3. Physical exam: interincisal distance, TMJ function/ROM, orofacial soft tissue, dental/pharyngeal/cervical assessment, neurological cranial nerve exam
4. Exclude red flags → emergency pathway if positive
5. Instrumental workup: MRI/CT (intracranial lesions), nerve conduction + trigeminal reflex (peripheral nerve), EMG (muscle activity), EEG (status epilepticus), USS (masseter thickness), FEES (aspiration risk), quantitative jaw tracking
6. Neurological vs. non-neurological pathway branching

### Treatment Summary

| Indication | Intervention | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Acute uncomplicated | NSAIDs + muscle relaxants | First-line expert consensus |
| Drug-induced | Discontinue offending agent (neuroleptics, antiemetics, succinylcholine) | Case-based |
| Metabolic (Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺) | Electrolyte correction | Physiological |
| Tetanus | Tetanus immunoglobulin + supportive care | RCT-level |
| Orofacial infection | Antibiotics + surgical drainage | Standard of care |
| Neurological/non-structural | Botulinum toxin type A (BoNT-A) — inhibits ACh at NMJ | Case series; significant functional improvement |
| Neurological spastic (stroke, DAI, CP, MS) | Baclofen (GABA-B agonist) oral or intrathecal; often combined with BoNT-A | Expert consensus |
| All cases | Physical therapy: passive stretching + jaw mobilisation, multiple daily sessions | Most RCT evidence (esp. HNC context) |
| Extra-articular/post-radiation | Coronoidectomy: resection of coronoid process → immediate large MIO gain; requires aggressive postop PT | Case series |
| Muscular contracture | Myotomy: less predictable, may compromise masticatory efficiency | Case series |
| Advanced fibrosis/post-oncologic | Free vascularized flap reconstruction | Specialist centres |

### Prognosis

- Most cases self-limiting, resolving within ~2 weeks
- Post-RT fibrosis: protracted, resistant to conservative management
- Tetanus: tied to infection severity and treatment timeliness
- BoNT-A: effective in severe fibrosis/contracture

## Limitations

- Narrative review only — no quantitative synthesis
- Most treatment evidence from case reports and expert opinion
- Few RCTs available (mainly rehabilitation/physical therapy)
- Authors acknowledge selection bias inherent to narrative format

## Glossary

- **Trismus (아관긴장증)**: Restricted mouth opening, defined as interincisal distance < 35 mm, due to masticatory muscle dysfunction of any cause
- **Botulinum Toxin Type A (보툴리눔독소A형, BoNT-A)**: Neurotoxin inhibiting acetylcholine release at the neuromuscular junction; used to reduce masticatory muscle hyperactivity in neurological and non-structural trismus
- **Coronoidectomy (관상돌기절제술)**: Surgical resection of the coronoid process of the mandible to release temporalis muscle fibrosis causing extra-articular trismus
- **Tetanospasmin**: Toxin produced by *Clostridium tetani*; blocks inhibitory GABA/glycinergic interneurons → sustained spastic contraction of jaw-closing muscles
- **Baclofen (바클로펜)**: GABA-B receptor agonist used as oral or intrathecal antispastic agent in neurological trismus (stroke, DAI, MS, cerebral palsy)
- **Malignant Hyperthermia (악성 고열증)**: Rare pharmacogenetic crisis triggered by succinylcholine or volatile anaesthetics → uncontrolled masseter/skeletal muscle calcium release and spasm
