## Why Ingested
골밀화 (Osseodensification, OD) 효과가 해면골 (Trabecular/Cancellous Bone) 존재 여부에 따라 어떻게 달라지는지를 조직형태학 (Histomorphometry)으로 정량화한 전임상연구. [[implants/versah-protocols/trisi-2016-osseodensification-low-density-bone-sheep]]의 골밀화 효과 근거를 피질골 우세 부위(해면골 제거)로 확장. 우븐본 (Woven Bone)·층판골 (Lamellar Bone)·%BIC·%BAFO를 3주·12주 두 시점에서 비교측정하여 [[overviews/bone-tissue-types-classification-overview]] 증거맵의 woven→lamellar 전환 타임라인 보강.

## One-line Summary
Sheep in vivo study (n=12, 3 and 12 weeks) comparing osseodensification instrumentation with versus without trabecular bone: presence of trabecular bone produced significantly higher %BIC and %BAFO, and more advanced woven-to-lamellar bone remodeling at both timepoints.

## 한줄요약
양(n=12) 전임상연구: 해면골 존재 조건에서 골밀화 식립 후 %BIC·%BAFO 유의하게 높았고(3주: p=0.009/0.043, 12주: p=0.010/0.008), 우븐본→층판골 전환이 더 진행됨.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First pre-clinical study explicitly isolating trabecular bone volume as independent variable in osseodensification outcomes
- Osseodensification in trabecular bone generates compacted bone chip autografts that act as nucleating surfaces → enhanced woven bone formation → faster lamellar conversion
- Even without trabecular bone, osseodensification did not cause necrosis, inflammation, microfractures, or dehiscence — safe in low-trabecular sites
- %BIC Exp. (with trabecular): 30.87% at 3 wk, 29.36% at 12 wk vs Neg. Ctrl: ~11% at both timepoints
- %BAFO Exp.: 15.36% at 3 wk, 18.12% at 12 wk vs Neg. Ctrl: ~11% at both timepoints

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Animal model: 12 adult sheep; ilium as implant site (naturally low-density bone)
- Experimental design: bilateral osteotomies per animal — one with trabecular bone intact (Exp.), one with trabecular bone surgically debrided (Neg. Ctrl)
- Implants: Ti-6Al-4V, 4 mm × 10 mm, machined surface (Emfils, Brazil); 85 N·cm insertion torque
- Osseodensification protocol: Densah multifluted tapered bur (Versah LLC) 2.0→2.8→3.8 mm, 1100 rpm CCW, saline irrigation
- Healing timepoints: 3 weeks (n=6) and 12 weeks (n=6)
- Histomorphometry: Stevenel's Blue + Van Gieson staining; %BIC (cancellous layer) and %BAFO (1 mm ROI from implant surface); ImageJ analysis
- Statistics: Shapiro-Wilk normality test → linear mixed model (SPSS v29)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**%BIC (overall, independent of time):**
- Exp.: 30.12% ± 7.5
- Neg. Ctrl: 10.94% ± 7.5
- p = 0.001

**%BIC by timepoint:**
- 3 weeks: Exp. 30.87% ± 11.2 vs Neg. Ctrl 11.42% ± 11.2 (p = 0.009)
- 12 weeks: Exp. 29.36% ± 11.2 vs Neg. Ctrl 10.47% ± 11.2 (p = 0.010)

**%BAFO (overall):**
- Exp.: 16.74% ± 2.9 vs Neg. Ctrl 11.38% ± 2.9 (p = 0.003)

**%BAFO by timepoint:**
- 3 weeks: Exp. 15.36% ± 3.9 vs Neg. Ctrl 11.48% ± 3.9 (p = 0.043)
- 12 weeks: Exp. 18.12% ± 3.9 vs Neg. Ctrl 11.28% ± 3.9 (p = 0.008)

**Histological findings:**
- 3 weeks: Exp. group showed increased woven bone; bone chips embedded in osteotomy wall
- 12 weeks: Exp. group showed lamellar bone remodeling around implant (advanced maturation); Neg. Ctrl showed only early lamellar reorganization

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single animal species (sheep ilium); may not translate directly to human jaw bone
- Only machined implant surface; surface-treated implants may behave differently
- No conventional drilling control group in this study (all osteotomies = osseodensification)
- Short follow-up (12 weeks); long-term bone remodeling not assessed
- Future: direct comparison OD vs conventional in low-trabecular bone; human clinical studies

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- **%BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: percentage of implant surface in direct contact with bone; measured in cancellous layer cross-section
- **%BAFO (Bone Area Fractional Occupancy)**: percentage of bone area within 1 mm ROI from implant surface
- **Osseodensification (OD)**: additive drilling technique (CCW Densah bur) compacting bone chips into osteotomy wall, increasing local bone density
- **Nucleating site**: bone chip autograft serving as scaffold surface for new bone cell adhesion and woven bone formation
- **Machined surface**: implant surface with no additional roughening treatment; smooth titanium baseline
