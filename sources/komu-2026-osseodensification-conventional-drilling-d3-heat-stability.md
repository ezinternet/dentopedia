## Why Ingested

This ex vivo study directly quantifies both primary stability gain and heat generation from osseodensification (OD) versus conventional drilling (CD) under non-irrigated conditions in D3-density bone — filling a critical safety gap (thermal osteonecrosis risk) not addressed by the stability-focused systematic review [[implants/osseodensification/banerjee-2024-osseodensification-versus-conventional-drilling-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Sixty implants placed in D3 bovine rib bone (OD n=30, CD n=30) without irrigation showed OD achieved significantly higher mean ISQ (77.30 ±1.91) versus CD (74.68 ±2.05; p<0.05). OD generated significantly more heat (Tmax 42.73 ±5.14°C) than CD (37.43 ±4.22°C; p<0.001), with the smallest 2.3mm Densah bur producing peak heat (45.45 ±5.05°C) and an inverse correlation between bur diameter and temperature. Both techniques remained below the critical 47°C osteonecrosis threshold, supporting OD use in D3 bone even without irrigation.

## 세줄요약

D3 밀도 우골(소 늑골) 60개 임플란트(골밀도화 30개, 일반 드릴링 30개)를 비관주 조건에서 식립했을 때, 골밀도화가 평균 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 77.30으로 일반 드릴링 74.68보다 유의하게 높았다(p<0.05). 열 발생은 골밀도화가 유의하게 높아 최고온도(Tmax) 42.73°C 대 37.43°C(p<0.001)였으며, 가장 작은 2.3mm Densah 버가 최고 발열(45.45°C)을 나타냈고 버 직경이 커질수록 발열이 감소하는 역상관 관계가 관찰됐다. 두 기법 모두 골괴사 임계값 47°C 미만을 유지해, 비관주 조건에서도 D3 골질에서 골밀도화가 안전하게 사용 가능함을 시사한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Simultaneous stability and heat measurement** in the same OD vs CD experiment under non-irrigated conditions — prior OD literature typically measured one or the other.
2. **D3 bone specificity:** Bovine rib bone classified as D3 density provides a clinically relevant model for the most common implant-site bone quality.
3. **Bur-diameter heat gradient:** Demonstrated a clear inverse relationship between sequential Densah bur diameter and heat output; smallest bur (2.3mm) = highest risk point.
4. **Safety validation:** Both OD and CD remained below the critical 47°C osteonecrosis threshold even without irrigation.
5. **OD stability advantage confirmed:** ISQ gain of ~2.6 points statistically significant (p<0.05), consistent with systematic review findings.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Model | D3-density bovine rib bone specimens (ex vivo) |
| Sample | 60 implants total: OD n=30, CD n=30 |
| Irrigation | Non-irrigated (dry drilling) throughout |
| Stability measurement | Resonance frequency analysis (RFA) → ISQ |
| Thermal measurement | Infrared thermography, recorded at each sequential drilling step |
| Bur protocol (OD) | Sequential Densah burs starting at 2.3mm |
| Statistical analysis | Group comparison with significance threshold p<0.05 |

| Group / Step | Mean Tmax (°C) | SD |

- Inverse correlation: larger bur diameter → lower heat generation
- Both groups below 47°C critical threshold

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Ex vivo / animal model:** Bovine rib bone approximates D3 density but lacks vascularization and periosteum; in vivo thermal dissipation will differ.
- **Single bone density tested:** Results specific to D3; applicability to D1, D2, D4 bone requires separate study.
- **Non-irrigated conditions only:** Designed to test worst-case scenario; irrigated OD may show different heat profiles.
- **Abstract-only data:** Specific statistical methods and full result tables not available (paper not in PMC).
- **Future work implied:** In vivo studies, different implant systems, and multiple bone densities needed to generalize safety conclusions.

## 7. Glossary

- **골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD):** Drilling technique using specially designed burs in reverse rotation to laterally condense rather than remove bone
- **일반 드릴링 (Conventional Drilling, CD):** Standard implant site preparation by sequential bone removal
- **임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ):** Dimensionless 1–100 scale derived from resonance frequency analysis; higher = greater stability
- **골괴사 임계값 (Osteonecrosis Threshold):** 47°C sustained for ≥1 minute causes irreversible bone cell death (Eriksson & Albrektsson)
- **D3 골질 (D3 Bone Density):** Misch classification; thin cortical bone with dense-to-fine trabecular core; most common in posterior maxilla
- **적외선 체열검사 (Infrared Thermography):** Non-contact thermal imaging used to record real-time temperature distribution during drilling
- **공명 주파수 분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA):** Non-invasive method measuring implant-bone stiffness via vibration frequency
