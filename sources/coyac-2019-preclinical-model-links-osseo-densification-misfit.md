## Why Ingested

위키는 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD)의 *이득* 쪽으로 편중돼 있다 — [[wiki/implants/huwais-2017-novel-osseous-densification-osteotomy-primary-stability]]의 삽입토크 상승·BIC 3배, [[wiki/implants/tabassum-2021-undersized-axial-compression-primary-stability]]의 언더사이즈 압축 → 토크·BIC 상승이 대표적이다. 그런데 이 두 편은 **모두 치유가 없는 계측**(벤치 / ex vivo)이고, 각자의 페이지가 스스로 "생체 내 치유 확인 필요"를 한계로 적어 두었다. 즉 위키에는 OD의 *천장*(어디까지가 이득이고 어디부터 해악인가)을 말해 줄 논문이 없다. Coyac 2019는 그 결손 구획을 채운다: 고미스핏(high misfit)이 골을 osseo-densify한 뒤 미세골절 → 광범위 골세포 사멸 → 흡수 우세 환경(TRAP·cathepsin K) → 신생골 형성 부재(ALP·collagen I 음성) → 조기 실패로 이어지는 생물학적 경로를 제시하며, 저자들이 직접 "excessive osseo-densification → osseo-destruction"으로 정식화했다.

두 번째 이유가 더 결정적이다. [[wiki/overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] (2026-07-17)는 자신의 최대 공백으로 **"사람 연구 3편 전부가 토크를 사후(post-hoc) 분류했다 — 아무도 토크를 배정하지 않았고, 노출을 실험적으로 배정한 유일한 연구(Trisi 2011, 양)는 해악을 찾지 못했다"**를 명시했다. Coyac은 **미스핏을 실험적으로 배정한 두 번째 연구**이며, Trisi와 정반대로 **해악을 찾았다** — 그것도 Trisi가 없다고 보고한 바로 그 지표(골세포 사멸)에서. 따라서 오버뷰의 안전 쪽 기둥("배정 설계 연구는 해악을 못 찾았다")은 더 이상 단일 증거가 아니며, 오버뷰가 스스로 `[미검증]`으로만 걸어 둔 기전 가교에 실제 생물학적 후보 기전이 생긴다.

**전이 한계를 반드시 함께 적재한다** (이 페이지가 "OD는 해롭다"로 오독되면 위키가 후퇴한다): ① 쥐 상악 ≠ 사람 턱, ② 발치와 ≠ 치유된 치조제, ③ 미스핏(언더사이즈 골삭제) ≠ 버 매개 OD — 다만 저자들이 densification을 misfit의 결과로 취급한다는 점에서 기계론적으로 연결된다. 이 논문의 주장은 *과잉*에 관한 것이지 OD 일반에 관한 것이 아니다.

## Three-line Summary

Preclinical in vivo animal study (rat maxillary extraction sockets; control = minimal implant–osteotomy misfit vs test = high misfit that osseo-densified the peri-implant bone) designed to link misfit, insertion torque, and implant stability to their short- and longer-term effects on peri-implant bone.

High-misfit implants produced micro-fractures and an extensive zone of dying osteocytes, a pro-resorptive environment (tartrate-resistant acid phosphatase activity, cathepsin K IHC), and no new bone formation (absent alkaline phosphatase activity, absent collagen I IHC); µCT, bone-implant contact (BIC) quantification, vimentin and IL1-β IHC showed early implant failure presenting as a crater-like lesion filled with fibrous, inflamed granulation tissue — **the abstract reports no n, no torque values, no misfit magnitudes, no BIC percentages, and no p-values** (abstract-only ingest; see Limitations).

By controlling every other risk indicator the authors conclude that excessive osseo-densification leads directly to osseo-destruction — the mechanistic ceiling on a densification-benefit literature built largely on healing-free bench measurement, but transfer is bounded by rat maxilla ≠ human jaw, extraction socket ≠ healed ridge, and misfit ≠ bur-mediated OD.

## 세줄요약

전임상 생체 내 동물실험 (쥐 상악 발치와; 대조군 = 임플란트–골삭제 간 최소 미스핏(misfit) vs 시험군 = 주위골을 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD)시키는 고도 미스핏): 미스핏·삽입 토크 (Insertion Torque, IT)·임플란트 안정성이 주위골에 미치는 단기·장기 효과를 연결하려는 설계.

고미스핏군에서 주위골 미세골절 + 광범위한 골세포 (Osteocyte) 사멸 구역, 흡수 우세 환경 (주석산 저항성 산성 인산분해효소 (Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase, TRAP) 활성 · 카텝신 K (Cathepsin K) 면역염색 (Immunohistochemistry, IHC)), 신생골 형성 부재 (알칼리성 인산분해효소 (Alkaline Phosphatase, ALP) 활성 및 제1형 교원질 (Collagen I) IHC 음성) 발생; 마이크로 CT (micro-Computed Tomography, µCT) · 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 정량 · 비멘틴 (Vimentin) · 인터루킨-1β (IL1-β) IHC로 조기 실패 확인 — 섬유성 염증성 육아조직으로 채워진 분화구 모양 병소로 발현. **초록에 표본수·토크값·미스핏 크기·BIC 수치·p값이 일절 없다** (초록 전용 인제스트).

저자 결론은 "다른 모든 위험지표를 통제했을 때 과도한 골밀도화는 곧바로 골파괴 (Osseo-destruction)로 이어진다" — 치유 없는 벤치 계측 위에 쌓인 OD 이득 문헌에 대한 기전적 천장을 제공하나, 쥐 상악 ≠ 사람 턱 · 발치와 ≠ 치유된 치조제 · 미스핏 ≠ 버 매개 OD라는 전이 한계 안에서만 읽어야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Assigns the mechanical exposure by design.** Misfit is set experimentally (minimal-misfit control vs high-misfit test), not observed and binned after the fact. Within the wiki's insertion-torque cluster this is only the second paper to do so — and the first to find harm.
- **Supplies a biological pathway from densification to failure**, staged with distinct markers: misfit-induced stress → micro-fractures + osteocyte death → pro-resorptive environment (TRAP, cathepsin K) → no new bone (absent ALP, absent collagen I) → early failure (crater-like lesion, fibrous inflamed granulation tissue; vimentin, IL1-β).
- **Reopens the osseocompression-necrosis mechanism** that the wiki's Trisi 2011 page records as refuted. Trisi reported *no* osteocyte lacunar emptying at 110 Ncm in sheep mandibular cortex; Coyac reports an *extensive zone of dying osteocytes* under high misfit in rat maxillary socket. Both can be true — which converts Trisi's refutation from universal to bed-and-magnitude-bounded.
- **Names an upper bound for a literature that has mostly measured lower bounds.** The held OD/undersizing papers ask "how much stability can compaction buy"; this asks "at what point does compaction stop buying stability and start destroying the bed."
- **Frames densification as a consequence of misfit**, not as a bur technique — the conceptual bridge (and the transfer limit) between this model and bur-mediated OD.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: preclinical in vivo controlled animal experiment (two-arm, control vs test), with a biomechanical/computational (stress/strain) component implied by the title and the "computational modeling" keyword.
- **Animal / model**: rat, implants placed in **maxillary extraction sites** (fresh sockets, not healed ridge).
- **Groups**:
  - Control — implants with **minimal misfit** relative to the osteotomy.
  - Test — implants with a **high degree of misfit**, which therefore **osseo-densified** the peri-implant bone.
- **Stated aim**: to understand the relationship between misfit, insertion torque, implant stability, and their cumulative short- and longer-term effects on peri-implant bone.
- **Outcome measures named in the abstract**:
  - µCT (micro-computed tomography) imaging
  - Bone-implant contact (BIC) quantification
  - Histology: micro-fractures, osteocyte viability/death
  - Enzyme histochemistry: TRAP activity (osteoclastic), ALP activity (osteoblastic)
  - IHC: cathepsin K (resorption), collagen I (bone formation), vimentin, IL1-β (inflammation)
- **NOT recoverable from the abstract** (and therefore not stated anywhere on this page): number of animals or implants, misfit magnitude (osteotomy-to-implant diameter differential), insertion torque values, implant stability values, sacrifice timepoints, BIC percentages, any p-value or effect size, and all finite-element parameters/outputs.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest.** No PMC copy exists; the entire quantitative layer of the paper (n, torque, misfit magnitude, BIC, timepoints, statistics, FEA parameters) is unavailable to this wiki. Any effect size attributed to this paper would be fabrication. Obtaining the full text would materially upgrade this page.
- **Species and site.** Rat maxilla is not human jaw. Rat bone volume, cortical thickness, remodeling rate, and healing kinetics differ from human, and the abstract offers no bridge argument. No held paper validates rat maxillary → human jaw transfer for this mechanism.
- **Fresh extraction socket ≠ healed ridge.** The model places implants into maxillary extraction sites. A fresh socket has a compromised, partly resorbing bony envelope with different vascularity and mechanical support than the healed dense crest where the wiki's clinical harm signal (Marconcini, Aldahlawi) actually lives. This is a compartment mismatch that must not be elided.
- **Misfit ≠ bur-mediated osseodensification.** The exposure is an undersized osteotomy producing geometric interference, not counterclockwise Densah-bur compaction. The paper treats densification as the *consequence* of misfit, which is the mechanistic bridge — but the two are not interchangeable interventions, and the abstract does not test a bur protocol.
- **"Excessive" is undefined.** The conclusion is explicitly about *excess*, yet the abstract provides no threshold, no dose axis, and no intermediate arm — only a two-point contrast (minimal vs high). The paper therefore establishes that a ceiling exists; it does not locate it. Nothing here licenses a clinical misfit or torque limit.
- **Two-arm contrast cannot yield dose-response.** With only minimal and high misfit, no monotonicity, threshold, or safe band can be inferred.
- **Industry co-authorship** (Nobel Biocare Services AG) is declared context, though the finding runs against the sponsor-favorable direction.
- **Future work implied**: locate the misfit/stress threshold at which densification inverts from beneficial to destructive; test whether the same pathway operates in healed ridges, in larger animals, and under bur-mediated OD rather than undersized drilling.

## 6. Related Work

- **Trisi 2011** (`trisi-2011-high-low-implant-torque-histology-sheep`): the wiki's pillar for "high torque does not cause necrosis" — sheep mandible, 110 Ncm, no osteocyte lacunar emptying, greater bone apposition at every timepoint. Coyac reports the opposite histologic signal (extensive osteocyte death, no bone formation) in a different bed under assigned misfit. The two findings do not literally conflict — different species, compartment, and exposure metric — but together they bound Trisi's refutation of osseocompression to its bed and magnitude rather than leaving it universal.
- **Huwais 2017** (`huwais-2017-novel-osseous-densification-osteotomy-primary-stability`): foundational OD bench study — OD raises insertion/removal torque, ~3× BIC, discrete peri-osteotomy BMD layer, and explicitly documents the spring-back/elastic-strain **interference fit**, i.e. misfit. Its own page flags that the 3× BIC is immediate post-insertion bench measurement, "not from biologically integrated implants." Coyac measures what that interference does after healing when it is excessive.
- **Tabassum 2021** (`tabassum-2021-undersized-axial-compression-primary-stability`): ex vivo goat femoral condyle; undersized diameter (± under-depth) raises insertion torque and %BIC, proposed as a type-IV bone strategy "pending confirmation in living bone models." Coyac is a living bone model of the same exposure axis (undersizing/misfit) — and it does not confirm; it shows the destructive branch.
- **Marconcini 2018** (`marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct`) and **Aldahlawi 2018** (`aldahlawi-2018-implant-torque-crestal-bone-remodeling`): the human harm signal (3-y MBL, recession, CSR 91.3% vs 98.2%; MBL 0.95 vs 0.18 mm). Coyac offers a candidate mechanism for a harm these observed but could not explain — but in a different species, compartment (socket vs healed crest), exposure (misfit vs achieved torque), and endpoint (total failure vs sub-millimetre MBL). Mechanistic plausibility, not confirmation.
- **Rittipakorn 2025** (`rittipakorn-2025-clockwise-osseodensification-primary-stability-cadaveric`): names "과압 시 골 소성변형 위험" (plastic deformation risk under over-compression) as background rationale for its clockwise-OD variant. Coyac is the biological consequence of that concern — though Rittipakorn is a formalin-fixed cadaveric study with no healing and thus structurally unable to observe it.
- **`high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview`**: the synthesis this paper most directly bears on — see the wiki page's Related Papers for the argument.

## 7. Glossary

- **Misfit**: the dimensional mismatch between an implant and its prepared osteotomy. Some misfit is *required* for primary stability and is conventionally created by undersizing the osteotomy; this paper's exposure variable is the *degree* of that misfit.
- **Osseo-densification (OD, 골밀도화)**: lateral compaction of peri-implant bone raising local bone mineral density. In this paper it is the *consequence* of high misfit, not a bur technique — distinguishing it from the Versah/Densah counterclockwise-drilling literature.
- **Osseo-destruction**: the authors' term for the terminal state of excessive densification — micro-fractures, osteocyte death, resorption without formation, and failure.
- **Osteocyte**: the mechanosensing cell entombed in mineralized bone matrix; its death removes the signal that maintains and remodels that matrix. "Dying osteocytes" is the pivotal marker here, and the exact marker Trisi 2011 reported as absent at 110 Ncm.
- **TRAP (Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase)**: enzyme histochemical marker of osteoclast activity, i.e. bone resorption.
- **Cathepsin K**: protease secreted by osteoclasts to degrade bone collagen; IHC marker of active resorption.
- **ALP (Alkaline Phosphatase)**: osteoblast activity marker; its *absence* here indicates no bone formation.
- **Collagen I**: principal organic matrix protein of bone; absent IHC indicates no new osteoid.
- **Vimentin**: intermediate filament marker of mesenchymal/fibroblastic cells — consistent with fibrous granulation tissue replacing bone.
- **IL1-β (Interleukin-1 beta)**: pro-inflammatory cytokine; marker of the inflamed granulation tissue in the failed sites.
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact, 골-임플란트 접촉률)**: proportion of implant surface in direct contact with bone; the standard histomorphometric osseointegration measure.
- **µCT (micro-Computed Tomography)**: high-resolution 3D X-ray imaging of bone microarchitecture.
