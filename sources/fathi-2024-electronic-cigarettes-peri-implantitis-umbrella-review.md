## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/calciolari-2026-tobacco-smoking-smoke-free-products]] (2026 SR+MA)는 전자담배·무연담배의 임플란트 생존율 데이터가 정량 pooling에 불충분하다고 결론 내렸는데, 본 umbrella review(Fathi 2024)는 그보다 앞서 5개 SR/MA를 정성 종합해 "전자담배는 전통담배 대비 임상적 염증 증상을 줄이거나 변화 없음, 그러나 니코틴은 여전히 치주·임플란트 건강에 해롭다"는 서술적(narrative) 근거를 제공한다 — 최신 정량 근거 공백(evidence gap)의 배경이 되는 조기 정성적 신호로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 5 systematic/meta-analysis studies (from an initial 501 records, PubMed/MEDLINE/EMBASE/Cochrane/Google Scholar search through December 2022) comparing electronic cigarettes vs traditional cigarettes vs nonsmokers on periodontal and peri-implant clinical outcomes.

All 5 included SRs showed that, compared with traditional tobacco smoke, e-cigarettes may reduce or not change clinical inflammatory symptoms of periodontitis/peri-implantitis (bleeding on probing, probing depth, peri-implant bone loss, treatment response); however, e-cigarettes still contain nicotine and are linked to a wide range of adverse oral-health consequences (mucosal lesions, caries, gingivitis, oral cancer risk) relative to nonsmokers.

Evidence is narrative/qualitative (umbrella of heterogeneous SRs, mostly low risk of bias per ROBIS, but underlying primary studies are largely cross-sectional/observational) — e-cigarettes should not be framed as a "safe" alternative for implant/periodontal health; long-term chronic peri-implantitis risk from e-cigarette use remains insufficiently characterized.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰(Umbrella Review) — 초기 501편에서 선별된 체계적 문헌고찰/메타분석 5편(PubMed·MEDLINE·EMBASE·Cochrane·Google Scholar, 2022년 12월까지 검색)을 종합해 전자담배(e-cigarette) 대 전통 담배(traditional cigarette) 대 비흡연자의 치주·임플란트주위염(peri-implantitis) 임상결과를 비교.

포함된 5개 SR 모두에서 전자담배는 전통 담배 대비 치주염·임플란트주위염의 임상적 염증 증상(탐침시 출혈, 탐침깊이, 임플란트주위 골소실, 치료반응)을 감소시키거나 변화 없음을 보였으나, 전자담배도 니코틴을 함유하며 구강점막병소·우식·치은염·구강암 위험 등 광범위한 구강건강 악영향과 연관됨.

근거는 정성적(narrative)이고 기저 원발연구 대부분이 단면/관찰연구이므로(ROBIS 평가상 대부분 낮은 편향위험이나) 전자담배를 임플란트·치주 건강에 "안전한" 대안으로 프레이밍해서는 안 되며, 장기 만성 임플란트주위염 위험에 대한 근거는 여전히 불충분함.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Implantology 2024;L(6):653–658
- **DOI**: 10.1563/aaid-joi-D-24-00157
- **Institution**: Dental Prosthodontics Department, Dental Materials Research Center, School of Dentistry, Isfahan University of Medical Sciences, Isfahan, Iran (lead); co-authors also Tehran/Iran, Tbilisi/Georgia, Free University of Brussels/Belgium

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review specifically synthesizing SR/MA-level evidence on e-cigarettes and peri-implantitis (as distinct from general periodontal smoking literature).
- Applies ROBIS risk-of-bias appraisal across the 5 included systematic reviews (4 of 5 low risk of bias, 1 moderate).
- Aggregates comparative outcome direction (nonsmoker < e-cigarette < traditional smoker gradient) across probing depth, plaque index, bleeding on probing, and peri-implant bone loss.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review (PRISMA-based) of systematic reviews/meta-analyses.
- **Databases**: PubMed, MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar, searched to December 30, 2022.
- **n**: 5 systematic reviews included (from 501 initial records → 19 first-screen full-text → 119 second-screen full-text assessed → 15 eligible → 5 selected for extraction); underlying primary-study counts per included SR ranged 5–99 studies.
- **Outcomes**: Peri-implant/periodontal clinical parameters — bleeding on probing, probing depth, plaque index, radiographic/peri-implant crestal bone loss, clinical attachment loss, proinflammatory cytokine levels, oral mucosal lesions, implant failure, and broader oral/systemic effects (cytotoxic/genotoxic/oncologic, oral microbiome, traumatic injury from device explosions).
- **Risk of bias**: ROBIS tool (2 independent reviewers), 4 domains (study eligibility, selection process, data collection/assessment, synthesis); studies classified high/moderate/low risk.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Included SRs: Yang et al (98 studies, EPHPP tool) — e-cigarettes endanger periodontal/dental/oral health via oral, throat, dental, periodontal, cytotoxic/genotoxic/oncologic, microbiome, and traumatic-injury effects.
- D'Ambrosio et al (18 studies, ROBINS-I) — e-cigarettes may reduce clinical inflammatory symptoms of periodontitis and hypothetically peri-implantitis vs traditional smoke, but nicotine still adversely affects periodontal/peri-implant health.
- Wasfi et al (93 studies, CASP/RoB) — nonsignificant differences in inflammation/immune response/periodontal-peri-implant parameters comparing daily e-cigarette users to nonsmokers, except oral-health inflammation (higher in e-cigarette users).
- Ralho et al (8 studies, ROBINS-I) — periodontal/peri-implant clinical/radiographic parameters (plaque index, clinical attachment loss, probing depth, peri-implant bone loss, proinflammatory cytokines) worse in e-cigarette/regular smokers vs nonsmokers; e-cigarette users more prone than nonsmokers to oral tissue changes but less than traditional smokers.
- Pesce et al (5 studies, NIH quality tool) — SUCRA ranking: nonsmokers best for probing depth/plaque index, e-cigarette users intermediate, traditional smokers worst; for bleeding on probing, e-cigarette users showed best results, followed by traditional smokers, nonsmokers ranked last (counterintuitive finding attributed to study heterogeneity).
- Overall pattern: gradient nonsmoker < e-cigarette user < traditional smoker for most inflammatory/clinical outcomes, but insufficient long-term evidence for chronic peri-implantitis risk specifically attributable to e-cigarettes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Umbrella-of-umbrella limitation: pools SR-level narrative conclusions rather than re-analyzing individual patient/study-level data; inherits heterogeneity (device types, e-liquid composition, follow-up duration, outcome definitions) from the 5 source SRs.
- Search cutoff December 2022 — predates several more recent, larger SR+MAs (e.g., Calciolari 2026) that directly quantify implant survival/CBL outcomes for e-cigarette users and find data still insufficient to pool.
- Very few of the underlying primary studies are peri-implant-specific (most address periodontal, not peri-implant, tissue), so peri-implantitis-specific conclusions are extrapolated/hypothetical in several of the source SRs (e.g., D'Ambrosio "hypothetically peri-implantitis").
- Self-reported vaping status, device/e-liquid heterogeneity, and predominantly cross-sectional primary-study designs limit causal inference.
- Authors call for new systematic reviews with better reporting systems and dedicated smoking-cessation-focused peri-implant research.

## 7. Glossary
- **E-cigarette (electronic cigarette)**: battery-powered device that heats a nicotine-containing liquid to produce an inhalable aerosol; marketed as a smoking-cessation aid/reduced-harm alternative to combustible tobacco.
- **Peri-implantitis**: plaque-associated inflammatory condition of peri-implant tissues with progressive loss of supporting bone, distinct from peri-implant mucositis (soft-tissue-only inflammation).
- **ROBIS**: Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews — a structured tool assessing bias across study eligibility, selection, data collection, and synthesis domains of a systematic review.
- **SUCRA**: Surface Under the Cumulative RAnking curve — a network meta-analysis ranking metric expressing the probability that an intervention is among the best options.
- **Umbrella review**: a review that systematically collects and appraises multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a related topic, rather than re-analyzing primary studies directly.
