## Why Ingested

Wen et al. 2026 논문(dental floss sequence RCT)의 참고문헌을 훑던 중 발견. 기존 [[wiki/periodontics/liu-2026-powered-manual-toothbrushes-orthodontic-sr-ma]]는 "칫솔 종류(전동 vs 수동)보다 칫솔질 기법의 질이 중요하다"고 결론지었는데, 본 RCT는 그 지점을 device-design 축(conventional vs orthodontic vs single-tufted, 각기 다른 술식 병행)으로 직접 확장·검증한다 — 특히 치은염 환자군에서 single-tufted toothbrush가 3개월 시점까지 지속적 우위를 보였다는 새로운 근거.

## Three-line Summary

Single-blind RCT (n=36 completers, fixed-orthodontic gingivitis patients, 3 groups of 12: conventional toothbrush [C-TB] + modified Bass, orthodontic toothbrush [O-TB] + Bass, single-tufted toothbrush [ST-TB] + Solo/circular technique, 3-month follow-up after professional debridement).

By 3 months, ST-TB maintained significantly lower TQHI (plaque) and BOP than both other groups (p<0.001/p<0.05), while O-TB's plaque advantage over C-TB (seen at weeks 1 and 6) had disappeared; ST-TB also had lower GI throughout all time points; posterior teeth showed worse scores than anterior across all groups regardless of brush type.

Small single-center sample (n=36), home brushing was unsupervised (compliance not verified), and technique co-varied with brush type so device vs. technique effects cannot be fully separated — but the durability of ST-TB's advantage to 3 months (vs. O-TB's fading effect) is the key new signal for orthodontic-gingivitis patients specifically.

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 RCT (n=36 완료, 고정성 교정장치 치은염 환자, 군당 12명: 일반칫솔[C-TB]+수정 Bass법, 교정용칫솔[O-TB]+Bass법, 총모칫솔[single-tufted, ST-TB]+Solo/원형법, 전문가 스케일링 후 3개월 추적).

3개월 시점에 ST-TB군은 TQHI(치면세균막)와 BOP(탐침시출혈) 모두 나머지 두 군보다 유의하게 낮게 유지(p<0.001/p<0.05)된 반면, O-TB의 치면세균막 우위(1·6주차에는 존재)는 3개월에 C-TB 수준으로 사라짐; ST-TB는 전 기간 GI(치은지수)도 가장 낮았고, 브러시 종류와 무관하게 후방치아가 전방치아보다 항상 더 나쁜 지표를 보임.

단일기관 소표본(n=36)에 가정 내 비감독 칫솔질(순응도 미확인)이며 술식이 브러시 종류와 함께 변화해 기기효과와 술식효과가 완전히 분리되지 않으나, ST-TB 효과가 3개월까지 지속(O-TB는 소멸)된 점이 교정용 치은염 환자에 특화된 새로운 신호임.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to compare conventional, orthodontic, and single-tufted toothbrushes together (rather than pairwise) in a **gingivitis-specific** fixed-orthodontic population, with matched, brush-specific brushing techniques (modified Bass / Bass / Solo circular respectively).
- Extends follow-up beyond the typical 72-hour short-term design used in prior single-tufted-toothbrush studies (Aeran et al., Cunha et al.) to a clinically meaningful 3-month window.
- First to stratify results by anterior vs. posterior tooth location for toothbrush-type comparisons.
- Demonstrates a **divergence over time**: O-TB's early (week 1, 6) plaque advantage over C-TB fades by 3 months, while ST-TB's advantage is durable — a finding with direct implications for oral hygiene instruction (OHI) product recommendations in orthodontic gingivitis patients.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Setting**: Istanbul Okan University, Faculty of Dentistry, Sept 2023–Apr 2024.
- **Participants**: 59 recruited, 36 completers (12/group); systemically healthy, 18–35y, non-smokers, ≥22 teeth with brackets, gingivitis (not periodontitis).
- **Sample size**: G*Power calculated minimum n=10/group (f=1.703, 95% CI, 80% power); 14/group targeted allowing 10% attrition.
- **Groups & instructions**:
  - C-TB (conventional toothbrush) — Modified Bass technique.
  - O-TB (orthodontic toothbrush, V-shaped bristle groove) — Bass technique.
  - ST-TB (single-tufted toothbrush) — Solo/single-tooth circular technique.
  - All groups also used an interdental brush; other oral hygiene products restricted.
- **Baseline intervention**: Professional mechanical debridement (ultrasonic + hand instruments) for all patients before randomization.
- **Outcome measures** (6 sites/tooth, single calibrated examiner, ICC 0.99): Turesky modification of Quigley-Hein plaque index (TQHI), gingival index (GI), bleeding on probing (BOP), probing pocket depth (PPD) — at baseline, week 1, week 6, month 3.
- **Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk normality; one-way ANOVA/Kruskal-Wallis between-group; paired t-test/Wilcoxon within-group; two-way mixed-design ANOVA for group×time and group×location×time interactions; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- No baseline differences between groups on any parameter (p>0.05).
- **TQHI (plaque)**: O-TB and ST-TB < C-TB at weeks 1 and 6; by month 3, O-TB rose to ≈ C-TB level (p<0.05), while ST-TB remained lowest of all three (p<0.001).
- **GI**: ST-TB significantly lower than C-TB and O-TB at every time point; no significant C-TB vs. O-TB difference in GI.
- **BOP**: O-TB and ST-TB < C-TB at weeks 1 and 6; by month 3 only ST-TB remained significantly lower.
- **PPD**: No significant between-group difference at any time point (p=0.424) — plaque/gingival gains did not translate into detectable pocket-depth differences over 3 months.
- **Location effect**: Posterior sites scored worse than anterior on TQHI/GI/BOP in all groups at all time points; ST-TB retained its advantage regardless of location, though even ST-TB performed worse posteriorly than anteriorly (mirroring all groups).
- Demographics: mean age 23.14±3.86y; 80.6% female; no significant between-group difference in age or orthodontic treatment duration.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small, single-center sample (n=36 analyzed, though power-justified).
- Home brushing unsupervised — compliance with assigned technique not directly verified (self-report risk).
- Toothbrush type and brushing technique were bundled per group (C-TB+modified-Bass, O-TB+Bass, ST-TB+Solo), so the device effect cannot be cleanly separated from the technique effect.
- Diet, lifestyle, and other confounders (besides smoking/alcohol exclusion) not controlled.
- Bracket-adjacent vs. non-bracket tooth surfaces and maxillary vs. mandibular arch differences not analyzed separately.
- Authors call for objective, examiner-independent methods (e.g., periodic computer-based intraoral plaque image analysis) in future confirmatory studies.

## 6. Related Work

- Contrasted against a prior SR+MA (cited in this paper's Background) finding no significant PTB-vs-MTB difference for plaque/gingivitis in fixed-orthodontic patients — consistent with wiki page [[wiki/periodontics/liu-2026-powered-manual-toothbrushes-orthodontic-sr-ma]].
- Cites Hasegawa et al. (first ST-TB vs. C-TB short-term biofilm-neoformation comparison in periodontally healthy adults) and Aeran et al./Cunha et al. (72-hour ST-TB vs. C-TB trials in orthodontic patients) as the sparse prior evidence base this RCT extends to a 3-month, gingivitis-specific design.
- Complements manual-technique evidence in [[wiki/periodontics/rajwani-2020-manual-toothbrushing-techniques-plaque-gingivitis-sr]] (modified Bass superiority over unstructured brushing) by applying technique-matched-to-device comparisons specifically in the fixed-orthodontic-gingivitis population.

## 7. Glossary

- **C-TB**: Conventional toothbrush.
- **O-TB**: Orthodontic toothbrush (V-shaped bristle groove designed around brackets).
- **ST-TB**: Single-tufted toothbrush (single small tuft of bristles, used with circular "Solo" technique on individual tooth surfaces).
- **TQHI**: Turesky modification of the Quigley-Hein plaque index.
- **GI**: Gingival index.
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing.
- **PPD**: Probing pocket depth.
- **Modified Bass technique**: Bristles at 45° to tooth long axis at gingival margin, gentle rolling motion swept toward occlusal surface.
- **Bass technique**: Bristles at 45° at gingival margin with short vibrating back-and-forth movements, no lifting of bristles.
- **Solo (single-tooth circular) technique**: Circular brushing motion applied to one tooth surface at a time, used with ST-TB.
