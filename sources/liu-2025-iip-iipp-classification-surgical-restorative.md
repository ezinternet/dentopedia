## Why Ingested
[[immediate-implant/immediate-implant.md]] 카테고리에 IIP/IIPP 케이스 선택 근거 추가 — 기존 타이밍 중심 분류(ITI 타입 1-4)를 보완하는 외과적+보철적 난이도 2축 분류체계 제안. [[immediate-implant/loading-protocol]] 결정에 직접 활용 가능한 실용적 틀.

## One-line Summary
Retrospective study (n=44, 53 implants, 12 years): proposed 2-axis (surgical × restorative difficulty) classification for IIP/IIPP — high-complexity cases showed 91.2% survival vs 100% in low/moderate, and 33.3% complication rate vs 0%.

## 한줄요약
후향적 연구 (n=44, 53 임플란트, 12년): 외과적×보철적 난이도 2축 IIP/IIPP 분류체계 제안 — 고난이도군 생존율 80–87.5%, 합병증률 33.3% vs 저난이도군 0%.

- **Journal**: Journal of Dental Sciences, Volume 20, Issue 3, Pages 1843–1852
- **Published**: May 21, 2025
- **PMID**: 40654454 / PMC: PMC12254871

- **Follow-up**: Minimum 12 months; Jan 2009–Feb 2024

## 2. Key Contributions
- First classification that simultaneously addresses surgical AND restorative difficulty for IIP/IIPP (vs. ITI timing-based Type 1–4)
- 4 surgical parameters: bone volume (CBCT), socket anatomy (Elian classification), soft tissue condition (biotype/KT/inflammation), anatomical challenges (IAC/sinus/nasal floor proximity)
- 4 restorative parameters: occlusal loading (parafunctions), aesthetic demands, patient factors (compliance/OH), prosthetic complexity
- Each axis scored Low/Moderate/High → 3×3 = 9 combined risk strata
- Validated: overall survival 96.2%; high×high group 80%, complication 33.3%

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective clinical study
- **n**: 44 patients (23F, 21M), mean age 56.3 ± 11.8 years; 53 implants
- **Distribution**: 52.8% maxillary anterior, 18.9% maxillary posterior, 15.1% mandibular anterior, 13.2% mandibular posterior
- **Difficulty distribution** (surgical): Low 15 (28.3%), Moderate 24 (45.3%), High 14 (26.4%)
- **Difficulty distribution** (restorative): Low 12 (22.6%), Moderate 27 (50.9%), High 14 (26.4%)
- **Period**: January 2009–February 2024
- **Outcomes**: Implant survival, MBL, probing depth, BoP, keratinized tissue, recession, PES/WES, patient satisfaction (0–10)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Combined difficulty | Survival | Complication |
|---|---|---|
| Low×Low | 100% (8/8) | 0% |
| Moderate×Moderate | 100% (17/17) | 11.8% |
| High×Moderate | 87.5% (7/8) | — |
| High×High | 80.0% (4/5) | 33.3% |

**MBL by surgical difficulty**:
- Low: 0.43 ± 0.31 mm
- Moderate: 0.69 ± 0.52 mm
- High: 1.12 ± 0.78 mm (p<0.001)

**Soft tissue recession**: Low 0.2±0.3 mm, Moderate 0.5±0.7 mm, High 1.1±1.0 mm (p<0.001)

**Esthetic scores (PES+WES)**: High restorative difficulty 17.8±2.5 vs Moderate 19.7±2.1 vs Low 21.1±1.6 (p<0.001)

**Patient satisfaction**: Low 9.3±0.8, Moderate 8.8±1.1, High 7.4±1.8

Overall: 96.2% survival (51/53); 2 failures both in high-difficulty groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small retrospective sample (n=53 implants over 15 years) — low statistical power for subgroup analyses
- Single-operator, single-institution — significant operator bias
- Classification not prospectively validated
- Intraclass correlation for classification scoring not reported
- Long enrollment period (2009–2024) includes significant technique evolution — cohort heterogeneity
- Prospective multicenter validation needed
