## Why Ingested

광생물조절 (photobiomodulation, PBM)이 구강·지방 유래 중간엽 줄기세포 (mesenchymal stem cells, MSCs)의 골형성분화를 증진한다는 주장이 개별 체외 (in vitro) 연구에 흩어져 있고, 어떤 조기 조건이 유효한지에 대한 합의된 프로토콜이 없었다. 이 SR은 19편의 체외 근거를 파장 660–1064 nm·에너지밀도 (energy density) 약 2–6 J/cm² 범위로 정리해, [[wiki/orthodontics/zhao-2025-periodontal-ligament-stem-cells-tissue]]가 정리한 PDLSC 골형성 조절 축에 광학 자극이라는 세 번째 축을 더하고, [[wiki/pdrn/lee-2024-impact-polydeoxyribonucleotides-morphology-viability-osteogenic]]와 함께 "단일 최적 조건은 없다"는 형태의 in vitro 골형성 유도 모듈 비교 틀을 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review without meta-analysis (PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science and Google Scholar searched to 30 November 2024; 1104 records screened, 19 in-vitro studies eligible; methodological quality appraised with the QUIN tool) of photobiomodulation (PBM) effects on osteogenic differentiation of oral- and adipose-derived human mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs).

PBM enhanced proliferation and osteogenic differentiation, most consistently in periodontal ligament stem cells (PDLSCs), dental pulp stem cells (DPSCs) and adipose-derived stem cells (ADSCs); wavelengths of 660–1064 nm and energy densities of approximately 2–6 J/cm² gave the most consistent statistically significant osteogenic responses, with ALP, RUNX2 and OCN upregulated and enhanced mineralization, mechanistically via Wnt/β-catenin, BMP/Smad and PI3K/Akt signalling activation.

The clinical value is a parameter starting point rather than a protocol — methodological heterogeneity, medium risk of bias in some studies from incomplete reporting, and the absence of any translational or clinical data mean standardized protocols and further studies are needed (abstract-only ingest).

## 세줄요약

메타분석 없는 체계적 문헌고찰 (SR) — PubMed·Scopus·Web of Science·Google Scholar에서 2024년 11월 30일까지 검색해 1104건 중 19편의 체외 (in vitro) 연구를 포함하고, QUIN 도구로 방법론적 품질을 평가했다.

광생물조절 (photobiomodulation, PBM)은 증식과 골형성분화를 향상시켰고, 특히 치주인대줄기세포 (periodontal ligament stem cells, PDLSCs)·치수줄기세포 (dental pulp stem cells, DPSCs)·지방유래줄기세포 (adipose-derived stem cells, ADSCs)에서 반응이 일관적이었다 — 통계적으로 유의한 골형성 반응은 파장 (wavelength) 660–1064 nm, 에너지밀도 (energy density) 약 2–6 J/cm² 구간에 몰렸고 ALP (부식성선회질, alkaline phosphatase)·RUNX2·OCN (오스텍알칼신, osteocalcin) 상향과 광물화 (mineralization) 증가가 관찰됐다.

임상적으로는 프로토콜이 아니라 파라미터 출발점일 뿐이며, 시험 설계·결과지표의 이질성과 일부 연구의 불완전 보고(QUIN 중간 위험도) 때문에 표준화 프로토콜과 임상 전이 연구가 더 필요하다 — 모든 근거가 체외(in vitro)이고 전문이 아닌 초록만 확보된 상태다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First dedicated synthesis of photobiomodulation effects on osteogenic differentiation of oral- and adipose-derived human MSCs, with the irradiation parameters extracted as a standalone variable rather than as a background detail.
- Consolidates 19 eligible in-vitro studies out of 1104 screened records and pins the most consistent osteogenic responses to wavelengths of 660–1064 nm and energy densities of approximately 2–6 J/cm².
- Names the cell-source hierarchy implied by the pooled narrative: PDLSCs, DPSCs and ADSCs respond most consistently.
- Links the osteogenic readout to mechanism (Wnt/β-catenin, BMP/Smad, PI3K/Akt) and to a quality appraisal (QUIN: low risk in most studies, medium risk in some due to incomplete reporting).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review of in-vitro studies, without meta-analysis (narrative synthesis of extracted parameters and outcomes).
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar; search up to 30 November 2024.
- **Screening**: 1104 records identified → 19 studies met eligibility criteria.
- **Appraisal**: methodological quality and risk of bias assessed with the QUIN tool (in-vitro dental research quality instrument).
- **Extraction**: cell source, laser parameters, osteogenic outcomes.
- **Outcomes**: proliferation, osteogenic differentiation (ALP, RUNX2, OCN), mineralization, and signalling-pathway activation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Records screened → included**: 1104 → 19 in-vitro studies.
- **Effective wavelengths**: 660–1064 nm (range associated with statistically significant osteogenic outcomes).
- **Effective energy density**: approximately 2–6 J/cm² (most consistent osteogenic responses).
- **Most responsive cell sources**: periodontal ligament stem cells (PDLSCs), dental pulp stem cells (DPSCs), adipose-derived stem cells (ADSCs).
- **Proliferation**: enhanced by PBM.
- **Osteogenic markers**: ALP, RUNX2 and OCN upregulated.
- **Mineralization**: enhanced.
- **Mechanism**: Wnt/β-catenin, BMP/Smad and PI3K/Akt signalling pathways activated.
- **Risk of bias (QUIN)**: low risk in most included studies; some studies at medium risk because of incomplete reporting.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All included evidence is in vitro (bench) — no animal or human clinical outcome is represented, so clinical efficacy and safety are unknown.
- No meta-analysis: heterogeneous study designs and outcome measures prevent pooled effect estimates, so the reported parameter ranges are consistency zones rather than validated thresholds.
- Some studies rated medium risk of bias on the QUIN tool due to incomplete reporting.
- Standardized protocols (wavelength, energy density, fluence, irradiation schedule) are needed before parameters can be transferred.
- Translational and clinical studies are explicitly called for to optimize PBM parameters and validate utility in regenerative dentistry and maxillofacial applications.
- Abstract-only ingest: per-study parameter tables, effect sizes and p-values were not available for this page.

## 7. Glossary

- **PBM (photobiomodulation)**: light-based therapy applied to cells or tissues to modulate cellular activity without thermal damage; wavelength and energy density are the two principal dosing variables.
- **MSC (mesenchymal stem cell)**: multipotent stromal cell able to differentiate into osteoblastic, adipogenic and chondrogenic lineages.
- **PDLSC / DPSC / ADSC**: periodontal ligament stem cell / dental pulp stem cell / adipose-derived stem cell — the oral and adipose cell sources pooled in this review.
- **ALP (alkaline phosphatase)**: early osteogenic marker, also called 부식성선회질.
- **RUNX2**: master transcription factor of osteoblast differentiation; **OCN (osteocalcin)**: late-phase osteogenic marker.
- **Energy density (fluence)**: delivered light energy per unit area, here reported in J/cm².
- **QUIN**: appraisal instrument for methodological quality of in-vitro dental research, used here as the risk-of-bias tool.
