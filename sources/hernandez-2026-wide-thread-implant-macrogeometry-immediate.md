## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/implants/heimes-2023-macrogeometry-primary-stability-implants-narrative-review]]는 매크로지오메트리-초기안정성 관계를 임플란트 전반에서 다루는 narrative review이나, wide-thread 디자인을 즉시식립(IIP) 프로토콜에 특화해 종합한 리뷰는 없었다. 본 SR(25편)은 wide-thread macrogeometry가 IIP에서의 초기안정성(IT/ISQ)·변연골소실(MBL)·생존율에 미치는 영향을 정리해 그 공백을 메운다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 25 studies (clinical, in vitro, FEA, and narrative reviews; searched Jan 2007–Aug 2025) evaluating wide-thread implant macrogeometry effects on primary stability, marginal bone loss (MBL), and survival specifically in immediate implant placement.

Wider/more aggressive threads and tapered bodies consistently improved insertion torque and ISQ, especially in D3-D4 low-density bone and post-extraction sockets (e.g. wide-thread SL implants 48.42Ncm vs standard SY 43.42Ncm; deeper-thread implants ISQ 70.13±1.12 vs shallower 65.58±1.37); clinical survival rates for immediately placed wide-thread implants ranged ~93.9–98.6%, comparable to delayed placement, with MBL generally within acceptable limits when adequate stability and loading protocol were achieved.

Evidence supports wide-thread macrogeometry as a biomechanically sound strategy to enhance primary stability under immediate placement, but small sample sizes, short follow-up, and lack of standardized outcome reporting across the 25 pooled studies (no meta-analysis performed) mean confirmation via well-designed multicenter RCTs with long-term follow-up is still needed.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립(Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)에서 wide-thread 매크로지오메트리가 초기안정성(식립 토크/ISQ)·변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)·생존율에 미치는 영향을 정리한 체계적고찰 (임상·in vitro·유한요소해석(FEA)·리뷰 25편, 2007.1–2025.8 검색).

넓거나 공격적인 나사산과 테이퍼형 바디는 저밀도 골(D3-D4)·발치 즉시식립 소켓에서 식립 토크와 ISQ를 일관되게 향상시켰고(예: wide-thread SL 48.42Ncm vs 표준 SY 43.42Ncm; 깊은 나사산 ISQ 70.13±1.12 vs 얕은 나사산 65.58±1.37), 즉시식립 wide-thread 임플란트의 임상 생존율은 ~93.9–98.6%로 지연식립과 대등했으며 적절한 안정성·로딩 프로토콜 하에서 MBL도 허용 범위 내였다.

Wide-thread 디자인은 즉시식립 초기안정성을 향상시키는 생체역학적으로 타당한 전략이나, 포함된 25편의 표본크기 부족·짧은 추적기간·비표준화된 결과보고(메타분석 미시행)로 인해 다기관 RCT 및 장기추적 연구를 통한 확증이 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First review, per the author, to specifically synthesize wide-thread macrogeometry (rather than implant design broadly) under immediate placement protocols.
- Integrates three evidence tiers — clinical, in vitro/FEA biomechanical, and narrative review — into one structured synthesis (25 records from 36 initially identified).
- Compiles a comparative table of insertion torque and ISQ values by thread design across studies.
- Reviews IIP vs delayed-placement survival comparisons (e.g. large retrospective cohort of 50,333 implants) alongside wide-thread-specific data.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: systematic review (no meta-analysis); single reviewer for screening/data extraction; no prospective protocol registration disclosed (acknowledged limitation).
- **Search**: PubMed (MEDLINE) as sole primary database, Jan 2007–Aug 2025, supplemented by manual/citation-tracking screening.
- **Inclusion**: clinical studies, in vitro experiments, FEA studies, and reviews assessing implant macrogeometry (especially wide-thread configurations) on insertion torque, ISQ, MBL, or survival in immediate placement.
- **Exclusion**: studies without biomechanical relevance, non-English publications, case reports, studies lacking quantitative stability/bone data.
- **Yield**: 36 records identified → 25 studies included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Insertion torque (clinical)**: wide-thread SL implants 48.42Ncm vs standard-thread SY implants 43.42Ncm (Menini, n=38 implants, 12-month follow-up; not statistically significant). Tapered vs cylindrical in mandible: 46.67±6.85Ncm vs 35.77±6.72Ncm (p=0.01; Lozano-Carrascal).
- **ISQ**: deeper-thread implants 70.13±1.12 vs shallower-thread 65.58±1.37 (Cucinelli, in vitro); tapered vs cylindrical in mandible 71.67±5.16 vs 57.15±4.83 (p=0.01), maxilla 67.2±4.42 vs 49.17±15.30 (p=0.01) (Lozano-Carrascal).
- **Survival — wide-thread/tapered IIP cohorts**: Wipawin 96% (3–5y, n=25 implants); Aung 93.88% cumulative (mean follow-up 7.43y, n=49 implants); Meijer 73.3% (1y, n=15 deep-thread NobelActive, early-failure-driven outlier).
- **Survival — IIP vs delayed (large-scale comparator)**: Chatzopoulos and Wolff retrospective, 50,333 implants/20,842 patients — delayed 98.6% vs immediate 98.4%, no significant difference. Patel SR+MA (10 studies): immediate 97.4% (332/341) vs delayed 97.5% (350/359), no significant difference.
- **MBL**: platform-switching implants showed bone gain (0.19±0.53mm) vs platform-matching residual loss (0.04±0.58mm) at 60 months (Messias RCT, MD=0.23mm, p=0.025); Aung cohort showed mesial bone gain from 3 months onward, stable to 5 years.
- Insertion torque ≥30Ncm alone was insufficient to guarantee survival per Oliveira's SR, but success rates >95% were consistent when this threshold was met.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No meta-analysis performed; heterogeneous outcome reporting across the 25 included studies precluded pooled quantitative synthesis.
- Single database (PubMed/MEDLINE) as primary source, single reviewer, no protocol registration — risk of selection bias acknowledged by the author.
- Most clinical evidence is short-to-medium term (<5 years); long-term (>5y) data on wide-thread designs specifically remain scarce.
- Small sample sizes and heterogeneity among implant systems limit robust quantitative comparison.
- Author calls for multicenter RCTs, in vivo biomechanical validation of FEA findings (dynamic IT/ISQ/micromovement data), and longer follow-up (>5 years).

## 7. Glossary

- **Macrogeometry**: implant body/thread-level design features (thread depth, pitch, taper, body shape) as distinct from surface micro-texture.
- **IT (Insertion Torque)**: rotational resistance (Ncm) at implant seating; primary-stability surrogate.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: resonance-frequency-analysis (RFA) index of implant stability, scale 1–100.
- **D3-D4 bone**: Misch bone-density classification for lower-density trabecular bone.
- **PS/PM**: Platform Switching / Platform Matching (implant-abutment interface configuration).
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss.
