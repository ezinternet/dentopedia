## Why Ingested
T2DM 임플란트 환자에서 peri-implantitis (치주염, Peri-Implantitis, PI) 발생률과 독립 위험인자를 다변량 로지스틱 회귀로 특정한 임상 코호트 연구. [[implants/peri-implantitis/sbricoli-2026-peri-implant-disease-prevalence-type2-diabetes]]의 유병률 데이터에 구체적인 염증 바이오마커(사이토카인, Cytokine) 궤적과 ISQ(임플란트 안정성 지수, Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 비교를 추가함.

## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (146 T2DM vs 60 controls) finding 43.2% peri-implantitis incidence in T2DM patients, with significantly elevated TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6 in gingival crevicular fluid and lower ISQ at 6 months; high HbA1c, smoking, and brushing frequency <1×/day were independent risk predictors.

## 한줄요약
T2DM 146명 vs 대조 60명 후향적 코호트: T2DM에서 PI 발생률 43.2%, 6개월 GCF TNF-α·IL-6 유의하게 높고 ISQ 낮음; 고 HbA1c·흡연·칫솔질 1회 미만이 독립 위험인자.

## 2. Key Contributions
- PI incidence rate specifically quantified in T2DM implant cohort: 43.2% (63/146)
- Longitudinal GCF cytokine (사이토카인) trajectory: TNF-α and IL-6 continue rising at 6 months in T2DM vs stabilizing in controls
- ISQ trajectory comparison: T2DM ISQ reaches only 65.88 at 6mo vs 70.88 in controls
- Multivariate logistic regression identifies 4 independent risk factors for PI
- Protective factor identified: brushing duration ≥3 minutes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort
- **Population**: 146 T2DM + 60 age/sex-matched normoglycemic controls; Tianjin Fifth Central Hospital, 2021–2023
- **Inclusion**: Age ≥40, T2DM diagnosis, single implant, healed site ≥4 months
- **Follow-up**: 1, 3, 6 months post-restoration
- **Outcomes**: PD, mSBI, mPLI, MBL (radiographic), GCF cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6), ISQ, PI diagnosis
- **Statistics**: t-test/χ², multivariate logistic regression for PI risk factors

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Periodontal indices at 6 months (T2DM vs control):**
- PD: 2.33 ± 0.27 mm vs 1.17 ± 0.11 mm
- MBL: 1.04 ± 0.17 mm vs 0.72 ± 0.09 mm
- mSBI: 1.09 ± 0.72 vs 0.17 ± 0.38

**GCF cytokines at 6 months:**
- TNF-α: 1.98 ± 0.18 ng/mL (T2DM) vs 0.70 ± 0.16 (control) — 2.8× higher
- IL-6: 4.88 ± 0.47 ng/mL (T2DM) vs 1.59 ± 0.08 — 3.1× higher
- IL-1β: 2.08 ± 0.28 ng/mL (T2DM) vs 0.43 ± 0.08 — 4.8× higher

**ISQ at follow-up:**
| Time | T2DM | Control |
|---|---|---|
| 1 mo | 55.49 | 55.48 |
| 3 mo | 61.07 | 65.62 |
| 6 mo | 65.88 | 70.88 |

**PI risk factors (multivariate logistic regression):**
- High HbA1c: OR significant (p <0.05)
- Smoking history: independent risk factor
- Brushing frequency <1×/day: independent risk factor
- Anterior tooth implant site: independent risk factor
- Brushing duration ≥3 min: protective factor

**PI incidence**: 63/146 (43.2%) in T2DM

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single center, retrospective
- No HbA1c threshold reported (classified as high/normal, not continuous)
- Only up to 6 months follow-up — long-term PI trajectory unknown
- No data on glycemic control changes during follow-up
- Control group smaller (60 vs 146)
