## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (25 bruxist patients, 148 NCCLs, 60 months) comparing glass hybrid (GH, Equia Forte Fil) to nano-ceramic composite resin (CR, Ceram.X One Universal); retention rates were similar — CR 73.5% vs GH 66.7% (p=0.464), Kaplan-Meier p=0.487.
TWI (Tooth Wear Index) was a significant predictor of CR retention loss but not GH; both materials showed significant marginal changes over time; no tooth sensitivity or secondary caries detected at any recall.
In bruxist patients — typically excluded from NCCL trials — both GH and CR are clinically acceptable at 60 months; GH offers cost-effectiveness, while TWI may guide material selection.

## 세줄요약

브럭시즘 환자(25명, NCCL 148개)에서 유리 혼합형 수복재(GH, Equia Forte Fil)와 나노도자기 복합레진(CR, Ceram.X One Universal)을 60개월 분구개내 RCT로 비교한 결과, 유지율 CR 73.5% vs GH 66.7% (p=0.464)로 유의한 차이 없음.
치아 마모 지수(TWI)가 CR 유지 실패와 유의하게 상관(GH는 아님); 두 재료 모두 시간 경과에 따른 변연 적합도 유의한 변화; 전 기간 치아 과민증·이차 우식 없음.
브럭시즘 환자 NCCL에서 두 재료 모두 임상적으로 허용 가능; GH는 비용 효율적, TWI는 재료 선택 지표로 활용 가능.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Single-center, split-mouth, double-blind RCT
- **Patients**: 25 bruxist individuals (bruxism diagnosed by clinical signs; polysomnography not used — limitation)
- **Lesions**: 148 NCCLs (25 baseline × ~6 per patient)
- **Follow-up**: Baseline, 6m, 12m, 24m, 36m, 48m, 60m
- **Recall rates**: 100% baseline → 88% at 12m → 60% at 36–60m (10 dropouts: COVID-19 and other reasons)
- **No rubber dam**: Stated as limitation

## Materials

| Parameter | Glass Hybrid (GH) | Composite Resin (CR) |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Equia Forte Fil (GC) | Ceram.X One Universal (Dentsply Sirona) |
| Type | High-viscosity glass hybrid ionomer | Nano-ceramic composite resin |
| Adhesive | Self-adhesive (no separate bonding) | Selective-etch + bonding agent |

## Key Results

### Retention
- CR: 73.5% (13/49 restorations lost)
- GH: 66.7% (16/48 restorations lost)
- Log-rank test: p=0.464 (ns)
- Kaplan-Meier survival curves: p=0.487 (ns)

### Tooth Wear Index (TWI) Correlation
- TWI significantly correlated with CR retention loss (p<0.05)
- TWI NOT significantly correlated with GH retention loss
- Implication: more severe bruxers may show CR failure earlier

### Marginal Adaptation
- Both materials: significant change over time (p<0.001)
- At 24m: GH worse than CR (p=0.002)
- At 36m and 60m: no significant difference between materials

### Marginal Discoloration
- Both materials: significant increase over time
- Between-material difference: not significant at 60m

### Sensitivity & Secondary Caries
- Zero cases of tooth sensitivity at all timepoints
- Zero cases of secondary caries at all timepoints

## Why Ingested

First 60-month split-mouth RCT specifically conducted in bruxist NCCL patients comparing glass hybrid (GH, Equia Forte Fil) to nano-ceramic CR. Bruxism is typically an exclusion criterion in NCCL trials, making this study clinically highly relevant for real-world populations where parafunctional loading is common. Demonstrates that GH — despite being a simplified, self-adhesive material — achieves comparable long-term retention to CR under parafunctional stress.

## Limitations

- Bruxism diagnosed by clinical signs only (no polysomnography); bruxism severity varies
- No rubber dam used during placement
- Dropout rate elevated (40% by 60m, partly COVID-19)
- Single-center design
- Evaluator blinding may be difficult to maintain at 60m
