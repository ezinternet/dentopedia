## Why Ingested

Surveillance topic bone-graft-material (allograft/xenograft/alloplast): the wiki holds beta-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP) only in ridge-preservation, GBR and animal-model contexts, and no page asks how an alloplast performs against allograft/autograft/xenograft in periodontal intrabony defects around natural teeth. This SR+MA fills that gap and adds a small, low-quality alloplast-specific counterpart to the broad regenerative ranking in [[wiki/periodontics/host-modulation/stavropoulos-2021-periodontal-regeneration-intrabony-nma]] (grafting/GTR/EMD all beat open-flap debridement), while sitting next to the β-TCP alveolar-ridge evidence in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/pezzella-2026-beta-tcp-alveolar-ridge-preservation-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (5 studies from 1979–2010, 171 patients / 254 sites, search to March 2020) of beta-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP) alone or combined versus allograft, autogenous bone, xenograft, hydroxyapatite or debridement alone in periodontal infra-bony defects around natural teeth; only 1 of 5 was randomized.

β-TCP beat debridement alone (PD reduction 6.20 vs 2.95 mm, CAL gain 5.80 vs 2.30 mm in one trial) but was comparable to or slightly below allograft/autograft; pooled two-wall SMD (2 studies per pool, fixed-effect) was PD 1.555, CAL 0.815, bone fill -1.189, but only bone fill survived the random-effect model, and heterogeneity was I² 73-92%.

β-TCP is a plausible alternative where autograft/allograft is unavailable or cost-limited, but the evidence is old, mostly non-randomized, tiny and internally inconsistent; it cannot support a ranking of β-TCP against other grafts.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (Systematic Review + Meta-analysis, SR+MA; 1979–2010년 연구 5편, 환자 171명/부위 254개, 2020년 3월까지 검색): 자연치 주위 치주 골내결손에서 베타-인산삼칼슘 (Beta-Tricalcium Phosphate, β-TCP) 단독·병용 vs 동종골(allograft)·자가골·이종골(xenograft)·수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA)·변연절제 단독 비교, 5편 중 무작위 배정은 1편뿐.

β-TCP는 변연절제 단독보다 우수했으나(한 시험에서 치주낭 깊이 (Pocket Depth, PD) 감소 6.20 vs 2.95 mm, 임상 부착 수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득 5.80 vs 2.30 mm) 동종골·자가골과는 비슷하거나 약간 낮았다; 2벽성 결손 풀링 표준화 평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD; 풀당 2편, 고정효과)는 PD 1.555, CAL 0.815, 골충전 (Bone Fill, BF) -1.189이나 랜덤효과 모형에서도 유의한 것은 BF뿐이었고 이질성 (I²)은 73-92%였다.

자가골·동종골을 쓸 수 없거나 비용이 문제일 때 β-TCP가 대안이 될 수 있으나, 근거가 오래되고 대부분 비무작위이며 규모가 작고 내부 불일치가 있어 β-TCP와 다른 이식재의 우열을 가리는 근거로는 쓸 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- SR+MA isolating β-TCP-based grafts (pure, biphasic HA/β-TCP, β-TCP + calcium sulfate, calcium phosphate cement) for infra-bony defects around natural teeth.
- Stratified pooling by defect morphology (two-wall vs three-wall) using SMD, with fixed- and random-effect models side by side.
- Concludes that β-TCP is a "promising alternative" bone substitute but explicitly calls for larger randomized trials.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR + MA following PRISMA; descriptive evidence tables plus SMD pooling (MedCalc 15.0), Cochran's Q and I² (cut-off >50%), fixed- and random-effect.
- **Databases**: Cochrane, MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Science Citation Index Expanded, plus manual search, up to March 2020; no language restriction.
- **n**: 74 records, 10 full texts, 5 included (kappa 0.91 for full-text review); 171 participants, 254 treated sites, age 20-71 years; 4 prospective, 1 retrospective; follow-up 12 months (3 studies), 18 months (1), not applicable (1).
- **Outcomes**: primary PD reduction, CAL gain, bone fill; secondary gingival recession (GR), BOP, plaque index, radiographic bone gain.
- **Included studies**: Strub 1979 (β-TCP paste vs frozen allograft, split-mouth), Snyder 1984 (β-TCP, no comparator), Zafiropoulos 2007 (60% HA/40% β-TCP biphasic + autogenous spongiosa vs spongiosa alone vs spongiosa + bovine xenograft; collagen membrane), Rajesh 2009 (Chitra calcium phosphate cement vs HA cement vs debridement; the only randomized trial, graft-company supported), Sukumar 2010 (β-TCP + calcium sulfate, no comparator).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Per-study (Table 1, as reported):

| Study | Arms | PD reduction | CAL gain | Bone fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strub 1979 (n=8, 47 defects) | TCP vs frozen allograft | 1.8 vs 2.0 mm | not reported | re-entry 1.2 vs 1.5 mm; radiographic 1.05 vs 0.9 mm; residual pocket >3 mm 38% vs 22% (Table 1; the Results text describes the same 38%/22% as PD reduction >3 mm) |
| Snyder 1984 (n=10, 18 mo) | TCP only | 3.6 mm | 1.2 mm | re-entry 2.8 mm |
| Zafiropoulos 2007 (n=64, 93 defects) | HA/β-TCP + ASB vs ASB vs ASB + BDX | not reported | 3.2 vs 3.4 vs 3.2 mm | re-entry 1.6 vs 2.8 vs 1.5 mm |
| Rajesh 2009 (n=60) | CPC vs HA vs debridement | 6.20 vs 4.05 vs 2.95 mm | 5.80 vs 3.55 vs 2.30 mm | not reported |
| Sukumar 2010 (n=21, 39 defects) | TCP/CaSO4 only | 1.98 mm | 1.68 mm | not reported |

Meta-analysis (each pool = 2 studies, SMD, Table 3/4):

| Defect | Outcome | Fixed-effect | Random-effect | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two-wall (N=33) | PD reduction | 1.555 (0.705-2.405), p=0.001 | 1.489 (-0.158-3.135), p=0.075 | 73.0% |
| Two-wall (N=33) | CAL gain | 0.815 (0.031-1.60), p=0.042 | 0.734 (-1.692-3.159), p=0.542 | 89.5% |
| Two-wall (N=26) | Bone fill | -1.189 (-2.107 to -0.271), p=0.013 | same, p=0.013 | 0% |
| Three-wall (N=26) | PD reduction | -0.273 (-1.029-0.484), p=0.464 | -0.172, p=0.750 | 46.6% |
| Three-wall (N=35) | CAL gain | 0.532 (0.051-1.013), p=0.031 | 0.718 (-0.323-1.759), p=0.173 | 72.5% |
| Three-wall (N=56) | Bone fill | 0.983 (0.401-1.565), p=0.001 | -0.088, p=0.955 | 92.0% |

Authors' reading: β-TCP > debridement alone; comparable to other grafts overall; slightly inferior to autograft/allograft in the fixed-effect models; autografts and allografts gave superior regeneration, especially in the harder two-wall defects; HA/β-TCP combined with growth factors or other grafts did better.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 5 studies (1979-2010), each pooled outcome rests on 2 studies; no randomization in 4 of 5; one trial is graft-company supported.
- "β-TCP" is heterogeneous across studies (pure TCP, biphasic HA/β-TCP, β-TCP + CaSO4, calcium phosphate cement), so pooled effects mix different materials.
- Source inconsistencies reproduced as written: (a) the abstract says two-wall bone fill was not significant, yet Results report p=0.013 under both models with a negative SMD (-1.189) while the text says it favored β-TCP and the raw means (1.2 vs 2.4 mm; 6.5 vs 7.7) favor the comparator; (b) Table 1 expands "CPC" as cetyl pyridinium chloride although the intervention is Chitra calcium phosphate cement; (c) I² of 73-92% is described as "absence of heterogeneity" when non-significant.
- Risk-of-bias text states all studies were "low risk", while Table 2 marks randomization, blinding and confounding control as "No" for most studies.
- Authors themselves note few studies, no RCT-level evidence, and small samples; they call for larger RCTs.

## 7. Glossary
- **β-TCP (beta-tricalcium phosphate)**: resorbable, osteoconductive synthetic (alloplast) bioceramic replaced by bone over roughly 0.5-1.5 years per the authors' background.
- **Infra-bony (intrabony) defect**: vertical periodontal bone defect apical to the alveolar crest; classified by number of residual bony walls (1-, 2-, 3-wall).
- **BCP (HA/β-TCP, biphasic calcium phosphate)**: here 60% hydroxyapatite / 40% β-TCP.
- **SMD (standardized mean difference)**: pooled effect-size metric; 0.2/0.5/0.8 = small/medium/large.
- **PD / CAL / BF**: pocket depth reduction, clinical attachment level gain, bone fill (re-entry or radiographic).
- **ASB / BDX**: autogenous spongiosa bone / bovine-derived xenograft.
