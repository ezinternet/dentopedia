## Why Ingested

The held dental-erosion prevalence papers cover risk groups ([[dental-erosion/dallavilla-2024-prevalence-erosive-tooth-wear-risk]]) and the primary dentition ([[dental-erosion/marschner-2024-prevalence-anamnestic-risk-factors-erosive]]); none gives a general-population pooled figure. This SR+MA supplies one headline number (40.8%) for all tooth wear, not only erosive wear, and is the quantitative counterpart to the qualitative FDI statement → [[dental-erosion/salari-2026-global-prevalence-tooth-wear-general-population]]

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis (PRISMA; searches to November 2024; 133 cross-sectional studies after STROBE screening) of tooth wear prevalence in the general population; tooth wear here spans attrition, erosion, abrasion and abfraction, and most included studies measured erosion.

Pooled global prevalence was 40.8% (95% CI 36.7-45.0) with I2 = 99.1%; Egger test showed no significant publication bias (p = 0.346); meta-regression found prevalence higher with larger sample size and lower with later publication year.

The headline number is useful as a burden estimate only: heterogeneity is near-total, no pooled estimates by wear type, age group or region are reported, and the authors' "increasing rate annually" conclusion conflicts with their own year meta-regression.

## 세줄요약

일반 인구의 치아 마모 (Tooth Wear) 유병률에 대한 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review)과 메타분석 (Meta-analysis): 2024년 11월까지 검색, STROBE 선별 후 단면연구 133편; 치아 마모는 교모 (Attrition)·침식 (Erosion)·마모 (Abrasion)·치경부 쐐기결손 기전 (Abfraction)을 모두 포함하며 포함 연구 다수는 침식을 측정.

전 세계 통합 유병률은 40.8% (95% CI 36.7-45.0), I2 99.1%; Egger 검정상 출판 편향 유의하지 않음 (p = 0.346); 메타회귀에서 표본 수가 클수록 유병률은 높고 출판 연도가 늦을수록 낮았음.

이 수치는 질병 부담의 대략적 추정으로만 쓸 것: 이질성이 거의 100%이고, 마모 유형·연령·지역별 통합 추정치가 없으며, 저자들의 "해마다 증가" 결론은 자체 연도 메타회귀와 모순됨.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First stated global pooled prevalence of tooth wear in the general population (per the authors).
- Meta-regression of prevalence on sample size and publication year.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA; PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus, ScienceDirect and Google Scholar; first search 21 July 2024, updated 26 November 2024; reference lists screened.
- Inclusion: cross-sectional studies reporting tooth wear prevalence, general population, full text, extractable data; Persian and English only.
- Quality: STROBE checklist; score below 16 = poor quality and excluded.
- Random-effects model (CMA v2); I2 for heterogeneity, Egger for publication bias; meta-regression on sample size and year.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 1900 records; 119 duplicates and 876 irrelevant removed; 403 removed on criteria; 369 excluded as low quality; 133 included.
- Abstract wording: "sample size of 92,153 individuals with TW"; the text does not clarify whether this is total participants or only affected individuals.
- Pooled prevalence 40.8% (95% CI 36.7-45); I2 99.1%; Egger p = 0.346.
- Meta-regression: prevalence rose with sample size and fell with later publication year (both p < 0.05; coefficients not given in the text).
- Included-study table (Table 1) lists per-study prevalence ranging from about 1.4% to 97.9% (e.g. a German adult cohort at 97.9%); wear type per study labelled Erosion, General, Attrition or Abrasion; most are labelled Erosion.
- Table 2 (factors): GERD and acidic drinks associated in some studies (GERD p < 0.001 in one, 0.07 and 0.297 in others; acidic drinks p = 0.81, 0.003, < 0.001 across three studies); sex results conflicting. This is a list of p-values from three or so studies, not a pooled association.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Authors: few eligible investigations, many non-English studies lost, lifestyle ignored in some studies, full text/extractable data missing for some.
- Reviewer notes: no pooled subgroup by wear type, age, region, diagnostic index (BEWE, TWI, questionnaire vs clinical); 369 of 502 quality-screened studies excluded by a 16-point STROBE cut-off with no stated rationale; questionnaire-only studies are included alongside clinical exams; no protocol registration stated; I2 99.1% makes the pooled value a descriptive mean rather than a stable estimate.
- Internal inconsistency: conclusion states prevalence is "increasing annually", but the year meta-regression shows prevalence decreasing with later publication year.
- Discussion cites Marschner as "2025" and attributes 35.6% to children with primary dentition; the held paper is dated 2024 and reports 35.6% (checked against the held page).

## 7. Glossary

- Tooth wear: cumulative non-carious loss of dental hard tissue (attrition, erosion, abrasion, abfraction).
- STROBE: reporting checklist for observational studies, used here as a quality score.
- Egger test: regression test for funnel-plot asymmetry.
- I2: percentage of variability across studies due to heterogeneity rather than chance.
