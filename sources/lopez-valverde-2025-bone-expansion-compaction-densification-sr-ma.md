## Why Ingested
위키의 `implants/versah-protocols` 카테고리에 골밀도화(Osteodensification, OD) 프로토콜의 최신 SR+MA가 없었음. López-Valverde 2025는 골확장(Bone Expansion, BE)·골압축(Bone Compaction, BC)·골밀도화(OD) 세 기법을 좁은 치조골(Narrow Alveolar Ridge, NAR)에서 직접 비교한 최초의 SR+MA로, ISQ·골밀도(Bone Density, BD)·치조제 확장(Crest Expansion, CE) 세 지표를 동시 메타분석함.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA (10 studies, 2025) comparing bone expansion, compaction, and osteodensification in narrow ridges: osteodensification significantly outperformed bone expansion on BD (SMD −0.71, p=0.002), CE (SMD −1.12, p=0.04), and ISQ (SMD −8.88, p=0.0005).

## 한줄요약
SR+MA(10편, 2025): 좁은 치조골에서 골확장·골압축·골밀도화 비교 — 골밀도화가 골밀도(SMD −0.71)·치조제 확장(SMD −1.12)·ISQ(SMD −8.88) 모두에서 골확장 대비 유의하게 우수.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA to directly compare **three ridge management techniques** (bone expansion vs bone compaction vs osteodensification) specifically in narrow alveolar ridges (NAR)
- Osteodensification (Versah Densah Bur protocol) significantly outperforms bone expansion on all three primary outcomes simultaneously — provides highest-level evidence for OD superiority
- ISQ advantage of OD over bone expansion: SMD −8.88 (95% CI −13.85 to −3.91, p=0.0005) — clinically relevant primary stability improvement
- Bone density advantage: SMD −0.71 (95% CI −1.15 to −0.27, p=0.002)
- Crest expansion advantage: SMD −1.12 (95% CI −2.20 to −0.04, p=0.04) — better volumetric ridge gain
- Identifies bone compaction as underrepresented in the literature — currently insufficient data to meta-analyze vs OD separately

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020); PROSPERO CRD42025646738
- **Search:** 2,464 records screened; 10 studies met inclusion criteria
- **Inclusion:** Studies in humans comparing at least 2 of {BE, BC, OD} in narrow ridges with quantitative outcomes (BD, CE, or ISQ)
- **Comparator of main meta-analysis:** Osteodensification vs Bone Expansion (most represented pair)
- **Outcomes:**
  - Bone Density (BD) — radiographic or histomorphometric
  - Alveolar Crest Expansion (CE) — mm
  - Implant Stability Quotient (ISQ, 임플란트 안정성 지수) — Osstell RFA
- **Statistical method:** Standardized Mean Difference (SMD) with 95% CI; random effects model; α=0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Osteodensification vs Bone Expansion (primary comparison):**
| Outcome | SMD (95% CI) | P value | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone Density (BD) | −0.71 (−1.15 to −0.27) | 0.002 | OD achieves higher BD |
| Crest Expansion (CE) | −1.12 (−2.20 to −0.04) | 0.04 | OD achieves greater CE |
| ISQ | −8.88 (−13.85 to −3.91) | 0.0005 | OD achieves higher ISQ |

Note: Negative SMD = favors osteodensification (OD group has higher absolute values on all 3 outcomes).

**Bone compaction data:** Insufficient studies for independent meta-analysis vs OD or BE; narrative synthesis only.

**Screening:** 2,464 records → 10 studies included; large attrition reflects strict PICO criteria for narrow ridge + comparative design.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 10 studies included out of 2,464 — evidence base remains sparse; high attrition reduces generalizability
- Bone compaction could not be meta-analyzed separately — insufficient data
- Heterogeneity in BD measurement methods (CBCT HU, histomorphometry, micro-CT) across included studies
- No long-term survival or MBL data: all outcomes are peri-operative/short-term (ISQ at placement or early healing, not 1–5 year MBL)
- Narrow ridge definition varies across studies — no consensus threshold for "narrow" alveolar ridge width
- Only Spanish-affiliated authors; single institution for this SR suggests risk of selective citation
- PROSPERO registration does not eliminate risk of outcome reporting bias in the underlying primary studies

## 7. Glossary
- **Osteodensification (골밀도화, OD):** Bone preparation technique using Versah Densah Burs in counter-clockwise direction; compacts bone laterally rather than removing it, preserving autogenous chips at the osteotomy walls — increases BD, CE, and primary stability in narrow ridges
- **Bone Expansion (골확장, BE):** Mechanical lateral expansion of narrow ridges using osteotomes or split-crest technique; spreads cortical plates without implant-site preparation rotating tools
- **Bone Compaction (골압축, BC):** Progressive osteotome condensation technique; distinct from OD in that it uses manual/mallet-driven tapered osteotomes without dedicated bur rotation
- **Narrow Alveolar Ridge (좁은 치조골, NAR):** Ridge width insufficient for standard-diameter implant placement without bone augmentation; typically <6 mm bucco-lingual width
- **SMD (표준화 평균차):** Standardized Mean Difference — allows pooling of studies using different BD/ISQ measurement scales; |SMD| >0.8 = large effect
- **PROSPERO:** International prospective register of systematic reviews; CRD42025646738 confirms pre-registration before data extraction
