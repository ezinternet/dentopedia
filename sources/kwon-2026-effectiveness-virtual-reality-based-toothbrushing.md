## Why Ingested
Adds an immersive-VR arm to the wiki's toothbrushing-instruction evidence: [[wiki/periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/choi-2017-dental-plaque-control-oral-hygiene-education-korea-sr]] found that disclosing-agent-based instruction outperformed verbal or model-based education, and this RCT tests VR against exactly that disclosing-agent comparator. It also relates to the Bass-technique evidence in [[wiki/periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/rajwani-2020-manual-toothbrushing-techniques-plaque-gingivitis-sr]] (the VR curriculum teaches Bass) and to the digital plaque-monitoring pages [[wiki/periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/giese-kraft-2022-digital-imaging-plaque-monitoring]] and [[wiki/periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/guo-2026-digital-technologies-plaque-assessment-sr-ma]], which cover digital plaque assessment rather than digital instruction delivery. Ingested from the 2026-09 PubMed surveillance sweep on digital oral hygiene education.

## Three-line Summary

Single-blind, two-arm RCT (n=30 healthy adults aged 20-39, 15 per arm, Seoul National University, January 2025) comparing about 5 minutes of immersive VR toothbrushing instruction (Meta Quest 3, Bass-method curriculum with a virtual mirror) against one-on-one hygienist instruction using plaque-disclosing agent, all participants receiving professional Airflow cleaning before a 2-week home period.

Mean plaque index (PI, Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein) fell by 0.44 (SD 0.48) in the VR group vs 0.07 (SD 0.26) in controls from baseline to 2 weeks (p=0.012); bleeding on probing was unchanged in both arms (0.05 and 0.04 to same values, p=0.785); attitude (p=0.008) and behavior (p=0.004) scores rose more in VR, knowledge did not (p=0.583); no dizziness was reported.

Promising but preliminary: tiny sample, 2-week follow-up, unblinded examiners, retrospective trial registration, and baseline attitude/behavior scores were lower in the VR arm (behavior 2.53 vs 3.87), so the KAB gains partly reflect catch-up; follow-up behavior scores were nearly identical (4.73 vs 4.53).

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 2군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 건강 성인 20-39세 n=30, 군당 15명, 서울대 치의학대학원, 2025년 1월): 몰입형 가상현실 (Virtual Reality, VR) 칫솔질 교육 (Meta Quest 3, 가상 거울을 활용한 바스법 커리큘럼, 약 5분)을 치면세균막 착색제 (plaque-disclosing agent)를 이용한 치과위생사 1:1 교육과 비교했고, 전원 Airflow 전문가 치면세정 후 2주간 자가 관리.

치면세균막 지수 (Plaque Index, PI)의 기저 대비 2주 감소는 VR군 0.44 (SD 0.48) vs 대조군 0.07 (SD 0.26) (p=0.012); 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)은 양군 모두 변화 없음 (p=0.785); 태도 (p=0.008)·행동 (p=0.004) 점수는 VR군에서 더 증가, 지식은 무변화 (p=0.583); 어지럼증 보고 없음.

유망하나 예비 단계: 표본이 작고 추적 2주, 검사자 비눈가림, 임상시험 사후 등록이며, VR군의 기저 태도·행동 점수가 더 낮아 (행동 2.53 vs 3.87) KAB 개선은 일부 따라잡기 효과일 수 있음 (추적 시 행동 4.73 vs 4.53으로 거의 동일).

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Investigations 2026;30(4)
- **DOI**: 10.1007/s00784-026-06819-6
- **PMID / PMCID**: 41843223 / PMC12995924
- **Institution**: Department of Preventive Dentistry & Public Oral Health, School of Dentistry, Seoul National University, Republic of Korea (with Dankook University and UC Irvine co-authors)
- **Trial registration**: KCT0010914 (CRIS), retrospectively registered August 19, 2025

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT to report both clinical indices (PI, BOP) and KAB scores after VR toothbrushing instruction in healthy adults, per the authors.
- Uses an active, clinically realistic comparator (hygienist instruction with plaque-disclosing agent) instead of no-intervention or verbal-only controls.
- Interactive VR (virtual mirror, Bass-method plaque-removal simulation with feedback gauge) rather than passive video viewing.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: single-blind, two-arm parallel RCT, 1:1 allocation (Excel randomization), CONSORT 2010; participants blinded, examiners not.
- **Population**: n=30 (15/15), age 20-39 (mean 24.93), at least 24 natural teeth, no electric toothbrush use, no orthodontic appliances; 15 male / 15 female.
- **Intervention**: VR curriculum, 4 modules (Bass technique at 45 degrees, most posterior molars, brushing sequence, "Toothbrushing Master" mirror simulation), about 5 min on Meta Quest 3.
- **Control**: one-on-one hygienist TBI using plaque-disclosing agent (Trace), about 5 min.
- **Common to both**: Airflow professional cleaning after education, standardized soft manual brush and 996-ppm fluoride toothpaste provided, 2-week home period with no other oral hygiene aids or dental visits allowed.
- **Outcomes**: PI (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein, 6 sites/tooth) at baseline, immediately post-education and 2 weeks; BOP (UNC probe, 6 sites/tooth) at baseline and 2 weeks; KAB questionnaire (8 knowledge, 9 attitude, 7 behavior items); VR group also DHI and modified SUS.
- **Power**: G*Power, effect size 0.25, 80% power, alpha 0.05, N=28 required.
- **Analysis**: independent t-test, chi-square, repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Control (n=15) | VR (n=15) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| PI baseline | 2.94 (0.25) | 2.92 (0.34) | - |
| PI after education | 2.34 (0.26) | 2.12 (0.35) | - |
| PI at 2 weeks | 2.87 (0.38) | 2.47 (0.58) | - |
| PI change (baseline minus 2 wk) | 0.07 (0.26) | 0.44 (0.48) | 0.012 (t28 = 2.68) |
| BOP baseline to 2 wk | 0.04 to 0.04 | 0.05 to 0.05 | 0.785 |
| Knowledge | 7.00 to 7.40 | 6.90 to 7.53 | 0.583 |
| Attitude | 37.70 to 38.30 | 35.40 to 39.06 | 0.008 |
| Behavior | 3.87 to 4.53 | 2.53 to 4.73 | 0.004 |

- Repeated-measures ANOVA: PI changed significantly over time (p=0.024). The Discussion text cites p=0.024 for the between-group PI comparison and p<0.001 for behavior, whereas the Results/Tables give p=0.012 and p=0.004; the tabulated values are used here.
- No dizziness reported (DHI); SUS Cronbach alpha 0.91; KAB Cronbach alpha 0.65 (low-acceptable).
- Immediately after education both arms improved (PI 2.94 to 2.34 control; 2.92 to 2.12 VR), and PI rebounded toward baseline by 2 weeks, more in controls.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample, 2-week follow-up; too short to show gingival inflammation (BOP) change.
- Examiners not blinded; trial registered retrospectively (after the January 2025 conduct).
- VR-arm baseline attitude (35.40 vs 37.70) and behavior (2.53 vs 3.87) scores were lower than control, and follow-up values were similar, so between-group KAB differences may partly reflect baseline imbalance; the abstract's "39.06 vs 38.30" and "4.73 vs 4.53" are follow-up values, not gains.
- The abstract says computer-generated randomization; Methods say Microsoft Excel.
- Healthy young adults with high baseline BOP-free status (BOP about 0.04-0.05); hardware cost; generalizability unknown. Authors call for larger samples, longer follow-up and rigorous blinding.

## 7. Glossary
- **Virtual reality-based toothbrushing instruction (VR TBI)**: headset-delivered, interactive toothbrushing training with a virtual dentition and mirror.
- **Plaque index (PI, Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein)**: 0-5 plaque score per site, here averaged over 6 sites per tooth.
- **KAB**: knowledge-attitude-behavior questionnaire.
- **DHI / SUS**: Dizziness Handicap Inventory / System Usability Scale.
- **Bass method**: sulcular brushing with the brush tilted 45 degrees toward the gingival margin.
