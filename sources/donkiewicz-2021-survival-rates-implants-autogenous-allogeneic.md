# Donkiewicz 2021 — Survival Rates of Dental Implants in Autogenous and Allogeneic Bone Blocks: A Systematic Review

## Why Ingested

Provides quantitative survival rate evidence comparing autogenous bone block (AUBB) versus allogeneic bone block (ALBB) grafting as pre-implant bone augmentation strategies, directly informing graft material selection decisions covered in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/benic-2014-horizontal-bone-augmentation-guided-regeneration]] and the broader bone graft selection matrix.

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review analyzed 100 studies to compare implant survival rates (ISRs) of implants placed in autogenous bone blocks (AUBB, n=6861) versus allogeneic bone blocks (ALBB, n=3434 implants).

No significant difference was found between overall ISR of AUBB (96.23 ± 5.27%) and ALBB (97.66 ± 2.68%) (p=0.54). ISRs were negatively affected by extraoral donor sites and total edentulism for AUBB, and by fresh-frozen allografts and vertical augmentations for ALBB.

ALBB is a feasible alternative to AUBB for pre-implant bone grafting, but the low evidence level, absence of comparative RCTs, and short ALBB follow-up limit conclusions.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest systematic review to date comparing implant survival in AUBB vs ALBB grafted sites (100 studies, 10,295 implants total).
- Establishes no statistically significant difference in ISR between AUBB (96.23%) and ALBB (97.66%) (p=0.54).
- Identifies specific subgroup factors that significantly affect ISR within each graft type.
- For AUBB: intraoral donor sites outperform extraoral sites (p=0.0003); cortical grafts outperform cortico-cancellous (p=0.005); partial edentulism outperforms total edentulism (98.08% vs 93.92%, p=0.0002); early placement outperforms immediate (97.60% vs 91.56%, p=0.018).
- For ALBB: processed allografts (98.81%) outperform fresh-frozen (95.94%, p=0.004); horizontal augmentations (100%) outperform vertical (97.11%, p=0.009).

---

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA guidelines), quantitative analysis.
- **Search**: MEDLINE (PubMed), EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL (January 1995–November 2021) + hand search of reference lists.
- **Inclusion criteria**: Human studies, English language, RCTs/controlled trials/prospective/retrospective designs, use of AUBB or ALBB, ≥10 subjects, ≥12 months follow-up, reporting ISR.
- **Quality assessment**: Modified NIH tool, Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS), Jadad scale.
- **Statistical analysis**: Shapiro-Wilk normality test, Mann-Whitney U test, Kruskal-Wallis test with Dunn's post-hoc test.
- **Study scope**: 100 studies total — 81 AUBB studies, 13 ALBB studies, 6 studies directly comparing both.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Low overall evidence level (mostly retrospective studies); high risk of bias in many included studies.
- Only 6 of 100 studies directly compared AUBB vs ALBB — most comparisons are indirect.
- No RCTs directly comparing AUBB and ALBB exist.
- ALBB follow-up (mean 25.1 months) considerably shorter than AUBB (mean 38.3 months), limiting long-term conclusions for ALBB.
- High heterogeneity in defect morphologies, graft types, surgical procedures, and membrane use across studies.
- Future need: prospective comparative RCTs with ≥5-year follow-up, standardized outcome reporting.

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| AUBB | Autogenous Bone Block (자가골 블록) — bone harvested from the patient's own body |
| ALBB | Allogeneic Bone Block (동종골 블록) — bone from a human donor |
| ISR | Implant Survival Rate (임플란트 생존율) — percentage of implants remaining in situ and functional |
| FDBA | Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft (동결 건조 동종골) — allograft freeze-dried to remove water while retaining structure |
| DFDBA | Demineralized Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft (탈회 동결 건조 동종골) — FDBA that has been demineralized to expose collagen matrix |
| Fresh-frozen allograft | Allograft preserved by freezing without additional processing; retains more antigens |
| NOS | Newcastle-Ottawa Scale — quality assessment tool for observational studies |
