## Why Ingested

수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)의 접착 수복 + 의도적 재식립 술식에서 임상 근거가 대부분 4-META/MMA-TBB (Super-Bond) 증례시리즈인 것을 [[resin-bonding/corbella-2025-surgical-techniques-vertical-root-fractures-sr]]가 정리했는데, 이 논문은 그 술식의 접착 재료·상아질 표면처리 조합을 체외(bench)에서 직접 비교한 유일한 재료 수준 근거다 — Super-Bond 계열 재료 연구 [[resin-bonding/kikuta-2025-fillers-4-meta-mma-tbb-resin-root-caries]]와 같은 축. 전문(PMC)을 확보해 근거 공백을 메우되 체외 연구라는 낮은 근거 수준 그대로 취급한다.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro bench study (80 bovine lower-incisor root models with a 300 um fracture gap, n=16/group, 5 groups, 50,000 cycles of 70 N loading simulating 1 month of mastication) comparing a 4-META/MMA-TBB resin control (SB-G) with a chemically cured bonding agent plus blue core composite (UB + ESTECORE, EC) under four dentin surface treatments for repair of vertically fractured roots.

EC-G (10% citric acid / 3% ferric chloride "10-3" solution + UB + EC) had the highest microtensile bond strength (46.43 MPa vs 39.36 MPa for SB-G; p=0.0092 vs SB-G, p=0.0027 vs EC, p=0.0051 vs EC-P) and the most cohesive-in-dentin failures; the 40% phosphate pretreatment (EC-P) leaked significantly more than other EC groups, but only at the 6.5 mm level (p=0.0227), with no difference at 1 mm or 13 mm.

Bench-only evidence (bovine teeth, 1 month of loading, no cores/crowns/periodontal tissue, no biocompatibility testing), so it supports a candidate material/pretreatment for the adhesive-repair + intentional-replantation technique but gives no clinical survival information.

## 세줄요약

체외 (In-vitro) 실험 연구 (소 하악 절치 80개로 만든 300 um 파절 간극 모델, 5군 군당 n=16, 70 N 50,000회 주기부하로 저작 1개월 모사) — 4-META/MMA-TBB 레진 (Super-Bond) 대조군과 화학중합형 본딩제 + 청색 코어용 복합레진 (UB + ESTECORE) 조합 4종의 상아질 표면처리를 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 수복용으로 비교.

EC-G군 (10% 구연산·3% 염화제2철 용액 "10-3" + UB + EC)이 미세인장결합강도 (Microtensile Bond Strength, μTBS) 최고 (46.43 MPa vs SB-G 39.36 MPa; SB-G 대비 p=0.0092) 및 상아질 내 응집파괴 비율 최고; 40% 인산 전처리군 (EC-P)은 미세누출이 유의하게 컸으나 6.5 mm 절단면에서만 (p=0.0227), 1 mm·13 mm에서는 차이 없음.

소 치아·저작 1개월 모사·코어/크라운/치주조직 없는 bench 연구라 접착 수복 + 의도적 재식립 술식의 후보 재료·전처리를 제시할 뿐 임상 생존 정보는 없음 — 근거 수준 낮음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First bench comparison (in this wiki) of dentin surface treatments (10-3 solution, 40% phosphate, 18% EDTA) combined with a chemically cured bonding agent + core composite for the "adhesive restoration + intentional replantation" repair of VRF.
- Tests an alternative to the unfilled 4-META/MMA-TBB resin (Super-Bond), whose stated disadvantages are poor operability, poor tooth/resin colour discrimination, long curing time, low strength and large polymerization shrinkage.
- Adds a cyclic-loading step (70 N, 2 Hz, 50,000 cycles in 37 C water, silicone "periodontal ligament") before microleakage and μTBS testing.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: in-vitro, 5 groups, a priori power analysis (G*Power; 16/group for effect size 0.4, alpha 0.05, power 0.8), 80 bovine lower incisors.
- **Model**: crown removed at CEJ; canal wall thinned to 1.5 mm; root cut longitudinally to 17 mm depth creating a 300 um gap; surfaces treated, re-bonded, 24 h in 37 C water.
- **Groups**: SB-G (10-3 + Super-Bond; control), EC (UB + EC), EC-G (10-3 + UB + EC), EC-P (40% phosphate + UB + EC), EC-E (18% EDTA + UB + EC). 10-3 and phosphate applied 10 s, EDTA 60 s.
- **Outcomes**: microleakage (50% silver nitrate; 4-class score at 1, 6.5, 13 mm; 32 data points/group/position; Kruskal-Wallis + Steel-Dwass), μTBS (20 beams of 1 mm2 per specimen; ANOVA + Tukey), failure mode (4 types; SEM on representative specimens).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| μTBS | EC-G highest, 46.43 MPa; SB-G (control) 39.36 MPa; ANOVA p<0.001 |
| EC-G vs others (μTBS) | vs SB-G p=0.0092; vs EC p=0.0027; vs EC-P p=0.0051 (Tukey) |
| Microleakage across cut positions | no difference among positions within a group (p>0.05); differences among groups at each position (Kruskal-Wallis p<0.001) |
| Microleakage post hoc | EC-P differed from the other EC groups only at 6.5 mm (Steel-Dwass p=0.0227); no difference at 1 mm or 13 mm |
| Failure mode, SB-G | type 1 adhesive 57.14%; type 2 cohesive-in-material 34.07% |
| Failure mode, EC | most type 4 mixed; SB-G, EC-G, EC-P, EC-E mostly type 1 adhesive; EC-G highest type 3 cohesive-in-dentin |

Tables 1-3 and Figures 3-4 (per-group microleakage scores, full failure-mode distribution) were not present in the retrieved PMC text; only the numbers quoted in the prose are recorded here.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Bovine teeth (authors acknowledge a review found bond-strength results with bovine teeth inconclusive as a human-tooth substitute).
- Only 50,000 cycles (about 1 month, the temporary-splint period); long-term durability and behaviour with cores and crowns not tested (authors).
- No blood contamination, periodontal tissue, or replantation step actually simulated, although the discussion assumes blood contamination clinically; biocompatibility of the core composite to periodontal cells explicitly left unproven (authors).
- Abstract states EC, EC-G and EC-E microleakage was significantly lower than EC-P, but the Results show that difference only at the 6.5 mm level.
- Conflict-of-interest/funding statements were not in the retrieved text; the study tested manufacturer products (Tokuyama ESTECORE/UB, Sun Medical Super-Bond).

## 7. Glossary
- **VRF**: vertical root fracture, a longitudinal fracture through the root.
- **4-META/MMA-TBB (Super-Bond)**: unfilled, chemically cured adhesive resin cement used for extraoral fragment bonding.
- **10-3 solution**: 10% citric acid with 3% ferric chloride, a dentin conditioner.
- **μTBS**: microtensile bond strength, tested on 1 mm2 beams.
- **Intentional replantation**: extraction, extraoral repair, and re-insertion of the tooth.
