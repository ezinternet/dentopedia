## Three-line Summary

Narrative review from Harvard School of Dental Medicine synthesizing the evidence on crack development mechanisms, prevention strategies, and the role of preventive endodontic interventions in reducing tooth loss attributable to dental cracks.

The review frames crack management within a preventive endodontic paradigm — emphasizing early identification, conservative intervention, and timely endodontic treatment to interrupt crack progression before irreversible pulpal compromise or tooth fracture occurs.

Key clinical recommendations include protective restoration before pulpal symptoms develop, patient risk-factor modification (bruxism, parafunction), and recognition that delayed intervention significantly worsens tooth loss outcomes in cracked teeth.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Frames dental crack management within a "preventive endodontics" framework — novel conceptual positioning
- Synthesizes crack development mechanisms (fatigue, occlusal overload, iatrogenic, material-related)
- Argues for pre-symptomatic intervention to interrupt crack → pulpitis → necrosis → fracture cascade
- Highlights bruxism/parafunction as modifiable risk factors in crack progression
- Provides evidence-based case for early cuspal coverage before endodontic involvement

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Narrative review
- Institution: Harvard School of Dental Medicine
- Scope: Crack development mechanisms, risk factors, prevention strategies, timing of endodontic intervention
- Literature coverage: Not quantified in abstract; comprehensive narrative approach

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Delayed intervention → significantly worse tooth loss outcomes (consistent with existing cohort data)
- Protective restoration before pulpal symptoms = preventive endodontic standard of care
- Bruxism, high occlusal load identified as key modifiable risk factors
- Specific effect sizes not available from abstract

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative review — selection bias possible; no PRISMA/AMSTAR methodology
- Harvard center perspective may not fully reflect general practice realities
- Preventive endodontics framework is conceptual — prospective RCT validation needed

## 6. Related Work
- Kaur 2026 umbrella review: endo + full cuspal coverage = best prognosis
- Kakka 2022: treatment outcome data (monitoring 80% stable at 3yr)
- Zhang 2024 SR+MA: delayed cuspal coverage → 11x tooth loss increase
- Chan 2025: 10–15yr survival post-RCT treatment of cracked teeth

## 7. Glossary
- Preventive endodontics: clinical philosophy prioritizing early intervention to prevent pulpal/periapical disease progression
- Pre-symptomatic intervention: restorative treatment before pulpitis symptoms develop
- Crack cascade: progressive crack → reversible pulpitis → irreversible pulpitis → necrosis → vertical root fracture/tooth loss
