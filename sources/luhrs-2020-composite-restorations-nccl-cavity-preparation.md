## Why Ingested

Requested topic: why cervical (NCCL) resin restorations come off ("서비컬 레진 탈락"). This is one of the few long-term (7.7-year) randomized comparisons of cervical cavity preparation (cleaning vs bur roughening vs groove vs flowable liner) and gives clinical support for roughening sclerotic dentin, which the wiki otherwise holds mostly as a bench SR and a guideline-type review. Reinforces [[resin-bonding/hamdi-2025-surface-pretreatments-sclerotic-dentin-bond-sr]] and [[nccl/goodacre-2022-nccl-morphology-etiology-restoration-guidelines]]; contrasts in method with the air-abrasion result in [[resin-bonding/forville-2026-air-abrasion-nccl-18m-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

Prospective randomized clinical trial of 85 Class V composite restorations in non-carious cervical lesions (NCCLs; 24 patients; enamel coronal margin, dentin cervical margin) comparing four dentin pretreatments: cleaning only (CLEAN), bur roughening + flowable (PREP_FLOW), roughening + fine groove (GROOVE), roughening + groove + flowable (GROOVE_FLOW); recall at 7.7 (± 0.35) years in 64 restorations (75.3%).

Overall retention was 82.8%; the no-preparation CLEAN group had the highest loss rate (27.8%), significantly higher than the three pooled prepared groups (p = 0.041), with no difference among the prepared groups (p = 0.328). Modified USPHS esthetics, marginal adaptation, anatomic form and marginal discoloration did not differ between groups; no secondary caries and one case of hypersensitivity.

Mechanically roughening sclerotic cervical dentin with a round bur (with or without a 0.5 mm groove or a flowable liner) appears to improve long-term retention versus cleaning alone; the study is small (per-group n = 11-18 at recall), single-center, used a three-step adhesive (Syntac) now rarely used, and rated each restoration as a whole without separating coronal and cervical margins.

## 세줄요약

비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 85개 (24명)의 5급 복합레진 수복물을 4가지 상아질 전처치 — 세정만 (CLEAN), 라운드 버 거칠기 + 플로어블 (PREP_FLOW), 거칠기 + 미세 그루브 (GROOVE), 거칠기 + 그루브 + 플로어블 (GROOVE_FLOW) — 로 무작위 배정한 전향적 무작위 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이며, 7.7 (± 0.35)년 시점에 64개 (75.3%)를 재평가했다.

전체 유지율은 82.8%였고, 전처치 없는 CLEAN군의 탈락률이 27.8%로 가장 높아 전처치 3군 합산 대비 유의하게 높았으며 (p = 0.041), 전처치 3군 사이에는 차이가 없었다 (p = 0.328). 수정 USPHS 기준 심미성·변연적합성·해부학적 형태·변연변색은 군 간 차이가 없었고 이차우식은 없었다.

경화상아질 (sclerotic dentin)을 라운드 버로 거칠게 하면 세정만 한 경우보다 장기 유지율이 높아 보이나, 군당 n = 11-18로 작은 단일기관 연구이며 3단계 접착제 (Syntac)를 썼고 치은측·교합측 변연을 구분해 평가하지 않은 한계가 있다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical and Experimental Dental Research 2020 (Oct)

- **Institution**: Hannover Medical School, Germany (ethics committee No. 4613)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Long-term (6.8-8.2 y) randomized comparison of four cervical dentin pretreatment protocols in NCCL Class V composite restorations.
- Cleaning only showed the highest loss rate (27.8%); roughening +/- groove +/- flowable were equivalent to each other.
- Notes that 0.5 mm groove and flowable liner ease handling but add no retention benefit; groove design had no prior clinical literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: prospective randomized clinical study, four groups, two blinded calibrated examiners, modified USPHS, chi-square, Cohen's kappa.
- **n**: 24 patients, 85 NCCLs; 64 (75.3%) re-examined at 7.7 (± 0.35) y.
- **Interventions**: CLEAN; PREP_FLOW (round bur roughening + flowable <= 0.5 mm); GROOVE (roughening + <= 0.5 mm groove); GROOVE_FLOW. All: enamel bevel 1-1.5 mm, selective enamel etch 36% H3PO4 30 s, Syntac three-step adhesive, Tetric EvoFlow (where used), Tetric EvoCeram <= 2 mm increments.
- **Outcomes**: retention, USPHS criteria, bleeding on probing (6 sites).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Recall n: CLEAN 12, PREP_FLOW 12, GROOVE 11, GROOVE_FLOW 18; 9 restorations lost; 2 excluded (replacement of unknown origin, partial crown).
- Overall retention 82.8%; CLEAN loss 27.8%; CLEAN vs pooled prepared groups p = 0.041; among prepared groups p = 0.328.
- USPHS Alpha (CLEAN / PREP_FLOW / GROOVE / GROOVE_FLOW): esthetics 91.7/75/100/83.3%; marginal adaptation 75/75/81.8/77.8%; anatomic form 100/91.7/90.9/88.9% (p = 0.716); marginal discoloration 83.3/58.3/81.8/66.7% (p = 0.613; one Charlie in GROOVE_FLOW 5.6%).
- No secondary caries; 1 hypersensitivity; BOP not different (p = 0.323); kappa = 1.
- Discussion benchmark: meta-analysis retention 82.6% at 5 y, 67.7% at 8 y (cited from Mahn 2015, not ingested here).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small groups, 24.7% attrition, older cohort, single center, one three-step adhesive.
- Coronal and cervical margins not rated separately.
- Chi-square only; no survival analysis; per-group loss denominators not tabulated.

## 7. Glossary
- **NCCL**: non-carious cervical lesion (erosion/abrasion/abfraction cervical defect).
- **Sclerotic dentin**: hypermineralized, tubule-obliterated dentin surface of cervical lesions with reduced bondability.
- **Modified USPHS**: Alpha/Bravo/Charlie/Delta clinical rating criteria after Cvar and Ryge.
- **Flowable liner**: thin low-viscosity composite layer (<= 0.5 mm) at the cervical area before the packable composite.
- **Groove**: fine retentive/defining cervical groove (max 0.5 mm) prepared with a size 010 round bur.
