## Why Ingested
CBCT/CT 기반 치조골 기하학 측정값의 메타분석으로 정성적 골질 분류에 정량적 근거를 제공 — [[overviews/bone-quality-implant-risk-modification-overview]] 및 [[bone-biology/palominozorrilla-2024-jawbone-quality-classification-scoping]]의 "골질 분류 검증 부재" 문제에 대한 정량화 접근의 보완.

## Three-line Summary
Meta-analysis (30 CBCT/CT studies, random effects model, PRIAMA guidelines): quantifies alveolar ridge geometry (ridge width, buccal/palatal cortical thickness) by jaw, region, and dentate state to construct a geometry-based bone classification that numerically maps onto Lekholm-Zarb Types I–IV.
Meta-regression confirmed significant variation across anatomical regions, jaw (maxilla vs mandible), and dentate vs edentulous state; quantitative thresholds from combined means enable objective bone-type prediction without subjective assessment; histomorphometry and HU density data were excluded.
First step toward replacing subjective bone-type classification with measurable CBCT geometry — but validated only against existing qualitative systems, not against implant outcomes (IT, ISQ, survival), leaving the full clinical utility chain incomplete.

## 세줄요약
메타분석(30편, CBCT/CT 중심, 랜덤효과, PRIAMA): 치조골 기하학적 측정값(능선 폭, 협측·구개/설측 피질 두께)을 부위·악골·치아 유무별로 정량화, 렉홀름-자르브 I-IV형에 수치 매핑.
해부 부위·악골·치아 유무에 따른 유의한 변동 확인(메타회귀); 통합 평균에서 정량 임계치 도출로 주관적 평가 없는 골 유형 예측 가능; 조직형태계측·HU 밀도는 미포함.
CBCT 기하학 측정으로 주관적 분류를 대체하는 첫 단계이나, 기존 정성적 체계와만 대조하고 임상 결과(IT·ISQ·생존율) 검증 없어 임상 유용성 사슬이 미완.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First meta-analysis quantifying geometric parameters of alveolar bone (ridge width, cortical thickness, cancellous dimensions) across dentate/edentulous states
- Constructs geometry-based quantitative bone classification from combined mean alveolar ridge data
- Demonstrates quantitative classification matches existing qualitative classifications (Lekholm-Zarb, Misch)
- Identifies key morphometric parameters: ridge width (RW), buccal cortical width (BW), palatal/lingual width (PW/LW)
- Meta-regression identifies differences by jaw region (anterior/premolar/posterior), jaw (maxilla/mandible), and dentate vs edentulous state

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Meta-analysis with random effects model; PRIAMA guidelines
- PECO framework: Human patients (live/cadaver), anatomical factors, outcomes = linear bone dimensions (mm)
- Databases: PubMed (MEDLINE) + EMBASE; no time restriction; last search August 2020
- Inclusion: CBCT/CT studies reporting RW, BW, PW/LW with jaw and region specified
- Exclusion criteria: non-human; sample <5; no anatomical region specification
- n=30 studies included; majority (27/30) live human subjects + CBCT
- Meta-regression: differences by region, jaw, dentate/edentulous state

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Combined means for alveolar ridge dimensions constructed per jaw region (anterior/premolar/posterior) and state (dentate/edentulous)
- Quantitative geometry-based classification proposed from these combined means — matches Type I-IV qualitative classification
- Meta-regression: significant differences across anatomical regions and dentate vs edentulous states
- Cortical thickness and trabecular bone ratio vary systematically by region
- Specific quantitative thresholds allow bone type prediction without subjective assessment

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=30 studies — heterogeneous in scanner settings, population demographics, measurement protocols
- CBCT voxel size and reconstruction settings not standardized across studies
- Does not include histomorphometry or density (HU) parameters — geometric only
- Single author study — no blinded reviewer (data extraction)
- Classification validation against clinical outcomes (IT, ISQ, survival) not performed

## 6. Related Work
- Lekholm & Zarb 1985: original qualitative classification (Type I-IV) — what this study tries to quantify
- Misch D1-D4: density-based classification — compared in this study
- Palomino-Zorrilla 2024: scoping review finding no validated classification — this study addresses one dimension of that gap
- Chatvaratthana et al.: cortical-cancellous ratio approach to D1-D4 mapping

## 7. Glossary
- **PRIAMA**: guidelines for meta-analysis of anatomical studies (similar role to PRISMA)
- **Ridge Width (RW)**: total alveolar ridge width at crest
- **Buccal cortical width (BW)**: thickness of the buccal cortical plate
- **Palatal/lingual width (PW/LW)**: thickness of palatal (maxilla) or lingual (mandible) cortical plate
- **Meta-regression**: statistical analysis to explain heterogeneity between studies by covariates (region, jaw, state)
- **Random effects model**: accounts for between-study variability in meta-analysis
