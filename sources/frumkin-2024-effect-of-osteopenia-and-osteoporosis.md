## Three-line Summary

Single-clinic retrospective study using two randomly sampled cohorts: 112 patients with at least one implant failure (1990-2020; 94 received a second implant) for second-failure risk, and 152 patients over 50 years (implants 1994-2018; 1229 implants, 134 failed, 89% survival) for first-failure risk.

Osteopenia or osteoporosis (pooled as one positive category) was associated with second implant failure (p = 0.044 vs healthy; p = 0.0353 vs no OPN/OP), first implant failure (chi-square p = 0.0202; multiple logistic regression p = 0.0065), and early failure (<1 year; p = 0.0044), while diabetes predicted first (regression p = 0.0297) but not second failure.

The text reports only p-values (no effect sizes or confidence intervals), pools OPN and OP, is heavily female (34 of 38 OPN/OP patients), and gives inconsistent cohort sizes (152 vs 146), so the association is supportive but cannot be translated into a magnitude.

## 세줄요약

단일 치과의원의 후향적 연구로, 무작위 추출한 두 코호트를 사용했다: 임플란트 실패 이력 환자 112명 (1990-2020, 94명이 재식립)은 두 번째 실패 위험, 50세 초과 152명 (1994-2018 식립, 임플란트 1229개 중 134개 실패, 생존율 89%)은 첫 번째 실패 위험 분석에 쓰였다.

골감소증 (osteopenia, OPN) 또는 골다공증 (osteoporosis, OP)은 하나의 양성 범주로 합쳐 분석되었으며 두 번째 실패 (건강군 대비 p = 0.044, OPN/OP 없는 군 대비 p = 0.0353), 첫 번째 실패 (카이제곱 p = 0.0202, 다중 로지스틱 회귀 p = 0.0065), 조기 실패 (식립 후 1년 미만, p = 0.0044)와 연관되었고, 당뇨는 첫 번째 실패 (회귀 p = 0.0297)만 예측하고 두 번째 실패는 예측하지 못했다.

본문에는 p값만 있고 효과크기·신뢰구간이 없으며 OPN과 OP를 합쳤고 OPN/OP 38명 중 34명이 여성이며 코호트 크기 기술이 불일치 (152명 대 146명)하므로, 연관성은 지지되나 크기를 임상 수치로 옮길 수는 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- OPN/OP associated with second implant failure (p = 0.044 vs healthy, p = 0.0353 vs no OPN/OP) in 112 patients with prior failure.
- OPN/OP associated with first implant failure (chi-square p = 0.0202; logistic regression p = 0.0065) in patients over 50 years; diabetes also associated (p = 0.0251; regression p = 0.0297) but not with second failure.
- Higher proportion of early failures (<1 year) in OPN/OP patients (p = 0.0044).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective observational archival study, STROBE, single clinic, consent for anonymous use of data; ethics review not required beyond that stated.
- **Sampling**: finite-population formula with 5% margin of error; population 6000 clinic patients; assumed failure proportions 7% (second-failure study; n = 86, raised 20% to 103) and 9.5% (first-failure study; n = 130, raised 20% to 156); analysed 94 (second) and 152 (first) patients.
- **Cohorts**: second-failure: 112 patients with at least one failure 1990-2020, 94 got a second implant; first-failure: patients over 50 years with implants 1994-2018, 1229 implants.
- **Exposure**: OPN/OP from health questionnaire, confirmed by physician reports, pooled as binary.
- **Implants**: endosteal titanium; 87% tapered internal hex (Tapered-Screw Vent, Legacy, Spiral), 5.5% spline external, 7.5% straight-body internal hex; length 11.5-16 mm.
- **Outcome**: implant failure (binary); early failure = under 1 year after implantation.
- **Statistics**: chi-square, Fisher exact if n < 6, multiple logistic regression (95% CI used for significance), Graph Pad Prism.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single clinic, retrospective; one principal operator.
- Only p-values reported; no ORs, HRs, or CIs in the text; tables absent from the text file.
- OPN and OP pooled; no density data, no medication (anti-resorptive) analysis.
- 34 of 38 OPN/OP patients female; no sex-stratified analysis.
- Inconsistencies: first-failure cohort 152 (Methods) vs 146 (Results) vs planned 156; the "1st failure analysis" description says patients over 50 but the cohort text for the second-failure study says no age limit.
- Early failure analysis is among failed implants only (early vs late), not a rate comparison.
- Authors call for studies of implant design as a confounder and note the mechanism (RANKL/OPG, bone turnover markers) is speculative.

## 7. Glossary

- **OPN (Osteopenia, 골감소증)**: milder reduction in bone density, precursor of osteoporosis.
- **OP (Osteoporosis, 골다공증)**: markedly reduced bone density with impaired microarchitecture and fracture risk.
- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: here, failure under 1 year after implantation.
- **RANKL/OPG**: ligand/decoy-receptor ratio regulating bone remodeling.
