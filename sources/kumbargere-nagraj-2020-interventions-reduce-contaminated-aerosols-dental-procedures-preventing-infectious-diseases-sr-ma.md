## Why Ingested

This Cochrane SR provides the highest-level synthesis of evidence on physical and antimicrobial interventions (HVE, rubber dam, antimicrobial coolants) to reduce aerosol contamination during dental procedures, directly relevant to dental infection-control protocol design. Despite very low certainty evidence, it remains the reference standard for aerosol mitigation, extending the mouthwash-specific findings of [[infection-control/marui-2019-preprocedural-mouthrinse-aerosol-sr-ma]] to the full spectrum of aerosol reduction strategies.

## Three-line Summary

This Cochrane systematic review and meta-analysis included 16 studies (425 participants) to assess the effectiveness of methods to reduce contaminated aerosols during dental procedures for preventing infectious diseases. The review found no studies that evaluated disease transmission via aerosols and all evidence was based on surrogate outcomes (bacterial colony-forming units, CFU).

For high-volume evacuation (HVE), a meta-analysis of three split-mouth RCTs (122 participants) showed a reduction in bacterial contamination at less than one foot from the patient's mouth (MD -47.41 CFU, 95% CI -92.76 to -2.06, I²=95%) but not at longer distances. Rubber dam use showed a reduction at one meter (MD -16.20 CFU, 95% CI -19.36 to -13.04, one RCT, 10 participants) and two meters (MD -11.70, 95% CI -15.82 to -7.58) from the mouth. Antimicrobial coolants (chlorhexidine, povidone iodine, cinnamon extract) reduced CFU compared to water.

The overall certainty of evidence was very low for all interventions due to risk of bias, inconsistency, and imprecision. No studies measured infection transmission or viral contamination. The authors concluded that while interventions may reduce bacterial contamination, reliable conclusions cannot be drawn. High-quality RCTs measuring direct outcomes (infection rates, viable particles) are needed.

## 세줄요약

이 코크란 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석은 치과 시술 중 오염된 에어로졸을 줄여 감염성 질환을 예방하는 방법의 효과를 평가하기 위해 16개 연구(참가자 425명)를 분석했다. 본 고찰에서는 에어로졸을 통한 질병 전파를 평가한 연구는 없었으며, 모든 근거는 대리 결과물(세균 집락형성단위, CFU)에 기반했다.

고용량 흡입기(HVE)의 경우, 3개 분할구강 무작위배정 임상시험(참가자 122명)의 메타분석에서 환자 구강에서 1피트(약 30cm) 미만 거리에서 세균 오염 감소를 보였으나(MD -47.41 CFU, 95% CI -92.76 to -2.06, I²=95%), 더 먼 거리에서는 효과가 없었다. 러버댐 사용은 구강에서 1미터(MD -16.20 CFU, 95% CI -19.36 to -13.04, RCT 1개, 참가자 10명) 및 2미터(MD -11.70, 95% CI -15.82 to -7.58) 거리에서 감소를 보였다. 항균 쿨런트(클로르헥시딘, 포비돈-아이오딘, 계피 추출물)는 물과 비교하여 CFU를 감소시켰다.

모든 중재에 대한 근거의 확실성은 비뚤림 위험, 불일치 및 부정확성으로 인해 매우 낮았다. 감염 전파나 바이러스 오염을 측정한 연구는 없었다. 저자들은 중재가 세균 오염을 감소시킬 수 있지만 신뢰할 수 있는 결론을 도출할 수 없다고 결론지었다. 직접적인 결과(감염률, 생존 가능한 입자)를 측정하는 고품질의 무작위배정 임상시험이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First Cochrane systematic review evaluating interventions to reduce contaminated aerosols during dental procedures specifically in the context of infectious disease prevention, including COVID-19.
- Comprehensive search across multiple databases (up to September 2020) with no language restrictions, identifying 16 eligible studies.
- No included studies measured the primary outcome of infection incidence in dental staff or patients, highlighting a critical evidence gap.
- All evidence was based on surrogate outcomes (bacterial CFU) with very low certainty, showing potential benefits from HVE (MD -47.41 CFU, 95% CI -92.76 to -2.06, I²=95%), rubber dam (MD -16.20 CFU, 95% CI -19.36 to -13.04), and antimicrobial coolants (chlorhexidine: MD -124.00, 95% CI -135.78 to -112.22; povidone iodine: MD -656.45, 95% CI -672.74 to -640.16; cinnamon: MD -644.55, 95% CI -668.70 to -620.40) compared to controls.
- Identified major gaps in research on other potential interventions (ventilation, ionization, ozonisation, UV light, fogging).
- Provided clear GRADE assessments for all comparisons, uniformly downgraded to very low certainty.
- Concluded that well-designed RCTs with direct outcome measurements (infection transmission, viable particles) are urgently needed.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis (Rapid Review)
- Search: Cochrane Oral Health's Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE (1946), Embase (1980), WHO COVID-19 Global literature database, ClinicalTrials.gov, Cochrane COVID-19 Study Register. Search date: 17 September 2020. No language restrictions.
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs and CCTs on aerosol-generating procedures (AGPs) performed by dental healthcare providers, evaluating methods to reduce contaminated aerosols in dental clinics (excluding preprocedural mouthrinses).
- Exclusion criteria: Laboratory studies with mannequins not including real patients.
- Studies included: 16 studies (11 RCTs, 5 CCTs) with 425 participants. Sample sizes ranged from 1 to 80.
- Risk of bias: Cochrane Risk of Bias tool for RCTs; for CCTs, randomization and allocation domains rated as high risk.
- Data synthesis: Random-effects meta-analysis using mean differences (MD) and 95% confidence intervals (CI). Generic inverse variance method for split-mouth studies. Heterogeneity assessed via I² statistic.
- GRADE: Used to assess certainty of evidence for primary outcomes in 'Summary of findings' tables.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome (Infection incidence) —** Not reported in any included study.
- **Primary outcome (Reduction in contamination, HVE vs no HVE at <1 foot) —** MD -47.41 CFU (95% CI -92.76 to -2.06, I²=95%, 3 split-mouth RCTs, 122 participants). This evidence was rated VERY LOW.
- **Primary outcome (Reduction in contamination, Rubber dam vs no rubber dam at 1 meter) —** MD -16.20 CFU (95% CI -19.36 to -13.04, 1 RCT, 10 participants). VERY LOW certainty.
- **Primary outcome (Reduction in contamination, Antimicrobial coolant vs water) —** Chlorhexidine: MD -124.00 CFU (95% CI -135.78 to -112.22, 1 RCT, 20 participants). Povidone iodine: MD -656.45 CFU (95% CI -672.74 to -640.16, 1 RCT, 40 participants). Cinnamon extract: MD -644.55 CFU (95% CI -668.70 to -620.40, 1 RCT, 40 participants). VERY LOW certainty.
- **Secondary outcomes —** Cost, acceptability, and feasibility were not reported in any included study.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No studies measured the primary outcome of disease transmission or infection rates, relying solely on a surrogate (bacterial CFU) that does not capture viral or fungal contamination.
- All evidence was rated VERY LOW certainty due to high/unclear risk of bias, substantial heterogeneity (particularly in HVE comparisons, I²=95%), small sample sizes, and wide confidence intervals.
- Minimal clinically important difference (MCID) for CFU reduction is unknown, making it difficult to interpret clinical relevance.
- Most comparisons based on single trials; limited possibility for meta-analysis.
- Lack of studies on many important interventions (ventilation, ionization, ozonisation, UV light, fogging).
- Did not include preprocedural mouthrinses or personal protective equipment (PPE), which are addressed in separate Cochrane reviews.
- Need for well-designed RCTs that measure direct outcomes (infection transmission, viable particles in aerosols with small particle size, patient-related outcomes, cost-effectiveness) and follow CONSORT 2010 guidelines.

## 7. Glossary

- **AGP (Aerosol-Generating Procedure)**: 에어로졸 발생 시술. Dental procedures that produce aerosols, such as using high-speed handpieces, ultrasonic scalers, air polishing, etc.
- **CFU (Colony-Forming Unit)**: 집락형성단위. A measure of viable bacterial or fungal cells, used as a surrogate outcome for aerosol contamination.
- **CCT (Controlled Clinical Trial)**: 대조 임상시험. A clinical trial that includes a control group but lacks random assignment to groups.
- **GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation)**: 근거의 확실성 평가 시스템. A systematic approach to rating the certainty of evidence in systematic reviews.
- **HEPA (High-Efficiency Particulate Air) Filter**: 고효율 미립자 공기 필터. A type of air filter that removes at least 99.97% of particles 0.3 μm in diameter.
- **HVE (High-Volume Evacuator)**: 고용량 흡입기. A suction device with a large opening (≥8mm) that removes up to 2.8 cubic meters of air per minute to capture aerosols.
- **RCT (Randomized Controlled Trial)**: 무작위배정 임상시험. A study design where participants are randomly assigned to intervention or control groups.
