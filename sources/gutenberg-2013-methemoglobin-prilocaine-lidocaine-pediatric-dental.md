## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=90 children aged 3–6 years undergoing general anesthesia for comprehensive dental rehabilitation; Anesth Prog 2013) comparing peak methemoglobin levels (SpMET) after prilocaine 4% plain 5 mg/kg vs lidocaine 2% with epinephrine 2.5 mg/kg vs no local anesthetic.

Prilocaine produced significantly higher peak SpMET (3.55%) than lidocaine (1.63%) and control (1.60%), with no statistically significant difference between lidocaine and control; peak SpMET occurred later after LA administration than no-LA (prilocaine 62.73 min; lidocaine 57.50 min; no LA 29.50 min).

All observed SpMET levels remained below the clinically symptomatic threshold (~10–15%), but this provides quantitative evidence that prilocaine produces approximately 2× the methemoglobin burden of lidocaine in pediatric dental patients — relevant when choosing LA in hypoxic or pulmonary-compromised children.

## 세줄요약

RCT (n=90, 3–6세, 전신마취 하 치과; Anesth Prog 2013) — 프릴로카인 4% plain 5 mg/kg vs 리도케인 2% + 에피네프린 2.5 mg/kg vs 무마취 3군 비교; 메트헤모글로빈 측정 (Masimo Radical-7).

피크 SpMET: 프릴로카인 3.55% > 리도케인 1.63% ≈ 무마취 1.60%; 피크 도달 시간: 무마취 29.5분 < 리도케인 57.5분 = 프릴로카인 62.7분.

임상 증상 역치(~10–15%) 미만이나 소아에서 프릴로카인이 리도케인 대비 ~2배 메트헤모글로빈 부담; 저산소증·폐질환 소아에서 프릴로카인 선택 시 특별 주의.
