## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=997 PFM precious alloy SCs; 1984–2009; median follow-up 4.33 years; Kaplan-Meier) documenting long-term clinical performance of PFM single crowns including chipping, recurrent caries, periodontitis, and loss of retention rates.

5-year survival: anterior 96.4%, posterior 97.5%; 10-year: anterior 92.3%, posterior 95.9%; chipping rare (1.7%, mainly early); periodontitis dominant long-term risk (10-year free-of-PE rate 72.2%).

PFM crowns demonstrate excellent long-term survival; technical complications (chipping, loss of retention) occur predominantly in early years; periodontitis risk accumulates continuously with age and service duration regardless of crown type.

## 세줄요약

PFM 귀금속 단단관 997개 후향 코호트(1984–2009, 중위 추적 4.33년): 5년 생존율 전치 96.4%·구치 97.5%, 10년 전치 92.3%·구치 95.9%.

치핑 1.7%·재발성 우식 1.3%·치주염 14.4% — 10년 치주염 무이벤트율 72.2%로 장기 사용 시 치주 합병증이 지배적 위험.

PFM 크라운 장기 생존율 우수; 기술적 합병증 초기 집중, 치주 위험은 사용 기간 비례 증가.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=997 PFM SCs; 1984–2009) reporting 5-year survival of 96.4% (anterior) and 97.5% (posterior), with chipping 1.7%, recurrent caries 1.3%, and periodontitis 14.4% as main complications.

## 한줄요약

후향 코호트(PFM 단단관 997개, 1984–2009): 5년 생존율 전치 96.4%·구치 97.5%, 치핑 1.7%·재발성 우식 1.3%·치주염 14.4% — 장기 사용으로 치주 합병증 위험 증가.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: International Journal of Prosthodontics, 27(2), March/April 2014, Pages 153–160
- Institution: University Medical Center Regensburg, Germany
- PMID: 24596914

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest single-center PFM crown cohort study (n=997) with up to 25-year follow-up
- Established chipping rate (1.7%) and loss of retention (LR) rate for PFM as significantly lower than reported for all-ceramic zirconia
- Documented that periodontitis (PE) is the dominant long-term risk (14.4%), increasing with service time
- Identified bruxism, antagonist type, and crown location as risk factors for chipping

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** Retrospective cohort
- **Sample:** 997 PFM precious alloy single crowns, standardized protocol
- **Period:** January 1984 – May 2009
- **Follow-up:** Median 4.33 years (inverse Kaplan-Meier method)
- **Outcomes:** Chipping/facing failure, recurrent caries (RC), periodontitis (PE), loss of retention (LR)
- **Analysis:** Kaplan-Meier survival curves; risk factors: bruxism, antagonist type, crown location

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | 5-year rate | 10-year rate |
|---|---|---|
| Survival (anterior) | 96.4% | 92.3% |
| Survival (posterior) | 97.5% | 95.9% |
| Free of chipping (anterior) | 98.9% | 98.9% |
| Free of chipping (posterior) | 98.2% | 97.3% |
| Free of recurrent caries | 98.7% | 97.2% |
| Free of periodontitis | 85.8% | 72.2% |
| Free of loss of retention (anterior) | 92.2% | 92.2% |
| Free of loss of retention (posterior) | 97.1% | 97.1% |

- Chipping/LR predominantly early (first few years); PE risk increases continuously over time
