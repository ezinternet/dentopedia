## Why Ingested

The wiki holds several peri-implant dehiscence GBR trials that compare *which material* fills the defect at 6 months to 1 year (e.g. [[wiki/bone-regeneration/cha-2024-gbr-dehiscence-synthetic-vs-bovine-rct]], DBBM vs synthetic) and an ex vivo study showing that particulate graft is displaced 15-40% during primary wound closure ([[wiki/bone-regeneration/raabe-2025-defect-morphology-membrane-fixation-graft]]), but no long-term (5-year) clinical data on whether a space-maintaining block form of DBBM keeps its advantage over particulate DBBM. This 5-year RCT fills that gap on the graft-*form* axis and adds a clinical outcome (buccal hard-tissue thickness on CBCT) to the mechanical-collapse rationale in the Raabe study.

## Three-line Summary

5-year follow-up of a two-arm parallel RCT (24 patients analyzed at 6 months, 22 at 5 years) of simultaneous GBR for buccal peri-implant dehiscence defects (vertical height >= 3 mm) in single-tooth implant sites, comparing an individually shaped deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM) block versus particulate DBBM, both under a native collagen membrane with pin fixation.

At 5 years mean buccal horizontal hard-tissue thickness at the implant shoulder was 1.36 +/- 1.16 mm (block) vs 0.07 +/- 0.16 mm (particulate) (adjusted mean difference -1.2 mm, 95% CI -1.9 to -0.6, p < 0.001); 45-degree thickness 0.34 vs 0.00 mm (difference -0.3 mm, p = 0.002); marginal bone level, bleeding on probing, plaque and implant survival were comparable, with no implant loss or peri-implantitis.

The block preserved more buccal contour long-term but still lost thickness (the particulate arm lost nearly all of it during the first 6 months), so the benefit is relative, not absolute; the trial was small (below the planned 34 patients), CBCT-based, and had no soft-tissue thickness or esthetic data.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 협측 치조골 열개 결손(수직 높이 3 mm 이상)이 있는 단일 임플란트 부위에서 동시 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)을 시행한 2군 평행 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)의 5년 추적 연구; 맞춤 성형한 탈단백 우골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 블록 대 입자형 DBBM 비교(6개월 24명 분석, 5년 22명).

줄2: 5년째 임플란트 어깨 높이의 협측 수평 경조직 두께는 블록군 1.36 +/- 1.16 mm, 입자군 0.07 +/- 0.16 mm(보정 평균차 -1.2 mm, 95% CI -1.9 ~ -0.6, p < 0.001); 45도 두께 0.34 대 0.00 mm(p = 0.002); 변연골 수준·치은 출혈·플라크·임플란트 생존은 군간 유사, 임플란트 상실·임플란트 주위염 없음.

줄3: 블록은 협측 윤곽을 더 오래 유지했지만 두께는 여전히 감소했고(입자군은 첫 6개월에 대부분 소실) 이점은 상대적이다; 표본이 계획(34명)보다 작고 CBCT 기반이며 연조직 두께·심미 평가가 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 5-year clinical data (the authors state none existed) on DBBM block versus particulate DBBM for GBR of peri-implant dehiscence defects (follow-up of the 6-month RCT by Benic et al. 2019, cited in the paper).
- Shows the block advantage in buccal hard-tissue thickness persists to 5 years at both the implant shoulder and the 45-degree corono-buccal position.
- Describes different time courses: particulate lost most thickness during early healing (then plateaued); block resisted early collapse but remodeled gradually afterwards.
- Reports that peri-implant health and implant survival were similar despite near-complete loss of the buccal bone plate in the particulate arm (no peri-implantitis in either arm).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: two-arm parallel RCT, 5-year follow-up of a previously published trial; single center.
- **Population**: adults (>= 18 y) needing a single-tooth implant with a buccal dehiscence defect (vertical height >= 3 mm) at placement; good general and periodontal health, full-mouth plaque and bleeding scores < 25%, probing depths < 4 mm; exclusions included systemic conditions affecting healing, pregnancy/breastfeeding, active periodontitis, smoking > 15 cigarettes/day.
- **Surgery**: full-thickness flap; Astra Tech OsseoSpeed EV implant in a prosthetically ideal position; defect measured with a calibrated probe; random allocation to particulate DBBM (Bio-Oss spongiosa, 0.25-1 mm) or an extraorally, individually shaped DBBM block (Bio-Oss block); native bilayer collagen membrane (Bio-Gide) overlapping the defect >= 2 mm, fixed with resorbable polylactide pins; re-entry at 6 months; screw-retained monolithic zirconia crown on titanium base; structured maintenance program.
- **Primary outcome**: buccal hard-tissue thickness on CBCT (post-op, 6 months, 5 years) - horizontal thickness (HT) at the implant shoulder and 45-degree thickness (45T), measured on the mid-buccal cross-section.
- **Secondary outcomes**: marginal bone level (periapical radiographs, ImageJ, first bone-to-implant contact), probing depth, bleeding on probing, plaque, keratinized mucosa width, implant survival, peri-implant conditions (2017 World Workshop case definitions).
- **Statistics**: linear mixed-effects models (group, time, interaction; patient random effect); GEE if assumptions failed; alpha 0.05; Stata v19.5.
- **Sample size**: 17 per group (34 total) planned to detect a 0.5 mm difference (SD 0.5 mm, 80% power); considered exploratory because no prior data for this material combination.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Flow**: 24 patients analyzed at 6 months; 22 completed 5 years (one particulate-group patient unreachable, one block-group patient died).
- **Horizontal thickness (block vs particulate, mean (SD) mm; adjusted difference (95% CI))**:
  - Post-op: 3.37 (0.61) vs 2.72 (0.72); -0.6 (-1.3; 0.3), p = 0.063
  - 6 months: 2.69 (1.25) vs 0.39 (0.70); -2.3 (-2.9; -1.6), p < 0.001
  - 5 years: 1.36 (1.16) vs 0.07 (0.16); -1.2 (-1.9; -0.6), p < 0.001
- **45-degree thickness (block vs particulate)**:
  - Post-op: 2.40 (0.57) vs 1.91 (0.70); -0.4 (-1.0; 0.0), p = 0.067
  - 6 months: 1.53 (0.93) vs 0.21 (0.53); -1.3 (-1.9; -0.6), p < 0.001
  - 5 years: 0.34 (0.38) vs 0.00 (0.00); -0.3 (-0.5; -0.1), p = 0.002
- **Change over time**: horizontal reduction post-op to 5 years -2.01 mm (block) vs -2.65 mm (particulate); from 6 months to 5 years -1.33 mm (block) vs -0.32 mm (particulate).
- **Marginal bone level**: 0.01 +/- 0.03 mm in both arms at 6 months; at 5 years 0.29 +/- 0.33 mm (block) vs 0.07 +/- 0.13 mm (particulate), adjusted difference -0.22 mm (95% CI -0.43 to 0.00), p = 0.051.
- **Clinical parameters**: BOP 5 years 0.21 vs 0.19 (p = 0.840); plaque 0.06 vs 0.06 (p = 1.000); probing depth at 5 years 3.25 (0.47) vs 2.68 (0.63) mm, adjusted difference -0.58 mm (95% CI -1.07 to -0.08), p = 0.024, favoring particulate (no significant group-by-time interaction, p = 0.16); keratinized mucosa 5 years 1.81 vs 2.27 mm (p = 0.267).
- **Peri-implant conditions**: mucositis 9/22 (40.9%) block vs 8/21 (38.1%) particulate; no peri-implantitis; no implant loss.
- **Complications**: two block-group cases of late graft separation (resembling sequestra), removed without compromising implant stability or function.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample, below the planned 34 patients (24 analyzed at 6 months, 22 at 5 years); sample size considered exploratory.
- CBCT artefacts may over- or underestimate bone; mucosal thickness could not be quantified (no soft-tissue retraction lip during scanning) and gingival biotype was not recorded at baseline.
- No objective esthetic evaluation (no Pink Esthetic Score).
- Post-op thickness was higher in the block arm at baseline (3.37 vs 2.72 mm horizontal, p = 0.063), so part of the 5-year gap reflects a larger starting volume.
- Internal inconsistencies noted on reading: (a) the paper says the horizontal reduction difference (-2.01 vs -2.65 mm; a 0.64 mm difference) "corresponds to" the adjusted effect of -1.2 mm, which is the 5-year between-arm difference, not the difference in change; (b) the keratinized-mucosa difference is described as significant at 6 months (p = 0.048) in the text but the table row labelled 1 year shows p = 0.050 and no 6-month row; (c) the mucositis denominators (22 and 21) exceed the 22 completers in total, so the unit (patient vs implant/site) is unclear. Text figures were used as printed.
- Authors call for future work on surgical time, cost, patient-reported outcomes and longer follow-up.

## 6. Related Work
- Benic et al. 2019 (the 6-month RCT this paper follows up): greater buccal horizontal thickness with DBBM block at 6 months - cited in the paper; not held in the wiki.
- Naenni 2020, Strauss 2025 (titanium-reinforced non-resorbable membranes more stable than collagen) and Jung 2020 (polyethylene glycol membrane with early graft collapse): cited by the authors as the same space-maintenance principle acting through the membrane rather than the graft.
- [[wiki/bone-regeneration/raabe-2025-defect-morphology-membrane-fixation-graft]]: ex vivo graft displacement during wound closure; pin fixation reduced it.

## 7. Glossary
- **DBBM**: deproteinized bovine bone mineral, a slowly remodeling xenograft (here Bio-Oss particulate vs Bio-Oss block).
- **GBR**: guided bone regeneration - membrane-protected space for bone regrowth.
- **HT / 45T**: horizontal thickness at the implant shoulder / thickness along a 45-degree line coronal to the implant axis (corono-buccal contour), measured on CBCT.
- **Dehiscence defect**: exposed implant surface on the buccal side at placement because the buccal bone wall is missing.
- **Non-contained (one-wall) defect**: defect with few residual bony walls, relying on the graft and membrane for mechanical space maintenance.
