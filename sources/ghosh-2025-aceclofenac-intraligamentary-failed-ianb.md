## Why Ingested

This paper addresses the practical clinical protocol for managing failed 하악 치조신경 차단 마취 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) in 불가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) — the most common cause of "마취가 안 되는 뜨거운 치아 (hot tooth)" in endodontic practice. It is the first RCT to compare aceclofenac premedication combined with articaine vs mepivacaine intraligamentary injection in a 2×2 factorial design, providing direct protocol guidance. It reinforces and extends the articaine-vs-mepivacaine debate opened in → [[local-anesthesia/alkandari-2024-articaine-versus-mepivacaine-ianb-irreversible-pulpitis]] and adds the NSAID premedication dimension covered at the umbrella level by → [[local-anesthesia/khademi-2023-premedication-inferior-alveolar-nerve-block-pulpitis-umbrella]].

## Three-line Summary

Prospective, randomised, double-blinded RCT in 126 mandibular molar patients with symptomatic irreversible pulpitis and confirmed failed primary IANB (85% failure rate), randomised 2×2 to aceclofenac/placebo premedication × articaine/mepivacaine intraligamentary injection.
Aceclofenac + 4% articaine intraligamentary achieved 87.1% success vs 66.7% (placebo + articaine), 60.6% (aceclofenac + mepivacaine), and 53.1% (placebo + mepivacaine); articaine groups were significantly superior to mepivacaine groups (p<0.05); aceclofenac augmented efficacy only in the articaine group (p=0.048, NSD for mepivacaine p=0.595).
Best-evidence supplemental protocol for failed IANB in SIP is aceclofenac premedication + 4% articaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine intraligamentary injection; mepivacaine without epinephrine remains inferior even with premedication.

## 세줄요약

불가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) 하악 대구치에서 1차 하악 치조신경 차단 마취 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 실패(85%) 후 126명을 2×2 무작위 이중맹검으로 배정 — 아세클로페낙 (Aceclofenac)/위약 × 아티카인 (Articaine)/메피바카인 (Mepivacaine) 치주인대 주사 (Intraligamentary Injection, IL).
아세클로페낙 + 4% 아티카인 치주인대주사 87.1% 성공, 위약 + 아티카인 66.7%, 아세클로페낙 + 메피바카인 60.6%, 위약 + 메피바카인 53.1%; 아티카인군이 메피바카인군보다 유의하게 우수(p<0.05), 아세클로페낙은 아티카인군에서만 유의미한 향상(p=0.048).
실패한 IANB의 최선 보완 프로토콜: 아세클로페낙 전처치 + 4% 아티카인 (1:100,000 에피네프린) 치주인대주사 — 에피네프린 없는 메피바카인은 전처치 추가에도 열등함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First 2×2 factorial RCT isolating both NSAID premedication (aceclofenac) and intraligamentary anesthetic agent (articaine vs mepivacaine) effects on failed-IANB rescue in SIP
- Establishes that aceclofenac premedication provides additive benefit only when paired with an epinephrine-containing articaine IL injection — not with plain mepivacaine
- Confirms an 85% primary IANB failure rate in SIP mandibular molars, consistent with the range from prior literature (70–81%)
- Demonstrates that intraligamentary 4% articaine with epinephrine significantly outperforms plain 3% mepivacaine as supplemental injection (77% vs 56.9%, p<0.05)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, randomised, parallel, double-blind RCT
- **Setting**: JSS Dental College and Hospital, Mysore, India (September 2023 – February 2024)
- **Registration**: CTRI/2023/08/056538
- **n**: 126 patients after confirmed failed IANB (of 148 with lip numbness; 85% IANB failure rate)
- **Primary IANB**: 1.8 mL 2% lidocaine with 1:80,000 epinephrine (Halsted approach); 15 min wait; lip numbness confirmed
- **Premedication**: 100 mg aceclofenac or matched placebo, administered 15 min before IANB
- **IL injection**: 0.6 mL per root (3 × 0.2 mL via pressure syringe, 30-G 12-mm needle at mesiobuccal and distobuccal line angles)
- **Agents**: 4% articaine 1:100,000 epinephrine (Septanest) or 3% plain mepivacaine (Scandonest)
- **Primary outcome**: 헤프트-파커 시각통증척도 (Heft-Parker Visual Analogue Scale, VAS) ≤54 mm during access preparation and instrumentation = success
- **Analysis**: Pearson's chi-square test, independent t-test

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Premedication | IL Agent | Success Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Aceclofenac | 4% Articaine + epi | 87.1% |
| Group 2 | Placebo | 4% Articaine + epi | 66.7% |
| Group 3 | Aceclofenac | 3% Plain Mepivacaine | 60.6% |
| Group 4 | Placebo | 3% Plain Mepivacaine | 53.1% |
| Articaine groups (1+2) | — | 4% Articaine | 77.0% |
| Mepivacaine groups (3+4) | — | 3% Mepivacaine | 56.9% |

- Primary IANB success rate: 15% (22/148)
- Group 1 vs Group 2: p = 0.048 (significant)
- Group 1 vs Group 3: p = 0.02 (significant)
- Group 3 vs Group 4: p = 0.595 (not significant)
- Articaine vs mepivacaine overall: p < 0.05

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Epinephrine concentration confound: articaine contains 1:100,000 epinephrine while mepivacaine is plain — vasoconstriction effect cannot be separated from local anesthetic agent effect
- Post-operative pain not recorded; long-term outcomes unknown
- Single-center study (India); patient demographics may limit generalizability
- Aceclofenac dose (100 mg) and timing (15 min before) may differ from other NSAID premedication protocols

## 7. Glossary

- **IL**: 치주인대 주사 (Intraligamentary Injection) — injection into the periodontal space at high pressure, delivering anesthetic into cancellous bone around root apex
- **SIP**: 불가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis) — inflamed pulp with VAS >54 mm and positive thermal/EPT
- **Aceclofenac**: COX-2 선택적 억제 비스테로이드 항염증약 (Selective COX-2 Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug, NSAID) — reduces prostaglandin-mediated peripheral sensitization in inflamed tissue
- **Heft-Parker VAS**: 170 mm 시각통증척도; ≤54 mm = mild/no pain = anesthesia success
