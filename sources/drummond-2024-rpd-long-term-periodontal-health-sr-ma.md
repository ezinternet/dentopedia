## Why Ingested

가철성 국소의치(Removable Partial Denture, RPD)를 신설 카테고리의 **spine**으로 세우기 위해 인제스트했다. 위키는 "발치 후 무엇으로 수복하나"에서 임플란트([[wiki/overviews/implants-clinical-decision-ladder]])와 고정성 보철([[wiki/overviews/prosthetic-materials-decision-ladder]]) 두 가지는 두껍게 갖췄으나 세 번째 가지인 RPD는 주제 논문 3편뿐이라, 국소의치가 답인 케이스에서 근거를 낼 수 없었다. 본 SR+MA는 46편·4,359 보철·4,072명 규모로 **RPD 자체의 장기 성적과 지대치에 미치는 영향**을 동시에 정량화해, 선택 결정의 기준선(5년 생존 ~95%)과 대가(지대치 발치 위험 OR 1.99)를 한 논문에서 제공한다. 기존 [[wiki/complete-denture/yoo-2023-implant-surveyed-crown-rpd-disabled-patients]]는 임플란트 보조 RPD의 특수 케이스라 통상 RPD의 기준선이 없었다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (46 studies, 4,359 prostheses, 4,072 participants, mean age 60 ± 5.2 years; PRISMA, RoB 2.0 / ROBINS-I / GRADE) asking whether long-term RPD use is deleterious to the remaining dentition.

Five-year prosthesis survival was 95.1% for cast-clasp RPDs and 91.7% for telescopic-crown-retained RPDs with no difference between designs (P = .71); abutment teeth carried a higher 5-year extraction risk (OR = 1.99, 95% CI 1.32–3.01) and nonvital abutments higher still (HR = 2.961, 95% CI 2.023–4.335), while tooth mobility (P = .98) and probing depth (P = .50) were unchanged and only gingival index rose (MD = 0.477, 95% CI 0.12–0.83).

The prosthesis survives well and the periodontium is largely stable, so the real long-term cost of an RPD is concentrated in the abutment teeth — especially nonvital ones — which reframes case selection around abutment vitality and prognosis rather than around the denture itself.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (46편, 보철 4,359개, 4,072명, 평균 60 ± 5.2세; PRISMA·RoB 2.0·ROBINS-I·GRADE): 장기간의 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Denture, RPD) 사용이 잔존치에 해로운가를 물었다.

5년 보철 생존율은 주조 클라스프 RPD 95.1%, 이중관 (telescopic crown) 유지 RPD 91.7%로 설계 간 차이가 없었고 (P = .71), 지대치는 5년 발치 위험이 유의하게 높았으며 (교차비 Odds Ratio, OR = 1.99, 95% CI 1.32–3.01) 실활 지대치는 더 높았다 (위험비 Hazard Ratio, HR = 2.961, 95% CI 2.023–4.335); 반면 치아 동요도 (P = .98)와 탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) (P = .50)는 변하지 않았고 치은지수 (Gingival Index, GI)만 상승했다 (평균차 Mean Difference, MD = 0.477).

보철은 잘 버티고 치주조직도 대체로 안정적이므로, RPD의 진짜 장기 비용은 **지대치**에 — 특히 실활 지대치에 — 몰려 있다. 케이스 선택의 축을 의치 자체가 아니라 지대치의 생활력과 예후로 옮겨야 한다는 뜻이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First meta-analytic pooling of RPD **prosthesis** survival and **abutment/periodontal** outcomes in the same review, so the benefit and the cost sit on one page.
- Establishes a usable 5-year baseline for the selection conversation: ~95% prosthesis survival.
- Separates what changes from what does not: gingival inflammation rises, but attachment-level proxies (mobility, probing depth) do not.
- Identifies **nonvital abutments** as the dominant risk multiplier (HR ≈ 2.96), a variable the clinician controls at planning time.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA-guided systematic review. Six databases plus grey literature searched April 2024 with no language or follow-up restriction. Inclusion limited to clinical studies of long-term RPD use. Risk of bias assessed with RoB 2.0 (randomised) and ROBINS-I (non-randomised); certainty with GRADE. Random-effects meta-analyses for survival and periodontal outcomes (α = .05). Screening yielded 46 of 5,577 records.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Ingested **abstract-only**: heterogeneity (I²), publication-bias assessment, and per-outcome GRADE certainty are unavailable, so the strength of each pooled estimate cannot be graded here.
- Pooling across cast-clasp and telescopic designs and across widely differing maintenance regimens; the abstract does not report whether recall interval was adjusted for.
- Mean participant age 60 ± 5.2 years — the estimates describe a late-middle-aged cohort and should not be extrapolated to much younger partially dentate patients.
- Survival is prosthesis-level; it does not tell us whether patients kept **wearing** the prosthesis (see the ~40% discontinuation signal in the wider literature).
- No comparator arm: this quantifies what happens with an RPD, not whether an alternative would have done better.

## 7. Glossary

- **RPD (가철성 국소의치, Removable Partial Denture)**: removable prosthesis replacing missing teeth while retaining natural abutment teeth.
- **Cast-clasp RPD (주조 클라스프 국소의치)**: RPD retained by cast metal clasps engaging undercuts on abutment teeth.
- **Telescopic-crown-retained RPD (이중관 유지 국소의치)**: RPD retained by primary copings cemented on abutments and matching secondary copings in the framework.
- **OR (교차비, Odds Ratio)** / **HR (위험비, Hazard Ratio)**: relative measures of event risk; HR incorporates time-to-event.
- **GI (치은지수, Gingival Index)**: ordinal score of gingival inflammation.
- **GRADE**: system for rating certainty of evidence per outcome.
