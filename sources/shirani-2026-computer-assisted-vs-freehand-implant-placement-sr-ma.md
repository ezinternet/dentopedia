## Why Ingested

기존 위키는 가이드 수술이 freehand보다 **정확도**(deviation mm/degree)에서 우수하다는 근거를 다수 보유한다 ([[digital-workflow/schiavon-2025-computer-assisted-immediate-implant-accuracy-nma]], [[digital-workflow/nava-2026-guided-surgery-immediate-implant-accuracy-nma]], [[immediate-implant/xing-2024-accuracy-immediate-implantation-digital-guided]]). 그러나 정확도 우위가 **임상·방사선학적 결과**(변연골소실 MBL, 생존율, 환자보고결과 PRO, 합병증)로 이어지는지는 별개 질문이다. 본 SR+MA (Shirani 2026, 9 RCT)는 전체 MBL·생존율에서 CAIS-vs-FHIP 유의차가 **없음**을 보고해, 정확도 논문들의 결론을 결과 영역에서 **한정(refine)**한다 — "정확도 이득 ≠ 생존율 이득". 또한 조직지지형(tissue-supported) 가이드가 오히려 freehand보다 MBL이 나쁠 수 있다는 caveat를 제공해 [[overviews/digital-workflow-decision-ladder]] 갱신 근거가 된다.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 9 RCTs (12 publications, 395 participants, 1,242 implants; search to Nov 2024) comparing computer-assisted implant surgery (CAIS) vs freehand implant placement (FHIP) on clinical/radiographic outcomes (MBL, survival, PROs, complications), graded with Cochrane RoB + GRADE.

Overall MBL and implant survival showed NO statistically significant CAIS-vs-FHIP difference (both >98% survival); subgroups favored CAIS in fresh sockets (MBL P=.04), patient satisfaction (P=.03) and pink esthetic score (P=.009), whereas FHIP had fewer biologic complications (P=.04) and lower MBL than tissue-supported CAIS (P=.03).

Accuracy superiority of guided surgery does NOT translate into survival superiority; choose CAIS for fresh-socket/esthetic cases but note tissue-supported guides can underperform open-flap freehand — evidence certainty very low to moderate, 6/9 studies high risk of bias.

## 세줄요약

9개 RCT(12편, 참가자 395명, 임플란트 1,242개, 2024년 11월까지 검색)를 종합한 SR+MA로, 컴퓨터보조 임플란트수술(CAIS)과 자유수식립(FHIP)을 임상·방사선(MBL, 생존율, PRO, 합병증) 결과로 비교하고 Cochrane RoB·GRADE로 평가.

전체 MBL·생존율은 CAIS-FHIP 유의차 없음(양쪽 생존율 >98%); 하위그룹에서 CAIS는 신선발치와(fresh socket) MBL(P=.04)·환자만족도(P=.03)·핑크심미점수(P=.009)에서 우수, 반면 FHIP는 생물학적 합병증이 적고(P=.04) 조직지지형 CAIS보다 MBL이 낮음(P=.03).

가이드 수술의 정확도 우위가 생존율 우위로 이어지지 않음 — 신선발치와·심미 증례엔 CAIS, 단 점막지지 가이드는 개방피판 freehand보다 나쁠 수 있음; 근거등급 very low~moderate, 9편 중 6편 high RoB.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Shifts the CAIS-vs-FHIP comparison from the **accuracy domain** (positional deviation) — where guided surgery is already established as superior — to the **clinical/radiographic outcome domain** (MBL, survival, PROs, complications), and finds accuracy advantage does NOT confer a survival advantage.
2. Subgroup signal that CAIS benefits are **context-specific**: fresh-socket placement, patient-reported and esthetic outcomes — not a blanket MBL/survival win.
3. Cautionary finding on **guide support type**: tissue- (mucosa-) supported CAIS had higher MBL than freehand, whereas an open-flap (freehand) approach performed better — support/registration quality matters more than "guided vs not."

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA-style SR+MA restricted to RCTs comparing CAIS and FHIP.
- **Included**: 12 publications derived from 9 RCTs; 395 participants; 1,242 implants.
- **Outcomes**: marginal bone loss (MBL), implant survival rate, patient satisfaction, pink esthetic score (PES), probing depth, complications (biologic).
- **Analysis**: meta-analysis with sensitivity and subgroup analyses (subgroups included fresh socket vs healed, tissue-supported vs open-flap/other support).
- **Certainty**: GRADE ratings ranged very low → moderate; 6 of 9 studies judged high RoB.
- (Pooled effect estimates, CIs, I² heterogeneity, and the study-by-study table are in the paywalled full text and are NOT reproduced here.)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Finding | P |
|---|---|---|
| Overall MBL | No significant CAIS-vs-FHIP difference | NS |
| Overall implant survival | No significant difference; both >98% | NS |
| MBL — fresh socket subgroup | CAIS lower (favored) | .04 |
| Patient satisfaction | CAIS higher | .03 |
| Pink esthetic score (PES) | CAIS better | .009 |
| Biologic complications | FHIP fewer | .04 |
| MBL — vs tissue-supported CAIS | FHIP lower (favored) | .03 |

- Sensitivity analyses confirmed robustness for most outcomes.
- Bottom line: both modalities achieve high survival (>98%). CAIS advantages concentrate in fresh-socket + esthetic/PRO endpoints; FHIP advantages in fewer biologic complications and when compared against tissue-supported guides (open-flap freehand).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest** — full methods/quantitative pooling not verified.
- Evidence certainty very low to moderate; 6/9 studies high RoB → conclusions tentative.
- Subgroup findings (fresh socket, tissue-supported) are hypothesis-level, susceptible to small-study and multiplicity effects.
- Does NOT address positional accuracy (the domain where guided surgery is strongest) — outcome-only scope.
- Heterogeneity in CAIS type (static vs dynamic navigation vs robotics) and support (tooth/mucosa/bone) not resolvable from abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAIS** — Computer-Assisted Implant Surgery (static guide or dynamic navigation).
- **FHIP** — Freehand Implant Placement (conventional, no guide/navigation).
- **MBL** — Marginal Bone Loss (radiographic peri-implant crestal bone change).
- **PES** — Pink Esthetic Score (peri-implant soft-tissue esthetic index).
- **Tissue-supported guide** — surgical guide resting on mucosa (vs tooth- or bone-supported), lower registration stability.
- **GRADE** — Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation (certainty of evidence).
