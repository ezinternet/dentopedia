## Why Ingested

Maxilla companion paper to [[wiki/implants/full-arch/de-araujo-nobre-2025-mandibular-all-on-four-20-25yr]], extending the same Malo Clinic cohort protocol to the upper jaw with 15–20 years of follow-up — the longest available maxillary All-on-4 dataset in this wiki. Provides the very-long-term endpoint (implant CSurR 90.7%, MBL 1.46 mm at 20yr) for the maxillary arch that complements the mandibular and SR/MA pages; critical for answering patient questions about lifetime durability and risk stratification (smoking, biological complications) for maxillary full-arch rehabilitation.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (n = 740 patients, 2960 implants) of immediate full-arch maxillary All-on-4 rehabilitation with 15–20 years follow-up at a single center (Malo Clinic, Lisbon).

Implant cumulative survival rate (CSurR) 90.7% and success rate (CSucR) 84.6% at 20 years; prosthetic success 98.1%; marginal bone loss (MBL) 1.07 mm at 15yr and 1.46 mm at 20yr; mechanical complications in 78.5% of patients; biological complications in 35.1% of patients.

Smoking (HR = 1.93) and biological complications (HR = 2.18) are the two principal risk indicators for implant failure; 38.8% loss to follow-up introduces substantial uncertainty (worst-case sensitivity: 59.4% survival).

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구 (환자 740명, 임플란트 2960개) — 즉시 전악수복 상악 All-on-4, 15~20년 추적 (단일 기관, Malo Clinic, 리스본).

임플란트 누적생존율 (CSurR) 90.7%, 누적성공률 (CSucR) 84.6% (20년); 보철 성공률 98.1%; 변연골소실 (MBL) 15년 1.07 mm, 20년 1.46 mm; 기계적 합병증 78.5%, 생물학적 합병증 35.1%.

흡연 (HR = 1.93)과 생물학적 합병증 (HR = 2.18)이 임플란트 실패의 핵심 위험지표; 38.8% 추적소실로 결과 해석에 주의 요망 (최악 시나리오 59.4% 생존).

## 1. Document Information

- **Study type**: Retrospective cohort study
- **Center**: Malo Clinic, Lisbon, Portugal (single center)
- **Ethics**: Ethical Committee for Health; Authorization nº 002/2025
- **Treatment period**: November 2002 – December 2008
- **Follow-up**: 15 and 20 years
- **Sample**: 740 patients (440 women, 300 men; mean age 55.3 years, range 20–88)
- **Implants**: 2960 (NobelSpeedy Groovy n=2851, Mk IV n=92, Mk III n=17; widths 3.3–5 mm, lengths 8.5–18 mm, anodically oxidized surface)
- **Lost to follow-up**: 287 patients (38.8%), 1148 implants (38.8%); 42 deceased, 246 unreachable

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Longest reported follow-up for immediate full-arch maxillary All-on-4: up to 20 years
2. Prosthetic success rate 98.1% at up to 20 years
3. Implant CSurR 90.7% and CSucR 84.6% at 20 years
4. MBL stable trajectory: 0.9–1.2 mm at 1yr → 1.18 mm at 5yr → 1.67 mm at 10yr → 1.07 mm at 15yr → 1.46 mm at 20yr
5. Identifies smoking + biological complications as dual risk indicators across three outcome models (implant failure, MBL > 3mm, and biological complications themselves)
6. Mechanical complications (78.5%) appear protective for MBL — patients require more maintenance appointments ("pseudo-maintenance protocol")

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective, single-center, STROBE-compliant
- **Surgical technique**: All-on-4 concept; posterior implants tilted 30–45° along anterior maxillary sinus wall; insertion torque 30–50 Ncm; 30° multi-unit abutments on posterior, 0° or 17° on anterior
- **Prosthetic protocol**: Provisional acrylic (same day), definitive ≥ 6 months post-op (titanium framework + ceramic or acrylic)
- **Maintenance**: 10d, 2, 4, 6 months, 1yr, then every 6 months; prosthesis removal + prophylaxis
- **MBL assessment**: Periapical radiographs (parallel technique), calibrated blinded assessor, iRYS v13.0 software; platform as reference
- **Statistical analysis**: Life tables (CSurR/CSucR), Cox proportional hazards (implant failure), binary logistic regression (MBL > 3mm, mechanical and biological complications); sensitivity analysis (best/worst case)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Implant Survival/Success

| Outcome | 15 years | 20 years |
|---|---|---|
| Cumulative Survival Rate (CSurR) | 94.1% | 90.7% |
| Cumulative Success Rate (CSucR) | 89.4% | 84.6% |
| Prosthetic success | — | 98.1% |

- Sensitivity analysis at 20yr: best case 93.1%; worst case 59.4%
- 170 implants failed (5.7%) in 101 patients (13.6%); 140 in first 180 months, 30 in 181–240 months

### Marginal Bone Loss

| Time | MBL (mean ± SD) |
|---|---|
| 15 years | 1.07 ± 1.38 mm |
| 20 years | 1.46 ± 1.56 mm |

Patients with MBL > 3mm: 63 patients / 95 implants at 15yr; 8 patients / 14 implants at 20yr

### Complications

| Complication | Patients | Implants |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical | 581 (78.5%) | — |
| Biological | 260 (35.1%) | 449 (15.2%) |
| Implant infections (year 1) | 81 | 117 (→ 7 failures) |
| Chronic peri-implant disease | 199 | 332 (→ 54 failures) |

### Risk Indicators (Multivariate)

| Model | Variable | Estimate | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failure (Cox) | Smoking | HR = 1.93 | Risk indicator |
| Implant failure (Cox) | Biological complications | HR = 2.18 | Risk indicator |
| MBL > 3mm (logistic) | Smoking | OR = 3.60 | Risk indicator |
| MBL > 3mm (logistic) | Biological complications | OR = 3.70 | Risk indicator |
| MBL > 3mm (logistic) | Mechanical complications | OR = 0.26 | Protective (pseudo-maintenance) |
| Biological complications (logistic) | Smoking | OR = 1.78 | Risk indicator |
| Biological complications (logistic) | Age > 60 | OR = 0.48 | Decreased risk (shorter exposure) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center (Malo Clinic), limiting external validity
- Retrospective design — unmeasured/missing data possible; MBL potentially underestimated (26% and 7% ineligible radiographs at 15yr and 20yr)
- Single outcome assessor for MBL
- 38.8% loss to follow-up — wide uncertainty (best 93.1%, worst 59.4%)
- Patients lost to follow-up were significantly older (p < 0.001); COVID-19 pandemic likely increased mortality in the 71–75 age cohort during the 20-year follow-up window
- Multivariable models lacked history of periodontitis and bacterial plaque as covariates
- Future studies should include different restorative materials and implant micro-designs

## 7. Glossary

- **All-on-4**: Immediate full-arch implant rehabilitation using 2 axial anterior + 2 distal-tilted posterior implants
- **CSurR (Cumulative Survival Rate)**: Proportion of implants/prostheses remaining in function (including those with complications)
- **CSucR (Cumulative Success Rate)**: Proportion meeting all predefined success criteria
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: Change in bone level at implant platform relative to surgery
- **Chronic peri-implant disease**: Pockets > 5mm + bleeding on probing + concurrent MBL and clinical attachment loss
