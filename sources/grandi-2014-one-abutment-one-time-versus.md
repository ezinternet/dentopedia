## Why Ingested

This multicentre RCT tests the non-removal (one-abutment-one-time) protocol in a context the held RCT [[implants/rios-santos-2020-one-abutment-one-time-rct]] did not cover — immediately loaded post-extractive single implants — extending that healed-site/subcrestal evidence. It also independently confirms the direction of effect later pooled by [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]]; its 2014 patients fall outside that review's 2020–2024 trial window, so the two are not double-counted.

## Three-line Summary

Multicentre RCT in two private clinics (28 patients randomized shortly before extraction, 3 excluded for buccal wall fracture, 12 definitive-abutment vs 13 provisional-abutment patients) comparing non-removal of a definitive platform-switched abutment with a provisional abutment later replaced by a custom abutment in immediately loaded post-extractive single implants, followed to 1 year after loading.

At 12 months no implant failed; the definitive-abutment group lost 0.11 mm (SD 0.06) of peri-implant bone versus 0.58 mm (SD 0.11) in the provisional-abutment group, a mean difference of 0.48 mm (95% CI 0.40–0.55, P<0.0001); one biological and one mechanical complication occurred and all were successfully treated.

Non-removal of the abutment placed at surgery maintained about 0.5 mm more bone in this setting, but the authors caution this may not have a clinical impact; evidence here is abstract-only with a small analyzed sample.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Randomized comparison of abutment non-removal versus abutment removal in **immediately loaded, post-extractive** single implants — an indication distinct from the healed-ridge/subcrestal OAOT RCTs already held.
- Quantified a 12-month marginal bone difference favouring the non-removal protocol: 0.11 mm vs 0.58 mm (mean difference 0.48 mm, 95% CI 0.40–0.55, P<0.0001).
- Reported no implant failures and only two minor, successfully treated complications (one biological in the definitive-abutment arm, one mechanical in the provisional-abutment arm).
- Explicitly flagged that the ~0.5 mm bone maintenance "may not have a clinical impact," tempering the result's clinical interpretation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: multicentre randomized controlled trial, two private clinics; randomization shortly before tooth extraction (randomization method and allocation concealment not described in the abstract).
- **Population**: 28 patients needing one single post-extractive implant in the maxilla or mandible from the left second premolar to the right second premolar area; three excluded after extraction for buccal wall fracture.
- **Analyzed groups**: definitive abutment (DA) n = 12; provisional abutment (PA) n = 13.
- **DA arm**: immediately restored with a definitive platform-switched titanium abutment; at 4 months an impression of the abutment was made using a retraction cord, and the abutment was not removed.
- **PA arm**: immediately restored with a platform-switched provisional titanium abutment; at 4 months the abutment was removed and the impression made directly on the implant platform, with a custom-made definitive abutment subsequently placed.
- **Both arms**: non-occluding provisional single crown; definitive restoration after 4 months.
- **Outcomes**: implant failures, complications, and marginal peri-implant bone level changes; follow-up to 1 year after loading.
- **Note**: radiographic measurement method, blinded outcome assessment, effect-size model, and power calculation are not reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only — full text not retrieved**: no Methods detail beyond the structured abstract, no Discussion, and no radiographic/methodological appraisal are available; all claims here are at abstract level.
- Small analyzed sample (12 DA, 13 PA after three exclusions for buccal wall fracture) from two private clinics; the abstract does not state a power calculation or a geographic setting.
- Single-tooth, post-extractive, immediately loaded indication with 1-year follow-up only — generalizability to healed sites, multiple units, or longer-term crestal stability is not established by this record.
- The clinical relevance of the 0.48 mm difference is explicitly qualified by the authors as possibly without clinical impact.
- **Conflict of interest**: Dr Tommaso Grandi and Dr Paolo Guazzi serve as consultants for JDentalCare; the study was completely self-financed and no funding was sought or obtained, not even in the form of free materials.

## 7. Glossary

- **DA**: definitive abutment — platform-switched titanium abutment placed at surgery and left in place.
- **PA**: provisional abutment — platform-switched titanium abutment removed at 4 months for a custom-made definitive abutment.
- **OAOT (One-Abutment-One-Time, 원-어버트먼트-원-타임)**: protocol placing the definitive abutment once at implant surgery and avoiding subsequent disconnection.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골소실)**: peri-implant crestal bone level change reported here at 12 months.
- **Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭)**: abutment narrower than the implant platform; used in both arms of this trial.
