Fills a gap in the TMJ section: no NMA had ranked all major nonpharmacological treatments simultaneously. Directly updates [[tmj/zhang-2021-exercise-vs-splint-tmd-sr-ma]] by expanding the exercise vs splint frame to include PBMT and MT — which rank above both. ## Three-line Summary

NMA (41 RCTs, 2021 adults, myogenic TMD; MEDLINE/Embase/CENTRAL/WoS/GS through Oct 2025; PROSPERO CRD420261278458) comparing 10 nonpharmacological modalities for pain and maximum mouth opening (MMO) using frequentist random-effects model and SUCRA rankings.

For pain, PBMT ranked highest (SUCRA 88.9%; SMD vs CON −1.49 [−2.07, −0.91]; SMD vs OST −1.43 [−2.29, −0.56]); MT ranked second (SUCRA 79.9%); exercise and occlusal splint both performed below conventional care threshold; for MMO, MT ranked highest (SUCRA 92.9%; SMD vs CON 2.79 [1.33, 4.26]) though funnel plot asymmetry suggests possible publication bias.

Clinical takeaway: for myogenic TMD, prioritize PBMT when pain relief is the primary goal and MT when mandibular mobility restoration is primary; exercise and occlusal splint are accessible adjuncts but show limited short-term ranking advantage for pain.

## 세줄요약

NMA (41 RCT, 2021명, 근육성 악관절증; MEDLINE 외 4개 DB, 2025년 10월까지; PROSPERO 등록) — 10가지 비약물 요법을 통증과 최대개구량(MMO)에 대해 빈도주의 랜덤효과 네트워크 메타분석으로 비교.

통증: 광생체조절치료 (Photobiomodulation Therapy, PBMT)가 SUCRA 88.9%로 1위(vs 대조군 SMD −1.49; vs 교합안정장치 SMD −1.43, 95% CI −2.29~−0.56); 도수치료 (Manual Therapy, MT) 2위(79.9%); 운동치료 (Exercise Therapy, EX)·교합안정장치(Occlusal Splint Therapy, OST)는 낮은 순위. MMO: MT 1위(SUCRA 92.9%; vs 대조군 SMD 2.79 [1.33, 4.26]), 단 깔때기 도식 비대칭으로 출판 편향 가능성.

임상 결론: 근육성 악관절증에서 통증 완화 주목표면 PBMT, 개구량 개선 주목표면 MT 우선 고려; 운동·안정장치는 접근성이 장점이나 단기 통증 순위에서 제한적.

## Key Contributions

- First NMA to simultaneously rank 10 nonpharmacological modalities for myogenic TMD using SUCRA
- PBMT demonstrated superior pain reduction vs OST, EX, ACU, CON (all with CIs excluding zero)
- MT showed significant advantage for MMO over CON, PBMT, ACU, EX
- Both exercise and occlusal splints ranked below PBMT/MT for short-term pain
- Inconsistency detected: ELEC vs MT comparison (p<0.05) — interpret indirect estimates cautiously
- Limitation: short-term outcomes only; publication bias possible for MMO (Egger p<0.05)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + frequentist random-effects NMA
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, Web of Science, Google Scholar (inception to Oct 2025)
- **Population**: Adults ≥18y with myogenic TMD (myofascial pain presentations)
- **Interventions**: PBMT, MT, EX, OST, ACU, ELEC, COMB, CBT, ESWT, Central neuromodulation vs CON
- **Outcomes**: Pain (VAS) and MMO (interincisal distance mm)
- **Analysis**: Stata v17, "network"+"mvmeta" packages; SUCRA for ranking; RoB 2.0
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD420261278458
- **Included**: 41 RCTs, 2021 participants; 2000–2025, median 2019

## Limitations

- Short-term outcomes only; durability unknown
- Clinical and methodological heterogeneity substantial (pain I² elevated)
- Publication bias possible for MMO (Egger p<0.05) — MT ranking for MMO should be interpreted cautiously
- No GRADE certainty assessment performed
- Inconsistency in ELEC vs MT comparison
- Variable protocol dosing across PBMT and MT studies

## Glossary

- **Myogenic TMD**: TMD subtype characterized by myofascial pain; excludes articular/disc-based presentations
- **PBMT (Photobiomodulation Therapy)**: Low-level laser therapy targeting mitochondrial photoacceptors to reduce inflammation and pain
- **SUCRA**: Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking Curve — higher = better relative rank; not a measure of absolute superiority
- **SMD**: Standardized Mean Difference — allows pooling across different pain scales
- **MT (Manual Therapy)**: Hands-on mobilization/manipulation of masticatory muscles and TMJ
