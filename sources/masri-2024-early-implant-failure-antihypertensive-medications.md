## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (Rabin Medical Center, Israel; 792 patients/2,971 implants, 2013–2018) comparing early implant failure (EIF ≤12 months from loading) in normotensive (NT), hypertensive-medicated (HTN+med), and hypertensive-unmedicated (HTN-med) patients.

HTN+med group (n=185/784 implants) had significantly lower EIF than NT (2.29% vs 4.33% implant-level, p=.01; 9.70% vs 14.50% patient-level, p=.047); HTN-med group (n=14/64 implants) had the worst outcomes (EIF 6.25% implant-level, 28.60% patient-level), though the tiny unmedicated HTN group limits conclusions.

The dual mechanism — antihypertensives eliminate HTN-induced calcium dysregulation AND add direct osteoanabolic effects (RAAS inhibition, beta-blockade, thiazide-driven osteoblast stimulation) — explains why HTN patients on medication outperform normotensive controls.

## 세줄요약

후향코호트(라빈메디컬센터, 이스라엘; 792환자/2,971임플란트, 2013–2018) — 정상혈압(NT)·항고혈압약 복용고혈압(HTN+med)·항고혈압약 미복용고혈압(HTN-med) 세 군의 조기임플란트실패(EIF, 하중 후 ≤12개월) 비교.

임플란트 수준 EIF: HTN+med 2.29% vs NT 4.33%(p=.01); 환자 수준: HTN+med 9.70% vs NT 14.50%(p=.047) — HTN+med가 유의하게 낮음. 미복용 HTN군은 임플란트 실패 6.25%, 환자 실패 28.60%로 최악(n=14, 소규모 제한).

고혈압 자체는 임플란트에 불리하지만 항고혈압약 복용 시 오히려 정상혈압 대조군보다 더 낮은 실패율 — 약물의 뼈 대사 촉진(RAAS 억제→RANKL 감소, 티아지드→조골세포 자극)이 기전.

## Summary

Retrospective cohort (Rabin Medical Center, Israel, 2013–2018; n=792 patients/2,971 implants) dividing patients into three groups: normotensive (NT, n=593/2,123 implants), hypertensive on antihypertensives (HTN+med, 185/784), hypertensive without medication (HTN-med, 14/64). Primary outcome: early implant failure (EIF) ≤12 months from loading.

EIF at implant level: HTN+med 2.29% vs NT 4.33% (p=.01). EIF at patient level: HTN+med 9.70% vs NT 14.50% (p=.047). Critical finding: HTN-med (unmedicated hypertensives) had the worst outcomes — implant-level EIF 6.25%, patient-level EIF 28.60%. Multivariate OR for HTN+med vs NT: 0.618 (p=.059, marginal significance after controlling age/gender/implant count).

Most common medications: beta-blockers 61%, ACEi 41%, ARBs 21%, CCB 19%, thiazides 5%.

**Three-part mechanistic argument**: (1) HTN itself impairs bone via calcium dysregulation; (2) antihypertensives restore normal bone metabolism; (3) antihypertensives have additional osteoanabolic effects (RAAS inhibition → RANKL reduction; thiazides → osteoblast stimulation; beta-blockers → reduced osteoclast activity).

**Key limitation**: HTN-med group very small (n=14); all AHT drugs grouped (no drug-specific analysis); no blood pressure classification data; retrospective design.
