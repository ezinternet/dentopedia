## Three-line Summary

Prospective 2-arm parallel RCT, 60 patients (92 implants), Zhejiang University China — comparing tapered vs cylindrical implants for ISQ and peri-implant bone changes at simultaneous lateral sinus floor elevation.

Tapered implants showed significantly higher ISQ at placement (70.48 vs 55.04, p<0.001) and at 6 months (80.83 vs 78.98, p=0.038); peri-implant bone changes (ESBG, MBL, 3D bone volume) were comparable between groups; 100% survival in both groups.

6-month follow-up only; long-term survival and marginal bone loss data not captured; tapered implant advantage in ISQ may attenuate over time as shown by converging values at 6 months.

## 세줄요약

전향적 2군 평행 RCT, 60명 (92개 임플란트), 중국 저장대학 — 측방 상악동거상술 동시 식립에서 테이퍼드 vs 원통형 임플란트 ISQ 및 골 변화 비교.

테이퍼드 임플란트가 식립 시 (70.48 vs 55.04, p<0.001) 및 6개월 (80.83 vs 78.98, p=0.038) ISQ 유의하게 높음; ESBG·MBL·3D 골 부피 변화량 두 군 동등; 6개월 생존율 100%.

6개월 추적만; 장기 생존율·MBL 미평가; 6개월 시점에서 값이 수렴하여 ISQ 우위가 장기적으로 지속되는지 불명.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Journal of Dentistry, 2026, Vol. 173, p.106807
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jdent.2026.106807 | PMID: 42235675
- Setting: Zhejiang University Stomatology Hospital, China; Faculty of Dentistry, HKU
- ClinicalTrials: ChiCTR2100042169

## 2. Key Contributions
- First prospective RCT directly comparing ISQ trajectories of tapered vs cylindrical implants at simultaneous LSFE
- Tapered implant: significantly higher ISQ at T1 (placement) — p<0.001
- Both groups ISQ rose over 6 months; tapered remained significantly higher at T2 — p=0.038 (modest difference)
- No significant difference in ESBG, MBL, or 3D bone volume between groups
- 100% survival rate both groups at 6 months
- GEE statistical model accounts for multiple implants per patient (clustering)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Prospective randomized clinical study (2-arm parallel)
- n=60 patients, 92 implants: tapered (mild apical taper, n=48) vs cylindrical (parallel-walled, n=44)
- Indication: LSFE + simultaneous implant placement in posterior maxilla
- ISQ: Osstell, at T1 (placement) and T2 (6 months healing)
- CBCT: ESBG, MBL, 3D bone volume at 6 months
- Statistics: Generalized estimating equations (GEE) for clustered implant data

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- ISQ T1: 70.48±8.02 (tapered) vs 55.04±12.47 (cylindrical), p<0.001
- ISQ T2: 80.83±3.43 vs 78.98±4.18, p=0.038 (modest but significant)
- ESBG: no significant difference between groups
- MBL: no significant difference
- 3D bone volume change: no significant difference
- Survival: 100% both groups at 6 months

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 6-month follow-up only — long-term survival and MBL not captured
- Single center (Zhejiang)
- No stratification by residual bone height (RBH) subgroup
- Both implants are "bone-level" but different brands — surface and design may confound
- Need: ≥12–24 month follow-up, multi-center, RBH stratified analysis

## 6. Related Work
- Cylindrical vs tapered ISQ: Mourao 2025 (JCP RCT) — tapered better early healing
- ISQ at LSFE sites is generally lower than non-grafted (soft graft material reduces primary stability)
- ESBG and MBL comparable for simultaneous vs delayed placement (prior SRs)

## 7. Glossary
- LSFE: Lateral sinus floor elevation (lateral window approach)
- ISQ: Implant Stability Quotient (0–100, Osstell RFA) — primary (T1) and secondary (T2) stability
- ESBG: Endo-sinus bone gain — new bone formation within the sinus
- MBL: Marginal bone loss at crestal level
- GEE: Generalized estimating equations — handles multiple implants per patient
