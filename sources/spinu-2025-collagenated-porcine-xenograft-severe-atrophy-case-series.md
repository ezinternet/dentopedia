## Why Ingested

측방 상악동거상술에서 돼지 유래 이종골 (Porcine Xenograft)의 인체 조직학 자료는 소 유래 탈단백우골무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)에 비해 거의 없다 — [[sinus-lift/lateral/galindomoreno-2022-maxillary-sinus-floor-augmentation-comparing]]는 6개월 단일 시점 조직형태계측만, [[bone-regeneration/wang-2025-porcine-bovine-xenograft-msfa-arp-sr-ma]]는 임상·조직 수치를 통합했지만 장기 추적이 없다. 본 증례군은 잔존골높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) ≤2 mm라는 극단적 위축에서 3.7·4.7·7.5개월 3개 시점 조직학 + 평균 46.8개월 임플란트 추적을 짝지어 보고해 그 공백을 메운다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series (single private clinic, Oradea, Romania; 3 partially edentulous patients aged 46–56, surgery May–November 2021) of lateral-window sinus floor elevation with a small-particle (0.25–1.00 mm) collagenated porcine xenograft followed by staged implant placement, with trephine core biopsies at 3.7, 4.7 and 7.5 months and a mean 46.8-month follow-up.

Mean vertical bone gain was 12.0 mm (baseline residual bone height 1.33 mm → 13.33 mm final), all 4 implants were functional with no failures at 38.3–52.2 months and marginal bone loss stayed <1 mm in the first year, while histology showed 20–30% new bone, 30–40% residual biomaterial and 30–50% connective tissue with hybrid bone formation on particle surfaces and TRAP-positive multinucleated giant cells at 7.5 months.

Within a 3-patient sample the material looks biocompatible, osteoconductive and slowly resorbing with stable long-term implant function, but the series is uncontrolled, single-centre and biopsy-based, so it cannot establish efficacy relative to bovine xenografts or any comparator.

## 세줄요약

후향적 증례군 (루마니아 오라데아 단일 개인 클리닉; 부분 무치악 3명, 46–56세, 수술 2021년 5–11월): 잔존골높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) ≤2 mm의 심한 후방 상악 위축에서 소입자 (0.25–1.00 mm) 콜라겐화 돼지 이종골 (Collagenated Porcine Xenograft)로 측방창 상악동거상술 후 단계적 임플란트 식립, 3.7·4.7·7.5개월 트레핀 생검 조직학 + 평균 46.8개월 추적.

평균 수직골증가 (Vertical Bone Gain) 12.0 mm (기저 RBH 1.33 mm → 최종 13.33 mm), 임플란트 4개 전부 기능 중·실패 0건 (추적 38.3–52.2개월), 1년차 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) <1 mm; 조직학은 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 20–30%·잔존 이식재 (Bone Substitute Material, BSM) 30–40%·결합조직 (Connective Tissue, CT) 30–50%, 입자 표면에 혼성골 (Hybrid Bone) 형성, 7.5개월 생검에서 주석산저항성산성인산분해효소 (Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase, TRAP) 양성 다핵거대세포 (Multinucleated Giant Cell, MNGC) 확인.

3명 표본에서 이 재료는 생체적합성·골전도성·서서히 흡수되는 거동과 장기 임플란트 안정성을 보였으나, 대조군 없는 단일기관 생검 연구이므로 소 이종골 등 다른 이식재 대비 우월성은 입증하지 못한다.

- **Material**: THE Graft™ (Purgo Biologics Inc., Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea), small-particle 0.25–1.00 mm collagenated porcine-derived xenograft; ~2 g per sinus mixed with autologous blood; covered with BioCover™ resorbable collagen membrane (30 × 40 mm), 4-0 PTFE sutures (Biotex™)
- **Devices**: Prama® (Sweden & Martina, Due Carrare, Italy) and Straumann® implants (Straumann Group, Basel, Switzerland), 12–13 mm length × 4 mm diameter

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pairs **human histology at three healing time points (3.7, 4.7, 7.5 months)** with **long-term implant follow-up (mean 46.8 months)** in the same three patients — the combination is rare, since biopsies at implant osteotomy are not routine practice.
- Documents a porcine xenograft used in the **most extreme atrophy tier (RBH ≤ 2 mm)** rather than the 4–6 mm range typical of most sinus-graft histology series.
- Reports **TRAP-positive multinucleated giant cells only in the 7.5-month biopsy**, giving direct human evidence of ongoing (not stalled) biomaterial degradation, plus hybrid bone formation on particle surfaces at all three time points.
- Describes a **clinically driven membrane perforation** — an 8 × 6 mm lateral window with intentional Schneiderian membrane perforation to enucleate an asymptomatic mucocele ("Crock-eye" technique), repaired with a collagen membrane, with uneventful healing and 100% implant survival.
- Provides a granular per-case table linking biopsy timing, implant positions, baseline RBH, final augmented height and follow-up length — the kind of per-case transparency a 3-case series can offer.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective case series; CARE-compliant; no comparator arm, no randomisation, no blinding.
- **Screening frame**: 83 patients underwent sinus augmentation at the clinic between 3 May and 30 November 2021; inclusion required RBH ≤ 2 mm, absence of active sinus pathology, lateral-window elevation with the same collagenated porcine bone mineral, return for implant placement allowing a clinically indicated biopsy, non-smoker, ASA I, complete records and consent → **3 patients included** (2 male, 1 female; mean age 50.3 y; range 46–56).
- **Surgery**: crestal incision with vertical releasing incisions, full-thickness flap; lateral window ~8 × 6 mm with a 3 mm round diamond bur; membrane elevated without perforation except the mucocele case; graft ~2 g of 0.25–1.00 mm collagenated porcine xenograft mixed with autologous blood; collagen membrane coverage; tension-free primary closure with 4-0 PTFE; immediate postoperative CBCT.
- **Staging**: two-stage protocol (implant placement after 4–8 months) because of the reduced residual cortical height; one case required an additional crestal (internal) sinus lift at implant placement because the mucocele case still lacked height for standard 12–13 mm implants.
- **Perioperative protocol**: amoxicillin/clavulanate 875/125 mg every 12 h starting 24 h preoperatively for 7 days (the authors acknowledge limited evidence for routine prophylaxis); 0.2% chlorhexidine rinse; articaine 4% with epinephrine 1:100,000; nimesulide 100 mg every 12 h postoperatively; no nose blowing/closed-mouth sneezing for 2–4 weeks.
- **Biopsy**: 5 mm trephine at implant osteotomy from the buccal wall adjacent to the osteotomy, containing new bone and xenograft but no cortical bone; sites re-grafted with the same material and covered with a collagen membrane; biopsy taken only where clinically indicated, never for research alone.
- **Histology**: 10% buffered formalin 48 h → decalcification in 10% tris-buffered EDTA at 37 °C for 7 days (ultrasonic decalcifier) → paraffin embedding → 2–4 μm sections → HE, Masson–Goldner trichrome and TRAP.
- **Histomorphometry**: research-grade scanning microscope + NIS-Elements (Nikon); "total scans" of whole implantation beds at ×200 with automated stage; one experienced oral pathologist not involved in surgery, not formally blinded/coded but unaware of clinical outcomes during assessment.
- **Follow-up**: 24 h, 1 week, 1 month, 3 and 6 months, then annually; CBCT immediately postoperatively and after implant placement; prostheses (porcelain fused-to-metal, screw-retained) at 6 months after implant placement.

**Clinical outcomes**
- Healing uneventful in all 3 cases; no intraoperative or postoperative complications other than the *intentional* perforation (repaired, no sequelae); no sinusitis, no wound dehiscence, no infection.
- Vertical gain from 1.33 mm mean baseline RBH to 13.33 mm final (12.0 mm mean gain).
- 100% implant survival (4 implants, 2 + 1 + 1) at mean 46.8 months (range 38.3–52.2); no mobility, no peri-implant infection, no peri-implant radiolucency; marginal bone loss <1 mm in year one and stable thereafter.
- Insufficient keratinized gingiva in one case at 3 months post-implant → palatal connective tissue graft.

**Authors' comparisons (their Discussion, not this study's data)**
- Troeltzsch SR: mean horizontal gain 3.7 ± 1.2 mm across particulate materials, weighted mean vertical gain 3.7 ± 1.4 mm.
- A 2011 meta-analysis: maximum vertical gain 4.62 mm; Orth et al.: mean 3.4 mm with simultaneous implantation. The authors read their 12.0 mm as "consistent with, or surpassing" these figures.
- Histomorphometric percentages (NB 20–30%, BSM 30–40%, CT 30–50%) are described as comparable to collagenated bovine xenograft series [54,55].

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **n = 3.** The authors state plainly that the small sample "limits the ability to generalize the findings" and "restricts the validity of the results"; the 100% survival and zero-complication record is explicitly attributed to small n, careful selection and operator experience, not to material superiority.
- **No comparator.** Single-arm design with one graft material, one clinic, one surgical team — bovine xenograft, allograft or graftless controls are absent, so the "porcine ≈ bovine" claim rests entirely on comparison against published numbers.
- **Biopsy representativeness.** Trephine cores at implant placement sample the buccal wall adjacent to the osteotomy, may not represent the whole grafted volume, and are 2-D area fractions rather than 3-D volumes; one core (case 2) showed no residual bone at all, underlining sampling variability.
- **Retrospective, single private clinic** → selection bias cannot be excluded; the 83 → 3 funnel (RBH ≤2 mm, non-smoker, ASA I, returned for staged placement, consented to biopsy) means the cohort is highly selected and healthier than a typical sinus-graft population.
- **Histology not formally blinded or coded** (pathologist unaware of clinical outcomes only); no second examiner or inter-examiner agreement statistic.
- **Measurement-definition caveat (reader's note, not the authors'):** the 12.0 mm "vertical gain" is the change from baseline RBH (1.33 mm) to final radiographic augmented height (13.33 mm), whereas the meta-analytic 3.4–4.62 mm figures the authors cite come from heterogeneous definitions of vertical gain; the comparison is descriptive rather than like-for-like.
- **Time points are uneven across cases** (3.7 / 4.7 / 7.5 months, one biopsy per case), so the apparent "progressive resorption" is cross-sectional between patients, not longitudinal within a patient.
- Authors' forward plan: continue collecting biopsies at **9 months** after grafting to assess resorption and new-bone maturation over time; call for larger multi-centre studies with direct material comparisons.

## 6. Related Work

- **Galindo-Moreno et al. 2022 (split-mouth RCT, n=10)** — the key human head-to-head of porcine bone mineral vs anorganic bovine bone in lateral sinus augmentation, both mixed 20:80 with autologous cortical bone, at 6 months: no group difference (grafted-area mineralized tissue 29.24% vs 32.51%, p=.570; remnant biomaterial 31.00% vs 31.21%, p>.999). Provides the controlled comparison this case series lacks.
- **Wang et al. 2025 (SR+MA, 10 studies, 452 sites)** — porcine vs bovine xenograft in MSFA and ARP: no significant difference in new bone (WMD 1.5, 95% CI −1.46–4.46, p=0.321, I²=44.6%) or any secondary histomorphometric/radiographic outcome.
- **Mordenfeld et al. 2010 (11.5-year re-entry biopsies)** — deproteinized bovine bone shows no clinically meaningful resorption over 11 years (lamellar bone 44.7%, marrow 38%, DPBB 17.3%); the contrast with TRAP-positive degradation at 7.5 months here reflects the porcine-vs-bovine resorption difference the authors discuss [26,52,61].
- **Nguyen et al. 2026 (prospective, 42 patients / 60 sites, RBH ≤4 mm)** — the closest design analogue in severe atrophy with 6-month histomorphometry, but with an allograft + β-TCP/HA + PRF composite (NB 31.28 ± 17.71%, RG 15.48 ± 12.33%).
- **Mucocele / membrane-perforation literature** cited by the authors [32,33,34,67] — well-managed perforations, including those created deliberately to enucleate sinus pathology, did not compromise healing in their series.
- **Porcine-xenograft material properties** [17,48–52] — greater porosity and interconnected pore systems than bovine, faster resorption, proteomic similarity to human demineralized bone matrix.

## 7. Glossary

- **RBH (Residual Bone Height)**: vertical bone height between the alveolar crest and the sinus floor before augmentation; ≤2 mm defines the severely atrophic tier in this study.
- **Collagenated porcine xenograft**: porcine-derived bone mineral that retains collagen, marketed for handling and early remodelling; here THE Graft™, 0.25–1.00 mm particles.
- **BSM (Bone Substitute Material)**: residual graft particles still present in the biopsy, quantified as an area percentage.
- **Hybrid bone**: newly formed bone deposited directly onto the surface of a graft particle, the histological signature of osteoconductive integration.
- **TRAP (Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase)**: osteoclast/macrophage enzyme marker; TRAP-positive multinucleated giant cells indicate active biomaterial degradation.
- **MNGC (Multinucleated Giant Cell)**: fused macrophage-derived cell at a foreign-body surface; small numbers indicate a mild foreign-body response.
- **Crock-eye technique**: the authors' cited method of accessing and enucleating a sinus mucocele through a controlled perforation of the Schneiderian membrane.
- **CARE guidelines**: CAse REport reporting checklist used for case reports and case series.
