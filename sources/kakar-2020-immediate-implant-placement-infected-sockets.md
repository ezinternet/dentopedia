## Why Ingested

Ingested in the 2026-09-29 surveillance on immediate implants with acute/purulent infection. It is the only held cohort testing a laser-decontamination plus in-situ-hardening alloplastic graft protocol in infected sockets, complementing the held syntheses [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]] and [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/saijeva-2020-immediate-implant-placement-non-infected-sockets]], and it is cited by [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/almugeiren-2024-placement-immediate-dental-implants-extraction]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective consecutive cohort (68 patients, 126 implants) of immediate implants in infected sockets using Er,Cr:YSGG laser decontamination, in situ hardening alloplastic graft in the buccal gap, and non-submerged healing.

105 of 110 implants (95.45%) survived after prosthetic loading (16 implants in 8 patients lost to follow-up; loading at 136 +/- 73 days).

Authors conclude survival is similar to published rates for non-infected sites; abstract-only, no infection definition or follow-up length available, single-center uncontrolled series.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Standardized three-part protocol for infected sockets: Er,Cr:YSGG laser decontamination, in situ hardening alloplastic bone graft substitute filling the implant-labial plate gap, non-submerged healing.
- 95.45% implant survival (105/110) after loading.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Retrospective record review identifying 68 patients treated with the protocol; 126 implants (65 maxilla, 61 mandible).
- Loading 136 +/- 73 days (range 37-400) after placement.
- Infection definition (acute vs chronic, purulence), laser settings, graft product, antibiotic regimen and follow-up duration are not in the abstract; full text not retrieved.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Lost to follow-up: 8 patients (16 implants) -> analyzable 110 implants.
- Survival: 105/110 = 95.45% (5 failures) after prosthetic loading.
- Note: 126 - 16 = 110; survival is per implant among those followed, not intention-to-treat.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingestion; no data on infection classification, follow-up length, bone/soft-tissue outcomes or complication timing.
- Uncontrolled (no non-infected or non-laser comparator), so the laser's specific contribution cannot be separated from the graft and non-submerged healing.
- 12.7% (16/126) of implants lost to follow-up; survival computed on remainder.
- Discrepancies noted: [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/almugeiren-2024-placement-immediate-dental-implants-extraction]] lists this study among RCTs (n=68) although it is a retrospective record review; [[sources/elaskary-2024-vst-immediate-implant-infected-sockets]] cites 48.75 months follow-up, which the abstract does not state.

## 7. Glossary

- **Er,Cr:YSGG laser**: erbium, chromium-doped yttrium-scandium-gallium-garnet laser used here for socket decontamination.
- **In situ hardening alloplastic graft**: synthetic bone substitute that sets within the defect.
- **Non-submerged healing**: implant left exposed to the oral cavity (transmucosal) rather than covered by flap.
