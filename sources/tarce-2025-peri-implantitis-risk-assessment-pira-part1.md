## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/peri-implantitis/smeets-2014-peri-implantitis-definition-etiology-review]]는 2014년 서술적 리뷰(narrative review) 수준으로 위험인자를 다루지만, 정량적 근거 등급이나 다중 SR을 아우르는 종합은 없었다. 본 논문(Tarce & Quirynen 2025)은 peri-implantitis 위험인자만을 대상으로 한 최초의 우산리뷰(umbrella review)로, 103편의 SR 후보 중 49편(환자 관련 30편 [15편 MA 포함], 치료 관련 19편 [9편 MA 포함])을 종합해 10개 환자 관련 위험인자를 확정한다. 동일 배치의 [[implants/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]]가 "임플란트 실패"라는 더 넓은 결과지표를 다루는 것과 달리, 본 논문은 "peri-implantitis 발생"이라는 좁고 특정된 결과지표에 집중해 상호 보강한다. 같은 호(JOMI 2025;40(5))에 실린 후속 논문(Part 2, Quirynen et al., PiRA 예측모델 개발)의 근거 기반이 되는 짝 논문이다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 49 systematic reviews (30 patient-related [15 with meta-analysis], 19 treatment-related [9 with meta-analysis]) screening 4,724 records down to 103 SR candidates, aimed at identifying preoperative peri-implantitis risk factors for a clinical risk-assessment tool (PiRA).

Ten patient-related risk factors significantly increased peri-implantitis susceptibility in at least one meta-analysis: history of periodontitis, implant location, smoking, number of teeth lost, recall compliance, and presence of systemic disease (notably diabetes) reached meta-analysis-level significance; bleeding on probing, poor oral hygiene, number of sites with PPD ≥5mm, and occlusal overload showed increased incidence without meta-analysis data. Treatment-related factors with meta-analysis-level significance included implant surface roughness and prosthetic suprastructure characteristics (crown-to-implant ratio, restoration type).

Most included SRs analyzed only one or a few risk factors in isolation without multifactorial modeling, so the relative importance of each factor remains unquantifiable — the review's stated purpose is to feed a subsequent multifactorial predictive tool (PiRA, see Part 2) rather than to rank factors definitively.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(Umbrella Review) — 체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, SR) 49편(환자 관련 30편[메타분석 15편 포함], 치료 관련 19편[메타분석 9편 포함])을 종합, 4,724편의 문헌을 스크리닝해 103편의 SR 후보를 도출한 뒤 임상 위험평가 도구(PiRA) 개발을 위한 술전 peri-implantitis 위험인자를 식별.

메타분석 근거로 유의성이 확인된 환자 관련 위험인자 10개: 치주염 과거력(History of Periodontitis), 임플란트 위치, 흡연(Smoking), 상실 치아 수, 리콜(recall) 순응도, 전신질환(특히 당뇨) 등이 메타분석 수준에서 유의했고, 탐침 시 출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BoP)·구강위생 불량·탐침깊이(Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) ≥5mm 부위 수·교합 과부하는 메타분석 없이 발생률 증가만 보고됨. 치료 관련 인자 중 임플란트 표면 거칠기(Surface Roughness)와 보철 상부구조 특성(Crown-to-Implant Ratio 등)이 메타분석 수준에서 유의했다.

대부분의 개별 SR이 한두 개 위험인자만 단독 분석해 다중 인자 보정(Multifactorial Modeling)이 없어 인자별 상대적 중요도는 정량화 불가능하다는 것이 핵심 한계이며, 저자들은 이를 보완할 다중인자 예측모델(PiRA, Part 2 참조) 개발의 근거 기반으로 본 리뷰를 활용했다고 명시한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review focused specifically on peri-implantitis risk factors (as opposed to broader implant failure or general peri-implant disease epidemiology).
- Identifies 10 patient-related risk factors with meta-analysis-level significance in at least one included SR, separating modifiable (smoking, BoP, plaque control, PPD ≥5mm sites, recall frequency, occlusal overload) from non-modifiable factors (history of periodontitis, implant location, number of teeth lost, systemic disease).
- Catalogs treatment-related confounders (implant surface roughness, cemented vs screw-retained, abutment material/connection, cement remnants, GBR at placement, immediate vs delayed placement, flapless approach, keratinized tissue) separately from patient factors, explicitly for use in a subsequent predictive model.
- Explicitly frames itself as evidence groundwork for a companion clinician-facing risk calculator (PiRA), developed in Part 2 (Quirynen et al., same issue).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review (SR of SRs), following Cochrane Collaboration recommendations and PRISMA 2020.
- **Databases**: PubMed (via MEDLINE) and Embase (via Ovid), English-language, published before August 2021; manual search of 9 journals' last 10 years; reference screening of included SRs.
- **n**: 4,724 records identified (2,161 MEDLINE + 2,563 Embase) → 2,865 after dedup → 103 SRs assessed for eligibility → 49 SRs included (47 database + 2 hand-search).
- **Inclusion criteria**: human subjects >18y; controlled design (≥2 cohorts/treatments compared); reported prevalence/OR/RR/z-score for peri-implantitis; ≥10 participants/group; ≥6 months follow-up.
- **Exclusion criteria**: zirconia/PEEK/custom/non-endosseous/non-screw-type implants; short (<8mm)/narrow (<3mm) implants; tooth-implant supported restorations; retrograde peri-implantitis; socket shield technique; major bone augmentation (fibular/iliac/skin flap, Le Fort I); medically compromised patients (cancer, immunosuppression, radiotherapy, genetic syndromes, Crohn's, neurological disability); antiresorptive medication history.
- **Outcomes**: primary — patient-related risk factors (prevalence, OR, RR, or z-score with 95% CI); secondary — treatment-related confounders (surgical/prosthetic protocol, site characteristics).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Patient-related factors, significant in ≥1 meta-analysis**: history of periodontitis, implant location, smoking, number of teeth lost, recall compliance, systemic disease (diabetes).
- **Patient-related factors, increased incidence but no meta-analysis available**: bleeding on probing, poor oral hygiene, number of sites with PPD ≥5mm, occlusal overload.
- **Patient-related factors, non-significant or unclear evidence**: age, sex, edentulism, chemical agents during SPT, other systemic diseases (cardiovascular disease, rheumatoid arthritis, lung disease, obesity, cancer, depression, osteoporosis), alcohol consumption.
- **Treatment-related factors, significant in ≥1 meta-analysis**: implant surface roughness (rough > moderately/minimally rough); prosthetic suprastructure characteristics (crown-to-implant ratio, suprastructure type — splinted crowns and full-arch fixed prostheses potentially higher risk).
- **Treatment-related factors, higher tendency / non-significant OR-RR**: GBR at implant placement, lack of keratinized tissue, abutment material (metal vs ceramic), abutment connection (external vs internal), cement remnants, surgical strategy (immediate vs delayed placement).
- Cemented restorations showed higher risk than screw-retained in several SRs, but the only meta-analysis on this topic failed to reach statistical significance.
- 30 SRs (15 with MA) on patient-related factors; 19 SRs (9 with MA) on treatment-related factors, tabulated in Appendix Tables 1 and 2 (not reproduced in the extracted text).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Lack of uniformity in peri-implantitis definition/diagnostic bone-level thresholds across included SRs, causing large prevalence variation.
- Lack of uniformity in definition/criteria/threshold of confounding factors across studies.
- No multifactorial modeling in any included SR/MA — relative contribution of each risk factor when others are controlled for remains unknown (only one non-SR study, Jemt 2017, attempted this).
- Interaction between modifiable and non-modifiable risk factors not addressed; would require larger samples and ≥4 subgroups.
- Heterogeneity of study designs (RCT vs cohort vs case-control vs cross-sectional) affects OR/RR comparability.
- RCTs are largely infeasible for many risk factors, so most evidence is observational/retrospective with attendant bias.
- Newer risk factors may lack sufficient SR-level evidence to be captured.
- Future work: development of a multifactorial predictive software tool (PiRA) — carried out in the companion Part 2 paper (Quirynen et al., retrospective study + predictive model).

## 6. Related Work
- Quirynen et al. 2025 (PiRA Part 2, JOMI 40(5):571–578, DOI 10.11607/jomi.11211): companion retrospective study (460 patients, 1,432 implants) that builds the PiRA predictive model on top of this umbrella review's risk-factor findings.
- Smeets et al. 2014: earlier narrative review of peri-implantitis definition/etiology; this umbrella review supersedes it in evidence rigor for risk-factor quantification (though not formally marked as `superseded_by`, since Smeets 2014 retains standalone value for CIST treatment framework, which is out of scope here).
- Dreyer et al. 2018 (ref 11), Ferreira et al. 2018 (ref 12), Monje et al. 2017 (ref 35): individual SRs on epidemiology/periodontitis-as-risk-factor/diabetes that are subsumed as source SRs within this umbrella review.

## 7. Glossary
- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a topic, rather than primary studies.
- **PiRA**: Peri-implantitis Risk Assessment — the clinician-facing risk calculator tool this review's findings feed into (developed in Part 2).
- **BoP**: Bleeding on Probing.
- **PPD**: Probing Pocket Depth.
- **GBR**: Guided Bone Regeneration.
- **OR/RR**: Odds Ratio / Relative Risk — effect-size measures used to quantify risk factor association strength.
