## Why Ingested

Ingested as part of the ongoing topical sweep on mandibular/alveolar "nutrient canals" (NC). This paper's primary focus is bifid mandibular canal (BMC) incidence, but it separately reports NC incidence (58.4–97.6%, including the retromolar canal) using high-resolution 3D-VIBE MRI — a fundamentally different imaging modality from the intraoral periapical radiograph (IOPAR) method used in [[radiology/kumar-2014-incidence-nutrient-canals-hypertensive]] (NC incidence 55.2% in hypertensives vs 36.2% in controls, IOPAR-based). The MRI figure is far higher than the periapical-radiograph figures reported elsewhere in the sweep, which is itself a useful cross-modality signal: NC visibility is heavily detection-method-dependent, so incidence numbers should never be compared across studies without noting the imaging modality.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective imaging study (n=154 patients / 249 evaluable hemi-mandibles after exclusions) using high-resolution 3D-VIBE MRI through the neurovascular bundle (NVB) to classify bifid mandibular canal (BMC) subtypes and, secondarily, to assess nutrient canal (NC) incidence.

BMC was found in 6.4% of hemi-mandibles (Type 1, one NVB splitting into two: 4.4%; Type 2, two separate NVBs: 2.0%); NC (including the retromolar canal) had a much higher MRI-detected incidence of 58.4–97.6%, and BMCs were essentially invisible on panoramic radiography or CT (only 1 of the MRI-identified BMCs was also seen on CT, none on panoramic radiography).

Clinically, MRI substantially outperforms panoramic radiography/CT for detecting both BMC and NC anatomic variants relevant to mandibular anesthesia/surgery risk, but this is a retrospective single-center dataset built from patients already being scanned for mandible lesions (not a healthy-population sample), so incidence figures may not generalize, and the wide NC range (58.4–97.6%) reflects the retrospective, non-standardized scoring rather than a single point estimate.

## 세줄요약

후향적 영상연구(환자 154명 / 제외 후 평가 가능한 반측하악골 249례)로, 고해상도 3D-VIBE MRI를 이용해 신경혈관다발(Neurovascular Bundle, NVB)을 통과 관찰하여 이분 하악관(Bifid Mandibular Canal, BMC) 아형을 분류하고, 부수적으로 영양관(Nutrient Canal, NC) 발생률을 평가했다.

BMC는 반측하악골의 6.4%에서 확인됐고(Type 1, NVB 하나가 둘로 분지: 4.4%; Type 2, 별개의 NVB 두 개: 2.0%), 후구치관(retromolar canal)을 포함한 NC의 MRI 검출 발생률은 58.4–97.6%로 훨씬 높았으며, BMC는 파노라마 방사선사진이나 CT에서는 사실상 거의 보이지 않았다(MRI로 확인된 BMC 중 CT로도 확인된 것은 1례뿐, 파노라마 방사선사진으로는 0례).

임상적으로 MRI는 하악마취·수술 위험과 관련된 BMC·NC 해부학적 변이 검출에서 파노라마 방사선사진/CT보다 크게 우수하지만, 이 연구는 이미 하악골 병변으로 MRI를 촬영 중이던 환자군에서 구성된 후향적 단일기관 데이터셋(건강한 일반 인구 표본이 아님)이라 발생률 수치가 일반화되지 않을 수 있고, NC의 넓은 범위(58.4–97.6%)는 단일 점추정치라기보다 후향적·비표준화된 판독을 반영한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies bifid mandibular canal (BMC) incidence using high-resolution 3D-VIBE MRI directly visualizing the neurovascular bundle (NVB), rather than inferring canal duplication from a bony canal outline on 2D/3D X-ray imaging.
- Proposes a 3-tier BMC subtype classification (Type 0 = no bifidity, Type 1 = single NVB splitting into two, Type 2 = two fully separate NVBs).
- Directly cross-validates MRI-identified BMC cases against panoramic radiography (PR) and CT on the same patients, quantifying how often each conventional modality actually detects a bifidity that MRI confirms.
- Secondarily reports nutrient canal (NC) incidence via MRI, adding an MRI-based data point to a literature otherwise dominated by periapical-radiograph and CBCT NC-incidence studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective imaging study.
- **Population**: 154 patient MRI datasets extracted from the institutional archive (patients originally scanned for mandible lesions, January 2015–April 2016), each contributing a right and left hemi-mandible evaluated independently → 308 candidate hemi-mandibles, with 59 excluded for NVB morphology issues or imaging artifacts, leaving 249 evaluable hemi-mandible cases.
- **Imaging**: High-resolution MRI using a three-dimensional volumetric interpolated breath-hold examination (3D-VIBE) sequence, tracing the neurovascular bundle (NVB) through the mandibular canal.
- **Classification**: Each hemi-mandible scored as Type 0 (no BMC — single NVB, no bifidity), Type 1 (BMC — one NVB with two NVBs occurring along its route), or Type 2 (BMC — two anatomically separate NVBs).
- **Cross-modality validation**: For each MRI-identified BMC case, the corresponding panoramic radiograph and/or CT (where available) was checked for whether the bifidity was independently detectable.
- **Secondary outcome**: Nutrient canal (NC) presence, including the retromolar canal, was also scored on the same MRI dataset.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- BMC overall incidence: 6.4% of 249 hemi-mandibles (Types 1 + 2 combined).
- Subtype distribution: Type 0 (no BMC) 233/249 (93.6%); Type 1 11/249 (4.4%); Type 2 5/249 (2.0%).
- NC (including retromolar canal) incidence on MRI: 58.4–97.6%.
- Cross-modality detection of MRI-confirmed BMC: essentially undetected by panoramic radiography (0 cases) or CT (1 case only, out of the MRI-positive set).
- Conclusion stated by the authors: BMC incidence per MRI was 6.4%; because BMCs were largely unverifiable by PR or CT, MRI is recommended for BMC assessment.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single-institution study drawn from patients already undergoing MRI for mandible lesions — not a healthy/screening population, so incidence estimates (both BMC 6.4% and NC 58.4–97.6%) may not generalize to the general dental population.
- 59/308 hemi-mandibles (~19%) were excluded for NVB morphology/artifact reasons, a substantial exclusion fraction that could bias the remaining sample.
- The wide NC incidence range (58.4–97.6%) suggests non-standardized or variably-defined scoring across the cohort rather than a single robust estimate; the abstract-only source (full text unavailable via PMC) means the exact scoring criteria and subgroup breakdown for NC could not be verified in this ingest.
- No statistical testing of BMC/NC prevalence against clinical covariates (age, sex, laterality) is reported in the abstract.
- Future work: prospective MRI screening in a general (non-lesion) population; standardized NC scoring criteria; direct inter-modality (MRI vs CBCT vs panoramic) agreement statistics (kappa) for both BMC and NC.

## 7. Glossary

- **BMC (Bifid Mandibular Canal)**: an anatomic variant in which the mandibular canal (containing the inferior alveolar neurovascular bundle) divides into two or more distinct branches, clinically relevant to failed/incomplete inferior alveolar nerve block and to surgical/implant risk of neurovascular injury.
- **NC (Nutrient Canal)**: small accessory canals branching from the mandibular canal (or independent of it) that carry neurovascular structures to the alveolar bone/periodontium; includes the retromolar canal.
- **NVB (Neurovascular Bundle)**: the inferior alveolar nerve, artery, and vein traveling together within the mandibular canal.
- **3D-VIBE (Volumetric Interpolated Breath-hold Examination)**: a T1-weighted, fat-suppressed, gradient-echo 3D MRI sequence used for high-resolution soft-tissue/neural imaging.
- **PR (Panoramic Radiography)**: standard 2D dental screening radiograph, used here as a conventional-modality comparator to MRI.
