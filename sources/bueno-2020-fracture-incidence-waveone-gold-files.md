## Why Ingested
Prospective clinical study evaluating fracture incidence of WaveOne Gold reciprocating single-file system in molar root canals — fills a gap in clinical fracture data for heat-treated NiTi instruments. Reinforces [[endodontics/shaping/almohareb-2023-cyclic-fatigue-niti-autoclave]] on cyclic fatigue resistance of heat-treated NiTi.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective clinical study by 3 calibrated specialists treating 750 maxillary and mandibular molars (2691 root canals) with WaveOne Gold single-file reciprocating system over 12 months.
Fracture incidence data reported for 1104 WaveOne Gold instruments used (38 small, 750 primary, 228 medium, 88 large) in canals with curvature <45°.
WaveOne Gold heat-treated reciprocating system shows low fracture incidence in experienced hands for single-session molar treatments with moderate curvature.

## 세줄요약
3명의 숙련된 전문의가 750개의 상하악 대구치(2691개 근관)를 WaveOne Gold 단일파일 시스템으로 12개월간 전향적으로 치료함.
1104개의 WaveOne Gold 기구(스몰 38, 프라이머리 750, 미디엄 228, 라지 88) 사용 시 45° 미만 만곡에서 파절 발생률 보고됨.
숙련된 술자의 단일세션 대구치 치료에서 열처리된 왕복운동 시스템의 낮은 파절 발생률을 시사함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First prospective clinical study reporting fracture incidence of WaveOne Gold files in molar teeth
- Large sample size: 750 molars, 2691 canals, 1104 instruments over 12 months
- Standardized protocol by 3 calibrated specialists reduces operator variability
- Single-session treatment protocol for all cases
- Curvature limitation (<45°) clearly defined using Schneider method

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective clinical study
- **n**: 750 maxillary and mandibular molars (2691 root canals), 1104 WaveOne Gold instruments
- **Outcomes**: Instrument fracture incidence by file size (small, primary, medium, large)
- **Inclusion**: Molars with curvature <45° (Schneider method)
- **Operators**: 3 experienced, calibrated specialists
- **Protocol**: Single-session treatments, standardized technique

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Experienced specialists only — results may not generalize to students or general practitioners
- Single file system (WaveOne Gold) — no direct comparison with other systems
- Curvature <45° exclusion — severe curvatures not evaluated
- Single-session treatments only — multi-visit scenarios not studied
- No control group with other instrumentation systems
- Exact fracture incidence numbers not available in abstract (full text needed for precise rates)

## 7. Glossary
- **WaveOne Gold (웨이브원 골드)**: 열처리된 니켈-티타늄 합금(골드 와이어 기술)을 사용한 단일파일 왕복운동 시스템
- **Reciprocating motion (왕복운동)**: 시계 방향과 반시계 방향으로 번갈아 회전하는 기구 운동 방식
- **Cyclic fatigue resistance (순환 피로 저항성)**: 반복적인 굽힘 응력 하에서 파절 없이 견디는 능력
- **Schneider method (슈나이더 방법)**: 근관 만곡 각도를 측정하는 표준화된 방법
- **Single-file system (단일파일 시스템)**: 한 개의 파일로 근관 성형을 완료하는 시스템
