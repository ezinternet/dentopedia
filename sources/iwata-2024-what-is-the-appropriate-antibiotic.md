## Why Ingested

No position paper — AAOMS 2022 [[drug/mronj/ruggiero-2022-aaoms-mronj-position-paper-update]] included — specifies perioperative antibiotic regimens for tooth extraction under antiresorptives; JACOJ 2023 merely defers to surgical prophylaxis guidelines. This is the **first study** to survey actual antibiotic practice (type/dose/duration) during extractions in high-dose denosumab (Denosumab, DMAb) cancer patients across 10 Japanese hospitals and correlate it with MRONJ onset (173 teeth, 18.4% tooth-level incidence). It supplies the high-dose-oncology counterpart to the same group's low-dose bisphosphonate single-dose protocol [[drug/mronj/iwata-2025-amoxicillin-duration-mronj-extraction-japan-retrospective]], extends the antibiotic-prophylaxis evidence base assembled in [[drug/mronj/vidovic-juras-2024-antibiotic-prophylaxis-dental-procedures]], and grounds the antimicrobial-stewardship (AMR) argument developed in [[drug/mronj/kwon-2023-mronj-position-papers-what-changed]].

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter retrospective study (10 Japanese hospitals, 2012–2022): 68 cancer patients on high-dose denosumab 120 mg q4wk underwent 173 tooth extractions; MRONJ developed after 32/173 teeth (18.4%).

Antibiotic practice was highly non-uniform across hospitals/surgeons; postoperative antibiotics were given after 91.9% of extractions (amoxicillin 78.0% of courses), yet no significant association existed between MRONJ and antibiotic dosage or duration (p=0.164–1.000 across all comparisons).

In the amoxicillin-only subset (123 teeth, 51 patients; MRONJ 21/123 teeth, 17.0%), neither dose (500 vs 750 mg/day, p=1.000) nor duration (2 vs 3 days, p=1.000; >3 days: 4/47 teeth, 8.5%) affected MRONJ — supporting a single preoperative amoxicillin dose as sufficient.

## 세줄요약

다기관 후향 연구(일본 10개 병원, 2012–2022): 고용량 데노수맙(Denosumab, DMAb) 120 mg/4주 투여 암 환자 68명에서 발치 173치 시행, 발치 후 약물관련 악골괴사(Medication-Related Osteonecrosis of the Jaw, MRONJ) 32/173치(18.4%) 발생.

항생제 투여 실태는 병원·술자 간 편차가 컸고, 발치 후 투여율은 91.9%(아목시실린(Amoxicillin, AMPC)이 코스의 78.0%)였으나 MRONJ 발생과 항생제 용량·투여기간 간 유의한 연관은 없었다(모든 비교에서 p=0.164–1.000).

AMPC 단독 투여 치아만 분석(123치, 51명; MRONJ 21/123치, 17.0%)해도 용량(500 vs 750 mg/day, p=1.000)·기간(2 vs 3일, p=1.000; 3일 초과 시 4/47치, 8.5%) 모두 영향 없음 — 술전 AMPC 단일 투여로 충분하다는 결론.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study worldwide to investigate current perioperative antibiotic administration during tooth extraction in high-dose denosumab patients and its association with MRONJ development
- Documents large inter-hospital/inter-surgeon variability in antibiotic type, dosage, and duration — quantifying the guideline vacuum (AAOMS 2022 silent; JACOJ 2023 defers to JCOPG-APS 2016, which has no antiresorptive category)
- Shows escalating antibiotic intensity does NOT buy MRONJ protection: no significant effect of route (oral vs IV), dose, or duration (up to 7 days) in 173 teeth, replicated in the 123-tooth AMPC-only subset
- Provides an AMR (antimicrobial resistance) stewardship argument: ~40% of teeth received antibiotics >3 days despite zero demonstrated benefit over shorter courses
- Concludes a single preoperative AMPC dose is likely adequate — aligning high-dose denosumab practice with the JCOPG-APS 2016 surgical prophylaxis standard (single preoperative dose to max 48 h)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design/population**: Retrospective medical-record review; inclusion ≥18 y, denosumab 120 mg q4wk for bone metastases/multiple myeloma, tooth extraction at 10 hospitals 2012–2022; exclusion: pre-existing MRONJ at extraction, jaw radiation history, jaw metastatic disease, opt-out
- **Sample**: 68 patients, 173 extracted teeth; primary outcome MRONJ development per AAOMS 2022 (exposed bone/fistula >8 weeks; stage 0 excluded)
- **Exposure variables**: perioperative antibiotic specifics — preoperative (type, dose, route; 71 antibiotic procedures: AMPC 250/500/1000 mg per dose, CLDM 600 mg, ABPC 1000 mg, CMZ 1000 mg, CTRX 1000 mg, CFPN-PI 100 mg, CVA/AMPC 250 mg) and postoperative (159 procedures: AMPC 500/750/1000 mg/day, AZM, CAM, CMZ, CLDM, CTRX, LVFX, ABPC+AMPC), plus duration (1–7 days)
- **AMPC-focused subset**: 123 teeth in 51 patients extracted under amoxicillin administration for regimen-homogeneous analysis
- **Statistics**: Ekuseru-Toukei 2016; Fisher's exact test / chi-squared test for categorical variables; significance p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**All-teeth analysis (n=173)**: MRONJ 32/173 teeth (18.4%). Preoperative antibiotics in 41.0% of teeth (vs 59.0% none); among 71 preoperative courses AMPC 52.1%, CLDM 22.5%, IV antibiotics 21.2%. Postoperative antibiotics in 91.9% (vs 8.1%); among 159 postoperative courses AMPC 78.0%.

**No significant associations (all teeth)**:

| Comparison | p |
|---|---|
| Route sequence, both pre+post (oral→oral vs IV-containing, 4 groups) | 0.336 |
| Duration, both pre+post (2 vs 3 vs ≥4 days) | 0.633 |
| Duration, only postoperative (1–2 vs 3 vs 4–5 days) | 0.164 |

Of 71 teeth with antibiotics >3 days, MRONJ in 11 (15.5%); longest course 7 days (2 teeth) — no MRONJ.

**AMPC-only subset (n=123 teeth, 51 patients)**: MRONJ 21/123 teeth (17.0%); patient-level MRONJ 15/51 (29.4%) — stage 1: 9, stage 2: 6, stage 3: 0. Characteristics: median age 69.0 (23–88); breast 35.3%, lung 23.5%, prostate 17.6%; chemotherapy 45.1%; diabetes 17.6%; steroid use 17.6%; median denosumab duration 6.0 months (range 0–108); drug holiday before extraction in only 4 patients (7.8%).

|---|---|
| Pre+post AMPC dose combinations (4 groups) | 0.269 |
| Only-postop daily dose (500 vs 750 mg/day) | 1.000 |
| Duration, pre+post (2 vs 3 days) | 1.000 |
| Duration, only postop (1–2 vs 3–4 days) | 0.264 |

>3 days AMPC: MRONJ 4/47 teeth (8.5%).

**Benchmark context cited**: high-dose denosumab vs zoledronate MRONJ risk 9.6% vs 4.8% (p=0.009, Ikesue); meta-analytic risk ratio 1.41 [1.01–1.95], p=0.04 (Jiang); German IV-penicillin regimen (Matsumoto 2017): MRONJ 3/40 teeth (7.5%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design; confounding by unmeasured factors cannot be excluded — authors acknowledge other determinants (e.g., ARA duration, symptomatic tooth presence per Soutome et al.) were outside the focused scope
- No multivariable adjustment; small subgroup cells forced coarse category merging (e.g., "among four groups", "2 days vs 3 days")
- Japanese-only cohort — recommended dosages may differ across ethnicities (per Tamargo et al. pharmacogenetic differences)
- Findings specific to high-dose denosumab; low-dose populations need separate evidence (later supplied by the same group's 2025 study)
- Authors call for prospective study designs to confirm the single-preoperative-dose policy

## 6. Related Work

- **Cabras et al. systematic review** (ref 22): 17 studies found lack of evidence that systemic antibiotics reduce MRONJ risk after extraction; 16 concerned bisphosphonates, only ONE prior study covered high-dose denosumab — the gap this paper fills
- **Matsumoto et al. 2017** (ref 23): German 19-patient/40-tooth study proposing intensive IV penicillin (10,000,000 IU) perioperatively; MRONJ 7.5% — deemed insufficient evidence by the review; Iwata's data show simpler oral regimens perform comparably without proven dose-duration benefit
- **JCOPG-APS 2016**: Japanese surgical antimicrobial prophylaxis guideline — AMPC first-line (CLDM/AZM/CAM if β-lactam allergic), single preoperative dose up to 48 h; no category exists for antiresorptive patients, which this study addresses empirically
- **Ikesue / Jiang**: high-dose denosumab carries higher MRONJ risk than zoledronate (9.6% vs 4.8%, p=0.009; RR 1.41) — motivating prevention research in this highest-risk group
- **Soutome et al.** (ref 26): ARA duration and symptomatic-tooth presence as MRONJ risk factors — reminder that antibiotic tuning cannot neutralize non-antibiotic determinants

## 7. Glossary

- **MRONJ** — Medication-related osteonecrosis of the jaw; exposed bone/intra- or extraoral fistula >8 weeks in antiresorptive-exposed patients without jaw radiation/metastasis (AAOMS 2022 staging; stage 0 excluded here)
- **High-dose denosumab (Dmab)** — 120 mg SC every 4 weeks (Xgeva) for bone metastases/multiple myeloma; RANKL inhibitor with higher MRONJ risk than zoledronate
- **AMPC** — Amoxicillin; first-line prophylactic antibiotic in Japanese guidelines (250–1000 mg/dose preoperative; 500–1000 mg/day postoperative in this cohort)
- **CLDM** — Clindamycin; alternative for β-lactam allergy (600 mg/dose)
- **JCOPG-APS 2016** — Japanese Clinical Practice Guidelines for Antimicrobial Prophylaxis in Surgery; recommends single preoperative dose to maximum 48 h
- **JACOJ 2023** — Japanese Allied Committee on Osteonecrosis of the Jaw position paper; defers antibiotic decisions to JCOPG-APS 2016
- **AMR** — Antimicrobial resistance; the stewardship rationale for avoiding unnecessary prolonged postoperative courses
- **Drug holiday** — Interruption of denosumab before extraction; practiced in only 7.8% of this cohort
