## Why Ingested

사용자가 요청한 PMC 전문 논문으로, 근관 내 투약제(intracanal medicament)로서 수산화칼슘[Ca(OH)2]의 항균 효과에 대한 근거를 다룬다. 60개 치아·21일 E. faecalis 감염 모델을 이용한 본 in-vitro 연구는 Ca(OH)2의 항균 열세를 CHX 및 RG-Dent 대비 정량적으로 제시하고, 적용 기간(2일 vs 7일)의 영향도 검증한다 — 기존 [[endodontics/regenerative/alovisi-2026-intracanal-medicaments-regenerative-endo-sr-ma]]가 다룬 intracanal medicament 주제를 일반 근관치료(비-재생근관치료) 맥락으로 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary


In-vitro comparative study (60 human single-rooted teeth, 21-day E.

faecalis intracanal infection model, 3 medicaments × 2 durations, n=10/subgroup): both 2% CHX gel (mean logCFU reduction 4.94±0.23) and RG-Dent (4.77±0.26, metronidazole/iodoform/dexamethasone paste) were significantly more effective than Ca(OH)2 (3.69±0.27) against E.

faecalis (P<0.001, Cohen's d>4); CHX showed marginal superiority over RG-Dent (P=0.036, d=0.69, medium); application duration (2 vs 7 days) had no significant effect on efficacy (P=0.78) and no interaction with medicament type (P=0.97).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First rigorous peer-reviewed comparative evaluation of RG-Dent (metronidazole + iodoform + dexamethasone paste) against E. faecalis — previously not systematically studied.
- Directly quantifies Ca(OH)2's antibacterial inferiority against mature (21-day) E. faecalis biofilm relative to CHX and RG-Dent, with large-to-very-large effect sizes.
- Tests whether extending intracanal medicament dwell time from 2 to 7 days improves antimicrobial efficacy — finds no benefit, challenging traditional 7–14 day protocols.
- Uses a standardized, blinded, adequately powered (a priori sample size via two-way ANOVA, f=0.40, power=0.80) in-vitro infection model with 21-day E. faecalis incubation to simulate mature, persistent intracanal infection rather than acute contamination.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Specimens**: 60 human mandibular premolars, single root/single straight canal (Vertucci Type I), standardized to 16mm root length. Excluded: caries, cracks, prior endodontic treatment, calcification.
- **Canal preparation**: ProTaper Universal NiTi rotary to size 30/.09 taper; irrigation with 5.25% NaOCl + 17% EDTA (3 min) + 5.25% NaOCl with ultrasonic activation (5 min) + saline final rinse. Apex sealed with flowable composite; external root coated with nail varnish. Autoclaved; sterility verified by BHI broth incubation.
- **Infection model**: E. faecalis ATCC 29212, 0.5 McFarland (~1.5×10^8 CFU/mL), 50µL inoculum per canal, anaerobic incubation (85% N2/10% H2/5% CO2) at 37°C for 21 days, BHI broth replenished every 48h — establishes long-term mature intracanal infection (not acute).
- **Groups**: n=20 each — Ca(OH)2 (Metapaste), 2% CHX gel (Clopexid), RG-Dent (YG-Dent) — each split into 2-day and 7-day application subgroups (n=10 each).
- **Sampling**: Pre-treatment (S1) and post-treatment (S2) via saline fill + circumferential filing + 3 paper points to working length; serial dilution, plating on Mueller-Hinton agar, 24h incubation, blinded CFU counting (logCFU/mL). Medicaments delivered via lentulo spiral filler; coronal seal with Cavit.
- **Outcome**: logCFU reduction = logCFU(S1) − logCFU(S2).
- **Statistics**: R v4.2.1, α=0.05. Two-way ANOVA (medicament × duration), one-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD (or Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn's if assumptions violated), paired t-tests/Wilcoxon for within-group changes, effect sizes (partial η², Cohen's d), 95% CI. Sample size a priori for f=0.40/power=0.80 (minimum 54; 60 enrolled).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Baseline**: mean 5.11±0.21 logCFU/mL across all 60 samples; no significant baseline differences among 6 subgroups (F(5,54)=0.82, P=0.54).
- **Within-group reductions** (mean logCFU before → after, reduction, Cohen's d):
  - RG-Dent 2-day: 5.11→0.32, reduction 4.79, d=19.7; 7-day: 5.09→0.34, reduction 4.75, d=18.7
  - CHX 2-day: 5.12→0.18, reduction 4.94, d=22.9; 7-day: 5.10→0.17, reduction 4.93, d=22.7
  - Ca(OH)2 2-day: 5.13→1.47, reduction 3.66, d=15.0; 7-day: 5.11→1.40, reduction 3.71, d=14.1
  - All reductions statistically significant (P<0.05).
- **Main effect of medicament**: F(2,54)=178.9, P<0.001, η²=0.869 (~87% of variance).
- **Main effect of duration**: F(1,54)=0.078, P=0.781, η²=0.001 (not significant).
- **Interaction**: F(2,54)=0.026, P=0.974, η²=0.001 (not significant).
- **Tukey HSD pairwise**: CHX vs Ca(OH)2: mean diff 1.25 (95% CI 1.10–1.40), P<0.001, d=5.08 (very large). RG-Dent vs Ca(OH)2: mean diff 1.09 (95% CI 0.92–1.25), P<0.001, d=4.13 (very large). CHX vs RG-Dent: mean diff 0.16 (95% CI 0.02–0.32), P=0.036, d=0.69 (medium); post-hoc power only 56% for this comparison — underpowered, larger sample needed.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro model cannot replicate host immune response, periapical tissue interaction, or in-vivo fluid dynamics.
- Monospecies (E. faecalis only) infection model does not reflect polymicrobial nature of clinical endodontic infections.
- Cytotoxicity to periapical tissues and effects on dentin structure/properties not assessed.
- RG-Dent contains metronidazole; effects on fetal organogenesis not fully characterized (relevant consideration for pregnant patients), though human data have not shown a clear teratogenic signal.
- Only two application time points evaluated (2 and 7 days); combination therapies (e.g., Ca(OH)2 + CHX) not tested.
- CHX vs RG-Dent comparison was underpowered (56% post-hoc power) — authors recommend larger samples to confirm this smaller effect.
- CFU counting captures only viable culturable bacteria, not total bacterial burden or biofilm architecture.
- Authors state translation to clinical practice requires confirmation in clinical (in-vivo) studies.

## 6. Related Work

- Evans et al. — E. faecalis maintains intracellular pH homeostasis via proton pumps even at pH 11–12, explaining Ca(OH)2 resistance.
- Siqueira and Lopes — dentin buffering reduces effective pH at the medicament-dentin interface.
- Teja et al. (n=400) — Ca(OH)2 alone significantly less effective than combinations with CHX, chitosan, or nanoparticles against E. faecalis in infected dentin.
- Çorak et al. — various intracanal medicaments outperformed Ca(OH)2 in simulated internal resorption cavities.
- Gomes et al.; Krithikadatta et al. — CHX broad-spectrum activity, substantivity (up to 12 weeks), dentinal tubule penetration; 2% CHX gel more effective than Ca(OH)2.
- Saatchi et al. (systematic review) — CHX (alone or combined with Ca(OH)2) more effective than Ca(OH)2 alone — corroborated by this study's d=5.08 finding.
- Parhizkar et al.; Hoshino et al.; Rahmati et al. — triple/double antibiotic paste (TAP/DAP) superiority over Ca(OH)2, with discoloration/cytotoxicity/resistance concerns.
- Sjögren et al. — basis for traditional 7–14 day medicament application recommendation, challenged by this study's duration-null finding.
- Ordinola-Zapata et al. (review) — notes limited evidence supporting 7-day over shorter intervals.

## 7. Glossary

- **E. faecalis**: Enterococcus faecalis, gram-positive facultative anaerobe, key pathogen in persistent/failed endodontic infections (detected in 24–77% of failed treatments).
- **Ca(OH)2**: Calcium hydroxide, traditional gold-standard intracanal medicament (pH ≈12.5, hydroxyl-ion-mediated antimicrobial action).
- **CHX**: Chlorhexidine, cationic bisbiguanide antiseptic; disrupts bacterial membranes, has substantivity in dentin.
- **RG-Dent**: Commercial intracanal medicament paste (metronidazole + iodoform + dexamethasone).
- **TAP/DAP**: Triple/double antibiotic paste.
- **logCFU reduction**: Log10 colony-forming-unit reduction, primary outcome measure of antibacterial efficacy.
- **Cohen's d**: Standardized effect size; d>4 considered "very large" in this study's framing.
- **Vertucci Type I**: Single canal from pulp chamber to apex (canal morphology classification).
