## Three-line Summary

Clinical RCT (J Esthet Restor Dent 2025, n=20 endocrowns, 10 glazed vs 10 adjusted+polished, endodontically treated lower first molars, CAD/CAM monolithic zirconia) evaluating enamel wear of opposing teeth over 2-year follow-up using intraoral scanner and Geomagic software.

Enamel wear was 0.422 ± 0.130 mm (polished) vs 0.491 ± 0.105 mm (glazed) at 2 years, with no statistically significant difference (p=0.108); 100% survival in both groups at all follow-up intervals.

Both glazing and polishing of monolithic zirconia endocrowns produce clinically acceptable antagonist enamel wear at 2 years; the small sample limits detection of a clinically meaningful difference between finishing methods.

## 세줄요약

임상 무작위대조시험 (J Esthet Restor Dent 2025, n=20, 유약 처리 10 vs 조정·연마 10, 근관치료 후 하악 제1대구치 CAD/CAM 단일구조 지르코니아 엔도크라운) — 2년간 대합치 법랑질 마모를 구강 내 스캐너+Geomagic 소프트웨어로 정량 평가.

2년 후 법랑질 마모: 연마군 0.422 ± 0.130 mm, 유약군 0.491 ± 0.105 mm — 통계적 유의차 없음(p=0.108); 양군 모두 100% 생존율.

단일구조 지르코니아 엔도크라운에서 유약 처리와 연마 모두 2년 기준 임상적으로 허용 가능한 법랑질 마모; 소규모 표본으로 임상적으로 의미 있는 차이 검출에 한계.

## 1. Key Results
- 20 patients; zirconia endocrowns on endodontically treated lower first molars; CAD/CAM
- ZG (glazed, n=10) vs ZP (adjusted+polished, n=10)
- 2-year follow-up: 6, 12, 18, 24 months; intraoral scanner + Geomagic software
- Survival: 100% (no replacements)
- Enamel wear ZP: 0.422±0.130mm; ZG: 0.491±0.105mm; NS (p=0.108)
- Clinical significance: both glazing and polishing are acceptable for endocrowns
