## Why Ingested

기존 [[resin/theracem-bisco-product-reference]]는 TheraCem의 지르코니아·법랑질·상아질 µSBS 데이터(Mahrous 2020)만 다루고 있어, 근관 내(post space) 상아질에 대한 최적 전처리 프로토콜은 공백이었다. 본 논문은 TheraCem 합착 전 CHX vs 이소프로필알코올(IA) vs 무처리(대조) 조건화가 fiber post push-out 결합강도(PBS)에 미치는 영향을 직접 비교해 그 공백을 메운다. 또한 [[resin-bonding/kiuru-2021-mmp-inhibitors-dentin-bonding-sr-ma]]의 "CHX는 즉시 결합강도에 영향 없음(p=0.308)"이라는 SR+MA 결론과 달리, 본 연구(self-adhesive cement 맥락)에서는 CHX가 즉시·후기 PBS 모두를 대조군 대비 유의하게 향상시켜 — 산부식 접착제와 non-etch self-adhesive cement 간 CHX 효과의 맥락 의존성을 시사한다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (54 single-canal mandibular premolars, 6 subgroups of n=9) comparing distilled-water control, 2% chlorhexidine (CHX), and 70% isopropyl alcohol (IA) root-dentin conditioning before fiber post cementation with TheraCem self-adhesive cement, testing immediate (24h) and late (post-thermocycling) push-out bond strength (PBS).

The IA group had the highest PBS (21.12 MPa immediate) and the control group the lowest after thermocycling (7.48 MPa); IA significantly outperformed both CHX and control at all timepoints and root levels (P<0.001), while CHX outperformed the water control (P<0.05); PBS declined significantly after thermocycling and from cervical to apical root thirds in every group.

Isopropyl alcohol conditioning of root-canal dentin before self-adhesive resin cement cementation of fiber posts meaningfully improves immediate and long-term bond durability compared to chlorhexidine or no conditioning — but the in vitro thermocycling protocol (5000 cycles ≈ 6 months) cannot fully predict clinical longevity.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 연구 (하악 단근관 소구치 54개, 6개 하위군 각 n=9) — 파이버 포스트를 TheraCem 자가접착 레진시멘트(Self-Adhesive Resin Cement)로 합착하기 전 증류수 대조군 vs 2% 클로르헥시딘(Chlorhexidine, CHX) vs 70% 이소프로필알코올(Isopropyl Alcohol, IA)로 근관 상아질을 전처리 후 즉시(24시간)·후기(열순환 후) 푸시아웃 결합강도(Push-out Bond Strength, PBS) 비교.

IA군이 즉시 PBS 최고치(21.12 MPa), 대조군이 열순환 후 최저치(7.48 MPa)를 기록; IA가 모든 시점·치근 부위에서 CHX·대조군을 유의하게 압도(P<0.001)했고, CHX는 증류수 대조군보다 우위(P<0.05); 모든 군에서 열순환 후, 그리고 치경부→치근단부로 갈수록 PBS가 유의하게 감소.

자가접착 레진시멘트로 파이버 포스트를 합착하기 전 이소프로필알코올로 근관 상아질을 전처리하면 클로르헥시딘이나 무처리 대비 즉시·장기 결합 내구성이 유의하게 향상되나, in vitro 열순환 프로토콜(5000회≈6개월)은 실제 임상 수명을 완전히 예측하지 못하는 한계가 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly compares CHX and isopropyl alcohol (IA) as dentin conditioning agents specifically for fiber post cementation with a self-adhesive resin cement (TheraCem), a combination not previously well-characterized.
- Demonstrates that IA's lower polarity (vs. ethanol) preserves dentinal tubule moisture better while still improving monomer wettability, translating into significantly higher immediate and aged PBS than both CHX and no conditioning.
- Shows CHX's benefit over an untreated control persists even with a non-etching self-adhesive cement, though the effect is smaller than with etch-and-rinse systems reported elsewhere in the literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: in vitro experimental
- **n**: 54 single-canal mandibular premolars, 6 subgroups of 9
- **Outcomes**: immediate (24h) and late (post-thermocycling, 5000 cycles 5-55°C ≈ 6 months clinical service) pushout bond strength (PBS) of fiber posts to root dentin, by conditioning agent (distilled water control / 2% CHX / 70% isopropyl alcohol) and by root third (cervical/middle/apical)
- Root canals instrumented with ProTaper rotary system, obturated with gutta-percha + AH26 sealer; post space prepared with Peeso reamers (7 mm from CEJ); #2 fiber posts cemented with TheraCem (Bisco) per manufacturer instructions; core build-up with Estelite Sigma Quick composite; sectioned into 1±0.1 mm slices (cervical/middle/apical) for universal-testing-machine push-out (500 N, 0.5 mm/min crosshead speed); failure mode assessed at ×40 stereomicroscopy.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Mean PBS (MPa) by group and root third (immediate / late after thermocycling):

| Group | Coronal (imm.) | Middle (imm.) | Apical (imm.) | Coronal (late) | Middle (late) | Apical (late) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Control | 16.66±1.91 | 12.80±2.44 | 7.73±2.35 | 13.22±2.41 | 9.61±1.70 | 7.48±1.20 |
| CHX | 17.75±2.97 | 14.34±3.13 | 10.75±2.63 | 17.04±1.19 | 14.13±1.81 | 10.04±2.41 |
| IA | 21.12±1.54 | 19.40±2.29 | 15.80±3.23 | 20.25±1.48 | 19.45±2.27 | 15.30±2.86 |

- IA significantly higher than CHX and control at all timepoints/sections (P<0.001).
- Control significantly lower than CHX, immediate and late (P<0.05).
- Significant PBS decline after thermocycling across all groups, irrespective of conditioning agent (P<0.003).
- Significant cervical→apical PBS decline in all groups (P<0.001).
- Predominant failure mode: adhesive failure at the cement-dentin interface (post fully enveloped by cement).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro design (single-canal mandibular premolars, standardized protocol) limits direct clinical generalization.
- Thermocycling (5000 cycles) simulates only ~6 months of clinical aging; longer-term in vivo durability unknown.
- Single cement system (TheraCem) and single post system (#2 fiber post) tested — findings may not generalize to other self-adhesive cements or post designs.
- Authors suggest future work should test combined CHX+IA conditioning and other adhesive/cement systems.

## 6. Related Work

- Durski et al. (cited in paper) — reported CHX improves PBS of intracanal posts via MMP inhibition; this study replicates a CHX-vs-control benefit but shows IA is superior to CHX for a self-adhesive cement specifically.
- Lecardelli et al. (cited in paper; source of the sample-size calculation) — showed IA outperformed CHX and distilled water in bonding durability at 24h and 6 months; this study extends that finding to fiber-post PBS with a calcium-silicate/10-MDP self-adhesive cement (TheraCem).
- Bitter et al., Cecchin et al. (cited in paper) — used ethanol for dentin moisture control and found higher bonding durability than CHX/water; this study's IA-vs-CHX result parallels the ethanol literature, consistent with [[resin-bonding/comba-2024-effects-etching-time-ethanol-wet]] and [[resin-bonding/talungchit-2014-ethanol-wet-bonding-chlorhexidine-resin-dentin-durability]] on alcohol-based dentin moisture control improving fiber-post/radicular bond strength.
- [[resin-bonding/kiuru-2021-mmp-inhibitors-dentin-bonding-sr-ma]] — SR+MA found CHX has no effect on *immediate* bond strength with etch-and-rinse/self-etch adhesives, only on aged bond strength; this study's finding that CHX improves immediate PBS with a non-etching self-adhesive cement suggests the CHX-immediate-bond relationship is adhesive-strategy-dependent.
- [[resin/theracem-bisco-product-reference]] — this study supplies TheraCem-specific dentin-conditioning-protocol evidence absent from the existing product-reference synthesis (which covers only zirconia/enamel/dentin µSBS, not post-space conditioning agents).

## 7. Glossary

- **PBS (Push-out Bond Strength)**: mechanical test measuring the force required to dislodge a cemented post from a sectioned root slice, expressed in MPa; a standard method for assessing fiber-post retention.
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: antimicrobial dentin conditioning agent that inhibits matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs), stabilizing the collagen hybrid layer.
- **IA (Isopropyl Alcohol)**: low-polarity alcohol used to displace residual dentin moisture before bonding; removes less water than ethanol, improving resin monomer wettability and degree of conversion.
- **Self-adhesive resin cement**: one-step, dual-cure luting cement (e.g., TheraCem) that chemically bonds to dentin hydroxyapatite without a separate etch/prime step; mildly acidic, causing limited demineralization/hybridization.
- **Thermocycling**: laboratory aging method alternating specimens between hot and cold water baths (here 5°-55°C, 5000 cycles) to simulate intraoral thermal stress and accelerate bond degradation.
- **Hybrid layer**: the resin-infiltrated, demineralized collagen zone at the adhesive-dentin interface; its integrity governs bond strength and durability.
