## Why Ingested

Direct head-to-head RCT test of whether adding photobiomodulation (PBM/low-level laser) to an 8% arginine desensitizing toothpaste produces an additive effect for dentin hypersensitivity (DH) — a combination-therapy question left open by the single-modality reviews we already hold. It reinforces [[dentin-hypersensitivity/faraoni-2023-laser-therapy-dentin-hypersensitivity-sr-ma]] (heterogeneous, inconsistent laser superiority) and [[dentin-hypersensitivity/shan-2021-low-level-light-therapy-dentin-hypersensitivity-sr-ma]] by supplying a 4-arm within-trial comparison showing no laser or combination advantage, and complements [[dentin-hypersensitivity/naghsh-2024-three-methods-dentin-hypersensitivity-rct]] (another multi-arm DH RCT) and the arginine-mechanism evidence in [[dentin-hypersensitivity/martins-2022-desensitizing-toothpastes-formulations-scoping-review]].

## Three-line Summary

Randomized, double-blind, parallel-group RCT (60 enrolled, 54 completed; 120 non-carious cervical lesion teeth) comparing 8% arginine toothpaste, 660 nm PBM, their combination, and a fluoride+sham-laser control for dentin hypersensitivity over 8 weeks.

All four arms achieved a large, statistically significant DH reduction over time (mean Schiff 5.0→1.9; VAS pre-intervention 6.3→1.8; both p<0.001), but there were NO significant between-group differences at any time point (p>0.05) and no additive effect of combining laser with arginine.

Clinically, neither 660 nm PBM nor arginine outperformed a fluoride-toothpaste-plus-oral-hygiene control, and the marked improvement in the control arm points to strong placebo/Hawthorne and hygiene-standardization effects that confound single-modality DH claims.

## 세줄요약

무작위 이중맹검 평행군 RCT(60명 등록·54명 완료, 비우식성 치경부병소 120치아)로 8% 아르기닌 치약, 660 nm 광생물조절(PBM/저출력레이저), 병용, 불소+가짜레이저 대조군을 8주간 비교.

네 군 모두 치아과민증이 유의하게 크게 감소(Schiff 평균 5.0→1.9, 시술 전 VAS 6.3→1.8, 둘 다 p<0.001)했으나, 모든 시점에서 군간 차이는 없었고(p>0.05) 레이저+아르기닌 병용의 상가효과도 없었다.

임상적으로 660 nm PBM도 아르기닌도 불소치약+구강위생 대조군을 능가하지 못했으며, 대조군의 뚜렷한 개선은 단일요법 DH 주장의 해석을 흐리는 강한 위약·호손 효과와 위생표준화 영향을 시사한다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Randomized, double-blind, parallel-group clinical trial (CONSORT-reported).
- **Journal**: Lasers in Medical Science, 2026; 41(1).
- **Registration**: Brazilian Clinical Trials Registry (ReBEC) RBR-2vxzvkz.
- **Ethics**: Lutheran University of Brazil REC, protocol 5.326.784.
- **Setting/period**: University dental clinics, Brazil; August 2022–December 2023.
- **Funding/conflicts**: Not extracted from PMC full text.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides a **4-arm within-trial** answer to whether adding PBM to 8% arginine toothpaste gives an additive/synergistic benefit for DH: it does not.
- Includes a **fluoride-toothpaste + sham-laser control**, exposing the large non-specific (placebo/Hawthorne/hygiene) component of DH improvement that many uncontrolled DH studies miss.
- Uses **two independent stimulus modalities** (evaporative air / Schiff scale and tactile explorer) plus VAS, and cross-validates them (tactile-positive teeth carried ~1.46 higher VAS units early).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Participants**: ≥18 y, non-carious cervical lesions with DH (Schiff 2–3 to air) in ≥2 non-adjacent teeth; periodontally healthy; strict exclusions (endodontic lesions, extensive restorations, prior periodontal therapy/bleaching, current desensitizers, pregnancy, chemo/radiotherapy).
- **Groups (n at baseline)**: Laser (LG, n=16), Arginine (AG, n=17), Laser+Arginine (LAG, n=11), Control (CG, n=16).
- **PBM protocol**: Photon Lase III diode, 660 nm continuous, 100 mW, spot 0.028 cm², 0.5 cm standoff via metallic spacer; 3 points/tooth × 28 s = 84 s/tooth; 8.4 J total; nominal 300 J/cm².
- **Arginine protocol**: 1.14% sodium monofluorophosphate + 8% arginine + calcium carbonate (Colgate Sensitive Pro Relief); ~0.25 g finger-applied 1 min; also used as daily toothpaste.
- **Control**: sham laser (audible signal, no emission) + 1,450 ppm fluoride toothpaste.
- **Randomization/blinding**: block randomization (Random.org) by independent researcher; sequentially numbered opaque sealed envelopes; participants and outcome assessor blinded; toothpastes decanted into identical opaque containers.
- **Outcomes**: baseline, 2, 4, 8 weeks; Schiff Cold Air Sensitivity Scale (0–3); binary tactile test (#5 explorer); VAS (0–10) pre- and immediately post-intervention.
- **Analysis**: linear mixed-effects models (group, time, group×time; random intercepts) for Schiff/VAS; GEE for binary tactile; R 4.3.1; α=0.05. Sample size 44 (effect size 0.53, α 5%, power 80%); 60 recruited for attrition.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Overall time effect** (all participants): Schiff 5.0(±0.8)→3.5→2.9→1.9(±1.3), Δ −3.1 (p<0.001); VAS pre-intervention 6.3→3.4→2.7→1.8, Δ −3.5 (p<0.001); VAS post-intervention 5.1→1.6.
- **Between-group Schiff** (baseline / 8 wk / Δ): LG 4.8/1.9/−3.0; AG 5.1/2.1/−2.9; LAG 5.1/2.0/−2.9; CG 5.1/1.6/−3.5. Group p-values 0.573 (baseline) to 0.695 (8 wk); Δ p=0.686.
- **Between-group VAS Δ**: LG −3.7, AG −3.7, LAG −3.4, CG −3.1 (p=0.966). No significant group×time interaction.
- **No additive effect**: combined LAG did not beat isolated AG or LG.
- **Tactile ↔ VAS (GEE)**: tactile-positive teeth had higher VAS at baseline (6.6 vs 5.1, p=0.037) and 4 wk (4.4 vs 2.4, p=0.009); borderline 2 wk (p=0.053); NS at 8 wk (p=0.823); ~1.46 VAS-unit increase early.
- **Control-arm improvement**: substantial DH reduction in CG attributed to placebo + Hawthorne effects and hygiene standardization.
- **Safety**: no adverse effects reported.
- **Sample**: 120 teeth, ~69% premolars; mean age 51.4 y; 76.6% female.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- DH etiology and symptom duration not individually stratified (multifactorial condition).
- Follow-up attrition produced **unbalanced final group sizes** (esp. LAG n=11); authors caution that the null between-group result should be read carefully (possible underpowering for group contrasts).
- Only 8-week follow-up; no factorial design to formally test interaction — authors call for larger factorial trials with longer follow-up.
- Strong control-arm response (placebo/Hawthorne) limits ability to isolate specific-treatment effects.
- Single 660 nm PBM dosimetry tested; other wavelengths/protocols not compared within-trial.

## 7. Glossary

- **DH (Dentin Hypersensitivity)**: short, sharp pain from exposed dentin to thermal/evaporative/tactile/osmotic stimuli not explained by other pathology.
- **PBM (Photobiomodulation)**: low-level light/laser therapy (here 660 nm) modulating neural activity and inflammation.
- **Schiff Cold Air Sensitivity Scale**: 0–3 evaporative-stimulus DH severity scale; 2–3 indicates DH.
- **VAS (Visual Analog Scale)**: 0–10 pain-intensity self-report.
- **Hydrodynamic theory**: fluid movement in dentinal tubules activating mechanosensitive nerves → DH pain.
- **Hawthorne effect**: behavior change (e.g., better hygiene) from awareness of being observed, inflating control-arm improvement.
- **GEE (Generalized Estimating Equations)**: regression for correlated repeated-measures binary outcomes.
