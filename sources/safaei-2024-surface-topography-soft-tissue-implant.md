## Why Ingested
Narrative review consolidating the *soft-tissue-axis* evidence for implant surface nanotopography (TiO2 nanotubes, anodization, nanopillars) that our existing nanofeatured-surface page [[implants/surface/serbanescu-2025-nanofeatured-titanium-surfaces-dental-implants]] deliberately left as lower-certainty background, and reaching the same dual soft-tissue + antibacterial conclusion as the new UV-photofunctionalization SR [[implants/surface/komatsu-2026-uv-photofunctionalization-surface-agnostic-biofilm-soft-tissue-sr]] via an independent mechanism (topography-driven cells/immune cells, not surface-agnostic reactivation). Also carries a concrete, cited claim we lacked: silver-containing nanotubular topography significantly decreased fibrous encapsulation in peri-implant soft tissue in vivo.

## Three-line Summary
Narrative review of the literature on Ti dental-implant surface nanotopography at the transmucosal region — engineering techniques (photolithography, electron-beam lithography, anodization, phase separation, nanoimprinting), topography-driven soft-tissue integration, antibacterial mechanisms, and topography-mediated immunomodulation (M1→M2 macrophage polarization).
Nanotopography at 10–100 nm prevents bacterial adhesion via mechano-bactericidal stretching/rupture and spatial confinement; 30 nm TiO2 nanotubes drive M2 (anti-inflammatory) macrophage polarization while 80–100 nm drive M1 (pro-inflammatory); silver-containing nanotubular surfaces show antibacterial activity and significantly decreased fibrous encapsulation in vivo; recommended roughness threshold for plaque no-promotion is Ra <0.2 µm (Bollen).
Evidence is preclinical (in vitro, rat/rabbit/dog/goat models) with few human trials; authors call for long-term in vivo/human studies, especially in compromised patients (diabetes, osteoporosis); no head-to-head clinical superiority established.

## 세줄요약
내러티브 문헌고찰: 티타늄 (Titanium, Ti) 임플란트 경점막부 (Transmucosal) 표면 나노형태 (Nanotopography) 공정(포토리소그래피·전자빔리소그래피·양극산화·상분리·나노임프린팅), 연조직 통합, 항균 기전, 표면 매개 면역조절 (M1→M2 대식세포 편극).
나노스케일(10–100 nm) 구조는 기계적 살균(막 신장·파열)과 공간구속으로 세균 부착을 억제하고, 30 nm 이산화티타늄 (Titanium dioxide, TiO2) 나노튜브는 항염증형 M2 대식세포를, 80–100 nm는 염증형 M1을 유도; 은(Ag) 함유 나노튜브 표면은 항균 활성과 함께 생체내 섬유성 피막 (Fibrous Encapsulation) 형성을 유의 감소; 플라크 무형성 거칠기 임계치는 Ra <0.2 µm (Bollen).
근거 대부분이 시험관내·실험동물(쥐·토끼·개·염소)이며 임상시험은 소수 — 저자들은 당뇨·골다공증 같은 전신질환 환자를 포함한 장기 생체내·인체 연구가 필요하다고 강조.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Frames implant surface nanotopography as a single lever on *two* outcomes at the transmucosal region: soft-tissue integration (epithelial/fibroblast sealing) and antibacterial function (mechano-bactericidal + drug-delivery).
- Compiles the nanotube-diameter → macrophage-polarization evidence: ~30 nm TiO2 nanotubes shift toward M2 (anti-inflammatory) / M2c, 80–100 nm toward M1 (pro-inflammatory), with underlying signaling (RhoA/ROCK, MAPK, NF-κB, integrin-FAK-PI3Kγ) summarized.
- Documents silver-containing nanotubular topography as dual antibacterial + anti-inflammatory: effective against S. aureus via membrane damage, mitigates inflammatory response, and significantly decreases fibrous encapsulation in peri-implant soft tissue in vivo.
- Positions drug-loaded (rifampicin/amoxicillin PLGA, metallic Ag/Zn nanoparticles) nanotubular surfaces as localized delivery systems against peri-implant biofilm, including antifungal action vs C. albicans/C. parapsilosis.
- Explicitly catalogs the translation gaps: sterilization protocols alter nanotopography (wet/dry autoclave compromise anodized TiO2 nanotubes), long-term Ti-particle release, and the need for long-term in vivo + human studies (6–8-week soft-tissue integration window vs post-surgical inflammation).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative review (non-systematic), expert opinion concluding section; no PRISMA protocol, no search-strategy/databases/n eligibility reported.
- **n**: ~187 cited primary/preclinical studies (in vitro + animal: Sprague-Dawley/Lewis rats, rabbits, beagle dogs, goats, mice) and a handful of human/in vitro tissue studies; 9 completed clinical trials (NCT identifiers, clinicaltrials.gov) tabulated as market-adjacent evidence.
- **Outcomes**: Soft-tissue integration (PIE hemidesmosome attachment, gingival fibroblast alignment/adhesion, collagen-fiber orientation, connective-tissue length), antibacterial/biofilm metrics, macrophage polarization phenotype, fibrous encapsulation, osseointegration/stability (secondary).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Nanofabrication resolution limits**: photolithography 50 nm features (600 nm nanogratings); electron-beam lithography nanopits 120 nm / nanodots 12 nm; anodization nanopillars/nanotubes 15–100 nm; phase-separation nanodots 20 nm, nanoislands 13/35 nm, dot/worm patterns 160 nm; nanoimprinting nanopits 50 nm.
- **Soft-tissue integration**: anodized vs non-anodized Ti — enhanced peri-implant soft-tissue sealing after transcutaneous implantation in goat tibia, 8 weeks (Chen et al.); alkali-heat-treated Ti (nano spikes/pores) — perpendicular collagen-fiber attachment in rabbit palatal mucosa at 8 weeks (Kato et al.); anodized nanotubular vs machined abutment — increased connective-tissue length and perpendicular collagen in beagle dog, 4 weeks (Wang et al.); organized TiO2 nanotubes promoted gingival-fibroblast alignment/adhesion/migration (Gulati et al.).
- **Antibacterial**: nanostructures (10–100 nm) impede bacterial adhesion/colonization vs enlarged contact area at higher micro-roughness (Al-Ahmad: TiUnite highest roughness → highest in vivo bacterial adhesion); mechano-bactericidal nanopillars/spinules/spikes induce membrane stretching + rupture (dragonfly-wing-inspired acid-etched nanopillars); Ra <0.4 µm recommended (Li), Ra <0.2 µm = plaque no-promotion threshold (Bollen).
- **Immunomodulation**: 30 nm vs 80 nm nanotube diameter — 30 nm improved M2 polarization and suppressed inflammation in vitro/in vivo; 80 nm triggered M1 and intense inflammation with osteogenesis/osteoclasia bias toward osteoclasia; 30 nm TiO2 nanotube induced M2c polarization of inflammatory monocytes.
- **Drug-loaded surfaces**: rifampicin-loaded PLGA on TiO2 nanotubes significantly inhibited S. epidermidis vs machined/acid-etched; amoxicillin-loaded PLGA on anodized Ti effective vs S. aureus and S. epidermidis; silver-embedded TiO2 nanotubes effective vs S. aureus (direct contact → cell-wall damage) and significantly decreased fibrous encapsulation; Zn/Ag (ZnO/Ag) nanoparticle-embedded TiO2 nanotubes antifungal vs C. albicans and C. parapsilosis and antibacterial vs S. mutans.
- **Commercial clinical trials tabulated**: e.g., NCT03833834 (NobelActive TiUltra, soft-tissue stability), NCT03582657 (KONTACT N, per-implantitis rate), NCT00782171 (SLActive roughness, implant stability).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Non-systematic narrative review — no search protocol, eligibility criteria, or risk-of-bias appraisal; conclusions rest on heterogeneous preclinical models.
- Preclinical-to-human evidence gap: most supporting data are in vitro or animal; few human trials, none establishing long-term clinical superiority of nanotopographic over conventional microrough surfaces.
- Translation blockers identified by the authors: absence of standardized sterilization protocols (autoclave alters nanotubular topography), long-term Ti-particle/ion release, mechanical stability of nanostructured surfaces (delamination risk at insertion), and regulatory (FDA/CE) approval timelines.
- Soft-tissue integration takes 6–8 weeks — a window in which post-surgical, pathogen-mediated inflammation can compromise the outcome; performance in compromised patients (diabetes, osteoporosis) untested. Authors call for long-term in vivo/human studies in such populations plus ML/AI-organized data platforms.

## 6. Related Work
- serbanescu-2025: systematic review of nanofeatured Ti surfaces vs microrough controls on **osseointegration** endpoints — this review extends that body of evidence into the soft-tissue/immune axis while sharing the antibacterial-coating (Ag, nanopillar) mechanism discussion.
- komatsu-2026 (UV-PF SR): independently concludes the same dual axis (biofilm suppression + soft-tissue sealing) but via a surface-agnostic reactivation mechanism rather than topography itself.
- canullo-2020 (SR+MA): clinical counterpoint — titanium abutment surface modifications showed no short-term effect on PI/BoP/PD (P=0.091/0.099/0.488), i.e., clinical soft-tissue-health indices did not move; the cellular/histologic gains this review reports did not translate to index-level clinical benefit.

## 7. Glossary
- **Transmucosal region**: the zone where the implant/abutment breaches the oral mucosa; its soft-tissue seal protects the underlying bone from the oral environment.
- **PIE (Peri-Implant Epithelium)**: epithelium attached to the implant surface via hemidesmosomes; inadequate hemidesmosome adhesion weakens the mucosal barrier.
- **Nanotopography**: surface structures with at least one dimension <100 nm (one of several definitions cited in the paper).
- **M1/M2 macrophage polarization**: pro-inflammatory vs anti-inflammatory (tissue-repair) macrophage phenotypes; M1≈IL-1/TNF-α-driven osteolysis risk, M2≈promotes soft-tissue healing and integration.
- **Mechano-bactericidal surface**: nanostructures (nanopillars, nanospears, nanospikes) that kill adhered bacteria by stretching/rupturing the membrane via adhesion forces.
- **Fibrous encapsulation**: avascular scar-like collagen capsule forming around an implant when tissue integration fails; silver-containing nanotubular topography reduced it in vivo.
- **TiO2 nanotubes**: ordered nanotubular titanium-dioxide structures made by anodization; serve as contact-guidance topography and drug/ion-delivery reservoirs.
