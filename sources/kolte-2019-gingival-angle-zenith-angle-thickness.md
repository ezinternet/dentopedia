## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional study of 160 periodontally healthy patients split into 21–40 y and 41–60 y groups with equal gender distribution, measuring gingival angle (GA), gingival zenith angle (GZA), and gingival thickness (GT) on maxillary central incisors, lateral incisors, and canines.

GA and GZA were consistently lower in females across tooth types and age groups; GT was 1.15 ± 0.21 (F) vs 2.56 ± 0.26 mm (M) in 21–40 y and 1.13 ± 0.21 vs 2.54 ± 0.27 mm in 41–60 y; no direct correlation between GA and GZA or between GZA and GT.

GA/GZA differ by sex but not by age, and gingival thickness does not predict zenith angle — zenith shape and tissue thickness appear to be independent design variables.
