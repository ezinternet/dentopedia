## Why Ingested
6.5mm 단축 임플란트의 즉시 부하 및 스플린팅 파트너 길이가 임상 결과에 미치는 영향을 후향적으로 분석. [[implants/short-narrow/anitua-2022-short-vs-longer-implants-single-crown]] 및 [[implants/short-narrow/barausse-2024-4mm-short-implants-posterior-atrophic-8year]]의 단축 임플란트 근거 체계에 추가하며, 스플린팅 인접 임플란트 길이 차이에 따른 변연골 소실 패턴을 다룬다.

## Three-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=16 patients, 48 implants, all 6.5mm BTI implants) comparing short-short (SS: both 6.5mm) versus short-long (SL: one 6.5mm + one longer) splinting configurations for immediately loaded two-implant fixed partial prostheses in premolar-molar regions over a mean 14-month follow-up at a private BTI center in Spain. Implant and prosthesis survival was 100% in both groups; distal marginal bone loss was significantly lower in the SS group (0.37 mm) than in the SL group (0.94 mm, p<0.05), with no significant difference in mesial bone loss. Splinting two equally short implants minimizes distal marginal bone loss compared to mixed short-long configurations, supporting biomechanically matched pairing; the small retrospective design, short follow-up (14 months), and author conflict of interest (BTI Scientific Director) limit conclusions.

## 세줄요약

## One-line Summary
Retrospective (n=16 patients, 48 implants, 6.5mm short): 100% implant and prosthesis survival at mean 14 months; short-short splinting showed significantly less distal marginal bone loss (0.37 mm) than short-long splinting (0.94 mm).

## 한줄요약
후향적(n=16, 임플란트 48개, 6.5mm 단축): 평균 14개월 생존율 100%; 단축-단축 스플린팅이 단축-장임플란트 스플린팅보다 원심 변연골 소실 유의하게 낮음(0.37 vs 0.94mm).

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Prosthodontics, 2019;28(2):e688–e693 (first published 28 January 2018)
- **Institution**: Private dental center (BTI Biotechnology Institute, Vitoria, Spain)
- **Funding**: None (EA is Scientific Director of BTI; COI noted)
- **Mean follow-up**: 14 ± 5 months

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates that immediate loading of 6.5mm implants in premolar-molar regions does not compromise survival
- Short-short splinting (both 6.5mm) produces less marginal bone loss than short-long splinting at distal aspect
- Possible biomechanical mechanism: reduced stress concentration at junction of implants with different stiffness/height
- Supports the use of very short implants (6.5mm) with immediate loading in posterior regions

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort
- **Patients**: 16 patients, 48 implants (6.5mm BTI Implant® with acid-etching surface)
- **Groups**:
  - Short-short (SS): 8 patients, 16 implants — both implants 6.5mm
  - Short-long (SL): 16 patients (8 additional), 32 implants — one 6.5mm + one longer implant
- **Prosthesis**: Two-implant fixed partial prostheses, premolar-molar regions, immediately loaded
- **Primary outcome**: Implant survival
- **Secondary outcomes**: Marginal bone stability, prosthesis survival
- **Radiographic assessment**: Standardized periapical radiographs at loading and follow-up

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant survival**: 100% both groups
- **Prosthesis survival**: 100% both groups
- **Marginal bone loss (distal)**:
  - Short-short: 0.37 ± 0.55 mm
  - Short-long: 0.94 ± 0.66 mm (p < 0.05)
- Mesial bone loss: not significantly different
- No significant difference in complications

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=16), single center, private practice
- Short mean follow-up (14 months); long-term data needed
- No control group (standard implants) for direct comparison
- Author COI: EA is BTI Scientific Director (implant manufacturer)
- Retrospective design limits causal inference

## 6. Related Work
- Anitua 2022 (short vs longer, single crown): reinforces short implant viability
- Barausse 2024 (4mm extra-short, 8yr): extends to even shorter implants
- Yu 2021 (MA short implants): broader meta-analytic context

## 7. Glossary
- **SS group**: Short-short — both implants 6.5mm splinted
- **SL group**: Short-long — one 6.5mm + one longer implant splinted
- **BTI Implant**: Bone and Tissue Institute brand implant (acid-etched surface)
- **Immediate loading**: Prosthesis placed same day or within 72 hours of implant placement
