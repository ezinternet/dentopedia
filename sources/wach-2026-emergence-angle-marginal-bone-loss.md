## Why Ingested

기존 [[overviews/implant-prosthesis-misfit-connection-mbl-overview]]는 보철 부적합·연결부가 변연골소실 (marginal bone loss, MBL)에 미치는 영향을 종합했지만, 크라운 돌출각 (emergence angle, EA) 자체의 영향은 [[prosthetic-materials/mikulas-2025-digital-impression-accuracy-peri-implant-emergence-profile-sr]]가 다룬 디지털 인상 정확도 관점 외에 별도 근거가 부족했다. 본 155환자·5년 후향적 연구는 EA가 단일/연결/브릿지 보철에서 MBL 및 피질화 지수 (Corticalization Index, CI)에 미치는 영향을 직접 비교해, 단일·연결 크라운에서는 EA-MBL 연관성이 거의 없고 브릿지에서만 약한(R²=7.9%) 통계적 유의성을 보인다는 근거를 보강한다. 즉 "돌출각이 클수록 골소실"이라는 단순 도그마를 largely 반박하는 임상 데이터로, 기존 동물실험 근거([[implants/soft-tissue/lee-2025-emergence-angle-soft-hard-tissue-splinted-implants]], [[implants/soft-tissue/strauss-2024-wide-emergence-angle-marginal-bone-loss-junctional-epithelium]])와 임상 관찰 사이의 간극을 메운다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study, n=155 patients / MIS implants (single crown / splinted crown / bridge restorations), 5-year radiographic follow-up (3-month and 60-month intraoral X-rays).

Mean emergence angle 31.8°±10.4°; no significant EA-MBL relationship for single crowns (p=0.369) or splinted crowns (p=0.176), but a weak, statistically significant relationship for bridges (p=0.042, R²=7.9%); no significant MBL or Corticalization Index (CI) differences among restoration types at 3 or 60 months.

Clinically, crown emergence angle alone is not a dominant driver of marginal bone loss for single or splinted restorations — other biological/biomechanical/systemic factors likely matter more — but the weak bridge-only signal and the 2D-radiograph limitation (no buccal/lingual assessment, no formal multivariable adjustment) mean the finding should not be over-generalized to very wide angles (e.g., 60° in preclinical models).

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구, n=155명 환자 / MIS 임플란트 (단일크라운/연결크라운/브릿지), 5년 방사선 추적관찰(3개월·60개월 구내촬영).

평균 돌출각 (emergence angle, EA) 31.8°±10.4°; 단일크라운(p=0.369)과 연결크라운(p=0.176)은 EA-변연골소실(marginal bone loss, MBL) 연관성 없음, 브릿지에서만 약하지만 통계적으로 유의한 연관성(p=0.042, R²=7.9%); 3개월·60개월 모두 보철 유형 간 MBL·피질화지수(Corticalization Index, CI) 유의차 없음.

임상적으로 크라운 돌출각 단독은 단일·연결 보철의 MBL 주요 결정인자가 아니며 다른 생물학적·생역학적·전신적 요인이 더 중요할 가능성이 크지만, 브릿지에서의 약한 신호와 2D 방사선 사진의 한계(협측/설측 미평가, 공식적 다변량 보정 없음)로 인해 60° 등 매우 큰 각도로 일반화해서는 안 된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly tests whether crown emergence angle (EA), measured on standardized 2D intraoral radiographs, predicts marginal bone loss (MBL) and radiographic corticalization (Corticalization Index, CI) across three prosthetic restoration types (single crown, splinted crown, implant-supported bridge) over a 5-year window.
- Novel combination of the conventional MBL endpoint with the CI texture-analysis metric (Kozakiewicz et al. framework), giving a broader radiographic picture of peri-implant bone adaptation than MBL alone.
- Large single-system cohort (155 patients, 536 mesial/distal EA measurements, single MIS implant system) with confounder exclusion (diabetes, osteoporosis, prior radiotherapy, active bisphosphonate therapy, diagnosed bruxism) to isolate the prosthetic-design signal.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Retrospective analysis of anonymized radiographic documentation from 155 patients with delayed-loading MIS implants, split into three groups: Group I single crowns, Group II splinted crowns, Group III implant-supported bridges.
- Follow-up window 3 months to 5 years post-loading; primary outcome MBL at 60 months; secondary/exploratory outcomes MBL at 3 months and CI at 3 and 60 months.
- Standardized intraoral radiography: DIGORA OPTIME (SOREDEX) DXR-50, fixed exposure (7 mA, 70 kV, 0.1 s), perpendicular beam-alignment positioning systems to minimize projection error. Measurements limited to mesial/distal interproximal aspects (no buccal/lingual assessment possible with 2D periapical films).
- Image analysis in ImageJ v1.54g; CI calculated per the Kozakiewicz et al. texture-analysis framework. Emergence angle measured by one examiner; MBL/CI measured by a second examiner (~90% repeatability on repeated measurements; no formal inter-observer agreement coefficient reported).
- Statistics: STATISTICA v13.1. ANOVA / Kruskal-Wallis for group comparisons as distribution-appropriate; simple (not multivariable) regression per restoration group to explore EA-vs-60-month-MBL association; two-sided α=0.05. Explicitly exploratory — no a priori sample-size calculation, no mixed-effects/GEE adjustment for intra-patient clustering (mesial+distal measurements per implant were averaged to one value per unit before analysis).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Mean EA overall: 31.8°±10.4° (median 31.3°, range 6.2–79.1°). By group: bridges 30±11° (n=218), splinted crowns 33±11° (n=208), single crowns 32±9° (n=110) — Kruskal-Wallis p=0.202, no significant between-group difference.
- MBL: 0.0±0.0 mm at baseline → 0.2±0.7 mm at 3 months → 0.9±1.2 mm at 60 months (median 0.43 mm), markedly right-skewed (most patients near-zero, a few high outliers up to 8.0 mm). No significant between-group MBL differences at 3 months (ANOVA p=0.195) or 60 months (p=0.081).
- EA-vs-60-month-MBL regression by group: single crowns p=0.369, R²=1.1% (not significant); splinted crowns p=0.176, R²=2.1% (not significant); bridges p=0.042, R²=7.9% (statistically significant but low explanatory value — described by the authors as "clinically weak").
- Corticalization Index rose over time in all groups (median 165→234 from 3 to 60 months) with no significant between-group differences at either timepoint (p=0.121 at 3mo, p=0.072 at 60mo) — i.e., restoration type did not significantly affect CI.
- Restoration type was significantly associated with anterior/posterior location (χ² p=0.0004: single crowns more anterior 14.84%, splinted/bridges more posterior including 34.38% splinted-posterior) but not with maxilla-vs-mandible (p=0.0767). A descriptive smoking-status association (p=0.0409) was noted but not causally interpreted (no multivariable adjustment).
- Discussion situates the bridge-only weak signal against Wach et al. 2024 ("New Radiological Corticalization Index," >2000 samples, same author group) showing restoration-type-dependent CI/MBL relationships, and against the preclinical beagle-dog histology of Lee et al. 2025 (EA 30° vs 60°) showing that very wide EA impairs connective-tissue attachment/junctional epithelium — implying the clinical null for single/splinted crowns may not extrapolate to extreme angles.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Two-dimensional radiographs only — no buccal/lingual/palatal bone assessment; authors note CBCT would theoretically help but is degraded by implant-related streak artifacts.
- No formal multivariable adjustment for confounders (implant geometry, insertion torque, site, platform-switching/abutment design, soft-tissue phenotype) — findings are exploratory/hypothesis-generating, not adjusted independent-effect estimates.
- No mixed-effects/GEE modeling for intra-patient clustering (multiple implants per patient); mesial/distal values pre-averaged, masking possible side-specific effects.
- Retrospective, single implant system (MIS), narrow observed EA range (no predefined threshold/cutoff analysis) — cannot identify a clinically actionable EA cutoff, and results may not generalize to patients with bruxism, systemic bone disease, or very large EA values.
- Authors call for prospective studies using validated 3D EA assessment methods (e.g., Mancini et al. STL-based restorative-angle analysis) that can separately evaluate buccal/labial contours, which 2D periapical films cannot capture.

## 6. Related Work

- Lops et al. 2022 (3-year retrospective, n=312 implants, internal conical connection) — no significant MBL difference between EA>30° and EA≤30° groups; concordant with the present single/splinted-crown null result.
- Kou et al. 2023 (502 single-implant crowns) — no EA-MBL correlation across anterior/premolar/molar sites; molar EA (34.5°±13.3°) significantly larger than anterior (21.7°±10.8°), supporting the interpretation that anatomic site, not EA per se, drives variability.
- Lee et al. 2025 (beagle-dog preclinical, EA 30° vs 60° in splinted restorations) — wide EA significantly increased bone remodeling and reduced connective-tissue attachment/junctional-epithelium collagen, providing histological mechanism for risk at extreme angles not captured by this retrospective's narrower observed range.
- Wach et al. 2024 ("New Radiological Corticalization Index," same senior author, >2000 implants) — established the CI framework used here and showed CI-MBL relationships are restoration-type-dependent (not yet in this wiki).
- Mancini et al. 2023 — 3D (STL) emergence-angle assessment method distinguishing biological vs aesthetic zone and labial vs interproximal EA, proposed as the next-step methodology beyond this study's 2D radiographic approach.

## 7. Glossary

- **Emergence angle (EA)**: the angle at which a prosthetic crown contour diverges from the implant/abutment axis as it passes through the peri-implant soft tissue toward the gingival margin; measured here on mesial/distal intraoral radiographs.
- **Marginal bone loss (MBL)**: radiographically measured vertical loss of crestal bone height around an implant, relative to a baseline (implant placement/loading) reference.
- **Corticalization Index (CI)**: a radiographic texture-analysis metric (Kozakiewicz et al. framework) quantifying the degree to which trabecular peri-implant bone remodels toward a denser, more cortical-like radiographic appearance under functional loading.
- **Delayed-loading protocol**: prosthetic restoration placed after a healing period post-implant-insertion (as opposed to immediate loading).
- **Platform switching (PS)**: a prosthetic-abutment design where the abutment diameter is narrower than the implant platform, referenced in the discussion as a potential MBL-mitigating factor independent of EA.
