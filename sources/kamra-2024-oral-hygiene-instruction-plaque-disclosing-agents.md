## Why Ingested

치태 착색제(플라크 디스클로징 에이전트, plaque-disclosing agent)를 활용한 구강위생교육이 자가 치태제거에 미치는 영향을 단일 증례로 보여주는 논문으로, 치태 지표(Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque index)와 치은 지표(Loe and Silness gingival index)의 연속적 추이를 시점별로 제시한다. 이는 [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/delia-2023-dental-biofilm-indices-narrative-review]]에서 논의된 치태 지수의 임상적 활용과 [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/sooampon-2026-dc-red-33-erythrosine-plaque-disclosure-rct]]의 치태 착색제 대체 근거와도 연결된다.

## Three-line Summary

Case report (n=1, adolescent male) using two-tone plaque-disclosing solution (PDS) with rolling and Bass brushing instructions and serial follow-up to track self-performed plaque control.

Plaque index (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein) improved from 2.0 (baseline) to 1.8 (12 h), 1.5 (24 h), 1.3 (1 week), 0.8 (4 weeks), and gingival index (Loe and Silness) improved from 2.5 to 2.3, 2.0, 1.9, 1.0 over the same timepoints.

Visual feedback with PDS + individualized brushing reinforcement supported progressive improvement in self-performed plaque control over 4 weeks; long-term maintenance was reported at 6 months by phone.

## 세줄요약

증례보고(case report): 17세 남성 1례에서 2색 치태착색제(two-tone plaque-disclosing agent)를 사용하여 자가 치태제거 능력을 평가.

치태지수(Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque index)는 baseline 2.0 → 12시간 1.8 → 24시간 1.5 → 1주 1.3 → 4주 0.8로, 치은지수(Loe and Silness gingival index)는 baseline 2.5 → 12시간 2.3 → 24시간 2.0 → 1주 1.9 → 4주 1.0로 점진적으로 감소.

치태 착색 시각화 + 구강위생교육·칫솔질 교정 반복이 4주간 자가 치태제어 개선에 기여하였고, 6개월 전화 추적에서 유지됨.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates stepwise reduction in plaque and gingival indices with repeated OHI + two-tone PDS reinforcement over 4 weeks in an adolescent.
- Reports serial values at baseline, 12 hours, 24 hours, 1 week, and 4 weeks for both indices (TQHPI-style Quigley-Hein modification and Loe-Silness GI).
- Highlights the role of visual feedback (new vs old plaque differentiation with two-tone disclosing solution) to motivate self-performed plaque control.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Case**: 17-year-old male, chronic localized gingivitis (no caries, no periodontal pockets; bleeding on probing, gingival redness).
- **Baseline assessment**: Two-tone PDS (DPI Alphaplac) applied; old plaque stained blue, new plaque stained pink. Indices recorded: Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque index (0-5), Loe and Silness gingival index (0-3).
- **Intervention**: OHI with demonstration of rolling and Bass techniques, soft-bristled toothbrush, triclosan + sodium monofluorophosphate toothpaste. Brushing pattern observed and corrected at each visit.
- **Follow-up**: 12 hours, 24 hours, 1 week, 4 weeks; clinical photographs and indices recorded each time. Reinforcement and motivation provided.
- **Long-term**: 6-month phone follow-up confirmed twice-daily brushing with instructed technique.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-patient case report (n=1); limited generalizability.
- Lack of control group; cannot isolate effect of PDS from repeated reinforcement/motivation alone.
- Follow-up limited to 4 weeks for clinical indices; 6-month data was by phone only (no index scores reported).
- No blinded examiner reported.

## 7. Glossary

- **PDS (plaque-disclosing solution)**: Dye solution that stains dental plaque to visualize its location and extent.
- **Two-tone PDS**: Formulation intended to differentiate newer vs older plaque (here, old plaque blue, new plaque pink).
- **Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque index**: Plaque scoring (0-5) assessing disclosed plaque on tooth surfaces.
- **Loe and Silness gingival index**: Gingival inflammation scoring (0-3), with bleeding recorded at 2.
- **Bass technique / rolling technique**: Manual toothbrushing methods taught to improve plaque removal.
- **OHI (oral hygiene instruction)**: Patient education on effective plaque control measures.
