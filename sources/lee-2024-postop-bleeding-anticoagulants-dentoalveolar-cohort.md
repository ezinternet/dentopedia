## Why Ingested
항응고제 복용 환자의 치조외과술 후 출혈 위험인자를 대규모 코호트(n=537)로 분석한 연구. 기존 [[drug/anticoagulants/madrid-2009-anticoagulants-oral-implant-therapy-sr]] 및 [[drug/anticoagulants/chahine-2019-anticoagulation-dental-procedures-sr]]의 SR 권고를 임플란트·발치·치주수술을 포함한 실제 임상 데이터로 검증한다.

## Three-line Summary
Retrospective cohort study (n=537) at Yonsei University Dental Hospital (2016–2021) using a standardized common data model to analyze post-operative bleeding in anticoagulant patients undergoing dentoalveolar surgery (extraction, periodontal flap, implant) across maintenance versus discontinuation groups.
Overall bleeding occurred in 6.5% (35/537); within the maintenance group, atrial fibrillation (OR=6.051) and vitamin K inhibitors (OR=3.679) were the dominant independent systemic risk factors, while extraction combined with bone grafting, multiple implants, and maxillary arch involvement were significant procedure-related risks.
All 35 bleeding episodes were controlled with local hemostatic measures; DOACs showed bleeding rates comparable to antiplatelet agents, confirming a safer profile than VKI in the maintenance setting.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 연세대학교 치과대학병원 후향적 코호트 (Retrospective Cohort) 연구 (n=537, 2016–2021): 공통 데이터 모델 (Common Data Model, CDM)을 이용해 항응고제 (Anticoagulant) 복용 환자의 치조외과술 (Dentoalveolar Surgery) 후 출혈 위험인자 분석, 유지군 (n=245)·중단군 (n=292) 비교.
줄2: 전체 출혈률 6.5%; 항응고제 유지군에서 심방세동 (Atrial Fibrillation, AF) (OR=6.051)·비타민K억제제 (Vitamin K Inhibitor, VKI) (OR=3.679)가 최고 독립 위험인자; 발치+골이식 (p=0.016)·다수 임플란트·상악 수술 (p=0.032)은 술식 관련 독립 위험인자.
줄3: 전체 출혈 사례 국소 지혈처치로 해결; 직접구강항응고제 (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC)는 항혈소판제 (Platelet Aggregation Inhibitor, PAI)와 유사 출혈률로 유지군에서 VKI보다 안전 프로파일 확인.

## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=537, Yonsei): 6.5% post-op bleeding overall; atrial fibrillation (OR=6.051) and vitamin K inhibitors (OR=3.679) most significant risk factors in the anticoagulant-maintenance group.

## 한줄요약
후향적 코호트(n=537): 전체 6.5% 출혈; 항응고제 유지군에서 심방세동(OR=6.051)·비타민K 억제제(OR=3.679)가 독립 위험인자이며, 발치+골이식·다수 임플란트·상악 수술이 술식 관련 위험인자.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First large-scale (n=537) cohort covering all dentoalveolar procedures (extraction, flap, implant) in anticoagulant patients
- Identified procedure-specific risk: extraction+bone graft (p=0.016), multiple implants, maxillary arch involvement (p=0.032) independently associated with bleeding
- Vitamin K antagonists (warfarin) carry 3.7× higher bleeding OR vs. DOACs/PAIs in maintenance group
- Atrial fibrillation is the dominant systemic risk factor (OR=6.05) — higher than anticoagulant type
- Used standardized common data model (CDM/SCRAP 2.0) — increases reproducibility and external validity

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, single center (Yonsei University Dental Hospital)
- **n**: 537 patients on anticoagulants undergoing dentoalveolar surgery
- **Groups**: Maintenance (n=245, anticoagulant continued) vs. Discontinuation (n=292, 1–7 days before surgery)
- **Procedures included**: Periodontal flap, tooth extraction, implant surgery
- **Exclusions**: Coagulation disorders unrelated to drugs (hemophilia, Von Willebrand, liver cirrhosis, thrombocytopenia)
- **Anticoagulant classes**: PAI (platelet aggregation inhibitors), VKI (vitamin K inhibitors), DOACs
- **Outcomes**: Post-operative bleeding event (primary); association with patient and procedural factors (multivariate logistic regression)
- **Hemostasis protocol**: Local measures (gelatin sponge, cellulose gauze, suture, tranexamic acid) standardized

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Overall bleeding rate**: 35/537 (6.5%)
  - Maintenance group: 21/245 (8.6%)
  - Discontinuation group: 14/292 (4.8%)
- **Anticoagulant type** (p=0.037): VKI had highest bleeding rate
- **Procedure-related factors**:
  - Extraction + bone graft: p=0.016
  - Multiple implants (vs. single): p=0.032
  - Maxillary arch involvement: p=0.032
- **Multivariate (maintenance group)**:
  - Atrial fibrillation: OR=6.051
  - VKI: OR=3.679
- **DOAC** patients: comparable to PAI (no significant difference in bleeding rates)
- All bleeding episodes controlled with local hemostatic measures; no transfusions required

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective single-center design (selection bias possible)
- Management protocol not fully standardized across operators
- Follow-up duration variable; no thromboembolic event tracking
- INR values not consistently recorded for all VKI patients
- Cannot establish causation between discontinuation and thromboembolism (underpowered)

## 6. Related Work
- Madrid 2009 SR (minor surgery): OAT continuation safe for single extraction/implant
- Chahine 2019 SR: VKA+NOAC continuation recommended for most dental procedures
- Xiang 2026 SR+MA: DOAC continuous vs. interrupted — minimal bleeding procedures
- Moldovan 2023 SR: anticoagulant oral surgery overview

## 7. Glossary
- **VKI**: Vitamin K inhibitor (warfarin)
- **DOAC**: Direct oral anticoagulant (dabigatran, rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban)
- **PAI**: Platelet aggregation inhibitor (aspirin, clopidogrel)
- **CDM/SCRAP**: Common Data Model / Severance Clinical Research Analysis Portal
- **INR**: International Normalized Ratio
