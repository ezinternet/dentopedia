## Why Ingested

Adds the systematic-review layer to the polypharmacy-to-dry-mouth thread in geriatric-dentistry: [[geriatric-dentistry/halasabalu-kalgeri-2026-polypharmacy-oral-health-elderly]] (narrative review of drug classes) and [[geriatric-dentistry/kumar-2025-nutritional-status-dry-mouth-older-adults]] (dry mouth and malnutrition) lack a synthesis of tooth-loss and psychological outcomes of polypharmacy-associated dry mouth.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (MEDLINE, EMBASE, PsycINFO, CINAHL; PROSPERO CRD42021288945) of 9 studies (8 cross-sectional, 1 longitudinal; 37,459 participants aged 65+) on health conditions of dry mouth with polypharmacy.

Natural teeth fell from 16 (SD 9) to 12 (SD 8) as medicines rose from 0 to 5; only 2 studies addressed psychological outcomes, with depression prevalence up to 64% in older adults with dry mouth.

Narrative synthesis only (heterogeneous outcomes, mostly cross-sectional designs); supports prevention through medication-regimen optimisation rather than treating dry mouth after it develops.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review; 4개 DB, PROSPERO 등록) — 다약제 복용 (polypharmacy) 65세 이상 노인의 구강건조증 (xerostomia, dry mouth) 관련 건강 상태를 다룬 9편 (횡단 8·종단 1, 37,459명).

복용 약물 수가 0→5개로 늘면 잔존 자연치아 수는 16개(SD 9)에서 12개(SD 8)로 감소; 심리적 결과를 본 연구는 2편뿐이며 우울증 유병률이 최대 64%.

결과 이질성으로 서술적 종합만 시행, 대부분 횡단연구라 인과 추론에 한계; 구강건조 발생 후 치료보다 약물 처방 최적화를 통한 예방을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Categorises dry-mouth-related conditions in polypharmacy older adults as physical (tooth loss, swallowing difficulty) versus psychological.
- Identifies a paucity of psychological-outcome studies (n = 2).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Databases: MEDLINE, EMBASE, PsycINFO, CINAHL. Population aged 65+. Quality appraisal with Joanna Briggs Institute tools. 6852 citations screened; 9 studies included; narrative synthesis.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 37,459 participants, mean age range 68.5-85.0 years; studies published 2005-2019.
- Medicines 0 to 5: natural teeth 16 (SD 9) to 12 (SD 8).
- Medicines 1 to 3 or more: dental complications 1 (SD 2) to 2 (SD 2).
- Depression prevalence up to 64% in older adults with dry mouth; six further psychological conditions (self-consciousness, feeling tense, difficulty relaxing, irritability, difficulty completing tasks, less life satisfaction).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-level only. Outcome heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis; 8 of 9 studies cross-sectional. Few psychological studies. Authors call for dry-mouth prevention via optimised medication regimes.
