## Why Ingested

Cited by [[implants/survival/krisam-2019-factors-affecting-the-early-failure]] as one of the references (10–18) behind its background statement that early failures account for about 2–6% of implants placed. Ingested to make that benchmark traceable and to extend the evidence base of [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]].

## One-line Summary

Retrospective study of 1,592 operations (1,017 patients, 3,082 anodized Nobel implants) at one private referral clinic, 2000–2011: early inflammation occurred in 2.1% of operations, and smoking and surgeon experience were associated with early implant failure (abstract only).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Large private-practice denominator (3,082 implants) for early complications.
- Early inflammation risk factors with ORs: periodontitis history 3.91 (1.86–8.21), two-stage surgery 3.70 (1.75–7.85), lower jaw 4.73 (2.12–10.57), per-implant 1.33 (1.07–1.67).
- Links surgeon experience and smoking to early implant failure in routine practice.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Retrospective study, one private referral clinic; 1,017 consecutive patients, 3,082 anodized-surface implants (Nobel Biocare), 1,592 operations, 2000–2011. Events: mucosal inflammation with bone loss, and implant failure, up to the first annual examination. Logistic multivariate analysis of associated factors.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Early inflammation: 33 patients/operations (2.1%); risk factors as above, plus later treatment year (OR 1.22 per year, 1.08–1.39) and mid-aged patients. Early implant failure: smoking and surgeon experience significant (P<0.05); rates and ORs not available in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract only, partly truncated in the PubMed record. Single implant system; referral clinic. Early failure defined up to the first annual examination (after loading), wider than pre-loading definitions.

## 7. Glossary

Early inflammation: mucositis with bone loss before the first annual check; two-stage: submerged healing with second surgery.
