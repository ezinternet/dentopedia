## Three-line Summary

In-vitro comparative study (n=96 round diamond burs, 6 groups) assessing 5 decontamination methods (manual scrubbing, hot air oven, glass bead sterilizer, ultrasonic cleaner, autoclave) used routinely in dental clinics.

Autoclave achieved the greatest colony-forming-unit (CFU) reduction for Streptococcus mutans (80%) and Lactobacilli (76%); glass bead sterilizer achieved the greatest reduction for Candida albicans (74%).

No method achieved complete/absolute decontamination of the burs — autoclave was the relatively best method overall, but organism-specific residual contamination persisted across all groups.

Streptococcus mutans(80% 감소)와 Lactobacilli(76% 감소)는 오토클레이브에서 CFU 감소율이 가장 컸고, Candida albicans는 유리구슬멸균(glass bead sterilizer, 74% 감소)에서 가장 컸다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides organism-specific (S. mutans, Lactobacilli, C. albicans), quantitative (% CFU reduction) comparative data for 5 decontamination methods routinely used on dental burs in clinic settings.
- Directly demonstrates that no single reprocessing method — including autoclave — achieves complete decontamination of a complex-geometry rotary instrument, reinforcing that presterilization cleaning matters as much as the terminal sterilization step.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

96 round diamond burs divided into 6 groups. Burs were used for access cavity preparation (to generate standardized clinical contamination), then subjected to baseline bacteriological culture. Post-contamination, burs underwent one of: manual scrubbing, hot air oven, glass bead sterilizer, ultrasonic cleaner, or autoclave; post-decontamination cultures were then compared to baseline to compute % CFU/mL reduction per organism (S. mutans, Lactobacilli, C. albicans).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Autoclave: 80% CFU reduction for S. mutans; 76% for Lactobacilli — highest among all tested methods for these two organisms.
- Glass bead sterilizer: 74% CFU reduction for C. albicans — highest for this organism.
- No method (including autoclave) achieved absolute/complete decontamination in any group.
- Overall ranking: autoclave judged the relatively best method among those tested.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only source; no access to full methodology (culture protocol details, statistical testing, exact n per group, control/baseline group composition) or discussion/limitations as stated by the original authors.
- In-vitro / bench study with a small single-instrument-type sample (diamond burs only); generalizability to other bur types (carbide, endodontic files) not established by this paper.
- No sterility assurance data (spore-based biological indicators) reported — endpoint is CFU reduction of vegetative/fungal organisms, not confirmed sterility.

## 7. Glossary

- **CFU (Colony-Forming Unit)**: 콜로니형성단위, a measure of viable bacterial/fungal load in a culture.
- **Glass bead sterilizer**: 유리구슬멸균기, a rapid heat-sterilization device using heated glass beads, historically used for chairside instrument tip sterilization.
- **Hot air oven**: 건열멸균기, dry-heat sterilization device.
