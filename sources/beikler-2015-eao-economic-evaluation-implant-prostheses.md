## Why Ingested

RPD 선택 결정 축에서 **비용은 환자가 실제로 결정을 내리는 변수인데 위키에 근거가 없었다.** [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]]가 15년 무작위 비교에서 보철 성공률이 동등하다고 결론지은 이상, 결정은 비용·침습도로 넘어가는데 그 축의 근거가 비어 있으면 사다리가 거기서 끊긴다. 본 EAO 합의문은 임플란트를 축으로 근관치료·치주치료·고정성/가철성 국소의치를 **비용효과 관점에서 직접 줄세운** 유일한 합의급 문헌이고, 여기서 가철성 국소의치는 비교군으로 등장한다. 임플란트 축([[wiki/overviews/implants-clinical-decision-ladder]])과 보철재료 축([[wiki/overviews/prosthetic-materials-decision-ladder]]) 어느 쪽도 경제성 평가를 담고 있지 않다.

## Three-line Summary

EAO consensus-conference narrative review of English-language cost-effectiveness literature comparing dental implant therapy against root canal treatment, periodontal treatment, and fixed or removable partial dentures; heterogeneity across studies prevented meta-analysis.

Tooth-preserving care won where it was viable — root canal treatment was the most cost-effective initial option for a restorable incisor, orthograde retreatment the most cost-effective salvage, and non-surgical periodontal therapy both more effective and cheaper than an implant crown for molars with Class I furcation invasion — while after failed endodontic retreatment, a single implant-supported crown became more cost-effective than fixed or removable partial dentures; for single missing teeth the implant-versus-fixed-bridge comparison split across evaluations, and mandibular implant overdentures improved OHRQoL at higher cost than complete dentures.

The consistent economic message is that the cheapest durable option is usually the one that keeps the natural tooth, and that prosthetic replacement choices should be costed against outcome (quality-adjusted tooth years), not against fee alone — though the authors close by noting the underlying evaluations are too few and too methodologically varied to settle the question.

## 세줄요약

유럽골유착학회 (European Association for Osseointegration, EAO) 합의회의의 서술적 문헌고찰: 임플란트 치료를 근관치료·치주치료·고정성/가철성 국소의치와 비용효과 (cost-effectiveness) 관점에서 비교했으며, 연구 간 이질성이 커서 메타분석은 시행하지 못했다.

치아를 살릴 수 있는 곳에서는 보존이 이겼다 — 수복 가능한 절치는 근관치료가 가장 비용효과적인 1차 선택, 실패 시 정방향 재치료가 가장 비용효과적, 1급 근분지부 병변 (Class I furcation invasion) 대구치는 비수술적 치주치료가 임플란트 단관보다 **더 효과적이면서 더 저렴**했다; 반면 근관 재치료까지 실패한 뒤에는 임플란트 단관이 고정성·가철성 국소의치보다 비용효과적이었고, 단일 결손에서 임플란트 vs 고정성 브릿지는 평가마다 결론이 갈렸으며, 하악 임플란트 오버덴쳐는 총의치보다 삶의 질은 좋았으나 비용이 더 들었다.

일관된 경제적 메시지는 **"가장 싼 내구적 선택지는 대개 자연치를 지키는 쪽"** 이며, 보철 대체 선택은 수가가 아니라 **결과 보정 지표(quality-adjusted tooth years)** 로 비용을 따져야 한다는 것이다. 단 저자들 스스로 기반 평가 연구가 너무 적고 방법론이 제각각이라 결론을 확정할 수 없다고 닫는다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- The only consensus-level attempt to rank implant therapy against tooth-preserving and prosthetic alternatives **on cost-effectiveness rather than survival alone**.
- Establishes a decision order that repeatedly favours retaining the natural tooth while it is restorable.
- Identifies the crossover point: once endodontic retreatment has failed, the implant single crown overtakes both fixed and removable partial dentures economically.
- Documents genuine disagreement in the single-tooth implant-versus-bridge comparison rather than smoothing it away.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review commissioned for an EAO consensus conference. English-language literature on cost-effectiveness of implant therapy versus alternatives (complete dentures, fixed partial dentures, root canal treatment, periodontal treatment). No meta-analysis was performed because of high heterogeneity in the economic methodologies used across the included evaluations.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Clinical situation | Most cost-effective option |
|---|---|
| Restorable central incisor, irreversible pulpitis + coronal lesion | Root canal treatment (initial) |
| Initial root canal treatment failed | Orthograde retreatment |
| Root canal retreatment failed | Extraction + single implant-supported crown > fixed or **removable** partial denture |
| Molar, Class I furcation invasion | Non-surgical periodontal therapy (more effective **and** cheaper than implant crown) |
| Single missing tooth | Split: two evaluations favour implant crown (better outcome, lower cost) vs fixed partial prosthesis; one finds implant costs more with greater survival |
| Edentulous mandible | Implant overdenture (2 or 4) improves OHRQoL but costs more than complete denture |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **No meta-analysis** and no pooled estimates — every row above is a narrative reading of a small number of evaluations.
- Published 2015; implant and laboratory costs, and the relative price of digital removable workflows, have moved since.
- Economic evaluations are jurisdiction-bound (fee schedules, insurance coverage, labour costs). None of the included work is Korean, so absolute conclusions do not transfer; the **ordering** is the transferable part, and even that only weakly.
- The removable partial denture appears only as a comparator, never as the indexed intervention, so its own cost-effectiveness profile is not characterised.
- Authors' own closing statement is that more evaluations following established health-economics methodology are needed.

## 7. Glossary

- **Cost-effectiveness analysis (비용효과분석)**: comparison of interventions by cost per unit of health outcome gained.
- **QATY (보정 치아 생존년, Quality-Adjusted Tooth Years)**: outcome measure combining tooth survival with quality weighting, analogous to QALY.
- **Orthograde retreatment (정방향 재근관치료)**: re-treatment of a failed root canal through the crown rather than surgically.
- **Class I furcation invasion (1급 근분지부 병변)**: early horizontal bone loss in the furcation, probeable but less than one-third of tooth width.
- **Implant overdenture (임플란트 오버덴쳐)**: removable complete denture retained by implants.
