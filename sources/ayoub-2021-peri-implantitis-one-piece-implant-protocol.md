## Three-line Summary

Case series (EC Dental Science 2021) reporting a multi-step peri-implantitis management protocol for one-piece implants using diode laser decontamination, albumin-coated allograft, sticky bone, and PRF membrane, with clinical and radiographic outcomes assessed.

The protocol successfully managed peri-implantitis: diode laser decontaminated infected bone/soft tissue without thermal damage; sticky bone + PRF supported bone regeneration; clinical parameters and radiographic bone levels improved across the case series.

Case-series evidence with no control group and a single-operator center; the protocol is exploratory and requires controlled trials before broad recommendation for one-piece implant peri-implantitis.

## 세줄요약

증례 시리즈 (EC Dental Science 2021) — 원피스 임플란트(One-piece Implant) 주위염(Peri-implantitis) 관리 프로토콜: 다이오드 레이저 소독 + 알부민 코팅 동종골 + 스티키본(Sticky Bone) + PRF 막 복합 적용, 임상·방사선학적 평가.

프로토콜 적용 후 치주낭 깊이·탐침 시 출혈 감소, 방사선학적 골 수준 개선; 다이오드 레이저는 열 손상 없이 오염 조직 소독; 스티키본+PRF는 골재생 지지.

대조군 없는 단일 술자 증례 수준 — 원피스 임플란트 주위염에 대한 근거 기반 권고를 위해서는 대조 연구 필요.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Case series. Patients with peri-implantitis around one-piece implants. Protocol: surgical debridement, diode laser decontamination, albumin allograft + sticky bone graft, PRF membrane placement. Clinical parameters (pocket depth, bleeding on probing) and radiographic bone levels assessed pre- and post-treatment.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Protocol successfully managed peri-implantitis in one-piece implant cases
- Diode laser effective for decontamination without thermal damage
- Sticky bone + PRF: supported bone regeneration
- Clinical and radiographic improvement demonstrated
- Protocol reproducible across case series

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Case-level evidence; limited generalizability. No control group. Single operator/center.

## 7. Glossary
- **One-piece implant**: Implant with integrated abutment (no separate abutment screw)
- **Sticky bone**: Autologous fibrin + allograft mixture with PRF
- **PRF**: Platelet-rich fibrin — autologous growth factor membrane
- **Piezosurgery**: Ultrasonic bone cutting with selective hard-tissue cutting
