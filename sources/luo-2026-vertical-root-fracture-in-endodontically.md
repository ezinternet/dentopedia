## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Aust Endod J 2026) of risk factors for vertical root fracture (VRF) in endodontically treated teeth, split into treatment-related factors (instrument-induced dentinal microcracks, dentine volume and fracture resistance after access cavity/canal preparation) and occlusal factors (high-risk occlusal forces, root stress changes from crown morphology and crown restoration).

The abstract reports no numeric results; its main finding is an evidence-imbalance statement: occlusal analysis techniques and force measurement methods remain limited, with far fewer studies than on treatment-related risk factors.

The review proposes flexible sensor technology as a future route to measure occlusal risk factors; as an abstract-only narrative review with no stated search method, it supports framing and research direction, not clinical thresholds.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰 (Narrative review; Aust Endod J 2026) — 근관치료치 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)의 위험인자를 치료 관련 요인(기구에 의한 상아질 미세균열 (Dentinal Microcrack), 접근와동·근관 성형에 따른 상아질 용적·파절저항성 변화)과 교합 요인(고위험 교합력, 치관 형태·치관 수복에 따른 치근 응력 분포 변화)으로 나누어 정리.

초록에는 수치 결과가 없으며, 핵심 메시지는 근거의 불균형: 교합 분석 기법과 교합력 측정 방법은 제한적이어서 치료 관련 위험인자 연구보다 연구 수가 훨씬 적음.

향후 교합 위험인자 탐색에 유연 센서 (Flexible Sensor) 기술을 제안; 초록만 확인된 내러티브 리뷰이고 검색 방법이 명시되지 않아 임상 기준치가 아니라 문제 틀 짜기·연구 방향 근거로만 사용 가능.

## 7. Glossary
- **Vertical root fracture (VRF)**: longitudinal fracture of the root, here in endodontically treated teeth.
- **Dentinal microcrack**: incomplete crack in root dentine, associated in the experimental literature with canal preparation instruments.
- **Fracture resistance**: load a root withstands before fracturing in laboratory testing.
- **Occlusal force**: bite load transmitted to the tooth and root, a candidate VRF risk factor.
- **Flexible sensor**: thin, conformable force-sensing device proposed by the authors for measuring occlusal loads.
