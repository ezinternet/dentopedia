## Why Ingested

Investigates how dental technical tools (grinding, polishing), chewing simulation, and thermocycling affect phase composition (monoclinic/tetragonal/cubic) of 3Y-, 4Y-, and 5Y-TZP zirconia, using both Garvie-Nicholson and Rietveld XRD methods. Key finding — mechanical processing transforms monoclinic phase *back* to tetragonal (not toward more monoclinic), contradicting some prior literature. Directly relevant to the wiki's coverage of LTD aging, zirconia surface adjustment, and phase stability.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/wertz-2021-the-influence-of-surface-preparation]]

## Three-line Summary

Mechanical surface processing (grinding, polishing) and chewing simulation caused the monoclinic phase fraction of 3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP dental zirconia to decrease — not increase — with polishing and chewing completely eliminating the monoclinic phase and driving conversion to tetragonal phases. The preferred tetragonal phase differed by yttria content: phase t for 3 mol% and phase t″ for 5 mol%. Rietveld refinement proved substantially more accurate than the Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya formula for quantifying all five crystallographic phases (m, t, t′, t″, c).

## 세줄요약

기계적 표면 처리(연삭·연마)와 저작 시뮬레이션은 3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP 치과용 지르코니아 (Y-TZP, Yttria-stabilized Tetragonal Zirconia Polycrystalline)의 단사정상 (Monoclinic Phase) 비율을 감소시키며, 연마·저작은 단사정상을 완전히 소멸시켜 정방정상 (Tetragonal Phase)으로 전환한다. 선호되는 정방정상은 이트리아 함량에 따라 달라지며 3 mol%에서는 t상, 5 mol%에서는 t″상이 주를 이룬다. 리트벨트 정밀화 (Rietveld Refinement)는 Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya 공식보다 훨씬 정확하게 5개 결정상(m, t, t′, t″, c) 전체를 정량 분석하였다.

## Key Contributions

1. Mechanical processing (grinding, polishing, chewing) transforms monoclinic phase fraction *toward* tetragonal — the opposite of thermal LTD degradation
2. Polishing alone and chewing simulation both completely eliminated the monoclinic phase fraction across all 3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP specimens
3. Grinding halved monoclinic fraction in 3Y-TZP but Garvie-Nicholson method could not detect this two-step transformation; Rietveld could
4. Yttria content determines preferred tetragonal phase: t for 3 mol% (3Y-TZP), t″ for 5 mol% (5Y-PSZ)
5. Thermocycling produced a slight *increase* in monoclinic fraction, consistent with LTD mechanism
6. No low-temperature degradation (LTD) was observed as a consequence of mechanical processing
7. Rietveld quantifies all five phases (m, t, t′, t″, c); Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya only resolves monoclinic vs. a single tetragonal peak

## Methodology

- **Specimens**: 7 types of commercially available zirconia plates (3Y, 4Y, 5Y-TZP, 2 manufacturers each), sintered in inFire HTCspeed furnace (1450°C/120 min), milled with Sirona inLab MC XL 5-axis mill
- **Treatments at 6 measurement positions**:
  - A: Untreated reference
  - B: Grinding only (Panther Flame 055M at 15,000 rpm, 6 min; then 055XF at 15,000 rpm, 5 min)
  - C: Grinding + polishing
  - D: Polishing only (goat hair bur + Panther diamond polishing agent, 2 min)
  - E: Grinding + polishing + chewing simulation + thermocycling (combined aging)
  - Thermocycling (TC): standard aging protocol
- **Surface roughness**: Confocal laser scanning microscopy (CLSM, Keyence VK-X1000/1050), Sa parameter per ISO 25178-2:2012; n=5 areas × 5 measurements per sample, 100 µm × 100 µm field
- **Phase analysis**:
  - XRD: Bruker D8 Discover, CuKα radiation (1.54 Å), 25°–75° range, three frames 1200 s each; VÅNTEC-500 area detector
  - Semi-quantitative: Garvie-Nicholson equation + Toraya correction (DIN EN ISO 13356)
  - Quantitative: Rietveld refinement with TOPAS 4.2; Rwp 1.5–2.2
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn post hoc test with Bonferroni correction; Cohen's r for effect size

## Key Results

**Surface roughness (Sa)**:
- Sa increased significantly after grinding (position B)
- Sa decreased significantly after polishing (positions C and D)
- Chewing simulation (E) did not significantly change Sa
- Positions C, D, E did not significantly differ from each other across all materials

**Phase composition — Rietveld**:
- Grinding (B): monoclinic fraction halved vs. untreated (A) in 3Y-TZP
- Polishing (C/D) and chewing (E): monoclinic phase entirely disappeared → converted to tetragonal
- Thermocycling: slight increase in monoclinic fraction
- 3Y-TZP: tetragonal phase t dominant; 5Y-PSZ: tetragonal phase t″ dominant
- Garvie-Nicholson method missed the intermediate grinding-induced reduction (halving), detecting only the final disappearance

**Phase composition — Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya**:
- Could not detect halving of monoclinic from A→B; only showed disappearance after polishing/chewing
- Absolute monoclinic fractions up to 3× lower than Rietveld — Rietveld more accurate
- Method limitation: not suitable for multi-phase systems with low monoclinic content

**Method comparison**:
- Rietveld resolves phases t, t′, t″, c individually — reveals yttria-composition-dependent tetragonal-phase preference
- t′ and t are very similar in "tetragonality" (t: 1.0159, t′: 1.0149) → potential misattribution; t′ distinction questionable
- Rietveld Rwp values 1.5–2.2 indicate excellent fit

## Limitations

- Limited number of specimens (2–3 per yttria content) and measurement positions — results are exploratory
- Manufacturer differences within each yttria class were within measurement accuracy; not independently powered
- Cannot determine failure mode: failure-controlled (local stress peaks → damage) vs. transformation-controlled (phase transformation → micro crack formation)
- Strong overlap of tetragonal phases t/t′ and t″/c in XRD makes precise distinction uncertain
- Two-plate experimental design to accommodate thermocycling treatment; potential confounding

## Glossary

- **Y-TZP**: Yttria-stabilized Tetragonal Zirconia Polycrystalline — dental zirconia stabilized with yttria (Y₂O₃)
- **LTD**: Low-Temperature Degradation — slow spontaneous t→m phase transformation at mouth temperatures accelerated by moisture
- **Rietveld refinement**: Full-pattern XRD fitting that quantifies all phases simultaneously; superior to peak-intensity ratios
- **Garvie-Nicholson**: Semi-quantitative XRD formula using peak intensities of m(111)/m(-111) vs t(111) — DIN EN ISO 13356 standard method
- **Toraya correction**: Empirical correction applied to Garvie-Nicholson α to give volume fraction Vm
- **Phases t, t′, t″**: Three tetragonal polymorphs differing in tetragonality (c/a ratio); t for low-yttria, t″ for high-yttria
- **Sa**: Arithmetical mean height of surface per ISO 25178-2 (3D roughness parameter)
- **Thermocycling (TC)**: Accelerated aging via cyclic thermal stress in water baths simulating intraoral thermal fatigue
