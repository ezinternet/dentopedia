## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Eur Endod J 2023) synthesizing the molecular biology of the inflammatory radicular cyst (IRC) — its transition from periapical granuloma (PG) via epithelial cell rests of Malassez (ERM) proliferation — and appraising non-invasive diagnostic methods against histopathology.

IRC formation proceeds through three stages (ERM proliferation driven by IL-1/IL-6/TNF-α/KGF/EGF → epithelial lining formation → cyst growth via MMP-mediated ECM degradation, RANKL/RANK-driven bone resorption, and VEGF-driven fluid accumulation), yet only ~20% of epithelium-containing PGs (~45% of all PGs) progress to IRC and the reason remains unknown; CBCT accuracy for PG-vs-IRC differentiation ranges widely (31–83%) across studies, while ultrasound (63–100%) and MRI (70.5–100%) show more consistent but still study-dependent accuracy.

Histopathology (serial sectioning of an excisional biopsy, not incisional/curettage) remains the only reliable gold standard for distinguishing PG from IRC, since the two conditions represent stages of the same inflammatory continuum and non-invasive imaging alone cannot substitute for it in guiding surgical-vs-nonsurgical endodontic treatment decisions.

## 세줄요약

서사적 문헌고찰 (Eur Endod J 2023) — 염증성 치근낭 (Inflammatory Radicular Cyst, IRC)의 분자생물학, 즉 치근단 육아종 (Periapical Granuloma, PG)에서 말라세즈 상피세포잔사 (Epithelial Cell Rests of Malassez, ERM) 증식을 통한 IRC 이행 기전을 종합하고, 비침습적 진단법을 조직병리와 비교 평가.

IRC 형성은 3단계로 진행됨(IL-1·IL-6·TNF-α·KGF·EGF에 의한 ERM 증식 → 상피 내벽 형성 → MMP 매개 세포외기질 분해·RANKL/RANK 골흡수·VEGF 매개 체액축적을 통한 낭종 성장); 그러나 상피세포를 포함한 PG(전체 PG의 ~45%)의 단 ~20%만이 IRC로 진행하며 그 이유는 아직 불명; CBCT의 PG-IRC 감별 정확도는 연구별로 31~83%로 편차가 크고, 초음파(63~100%)·MRI(70.5~100%)는 상대적으로 일관되나 여전히 연구 의존적.

조직병리(절제생검의 연속절편, 절개생검/소파술 아님)가 PG와 IRC를 구별하는 유일한 신뢰 가능한 표준검사로 남아있음 — 두 병태가 동일한 염증 연속체의 서로 다른 단계이기 때문에 비침습적 영상만으로는 대체 불가하며, 이는 수술적 vs 비수술적 근관치료 결정에 직결됨.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates the current molecular biology of IRC etiopathogenesis into a three-stage model: ERM proliferation → epithelial lining formation → cyst growth/expansion.
- Tabulates the growth factors (EGF, PDGF, TGF-α, TGF-β, KGF, VEGF) and interleukins (IL-1 through IL-33) implicated in IRC formation, with target cells and biological effects.
- Reviews and critiques three competing cyst-lining-formation theories (nutritional deficiency, abscess, merging of epithelial strands) — none fully explains why only a minority of epithelialized PGs become IRCs.
- Summarizes Simon's (1980) true-cyst vs bay-cyst classification and its clinical relevance to surgical-vs-nonsurgical treatment prognosis.
- Compiles a comparative accuracy table (Table 3) of CBCT, ultrasound, MRI, and cytokine/fluid-aspiration methods against histopathology across 16 published studies.
- Details histological differential-diagnosis pitfalls (random/incisional sectioning can falsely suggest epithelial lining; serial sectioning of excisional biopsy required).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative literature review (not a systematic review/meta-analysis) synthesizing histologic, immunohistochemical, and diagnostic-imaging studies on IRC etiopathogenesis, classification, histology, and differential diagnosis from PG. No PRISMA protocol, search strategy, or quality appraisal reported — the paper is a mechanistic/biological synthesis with an embedded narrative appraisal of the non-invasive-diagnostic-accuracy literature (Table 3 aggregates study-level accuracy/sensitivity/specificity figures from CBCT, ultrasound, MRI, and cytokine-aspiration studies without pooling/meta-analytic synthesis).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Epidemiology**: IRCs account for ~54% of 317 biopsied apical lesions in one series (Alotaibi 2020); 46.6–68% of all maxillary cystic lesions are IRCs. In a 256-tooth histopathology series (Ramachandran Nair 1996): 35% periapical abscess, 50% PG, 15% IRC (9% true cyst + 6% bay cyst).

**Etiopathogenesis**: ~45% of all PGs contain epithelial cells, but only ~20% of epithelium-containing chronic periapical lesions become IRCs — mechanism for this selective progression remains unknown. Key mediators: IL-1/IL-6/TNF-α drive ERM proliferation; NF-κB regulates IL-1/IL-6/TNF-α/MMP expression; KGF (stimulated by IL-1/6/8, TNF-α, PDGF) is a potent epithelial mitogen; MMP-13 shows the strongest association with PG→IRC transformation; RANKL/RANK/OPG axis and VEGF drive bone resorption and intracystic fluid accumulation respectively, enabling cyst expansion.

**Classification**: True cysts (self-contained, no direct apical-foramen connection) generally require surgical intervention; bay cysts (directly connected to apical foramen) may resolve with non-surgical root canal therapy alone.

**Diagnostic accuracy vs histopathology (Table 3, selected)**:
| Method | Studies (accuracy range) |
|---|---|
| CBCT | 31.1%–83% (AUC 0.44–0.76 across 7 studies) |
| Ultrasound ± Doppler | 62.5%–100% across 8 studies |
| MRI | 70.5%–100% (2 studies) |
| Cytokine/fluid aspiration | qualitative only (high IL-1α, TNF-α, MCP-1, RANTES in IRC fluid) |

Histopathology (serial-sectioned excisional biopsy, H&E ± Masson's trichrome/Brown-Brenn stains) remains the gold standard; incisional/random sectioning risks false-positive epithelial-lining findings that mimic IRC in what is actually PG.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative (non-systematic) review — no formal search strategy, inclusion criteria, or risk-of-bias appraisal; diagnostic-accuracy figures in Table 3 are pooled qualitatively, not meta-analytically.
- The central biological question (why only ~20% of epithelialized PGs progress to IRC) is explicitly stated as unresolved by the authors — the review synthesizes candidate mechanisms without settling the question.
- Non-invasive diagnostic accuracy data spans small, heterogeneous single-center studies (n range ~11–118) with inconsistent index tests (CBCT grey-scale values, PSI, lesion volume) — cross-study comparison is limited.
- Authors call for further investigation into the specific triggers of epithelial proliferation and for development of more predictable non-/minimally-invasive diagnostic methods.

## 7. Glossary

- **IRC (Inflammatory Radicular Cyst)**: 염증성 치근낭 — epithelium-lined pathological cavity arising from chronic apical periodontitis, downstream of periapical granuloma.
- **PG (Periapical Granuloma)**: 치근단 육아종 — chronic inflammatory periapical lesion without a defined epithelial-lined cavity (though may contain epithelial islands).
- **ERM (Epithelial Cell Rests of Malassez)**: 말라세즈 상피세포잔사 — dormant epithelial remnants in the periodontal ligament, the proliferative source of IRC lining epithelium.
- **AP (Apical Periodontitis)**: 근단치주염 — the umbrella clinical diagnosis encompassing both PG and IRC (and periapical abscess), not histologically distinguishable by clinical exam alone.
- **True cyst vs bay cyst**: Simon's (1980) classification by whether the cystic lumen has a direct connection to the root canal apical foramen (bay cyst = connected, may heal non-surgically; true cyst = self-contained, generally needs surgery).
- **KGF (Keratinocyte Growth Factor)**: 각질형성세포 성장인자 — epithelial-mitogenic cytokine central to ERM proliferation stage.
- **RANKL/RANK/OPG**: 파골세포분화인자/수용체/오스테오프로테게린 — bone-resorption signaling axis driving cyst expansion into surrounding bone.
- **PSI (Periapical Status Index)**: CBCT-based periapical lesion severity scoring index used in several accuracy studies cited.
