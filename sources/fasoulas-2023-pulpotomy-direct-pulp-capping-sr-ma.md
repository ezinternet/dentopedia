## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis comparing efficacy of materials used in direct pulp capping (DPC) and pulpotomy procedures in carious permanent teeth, published in Clinical and Experimental Dental Research (2023). Searched four databases plus clinical trial registries; included only randomized controlled trials comparing ≥2 materials. Applied Bayesian network meta-analysis methodology alongside pairwise comparisons.

Biodentine and mineral trioxide aggregate (MTA) demonstrated significantly higher success rates compared to calcium hydroxide (Ca(OH)₂) as gold-standard control. Pulpotomy procedures generally showed higher overall success rates compared to direct pulp capping. Success rates remained consistent across varied follow-up periods (6 months to 5 years), though heterogeneity in outcome definitions was present.

Heterogeneous outcome definitions across trials and varied follow-up durations limit evidence synthesis. Limited RCT-only evidence base restricts clinical material options. Calcium hydroxide remains a valid baseline comparator despite lower success rates in meta-analysis, given its long clinical safety record. Biocompatible calcium silicate cements (Biodentine) offer material properties advantages over traditional Ca(OH)₂ and warrant selection in evidence-based treatment planning.

## 세줄요약

우식 노출 영구치의 직접치수복조(DPC)·생활 치수 요법(pulpotomy) 재료 효과 비교 SR+MA (CEDR 2023). 4개 DB + 임상시험 레지스트리 검색, RCT만 포함, ≥2개 재료 비교. Bayesian network MA + pairwise 적용.

Biodentine·MTA가 Ca(OH)₂ 대비 유의미 높은 성공률. Pulpotomy가 DPC보다 일반적으로 성공률 높은 경향. 추적 기간(6개월~5년) 다양하나 성공률 방향 일관.

Outcome 정의 불일치·추적기간 이질성. RCT 근거 제한적. Ca(OH)₂는 오랜 임상 안전 기록으로 기준 유지. 칼슘 실리케이트 시멘트(Biodentine)는 물성 장점과 근거기반 치료 선택 지지.
