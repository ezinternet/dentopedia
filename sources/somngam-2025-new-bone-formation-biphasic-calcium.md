## Why Ingested

Surveillance hit (bone-graft-material topic): the first network meta-analysis (NMA) to rank biphasic calcium phosphate (BCP) against autograft, allograft, beta-TCP and xenograft for histomorphometric new bone formation after lateral sinus augmentation. It extends the pairwise BCP-vs-DBBM comparison in [[sinus-lift/lateral/alkandari-2025-bcp-dbbm-sinus-elevation-sr-ma]] into a four-material ranking and adds the histomorphometric layer that the survival-only review [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]] could not resolve.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and random-effects network meta-analysis (PRISMA-NMA, PROSPERO CRD42024607526) of 11 RCTs (283 patients, 362 lateral sinus augmentations, healing 5-10 months) comparing biphasic calcium phosphate (BCP) with autograft (AB), freeze-dried allograft (AL), beta-TCP and deproteinized bovine bone xenograft (Xeno).
Versus BCP, new bone formation was +12.33% for AB (95% CI 10.74-13.93), +5.14% for AL (3.33-6.95) and -4.14% for Xeno (-6.38 to -1.90); SUCRA ranking AB > AL > BCP > TCP > Xeno, and Xeno left 6.21% more residual graft than BCP (2.81-9.61).
BCP gives sufficient bone formation and outperforms xenograft histomorphometrically, but the evidence is mostly 60:40 HA/TCP, residual height of 6 mm or less, 5-10 months follow-up, and almost no implant-survival data.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First NMA to place BCP within a ranked network of AB, AL, TCP and Xeno for sinus-augmentation new bone formation.
- Quantifies the gap: BCP is 12.33% below autograft and 5.14% below freeze-dried allograft but 4.14% above deproteinized bovine bone.
- Shows xenograft retains the most residual graft (pairwise +6.21% vs BCP), consistent with slower resorption of HA-dominant scaffolds.
- Explicitly lists the evidence gaps: HA/TCP ratio, residual height, smoking, long-term survival and cost-effectiveness.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR + random-effects NMA (Stata 14.1 `network`), PRISMA-NMA; PROSPERO CRD42024607526; RoB 2.0 risk-of-bias.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Cochrane, Scopus, Embase, inception to 25 Nov 2024; English, human studies.
- **Eligibility**: RCTs of lateral sinus augmentation with implants, BCP vs another bone substitute, histomorphometric new bone as a percentage; combined materials and additive-supplemented grafts excluded.
- **Selection**: 268 records, 131 removed before screening (37 duplicates, 94 ineligible), 32 full texts, 21 excluded, 11 RCTs included.
- **n**: 283 patients, 362 sinus augmentations, healing 5-10 months. Allografts all FDBA; xenografts all DBB. Network: AB, AL, TCP, BCP, Xeno.
- **Outcomes**: primary new bone formation (%); secondary residual graft material (%), pairwise only (9 studies), implant survival (reported by few trials).
- **Methods checks**: global inconsistency test, node-splitting, comparison-adjusted funnel plot, SUCRA, league table; sensitivity analysis restricted to 60:40 HA/TCP.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Comparison vs BCP (NMA, new bone formation) | Difference | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Autograft (AB) | +12.33% | 10.74 to 13.93 |
| Allograft (AL, FDBA) | +5.14% | 3.33 to 6.95 |
| Beta-TCP | -2.26% (n.s.) | -10.53 to 6.01 |
| Xenograft (DBB) | -4.14% | -6.38 to -1.90 |

- SUCRA ranking: AB > AL > BCP > TCP > Xeno. Global inconsistency p = 0.6153; no funnel-plot evidence of small-study effects.
- Residual graft (pairwise vs BCP): Xeno +6.21% (2.81 to 9.61); AL -14.71% (-20.59 to -8.82); AB -28.1% (-33.34 to -22.86); TCP -2.4% (n.s.).
- Heterogeneity: I2 = 0% in all pairwise comparisons except TCP (single study). Sensitivity analysis limited to 60:40 HA/TCP stayed consistent (I2 0%).
- Risk of bias: 7 RCTs some concerns, 1 high risk, 3 low risk.
- Only BCP vs Xeno has a real evidence base (Cordaro 2008, Froum 2008, Lindgren 2009, Schmitt 2013, Oh 2019, Arunjaroensuk 2024); TCP rests on one trial (Jelusic 2017).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- BCP almost entirely 60:40 HA/TCP (one 70:30 trial); results may not transfer to other ratios.
- Residual bone height of about 6 mm or less in included trials; sinus width and healing-period effects not analyzable.
- Implant survival rarely reported; no long-term (5-10 year) BCP data; smoking subgroup not analyzable (mostly non-smokers or under 10 cigarettes/day).
- NMA for residual graft impossible (autograft residue undetectable); English-only, no grey literature, no manual journal search.
- No established threshold for a clinically meaningful difference in new bone percentage; cost-effectiveness not assessed.

## 6. Related Work
- Alkandari 2025: pairwise BCP vs DBBM SR+MA (+3.48% new bone for BCP) - same direction as the NMA Xeno result.
- Del Fabbro 2025: SR of 49 RCTs, survival equivalent across materials - this paper adds the histomorphometric ranking.
- Thomas 2026: SR of alloplastic vs biologic grafts, same hierarchy of autograft above alloplast above xenograft in new bone.

## 7. Glossary
- **BCP (biphasic calcium phosphate)**: synthetic ceramic of hydroxyapatite (HA) plus beta-TCP; TCP fraction drives faster resorption.
- **NMA (network meta-analysis)**: simultaneous direct plus indirect comparison of several interventions, yielding a ranking.
- **SUCRA**: surface under the cumulative ranking curve; higher value means higher probability of ranking best.
- **FDBA / DBB**: freeze-dried bone allograft / deproteinized bovine bone (xenograft).
- **Histomorphometric new bone formation**: percentage of new bone area in a core biopsy from the augmented sinus.
