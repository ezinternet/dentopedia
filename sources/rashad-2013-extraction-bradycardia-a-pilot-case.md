## Why Ingested

발치 중 발생하는 미주신경성 실신 (Vasovagal Syncope, VVS) 논의는 [[wiki/drug/systemic-disease/li-2026-syncope-in-dentistry-risk-factors]]·[[wiki/drug/systemic-disease/malave-2022-vasovagal-reactions-during-interventional-pain]] 같은 서술적 고찰에 의존하고 정작 **단순 발치 중 심박수가 실제로 어떻게 변하는지**를 측정한 1차 자료가 위키에 없었다. 본 전향적 case-crossover 연구는 마취 충분·최소 외상 조건에서도 심박수가 유의하게 하강함을 보여, 삼차신경-심장 반사 (Trigeminocardiac Reflex, TCR) 가설의 임상 실측 근거로 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective case-crossover pilot study (n = 61 simple posterior mandibular molar extractions in normotensive, systemically healthy adults; 27 male/34 female, age 18–59) measuring heart rate (HR) with a pulse oximeter before anesthesia, during extraction (lowest value), and immediately after.

Mean HR fell significantly across all three time points — 88.4 ± 14.1 → 86.1 ± 13.4 → 81.4 ± 11.4 bpm (p = 0.000 for each of the three pairwise comparisons) — while age group had no effect (p = 0.70/0.88/0.84) and female HR did not drop from before to during extraction (p = 0.97).

The decline was not preceded by a rise, so the authors argue against a biphasic vasovagal reflex and for a peripheral trigeminocardiac mechanism; the study is a single-center pilot with no control arm and no BP measurement reported per patient.

## 세줄요약

전향적 case-crossover 예비연구 (Prospective Case-crossover Pilot Study), n = 61 — 전신질환 없는 정상혈압 성인(남 27·여 34, 18–59세)의 하악 후치아 단순 발치에서 국소마취 전·발치 중(최저치)·발치 직후 심박수 (Heart Rate, HR)를 펄스옥시미터로 측정.

평균 심박수는 88.4 ± 14.1 → 86.1 ± 13.4 → 81.4 ± 11.4 bpm으로 세 시점 모두 유의하게 감소했고(p = 0.000, 세 쌍비교 각각), 연령군 간 차이는 없었으며(p = 0.70/0.88/0.84) 여성은 마취 전 대비 발치 중 감소가 유의하지 않았다(p = 0.97).

심박수 하강이 선행 상승 없이 나타나 저자들은 이상성(二相性) 미주신경성 반사를 반박하고 말초 삼차신경-심장 반사 (Trigeminocardiac Reflex, TCR)를 주장하나, 단일기관 예비연구·대조군 없음·환자별 혈압 미보고가 한계다.

- **Journal**: Head & Face Medicine 2013;9:29
- **DOI**: 10.1186/1746-160X-9-29
- **PMID / PMCID**: 24456612 / PMC3854007
- **Institution**: Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Department, Azad University of Medical Sciences, Tehran, Iran (protocol peer-reviewed and approved by the university)
- **Registration / ethics**: Helsinki Declaration followed; chair-side written informed consent

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides direct, per-patient hemodynamic measurement (not inference) of HR during simple extraction in a homogenous, systemically healthy population — the exact scenario in which dental-office syncope is usually described.
- Finds a **monophasic** decline (no preceding increase), which the authors use to challenge the widely taught biphasic vasovagal reflex (VVR) explanation and to support a peripheral trigeminocardiac reflex (TCR) mechanism.
- Shows the decline occurs despite adequate local anesthesia and deliberately minimal surgical trauma, i.e. it is not simply a pain- or anxiety-driven event.
- Reports that the fall is robust in males but absent from before-to-during extraction in females (p = 0.97) — a sex-specific nuance rarely reported in this literature.
- Argues for routine HR monitoring even in "simple" extractions, framing bradycardia → syncope as a predictable rather than accidental event.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective case-crossover (each patient is his/her own control across three time points); described by the authors as a pilot study. Simple consequential (non-random) sampling.
- **Setting / period**: single center, Tehran, Iran; late 2011.
- **Inclusion**: need for simple extraction of one posterior mandibular tooth; fully erupted third molars of level A depth and Class I available space. **Exclusion**: history of cardiovascular or pulmonary disease; cases requiring flap, decoronation, or root fragmentation.
- **n**: 61 patients / 61 teeth (34 third, 14 second, 13 first mandibular molars).
- **Anesthesia**: up to two cartridges of 4% articaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine (Ultracaine DSF, Sanofi-Aventis), conventional inferior alveolar nerve block; anesthesia verified with a dental explorer before extraction.
- **Operator**: all extractions by one oral and maxillofacial surgeon, with care to avoid excess trauma or inadequate anesthesia.
- **Measurement**: Nellcor OxiMax IM-N-550 pulse oximeter; HR recorded (1) before local anesthetic injection, (2) during extraction — lowest HR detected, (3) immediately afterwards. Blood pressure is discussed in the introduction but no per-patient BP data are reported in the results.
- **Statistics**: PASW Statistics 18; mean HR (MHR) ± SD; paired t-test for pairwise comparisons; significance p < 0.05. Also stratified by age group (Table 1) and sex (Table 2).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Demographics**: 61 patients; 27 male (44.3%), 34 female (55.7%); age 18–59 years, 31.1 ± 9.9; largest band 21–30 years (42.6%).
- **Global MHR (Table 1, Total)**: before 88.4 ± 14.1 → during 86.1 ± 13.4 → after 81.4 ± 11.4 bpm. All three pairwise comparisons significant, **p = 0.000 each** (abstract states p < 0.05 for all three).
- **By age group** — no significant differences between age groups before (p = 0.70), during (p = 0.88), or after (p = 0.84) extraction. Group extremes: 46–50 y (n = 1) 102/100/96 bpm; 15–20 y (n = 3) 86.7 ± 14.6/88.7 ± 3.5/80 ± 8.0; 31–35 y (n = 8) 94.4 ± 21.5/90 ± 17.8/83.2 ± 9.3.
- **By sex (Table 2)**: male (n = 27) 87.2 ± 13.8 → 85.7 ± 13.5 → 80.0 ± 11.7; female (n = 34) 89.4 ± 14.5 → 88.0 ± 13.1 → 82.6 ± 11.2. Post-extraction MHR lower than both during and before in both sexes (p = 0.000). **Exception**: female MHR during extraction vs before was not different (p = 0.97).
- **Adverse events**: no case of VVR/syncope occurred in the series.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Pilot / no control arm**: single-group before-during-after comparison; the "crossover" is temporal within-patient, so no un-anesthetized or non-extraction comparison group exists to separate procedure effect from time/diurnal/positional drift.
- **BP not reported**: the introduction promises SBP/DBP discussion but no BP values are given in Results, so the reflex cannot be characterized as cardioinhibitory vs vasodepressor. The conclusion sentence ("heart rate **and blood pressure** alterations...") overstates what was measured.
- **PRN reporting discrepancy (source-data issue)**: the abstract rounds the means to 88/86/81 and states "p < 0.05 for all three", whereas the Results/Figure 1 legend state p = 0.000 for each comparison. Table values (88.4/86.1/81.4) are used on this page; the discrepancy is logged as a source-data issue.
- **Sampling**: consecutive/consequential sampling at one center, one operator, one tooth type (posterior mandibular, mostly third molars) — limits generalizability to maxillary, surgical, or systemically compromised patients.
- **No anxiety/pain measurement**: pain scores and anxiety scales were not recorded, so the claim that the response is independent of pain relies on the surgical protocol (minimal trauma), not on measured pain.
- **Single intraoperative HR value** (the lowest detected) rather than continuous recording — transient bradycardia at anesthetic injection, reported by prior authors, could not be captured.

## 6. Related Work

- **Paramaesvaran & Kingon**: BP and HR during extraction in 30 patients; reported transient bradycardia upon local anesthetic administration — consistent with the present time-course findings.
- **Alemany-Martinez et al.**: nine-point HR/BP measurement during surgical extractions; recorded an *increase* from baseline to immediately before extraction and a transient bradycardia concurrent with elevated SBP/DBP — the present study's mono-phasic decline differs in the pre-extraction segment (no incision/osteotomy/suturing steps here).
- **Schaller et al. (TCR literature since 1999)**: central trigeminocardiac reflex with ~20% HR drop in skull-base/neurosurgical procedures; the present study is presented as one of the first descriptions of a *peripheral* TCR during tooth extraction.

## 7. Glossary

- **VVR (Vasovagal Reflex / Vasovagal Syncope)**: neurally mediated transient loss of consciousness, classically biphasic (initial sympathetic rise, then vagal bradycardia/hypotension).
- **TCR (Trigeminocardiac Reflex)**: reflex arc from trigeminal afferents (sensory nucleus of CN V) via reticular internuncial fibers to vagal motor nucleus efferents, producing bradycardia/hypotension on trigeminal stimulation.
- **MHR (Mean Heart Rate)**: arithmetic mean of measured HR values in a group (bpm).
- **Case-crossover**: design in which each subject serves as his/her own control at different time points of the same exposure.
- **TLOC (Transient Loss of Consciousness)**: umbrella term for syncope and similar brief consciousness loss.
