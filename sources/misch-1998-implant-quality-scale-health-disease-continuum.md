## Why Ingested

The survival folder needs a clinical grading tool for implant health, not just a binary success/failure call. This Misch 1998 paper presents the implant quality scale (adapted from James), which places each implant on a health-to-disease continuum with treatment implications. It complements the 2008 Pisa definitions in [[implants/survival/misch-2008-implant-success-survival-failure-icoi-pisa]].

## Three-line Summary

Clinical assessment paper (Oral Health, 1998) introducing the implant quality scale, a health-to-disease continuum for implants — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

Pain and mobility are the primary criteria; probing depth and progressive bone loss indicate disease, and any pain, vertical mobility, uncontrolled bone loss or generalized periradiolucency warrants removal.

The scale is a clinical rubric; the abstract gives no validation or reliability data, so it should be cited as a clinical framework.

## 세줄요약

임상 평가 논문 (Oral Health, 1998) — 임플란트의 건강-질환 연속체를 나타내는 임플란트 품질 척도 (implant quality scale) — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

통증과 동요도가 주요 기준이며, 탐침 깊이와 진행성 골소실은 질환을 시사; 통증·수직 동요·조절되지 않는 골소실·광범위 방사선 투과상이 있으면 제거를 고려.

임상 루브릭이며 초록에 검증·신뢰도 자료가 없으므로 임상 틀로 인용할 것.
