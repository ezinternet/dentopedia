## Why Ingested

Cochrane-level SR+MA providing the highest-tier comparative evidence on horizontal and vertical bone augmentation for implant treatment — a foundational evidence anchor for [[bone-regeneration/vorovenci-2024-horizontal-ridge-augmentation-od-gbr-ridge-split-sr-ma]] and [[bone-regeneration/benic-2014-horizontal-bone-augmentation-guided-regeneration]], which extend and refine this 2009 baseline. The review's key finding (short implants preferred over vertical augmentation in resorbed mandibles due to higher complication rates) is a frequently-cited clinical decision threshold missing from the wiki until now.

## Three-line Summary

Cochrane systematic review and meta-analysis of 13 RCTs evaluated horizontal (3 trials) and vertical (10 trials) bone augmentation techniques for dental implants.

Vertical augmentation was associated with more implant failures (OR=5.74, borderline p=0.06) and complications (OR=4.97, p<0.05) compared to short implants; osteodistraction gained more bone than inlay grafts (MD 3.25 mm).

Short implants may be preferable to vertical augmentation in resorbed mandibles; bone substitutes are a valid alternative to autogenous bone with less morbidity.

## 세줄요약

13개 RCT를 포함한 Cochrane 체계적 고찰 및 메타분석으로, 수평 및 수직 골증대술을 임플란트 치료에 평가했다.

수직 골증대술은 단축 임플란트에 비해 임플란트 실패(OR=5.74, 경계 p=0.06) 및 합병증(OR=4.97, p<0.05)이 더 많았고, 골신장술이 삽입골 이식보다 골증가량이 많았다(MD 3.25 mm).

흡수된 하악에서는 단축 임플란트가 수직 증대술보다 우선 고려될 수 있으며, 골대체재는 자가골의 유효한 대안이다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, Issue 4, 2009
- **DOI**: 10.1002/14651858.CD003607.pub4
- **Institution**: Cochrane Oral Health Group
- **Study type**: Cochrane Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis (SR+MA)
- **Number of included RCTs**: 13 (3 horizontal augmentation trials, 10 vertical augmentation trials)
- **Patients**: 106 horizontal augmentation patients, 218 vertical augmentation patients
- **Follow-up**: 4 months – 3 years

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides Cochrane-level comparative evidence that **short implants outperform vertical bone augmentation** in severely resorbed mandibles (fewer failures, fewer complications)
- Quantifies the complication burden of vertical augmentation (OR=4.97, up to 60% complication rates in some trials)
- Demonstrates that **bone substitute materials are a valid alternative to autogenous bone** in GBR, with reduced donor-site morbidity and equivalent or better outcomes in some comparisons
- Shows osteodistraction (골신장술) achieves 3.25 mm more bone gain than inlay grafting
- Patients prefer bone substitute block over iliac crest autograft (OR=0.03, p=0.02)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search strategy**: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, EMBASE; language-unrestricted
- **Inclusion criteria**: RCTs of horizontal or vertical bone augmentation prior to or simultaneous with implant placement
- **Outcomes assessed**: prosthesis failure, implant failure, complications (biological and prosthetic), bone gain (radiographic/histomorphometric), patient preference/satisfaction
- **Statistical analysis**: random-effects models; pooled OR and mean differences (MD) with 95% CI
- **Risk of bias assessment**: Cochrane risk-of-bias tool applied to each included trial

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Vertical Augmentation vs Short Implants
| Outcome | OR / MD | p-value |
|---|---|---|
| Implant failures | OR = 5.74 | 0.06 (borderline) |
| Complications | OR = 4.97 | < 0.05 |

### Osteodistraction vs Inlay Grafting
| Outcome | MD | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Bone gain | +3.25 mm | Osteodistraction superior |

### Bone Substitute vs Autogenous Bone (GBR)
| Outcome | MD / OR | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Bone gain | +0.60 mm | Bone substitute marginally superior |
| Patient preference | OR = 0.03 | Strong preference for bone substitute over iliac graft |

### Complication rates
- Vertical augmentation overall: up to 60% complication rate
- Complications included membrane exposure, infection, graft failure, nerve injury

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small number of included trials with small sample sizes — underpowered for definitive conclusions
- Short follow-up in several studies; long-term survival data lacking
- High risk of bias identified in several included RCTs (allocation concealment, blinding)
- Significant clinical heterogeneity limited the scope of meta-analyses
- Authors conclude findings require confirmation with larger, longer-term RCTs
- Cochrane review reflects evidence available through 2008; subsequent updates would be needed

## 7. Glossary

- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration)**: 골유도재생술 — barrier membrane technique to confine bone regeneration
- **Osteodistraction (골신장술)**: gradual bone lengthening via distraction osteogenesis
- **Onlay graft (피개골 이식)**: bone block placed on top of the ridge
- **Inlay graft (삽입골 이식)**: bone placed within an osteotomy in the ridge
- **OR (Odds Ratio)**: odds ratio — measures relative likelihood of an event between two groups
- **MD (Mean Difference)**: difference in means between groups in continuous outcomes
- **RCT**: 무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial)
