## Why Ingested

기존 [[botulinum-toxin/de-la-torre-canales-2024-botulinum-toxin-a-myogenous]] (umbrella review)는 효과 지속기간과 용량을 전면에서 다루지 않았는데, 본 SR은 RCT만을 대상으로 효과 지속기간 (8~24주)과 용량 매개변수를 명시적으로 종합한다. 또한 [[botulinum-toxin/minston-2025-effect-pain-following-botulinum-toxin]], [[botulinum-toxin/kim-sr-2023-effect-botulinum-toxin-masticatory-muscle]] 등 위키 보유 개별 RCT가 이 SR의 포함 연구이므로 상위 수준에서 맥락화하고, [[botulinum-toxin/abdulrahman-2026-botulinum-toxin-myofascial-pain-masseter-temporalis]] (2년 전향 연구)의 장기 효과 주장과 대비하기 위해 인제스트했다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review without meta-analysis of 10 randomized clinical trials (PROSPERO CRD420251178977; five databases searched to 22 Dec 2025; RoB-2 and GRADE) of intramuscular botulinum toxin type A (BoNT-A) for myogenous temporomandibular disorders (TMD).

In placebo-controlled trials BoNT-A did not consistently beat saline, while trials in refractory myofascial pain reported larger pain reductions (for example about 50% with higher pressure pain threshold); effects were generally described as lasting 8 to 24 weeks, total doses of 40 to 100 U showed no consistent dose-response, and adverse events were mostly mild and transient.

Most trials carried some risk-of-bias concerns and protocols were heterogeneous (pooling judged inappropriate), so BoNT-A is not supported as first-line care and may be considered only in selected refractory cases after failed conservative therapy.

## 세줄요약

무작위 임상시험 (Randomized Clinical Trial, RCT) 10편을 메타분석 없이 종합한 체계적 고찰 (Systematic Review, SR) — 근육성 측두하악장애 (Myogenous Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에 대한 보툴리눔 독소 A형 (Botulinum Toxin Type A, BoNT-A) 근육 내 주사 (PROSPERO 등록, 5개 DB, 2025년 12월 22일까지 검색, RoB-2·GRADE 적용).

위약 대조 시험에서 BoNT-A는 생리식염수 대비 일관된 우월성을 보이지 못했고, 난치성 근막통 (refractory myofascial pain) 시험에서는 더 큰 통증 감소(약 50% 감소, 압통역치 증가)가 보고됨; 효과는 대체로 8~24주 지속, 총 용량 40~100 U 범위에서 일관된 용량-반응 없음, 이상반응은 대부분 경미·일과성.

대부분 시험이 비뚤림 위험 (Risk of Bias) "일부 우려"였고 프로토콜 이질성이 커 통합 불가 — 1차 치료로 권고되지 않으며, 보존적 치료 실패 후 선별된 난치성 환자에서만 고려.

## 2. Key Contributions
- RCT-only systematic review focused on duration of clinical effect and dosage parameters of BoNT-A in myogenous TMD.
- Qualitative synthesis (no meta-analysis); GRADE applied per outcome (pain, function, duration, dose-response, adverse events), but the full GRADE/Summary-of-Findings table is in Supplementary Material 2, not in the retrieved text.
- Conclusion: not first-line; adjunct or second-line in selected refractory patients within multimodal care.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PICOS: patients with myogenous TMD by RDC/TMD or DC/TMD; intramuscular BoNT-A to masticatory muscles; comparators placebo, conservative therapy or none; primary outcomes pain (VAS/NRS) and duration of effect (time to relapse); secondary outcomes MMO, tenderness, adverse events; RCTs only.
- Databases: MEDLINE/PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS; no language/date limits; last search 22 Dec 2025. Two independent reviewers (Rayyan); RoB-2; GRADE.
- Flow: 179 records, 24 duplicates removed, 155 screened, 36 full texts, 26 excluded, 10 RCTs included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Trial (year) | n | Population | Total dose | Comparator | Follow-up | Main result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foscaldo 2025 | 40 | awake bruxism, DC/TMD | 40 U | EMG biofeedback | 6 mo | no pain difference; bruxism reduced only with biofeedback |
| Yurttutan 2019 | 73 | bruxism-related myogenic, RDC/TMD | 90 U | occlusal splint | 6 mo | pain down in all groups; BoNT-A groups superior to splint alone (per Table 2) |
| De la Torre Canales 2024 | 28 | refractory myofascial pain | 80 U | saline | 6 mo | about 50% pain reduction, higher PPT vs placebo |
| De la Torre Canales 2021 | 54 | refractory myofascial pain | 80 U | acupuncture; saline | 1 mo | pain down in all groups; only BoNT-A raised PPT |
| Minston 2024 | 45 | DC/TMD myalgia | 100 U | saline | 2 mo | no significant difference vs placebo |
| Sitnikova 2024 | 57 | myogenic, crossover | 50 U | saline | 32 wk | both improved; no BoNT-A superiority; effect up to 16 wk |
| Montes-Carmona 2021 | 60 | myofascial | 100-150 U | lidocaine; saline | 6 mo | pain/function superior to lidocaine and saline |
| Reeve 2024 | 75 | myalgia | 100 U | saline | 3 mo | both groups reduced pain similarly (needling effect suggested) |
| De la Torre Canales 2022 | 80 | persistent myofascial | 40/70/100 U | saline | 180 d | mandibular movement improved vs placebo regardless of dose |
| Kim 2023 | 21 | myogenic + headache, pilot | max 150 U | saline | 12 wk | less pain, tender points, headache vs placebo |

- Duration: effects described over 8 to 24 weeks; peak 8 to 16 weeks then gradual decline; differences in the crossover trial mainly weeks 8 to 16.
- Dose: 40 to 100 U total in dose-comparison trials, no consistent superiority of higher doses; units are formulation-specific.
- Adverse events: transient weakness, smile asymmetry, headache, fatigue, reduced EMG activity; isolated mild dysphagia; no serious or permanent events.
- Risk of bias: mostly "some concerns" overall, none high.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No pooling (heterogeneous formulations, muscles, doses, comparators, follow-up); GRADE details are only in the supplement.
- Internal inconsistency: the Results text states placebo-controlled trials showed no significant BoNT-A vs saline difference "across all included studies", yet Table 2 and the Discussion report superiority over placebo in refractory myofascial pain trials; Yurttutan 2019 is also stated differently in Table 2 (BoNT-A superior to splint alone) and in the Results text (no consistent differences vs conservative therapies). Logged as source-data-issue.
- Authors call for preregistered, standardized-dose RCTs with long follow-up.

## 7. Glossary
- **BoNT-A**: botulinum toxin type A, reversible chemodenervation agent.
- **Myogenous TMD**: TMD of masticatory-muscle origin (myalgia, myofascial pain).
- **Refractory myofascial pain**: pain persisting after conventional conservative therapy.
- **PPT**: pressure pain threshold.
- **RoB-2**: Cochrane Risk of Bias tool version 2.
