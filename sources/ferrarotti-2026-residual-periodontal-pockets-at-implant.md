## Why Ingested

The wiki's peri-implantitis risk hierarchy rests on umbrella reviews that treat "history of periodontitis" as one binary exposure ([[implants/peri-implantitis/giok-2024-risk-factors-peri-implantitis-umbrella]]) and on long-term cohorts where periodontitis history is modified by maintenance compliance ([[implants/survival/roccuzzo-2022-implants-periodontitis-history-20year-prospective]]). This systematic review asks a narrower, chairside-relevant question: among patients whose periodontitis has already been treated, does the periodontal status left at implant placement (residual pockets) carry additional peri-implantitis risk? It was ingested as new primary-synthesis evidence that refines the "periodontitis history" anchor into a measurable pre-placement endpoint.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42024567630; MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane to December 2025; 3870 records) qualitatively synthesizing 4 cohort studies (3 retrospective, 1 prospective; 70-100 patients, 165-260 implants each; follow-up 12-276 months) on residual probing pocket depth (PPD) after active periodontal therapy and later peri-implantitis in treated periodontitis patients.

All 4 studies directionally linked persisting residual pockets to more peri-implantitis: Vagia 2021 HR 1.06 (1.02-1.10) per residual site with PPD >3 mm; Kumar 2018 OR 2.73 (1.45-5.16); Zhang 2018 >10% of sites with PPD >=6 mm significantly associated (HR 3.62 [1.05-12.51] in Table 2, HR 6.71 [1.84-24.47] in the text); Pjetursson 2012 4.1 vs 1.9 residual pockets in patients with vs without peri-implantitis. No meta-analysis was possible, and no study assessed furcation involvement.

Residual pockets look like a plausible, modifiable risk indicator that argues for periodontal re-evaluation before placement and tighter supportive care, but only 4 heterogeneous observational studies (moderate risk of bias in 3) with non-uniform exposure/outcome definitions and maintenance regimens support it, so no pocket-depth threshold can be set.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR; PROSPERO CRD42024567630; 4개 DB, 2025년 12월까지, 3870편 검색)으로 코호트 4편(후향 3·전향 1; 연구당 환자 70-100명·임플란트 165-260개; 추적 12-276개월)을 정성적으로 종합 - 활성 치주치료 후 잔존 치주낭 (Residual Pocket, PPD)과 이후 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 발생의 관계.

4편 모두 잔존 치주낭이 임플란트주위염 증가와 연관된 방향: Vagia 2021 PPD >3 mm 잔존 부위당 HR 1.06 (1.02-1.10); Kumar 2018 OR 2.73 (1.45-5.16); Zhang 2018 PPD >=6 mm 부위 >10% 시 유의(표 HR 3.62 [1.05-12.51], 본문 HR 6.71 [1.84-24.47]); Pjetursson 2012 임플란트주위염군 4.1개 vs 비발생군 1.9개 잔존 치주낭. 메타분석 불가, 분지부병변 (Furcation Involvement)은 평가한 연구 없음.

식립 전 치주 재평가와 지지 치주/임플란트 유지관리 강화의 근거가 되는 위험 지표이나, 이질적인 관찰연구 4편(3편 중등도 편향위험)뿐이고 노출·결과 정의와 유지치료 방식이 제각각이라 확정적 치주낭 깊이 기준은 제시할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic review that targets residual periodontal pockets (and furcation involvement) at the time of implant placement, rather than periodontitis history or status at peri-implantitis diagnosis, as the exposure.
- Shows the direction of association is consistent across the only 4 eligible cohorts despite heterogeneous definitions.
- Identifies one study (Kumar 2018) in which gingivitis/periodontitis on adjacent teeth at implant restoration was associated with peri-implantitis (OR 8.0, 95% CI 1.2-70.1).
- Documents the evidence gap: no furcation data, no standardized peri-implantitis case definition, no uniform supportive-care reporting.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review, PRISMA-conformant, prospectively registered (PROSPERO CRD42024567630); qualitative synthesis because of heterogeneity.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Scopus, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL to December 2025 plus 8-journal manual search (last 5 years) and reference screening.
- **PICO**: Patients with >=1 implant and full-mouth periodontal exam after active periodontal therapy and before placement/loading; exposure residual PPD >4 mm and/or furcation involvement >=II; comparison no residual PPD/furcation; primary outcome peri-implantitis incidence; designs RCT, CCT, prospective/retrospective cohorts, case series with >=1 year follow-up after loading. Exclusions: exclusively diabetic or exclusively smoking cohorts, case reports, animal/in vitro.
- **Screening**: duplicate independent screening, Cohen's kappa 0.93 (title/abstract) and 0.97 (full text); risk of bias with Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (kappa 0.98).
- **n**: 3870 records; 1746 duplicates removed; 2109 screened at title/abstract; 15 full texts; 11 excluded; 4 included.
- **Outcomes**: peri-implantitis incidence (primary); implant loss, mucositis, peri-implant PPD, marginal bone level (secondary).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Included studies (published 2012-2021; USA, Switzerland, France, China; mean age 44.5-68.9 years; smokers included in all):

| Study | Design | Follow-up | Patients/implants | Exposure threshold | Key result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kumar 2018 | Retrospective | >60 months | 86 / 222 | Residual PPD >=5 mm at placement | OR 2.73 (1.45-5.16), mixed-effects model; adjacent-tooth gingivitis/periodontitis OR 8.0 (1.2-70.1) |
| Pjetursson 2012 | Retrospective | mean 94.8 (36-276) months | 70 / 165 | Residual pockets PPD >=5 mm after active therapy | 4.1 vs 1.9 residual pockets (peri-implantitis vs none); no implant-level stratified data; implant survival 95.8%; peri-implantitis 22.2% (level 1) / 8.8% (level 2) |
| Vagia 2021 | Retrospective | mean 9.4 years | 86 / 260 | Residual PPD >=5 mm grouping; Cox model per site with PPD >3 mm | PIKA HR 1.06 (1.02-1.10); PIBE HR 1.07 (1.03-1.12); survival 99.2%; peri-implantitis 20.1% (PIKA) / 17.8% (PIBE) |
| Zhang 2018 | Prospective | 12-60 months | 100 / 214 | >10% of sites with PPD >=6 mm | Table 2: HR 3.62 (1.05-12.51); text: HR 6.71 (1.84-24.47); survival 100%; peri-implantitis 11.2% implant level, 16% patient level |

Event counts reported in the text:
- Vagia 2021: 50 patients (157 implants) with residual PPD >=5 mm vs 36 patients (103 implants) without; peri-implantitis in 11 patients / 14 implants, of which 9 patients / 12 implants were in the residual-pocket group and 2 patients / 2 implants in the non-residual group.
- Kumar 2018: 37 patients (108 implants) with residual PPD >=5 mm vs 49 patients (114 implants) without; 129 implants with peri-implantitis (76 residual-pocket group, 53 non-residual) vs 93 without.
- Zhang 2018: of 24 implants with peri-implantitis, 21 were in patients with >10% sites PPD >=6 mm and 3 in patients with <=10%; of 190 implants without peri-implantitis, 97 vs 93.

Risk of bias: the 3 retrospective studies moderate; the prospective study (Zhang) low. Furcation involvement: no study evaluated it. Peri-implantitis definitions varied (PPD >4 to >=6 mm thresholds with bleeding/suppuration and radiographic bone loss >=2 mm or >=3 mm); the authors note that more inclusive case definitions seemed to find stronger associations.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 4 studies; 3 of 4 retrospective, so selection bias and incomplete exposure ascertainment are likely.
- Heterogeneous residual-PPD thresholds (>=3 to >=6 mm), peri-implantitis definitions, follow-up (12-276 months), and level of analysis (patient vs implant); none used the 2017 World Workshop periodontitis classification; non-independence of implants within patients not always handled.
- Supportive care ranged from 3-month university recalls to self-reported annual visits, so residual-PPD effect cannot be separated from maintenance quality; smokers included in all cohorts.
- No furcation data; no formal publication-bias assessment (<10 studies); internal inconsistency in the Zhang HR between Table 2 and text.
- Future: prospective designs with standardized definitions of residual periodontitis and peri-implantitis, multivariable adjustment (smoking, systemic disease, implant factors, SPC/SPIC adherence), and furcation-specific studies.

## 6. Related Work
- Roccuzzo 2022: 20-year cohort showing periodontitis history lowers implant survival with SPT compliance as the key modifier.
- Giok 2024 / Tarce 2025: umbrella reviews placing periodontitis history among the top peri-implantitis risk factors.
- Lee 2026: SPT largely offsets periodontitis-related survival penalty.

## 7. Glossary
- **Residual PPD**: probing pocket depth persisting after active periodontal therapy (definitions ranged from >=3 to >=6 mm).
- **APT**: active periodontal therapy.
- **SPC / SPIC**: supportive periodontal care / supportive peri-implant care.
- **PIKA / PIBE**: two peri-implantitis case definitions used by Vagia 2021 (Karoussis et al. vs Berglundh et al.).
- **NOS**: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale for observational study quality.
