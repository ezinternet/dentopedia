## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=377 implants/196 patients): RAS inhibitors significantly associated with higher secondary implant stability (ISQ +3.59 at exposure, p=0.025); beta-blockers showed no significant benefit.

## 한줄요약
후향코호트(377 임플란트/196환자): 레닌-안지오텐신계 억제제가 이차안정성 유의하게 향상(ISQ +3.59, p=0.025), 베타차단제 단독은 유의하지 않음.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=377 implants/196 patients, 2015–2020, Baden-Württemberg, Germany); implant stability quotient (ISQ) measured at insertion (primary stability) and at exposure ~120 days (secondary stability) via resonance frequency analysis; groups: NAH (331), BB (13), BB+RAS (11), RAS (22); multivariable linear mixed regression adjusted for age, sex, jaw, bone status.

RAS inhibitors associated with significantly higher secondary ISQ (reg. coeff. +3.59, 95% CI 0.46–6.71, p=0.025); beta-blockers showed no significant effect (reg. coeff. −0.93, p=0.648); combination group non-significant (reg. coeff. +2.71, p=0.224); primary stability at insertion unchanged across all groups.

First clinical study to separate RAAS inhibitors from beta-blockers in implant stability — RAAS pathway (Ang II suppression per Shimizu 2008) appears clinically relevant to osseointegration; β-blocker null result despite animal evidence (Uchibori 2020, Huang 2024) highlights the importance of drug-class subgroup analyses.

## 세줄요약

후향코호트(377임플란트/196환자, 2015–2020, 독일); 공명주파수분석(Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)으로 삽입 시 일차안정성·노출 시(~120일) 이차안정성 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 측정; 4군(정상혈압·베타차단제(BB)·BB+레닌-안지오텐신-알도스테론계 (RAAS)·RAAS 단독); 다변량 선형혼합 회귀 분석.

RAAS 억제제군: 이차 ISQ 유의하게 높음(회귀계수 +3.59, 95% CI 0.46–6.71, p=0.025); 베타차단제 단독: 유의하지 않음(회귀계수 −0.93, p=0.648); 일차안정성은 군간 차이 없음.

임플란트 안정성에서 RAAS 억제제 대 BB 분리 비교 최초 임상 연구 — Shimizu 2008의 Ang II→RANKL 경로 차단이 임상적으로 유효함을 시사; 동물 근거(Uchibori 2020, Huang 2024)와 달리 BB 단독 효과 없음 — 약물 클래스별 하위군 분석의 중요성.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 항고혈압제 약물군(RAS 억제제 vs BB)을 구분하여 ISQ에 미치는 영향을 최초로 비교한 임상 연구
- RAS 억제제가 이차 임플란트 안정성(골유착 후 ISQ)에 유의한 양성 효과 확인
- 베타차단제 단독군에서는 유의한 효과 없음 — 선행 동물 연구와의 불일치 제시
- 항고혈압제 복용군과 비복용군 간 일차안정성(삽입 직후 ISQ)은 차이 없음 확인
- 현재까지 항고혈압제 복용과 임플란트 안정성에 관한 최대 규모 데이터셋

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Study design**: Retrospective cohort, 2015–2020, tertiary outpatient clinic
- **Population**: 196 patients (51% male), median age 65 (range 26–93); 377 implants
- **Groups**: NAH (331 implants), BB (13), Comb BB+RAS (11), RAS (22)
- **Implant systems**: Straumann SLA (76.7%), Thommen Inicell (23.3%)
- **Outcome measures**: ISQ at insertion (primary) and at exposure ~120 days (secondary) via Osstell Mentor
- **Statistics**: Multivariable linear mixed regression; confounders = age, sex, jaw, region, bone status, implant system, healing time
- **Inclusion**: Periodontally healthy, no antiresorptives, no heavy smoking (>20/day), no severe systemic disease (ASA ≤II)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Group | ISQ at Insertion | ISQ at Exposure |
|---|---|---|
| NAH | 71.8 ± 8.7 | 73.7 ± 8.1 |
| AH (all) | 74.1 ± 5.6 | 75.7 ± 5.9 |
| BB only | 71.7 ± 5.4 | 72.00 ± 6.4 |
| Comb (BB+RAS) | 77.0 ± 5.5 | 78.36 ± 5.1 |
| RAS only | 74.0 ± 5.2 | 76.64 ± 5.0 |

- **RAS at exposure**: reg. coeff. 3.59 (95% CI 0.46–6.71, p<0.05) ✅ significant
- **Comb at exposure**: reg. coeff. 2.71 (95% CI -1.67–7.10, p=0.224) NS
- **BB at exposure**: reg. coeff. -0.93 (95% CI -4.91–2.51, p=0.648) NS
- Age ≥70 more likely on AHT: OR 2.30 (95% CI 1.17–4.64, p=0.009)
- Mandibular vs maxillary: reg. coeff. +8.18 (p<0.001)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small AH subgroup (n=46 implants) → low statistical power for BB and Comb groups
- No blood pressure data available
- No data on drug duration
- Selective vs non-selective beta-blockers not differentiated
- All ISQ values >70 → clinical relevance of differences is theoretical
- Retrospective design: selection bias, no randomization
- Future: large prospective RCTs needed

## 6. Related Work
- Wu 2016 (PMID 26935774): only prior study on AHT and implant survival (HR 0.12)
- Masri 2024: EIF rates by AHT status in retrospective cohort
- Hamade 2024: SR+MA on hypertension and dental implants (null result for failure)
- Chidiac 2026: 10-year KM showing AHT protective effect

## 7. Glossary
- ISQ: 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient) — resonance frequency analysis output (1–100)
- RAS inhibitors: ACE inhibitors + ARBs + renin inhibitors
- Primary stability: ISQ at implant insertion
- Secondary stability: ISQ at implant exposure (~120 days) — reflects osseointegration
- tRAS: tissue renin-angiotensin system
- ATII: angiotensin II
