## Why Ingested

Italian Consensus Conference (29 experts, modified Delphi, GRADE framework) providing structured evidence-based guidance across all major full-arch decision points. Complements the existing tilted vs. axial SR+MA [[implants/full-arch/del-fabbro-2022-full-arch-tilted-axial-implants-sr-ma]] and the global edentulous maxilla consensus [[implants/full-arch/schwarz-2026-global-consensus-edentulous-maxilla-guidelines]] by adding operationalized surgical, prosthetic, and maintenance recommendations with GRADE evidence certainty ratings and specific numerical thresholds for clinical decision-making.

## Three-line Summary

Italian Consensus Conference (29 experts, modified Delphi, 3 rounds, ≥90% threshold, literature 2015–2026, GRADE framework) produced seven evidence-based statements on full-arch implant rehabilitation covering periodontal risk, GBR, implant number, tilt configuration, prosthetic material, maintenance, and systemic risk.

Key quantitative findings: 4 vs 6 mandibular implants show equivalent 5-year survival (98.6% vs 99.4%, p=0.136); tilted vs axial implants show equivalent short-term MBL (MD=0.00 mm, 95% CI −0.01 to 0.02); monolithic zirconia prosthesis survival 97.23% at ~50 months; structured maintenance reduces peri-implantitis risk by ~75%; bruxism OR for implant failure = 2.189.

All seven statements achieved ≥90% consensus; six statements at High certainty of evidence and one (GBR, Statement 2) and one (Systemic risk, Statement 7) at Moderate; all recommendations graded Strong.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Seven operationalized GRADE-graded consensus statements** covering all major full-arch clinical decisions in a single document with explicit numerical thresholds.
2. **Statement 3**: Largest controlled dataset (Caramês et al., n=943 patients, 5989 implants) — 4 vs 6 mandibular implant survival equivalent at 5 years (98.6% vs 99.4%, p=0.136).
3. **Statement 4**: Tilted implants equivalent to axial — MD=0.00 mm MBL (95% CI −0.01 to 0.02, p=0.75) — consistent with ITI 6th Consensus.
4. **Statement 5**: Monolithic zirconia preferred — 97.23% prosthesis survival over ~50 months; veneered zirconia chipping 15–54%.
5. **Statement 6**: Structured maintenance ~75% peri-implantitis risk reduction; risk-stratified recall (low 6mo, moderate 4mo, high 3mo) with explicit intervention thresholds.
6. **Bruxism operationalization**: three-tier diagnostic system (possible/probable/definite), STAB tool recommended, OR for implant failure = 2.189 in probable bruxers.
7. **MRONJ framework**: SIPMO-SICMF stratification aligned with AAOMS 2022 — operationalized contraindication levels for HD-BMA and LD-BMA patients.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Modified Delphi: 3 iterative rounds; Round 1 pre-conference anonymous voting; Round 2 in-person discussion at Falcade (February 2026); Round 3 post-conference refinement and final vote.
- 100% response rate across all three rounds.
- GRADE certainty: High (Statements 1, 3, 4, 5, 6), Moderate (Statements 2, 7).
- Strength: All seven recommendations = Strong.
- Agreement range: 90% (Statement 2) to 98% (Statement 6).
- Literature: priority to SR+MA, RCTs, prospective cohorts; narrative reviews and retrospective studies included when higher-level evidence unavailable.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Statement 1: Periodontal Risk Assessment (Evidence: High, 96% agreement)
- Peri-implantitis risk in periodontitis patients: RR = 4.09 (95% CI 1.93–8.58)
- Non-compliant patients: OR = 14.59 for implant loss (95% CI 1.30–164.29; 20-year follow-up, Roccuzzo et al.)
- Stability criteria (partially edentulous): FMBS < 15%, FMPS < 20%, no PD ≥ 5 mm with BoP, no radiographic progression (all four simultaneously, minimum 6 months post-active therapy)
- Fully edentulous: "biological readiness" — smoking ≤10 cig/day, HbA1c < 7.5%, mucosal phenotype assessment
- Tools: PRA + 2017/2018 Staging/Grading + IDRA (8 vectors, perio-tools.com/idra)

### Statement 2: Guided Bone Regeneration (Evidence: Moderate, 90% agreement)
- Implant survival in regenerated bone comparable to native bone (Elnayef et al. meta-analysis)
- Simultaneous GBR: ISQ ≥ 70 or insertion torque ≥ 35 Ncm, defect ≤ 2 mm (no apical dehiscence beyond apical third)
- Staged GBR (6-month healing): defects > 2 mm, compromised wound healing, anatomically complex sites
- GBR indicated only when prosthetically driven placement impossible in pristine bone after alternative strategies (tilting, basal bone, short implants) excluded

### Statement 3: Implant Number—Mandible (Evidence: High, 94% agreement)
- Four-implant design: 5-year cumulative survival 98.6%
- Six-implant design: 5-year cumulative survival 99.4%
- p = 0.136 (no significant difference); n = 943 patients, 5989 implants (Caramês et al.)
- Six implants preferred: bruxism, severe ridge atrophy, uncontrolled systemic comorbidities, full opposing fixed dentition
- Four-implant terminal minimum geometry: length ≥ 10 mm, diameter ≥ 4 mm
- When minimum geometry cannot be achieved: six-implant configuration biomechanically preferable

### Statement 4: Tilted vs. Axial Implants (Evidence: High, 94% agreement)
- MBL difference: MD = 0.00 mm (95% CI −0.01 to 0.02; p = 0.75) — short-term
- Small differences may emerge at longer follow-up
- Tilted preferred when: enables avoidance of anatomical structures, reduces GBR need, improves A-P spread
- Conical connections preferred: MBL at 2–5yr — external hex 1.03 mm, flat-to-flat 0.73 mm, narrow-cone 0.45 mm
- Cantilever limit: ≤1.5× A-P spread; absolute max 15 mm (mandible), 10 mm (maxilla)
- s-CAIS accuracy: 1.11 mm coronal, 1.40 mm apical, 3.51° angular (latest meta-analysis)

### Statement 5: Prosthetic Materials (Evidence: High, 91% agreement)
- Monolithic zirconia: prosthesis survival 97.23%, complication rate 2.25% (mean follow-up 49.7 months)
- Veneered zirconia: chipping rates 15–54%
- Bruxism (probable): OR for implant failure = 2.189 (95% CI 1.337–3.583; p = 0.002); n = 27 studies, 12,369 implants
- Monolithic zirconia preferred in bruxers; minimum prosthetic vertical dimension 10 mm for zirconia

### Statement 6: Maintenance Protocols (Evidence: High, 98% agreement)
- Peri-implantitis risk reduction: ~75% with structured maintenance (meta-analytic evidence)
- Low-risk: 6-month recall; moderate-risk: 4-month; high-risk: 3-month
- Each visit: 6-site probing, BoP, FMPS, biofilm removal, home care, occlusal screening
- Radiographic intervals: annually (low), 12–18 mo (moderate), 6–12 mo (high)
- Intervention thresholds: PD ≥ 5 mm + BoP → non-surgical + antiseptic + re-eval 2 months; PD ≥ 6 mm or persistent BoP → antimicrobials + surgical referral; MBL ≥ 2 mm → surgical re-eval

### Statement 7: Systemic Risk Stratification (Evidence: Moderate, 94% agreement)
- Absolute contraindications: active malignancy (oral/maxillofacial), HD radiotherapy (>50–60 Gy), HD-BMA for oncology (R+/R++), ANC < 1000/mm³ (chemotherapy/transplant <6 mo), uncontrolled bleeding disorders, severe mental health conditions precluding consent
- Relative contraindications: HbA1c ≥ 7.5%, heavy smoking >10 cig/day, prior lower-dose HN RT, LD-BMA (osteoporosis), antiangiogenics, immunosuppression, corticosteroids ≥10 mg/day ≥3 months, Sjögren/xerostomia
- Advanced age alone: NOT a contraindication
- LD-BMA Rx (osteoporosis): feasible with multidisciplinary consultation + local infection control + informed consent
- Antibiotic prophylaxis: amoxicillin 2–3 g (single pre-op dose, 1 hour before) or clindamycin 600 mg; extend to 6 days post-op only with simultaneous GBR/sinus elevation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Evidence heterogeneity precluded formal meta-analysis for some questions.
- Panel predominantly Italian — generalizability limited to Italian clinical settings.
- Prosthetic material evidence (Statement 5) primarily short- to mid-term (< 10 years).
- Patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) not systematically addressed.
- Cost-effectiveness beyond scope.
- Limited evidence in systemically compromised patients (uncontrolled diabetes, immunosuppression, HN radiotherapy history).
- Future priorities: >15-year prospective studies (4 vs 6 implants), RCTs monolithic zirconia vs metal-acrylic, digital workflow evaluation, PROMs integration, cost-effectiveness analyses.
