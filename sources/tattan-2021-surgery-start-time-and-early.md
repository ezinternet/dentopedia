## Why Ingested
A novel, chance-finding candidate risk factor — surgery start time (time of day) — that no page in the wiki currently addresses, tested within a case-control cohort whose other findings (ridge augmentation, simultaneous grafting, intra-operative complications, sedation) map directly onto the procedural-risk cluster in [[wiki/implants/survival/clauser-2022-early-implant-failure-grafted]] and [[wiki/overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]; SST itself was non-significant and is recorded here as a hypothesis-generating null that needs replication.

## Three-line Summary
Single-center case-control study (University of Iowa academic dental setting; 61 patients with at least one early implant failure vs 140 age- and gender-matched, randomly selected non-EIF controls) evaluating surgery start time (SST) and other patient- and therapy-related variables for early implant failure (EIF). 

SST was **not** significantly associated with EIF (HR 1.9 for afternoon implant placement, 95% CI 0.9–3.9; p = .105), whereas pre-placement ridge augmentation (HR 7.5, 95% CI 2.2–25.1; p = .001), intra-operative complications (HR 5.9, 95% CI 2.2–16.3; p < .001), simultaneous soft tissue grafting (HR 5.03, 95% CI 1.3–19.5; p = .020), simultaneous bone grafting (HR 3.7, 95% CI 1.6–8.8; p = .002), and placement with sedation (HR 3.4, 95% CI 1.5–7.5; p = .002) significantly increased multivariable EIF risk.

Clinical implication: the procedural-complexity cluster (grafting, intra-operative complications, sedation) dominates the multivariable risk model, and the earlier-noticed time-of-day signal did not survive as a statistically significant effect — a single-center, abstract-only case-control finding that must be replicated before SST informs scheduling.

## 세줄요약
단일 기관 환자대조연구 (미국 아이오와대 학술 치과 환경; 조기 임플란트 실패 환자 61명 vs 나이·성별 매칭 무작위 비실패 대조군 140명) — 수술 시작 시각 (Surgery Start Time, SST)과 기타 환자·치료 관련 변인이 조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF)에 미치는 영향 평가.

SST는 EIF와 유의하게 연관되지 않았다 (오후 식립 위험비 (Hazard Ratio, HR) 1.9, 95% CI 0.9–3.9; p = .105). 반면 식립 전 치조제 증대 (HR 7.5, 95% CI 2.2–25.1; p = .001), 수술 중 합병증 (HR 5.9, 95% CI 2.2–16.3; p < .001), 동시 연조직 이식 (HR 5.03, 95% CI 1.3–19.5; p = .020), 동시 골이식 (HR 3.7, 95% CI 1.6–8.8; p = .002), 진정 하 식립 (HR 3.4, 95% CI 1.5–7.5; p = .002)이 다변량 모델에서 EIF 위험을 유의하게 높였다.

임상적 의미: 술식 복잡도 군집 (이식·수술 중 합병증·진정)이 다변량 위험 예측을 지배하며, 앞서 주목된 시간대(오전·오후) 신호는 통계적 유의성에 도달하지 못했다 — 단일 기관·초록만 수집 환자대조연구 결과이므로 SST를 스케줄링 결정에 반영하기 전 재현 검증 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First characterization in the wiki of **surgery start time (SST)** as a candidate early-failure risk factor — framed as a novel/chance finding, and reported as **non-significant** (afternoon HR 1.9, 95% CI 0.9–3.9, p = .105).
- Quantifies a **procedural-complexity cluster** with large multivariable hazard ratios: pre-placement ridge augmentation HR 7.5, intra-operative complications HR 5.9, simultaneous soft tissue grafting HR 5.03, simultaneous bone grafting HR 3.7, placement with sedation HR 3.4.
- Reports effects on the HR (hazard-ratio) scale with full 95% CIs and p-values — richer effect-size reporting than many early-failure abstracts (which often give ORs without CIs).
- Suggests that operative-context factors (concurrent grafting, sedation, procedural difficulty) may carry more early-failure risk signal than chronological scheduling.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Case-control study.
- **Setting**: Academic dental setting (University of Iowa), USA.
- **Cases**: 61 patients who had at least one early implant failure (EIF).
- **Controls**: 140 age- and gender-matched, randomly selected, non-EIF controls.
- **Data source**: Electronic health records.
- **Variables**: surgery start time (SST) and other patient- and therapy-related variables.
- **Analysis**: bivariate and multivariable analyses to identify associations between EIF and the variables, including SST. Effect estimates reported as hazard ratios (HR) with 95% CI.

No bivariate model table, no control/exposure counts by SST group, and no SST definition (clock-hour cut-points) are available from the abstract — the exact definition of "afternoon" is not recoverable from the retrieved text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Single-center** academic-institution case-control with a modest sample (61 cases / 140 controls) — **the SST finding (and the larger HRs) need replication in other settings before use.**
- **SST is a novel/chance finding**: the abstract reports it as non-significant, so it is hypothesis-generating rather than an established risk factor; effect direction (afternoon > morning, HR 1.9) is suggestive but CI crosses 1.
- **Abstract-only retrieval** (`full_text: false`): the SST definition (time cut-points), the bivariate model, adjustment set, and matching details beyond age/gender are not available; residual confounding (case complexity, operator, case-mix) cannot be assessed.
- **Confounding within the "complexity" cluster**: pre-placement ridge augmentation, simultaneous bone/soft tissue grafting, and intra-operative complications are correlated with one another and with case difficulty — individual HRs likely share variance.
- **Very wide CIs** on several HRs (e.g. pre-placement ridge augmentation 2.2–25.1; simultaneous soft tissue grafting 1.3–19.5) indicate imprecision from the small case count.

## 7. Glossary
- **SST**: Surgery start time — the clock time at which implant placement began (morning vs afternoon in this study).
- **EIF**: Early implant failure — loss of the implant before prosthetic loading / in the early post-placement window.
- **HR**: Hazard ratio — effect estimate from the multivariable model (reported here rather than OR).
- **Ridge augmentation**: Pre-implant bone augmentation of the alveolar ridge before implant placement.
- **Simultaneous bone / soft tissue grafting**: Grafting performed during the same surgery as implant placement.
