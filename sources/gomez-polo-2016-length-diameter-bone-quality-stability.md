## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/isq/lages-2018-isq-insertion-torque-correlation-sr]]가 IT–ISQ 상관관계를 SR 수준(12편)에서 다뤘다면, 본 논문은 단일 longitudinal 임상연구(n=88 implants)로 그 상관관계뿐 아니라 임플란트 직경·길이·골질이 IT/1차 ISQ/2차 ISQ 각각에 미치는 독립적 영향을 다변량 회귀로 분해해 보강한다. 또한 [[implants/isq/javed-2013-primary-stability-osseointegration-factors-influence]]가 정리한 "골질·임플란트 형태·술식" 3요인 프레임워크에 구체적 수치(직경별 IT 26.5 vs 33.8 N/cm, 골타입별 ISQ 70.5–77.4)를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Longitudinal clinical study of 88 self-tapping InHex implants (63 partially edentulous patients), analyzing the effect of implant diameter (3.75 vs 4.25 mm), length (10 vs 11.5 mm), and Lekholm-Zarb bone quality on insertion torque (IT) and primary/secondary implant stability quotient (ISQ).

IT and primary ISQ were significantly affected by diameter (26.5 vs 33.8 N/cm; 75.0 vs 77.0 ISQ) and bone type (type 1+2: 34.9 N/cm/77.4 ISQ vs type 4: 20.6 N/cm/70.5 ISQ), but not by length; IT correlated moderately with primary ISQ (Pearson r=0.56, P<.01) but not with secondary ISQ (r=0.14, P=.097); secondary ISQ showed regression-to-the-mean behavior (high primary ISQ values decreased, low values increased over the 8-week osseointegration period).

Diameter and bone density are the dominant drivers of primary mechanical stability, while length has minimal influence in this range (10–11.5 mm); IT should not be used as a proxy for post-osseointegration (secondary) stability.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 종적 임상연구(63명 부분무치악 환자, InHex self-tapping 임플란트 88본)로 임플란트 직경(3.75/4.25mm)·길이(10/11.5mm)·Lekholm-Zarb 골질 분류가 삽입토크 (Insertion Torque, IT) 및 1차/2차 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)에 미치는 영향을 분석.

직경과 골타입이 IT·1차 ISQ에 유의한 영향(직경별 IT 26.5 vs 33.8 N/cm, 골타입 1+2 대비 타입4 ISQ 70.5)을 미쳤으나 길이는 유의한 영향 없음; IT와 1차 ISQ는 중등도 상관(Pearson r=0.56, P<.01)이나 2차 ISQ와는 무상관(r=0.14, P=.097).

직경·골밀도가 1차(기계적) 안정성의 주요 결정인자이며 이 범위(10–11.5mm)에서 길이의 영향은 미미; IT는 2차(생물학적) 안정성의 대리지표로 사용해서는 안 됨(초기 높은 ISQ는 시간이 지나며 감소, 낮은 ISQ는 증가하는 평균회귀 경향 관찰).

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery 2016;74(7):1344-1353
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.joms.2016.03.011
- **Institution**: Complutense University of Madrid, School of Dentistry / San Carlos Hospital, Madrid, Spain

## 2. Key Contributions
- Directly compares IT, primary ISQ, and secondary ISQ across the same 3 independent variables (diameter, length, bone type) in one cohort, with multivariate linear regression quantifying each predictor's contribution (R² 0.097–0.252).
- Demonstrates a moderate, statistically significant Pearson correlation between IT and primary ISQ (r=0.56, P<.01) but no significant correlation with secondary ISQ (r=0.14, P=.097; Spearman 0.19, P=.036 — inconsistent between tests), reinforcing that IT is a primary-stability-only measure.
- Identifies a regression-to-the-mean pattern in the primary→secondary ISQ transition: implants with the highest primary ISQ (mean 78.6) tended to decrease by the 8-week secondary measurement, while those with the lowest primary ISQ (mean 73.8) tended to increase (Tukey post hoc, P<.01), all implants converging toward the group mean.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective longitudinal clinical study (single surgeon, single implant system)
- **n**: 88 self-tapping InHex implants (Mozo-Grau, Valladolid, Spain; grade IV pure titanium) in 63 partially edentulous patients treated Jan–Dec 2014
- **Groups**: Diameter 3.75 mm (n=49) vs 4.25 mm (n=39); Length 10 mm (n=29) vs 11.5 mm (n=59); Lekholm-Zarb bone type 1+2 merged (n=37), type 3 (n=43), type 4 (n=8)
- **Measurements**: IT recorded at final seating (motor cutoff, 15–50 N/cm range); ISQ via Osstell RFA with SmartPeg, mean of buccolingual + mesiodistal axes, at placement (primary) and 8 weeks post-placement per conventional loading protocol (secondary)
- **Statistics**: Paired Student t test, 1-way ANOVA with Tukey post hoc, Pearson/Spearman correlation, multivariate linear regression (categorical bone-type variable dummy-coded); IBM SPSS 22.0, α=.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Mean IT: 29.8 ± 10.7 N/cm (range 15.0–50.0). Diameter: 26.5 vs 33.8 N/cm (P=.002). Bone type: 34.9 (1+2) / 27.1 (3) / 20.6 (4) N/cm (P<.001). Length: not significant (28.3 vs 30.5 N/cm, P=.361).
- Mean primary ISQ: 75.9 ± 4.8 (range 63.0–85.0). Diameter: 75.0 vs 77.0 (P=.043, small effect). Bone type: 77.4 (1+2) / 75.6 (3) / 70.5 (4) (P=.011). Length: not significant.
- Mean secondary ISQ: 76.6 ± 4.1 (range 64.0–86.0). Diameter: 77.4 (3.75mm) vs 75.5 (4.25mm) (P=.029 — inverse direction vs primary). Length: 77.8 (10mm) vs 76.0 (11.5mm), borderline (P=.075). Bone type: not significant (P=.529).
- IT vs primary ISQ: Pearson r=0.56, Spearman r=0.57 (both P<.01), R²≈32%. IT vs secondary ISQ: Pearson r=0.14 (P=.097, NS), Spearman r=0.19 (P=.036).
- Multivariate regression: IT model (diameter + bone 1+2) explained 25.2% variance; primary ISQ model (bone 1+2 + bone 3) explained 15.8%; secondary ISQ model (diameter + length) explained only 9.7%.
- Primary→secondary ISQ transition: 45/88 (51.1%) increased, 33/88 (37.5%) decreased, 10/88 (11.4%) unchanged; mean variation ~4.6–4.7 ISQ points in either direction; implants that decreased had higher starting primary ISQ (78.6) than those that increased (73.8), P<.01.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single implant system (InHex, self-tapping), single surgeon, single institution — generalizability to other macro-designs/surfaces not established.
- Narrow length range compared (10 vs 11.5 mm only) — may understate length effects seen at more extreme lengths in other studies.
- Bone type classified by surgeon tactile sense (Lekholm-Zarb), not CT/CBCT densitometry — subject to inter-observer variability despite cited reliability.
- Secondary ISQ measured at a single fixed 8-week timepoint; no longer-term follow-up to see whether the regression-to-the-mean pattern persists or reverses.
- Low R² (9.7–25.2%) across regression models indicates diameter/length/bone-type explain only a minority of stability variance — unmeasured factors (surgical technique, cortical engagement, individual bone remodeling rate) likely dominate.

## 6. Related Work
- lages-2018 (SR, 12 studies): pooled IT–ISQ correlation NS (r=0.366, P=.079) — this single-cohort study found a significant r=0.56 for primary ISQ specifically, illustrating between-study heterogeneity that the SR captures.
- javed-2013 (narrative review): established the 3-factor framework (bone quality, implant geometry, surgical technique) that this paper's design directly operationalizes for 2 of those factors (diameter as geometry proxy, bone type).
- kim-2009: system-agnostic bone-driven ISQ healing curve — consistent with this paper's finding that bone type dominates primary but not secondary ISQ variance.

## 7. Glossary
- **IT (Insertion Torque)**: Rotational resistance (N/cm) recorded during implant placement; a proxy for primary mechanical engagement/bone density.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: 0–100 scale output of resonance frequency analysis (RFA), reflecting implant-bone interface stiffness.
- **Primary stability**: Mechanical stability from bone engagement at placement, prior to any biological remodeling.
- **Secondary stability**: Biological stability from osseointegration/new bone apposition, measured post-healing (here at 8 weeks).
- **Lekholm-Zarb classification**: 4-category tactile/radiographic bone quality classification (type 1 = dense homogeneous to type 4 = thin cortex/low-density trabecular core).
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: Non-invasive method using a transducer (SmartPeg) to measure implant-bone interface resonance frequency, converted to ISQ.
