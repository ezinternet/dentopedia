## Why Ingested

[[implants/saxena-2024-guided-implant-drilling-bone-temperature]]의 2024년 SR은 "가이드 드릴링이 슬리브의 외부 관주 차단으로 골온도를 높인다"는 결론을 11편 종합으로 내렸지만, 그 SR이 인용한 원 실험 데이터의 세부(2×2 factorial, entrance/middle/bottom 깊이별 통계)를 직접 확인할 필요가 있었다. 본 논문(Marković 2016)은 가이드 vs 비가이드 × 25°C vs 5°C saline의 2×2 in vitro 실험으로 그 SR의 핵심 근거 중 하나이며, 냉각 saline(5°C)의 효과 크기를 depth별 수치로 제공해 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro thermographic study, 48 bovine rib specimens, 2×2 factorial design (surgical drill guide present/absent × saline irrigant at 25°C vs 5°C cooled), measuring bone temperature change at 3 osteotomy depths (entrance/middle/bottom) during pilot-drill implant site preparation.

Drill guide use significantly raised bone temperature at the osteotomy entrance only (ANOVA-type statistic 30.952, p<0.001; max increase 7.48°C with guide + 25°C saline); cooled 5°C saline significantly lowered bone temperature at all three depths (p<0.001 at entrance/middle/bottom) regardless of guide use, and also reduced drill-guide heating itself (median difference 2.785–7.185°C, p<0.001); guide-temperature and entrance bone-temperature were strongly correlated (Spearman rho=0.868, p<0.001).

None of the experimental conditions exceeded the 47°C/1-minute thermal-necrosis threshold, but guided surgery generates measurably more heat at the cortical entrance where irrigation is most obstructed by the metal sleeve — cooled irrigant is the most consistent, technique-independent mitigation; limitations include bovine-rib (not human jaw) bone model and dry-field infrared measurement (no active fluid during recording).

## 세줄요약

체외(In Vitro) 열화상 연구, 소 늑골(Bovine Rib) 시편 48개, 2×2 요인설계(수술용 드릴 가이드 사용/미사용 × 관주액 온도 25°C vs 냉각 5°C), 파일럿 드릴(Pilot Drill)로 임플란트 식립부 형성 시 3개 깊이(입구/중간/바닥)에서 골온도 변화 측정.

드릴 가이드 사용은 식립부 입구(Entrance)에서만 골온도를 유의하게 상승시켰고(ANOVA형 통계량 30.952, p<0.001; 가이드+25°C 조건 최대 상승 7.48°C), 냉각 5°C 식염수는 가이드 사용 여부와 무관하게 3개 깊이 모두에서 골온도 상승을 유의하게 감소시켰으며(p<0.001), 드릴 가이드 자체의 온도 상승도 줄였다(중앙값 차이 2.785–7.185°C, p<0.001); 가이드 온도와 입구부 골온도는 강한 양의 상관관계(Spearman rho=0.868, p<0.001)를 보였다.

모든 실험 조건에서 골괴사 역치(47°C/1분)를 초과하지 않았으나, 가이드 수술은 금속 슬리브가 관주를 차단하는 피질골 입구부에서 측정 가능한 수준의 추가 발열을 유발함 — 냉각 관주액이 술식과 무관하게 가장 일관된 완화책이며, 소 늑골 모델(인간 하악골과 상이)과 건조 조건 적외선 측정(측정 중 관주액 미분사)이라는 한계가 있다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Vojnosanitetski Pregled (Vojnosanit Pregl) 2016;73(8):744–750
- **DOI**: 10.2298/VSP141208041M
- **Institution**: Faculty of Dentistry, University of Belgrade / Military Medical Academy, Belgrade, Serbia

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study to isolate the interaction (or lack thereof) between surgical drill guide use and irrigant temperature on bone thermal change using real-time infrared thermography at 3 discrete osteotomy depths.
- Demonstrates the drill guide's thermal penalty is depth-specific (significant only at entrance/cortical level), not uniform along the osteotomy.
- Quantifies drill-guide-sleeve heating itself and shows it correlates strongly with adjacent bone temperature (rho=0.868), supporting a conduction mechanism (metal-sleeve friction heat → adjacent cortical bone) in addition to the irrigation-obstruction mechanism.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro, randomized 2×2 factorial (drill guide: with/without; saline: 25°C/5°C), bovine rib model.
- **n**: 48 specimens (from 4 bovine ribs, 12 per experimental group).
- **Instruments**: Varioscan R high-resolution 3021 infrared thermographic camera (±0.03°C resolution); pilot drill 2.35 mm diameter, 13 mm intraosseous length (SKYplanX, Bredent); conventional handpiece (W&H); drill speed 600 rpm; irrigation 50 mL/min.
- **Outcomes**: Primary — bone thermal change (max − baseline) at entrance/middle/bottom. Secondary — drill-guide temperature change; Spearman correlation between guide and entrance bone temperature.
- **Statistics**: Brunner and Langer nonparametric analysis for longitudinal data (ANOVA-type statistic); Exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test for guide-temperature comparison; Cabilio-Masaro symmetry test; Spearman correlation. Two-sided p<0.05 (one-sided for the guide-temperature Wilcoxon test).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Entrance: guide effect significant (ATS=30.952, df=1, p<0.001); saline-temperature effect significant (ATS=85.576, df=1, p<0.001); interaction NS (ATS=0.955, p=0.328). Max increase 7.48°C (guide + 25°C saline).
- Middle: saline-temperature effect significant (ATS=36.814, p<0.001); guide effect NS (ATS=3.637, p=0.056); interaction NS (p=0.742).
- Bottom: saline-temperature effect significant (ATS=26.064, p<0.001); guide effect NS (ATS=0.072, p=0.787); interaction NS (p=0.298); mean increase <1°C under all conditions.
- Drill-guide temperature change: 25°C saline mean 5.07±2.56°C vs 5°C saline mean 0.02±1.91°C; median difference significant (p<0.001, 95% CI 2.785–7.185).
- Guide-temperature vs entrance bone-temperature: Spearman rho=0.868, p<0.001.
- No experimental condition exceeded the 47°C/1-min necrosis threshold.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Bovine rib model differs thermally/structurally from human jaw bone; red wax substitute for oral soft tissue.
- Dry-field infrared measurement required isolating the site from irrigant (cofferdam) during recording — cannot capture real-time fluid-cooling dynamics.
- Only the pilot drill (mandatory first drill in guided protocols) was tested — later, wider drills used after guide removal were not evaluated.
- Flapless approach only (worst-case thermal scenario: double barrier of guide + soft tissue) — not directly generalizable to flapped guided surgery, though cited literature (Migliorati et al.) found no significant flap-vs-flapless difference.
- Outcome assessor not blinded to surgical approach (stent outline visible on thermograms), though blind to saline temperature.

## 6. Related Work
- Misir et al. 2009 (J Oral Maxillofac Surg): found significantly higher bone temperatures at ALL depths with guided drilling — contrasts with this study's entrance-only finding; attributed to their cortical-only bone model / higher drilling speed.
- Migliorati et al. 2013 (Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants): internal bone temperature during guided surgery, in vitro — found flap vs flapless guided surgery difference insignificant.
- Isler et al. 2011 (Int J Med Sci): irrigation-temperature effect on bone healing — higher osteoblast activity and bone marrow dynamics with 4°C vs 25°C saline, though amount of newly formed bone unaffected.
- [[implants/saxena-2024-guided-implant-drilling-bone-temperature]]: 2024 systematic review synthesizing 11 studies (including conceptually related work) on guided-vs-non-guided osteotomy heat, reaching the same directional conclusion (guide sleeves obstruct irrigation → higher peak temperature) and recommending chilled (~10°C) saline as the most consistent mitigation.

## 7. Glossary
- **Osteotomy**: Surgical preparation (drilling) of the bone site for implant placement.
- **Thermal bone necrosis**: Bone cell death from excessive heat; classically defined threshold of 47°C sustained for ≥1 minute (Eriksson & Albrektsson).
- **Brunner-Langer nonparametric analysis**: Statistical method for longitudinal/repeated-measures data without assuming normal distribution, used here for the 2×2 factorial with 3 depth levels.
- **Guide sleeve**: Metal or plastic cylinder embedded in a surgical drill guide/stent that constrains drill trajectory but also physically obstructs external irrigant flow to the drill tip.
