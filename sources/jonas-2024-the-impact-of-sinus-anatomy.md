## Why Ingested

조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure) 주제 배치의 일부로, 상악동 거상술이라는 특정 맥락에서 상악동 해부구조 (골 중격, 외측벽 두께)와 흡연이 EIF와 연관됨을 보고하는 후향적 코호트다. 일반 EIF 위험요인 문헌에 부위 특이적 해부학 변수를 보강한다. → [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]

## Three-line Summary

Single-center retrospective cohort (Rabin Medical Center, Israel) of 159 lateral-window sinus floor augmentations in 143 patients (residual ridge height 3.8 ± 1.4 mm; xenograft Bio-Oss; simultaneous or delayed placement), analysed per sinus, asking whether preoperative CBCT/MDCT sinus anatomy predicts early implant failure (EIF, defined as implant removal before temporary or permanent abutment attachment).

At least one EIF occurred in 21 of 159 sinuses (13.2%); in the multivariable GEE model bony septa were associated with lower odds of EIF (OR 0.13, p = .033), smoking with higher odds (OR 6.09, p = .0019), and lateral wall thickness was borderline (OR 0.67 per mm, p = .054), while residual bone height, Schneiderian membrane perforation (18.2%), diabetes and sinus shape showed no significant association.

The authors argue pre-surgical planning should look beyond residual bone height to sinus anatomy, but the design is retrospective with multiple surgeons, only 21 events, and the lateral wall effect was not significant at the 5% level in the adjusted model despite being called protective in the conclusions.

## 세줄요약

이스라엘 라빈 메디컬센터의 단일기관 후향적 코호트: 측방 접근 상악동 거상술 (lateral window sinus floor augmentation) 159건 (환자 143명, 잔존 치조골 높이 3.8 ± 1.4 mm, Bio-Oss 이종골 이식, 동시 또는 지연 식립)을 상악동 단위로 분석하여, 술 전 CBCT/MDCT상 상악동 해부구조가 조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure, EIF; 임시 또는 최종 지대주 연결 전 임플란트 제거)를 예측하는지 평가했다.

159개 상악동 중 21개 (13.2%)에서 EIF가 1개 이상 발생했고, 다변량 GEE 모델에서 골 중격 (bony septa) 존재는 EIF 오즈 감소 (OR 0.13, p = .033), 흡연은 오즈 증가 (OR 6.09, p = .0019), 외측벽 두께는 경계 수준 (mm당 OR 0.67, p = .054)이었으며, 잔존골 높이, 상악동 점막 천공 (18.2%), 당뇨, 상악동 형태는 유의하지 않았다.

저자들은 술 전 계획에서 잔존골 높이 외에 상악동 해부구조를 평가해야 한다고 주장하나, 후향적 설계에 다수 술자, 사건 21건에 불과하고, 다변량에서 외측벽 효과는 5% 수준에서 유의하지 않았음에도 결론에서 보호인자로 서술되어 있어 과대해석에 주의해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Radiographic assessment (CBCT/MDCT) of sinus anatomy (septa, alveolar antral artery, membrane thickness, lateral wall width, residual height, shape) against EIF after lateral-window augmentation.
- Authors state this is one of the first studies of the lateral wall width and of septa in relation to EIF.
- Reports a multivariable (GEE) finding that septa are associated with lower EIF odds and smoking with higher odds.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, single center, ethics approval 0674-19-RMC, informed consent signed; consecutive patients.
- **Inclusion**: age over 18, lateral-approach sinus floor augmentation, implants placed and followed at least to temporary or permanent rehabilitation, preoperative CBCT or MDCT. Exclusion: incomplete documentation.
- **Surgery**: patients with residual height under 5 mm; lateral window (Boyne and James); perforations covered with cross-linked collagen membrane (Ossix Plus); xenograft Bio-Oss; simultaneous placement or delayed placement 4 months after augmentation (exposure after further 5 months); prosthetic loading 9 months postoperatively.
- **Unit of analysis**: each sinus. Two surgeons measured scans twice (2-week interval); disagreement over 5% resolved by a third.
- **EIF definition**: removal of an implant before attachment of a temporary or permanent abutment; one or more EIF in a sinus classifies the sinus as EIF.
- **Statistics**: chi-square, Mann-Whitney; multivariable Generalized Estimating Equations (binomial, logit, exchangeable correlation), variables with univariate p under .1, VIF under 5; p under 5% significant.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design; multiple surgeons of differing experience (stated by the authors).
- Only 21 EIF sinuses and about 14 smokers (8.8% of 159), so estimates are imprecise; CIs not reported in the text.
- Authors postulate residual height null result may reflect single minimum-height measurement across a parabolic sinus floor; suggest per-implant-site assessment.
- Internal inconsistency: lateral wall width has p = .054 in the multivariable model, yet the Conclusions call thicker lateral walls "statistically significant protective factors" and the Discussion quotes a 33% reduction per mm as a risk factor finding. Also the Results sentence says septa "correlated with procedural success". Table values are not in the retrieved text.
- Mechanistic explanations (septa as extra bone source) are the authors' assumptions, not tested.
- Single-center, Bio-Oss only, residual height under 5 mm; generalisability limited.

## 7. Glossary

- **EIF (Early Implant Failure, 조기 임플란트 실패)**: here, removal before abutment attachment.
- **Underwood septa (골 중격)**: bony partitions within the maxillary sinus.
- **AAA (Alveolar Antral Artery, 치조동맥)**: vessel in the lateral sinus wall.
- **GEE (Generalized Estimating Equations, 일반화 추정 방정식)**: regression accounting for within-patient clustering.
- **Schneiderian membrane (상악동 점막)**: sinus lining mucosa.
