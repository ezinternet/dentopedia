## Why Ingested

UV-PF 클러스터에 소규모 임상 근거가 추가됨. Lang 2022([[implants/surface/lang-2022-photofunctionalization-implant-stability-marginal-bone-loss-sr-ma]])의 ISQ 개선 메타분석과 같은 방향의 임상 데이터를 사포처리 산부식 (Sandblasted, Acid-etched, SA) + UV 조사 프로토콜로 split-mouth에서 직접 측정하며, in vitro 세포 증식 개선이 같은 조건에서 확인됐는지를 병렬 보고하는 보기 드문 혼합 설계다.

## One-line Summary

In vitro + split-mouth clinical study (n=10, 20 implants, 14-week ISQ follow-up): UV irradiation of SA titanium disks significantly increased cell proliferation at 48 h and implant stability at measurement intervals 3, 4, and 6; wettability and surface roughness improved; type I collagen unaffected.

## 한줄요약

In vitro + split-mouth 임상 연구 (n=10, 임플란트 20개, 14주 ISQ 추적): 사포처리 산부식 (SA) 티타늄에 자외선 조사 시 세포 증식(48시간) 및 임플란트 안정성지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ; 측정 주기 3·4·6)에서 유의 개선, 1형 콜라겐 생성은 차이 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Parallel in vitro + clinical design: confirms cell-level and clinical-level responses in the same paper for the same UV protocol.
- In vitro finding: UV irradiation improved contact angle and roughness of SA disks; cell proliferation increased at 48 h (p<0.05); viability and type I collagen were unaffected.
- Clinical finding: UV-irradiated implants showed significantly greater ISQ stability at measurement intervals 3, 4, and 6 (biweekly assessments up to week 14) — consistent with the accelerated osseointegration signal of the broader literature.
- Notable null finding: type I collagen production did not differ — suggests UV-PF affects early attachment/proliferation kinetics but not late-stage matrix synthesis.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **In vitro**: smooth machined Ti disks and SA Ti disks (irradiated 30 min or not); wettability, roughness (Ra), cytotoxicity, cell proliferation (time points not specified), adhesion, type I collagen
- **Clinical**: 10 patients, randomly assigned SA + UV (5 min pre-treatment) on one side, non-irradiated on contralateral side; ISQ measured biweekly by Osstell device to week 14
- **Statistics**: not specified in abstract

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Contact angle: SA alone highest; UV irradiation significantly decreased it (p<0.05)
- Roughness: SA + UV combination highest (p<0.05)
- Cell viability: no significant difference at any time point
- Cell proliferation: SA + UV significantly increased at 48 h (p<0.05)
- Type I collagen: no significant difference
- ISQ (clinical): UV-irradiated implants greater at measurement intervals 3, 4, 6 (p<0.05); intervals 1, 2, 5 not significant

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Very small sample (n=10 patients, 20 implants); underpowered for subgroup analyses
- 5-minute UV pre-treatment — shorter than most protocols (12–48 h standard, or 15–20 min in other clinical studies); actual UV device and wavelength not specified in abstract
- No wavelength specification: cannot determine whether this is UVC or UVA; limits interpretation in context of Houshmand 2023 wavelength dependency
- Primary endpoints are stability/ISQ through 14 weeks only; no longer-term MBL or survival data
- No histological or surface carbon analysis; mechanism is inferred

## 7. Glossary

- SA: sandblasted acid-etched surface (comparable to SLA)
- ISQ: Implant Stability Quotient (1–100 scale, resonance frequency analysis)
- Split-mouth: each patient serves as their own control (one side UV, one side not)
